# Risk Sensitivity and Stationary Equilibrium in Incomplete Markets

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究具有风险敏感偏好的不完全市场经济中的平稳均衡与比较静态。我们证明，对于任意非负的风险敏感程度，平稳均衡均存在。在收入独立的条件下，更高的风险敏感性在固定价格下弱性地提高总储蓄，并弱性地降低最小和最大均衡利率。在随机单调马尔可夫收入条件下，当较低的风险敏感性参数足够小或较高的参数足够大时，这些比较结论成立。然而，对于中间值的参数对，更高的风险敏感性可能降低储蓄，这与文献中已有结果相反，并可能提高最小和最大均衡利率。一个精确分解识别了驱动这些反转的力量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study stationary equilibrium and comparative statics in an incomplete market economy with risk-sensitive preferences. We prove that a stationary equilibrium exists for every nonnegative degree of risk sensitivity. With independent income, greater risk sensitivity weakly raises aggregate saving at fixed prices and weakly lowers the smallest and the largest equilibrium interest rates. With stochastically monotone Markov income, these comparisons hold when the lower risk-sensitivity parameter is sufficiently small or the upper parameter sufficiently large. For pairs of intermediate values, however, greater risk sensitivity can lower saving, contrary to an existing result in the literature, and can raise the smallest and largest equilibrium rates. An exact decomposition identifies the forces driving these reversals.
