# CFP-R4 Survives Severe Tests on Inflation Expectations and Climate Belief

[查看原文](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24116951)

## 一句话结论

> 论文用密歇根消费者调查、CPI与汽油价格数据以及气候信念调查，检验CFP Route 4（信息限制）关于人们能察觉大幅显著价格变化却忽略缓慢月度趋势的预测，结果显示该假说在通胀预期与气候信念两项独立测试中均未被证伪。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

校正冻结问题（Correction Freeze Problem, CFP）的路径4——信息限度——在两项新测试中经受住了为其设置的所有证伪条件，这两项测试分别针对通胀预期和气候信念。两项测试的设计与运行均独立于CFP的表述方式，也独立于任何希望其通过的意愿：预测均从CFP自身文本中推导得出，并在数据开放之前即已固定。通胀测试使用了2000至2026年的官方月度数据（密歇根大学消费者调查、美国劳工统计局CPI-U、美国能源信息署汽油价格）。路径4预测人们能察觉大幅、显著的价格变化，却会遗漏缓慢的月度趋势，而数据恰好显示了这一模式：a）在汽油价格变动达到或超过10%的42个月中，预期通胀率同向变动的比例为94%，而其他月份为62%。b）预期变化与月度CPI趋势方向一致的比例为51%，处于随机水平，接近预测的最佳可能准确率0.525。气候测试将“美国人心中的气候变化”（Climate Change in the American Mind, CCAM）调查（2008-2025年）的37,417名受访者与NOAA区域温度数据进行了匹配。该测试将长期区域变暖与最近12个月的天气状况加以区分。认为全球变暖正在发生的信念对长期趋势未表现出正向响应，这与路径4的预测一致；预注册的证伪条件未被触发。有两项发现对这些结果构成限定：1）月度CPI趋势对预期水平有一个虽小但统计上真实的效果。2）近期天气按预测方向影响了气候信念，但未达到显著水平。预期与已经经历的通胀（相关系数0.74）的追踪紧密程度远高于与尚未到来的通胀（0.32）。因此，路径4仍然成立：它经受了独立数据的检验，本可能失败，但并未失败。这些结果是存续，而非最终确认。决定性测试（在县级层面、在新数据发布之前进行）已被明确界定且触手可及。CFP的其他三条路径（阈值棘轮、成本崩塌和模糊性替代）仍未经检验，因为识别其参数所需的数据尚不可得。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Route 4 of the Correction Freeze Problem (CFP), the information limit, survived every falsifier set for it in two new tests, on inflation expectations and on climate belief. Both tests were designed and run independently of how CFP was formulated and of any wish for it to pass: the predictions were derived from CFP’s own text and fixed before the data were opened. The inflation test used official monthly data for 2000-2026 (University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers, BLS CPI-U, EIA gasoline prices). Route 4 predicts that people detect large, salient price changes and miss the slow monthly trend, and the data show exactly that pattern: a) In the 42 months when gasoline moved 10% or more, expected inflation moved in the same direction 94% of the time, against 62% in other months. b) Changes in expectations matched the direction of the monthly CPI trend 51% of the time, which is chance level and close to the predicted best-possible accuracy of 0.525. The climate test matched 37,417 respondents of the Climate Change in the American Mind (CCAM) survey (2008-2025) to NOAA regional temperatures. It separated long-term regional warming from the most recent 12 months of weather. Belief that global warming is happening showed no positive response to the long-term trend, as Route 4 predicts; the pre-registered falsifier was not triggered. Two findings qualify these results: 1) The monthly CPI trend has a small but statistically real effect on the level of expectations. 2) Recent weather moved climate belief in the predicted direction, but not significantly. Expectations track inflation already experienced (correlation 0.74) far more closely than inflation still to come (0.32). Route 4 is therefore alive: it was exposed to independent data and could have failed, and it did not. These results are survival, not final confirmation. The decisive tests, at county level and before new data are released, are specified and within reach. The other three routes of CFP (threshold ratchet, cost collapse and ambiguity substitution) remain untested, because the data needed to identify their parameters are not yet available.
