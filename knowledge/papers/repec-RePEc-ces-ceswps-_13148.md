<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

稳定匹配机制因其理想的理论特性，已在全球范围内的学校选择和大学招生系统中得到广泛应用。但当市场中很大一部分学生未注册其被分配的项目时，其中一些理想特性便不再成立。利用SISU（Sistema de Seleção Unificada，负责协调巴西最大的大学招生系统的巴西平台）的数据，我们发现通过该系统录取的学生中约有半数在机制的第一阶段未进行注册。我们表明，这导致很大比例的学生对其向匹配系统报告偏好感到后悔。此外，较低的注册率促使许多学生采取策略性行为。我们发现，低注册率的主要原因是存在许多不参与集中清算中心的机构。我们针对这一问题提出了若干解决方案，例如为学生提供关于录取概率信息的更好获取渠道。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Stable matching mechanisms have been widely implemented worldwide in school choice and college admission systems due to their desirable theoretical properties. But when a large fraction of students in the market do not enroll in their assigned program, some of these desirable properties cease to hold. Using data from SISU (Sistema de Seleção Unificada), the Brazilian platform responsible for coordinating Brazil's largest college admission system, we find that roughly half of the students admitted through this system do not enroll in the first phase of the mechanism. We show that this drives a large proportion of students to experience regret regarding the preferences they reported to the matchmaker. Moreover, low enrollment rates incentivize many students to behave strategically. We find that the leading cause of low enrollment rates is the existence of many institutions that do not participate in the centralized clearinghouse. We propose a few solutions to this problem, such as granting students better access to information regarding enrollment probabilities.

</details>
