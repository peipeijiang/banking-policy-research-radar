<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们分析了具有相互依赖价值和横向差异化投标者的拍卖，其中对一个投标者的好消息对其他投标者而言是坏消息。这产生了一种“赢家之喜”（winner's bliss），逆转了众所周知的“赢家诅咒”（winner's curse）：获胜意味着购买了一件比朴素估计所暗示的更有价值的物品。我们的主要结果关于英式拍卖（English auction）。我们引入了“自身效应性质”（own effect property），推广了单交叉条件（single-crossing condition），以证明有效的事后均衡（ex-post equilibrium）的存在性。关于收益，我们证明在关联信号（affiliated signals）下，英式拍卖产生的收益不超过第二价格拍卖（second-price auction），而独立信号（independent signals）则产生标准的收益等价（revenue equivalence）。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We analyze auctions with interdependent values and horizontally differentiated bidders, where good news for one bidder is bad news for the other bidders. This generates a winner's bliss, reversing the well-known winner's curse: winning means buying an object that is more valuable than a naïve estimate suggests. Our main result is about the English auction. We introduce the ``own effect property,'' generalizing the single-crossing condition, to prove the existence of an efficient ex-post equilibrium. Regarding revenue, we show that with affiliated signals, the English auction yields no more revenue than the second-price auction, while independent signals yield the standard revenue equivalence.
