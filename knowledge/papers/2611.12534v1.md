<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个两人背包博弈，用以研究个体能力约束、共享资源竞争以及制度不对称条件下的资源配置问题。参与者选择可行的资源子集，其收益取决于竞争损失、分配规则以及不对称的制度收益与成本。我们推导了总福利函数，并证明分配系数在不直接影响总福利的情况下重新分配收益，而制度参数则通过其净贡献影响福利。因此，具有相同福利效应的制度安排可能引致不同的策略激励与纳什均衡结果。我们分析了能力约束与制度优先权如何塑造均衡配置及其福利性质。一个核心结果涉及共享资源的社会效率与策略稳定性之间的区分。我们推导了共享在社会意义上优于排他性分配但在策略上仍不稳定的条件，其原因在于非优先参与者可以通过放弃该资源来提高自身收益。反之，即使排他性分配带来更高的总福利，共享仍可能在策略上保持稳定。这些发现表明，在制度不对称条件下，社会效率与策略稳定性未必一致。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a two-player knapsack game to investigate resource allocation under individual capacity constraints, competition over shared resources, and institutional asymmetry. Players select feasible subsets of resources, while their payoffs depend on competitive losses, allocation rules, and asymmetric institutional benefits and costs. We derive the aggregate welfare function and show that allocation coefficients redistribute payoffs without directly affecting total welfare, whereas institutional parameters influence welfare through their net contribution. Consequently, institutional arrangements with identical welfare effects may induce different strategic incentives and Nash equilibrium outcomes. We analyze how capacity constraints and institutional priority shape equilibrium configurations and their welfare properties. A central result concerns the distinction between the social efficiency and strategic stability of shared resources. We derive conditions under which sharing is socially preferable to exclusive allocation yet remains strategically unstable because the non-priority player can increase their payoff by abandoning the resource. Conversely, sharing may remain strategically stable even when exclusive allocation yields higher aggregate welfare. These findings demonstrate that social efficiency and strategic stability need not coincide under institutional asymmetry.
