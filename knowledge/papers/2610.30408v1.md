# The Limits of Price Discrimination When the Market Is Unknown

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30408v1)

## 一句话结论

> 在仅知可能价值集合而不知市场分布的情形下，刻画价格歧视（市场分割）可实现的消费者剩余与生产者剩余组合构成凸多边形，并给出连续价值下的消费者剩余上界。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Bergemann、Brooks 和 Morris（2015）刻画了在价值分布已知时，市场细分所能产生的消费者剩余与生产者剩余组合。我仅固定可能价值的集合，并刻画某些市场与某些市场细分所能产生的组合。这些组合构成一个凸多边形。其外边缘是一个市场在统一定价利润被设上限时的最大有效剩余，扣除该利润后的净值，且截断的等收入市场能够达到该边界。在那些价值点上不需要市场细分；严格介于两个价值之间时，要达到前沿需要将市场拆分为两个极端市场。当价值连续分布至 $\bar{v}$ 时，若生产者剩余（卖方利润）为 $π$，消费者剩余至多为 $π\ln(\bar{v}/π)$，该上界无需市场细分即可达到，且没有任何市场能给消费者超过 $\bar{v}/e$ 的剩余。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Bergemann, Brooks, and Morris (2015) characterize the consumer and producer surplus pairs that segmentation can produce when the distribution of values is known. I fix only the set of possible values and characterize the pairs that some market and some segmentation can produce. They form a convex polygon. Its outer edge is the largest efficient surplus a market can have when its uniform-price profit is capped, net of that profit, and truncated equal-revenue markets attain it. At the values no segmentation is needed; strictly between two values, reaching the frontier requires splitting a market into two extremal markets. With a continuum of values up to $\bar{v}$, consumer surplus is at most $π\ln(\bar{v}/π)$ when producer surplus (the seller's profit) is $π$, this bound is reached without segmentation, and no market gives consumers more than $\bar{v}/e$.

</details>
