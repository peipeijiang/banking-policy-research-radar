# Protecting Losers Undermines Auction Credibility

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12204v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文研究第二价格拍卖中保护落败者估值信息与拍卖可信度之间的冲突，证明确定性私下通信协议下保护下尾隐私会迫使首个信息性问题暴露最高估值，从而使卖方能不可检测地操纵支付并增加收入。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

第二价格拍卖必须确定其价格，但无需了解落败者的估值低于该价格多少。我们表明，保护这些信息不被卖方获知与拍卖的可信性相冲突。在采用私人通信的确定性协议中，下尾隐私迫使第一个信息性问题揭示某竞标者是否拥有最高可能估值。卖方随后可以在没有任何竞标者察觉偏离的情况下操纵赢家的支付，在某些估值组合上增加收益，且从不降低收益。降序协议隐藏了低于价格的落败估值，但允许操纵；在强制出售的情况下，可信的升序协议会揭示这些估值。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A second-price auction must determine its price but need not learn how far losing values lie below it. We show that protecting this information from the seller conflicts with auction credibility. In deterministic protocols with private communication, lower-tail privacy forces the first informative question to reveal whether a bidder has the highest possible value. The seller can then manipulate the winner's payment without any bidder detecting the deviation, increasing revenue at some valuation profiles and never reducing it. A descending protocol conceals losing values below the price but permits manipulation; with mandatory sale, a credible ascending protocol reveals them.

</details>
