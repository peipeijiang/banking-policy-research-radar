# Social Network Structure, Wealth, and Wealth Inequality Across Cultures

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25488v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该研究通过全球46个社区的调查数据，分析物质财富与社会网络结构（如借贷、食物共享等）的关系，发现财富与网络连接数量及同质性相关，且财富不平等与网络隔离相关。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

尽管理论将财富不平等与社会结构联系在一起，但实证证据仅限于少数基于在线社交媒体数据的研究。本研究采用了一种截然不同的数据类型，将全球覆盖范围扩展至多种类型迥异的社会，并探讨了新的问题。具体而言，我们收集了全球46个社区中约3500个共享单元（家庭）的数据，这些社区代表了相当大的人类社会与文化多样性。在每个社区中，我们分析了人们的物质财富与社会网络结构之间的关系，这些网络包括借钱、分享食物、共同劳动、社交等。在几乎所有社区中，一个共享单元的物质财富与其提供帮助和接受帮助的其他共享单元数量呈正相关。共享单元的财富还与其所连接共享单元的相对财富相关——这是一种经济同质性现象。值得注意的是，财富不平等程度更高的社区，其网络结构也表现为较贫困的共享单元与较富裕的共享单元之间的联系较少。我们通过其他社区层面的环境、制度和经济属性来补充这一独特的多文化数据集，为未来研究财富与社会网络共同决定机制开辟了新途径。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Despite theory tying wealth inequality to social structure, empirical evidence has been limited to a few studies based on online social media data. This study uses a very different type of data, expands the global coverage to very different types of societies, and investigates new questions. In particular, we collect data from ~3500 sharing units (households) in 46 communities across the globe, representing considerable human social and cultural diversity. In each, we analyze the relationship between people's material wealth and the structure of social networks: borrowing money, sharing food, working together, socializing, etc. In almost all communities, a sharing unit's material wealth is positively associated with the number of other sharing units it both helps and is helped by. A sharing unit's wealth is also associated with the relative wealth of the sharing units to which it is linked---a form of economic homophily. Notably, communities with greater wealth inequality are also characterized by a network structure in which poorer sharing units are less well connected to wealthier ones. We augment our unique cross-cultural data with other community-level environmental, institutional, and economic attributes, opening new avenues for future research into the co-determination of wealth and social networks.
