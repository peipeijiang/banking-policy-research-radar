# Information for nothing and authority for free

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09409v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究无货币转移时委托人如何最优设计机制，发现最优机制无需代理人报告，而是赋予其自由信息和决策权。

<details open>
<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

委托人需要决定是否实施一个项目。她私下知晓成本，而代理人私下知晓收益。货币转移不可行，且与委托人相比，代理人并未完全内化成本。我们证明，委托人最优机制并不要求代理人报告信息。相反，该机制要么忽略代理人，要么赋予代理人免费信息与完全决策权。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A principal must decide whether to implement a project. She privately knows the cost, an agent privately knows the benefit. Monetary transfers are not available, and compared to the principal, the agent does not fully internalize the cost. We show that the principal-optimal mechanism does not require the agent to report. Instead, it either ignores the agent or endows the agent with free information and full decision authority.

</details>
