<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

自动化市场日益通过自动做市商（automated market makers, AMMs）路由来自学习型交易智能体的订单流。我们将交易者原型建模为由其奖励函数定义的人工智能体，并分析它们在恒定乘积AMM上产生的反馈。同等规模的羊群主导型市场与套利主导型市场具有相同的收益分布和换手率，因此任何基于波动率、风险价值、压力频率或成交量的规则都无法将它们区分开来；然而，它们的压力事件在持续时间上的差异程度随反馈噪声比$β/s$无界增长。序列统计量可从$Θ((s/β)^2\log(1/δ))$个观测中识别反馈符号，且该速率是紧的。当智能体共同交易时，逐利型智能体会顺势追随羊群而非逆势而为，导致全有或全无的参与模式。赚取买卖价差做市商能够恢复稳定性，但使流动性变得脆弱，表现为不连续的崩溃和滞后现象；有效的政策工具是做市商—吃单者定价（maker–taker pricing），而非统一费率。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Automated markets increasingly route order flow from learning trading agents through automated market makers (AMMs). We model trader archetypes as AI agents defined by their reward functions and analyze the feedback they create on a constant-product AMM. Herd-dominated and arbitrage-dominated markets of equal size have identical return distributions and turnover, so no rule based on volatility, value-at-risk, stress frequency, or volume can tell them apart; yet their stress episodes differ in length by a factor unbounded in the feedback-to-noise ratio $β/s$. Serial statistics identify the feedback sign from $Θ((s/β)^2\log(1/δ))$ observations, and this rate is tight. When agents trade together, profit-seeking agents ride herds rather than lean against them, leading to all-or-nothing participation. Spread-earning market makers restore stability but make liquidity fragile, with discontinuous collapse and hysteresis; maker--taker pricing, not a uniform fee, is the effective instrument.
