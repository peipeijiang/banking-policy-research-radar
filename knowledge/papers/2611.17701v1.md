# Truthful compromise and insurance on a sphere

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17701v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在没有转移支付的情况下，当行动强度固定时，公共选择能否为相互冲突的利益提供保障？我们要求对每一种递增凹的与私人理想一致性的评价都满足真实报告。在球面上，对于至多具有三个理想方向的偏好剖面，效率要求固定权重的随机独裁；在二维和三维中，较弱的检验条件即已足够。从四维开始，二元效率允许委员会通过将结果风险置于与其报告正交的方向来为成员提供保障。我们识别出所有具有这些激励和二元效率的机制所共有的限制：二元获胜概率决定了每个偏好剖面上的平均结果，以及平面三阵营偏好剖面上的个体一致性分布。当这些概率仅取决于阵营规模时，在平衡的等边分歧下实现完全保险要求维度与人口成正比。相比之下，委员会能够在与人口无关的维度中，将每个参与者相对于确定的零一致性而言的福利损失降低到等权重随机独裁损失的任意规定正比例。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Can public choice without transfers insure conflicting interests when action intensity is fixed? We require truthful reporting for every increasing-concave evaluation of alignment with private ideals. On a sphere, efficiency at profiles with at most three ideal directions forces fixed-weight random dictatorship; weaker tests suffice in two and three dimensions. From four dimensions, binary efficiency permits committees to insure members by placing outcome risk orthogonally to their reports. We identify restrictions shared by all mechanisms with these incentives and binary efficiency: binary winning probabilities determine mean outcomes at every profile and individual alignment distributions at planar three-camp profiles. When these probabilities depend only on camp size, complete insurance at balanced equilateral disagreement requires dimension proportional to population. In contrast, committees reduce each participant's welfare loss relative to certain zero alignment to any prescribed positive fraction of the equal-weight random-dictatorship loss in a population-independent dimension.
