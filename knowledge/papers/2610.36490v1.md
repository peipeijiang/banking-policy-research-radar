<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们将一个中介者引入到具有可验证（硬）证据的沟通环境中，并精确刻画了中介何时能够诱导出每一个服从性结果——即接收者愿意遵循的每一种依赖于类型的行动分布——包括实现发送者承诺（贝叶斯说服）收益的结果。对于类型无关偏好和一般偏好而言，每种类型都必须拥有一条只有该类型才能发送的信息，这是必要条件。该条件对于类型无关偏好也是充分的，而一般情形还要求各类型的效用满足特定的对齐关系。我们还通过类似于Blackwell实验比较的混淆条件，按照在（中介）均衡下能够诱导出的、以发送者类型为条件的行动分布，对证据结构进行了排序。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce a mediator to a communication setting with verifiable (hard) evidence and characterize precisely when mediation can induce every obedient outcome--every type-contingent distribution of actions that the receiver is willing to follow--including the outcome that delivers the sender's commitment (Bayesian persuasion) payoff. For both type-independent and general preferences, it is necessary for each type to have a message he alone can send. This is also sufficient for type-independent preferences, whereas the general case also requires a particular alignment of the types' utilities. We also rank evidence structures by the distributions of actions conditional on the sender's type that they can induce at (mediated) equilibrium, through garbling conditions analogous to Blackwell's comparison of experiments.

</details>
