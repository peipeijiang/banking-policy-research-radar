<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

利率上升可能通过降低保险公司资产的市场价值并提高保单持有人的外部回报，从而使其面临退保风险。本文构建了一个针对保险业特有挤兑机制的最小化模型，在该模型中，由于早期退保可能耗尽支撑续期价值的资产池，战略互动应运而生。我们分析了一个战略性退保博弈，其中挤兑是由资产价值、合同退保请求权以及依赖给付的续期利益共同决定的均衡结果。该模型为基本面驱动型挤兑和自我实现型挤兑得出了闭式利率阈值。资本化程度较薄会弱化联合退保的临界值。续期利益提高了退保阈值，但也可能产生战略互补性。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Rising interest rates can expose life insurers to surrender risk by reducing the market value of their assets and raising policyholders' outside returns. This paper develops a minimal model of an insurance-specific run mechanism in which strategic interaction arises because early surrenders can deplete the asset pool backing continuation values. We analyze a strategic surrender game in which a run is an equilibrium outcome determined jointly by asset values, contractual surrender claims, and payoff-dependent continuation benefits. The model yields closed-form interest-rate thresholds for fundamentals-driven and self-fulfilling runs. Thinner capitalization weakly lowers the joint-surrender cutoff. Continuation benefits raise surrender thresholds but can also create strategic complementarity.
