<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文提出以远低于传统理论所隐含成本的方式生成年金支付流。对于给定的投资范围，这能够设计一种成本最低的标准养老金，在规定期限内提供储蓄账户单位的精确月度支付。这种标准养老金动态投资于储蓄账户和股票市场的增长最优投资组合，以尽可能低的成本高精度地复制无风险支付。由于这种以数学为基础的标准养老金可靠，且成本比传统养老金低几个数量级，它为改善养老金体系的健康和为退休人员提供更多福利提供了独特的机会。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The paper proposes generating annuity payment streams at far lower costs than those implied by traditional theories. For a given investment universe, this enables the design of a least expensive standard pension, providing accurate monthly payments of savings account units over a set period. Such a standard pension invests dynamically in the savings account and the equity market's growth-optimal portfolio to replicate riskless payments with high accuracy at the lowest possible cost. Since this mathematically grounded standard pension is reliable and, by orders of magnitude, less expensive than traditional pensions, it provides a unique opportunity to improve the health of pension systems and provide more benefits for retirees.
