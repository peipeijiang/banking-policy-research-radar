<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

网络往往在确定哪些节点将持续存在之前就被构建。我们研究的是这样一种情景下的设计问题：节点独立失败，链接成本高昂且必须预先建立，幸存节点的收益随距离递减。示例包括供应商网络、银行间网络和基础设施网络。在所有可能的网络中，完全网络、星形网络和空网络配置在特定的链接成本范围内各自实现福利最优。然而，当节点数为四个或更多时，故障引入了一个成本区间，在该区间内每个最优网络包含的链接数多于星形网络但少于完全网络。在该区间的低端，我们证明最优网络恰好是从完全网络中移除一个匹配（matching）所得到的网络。我们针对完全多部网络（complete multipartite networks）开发了一种精确算法。该算法通过动态规划找到最优网络，其运算次数随节点数的四次方增长，尽管该类网络本身的数量呈超多项式增长。对于最多六个节点的网络，在明确条件下将设计限制在该类网络中不会造成福利损失。我们还刻画了该类网络中的成对稳定网络（pairwise-stable networks），并证明其对每一水平的链接成本均存在。最后，我们量化了误判故障风险的代价。高估节点存活率会导致过度建设，并可能产生低于不建设任何网络的福利水平，而低估节点存活率则永远不会如此。在故障风险不确定的情况下，设计应倾向于保守的可靠性估计。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Networks are frequently constructed before it is determined which nodes will persist. We address the design problem in scenarios where nodes fail independently, links are costly and must be established in advance, and the benefits to surviving nodes diminish with distance. Examples include supplier, interbank, and infrastructure networks. Among all possible networks, the complete, star, and empty configurations are each welfare-optimal within specific ranges of link costs. However, with four or more nodes, failures introduce a range of costs in which every optimal network contains more links than the star but fewer than the complete network. At the lower end of this range, we show that the optimal networks are exactly those obtained by removing a matching from the complete network. We develop an exact algorithm for complete multipartite networks. It finds an optimal network by dynamic programming in a number of operations that grows with the fourth power of the number of nodes, although the class itself grows superpolynomially. For networks with up to six nodes, restricting the design to this class results in no loss under explicit conditions. We also characterize pairwise-stable networks within this class and establish their existence for every level of link cost. Finally, we quantify the cost of misjudging failure risk. Overestimating node survival leads to overbuilding and can yield lower welfare than building nothing, whereas underestimating it never does. Under uncertain failure risk, design should therefore favor conservative reliability estimates.
