# Lost in the Bins: Treatment Effects with Discretized Covariates

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

均值独立限制被广泛用于识别平均处理效应，但用于构建这些限制的协变量往往以分组或粗化形式被观测或使用。本文表明，在精细测量的协变量被粗化版本替代后，以该协变量为条件的均值独立未必仍然成立。因此，粗化可能改变比较的识别内容，而不仅仅是估计的精度。我刻画了由此产生的针对ATT和ATE的粗化偏误。对于ATT，偏误由未处理潜在结果中的组内构成驱动。对于ATE，还会出现一个额外的处理效应异质性项。随后我引入$c$-均值依赖，这是在粗化层面上对精确均值独立的非参数松弛，并推导平均处理效应的尖锐界。参数$c$决定了比较可以在粗化协变量的相邻取值之间延伸多远。我提出了所得界函数的样本类比估计量和自助法推断程序。在使用Angrist（1998）数据对自愿兵役的一个应用中，对早期收入的正效应（主要反映军饷）即使在最弱形式的$c$-均值依赖下也成立，而一旦放松均值独立，关于后期对平民收入效应的证据则不确定。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Mean independence restrictions are widely used to identify average treatment effects, but the covariates used to formulate these restrictions are often observed or used in coarsened form. This paper shows that mean independence conditional on a finely measured covariate need not hold after the covariate is replaced by a coarsened version. Coarsening can therefore change the identifying content of the comparison rather than only the precision of estimation. I characterize the resulting coarsening bias for the ATT and ATE. For the ATT, the bias is driven by within-cell composition in untreated potential outcomes. For the ATE, an additional treatment effect heterogeneity term appears. I then introduce $c$-mean dependence, a nonparametric relaxation of exact mean independence at the coarsened level, and derive sharp bounds for average treatment effects. The parameter $c$ determines how far comparisons may extend across neighboring values of the coarsened covariate. I propose sample analogue estimators and bootstrap inference procedures for the resulting bound functions. In an application to voluntary military service using data from Angrist (1998), positive effects on early earnings, which primarily reflect military pay, hold even under the weakest form of $c$-mean dependence, whereas the evidence on later effects on civilian earnings is inconclusive once mean independence is relaxed.
