<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究经济激励如何影响那些已将福利与兼职工作相结合的残疾保险（DI）领取者的劳动供给决策。我们利用比利时适应性工作计划的一个显著特征，即DI福利随着收入增加而逐步减少，且边际削减率在预设阈值处急剧上升。由于参与者无法自由调整工作时间，这一设定使我们能够估计更高的隐性税负如何影响从部分工作状态退出的转变。利用行政数据和回归拐点设计（Regression Kink Design），我们发现更高的隐性税率会诱导两个相反方向的退出：转向全职就业和退回全额DI。每工作日隐性边际税增加一欧元，使退回全额DI的概率提高3.0个百分点，使恢复全职就业的概率提高1.5个百分点，对应的弹性分别为0.84和0.85。一个简单的劳动供给框架可以解释这些方向相反的反应。我们的研究结果表明，部分福利方案的经济设计可以决定部分工作究竟是通向全职就业的桥梁，还是反而使领取者重新回到全额福利依赖状态。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies how financial incentives shape labor supply decisions among disability insurance (DI) recipients who already combine benefits with part-time work. We exploit a distinctive feature of the Belgian Adapted Work Program, in which DI benefits are progressively withdrawn as earnings increase and the marginal withdrawal rate rises sharply at predefined thresholds. Because participants cannot freely adjust their working hours, this setting allows us to estimate how higher implicit taxation affects transitions out of partial work. Using administrative data and a Regression Kink Design, we find that higher implicit tax rates induce exits in two opposite directions: toward full-time employment and back to full DI. A one euro increase in the implicit marginal tax per working day raises the probability of returning to full DI by 3.0 percentage points and of resuming full-time employment by 1.5 percentage points, corresponding to elasticities of 0.84 and 0.85. A simple labor supply framework rationalizes these divergent responses. Our findings show that the financial design of partial-benefit schemes can determine whether partial work serves as a bridge toward full employment or instead leads recipients back to full benefit dependency.
