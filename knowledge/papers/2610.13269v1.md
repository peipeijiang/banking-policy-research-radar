# Intentionally Overidentifying Local Projections

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

局部投影（LPs）已成为宏观经济学中估计动态效应的标准工具，并且通常在给定滞后阶数下使用普通最小二乘法（OLS）来实施。我们提出了一种替代方法，利用额外的滞后项作为排除性工具变量（IVs）。在具有鞅差冲击的有限阶模型下，由此得到的（最优加权）IV估计量的渐近效率永远不会低于OLS，并且正如我们所展示的，当数据具有持续性时，该方法可以带来显著的效率提升。对于具有独立同分布误差的单位根AR(1)数据，我们的基准IV估计量（带有一个过度识别约束）的渐近方差为$2-h^{-1}$，而Montiel Olea和Plagborg-Møller（2021）中OLS滞后增广估计量在整数传播期$h$下的渐近方差为$h$。随后，我们发展了基于IV的LP推断方法，该方法对未知持续性和中度局部模型误设具有稳健性，而基于递归VAR的推断方法通常对后者不稳健。我们还刻画了误设程度与额外滞后控制变量数量之间的权衡关系。通过模拟和实证应用，我们证明所提出的方法在有限样本中能够为脉冲响应产生比OLS显著更短的置信区间。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Local projections (LPs) have become a standard tool for estimating dynamic effects in macroeconomics and are typically implemented using ordinary least squares (OLS) for a given lag order. We propose an alternative approach that exploits additional lags as excluded instrumental variables (IVs). Under a finite-order model with martingale-difference shocks, the resulting (optimally weighted) IV estimator is never asymptotically less efficient than OLS and, as we show, can deliver substantial efficiency gains when data are persistent. For the unit-root AR(1) data with i.i.d. errors, our baseline IV estimator (with one overidentifying restriction) has asymptotic variance $2-h^{-1}$, compared with $h$, the asymptotic variance of the OLS lag-augmented estimator in Montiel Olea and Plagborg-Møller (2021), for an integer propagation horizon $h$. We then develop IV-based LP inference that is robust to unknown persistence and moderate local model misspecification, whereas the recursive VAR-based inference is generally not robust to the latter. We also characterize the trade-off between the degree of misspecification and the number of additional lag controls. Through simulations and an empirical application, we demonstrate that the proposed method can yield meaningfully shorter confidence intervals for impulse responses than OLS in finite samples.
