金融科技（Financial technology，FinTech）通过重塑客户获取和体验金融服务的方式，改变了银行业。本研究考察了马来西亚使用金融科技赋能银行服务的客户中，感知易用性、感知安全性和服务质量与客户满意度之间的关系，并以信任作为中介变量进行检验。研究采用定量、横断面相关性设计。数据通过Qualtrics在线自填问卷收集，对象为300名18岁及以上、在过去三个月内至少使用过一项金融科技赋能银行服务的马来西亚商业银行客户。测量题项改编自成熟量表，采用五点李克特量表进行测量。数据分析采用描述性统计、Cronbach's alpha、Pearson相关、多元回归以及Hayes的PROCESS Model 4进行中介分析。研究结果表明，感知易用性、感知安全性和服务质量与客户满意度呈显著正相关。服务质量表现出最强的独特贡献（β = .383）。信任部分中介了所有三个预测变量与客户满意度之间的关系。本研究通过提供来自马来西亚银行客户的实证证据，并突出信任作为连接金融科技赋能服务特征与客户满意度的机制的作用，为金融科技和数字银行文献做出了贡献。研究结果为马来西亚银行和金融科技服务提供商在提升服务质量、安全性、易用性和客户信任方面提供了实践启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Financial technology (FinTech) has transformed the banking industry by reshaping how customers access and experience financial services. This study examines the relationships between perceived ease of use, perceived security, and service quality and customer satisfaction among customers using FinTech-enabled banking services in Malaysia, with Trust examined as a mediator. A quantitative, cross-sectional correlational design was employed. Data were collected through an online self-administered questionnaire using Qualtrics from 300 Malaysian commercial banking customers aged 18 years and above who had used at least one FinTech-enabled banking service within the previous three months. Measurement items were adapted from established scales and measured using a five-point Likert scale. Data were analysed using descriptive statistics, Cronbach’s alpha, Pearson correlation, multiple regression, and Hayes’ PROCESS Model 4 for mediation analysis. The findings indicate that perceived ease of use, perceived security, and service quality were positively and significantly associated with customer satisfaction. Service quality demonstrated the strongest unique contribution (β = .383). Trust partially mediated the relationships between all three predictors and customer satisfaction. The study contributes to the FinTech and digital banking literature by providing empirical evidence from Malaysian banking customers and highlighting the role of Trust as a mechanism linking FinTech-enabled service characteristics with customer satisfaction. The findings provide practical implications for Malaysian banks and FinTech service providers seeking to improve service quality, security, ease of use, and customer trust.
