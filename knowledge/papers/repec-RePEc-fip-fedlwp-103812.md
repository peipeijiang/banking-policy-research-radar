# On the Complementarity of K-12 Personal Finance and Economic Education: Evidence from Online Modules

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/fip/fedlwp/103812.html)

## 一句话结论

> 利用240万份K-12在线模块作业数据，通过模块完成顺序的变异估计经济学与个人理财教育之间的方向性溢出效应，发现先学经济学对个人理财成绩有显著正向影响，反之效应微弱。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们汇总了来自教育在线平台上51.3万名K-12学生完成的240万份经济学和个人理财模块作业的数据。我们利用这些学生完成模块顺序的差异——先完成经济学还是先完成个人理财——来估计方向性溢出效应。溢出效应是单向的。在控制学校人口统计学特征后，先学习经济学的学生在个人理财后测中得分高出3.42分；在同一设定下，先学习个人理财使经济学后测成绩提高0.15分。使用结果发生后暴露的安慰剂检验表明，每个治疗前模块的回报为0.8至1.4分。这种优势并未随着两门学科之间间隔的扩大而缩小，并且在低表现学生中以原始分数计算最大，为5.1分对1.6分，尽管这主要是因为高表现学生处于测试上限。经济学似乎提供了个人理财所不具备的基础概念。当两门学科都教授时，顺序很重要：先学经济学是更好的顺序，并且一两个经济学模块就能获得大部分可用收益。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We compile data from 2.4 million economics and personal finance module assignments completed by 513,000 K-12 students on an educational online platform. We exploit variation in the order in which these students complete the modules–economics or personal finance first– to estimate directional spillovers. The spillover runs one way. Controlling for school demographics, students who take economics first score 3.42 points higher on personal finance post-tests; taking personal finance first raises economics post-tests by 0.15 points on the same specification. A placebo using exposure that occurs after the outcome puts the return at 0.8 to 1.4 points per pre-treatment module. The advantage does not shrink as the gap between subjects grows, and it is largest in raw points for lower-performing students, 5.1 compared with 1.6, though mostly because higher-performing students sit at the test ceiling. Economics appears to supply foundational concepts that personal finance does not. When both subjects are taught, the order matters: economics first is the better order, and one or two economics modules capture most of the available gain.

</details>
