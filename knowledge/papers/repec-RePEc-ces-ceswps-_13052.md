<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在社会各阶层之间的不平等在马尔萨斯时代（Malthusian epoch）何以能够持续存在？既然富裕会带来更大的繁殖成功率，从而稀释继承财富并随时间推移缩小差距，不平等为何没有自行消解？本文揭示了“不平等悖论”（Paradox of Inequality），为其建立了稳健的理论基础，并提出了解决之道。分析表明，精英阶层奢侈的、非生产性的地位展示在不经意间将资源从繁殖中转移出去，从而限制了继承财富的稀释，并使不平等得以代际延续。颇具讽刺意味的是，挥霍反而保全了它看似浪费掉的财富。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How could inequality between social classes have ever persisted during the Malthusian epoch, as affluence led to greater reproductive success, diluting inherited wealth and narrowing disparities over time? This paper uncovers the Paradox of Inequality, establishes its robust theoretical foundations, and provides its resolution. The analysis suggests that elites’ lavish, nonproductive displays of status inadvertently diverted resources from reproduction, limiting the dilution of inherited wealth and sustaining inequality across generations. Ironically, extravagance preserved the wealth it seemed to squander.
