<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

长期减灾（DRR）需要投资决策考虑不确定的灾害条件和累积的备灾能力。本研究开发了一种新颖的随机动态控制模型，用于在反复性和灾难性不确定性下进行最优DRR投资。该模型结合了扩散过程和泊松跳跃过程，并将备灾资本视为一种内生存量，通过投资积累、折旧并减轻经济损失。我们从Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman方程中推导出状态依赖的投资规则，以在无限时间范围内最小化预期贴现投资支出和灾害损失。
蒙特卡洛策略比较和数值实验考察了灾害风险结构如何与投资决策相互作用，从而塑造备灾积累和经济损失。结果表明，DRR投资的经济价值取决于对不断变化条件的调整和灾害损失机制。较低的支出可以与更高的备灾水平共存，而相似的预期损失可能掩盖不同的上尾风险。因此，有效投资需要同时关注累积备灾和灾害风险结构。
这些发现支持长期DRR规划，将投资时机、资本维护和恢复条件与剩余损失评估相结合。上尾指标补充预期损失评估，但不被直接优化。该框架为动态管理备灾资本提供了基础。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Long-term disaster risk reduction (DRR) requires investment decisions that account for uncertain disaster conditions and accumulated preparedness capacity. This study develops a novel stochastic dynamic control model for optimal DRR investment under recurrent and catastrophic uncertainty. The model combines diffusion and Poisson jump processes and treats preparedness capital as an endogenous stock that accumulates through investment, depreciates, and mitigates economic damage. We derive a state-dependent investment rule from the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation to minimize expected discounted investment expenditure and disaster damage over an infinite horizon.
  Monte Carlo policy comparisons and numerical experiments examine how disaster risk structures interact with investment decisions to shape preparedness accumulation and economic losses. The results show that the economic value of DRR investment depends on adjustment to evolving conditions and disaster loss mechanisms. Lower expenditure can coexist with greater preparedness, while similar expected losses can conceal different upper-tail risks. Effective investment therefore requires attention to both accumulated preparedness and the structure of disaster risk.
  These findings support long-term DRR planning that integrates investment timing, capital maintenance, and recovery conditions with residual loss assessment. Upper-tail indicators complement expected-loss evaluation without being directly optimized. The framework provides a basis for dynamically managing preparedness capital.
