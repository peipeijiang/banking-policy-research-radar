本文针对随机系数分布的平均泛函（如平均支付意愿和平均弹性）提出了一种推断方法，其中该分布采用Heiss、Hetzenecker和Osterhaus（2022）提出的惩罚固定网格估计量进行非参数估计。我们建立了以惩罚伪真实值处泛函为中心的相应惩罚插入估计量的渐近正态性，并提出了一种考虑正则化偏差的置信区间。该方法适用于一大类线性和非线性泛函，并允许研究者使用密集网格来减少近似偏差，同时保持有效的推断。蒙特卡洛模拟表明，所提出的区间在有限样本中既能达到接近名义水平的覆盖率，又能保持信息量。一项针对出行方式需求的经验应用表明，相对于标准参数模型，灵活的非参数设定可以产生具有经济意义的显著差异。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops an inference procedure for average functionals of random-coefficient distributions, such as mean willingness-to-pay and average elasticities, when the distribution is estimated nonparametrically using the penalized fixed-grid estimator of Heiss, Hetzenecker, and Osterhaus (2022). We establish asymptotic normality of the corresponding penalized plug-in estimator centered at the functional evaluated at the penalized pseudo-true value and propose a confidence interval that accounts for the regularization bias. Our method applies to a broad class of linear and nonlinear functionals and allows researchers to use dense grids to reduce approximation bias while maintaining valid inference. Monte Carlo simulations show that the proposed intervals achieve coverage close to the nominal level while remaining informative in finite samples. An empirical application to travel mode demand illustrates that flexible nonparametric specifications can yield economically meaningful differences relative to standard parametric models.
