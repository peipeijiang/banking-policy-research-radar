<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

稳定币可以在可编程网络上持续转移价值，Web3钱包可以通过密码学签名授权这些转移，但这两项能力单独存在时都无法构成完整的商业交易。区块链可以证明某笔交易满足了协议规则，钱包可以证明存在足够的签名权限；但二者都无法证明是谁发起了该交易、代表哪个法律实体、依据何种当前权限、基于哪些当前合规证据、按照哪项确切指令，以及具有何种结算与会计结果。我们描述ChainIT交易真相架构（ChainIT Transaction Truth Architecture），这是一种零信任控制、执行与证据架构，它将经过验证的个人和组织身份、ChainIT权限协议（ChainIT Authority Protocol）的组织权限记录与权限策略、交易特定的运行时合规决策、精确摘要批准、原子容量预留以及一次性执行授权凭证（Execution Authorization Credential）置于钱包执行之前，并在交易之后记录结算、对账和生命周期证据。该架构区分了五个边界：对密钥的控制不等于身份，身份不等于组织权限，权限不等于合规，合规不等于执行权限，网络确认不等于完整的商业结算。我们描述了钱包与托管配置，包括一种多方计算（MPC）配置，其中托管安全性与业务权限保持独立；还描述了稳定币与Web3证据域、分层最终性、截至2026年9月的美国稳定币监管背景，以及一种分阶段部署方案，其中试点成功要求控制不变量在攻击和故障条件下仍然成立。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Stablecoins can move value continuously across programmable networks, and Web3 wallets can authorize those transfers with cryptographic signatures, but neither capability, standing alone, establishes a complete commercial transaction. A blockchain may prove that a transaction satisfied protocol rules, and a wallet may prove that sufficient signing authority existed; neither proves who initiated the transaction, for which legal entity, under what current authority, against which current compliance evidence, on what exact instruction, or with what settlement and accounting outcome. We describe the ChainIT Transaction Truth Architecture, a zero-trust control, execution, and evidence architecture that places verified individual and organizational identity, the organizational authority record and authority policy of the ChainIT Authority Protocol, transaction-specific runtime compliance decisions, exact-digest approval, atomic capacity reservation, and a single-use Execution Authorization Credential in front of wallet execution, and that records settlement, reconciliation, and lifecycle evidence after the transaction. The architecture separates five boundaries: control of a key is not identity, identity is not organizational authority, authority is not compliance, compliance is not execution authority, and network confirmation is not complete commercial settlement. We describe wallet and custody profiles, including a multi-party computation (MPC) profile in which custody security is kept independent of business authority, stablecoin and Web3 evidence domains, layered finality, the U.S. stablecoin regulatory context as of September 2026, and a phased deployment in which pilot success requires that the control invariants hold under attack and failure.
