# Instrumental-Variable Estimation of Fixed-Effects Fractional-Outcome Panel Data Models, with an Application to a Major Disability Support Policy Reform

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个固定效应估计框架，用于具有logit条件均值的分数结果面板模型。成对拟差分从矩条件中消除个体效应，得到一个广义矩估计量，在所述矩限制下，即使时间期数有限且固定，该估计量也是一致的。该框架允许个体效应与协变量之间存在无限制的依赖关系，通过工具变量容纳内生回归元，并可扩展到总和为一的多元份额。边界观测无需调整，平均半弹性可以在不恢复个体效应的情况下进行估计。我们将该框架应用于澳大利亚国家残疾保险计划（NDIS），使用2011-2023年HILDA（澳大利亚家庭、收入与劳动力动态）调查数据以及基于该计划分阶段地理推广的工具变量。我们考察了NDIS领取如何影响领取者及其他家庭成员在有偿工作、家务劳动、残疾照护和其余活动之间的时间分配。估计结果表明，领取者减少了工作和残疾照护，而其他家庭成员增加了残疾照护。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a fixed-effects estimation framework for fractional-outcome panel models with a logit conditional mean. Pairwise quasi-differencing eliminates individual effects from the moment conditions, yielding a generalized method of moments estimator that is consistent under the stated moment restrictions even when the number of time period is finite and fixed. The framework allows unrestricted dependence between individual effects and covariates, accommodates endogenous regressors through instrumental variables, and extends to multivariate shares that sum to one. Boundary observations require no adjustment, and average semi-elasticities can be estimated without recovering the individual effects. We apply the framework to Australia's National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS), using HILDA (Household, Income and Labour Dynamics in Australia) survey data for 2011-2023 and instruments based on the scheme's staggered geographic rollout. We examine how NDIS receipt affects the allocation of time across paid work, housework, disability care, and remaining activities for recipients and other household members. The estimates indicate that recipients reduce work and disability care, while other household members increase disability care.
