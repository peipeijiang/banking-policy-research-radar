<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

股票和债券投资者将公司信息的不同方面视为价值相关，从而生成不同的文本到收益的映射（“词典”）。比较股票和债券对财报电话会议的反应，债券持有人更强调运营和下行风险（相对于增长和创新）。虽然这与资产收益差异一致，但词典差异跟随的是投资者群体而非收益结构。在那些事实上是股票但由债券持有人定价的债券中——即公司价值低于优先债务面值的次级债务——债券投资者继续通过债权人的视角解读信息。被一种词典忽略而未被另一种词典忽略的信息会导致价格反应不足。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Stock and bond investors treat different aspects of firm information as value-relevant, generating distinct mappings of text to returns (“dictionaries”). Comparing stock and bond responses to earnings calls, bondholders emphasize operations and downside risk (relative to growth and innovation). While consistent with asset payoff differences, dictionary differences follow the investor cohort and not the payoff structure. Among bonds that are de facto equities but priced by bondholders—junior debt for which firm value is less than senior debt face value—bond investors continue to interpret information through a creditor’s lens. Information ignored by one dictionary but not the other generates price underreaction.

</details>
