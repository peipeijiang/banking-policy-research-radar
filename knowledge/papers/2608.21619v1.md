<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

拒绝采样需要一个在已知常数上支配目标的提议分布，而对于非高斯状态空间模型，这一常数通常无法获得。我们为潜在状态路径构造了这样一个提议分布，从而得到独立的精确平滑抽样以及一个无偏似然估计器，其相对方差在接受概率为$p$时每次抽样至多为$1/p-1$。该方法适用于具有仿射高斯动态和log凹观测密度的标量状态，包括多元观测情形。通过转移扭曲（transition twisting）使目标与提议的对数比率可分离，并利用切线扭曲（tangent-line twists）使每一项非正，从而得到一个可达的、尖锐的支配常数。采用压扩（companding）节点布置时，对于长度为$T$、每个日期有$G$个节点的样本，累积包络误差为$O(T/G^2)$，因此取$G\propto\sqrt{T}$可使接受概率保持远离零；对于随机波动率模型，所需条件几乎必然成立。一个更简单的以模式为中心的网格在经验上表现出相同的缩放规律。在$T=2{,}000$时，接受率为$75\%$，而高斯包络的接受率约为$10^{-16}$。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Rejection sampling requires a proposal that dominates the target by a known constant, generally unavailable for non-Gaussian state space models. We construct such a proposal for the latent state path, yielding independent exact smoothing draws and an unbiased likelihood estimator whose relative variance is at most $1/p-1$ per draw at acceptance probability $p$. The method covers scalar states with affine Gaussian dynamics and log-concave observation densities, including multivariate observations. Transition twisting makes the log target-to-proposal ratio separable, and tangent-line twists make each term nonpositive, producing an attained, sharp dominating constant. With a companding node placement, the accumulated envelope error is $O(T/G^2)$ for a sample of length $T$ with $G$ nodes per date, so $G\propto\sqrt{T}$ keeps acceptance bounded away from zero; for stochastic volatility, the required conditions hold almost surely. A simpler mode-centered grid shows the same scaling empirically. At $T=2{,}000$, acceptance is $75\%$, versus roughly $10^{-16}$ for the Gaussian envelope.
