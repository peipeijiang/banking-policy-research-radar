<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

经过数十年的贸易自由化，2020年代标志着全球化叙事发生了深刻转变，关税再次成为贸易政策的核心工具。这一逆转也对贸易政策效果的评估方式提出了挑战。现有实证文献大多旨在评估双边关税削减或取消以及与之相关的优惠优势侵蚀。而多边关税上升则提出了一个不同的问题：它们不仅改变了直接受影响出口商所面临的贸易成本，也改变了所有竞争供应商的相对市场准入，从而可能强化现有的优惠待遇。本文构建了一个实用框架，用于在这一新的政策环境中将关税变化转化为贸易效应。我们认为，自由化与保护主义不应被视为对称的政策实验：它们的效果通过相对市场准入的不同变化发挥作用，因此需要审慎考虑如何度量关税冲击并将其纳入实证贸易模型和反事实评估之中。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

After decades of trade liberalization, the 2020s mark a profound shift in the globalization narrative, with tariffs once again becoming a central instrument of trade policy. This reversal also challenges how the effects of trade policy are evaluated. Much of the empirical literature has been developed to assess bilateral tariff reductions or elimination and the associated erosion of preferential advantages. Rising multilateral tariffs pose a different question: they alter not only the trade costs faced by directly affected exporters but also the relative market access of all competing suppliers, potentially reinforcing existing preferences. This paper develops a practical framework for translating tariff changes into trade effects in this new policy environment. We argue that liberalization and protectionism should not be treated as symmetric policy experiments: their effects operate through different changes in relative market access and therefore require careful consideration of how tariff shocks are measured and incorporated into empirical trade models and counterfactual evaluations.

</details>
