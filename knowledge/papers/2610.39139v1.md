# The Welfare Limits of Market Segmentation with Captive Consumers

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在存在专属消费者与争夺性消费者的双寡头市场中，我刻画了在某种市场构成与某种公开市场分割下可能出现的每一对消费者剩余与生产者剩余，并构造了一个能产生每一对剩余的市场。该集合呈透镜状。在任意行业利润水平下，当专属消费者与争夺性消费者被完全分离时，消费者境况最差；而在一个未分割、且两家企业拥有相同数量专属消费者的市场中，消费者境况最好。在任何分割方式下，专属消费者基数不相等且存在部分争夺性消费者的市场都无法达到这一上边界。在Bertrand竞争与垄断之间，该边界本身严格低于如果所有人都支付一个产生相同利润的单一确定性价格时消费者所能获得的结果。企业必须进行随机化，而在需求向下倾斜的情况下，由此产生的价格离散会降低消费者剩余。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In a duopoly with captive and contested consumers, I characterize every pair of consumer and producer surplus that can arise under some market composition and some public segmentation, and construct a market that produces each pair. The set is lens-shaped. At any level of industry profit, consumers do worst when captive and contested consumers are perfectly separated and best in an unsegmented market in which the two firms have equally many captives. A market with unequal captive bases and some contested consumers cannot reach this upper frontier under any segmentation. Between Bertrand competition and monopoly, the frontier itself lies strictly below what consumers would get if everyone paid a single deterministic price yielding the same profit. Firms must randomize, and with downward-sloping demand the resulting price dispersion lowers consumer surplus.

</details>
