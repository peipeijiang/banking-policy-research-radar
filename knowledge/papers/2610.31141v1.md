# Intraday Forecasting Ability and Liquidity Provision of Institutional Traders

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31141v1)

> 利用股票交易所高频数据，将买卖执行匹配为头寸，发现机构交易者具有持续的日内预测能力，且预测能力与流动性提供（被动执行次数）均显著提升自营交易盈亏。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究利用来自某证券交易所的高频数据集，考察预测能力与流动性提供如何与机构交易者的自营日内盈利能力相关联。我们发现交易者中存在持续的预测能力。具体而言，我们证明，基于这些技能娴熟交易者的执行流所构建的假设性流跟随策略，在不同交易频率下产生的年化夏普比率介于16至63之间。我们将买入和卖出执行匹配为头寸，将已实现损益（P&L）与期限匹配的预测代理变量以及两笔执行的流动性角色相联系。我们的研究结果表明，预测能力的提升与盈利能力之间存在直接相关性，预测能力代理变量每增加一个标准差，每个匹配头寸的损益相应高出1.02至1.19个货币单位。此外，我们通过匹配头寸中被动执行的数量（零次、一次或两次）来衡量该头寸内的流动性提供强度。在这些回归中，一次被动执行的损益比无被动执行高出1.28至1.31个货币单位，而两次被动执行比一次被动执行进一步高出3.79至3.82个货币单位，凸显了流动性提供所带来的显著财务收益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study uses a high-frequency dataset from a stock exchange to examine how forecasting ability and liquidity provision relate to institutional traders' proprietary intraday profitability. We discover persistent forecasting abilities among traders. Specifically, we demonstrate that hypothetical flow-following strategies based on the execution flows of these skillful traders yield annualized Sharpe ratios ranging from 16 to 63 across various trading frequencies. We match buy and sell executions into positions, linking realized profit and loss (P&L) to horizon-matched forecasting proxies and the liquidity roles of both executions. Our findings indicate a direct correlation between improved forecasting ability and profitability, where a one-standard-deviation increase in the forecasting ability proxy is associated with 1.02 -- 1.19 currency units higher P&L per matched position. Additionally, we measure liquidity-provision intensity within a matched position by its number of passive executions: zero, one, or two. In these regressions, P&L is 1.28 -- 1.31 currency units higher for one passive execution than for none, and a further 3.79 -- 3.82 higher for two than for one, underscoring the significant financial benefits of liquidity provision.
