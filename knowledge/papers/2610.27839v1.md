<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

给定一个在受限估值域上对单一代理人的激励相容机制，我们考虑其在整个估值空间上的激励相容扩展。我们识别出两种极端扩展：“保留菜单”（keep-the-menu）扩展，它在逐点上最小化买方收益并最大化规范价格；以及“保留定价”（keep-the-pricing）扩展，它在逐点上最大化买方收益并最小化规范价格。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Given an incentive-compatible mechanism for a single agent on a restricted domain of valuations, we consider its incentive-compatible extensions to the entire valuation space. We identify two extreme extensions: the "keep-the-menu" extension, which minimizes buyer payoffs and maximizes canonical prices pointwise, and the "keep-the-pricing" extension, which maximizes buyer payoffs and minimizes canonical prices pointwise.

</details>
