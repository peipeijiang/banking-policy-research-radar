# Defense Feedback and Global-Game Selection

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00323v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

全球博弈在参与者的激励随私人信息单调变动时，会选择一个唯一的临界值。我们表明，当一位拥有私人信息的参与者可以采取一种代价高昂、同时且不可观测的行动来捍卫现状时，这一逻辑可能会失效。在一个货币攻击模型中，防御者仅在中端信号处采取行动。沿着交易者的临界值方程，这种区间响应产生了一个内生的防御损失项，该项在激活组成部分的端点处消失，但在中间区域可能主导递减的基准收益差。我们刻画了一个防御不活跃且唯一性得以保持的原始参数区域，以及一个有限累积分布函数超调条件，在该条件下，两个活跃防御临界值根与不活跃基准根共存。由于边际无差异未必意味着全局激励，我们还给出了真正的单调临界值最佳反应得以成立的充分条件。最后，向外舍入的区间算术证明，一个适当先验参数向量恰好具有三个对称单调临界值均衡，并且这种拓扑结构在局部持续存在。该结果分离出全局博弈选择的一个基于收益的边界：隐藏干预可以在没有公共信号或信息层级扰动的情况下恢复多重性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Global games select a unique cutoff when a player's incentive moves monotonically with private information. We show that this logic can fail when a privately informed participant can take a costly, simultaneous, and unobserved action that defends the status quo. In a currency-attack model, the defender acts only at intermediate signals. Along the traders' cutoff equation, this interval response creates an endogenous defense-loss term that vanishes at the endpoints of an activation component but can dominate the decreasing benchmark payoff margin in between. We establish a primitive region in which defense is inactive and uniqueness survives, and a finite-CDF overshoot condition under which two active-defense cutoff roots coexist with the inactive benchmark root. Because marginal indifference need not imply global incentives, we also give sufficient conditions for a genuine monotone cutoff best response. Finally, outward-rounded interval arithmetic proves that a proper-prior parameter vector has exactly three symmetric monotone-cutoff equilibria and that this topology persists locally. The result isolates a payoff-based boundary of global-game selection: hidden intervention can restore multiplicity without public signalling or a perturbation of the information hierarchy.

</details>
