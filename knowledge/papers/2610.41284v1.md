<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究在生成一维状态后验均值的规定分布时是否需要随机化。基于后向Monge鞅传输（backward Monge martingale transport），我们证明：对于具有有限一阶矩的无原子先验，其每一个保均值收缩（mean-preserving contraction）都容许一个确定性实现。因此，只要目标和约束仅依赖于后验均值分布，将注意力限制在确定性信号上就不会改变设计者的价值。我们将实现分解为非分离区间上的独立问题，并描述了一个基于条形码传输（barcode transport）的构造性程序。我们还刻画了下等值集或上等值集何时可以呈现嵌套区间形式，并给出了在买方最优学习的垄断定价以及具有模糊接收者偏好的稳健说服中的应用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study whether randomization is needed to generate a prescribed distribution of posterior means for a one-dimensional state. Drawing on backward Monge martingale transport, we show that every mean-preserving contraction of an atomless prior with finite first moment admits a deterministic implementation. Consequently, restricting attention to deterministic signals leaves the designer's value unchanged whenever objectives and constraints depend only on the posterior-mean distribution. We decompose implementation into independent problems on non-separating intervals and describe a constructive procedure based on barcode transport. We also characterize when lower or upper contour sets can take a nested-interval form, and provide applications to monopolistic pricing with buyer-optimal learning and robust persuasion with ambiguous receiver preferences.
