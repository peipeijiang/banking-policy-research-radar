本文构建了一个关于带符号最优传输的理论框架。由连续传输方程诱导的全局平坦度度量被用作正则化项。我们从调和分析的视角考察传输网络，证明了变分问题中最优耦合的存在性与唯一性，并提出了一种算法方案，该方案在双边际约束下保证了信息的无损传输。为将理论与实际相衔接，我们为所得最优估计量设计了一套实证分析框架，该框架具有足够的通用性，能够同时适用于网络的时间序列分析与面板结构分析，这得益于局部紧阿贝尔群所固有的不变性性质。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops a theoretical framework for signed optimal transport. A global flatness measure induced by the continuum transport equation serves as the regularizer. We examine transport networks from a harmonic analysis perspective, prove the existence and uniqueness of the optimal coupling in the variational problem, and provide an algorithmic scheme that guarantees lossless information transport under bi-marginal constraints. To bridge theory with practice, we design an empirical analysis framework for the resulting optimal estimators, which is sufficiently general to accommodate both time-series and panel-structural analyses of the network, owing to the intrinsic invariance properties of locally compact abelian groups.
