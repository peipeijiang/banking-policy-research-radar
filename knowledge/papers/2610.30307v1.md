# The Customer Is the Builder: How AI Coding Agents Are Rewriting the Economics of Software

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30307v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文利用基准任务能力增长、开源替代品搜索、Craft套件开发案例和组织调查数据，研究AI编码代理如何影响企业软件自建与购买的替代边界，发现自建能力快速提升但市场购买份额仍上升、仅32%受访者因自建放弃购买，提示个体购买决策可能先于整体市场转变。

AI编码智能体可能会改变组织是购买软件还是自行构建所需功能的选择。我们聚焦于组织和专业采购者，他们的领域知识有助于其为自身工作指定和评估工具。我们将软件替代前沿定义为：客户能够以可接受的标准构建和维护、且成本低于购买的功能集合。利用公开数据，我们将不断增长的构建能力与市场变化指标进行比较。自2023年以来，领先智能体能够以50%成功率完成的基准任务长度（以人类工作时间衡量）大约每129天翻一番。一次广泛搜索识别出575个针对选定商业产品的潜在开源替代方案，尽管部分功能匹配仍需独立验证。Craft套件在12.0天的公开开发时间内产出了12个应用仓库，但这些仍为早期版本，其完整性和可靠性尚未得到验证。市场证据喜忧参半：购买软件获得的美国投资份额有所上升，追踪到的商业安装量也在增长。然而，一项组织调查中32%的受访者报告称，他们至少放弃了一次软件采购，因为其组织可以用编码智能体构建该功能。这些自我报告并不能确定被替代的支出规模，也无法确定所构建工具的可靠性。这一差距引出一项警示：客户的构建能力可能在广泛的采购转变变得可见之前，就已影响个体的购买决策。我们利用基准预测和可观察的市场检验来考察这一可能性。这些预测是一种外推，而非对客户转换行为的预报。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

AI coding agents could change whether organizations buy software or build the functionality they need. We focus on organizations and professional buyers whose domain knowledge can help them specify and evaluate tools for their work. We define the software substitution frontier as the functions customers could build and maintain at an acceptable standard for less than buying them. Using public data, we compare growing capacity to build with indicators of market change. Since 2023, the length of benchmark tasks that leading agents can complete at 50 percent success, measured in human working time, has doubled about every 129 days. A broad search identified 575 potential open-source alternatives to selected commercial products, although some functional matches still require independent validation. The Craft suite produced 12 application repositories over 12.0 days of public development, but these remain early versions with unverified completeness and reliability. Market evidence is mixed: purchased software gained US investment share and tracked commercial installations grew. Yet 32 percent of respondents to an organizational survey reported forgoing at least one software purchase because their organization could build the functionality with coding agents. These self-reports do not establish the spending displaced or the reliability of the resulting tools. The gap motivates a warning: customers' capacity to build could affect individual buying decisions before a broad purchasing shift becomes visible. We examine this possibility using benchmark projections and observable market tests. The projections are an extrapolation, not a forecast of customer switching.
