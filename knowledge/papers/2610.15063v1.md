<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

随机实验常与行政数据链接以测量结果，但粗粒度标识符可能导致多个行政记录与每个实验单元相一致。我们证明，无需解决这些模糊链接或指定结果模型，即可实现样本平均处理效应的无偏估计。我们的关键链接要求是，真实的行政结果包含在每个实验单元的候选结果之中。我们刻画了链接模糊性带来的精度损失，并研究了两种互补的缓解方法：回归调整和基于观测到的链接特征进行重加权。我们建立了这些估计量的有限样本和渐近性质，并发展了有效的基于随机化的推断和大样本推断。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Randomized experiments are often linked to administrative data to measure outcomes, but coarse identifiers can leave multiple administrative records consistent with each experimental unit. We show that unbiased estimation of the sample average treatment effect is possible without resolving these ambiguous links or specifying an outcome model. Our key linkage requirement is that the true administrative outcome is contained among the candidate outcomes for each experimental unit. We characterize the precision cost of linkage ambiguity and study two complementary ways to mitigate it: regression adjustment and reweighting based on observed linkage characteristics. We establish finite-sample and asymptotic properties of these estimators and develop valid randomization-based and large-sample inference.

</details>
