<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考虑经典的讨价还价问题：两个参与者必须就一个“公平”的分配 $(x,y) \in Ω\subset \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^2$ 达成一致，其结果是参与者1获得 $x$，参与者2获得 $y$。解决这个问题的通常方法是施加若干公平分配应具备的理想公理，然后得出结论：解由这些公理唯一确定。我们采取一种正交的视角，避免对公理的讨论，并追问不同且相互矛盾的公理是否可能产生相似的解。为此，我们在讨价还价问题的解空间上定义了一个自然的度量。该度量揭示了Nash（1950）、Raiffa（1953）和Kalai-Smorodinsky（1975）这三个著名解之间强烈的内部一致性。它识别出了Utilitarian解和Perles-Maschler解（1981）的问题情形。Anbarci-Bigelow（1988）的面积单调解被证明几乎完美地位于从Nash到Kalai-Smorodinsky的最短路径上。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We consider the classic bargaining problem: two players have to agree on a `fair' division $(x,y) \in Ω\subset \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^2$ which results in Player 1 receiving $x$ and Player 2 receiving $y$. The usual approach to this problem has been to impose a number of desirable axioms that a fair division should have and then conclude that the solution is uniquely defined by the axioms. We take an orthogonal view, avoid the discussion of axioms, and ask whether different and contradicting axioms may give rise to similar solutions. To this end, we define a natural metric on the space of solutions to the bargaining problem. The metric shows a strong internal consistency between the three celebrated solutions due to Nash (1950), Raiffa (1953) and Kalai-Smorodinsky (1975). It identifies problematic cases for the Utilitarian solution and the Perles-Maschler solution (1981). The area monotonic solution of Anbarci-Bigelow (1988) is shown to be nearly perfectly located on a shortest path from Nash to Kalai-Smorodinsky.

</details>
