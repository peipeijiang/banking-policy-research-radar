<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

将全额健康保险扩展至被排除在外的群体，是否会对已参保人群产生健康外部性？2022年，哥伦比亚将约两百万委内瑞拉移民的医疗服务从仅限急诊扩展至全面可及。本文通过比较项目启动前后暴露程度不同（项目参与率高低不同）的市镇，发现该项目降低了哥伦比亚人的死亡率，每覆盖300名移民即挽救一条哥伦比亚人的生命。这一效应可由受益人对医疗服务更高效的利用以及为新参保患者提供规律且及时的支付来解释。所挽救的哥伦比亚人死亡是及时医疗救治本可避免的死亡。该项目的净财政成本为每挽救一条哥伦比亚人生命93,000美元，远低于统计生命价值（value of a statistical life）。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Does extending full health coverage to an excluded group induce health externalities for the population already insured? In 2022 Colombia expanded health services from emergency only to full access to approximately two million Venezuelan migrants. Comparing municipalities more and less exposed to program take-up before and after its onset, this paper finds that the program reduced mortality rates for Colombians, saving one Colombian life for each 300 migrants covered. The effects are explained by a more efficient use of the health services by beneficiaries and regular and timely payments for the newly insured patients. The Colombian deaths saved are those that timely health treatment prevents. The net fiscal cost of the program is USD 93,000 per Colombian life saved, well below the value of a statistical life.
