<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

法律面前人人平等是一项人权，而贫困被告获得高质量法律援助对于落实这一权利至关重要。在所有被告均能获得律师的情况下，我研究了拒绝法律援助对法院判决结果的影响。我将双重机器学习方法与澳大利亚新南威尔士州一个将援助与法院判决结果相关联的新行政数据集相结合，以学习分配函数，其输入是已知的。我发现，未通过经济状况调查并聘请私人律师的申请人，其被监禁的可能性比若通过调查并依赖法律援助时低10个百分点。鉴于平均监禁时长接近四年，这一差距意义重大。然而，有证据表明，若他们被监禁，则实际服刑时间可能更长。政府倾向于广泛提供援助而非增加每起案件分配的时间，这一偏好或许可以解释上述模式。

关键词：贫困辩护，犯罪，刑事司法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Equality before the law is a human right, and access to high-quality legal aid for indigent defendants is essential to enforce it. In a context where all defendants have access to a lawyer, I study the impact of denying legal aid on court outcomes. I combine double machine learning and a new administrative dataset linking aid to court outcomes in New South Wales, Australia, to learn the assignment function, whose inputs are known. I find that applicants who fail the means test and hire private lawyers are 10 percentage points less likely to be incarcerated than if they passed and relied on legal aid. Given an average incarceration length of nearly four years, this gap is significant. However, I find evidence suggesting that they spend more time in jail if they are incarcerated. A government preference for broad access to aid over allocated time per case could explain this pattern.
