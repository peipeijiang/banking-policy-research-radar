<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个动态双向匹配市场，其中不同类型的智能体随时间到达，根据兼容性网络等待匹配，并且如果其随机耐心到期则放弃系统。决策者收集匹配奖励并支付持有成本和放弃成本，并寻求在有限时间范围内最大化期望净价值。我们通过策略相对于流体基准的近似比来评估策略，该流体基准是线性规划松弛的最优值，该线性规划为每个策略的期望净价值提供上界。在一般位置间隙（general position gap, GPG）条件下，该条件要求该线性规划的唯一最优对偶解在到达率的微小扰动下保持最优，我们提出一种最长队列策略。该策略首先使用该对偶解来识别一组良好匹配类型和一组关键智能体类型。在每次到达时，它仅考虑良好匹配，并在可用性约束下将到达者与队列最长的关键伙伴匹配。在高容量情形中，即到达率按 s 缩放时，我们证明该策略在每个规模 s >= 1 和每个时间范围 T >= 1 下至少达到 1-O(1/s) 的近似比，因此当 s 趋于无穷时该比值趋于 1。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a dynamic two-way matching market in which agents of heterogeneous types arrive over time, wait to be matched according to a compatibility network, and abandon the system if their random patience expires. The decision maker collects match rewards and pays holding and abandonment costs, and seeks to maximize the expected net value over a finite horizon. We evaluate policies by their approximation ratio against a fluid benchmark, the optimal value of a linear-programming relaxation that upper bounds the expected net value of every policy. Under a general position gap (GPG) condition, which requires the unique optimal dual solution of this linear program to remain optimal under small perturbations of the arrival rates, we propose a longest-queue policy. The policy first uses this dual solution to identify a set of good match types and a set of critical agent types. Upon each arrival, it considers only good matches and matches the arrival to the critical partner with the longest queue subject to availabilities. In the high-volume regime, in which arrival rates are scaled by s, we prove that the policy attains an approximation ratio of at least 1-O(1/s) at every scale s >= 1 and every horizon T >= 1, so the ratio tends to 1 as s goes to infinity.

</details>
