<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Dobzinski、Lavi和Nisan（FOCS'08）提出的自适应钳制拍卖（adaptive clinching auction）是预算约束投标人机制设计的基石，此后激发了大量向更丰富市场设置的扩展。自适应钳制具有令人信服的保证：它是帕累托最优的，并且只要预算不能被虚报，真实投标就是一个占优策略。然而，在许多现实环境中，身份创建成本极低，这使得对女巫攻击（Sybil attack）的抵御能力成为一项额外需求。当投标人可以将其预算分散到虚假身份上时，自适应钳制的各项保证是否仍然成立，此前一直是一个未解决的问题。我们引入了一个自适应钳制的广义框架，并证明它满足女巫鲁棒激励相容性（Sybil-robust incentive compatibility）：只要投标人的总报告预算不超过其可执行的真实预算，它就不能通过分裂为任意有限数量的身份并提交任意价值报告而获益。由此，女巫鲁棒性加入了自适应钳制的理想性质列表。

我们的工作受到去中心化金融中实际问题的启发，其中自动做市商（automated market makers, AMMs）是使用最广泛的应用之一。我们设计了一种新的AMM机制，以两种方式扩展了经典的自适应钳制：它支持双边市场并抵御双边策略，且允许供给函数依赖于拍卖状态。其单边特例被我们的广义自适应钳制框架所涵盖，我们的证明技术表明，完整的双边机制同时满足女巫鲁棒激励相容性和一种松弛的帕累托最优性概念。我们通过不可能性结果论证这些保证本质上是最优可能的。更广泛地说，我们的工作揭示了经典算法博弈论与现代去中心化区块链之间的新联系。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The adaptive clinching auction of Dobzinski, Lavi, and Nisan (FOCS'08) is a cornerstone of mechanism design with budget-constrained bidders, and it has since inspired a broad range of extensions to substantially richer market settings. Adaptive clinching enjoys compelling guarantees: it is Pareto optimal, and truthful bidding is a dominant strategy whenever budgets cannot be overreported. In many real-world environments, however, identities are cheap to create, making resilience to Sybil attacks an additional requirement. Whether the guarantees of adaptive clinching survive when a bidder can split its budget across fake identities has remained open. We introduce a generalized framework for adaptive clinching and prove that it satisfies Sybil-robust incentive compatibility: a bidder cannot benefit from splitting into any finite number of identities with arbitrary value reports, provided their aggregate reported budget does not exceed the bidder's enforceable true budget. Sybil resilience thus joins the list of ideal properties of adaptive clinching.
  Our work is motivated by practical problems in decentralized finance, where automated market makers (AMMs) are among the most widely used applications. We design a new AMM mechanism that extends classical adaptive clinching in two ways: it supports a two-sided market and protects against two-sided strategies, and it allows the supply function to depend on the auction state. Its one-sided special case is captured by our generalized adaptive clinching framework, and our proof techniques show that the full two-sided mechanism simultaneously satisfies Sybil-robust incentive compatibility and a relaxed notion of Pareto optimality. We argue that these guarantees are essentially the best possible via impossibility results. More broadly, our work reveals new connections between classical algorithmic game theory and modern decentralized blockchains.
