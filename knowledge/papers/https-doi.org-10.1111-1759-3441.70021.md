<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察货币政策如何通过澳大利亚经济传导，尤其关注宏观经济、金融和资产市场变量的相对反应。使用1991年第一季度至2023年第四季度的季度数据，我们估计了一个包含九个内生变量的开放经济结构向量自回归（SVAR）模型。货币政策冲击通过基于经济理论的短期同期约束进行识别，并估计了一个恰好识别的递归SVAR。脉冲响应结果揭示了货币政策在整个经济中传导的显著异质性。紧缩性现金利率冲击会降低通胀，这与传统货币政策传导一致，尽管估计的响应存在相当大的不确定性。实际GDP的响应相对较弱，而住宅房地产价格在金融和资产市场变量中表现出最清晰且最持久的响应。广义货币和私营部门信贷的响应估计精度较低，而汇率响应偏离了传统预测。总体而言，研究结果表明澳大利亚的货币政策通过多个相互关联的传导渠道发挥作用，其中通过政策利率渠道和住房/资产价格渠道的效果尤为显著。这些结果凸显了金融和资产市场状况在理解开放经济中货币政策传导和有效性方面的重要性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines how monetary policy is transmitted through the Australian economy, with particular attention to the relative responses of macroeconomic, financial and asset‐market variables. Using quarterly data from 1991Q1 to 2023Q4, we estimate an open‐economy structural vector autoregressive (SVAR) model comprising nine endogenous variables. Monetary policy shocks are identified through short‐run contemporaneous restrictions grounded in economic theory, and an exactly identified recursive SVAR is estimated. The impulse‐response results reveal substantial heterogeneity in monetary policy transmission across the economy. A contractionary cash‐rate shock reduces inflation, consistent with conventional monetary transmission, although the estimated response is subject to considerable uncertainty. Real GDP responds comparatively weakly, whereas residential property prices display the clearest and most persistent response among financial and asset‐market variables. The responses of broad money and private‐sector credit are less precisely estimated, while the exchange‐rate response departs from the conventional prediction. Overall, the findings indicate that monetary policy in Australia operates through multiple, interconnected transmission channels, with particularly pronounced effects through the policy‐rate and housing/asset‐price channels. These results underscore the importance of financial and asset‐market conditions in understanding the transmission and effectiveness of monetary policy in an open economy.
