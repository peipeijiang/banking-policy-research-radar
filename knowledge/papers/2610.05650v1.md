<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当拍卖竞标者对其对手产生误判时，卖方不仅可以改变信息租金的数额，还可以改变其交付的状态。我们追问这种支付设计机会在何种条件下能够经受有限投标界面、非负支付限制以及与相同信念下传统拍卖的比较。我们将支付设计表述为以非负竞标者租金为变量的线性规划。当某一状态对于其预期类型而言成本最低，且对潜在模仿者而言吸引力最小时，该状态可以安全地承载某一报告的租金。我们刻画了这种对齐关系，当对齐不完美时界定了受限支付架构的成本，并在排序冲突时使用影子价格。自身类型投影为固定分配产生了唯一的最优预期支付规则。连续投标区间在每个价值上实现了真实报告，但产生了混同损失和区间内剩余。在一个独立的平滑信念模型下，可行的真实规则和收入上界支持与相同信念下第一价格均衡的比较。使用1，000条eBay证明集拍卖记录进行的校准展示了一种依赖于分区的反转：在一种情景下，第一价格在十个等概率区间上优于所有完全真实、无补贴的规则，而在十个等金额区间上更丰富的或有规则则优于第一价格。运营者应联合选择界面分辨率、支付依赖性和诚信容忍度。验证支持激励的概率，在相同信念下比较各种形式，并区分重新设计与部署。任何收益优势必须覆盖实施成本，才能意味着利润优势。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When auction bidders misperceive their rivals, a seller can change not only the amount of information rent but also the states in which it is delivered. We ask when this payment-design opportunity survives a finite bidding interface, nonnegative-payment restrictions, and comparison with conventional auctions under the same beliefs. We formulate payment design as a linear program in nonnegative bidder rents. A single state can safely carry a report's rent when it is both cheapest for its intended type and least attractive to potential mimics. We characterize this alignment, bound the cost of a restricted payment architecture when alignment is imperfect, and use shadow prices when the rankings conflict. Own-type projection yields a unique optimal expected-payment rule for a fixed allocation. Contiguous bid bands implement truthful reporting at every value, but create pooling losses and within-band surplus. Under a separate smooth-belief model, feasible truthful rules and revenue upper bounds support comparisons with a first-price equilibrium under the same beliefs. A calibration using 1,000 eBay proof-set auction records illustrates a partition-dependent reversal: first price dominates all exactly truthful, no-subsidy rules on ten equal-probability bands in one scenario, while a richer contingent rule on ten equal-dollar bands outperforms it. An operator should choose interface resolution, payment dependence, and integrity tolerances jointly. Validate the probabilities that support incentives, compare formats under the same beliefs, and distinguish redesign from deployment. Any receipt advantage must cover implementation costs before it can imply a profit advantage.
