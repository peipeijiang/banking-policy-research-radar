<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文基于乌克兰国家银行2025—2026年的统计数据，考察了全面战争条件下乌克兰家庭的储蓄与投资行为。研究论证了有必要将储蓄行为（将资金存放于存款、现金或外币）与投资行为（主要是购买国内政府债券）加以区分，二者受不同行为逻辑支配。研究确认，家庭投资行为在很大程度上符合理性模型：政府债券持有量的增长快于存款增长，其原因在于更高的实际收益率以及票息收入的免税地位。2025年期间，家庭持有的国内政府债券从785亿格里夫纳增长至1119亿格里夫纳（+42.6%），增速接近家庭存款增速（+14.4%）的三倍，这一差距可由债券约15—16%的名义收益率（经通胀调整后的实际收益率约为8%）相对于10.42%的存款利率来解释。相比之下，储蓄行为则表现出对理性预期的系统性偏离：家庭通胀预期并不对官方消费者价格指数（CPI）作出反应，而是对一组范围狭窄的可见价格冲击（燃料、电力）作出反应；家庭汇率预期仍比企业更为悲观，而实际现金外币需求却在下降，表明所表达的担忧与实际行为之间存在差距。在储蓄行为方面，自全面入侵开始以来，家庭存款账户数量仅增长17%，而平均账户余额上升63%，表明家庭正在将资金集中到现有账户中，而非开立新账户。研究表明，乌克兰国家银行金融压力指数中的家庭行为子指数在方法论上被视为系统性风险的独立来源，不能还原为利率因素。本文的结论是，刺激投资行为与储蓄行为需要差异化的政策工具：对投资行为而言需要价格信号，对储蓄行为而言则需要基于沟通的工具。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The article examines the saving and investment behavior of Ukrainian households under conditions of full-scale war, based on statistical data of the National Bank of Ukraine for 2025–2026. The study substantiates the need to distinguish between saving behavior (holding funds in deposits, cash, or foreign currency) and investment behavior (primarily the purchase of domestic government bonds) as strategies governed by different behavioral logics. It is established that household investment behavior largely conforms to the rational model: the growth in government bond holdings outpaces deposit growth and is explained by higher effective yield and the tax-exempt status of coupon income. Household holdings of domestic government bonds grew from UAH 78.5 billion to UAH 111.9 billion over 2025 (+42.6%), nearly three times faster than the +14.4% growth in household deposits, a gap explained by bond yields of roughly 15-16% nominal (about 8% in real, inflation-adjusted terms) against a 10.42% deposit rate. Saving behavior, by contrast, shows systematic deviations from rational expectations: household inflation expectations respond not to the official consumer price index but to a narrow set of visible price shocks (fuel, electricity); household exchange-rate expectations remain more pessimistic than those of businesses, while actual demand for cash foreign currency is declining, indicating a gap between stated concerns and actual behavior. Within saving behavior, the number of household deposit accounts grew by only 17% since the onset of the full-scale invasion, while the average account balance rose by 63%, indicating that households are consolidating funds in existing accounts rather than opening new ones. The household behavior sub-index within the National Bank of Ukraine's Financial Stress Index is shown to be methodologically treated as an independent source of systemic risk, not reducible to interest rates. The article concludes that stimulating investment and saving behavior requires differentiated policy tools: a price signal for investment behavior and communication-based instruments for saving behavior.
