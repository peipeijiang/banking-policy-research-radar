<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

研究者和政策制定者常常同时针对众多单元做出大量决策，所使用的单元层面效应估计带有噪声。研究者通常假设单元层面效应来自一个共同的效应分布，从而实现最优决策。然而在应用中，这一效应分布对计量经济学家而言是未知的，这给最终的决策问题引入了不确定性。本文考虑量化这种不确定性的问题。例如，研究者可能试图理解一个知晓效应分布的“神谕”决策者会如何比较不同的可行决策，或者理解神谕者可能会做出哪些决策。从神谕决策者的视角分析这些问题，涉及在给定单元层面效应估计值的情况下比较其条件均值，这表明一个充分的推断目标是这些条件均值的一致覆盖。基于条件均值的一致覆盖与多重检验之间的等价性，我提出了满足这一覆盖要求的方法。最后，我考虑了一个应用于识别高经济流动性社区问题的案例。我的方法相对于现有方法收紧了推断，并识别出可以确信地纳入神谕选择的单元。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Researchers and policymakers frequently make a large number of decisions simultaneously over many units using noisy estimates of unit-level effects. Researchers often assume that unit-level effects are drawn from a common effect distribution, allowing for optimal decision-making. In applications, however, this effect distribution is unknown to the econometrician, introducing uncertainty in the ultimate decision-making problem. This paper considers the problem of quantifying this uncertainty. For instance, researchers may seek to understand how an oracle decision maker with knowledge of the effect distribution would compare different feasible decisions, or understand which decisions an oracle would plausibly make. Analyzing these questions from the perspective of an oracle decision maker involves comparing the conditional means of unit-level effects given estimates thereof, suggesting a sufficient inferential objective of uniform coverage of these conditional means. Based on an equivalence between uniform coverage of conditional means and multiple testing, I develop methods that satisfy this coverage. Finally, I consider an application to the problem of identifying high-economic-mobility neighborhoods. My methods sharpen inference relative to existing methods and identify units that can be confidently included in oracle selections.
