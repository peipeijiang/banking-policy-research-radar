<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

GARCH-LSTM混合模型在波动率预测文献中日益流行。迄今为止，这些模型几乎只关注点估计和RMSE、MAPE等精度指标，而未能评估这些点估计的不确定性。我们从两个方面弥补这一空白。首先，我们通过以ARMA结构对条件均值建模来扩展现有的GARCH-LSTM模型。其次，我们开发了一种前向bootstrap算法，为未来已实现波动率构建多步向前预测区间。为了解决预测区间覆盖率不足这一众所周知的问题，我们实施了两种解决方案。一种从经验残差分布中抽取bootstrap新息，以模拟金融序列的厚尾特性。另一种利用conformal prediction（共形预测）的原理系统地扩展预测区间长度。我们在具有厚尾和时变GARCH过程的模拟中，以及对2020年至2025年四个美国股票指数的滚动窗口预测中评估了这些方法。基于ARMA的混合模型在大多数设定下给出最低的点预测误差，而将两种补救措施与基于均值的混合模型相结合，在竞争方法中产生的区间最接近名义水平。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

GARCH-LSTM hybrid models have become increasingly popular in the volatility forecasting literature. To date, these models have almost exclusively focused on point estimation and accuracy measures such as RMSE and MAPE, while failing to assess the uncertainty of these point estimates. We approach this gap in two ways. First, we extend the existing GARCH-LSTM model by modeling the conditional mean with an ARMA structure. Second, we develop a forward bootstrap algorithm to build multi-step-ahead prediction intervals for future realized volatility. In order to address the notorious undercoverage of prediction intervals, we implement two solutions. One draws bootstrap innovations from the empirical residual distribution to mimic the fat-tail property of financial series. The other utilizes principles from conformal prediction to systematically extend the prediction interval length. We evaluate the methods in simulations with heavy-tailed and time-varying GARCH processes and in rolling-window forecasts of four U.S. stock indices from 2020 to 2025. The ARMA-based hybrid gives the lowest point-forecast errors in most settings, and combining both remedies with the mean-based hybrid yields intervals closest to the nominal level among competing methods.
