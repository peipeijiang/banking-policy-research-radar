<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文分析了2021年1月至2024年3月期间两家公司的月度报告：一家是做市商Virtu，另一家是经纪自营商SoHo。尽管股票订单的概率规律相对稳定，但标准方法——线性回归和LSTM——对其的预测能力较差。然而，月度间的相关性高度稳定且可预测。本文研究了十六个数据集的分布，这些数据集来源于经纪商根据监管要求报告的26个股票交易参数。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The paper analyzes monthly reports of two companies: one market maker, Virtu, and one broker-dealer, SoHo, in the period between January 2021 and March 2024. The predictive power of standard methods for the probability laws of stock orders- linear regression and LSTM- is poor, despite their probability laws being relatively stable. However, the month-to-month correlations are highly stable and predictable. In this paper, we study the distributions of the sixteen datasets resulting from the 26 stock-trading parameters reported by brokerages under regulatory requirements.
