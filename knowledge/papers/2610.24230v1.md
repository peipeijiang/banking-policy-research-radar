<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了具有纳什议价以及事前和事后异质性的经典搜寻-匹配模型中的均衡排序。在搜寻条件下按类型匹配的标准条件下，匹配剩余保留了Becker无摩擦基准的两个性质——单交叉性(single-crossing)和单峰性(single-peakedness)——但必然丧失了第三个性质，即互反性(reciprocity)。相反，搜寻产生了共识：类型区间共享相同的剩余最大化伴侣。对于此类区间，在相遇的条件下，错配的配对比完全按类型匹配的配对更有可能匹配。有两股力量驱动了这一结果：在搜寻中，外部选项随生产率上升的幅度小于一比一，而对于合适伴侣较少的代理人，外部选项下降得更多。在全局层面上，匹配概率曲面的上等值集在某一内生阈值以下构成格(lattices)，但在该阈值以上几乎从不构成格。这意味着MLRP对某些对数凹生存函数不成立。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study equilibrium sorting in the canonical search-and-matching model with Nash bargaining and ex-ante and ex-post heterogeneity. Under standard conditions for assortative matching under search, match surplus retains two properties of Becker's frictionless benchmark---single-crossing and single-peakedness---but necessarily loses the third, reciprocity. Instead, search generates consensus: intervals of types share the same surplus-maximizing partner. For such intervals, mismatched pairs are more likely to match, conditional on meeting, than perfectly assortative ones. Two forces drive this: with search, outside options rise less than one-for-one with productivity and fall more for agents with fewer suitable partners. Globally, upper contour sets of the match-probability surface are lattices below an endogenous threshold but almost never above it. This means that MLRP fails for some log-concave survival function.

</details>
