# Participation, selection and indicative bidding in auctions with costly entry

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究带有昂贵进入成本的拍卖，其中竞拍者在进入前对其关联的私人估值仅有不完全信息。我们考察指示性投标——一种要求在进入前提交非约束性初步报价的机制——是否能刺激参与并改善进入者选择，并通过受控实验室实验将其表现与无限制进入和设上限进入进行比较。当进入成本较高时，指示性投标能显著增加收入，主要途径是使参与度超出理论预测。当进入成本较低时，其预测的收入优势因高于预测的选择低效率而被削弱。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study auctions with costly entry in which bidders have incomplete information about their affiliated private valuations prior to entry. We examine whether indicative bidding--a mechanism requiring non-binding preliminary bids before entry--can stimulate participation and improve entrant selection, and we compare its performance with unrestricted and capped entry in a controlled laboratory experiment. When entry costs are high, indicative bidding generates significantly more revenue, primarily by increasing participation beyond theoretical predictions. When entry costs are low, its predicted revenue advantage is attenuated by higher-than-predicted selection inefficiency.
