# Why Good Experience Can End Joint Activity

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02292v1)

## 一句话结论

> 构建偏好学习与归因成本的动态博弈模型，证明在反馈精度与偏好持久性满足特定条件时，学习伙伴偏好会使双方境况变差并导致联合活动终止，且停止后信念固定难以恢复。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

通过共同活动了解伴侣的偏好，可能使双方境况都比从该活动获得无反馈时更差。我们研究一个回应者，他重视伴侣的收益，但当参与被归因于个人兴趣时会承担一项成本。双方都会观察到关于回应者是否想要该活动的噪声证据，且该证据仅在参与之后才可获得。兴趣的证据会提高这一成本，从而抑制在不想要的日子参与；而只在想要的日子参与则会过于暴露而难以维持。在基于当前收益的选择下，我们推导出一个条件，在该条件下，学习可以在从不使停止对任何一方有利的情况下终止参与。由情侣日记所描述的反馈准确性和偏好持久性在基线收益下满足该条件。一旦活动停止，不再有进一步证据到来，且关于回应者总体上想要该活动的信念保持固定，因此等待可能永远无法恢复它；而不参与即可获得的偏好信息则可以。一个具有高度持久偏好的独立前瞻性模型说明了为什么这一补救措施也可能降低联合福利。当参与精确揭示偏好时，回应者可能接受当前损失以建立缺乏兴趣，从而使接受不那么暴露，进而维持在想要的日子参与。活动之外的免费日常揭示消除了这一激励。在模型的基线下，它在具有精确参与反馈的单调马尔可夫均衡中降低了最佳联合价值，但在反馈弱至日记水平时则提高了它。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Learning a partner's preferences through joint activity can make both partners worse off than receiving no feedback from the activity. We study a responder who values the partner's payoff but bears a cost when joining is attributed to personal interest. Both partners observe noisy evidence of whether the responder wanted the activity, available only after participation. Evidence of interest raises this cost, discouraging joining on unwanted days; joining only on wanted days would then be too revealing to sustain. With choices based on current payoffs, we derive a condition under which learning can end participation without ever making stopping beneficial to either partner. Feedback accuracy and preference persistence described by couples' diaries satisfy this condition at baseline payoffs. Once activity stops, no further evidence arrives and the belief that the responder generally wants the activity stays fixed, so waiting may never restore it; preference information available without participating can. A separate forward-looking model with highly persistent preferences shows why this remedy can also reduce joint welfare. When participation reveals preferences exactly, a responder may accept at a current loss to establish a lack of interest, making acceptance less revealing and so sustaining participation on wanted days. Free daily revelation outside the activity removes this incentive. At the model's baseline, it lowers the best joint value in monotone Markov equilibria with exact participation feedback, but raises it with feedback as weak as the diary's.

</details>
