# Allocative Efficiency and the Returns to Innovation

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14969v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文在数量竞争网络中分析创新者利润未内部化配置效率变化，并估计美国上市公司研发的社会回报约为私人回报的5.39倍。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当网络中的生产者进行数量竞争时，创新者的利润不包含其创新所引起的配置效率变化，无论是收益还是损失，因为其自身选择已是最优的，而对其利润的其他影响都是转移支付。在固定配置下，工艺创新者还会放弃其自身及下游对其创新所增加的产出所获得的加成，对于2015年美国上市公司而言，这相当于其成本节约的14%至26%。对于占销售额68%的创新者而言，配置效率的变化超过私人回报的十分之一。在马尔可夫研发博弈中，研发在企业间的重新配置同样对创新者具有外部性。竞争对手的利润和市场价值甚至可能在符号上误判来自它们的商业窃取的福利成本。在估计溢出效应的情况下，研发的社会回报是私人回报的5.39倍，而在基于产出并估计商业窃取的核算下为2.53倍，这主要是因为该核算将竞争对手损失的销售额计入创新者的贡献。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When producers in a network compete in quantities, an innovator's profit contains none of the change in allocative efficiency that its innovation causes, whether a gain or a loss, because its own choices are optimal and other effects on its profit are transfers. A process innovator also forgoes its own and downstream markups on the output its innovation adds at a fixed allocation, worth 14 to 26 percent of its cost saving for U.S. public firms in 2015. For innovators accounting for 68 percent of sales, the change in allocative efficiency exceeds a tenth of the private return. In a Markov R&D game, reallocation of R&D across firms is likewise external to the innovator. Rivals' profits and market values can misstate the welfare cost of business stealing from them even in sign. With estimated spillovers, the social return to R&D is 5.39 times the private return, against 2.53 in output-based accounting with estimated business stealing, mainly because that accounting credits the innovator with its rivals' lost sales.

</details>
