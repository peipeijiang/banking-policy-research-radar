<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察了2007年至2025年间欧元区公司债券与股票收益之间不断演变的关系。尽管理论预测在市场承压和负需求冲击期间，两者应呈负相关（或至少相关性降低），但实证证据表明这种关系随时间存在显著的不稳定性。我们使用严格匹配的发行人样本构建了股票指数和公司债券指数，使两个指数的成分企业完全相同。随后，我们运用动态条件相关（Dynamic Conditional Correlation）模型分析了它们在欧元区主要市场中相关性的动态变化。结果表明，两者相关性历史上为负，但自2009年以来逐渐趋向中性，且可能迅速向任一方向转变。我们发现，无论在高通胀还是低通胀环境下，贴现率渠道都是共同变动的主导驱动因素。相比之下，金融压力仅在低通胀体制下降低相关性——这与避险（flight-to-quality）动态一致——但在高通胀体制下并非如此。这表明，在高通胀时期，投资者不能依赖政策引致的贴现率压缩来产生有利于债券的跨资产套利机会。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines the evolving relationship between corporate bond and stock returns in the euro area from 2007 to 2025. While theory predicts negative (or at least lower) correlations during periods of market stress and negative demand shocks, empirical evidence shows substantial instability over time. We construct equity and corporate bond indices using a strictly matched issuer universe, such that the constituent firms are identical across the two indices. We then analyse their correlation dynamics across major euro area markets using Dynamic Conditional Correlation models. Results indicate that correlations, historically negative, have since 2009 gradually trended toward neutrality, but can shift in either direction rapidly. We show that the discount-rate channel emerges as the dominant driver of co-movement in both low- and high-inflation environments. By contrast, financial stress reduces correlations – consistent with flight-to-quality dynamics –only in low-inflation regimes but not in high inflation regimes. This indicates that investors cannot rely on policy-induced compression in discount rates to generate cross-asset arbitrage opportunities in favour of bonds when inflation is high.
