<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们引入了平衡相关参数化（balanced correlation parametrization, BCP），它将正定相关矩阵一一映射到对称空心矩阵集合上。该参数化的一个关键特征是，变换及其逆变换均具有闭式表达。它结合了边际相关与偏相关，具有逆一致性，并在变量置换和符号变化下具有等变性，同时保持块结构。BCP的一个新性质是，在适当的因子条件下，当维数趋于无穷时，它保持相关稀疏性，这为稀疏高维相关建模提供了基础。使用1765只股票在15年间的标准化特质日收益率进行的一个实证示例表明，BCP保留了冲击相关性的近似稀疏模式和幅度，并且在稀疏坐标约束下能够给出准确的正定重构。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce the balanced correlation parametrization (BCP), which maps positive-definite correlation matrices one-to-one onto the set of symmetric hollow matrices. A key feature of the parametrization is that both the transformation and its inverse are available in closed form. It combines marginal and partial correlations, is inverse-consistent and equivariant under variable permutations and sign changes, and preserves block structure. A novel property of BCP is that it preserves correlation sparsity as the dimension tends to infinity under suitable factor conditions, providing a basis for sparse high-dimensional correlation modeling. An empirical illustration using standardized idiosyncratic daily stock returns for 1,765 equities over 15 years shows that BCP retains the approximate sparsity pattern and magnitudes of shock correlations, and that it yields accurate positive-definite reconstructions under sparse coordinate restrictions.
