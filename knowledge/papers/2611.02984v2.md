<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人们在汇总意见的同时面临将对象分类的问题。我们对这种具有多重归属的分类聚合问题进行了公理化研究，即每个个体为每个对象分配一个类别，而最终的分类结果可能分配多个类别。在自然设定和公理（即独立性与支持性）下，我们的每个新公理——责任性、分裂稳定性与拒绝稳定性——都刻画了我们所称的寡头规则（junta rules）。这些规则恰好当某个固定联盟的成员为某对象分配某类别时，才为该对象分配该类别。能够确保某种分配成立的联盟构成一个筛（grill），这是一种由超滤子（ultrafilter）所蕴含的拓扑结构。因此，本研究与以决定性联盟结构为特征的阿罗定理（Arrow's theorem）文献相呼应。本文还研究了无限人口设定，以厘清各公理之间的差异。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

People face the problem of classifying objects into categories while aggregating their opinions. We axiomatically study such a classification aggregation problem with multiple memberships, meaning that each individual assigns one category to every object, whereas the resulting classification may assign more than one category. Under the natural settings and axioms (i.e., Independence and Supportiveness), each of our new axioms -- Responsibility, Split Stability and Rejection Stability -- characterises what we call the junta rules. The rules assign a category to an object exactly when some member of a fixed coalition does so. The coalitions that can secure an assignment form a grill, a topological structure implied by an ultrafilter. Hence, the study parallels the literature on Arrow's theorem characterised by the decisive coalition structures. An infinite population setting is also investigated to clarify the differences among the axioms.

</details>
