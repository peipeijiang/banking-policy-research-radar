<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

国家规划将人口、人力资本和人工智能准备度分别记入不同的账目。人口统计核算已从人头计数发展到技能调整存量，并且仍在争论一旦对技能进行建模后年龄结构还保留多少影响，然而现有单位均未纳入使准备度转化为生产能力所需的条件。本研究提出有效认知人口（Effective Cognitive Population, ECP），这是一种可分解的单位，依据能力以及能力得以发挥的条件对人口进行加权，其锚定于世界银行人力资本指数增强版（Human Capital Index Plus, HCI+）以及国际货币基金组织人工智能准备度指数（AI Preparedness Index）的非重叠维度。该架构在原则上具有可移植性；本文测试的案例是人工智能，因其已有公开的准备度指数。针对144个国家，HCI+作为生产率水平，AI机会利用数字基础设施和创新整合，转化治理利用监管和伦理，基准设定为ECP = N × H × (1 + A × C)。在与2024年基于相同人口基数的总产出对比中，ECP将准则R²从HCI+调整存量的0.849提升至0.882，并将留一国家交叉验证的均方根误差（RMSE）从0.723降至0.641，劳动年龄人口比较结果一致，且自助法置信区间排除零值。144个国家中有89个相对于人头计数至少移动了十个排名位次，其中大部分变动源于人力资本调整本身。结果在不同分母、数据版本、聚合形式以及包含27条规则的多元宇宙分析中均保持稳健。A与C的直接交互作用未获得统计支持，因此该结合是一种规划规则而非因果互补关系。ECP是一种诊断性账目，其范围不包括人口下降预测及人工智能因果生产率效应的估计。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

National planning counts population, human capital, and artificial-intelligence preparedness in separate ledgers. Demographic accounting has advanced from headcount to skills-adjusted stocks and still debates how much age structure retains once skills are modeled, yet no existing unit carries the conditions under which preparedness becomes productive capacity. This study introduces the Effective Cognitive Population (ECP), a decomposable unit that weights population by capability and by the conditions under which capability is deployed, anchored to the World Bank Human Capital Index Plus (HCI+) and the non-overlapping dimensions of the IMF AI Preparedness Index. The architecture is portable in principle; the case tested here is artificial intelligence, which has a published preparedness index. For 144 countries, HCI+ becomes a productivity level, AI opportunity uses digital infrastructure and innovation integration, conversion governance uses regulation and ethics, and the benchmark is ECP = N H(1 + AC). Against 2024 total output on identical population bases, ECP raises criterion R-squared from 0.849 for the HCI+-adjusted stock to 0.882 and lowers leave-one-country-out RMSE from 0.723 to 0.641, with the working-age comparison identical and bootstrap intervals excluding zero. Eighty-nine of 144 countries move at least ten rank positions from headcount, mostly through the human-capital adjustment itself. Results are stable across denominators, vintages, aggregation forms, and a 27-rule multiverse. The direct A by C interaction is not statistically supported, so the conjunction is a planning rule rather than causal complementarity. ECP is a diagnostic ledger whose scope excludes forecasts of population decline and estimates of AI's causal productivity effect.
