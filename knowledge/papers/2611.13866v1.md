# Economic Benefits of Cross-Border Electricity Trade and Domestic Electricity Production in Switzerland

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13866v1)

> 论文使用Nexus-e框架和电力市场模型模拟瑞士在自给自足与自由贸易情景下的发电容量和电网扩展，评估2040年跨境电力贸易的福利效应，发现约15亿欧元的净收益，并包含从生产者到消费者的18亿欧元转移。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文评估了在能源价格上涨和地缘政治不确定性背景下跨境电力贸易的经济效益。瑞士因其地理位置及与欧盟电力市场的一体化程度有限，提供了一个尤为有力的案例。我们首先使用Nexus-e框架模拟了自给自足和自由贸易情景下所需的发电容量和输电网扩展。基于这些容量配置，一个基本面电力市场模型模拟了2040年预测需求和发电条件下的批发市场结果。通过综合考虑消费者剩余和生产者剩余，以及对发电容量和电网基础设施的投资，我们提供了跨境电力贸易福利效应的全面评估，估计收益为15亿欧元。更具体地说，跨境电力贸易的开放准入意味着从主要为公有制的生产者向消费者转移约18亿欧元。最后，我们提供了更广泛宏观经济效应的示意性非因果映射。在价格趋同假设下，隐含的需求调整对应于约70亿欧元的GDP等效效应。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper assesses the economic benefits of cross-border electricity trade in the context of rising energy prices and geopolitical uncertainty. Switzerland provides a particularly compelling case because of its geographical location and limited integration into the EU electricity market. We first use the Nexus-e framework to simulate the generation capacity and transmission grid expansion required under autarky and free-trade scenarios. Based on these capacity configurations, a fundamental power market model simulates wholesale market outcomes under projected demand and generation conditions for 2040. By jointly accounting for consumer and producer surplus, as well as investments in generation capacity and grid infrastructure, we provide a comprehensive assessment of the welfare effects of cross-border electricity trade, leading to an estimated EUR 1.5 billion gain. More specifically, open access to cross-border electricity trade implies a EUR 1.8 billion transfer from largely publicly owned producers to consumers. Finally, we provide an illustrative, non-causal mapping of broader macroeconomic effects. Under a price-convergence assumption, the implied demand adjustment corresponds to a GDP-equivalent effect of approximately EUR 7 billion.
