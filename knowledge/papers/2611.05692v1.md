<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们沿着两个维度发展了一般性的动态机制设计理论：隐藏行动、信息生产与披露的联合内生性与交互作用；以及跨经济环境和可行性结构的一般性。我们容纳相关私人信息、相互依赖偏好以及可能为无限维的配置。我们引入联合完美相关贝叶斯均衡（joint perfect correlated Bayesian equilibrium），在该概念下建立动态显示原理，并递归刻画激励相容性。聚焦于私人价值环境，使我们能够通过选择最优性、报告包络一阶条件、全局积分单调性和历史报告激励来刻画联合激励。我们推导出精确的动态信息租金恒等式、条件收入等价和动态虚拟剩余。比较最优、两种次优和第三优合约，区分了信息租金、隐藏行动实施成本和直接信息生产支出，揭示了非可加交互作用和分支结果。我们刻画了可行与最优披露。应用表明，Milgrom-Weber收入排名在内生信息获取下可能逆转，而最优金融合约披露可能缺失、为内部解或完全披露。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a general theory of dynamic mechanism design along two dimensions: joint endogeneity and interaction of hidden actions, information production, and disclosure; and generality across economic environments and feasibility structures. We accommodate correlated private information, interdependent preferences, and potentially infinite-dimensional allocations. We introduce joint perfect correlated Bayesian equilibrium, establish a dynamic revelation principle under this concept, and recursively characterize incentive compatibility. Focusing on a private-values environment allows us to characterize joint incentives through choice optimality, reporting-envelope first-order conditions, global integral monotonicity, and history-report incentives. We derive an exact dynamic information-rent identity, conditional revenue equivalence, and dynamic virtual surplus. Comparing first-best, two second-best, and third-best contracts distinguishes information rents, hidden-action implementation costs, and direct information-production expenditures, revealing nonadditive interactions and branched outcomes. We characterize feasible and optimal disclosure. Applications show the Milgrom-Weber revenue ranking can reverse with endogenous information acquisition, while optimal financial-contract disclosure may be absent, interior, or complete.
