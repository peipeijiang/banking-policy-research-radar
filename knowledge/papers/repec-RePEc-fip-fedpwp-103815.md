<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

发明家团队目前产生了超过80%的美国专利，而1976年这一比例仅为45%。同一时期，发明家就业越来越集中于大企业，其集中度上升幅度是整体就业集中度的六倍。为解释这些趋势，我们构建了一个新的企业内内生团队形成模型，并将其嵌入发明家劳动力市场的一般均衡模型。团队通过提高发明家之间有效匹配的可能性，增加了企业的规模收益。这些匹配的价值随时间上升，可能由知识专业化程度提高所驱动。这导致在均衡状态下大企业扩张、小企业收缩，从而解释了所观察到的发明家就业集中度上升。由于企业不会将知识溢出内部化，发明家在企业之间存在错配。团队生产的兴起通过将发明家从小企业重新配置出去，使这种错配放大了六倍，而小企业产生的知识溢出比例过高。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Teams of inventors now produce over 80 percent of U.S. patents, up from just 45 percent in 1976. Over the same period, inventor employment has become increasingly concentrated in large firms, rising six times more than overall employment concentration. To explain these trends, we develop a new model of endogenous team formation within firms and embed it in a general equilibrium model of the inventor labor market. Teams increase firms’ returns to scale by increasing the likelihood of productive matches between inventors. The value of these matches rises over time, possibly driven by increasing knowledge specialization. This causes large firms to expand and small firms to shrink in equilibrium, accounting for the observed increase in inventor employment concentration. Because firms do not internalize knowledge spillovers, there is misallocation of inventors across firms. The rise of team production amplifies this misallocation by a factor of six by reallocating inventors away from small firms that generate disproportionately large knowledge spillovers.

</details>
