<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

慈善捐赠者往往看重不同的公共服务，而预算主管部门则在一个共享的组合中分配公共支出。政策框架常常试图确保私人资金补充而非取代公共资金。我们区分了基线附加性（baseline additionality），即维持赠礼前的公共资金，与组合保全（portfolio preservation），即在所有赠礼在自由裁量预算下汇集后维持某项服务的供给。对某一服务的较大赠礼会提高每项服务的自由裁量供给，并减少其他服务可用的保护余量。这种跨捐赠者的财政外部性产生了一个精确的可行性结果：如果某项服务的最大允许下限低于其赠礼后的自由裁量供给，那么每一项有效的保护改革都会使该服务相对于组合参照减少，包括对其自身拨款的完全保护。反之，一项在保全所有其他服务的同时扩大至少一项捐赠者支持服务的改革，恰好当每个最大下限达到参照时存在。本文刻画了完整的保全集及其福利最大化元素。在对数偏好下，捐赠份额决定可保护性，而异质性捐赠者估值决定福利归属。因此，基线附加性可能成立，而某项服务却失去了由其他赠礼产生的财政收益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Philanthropic donors often value different public services, while a budget authority allocates public spending across a shared portfolio. Policy frameworks frequently seek to ensure that private finance supplements rather than displaces public funding. We distinguish baseline additionality, which preserves pre-gift public funding, from portfolio preservation, which preserves a service's provision after all gifts are pooled under discretionary budgeting. A larger gift to one service raises the discretionary provision of every service and reduces the protection slack available to the others. This cross-donor fiscal externality yields an exact feasibility result: if a service's maximum permissible floor lies below its discretionary post-gift provision, every effective protection reform reduces that service relative to the portfolio reference, including full protection of its own appropriation. Conversely, a reform that expands at least one donor-backed service while preserving all others exists exactly when every maximum floor reaches the reference. The paper characterizes the complete preservation set and its welfare-maximizing element. Under logarithmic preferences, donation shares determine protectability, while heterogeneous donor valuations determine welfare incidence. Baseline additionality may therefore hold while a service loses fiscal benefits generated by other gifts.

</details>
