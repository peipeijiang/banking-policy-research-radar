# When Does Inexact Matching Ensure Balance and Inference without Adjustment?

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一对一匹配（one-to-one matching）且不重复抽样是在观察性研究设计中构建可比处理组与对照组样本的经典方法。它将每个处理单元与一个不同的对照单元配对，同时最小化协变量距离目标函数。当协变量为连续变量时，匹配对通常并非精确匹配，这会导致下游分析产生偏差。其关键理论性质，例如匹配对之间由此产生的不平衡以及在何种情况下该不平衡可忽略从而支持有效推断，仍不明确。本文基于$d$维连续协变量和二次协变量距离目标函数，对一对一匹配进行分析。首先，我们发现当$d\leq 3$时，在倾向得分（propensity score）满足标准条件以确保每个处理样本附近有充足对照样本的情况下，不平衡（组内均值之差）在具有共同一阶和二阶导数界的平滑函数族上一致地是根$n$可忽略的。然而，这种平衡受到维度限制，因为我们构造了当$d=4$时不平衡为根$n$不可忽略、当$d>4$时不平衡主导根$n$速率的例子。其次，我们证明当$d\leq 3$时，匹配设计允许对处理组平均处理效应（average treatment effect on the treated）、分布处理效应（distributional treatment effects）和分位数处理效应（quantile treatment effects）进行有效的Wald型推断和自助法（bootstrap）推断。因此，同一结果盲匹配设计无需针对推断目标量身定制即可支持多种下游推断。最后，基于匹配设计的配对随机化推断（paired randomization inference）在$d\leq 3$时在超总体（super-population）意义下渐近有效，但当$d=4$时可能失效。我们通过数值实验验证了理论结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

One-to-one matching without replacement is a classical approach to constructing comparable treated and control samples in the design of observational studies. It pairs each treated unit with a distinct control while minimizing a covariate distance objective. With continuous covariates, the matched pairs generally remain inexact, which contributes to bias in downstream analysis. Its key theoretical properties, such as the resulting imbalance between matched pairs and when it is negligible to support valid inference, remain unclear. In this paper, we analyze one-to-one matching based on $d$-dimensional, continuous covariates with a quadratic covariate-distance objective. First, we find that when $d\leq 3$, under standard conditions on the propensity score ensuring abundant control samples near each treated sample, the imbalance (difference between within-group averages) is root-$n$ negligible uniformly over the family of smooth functions with a common first- and second-order derivative bound. However, such balance is subject to a dimension restriction, as we construct examples in which the imbalance is root-$n$ non-negligible when $d=4$ and dominates root-$n$ rate when $d>4$. Second, we show that when $d\leq 3$, the matched design allows valid Wald-type and bootstrap inference for the average treatment effect on the treated, distributional treatment effects, and quantile treatment effects. Thus, the same outcome-blind matched design supports various downstream inferences without having to tailor the design to the targets. Finally, paired randomization inference based on the matched design is asymptotically valid in the super-population sense for $d\leq 3$ but can fail when $d=4$. We corroborate the theoretical results with numerical experiments.
