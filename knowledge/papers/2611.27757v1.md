# Eliminating Make-Whole Uplifts with a Minimum-Cost Uniform Price Adder

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27757v1)

## 一句话结论

> 针对电力市场联合优化中LMP低于报价导致场外补足支付的问题，提出LMP+U统一价格加价机制，在阻塞网络中最小化需求支付、消除补足支付并保持真实报价激励。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

能量与灵活爬坡备用的协同优化可能导致发电机组的调度价格（节点边际电价，LMP）低于其提交的能量报价，从而需要市场外补偿支付（make-whole payments）。此类支付在发电机组之间造成歧视性结算，并可能激励策略性报价以获取更高利润。本文提出LMP+U，一种适用于输电受限网络的市场内统一价格加价机制，将其加至LMP之上，该机制在零市场外补偿上调的约束下最小化需求支付，保留基于LMP的阻塞费用，并为价格接受型发电机组提供局部真实报价激励。我们还识别了在传统LMP配合补偿上调机制下，价格接受型发电机组可通过抬高其能量报价来增加利润的条件。数值仿真验证了LMP+U的特性及其在阻塞网络中相较于传统LMP的优势。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Co-optimizing energy and flexible ramping reserves may dispatch generators at locational marginal prices (LMPs) below their submitted energy offer prices, necessitating out-of-market make-whole payments. Such payments create discriminatory settlements among generators and may incentivize strategic bidding for higher profits. This paper proposes LMP+U, an in-market uniform price adder to LMP for transmission-constrained networks, which minimizes demand payments subject to zero out-of-market make-whole uplift, preserves LMP-based congestion charges, and provides locally truthful bidding incentives for price-taking generators. We also identify conditions under which a price-taking generator can increase its profit by inflating its energy offer under conventional LMP with make-whole uplift. Numerical simulations illustrate the properties of LMP+U and its advantages over conventional LMP in congested networks.

</details>
