<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文在有限状态安斯康姆-奥曼框架中构建了一个可识别的平滑模糊性模型，其中模糊感知和模糊态度表征可能依赖于行为的内生确定性等价效用水平。该框架区分了标准平滑模糊性模型中被固定住的两种效用水平依赖来源。在显示性单元内部的中性转移实验可恢复条件信念，而跨单元的补偿实验可恢复单元权重和局部显示的模糊态度尺度。这些对象具有不同的行为内容。在将水平内结构全局扩展之后，模糊感知在每个确定性等价水平上均可识别，而可允许的水平特定全局态度指数的变化本身未必反映行为上可识别的模糊态度变化。跨水平限制条件决定了局部显示的态度尺度何时可以改由一个共同的全局模糊态度指数来表示。基准刻画产生了一个双重隐式的平滑模糊性表征，其具有水平特定的感知以及可能水平特定的全局态度指数。要求一个共同的模糊态度指数则得到一个中间性的稳定态度模型，其中仅感知可能变化；同时施加感知稳定性，则在所维持的满支撑、中性分离、固定划分且至少具有三个显示性单元的环境中，得到标准的可识别平滑模糊性。因此，本文的贡献在于将平滑模糊性中的效用水平依赖行为分解为分别可显示的感知成分和态度成分。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops a model of identifiable smooth ambiguity in a finite-state Anscombe-Aumann framework in which ambiguity perception and the ambiguity-attitude representation may depend on the act's endogenous certainty-equivalent utility level. The framework separates two sources of utility-level dependence that standard smooth-ambiguity models hold fixed. Neutral-transfer experiments within revealed cells recover conditional beliefs, while compensation experiments across cells recover cell weights and locally revealed ambiguity-attitude scales. These objects have distinct behavioral content. After the within-level structure is extended globally, ambiguity perception is identified at each certainty-equivalent level, whereas variation in admissible level-specific global attitude indices need not by itself reflect behaviorally identified variation in ambiguity attitude. Cross-level restrictions determine when the locally revealed attitude scales can instead be represented by one common global ambiguity-attitude index. The baseline characterization yields a doubly implicit smooth-ambiguity representation with level-specific perception and potentially level-specific global attitude indices. Requiring one common ambiguity-attitude index gives an intermediate stable-attitude model in which only perception may vary; imposing perception stability as well yields standard identifiable smooth ambiguity within the maintained full-support, neutrally separated, fixed-partition environment with at least three revealed cells. The contribution is therefore a behavioral decomposition of utility-level dependence in smooth ambiguity into separately revealed perception and attitude components.
