<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文将宏观经济学中的两大文献——合成控制法（Synthetic Control Method, SCM）与动态随机一般均衡（Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium, DSGE）模型——融合为一个统一框架。SCM能够有效估计因果处理效应，但缺乏对 underlying economic mechanisms 的洞察，且不提供政策分析框架：其估计效应是一个简约式对象，会随政策体制变化而移动，从而暴露于卢卡斯批判之下。通过将SCM结果纳入一个具有微观基础的DSGE框架，并将处理表示为加载于模型结构冲击之上的可观测事件，我们识别了SCM所估计处理效应背后的传导渠道，并增强了结构解释。我们的方法恢复了政策不变的结构性原语，而非依赖于体制的缺口，从而使得对卢卡斯批判具有稳健性的反事实政策分析成为可能。由此，它将合成控制的实证透明度与DSGE模型的结构纪律统一起来——这两个互补的文献长期以来各自独立发展。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper merges two large literatures in macroeconomics, Synthetic Control Method (SCM) and Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE) models, into a single framework. SCM effectively estimates causal treatment effects but lacks insights into underlying economic mechanisms, and it does not offer a framework for policy analysis: its estimated effect is a reduced-form object that shifts with the policy regime, leaving it exposed to the Lucas critique. By integrating SCM outcomes into a micro-founded DSGE framework, representing the treatment as an observable event that loads on the model’s structural shocks, we identify the transmission channels underlying the SCM-estimated treatment effects and enhance structural interpretation. Our approach recovers policy-invariant structural primitives rather than the regime-dependent gap, enabling counterfactual policy analysis that is robust to the Lucas critique. It thereby unites the empirical transparency of synthetic controls with the structural discipline of DSGE models – two complementary literatures that have long developed in isolation.
