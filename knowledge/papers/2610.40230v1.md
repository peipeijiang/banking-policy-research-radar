# Who Says What: Strategic Communication in Networks

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40230v1)

## 一句话结论

> 构建一个私人信息代理人通过社会网络沟通并选择行动的多人协调博弈，证明均衡行动为按度折扣Bonacich中心性加权的期望理想点凸组合，并刻画真实/过度/低报信息的激励条件及内生偏误下的纳什均衡。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个多人沟通协调博弈，其中拥有私人信息的代理人通过社交网络进行沟通，并在选择最优行动时权衡自身及邻居的最优偏好点（bliss points）。我们证明，均衡行动是所有参与者期望最优偏好点的凸组合，其权重由度折扣Bonacich中心性（degree-discounted Bonacich centrality）测度决定。我们还刻画了参与者何时如实报告、过度报告或低报其私人信息，并证明虚假报告的激励关键取决于接收者在网络中的位置。一个引人注目的含义是，即使在相同偏好下，完全揭示性沟通也可能无法实现，并且在星形网络中信息传递可能保持粗糙，而在完全网络中则几乎变得完全信息。最后，当偏差（biases）为内生选择时，对邻居偏差的策略性反应由其中心性决定，而无偏性可能作为纳什均衡（Nash equilibrium）出现。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a multiplayer communication coordination game in which privately informed agents communicate through a social network and choose their optimal actions while balancing them against their own bliss points and those of their neighbours. We show that equilibrium actions are convex combinations of the expected bliss points of all players, with weights determined by a degree-discounted Bonacich centrality measure. We also characterize when players report truthfully, over-report, or under-report their private information, and show that the incentive to misreport depends critically on the receiver's position in the network. A striking implication is that even under identical preferences, fully revealing communication may not be realized, and information transmission may remain coarse in a star network while becoming almost fully informative in a complete network. Finally, when biases are endogenously chosen, strategic responses to neighbours' biases are governed by their centralities, and unbiasedness may emerge as a Nash equilibrium.

</details>
