<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究时变量分位数VAR模型，其中条件分位数不通过递归线性传播，并开发了一套脉冲响应分析工具包，据我们所知，该工具包在其他文献中尚未建立。例如，我们为分位数VAR回归建立了Bahadur表示，通过平滑检验函数推断模型参数，并通过局部投影定义和研究了时变τ-分位数脉冲响应的最佳线性投影。对1919-2025年美国工业生产增长和信用利差的应用揭示了尾部关系中的结构性转变，表明下行不对称性在较长预测期上减弱。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study time-varying quantile VAR models in which the conditional quantile does not propagate linearly through the recursion, and develop a toolkit for impulse response analysis, which to the best of our knowledge has not been established elsewhere. For example, we establish Bahadur representation for quantile VAR regression, infer model parameters via smoothed check function, and define and investigate the best linear projection of the time-varying tau-quantile impulse response via local projections. An application to U.S. industrial production growth and credit spreads from 1919-2025 uncovers structural shifts in tail relationships, demonstrating downside asymmetry diminishes at longer forecast horizons.
