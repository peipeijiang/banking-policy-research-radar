# When Is Mediated Communication Possible?

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10300v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文刻画了对称线性二次博弈中中介沟通何时能提供信息，发现取决于类型偏好与对手行动的交互结构，并在Bertrand寡头示例中说明私人成本下无法沟通而私人需求下可能沟通。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究参与者何时能够通过中介真实地共享私人信息。我们的分析聚焦于具有策略互补性和独立私人价值的对称线性二次博弈。我们刻画了中介沟通何时具有信息性，即能够引致与无沟通情形下唯一贝叶斯-纳什结果不同的结果。一个鲜明的二分法浮现出来。当偏好更高自身行动的参与者从对手更高行动中获益较少时，无论类型分布如何，信息性沟通都不可能实现。相反，当这些类型从对手更高行动中获益更多时，信息性沟通可能实现，当且仅当相关极端类型距离均值足够远。在线性需求伯特兰寡头垄断中，私人成本下信息性沟通不可能实现，但在私人需求下可能出现。最后，信息性沟通弱性地有利于每一种类型。但每当信息共享能够提高总福利时，沟通下的最大总收益严格低于在没有说真话约束下可实现的最大收益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study when players can truthfully share private information through a mediator. Our analysis focuses on symmetric linear-quadratic games with strategic complements and independent private values. We characterize when mediated communication is informative, meaning that it can induce an outcome different from the unique Bayes-Nash outcome without communication. A sharp dichotomy emerges. When types that prefer higher own actions benefit less from higher opponent actions, informative communication is impossible regardless of the type distribution. By contrast, when those types benefit more from higher opponent actions, informative communication is possible if and only if the relevant extreme type lies sufficiently far from the mean. In a linear-demand Bertrand oligopoly, informative communication is impossible under private costs but may arise under private demand. Finally, informative communication weakly benefits every type. But whenever information sharing can raise aggregate welfare, the maximum aggregate payoff under communication is strictly below the maximum payoff achievable without truth-telling constraints.

</details>
