# Creators Have Difficulty Abandoning Ideas They Generated

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05115v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文通过激励相容实验发现，人们在选择自己生成的创意时比选择他人创意时更不挑剔，表现出'创意禀赋效应'，且该效应难以通过信息干预消除，但时间间隔可提高选择性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

创造力研究者通常将创造过程分为两个阶段：生成与选择。尽管关于想法生成的心理学已有大量研究（例如，哪些因素能带来更多新颖且有用的想法），但人们对选择过程的本质，以及生成与选择如何相互作用，了解仍相对有限。本文探讨了想法的生成行为可能如何扭曲后续的选择过程。通过一种激励相容的实验范式，让成对的参与者审阅相同的想法，并仅因提交高质量想法而获得奖励，我们发现，当人们在从自己的想法中进行选择时，会比从他人的想法中选择时提交更多低质量的想法（即“创造禀赋效应”）。这一效应在两个领域的三个任务中均得到验证，并且对信息干预（即明确告知参与者该效应的存在）具有抵抗力。然而，让参与者在数月后重新审视自己的想法，会提高其选择性。本文进一步讨论了对个人与组织的广泛启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Creativity researchers often distinguish between two stages of the creative process: generation versus selection. While much is known about the psychology of idea generation (e.g., the factors that lead to a greater number of novel and useful ideas), less is understood about the nature of selection, or how generation and selection interact. Here we investigate how the act of generating ideas may potentially distort the selection process. Using an incentive-compatible paradigm in which pairs of participants reviewed the same ideas and were rewarded for submitting only high-quality ideas, we find that people submit a greater number of lower-quality ideas when selecting among their own ideas than when selecting among another person's ideas (the Creative Endowment Effect). This effect generalizes across three tasks in two domains and is resistant to an informational intervention (i.e., explicitly telling people about the effect). However, having participants revisit their ideas several months later increases their selectivity. The broader implications for individuals and organizations are discussed.
