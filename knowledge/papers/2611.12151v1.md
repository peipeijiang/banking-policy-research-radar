<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究在对称联合约束下，当代理人对自身份额具有连续、严格单峰偏好时，固定总量的分配问题。每个代理人报告一个完整的偏好，包括对其峰值两侧的比较。对于任意有限人口规模和任意非空闭凸置换不变可行集，我们通过效率、对称性、个体策略防操纵性和自身坐标仅峰值性来刻画均匀收益规则。最后一个条件要求，若偏好发生变化但代理人的峰值保持不变，则其自身份额也保持不变；该条件对他人的份额不施加直接限制。该刻画将唯一性与连续性分离，并产生了一个连续刻画，在紧可行集为多面体或相对满维且严格凸的情况下具有个体激励。我们进一步证明，对于从两个到六个代理人的每种人口规模以及每个非空紧凸置换不变可行集，仅效率、对称性和个体策略防操纵性即可刻画均匀收益。证明从单边报告、可行转移和端点报告所能达到的份额中恢复分配。一个共同的归纳法适用于每个人口规模，并针对五个和六个代理人增加了额外的起始配置。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the division of a fixed total under symmetric joint constraints when agents have continuous, strictly single-peaked preferences over their own shares. Each agent reports a complete preference, including comparisons across the two sides of her peak. For any finite population and any nonempty closed convex permutation-invariant feasible set, we characterize the uniform-gains rule by efficiency, symmetry, individual strategy-proofness, and own-coordinate peak-onlyness. The last condition requires that a change of preference leaving an agent's peak fixed also leave her own share fixed; it places no direct restriction on others' shares. The characterization separates uniqueness from continuity and yields a continuous characterization with individual incentives for compact feasible sets that are polyhedral or relatively full-dimensional and strictly convex. We then show that, for every population of two through six agents and every nonempty compact convex permutation-invariant feasible set, efficiency, symmetry, and individual strategy-proofness alone characterize uniform gains. The proof recovers allocations from the shares attainable by unilateral reports, feasible transfers, and endpoint reports. A common induction applies at each population, with additional starting configurations for five and six agents.
