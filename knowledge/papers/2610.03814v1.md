<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

对于某个整数 $\ell\geq 2$，考虑 $m=2\ell-1$ 个投票者，每个投票者对 $n$ 个备选项有一个偏好排序。Smith 集（Smith set）是最小的非空备选项集合，其中每个备选项在成对多数比较中都击败该集合外的每一个备选项。在标准的无偏文化（impartial culture）基准下，即每个投票者对所有备选项均匀且独立地选择一个随机偏好排序，我们研究当 $\ell$ 固定且 $n\to \infty$ 时，Smith 集基数为 $s$ 的渐近概率。

首先，我们证明 Smith 集具有常数基数 $s$ 的概率以 $Θ_{\ell,s} (n^{-s(\ell-1)/\ell})$ 的速率趋于零。其次，如果 $\min\{s,n-s\}\to\infty$，我们证明该概率以超多项式速率衰减。进一步，当 $s$ 和 $n-s$ 均为 $Θ(n)$ 时，我们证明该概率以指数速率衰减，并确定精确的指数底数。最后，我们证明 Smith 集包含所有备选项的概率以 $Θ_\ell(n^{-(\ell-1)/\ell})$ 的速率趋近于 1。这些定理在无偏文化下解决了 [Liu et al, Ann Stat 2026] 中的猜想。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

For some integer $\ell\geq 2$, consider $m=2\ell-1$ voters, each of which has a preference ranking over $n$ alternatives. The Smith set is the smallest nonempty set of alternatives each of which defeats every alternative outside the set in a pairwise majority comparison. Under the standard benchmark impartial culture where each voter uniformly and independently chooses a random preference ranking over all alternatives, we study the asymptotic probability that the Smith set has cardinality $s$ for fixed $\ell$ and $n\to \infty$.
  First, we prove that the probability that the Smith set has constant cardinality $s$ goes to zero at a rate of $Θ_{\ell,s} (n^{-s(\ell-1)/\ell})$. Next, if $\min\{s,n-s\}\to\infty$, we show that this probability decays superpolynomially. Further, when $s$ and $n-s$ are both $Θ(n)$, we prove that the probability decays exponentially and determine the exact exponential base. Finally, we prove that the probability that the Smith set contains all alternatives approaches one at a rate of $Θ_\ell(n^{-(\ell-1)/\ell})$. These theorems resolve the conjectures in [Liu et al, Ann Stat 2026] under impartial culture.

</details>
