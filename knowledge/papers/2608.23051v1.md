# Female Nomination and Party Vote Share in US Gubernatorial Elections

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23051v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文利用1980-2024年美国州长选举的县级面板数据，通过堆叠双重差分法估计女性提名对政党得票率的因果效应，发现自2016年以来共和党提名女性导致得票率下降6.5个百分点，而民主党无显著差异。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当一个政党提名女性竞选行政职位时，其得票率会发生什么变化？关于女性候选人的证据主要来自立法机构选举，且研究设计通常将不同政党和时期合并为一个平均效应，该效应通常为零。本文构建了一个新的美国州长选举县级面板数据——涵盖1980年至2024年间49个州的578场选举，数据基于原始资料重建并经审计——并采用堆叠双重差分设计，围绕州内候选人从男性向女性转变的案例，估计了政党特定且随时间变化的提名效应。平均零效应掩盖了显著的不对称性。自2016年以来，共和党提名女性候选人后，县级得票率下降6.5个百分点，该结果在队列、加权和推断检验中均保持稳健。对于民主党提名人，未检测到可比较的平均差异。选票调查诊断结果与需求侧歧视相符，但无法识别该机制渠道。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

What happens to a party's vote share when it nominates a woman for executive office? The evidence on female candidates is dominated by legislative races and by designs that pool parties and periods into a single average effect, which is typically null. This paper builds a new county-level panel of US gubernatorial elections - 578 races in 49 states from 1980 to 2024, reconstructed and audited from primary sources - and estimates party-specific, time-varying nomination effects with a stacked difference-in-differences design around within-state transitions from male to female nominees. The average null conceals a sharp asymmetry. Republican transitions to a female nominee since 2016 are followed by a county-level vote-share decline of 6.5 percentage points, robust across cohort, weighting and inference checks. No comparable average differential is detected for Democratic nominees. Survey-ballot diagnostics are compatible with demand-side discrimination but cannot identify that channel.
