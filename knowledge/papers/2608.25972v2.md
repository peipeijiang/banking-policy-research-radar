<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

双重股权结构将控制权分配给创始人，其企业特定投资驱动企业价值，但控制权与所有权分离，从而增加了代理成本。我们动态地分析了这一权衡。利用覆盖52年的美国双重股权公司新数据以及双重差分设计，我们表明，双重股权资本重组后估值上升，但随时间推移而下降，而创新产出则持续增加。这些效应集中于企业特定投资较多的行业。我们在股票统一方面也发现了相应的结果。成熟的双重股权公司的投资对机会的敏感度较低，且投票溢价随成熟度而增加。我们的结果支持动态处理效应，并产生了新的政策启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Dual-class shares allocate control to founders whose firm-specific investments drive firm value but separate control from ownership, raising agency costs. We analyze this trade-off dynamically. Using new data on US dual-class firms spanning 52 years and difference-in-differences designs, we show that valuations rise following dual-class recapitalizations but decline over time, whereas innovative output increases persistently. These effects are concentrated in industries with greater firm-specific investments. We find corresponding results for stock unifications. Investment by mature dual-class firms is less sensitive to opportunities and voting premia increase with maturity. Our results support dynamic treatment effects and yield new policy implications.
