<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出一种非参数工具变量模型中均值外生性的检验。在完备性条件下，原假设等价于一个涉及普通回归函数的条件矩限制，因此该检验避免了结构函数的估计及其不适定逆问题。我们构造一个残差标记的经验过程，并通过一个二阶辅助U过程对其进行分析。我们推导了其在原假设下以及$n^{-1/2}$阶Pitman型局部备择下的极限分布，并建立了对固定全局备择的一致性。分析揭示了一种非参数估计效应，该效应使得直接乘子自助法失效。我们提出一种计算简单的乘子自助法，并配以量身定制的指示函数修正以考虑该效应，同时建立了其渐近有效性。模拟表明，在所考虑的非参数设计中，该检验具有良好的水平控制，并且相对于现有方法具有功效增益。对工资方程的一个应用提供了反对学校教育均值外生性的证据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a test of mean exogeneity in nonparametric instrumental variable models. Under completeness, the null is equivalent to a conditional moment restriction involving the ordinary regression function, so the test avoids estimation of the structural function and its ill-posed inverse problem. We construct a residual-marked empirical process and analyze it through an auxiliary U-process of order two. We derive its limiting distributions under the null and Pitman-type local alternatives of order $n^{-1/2}$, and establish consistency against fixed global alternatives. The analysis reveals a nonparametric estimation effect that invalidates a direct multiplier bootstrap. We propose a computationally simple multiplier bootstrap with a tailor-made indicator correction to account for this effect, and establish its asymptotic validity. Simulations show good size control and power gains over existing methods in the nonparametric designs considered. An application to a wage equation provides evidence against the mean exogeneity of schooling.
