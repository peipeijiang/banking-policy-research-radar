<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

《中国为何成功》一书提供了现有著作所不具备的独特视角：一个统一且具有解释力的框架，将中国的崛起从其古老的文明根基追溯至二十一世纪的技术腾飞。该书并未将中国的成功视为意识形态上的例外或历史偶然，而是揭示了贯穿中国数千年治理并推动其现代经济奇迹的普遍制度逻辑——即规范化的社会流动机制、功能性的土地与税收制度，以及强大的国家能力。本书直接挑战了西方发展正统观念，证明制度可以通过增长来构建，而非必须先于增长而存在。对于全球范围内寻求从这一最具深远意义的发展故事中汲取可迁移经验的政策制定者、学者和发展实践者而言，本书提供了目前最具历史深度、分析严谨性和前瞻性的论述。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Why China Succeeds offers what no existing book does: a single, unified explanatory framework that traces China's rise from its ancient civilizational foundations to its twenty first century technological ascent. Rather than treating China's success as ideologically exceptional or historically accidental, it reveals the universal institutional logic (regulated social mobility, functional land and taxation systems, and formidable state capability) that has sustained Chinese governance across millennia and powered its modern economic miracle. The book directly challenges Western development orthodoxy by demonstrating that institutions can be built through growth, not before it. For policymakers, scholars, and development practitioners worldwide seeking transferable lessons from the world's most consequential development story, this book provides the most historically grounded, analytically rigorous, and forward-looking account available.
