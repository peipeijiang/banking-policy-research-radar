<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我研究具有可转移效用的随机提议者联盟博弈中平稳期望收益的唯一性。提议者与联盟盈余的独立性并不能保证唯一性，即使盈余严格单调且每个均衡都具有即时协议的特征。对于$M^\natural$-凹特征函数（包括非单调的情形），我证明：只要每个均衡都具有即时协议的特征，平稳期望收益就是唯一的，并且关于基本参数连续。为证明唯一性，我识别出最大化联盟的一种公平分组性质，并证明$M^\natural$-凹性在每个无延迟候选收益向量处蕴含该性质。利用公平分组，我证明每个无延迟均衡收益向量都是孤立的且具有正局部指数。对于保持每个均衡中即时协议的参数变化，某一参与者对包含她的每个联盟的贡献的均匀增加会严格提高她的期望收益，而其贴现因子的增加则会弱提高她的期望收益。我将这些结果应用于具有协调成本的合资企业以及具有部长专业能力的内阁组建。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I study uniqueness of stationary expected payoffs in random-proposer coalitional bargaining with transferable utility. Proposer independence of coalition surplus does not guarantee uniqueness, even when the surplus is strictly monotone and every equilibrium features immediate agreement. For $M^\natural$-concave characteristic functions, including non-monotone ones, I show that stationary expected payoffs are unique and continuous in the primitives, provided that every equilibrium features immediate agreement. To prove uniqueness, I identify an equitable grouping property of maximizing coalitions and show that $M^\natural$-concavity implies it at every no-delay candidate payoff vector. Using equitable grouping, I show that every no-delay equilibrium payoff vector is isolated and has positive local index. For parameter changes that preserve immediate agreement in every equilibrium, a uniform increase in a player's contribution to every coalition containing her strictly increases her expected payoff, while an increase in her discount factor weakly increases it. I apply these results to joint ventures with coordination costs and government formation with ministerial expertise.
