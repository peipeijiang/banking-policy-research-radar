# Moral Consumers and the Environment

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09515v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文构建消费者具有康德式道德偏好的模型，分析环境有害商品的最优税收，发现最优税取决于消费者是否意识到税收返还，并在同质与异质道德下给出Pigovian税最优的条件。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当消费者具有“做正确之事”的康德式愿望时，对有害环境商品的征税问题。消费者评估其购买策略——一种规定是否购买的规则——的依据是：如果其他人以由她的道德程度所给定的概率遵循同一规则，她将获得的物质收益。在道德异质的有限人口中，均衡存在且唯一。最优税率关键取决于消费者是否意识到税收收入会返还给他们。如果消费者意识到这一点，那么在道德同质的情况下，庇古税是最优的，无论消费者对环境成本的了解如何；我们还给出了在道德异质情况下庇古税仍为唯一最优的条件。如果消费者没有意识到这一点，最优税率较低，且消费者的道德程度越高，税率越低。在诸如欧盟排放交易体系这样的配额制度下，任何至少与庇古配额同样严格的排放上限都允许存在一个均衡，在该均衡中，道德对了解该制度的消费者没有影响。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the taxation of environmentally harmful goods when consumers have a Kantian desire to ``do the right thing''. A consumer evaluates her buying strategy, a rule that specifies whether to buy, by the material payoff she would obtain if others followed the same rule with a probability given by her degree of morality. Equilibrium exists and is unique in finite populations with heterogeneous morality. The optimal tax depends crucially on whether consumers are aware that tax revenue is returned to them. If they are, the Pigovian tax is optimal under homogeneous morality, whatever consumers know about environmental costs, and we give conditions under which it remains the unique optimum under heterogeneous morality. If they are not, the optimal tax is lower, and more so the more moral consumers are. Under a quota system such as the EU Emissions Trading System, any cap at least as stringent as the Pigovian quota admits an equilibrium in which morality has no effect on consumers who are aware of the system.
