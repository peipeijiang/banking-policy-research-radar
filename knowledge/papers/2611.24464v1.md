推荐规则既影响卖家的激励，也影响消费者预期可找到的替代选择。我们构建了一个框架来评估这些规则，考虑异质性卖家、内生定价与广告以及 costly consumer search（ costly consumer search）。对于一大类可分离推荐评分，我们建立了唯一市场均衡存在的条件。三个应用说明了该框架的用途。当产品在质量上充分差异化时，对低价格的更强奖励未必会降低均衡价格并使消费者受益。质量引导可以降低每种产品的价格并使消费者受益，同时提高平均交易价格。在匹配分布异质的情况下，在广告固定时，对最佳初始检查的奖励可以帮助消费者，而当广告调整时会伤害消费者。一个两类型设计扩展表明，在可实施结果上不存在有限最优解会同时对质量和低价格赋予正权重，即使平台重视消费者剩余。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Recommendation rules affect both sellers' incentives and the alternatives consumers expect to find. We develop a framework for evaluating these rules with heterogeneous sellers, endogenous pricing and advertising, and costly consumer search. For a broad class of separable recommendation scores, we establish conditions under which a unique market equilibrium exists. Three applications illustrate the use of the framework. A stronger reward for low prices does not necessarily lower equilibrium prices and benefit consumers when products are sufficiently differentiated in qualities. Quality guidance can lower the price of each product and benefit consumers while raising the average transaction price. With heterogeneous match distributions, a reward for the best initial inspection can help consumers with advertising fixed and harm them when advertising adjusts. A two-type design extension shows that no finite optimum over implementable outcomes uses positive weights on both quality and low prices, even when the platform values consumer surplus.
