<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考虑层级因果系统中的反身性，在此类系统中，高层状态约束了仍可容许的低层动力学[Wilcox and Gebbie (2014)，Gebbie (2026)]。我们探讨当局部活动由事件驱动而因果主张以日历时间表述时，这种依赖于状态的自上而下约束是如何实现的。如果由行动者条件化的约束同时支配着可容许的事件转移及其时序，那么事件过程便通过依赖于状态的时间变换被带入日历时间。若可容许约束或兼容的时序法则并非唯一，则即便局部事件动力学允许马尔可夫表示，所导出的日历时间因果法则也未必唯一。层级因果性明确保留了跨层约束与事件协调，而日历时间因果法则必须由此构建。在此层级表示中，反身性是通过依赖于状态的自上而下约束以及事件到日历时间的映射，对因果循环所进行的内生闭合。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We consider reflexivity in hierarchical causal systems in which higher-level states constrain the lower-level dynamics that remain admissible [Wilcox and Gebbie (2014),Gebbie (2026)]. We ask how such state-dependent top-down constraints are realised when local activity is event driven while causal claims are made in calendar time. If an actor-conditioned constraint governs both the admissible event transitions and their timing, the event process is carried into calendar time by a state-dependent time change. If either the admissible constraint or the compatible timing law is not unique, the induced calendar-time causal law need not be unique even when the local event dynamics admit a Markov representation. Hierarchical causality keeps explicit the cross-level constraint and event coordination from which such a calendar-time law must be constructed. Within this hierarchical representation, reflexivity is the endogenous closure of the causal loop through state-dependent top-down constraint and an event-to-calendar time map.
