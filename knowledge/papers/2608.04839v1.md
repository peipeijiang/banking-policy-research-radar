<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在饱和固定效应回归中，高斯推断并不依赖于总识别变异，而是依赖于其集中度，该集中度通过残差化处理的自我标准化杠杆值 $λ_n$ 来度量。当有限数量的得分权重持续存在时，$t$ 统计量收敛于原始误差与高斯分量的卷积。在完全集中度下，其零分布随具有等方差的不同对称误差律而变化，因此不存在固定临界值具有一致有效性。我们转而仅从设计矩阵构造消除干扰项的对比量。这些对比量以相同方式消除固定效应，并在对称、任意异方差误差下产生有限样本精确符号翻转推断，无需同方差假设或对固定效应维度的限制。在双向设计中，可容许对比量构成观测多重图的循环空间。其效率由可观测的捕获比率 $κ$ 概括，该比率等于皮特曼效率。由此产生的设计问题涉及捕获—粒度权衡：粗糙支撑集最大化捕获量，但减少随机化符号的数量。循环打包提供了足够粒度的支撑集。在匹配的雇主—雇员数据上，一种利用结构的算法实现了 $κ\approx 0.51$，而朴素打包仅为 $0.26$。在Grunfeld投资回归中，实现的得分集中度为 $0.739$，对应 $N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{score}}=1.80$，而 $32$ 个有效支撑集达到 $κ=0.627$。由此得到的精确 $95\%$ 置信区间为 $[0.150,0.450]$。一个工人—企业应用展示了该方法对大型网络的可扩展性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In saturated fixed-effects regressions, Gaussian inference depends not on total identifying variation but on its concentration, measured by the self-normalized leverage $λ_n$ of the residualized treatment. When finitely many score weights remain persistent, the $t$-statistic converges to a convolution of raw errors and a Gaussian component. At full concentration, its null distribution varies across symmetric error laws with equal variance, so no fixed critical value is uniformly valid. We instead construct nuisance-annihilating contrasts from the design alone. These eliminate the fixed effects identically and yield finite-sample exact sign-flip inference under symmetric, arbitrarily heteroskedastic errors, with no homogeneity assumptions or restrictions on the fixed-effect dimension. In two-way designs, admissible contrasts form the cycle space of the observation multigraph. Their efficiency is summarized by an observable capture ratio $κ$, which equals Pitman efficiency. The resulting design problem involves a capture--granularity trade-off: coarse supports maximize capture but reduce the number of randomization signs. Cycle packing provides sufficiently granular supports. On matched employer--employee data, a structure-exploiting algorithm achieves $κ\approx 0.51$, compared with $0.26$ for naive packing. In the Grunfeld investment regression, realized score concentration is $0.739$, corresponding to $N_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{score}}=1.80$, while $32$ valid supports attain $κ=0.627$. The resulting exact $95%$ confidence interval is $[0.150,0.450]$. A worker--firm application demonstrates scalability to large networks.
