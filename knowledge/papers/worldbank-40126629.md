<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

尼日利亚青春期女性的教育获得水平仍然偏低，尤其是在北部各州，早婚和有限的学校教育限制了女性的经济机会。本研究评估在尼日利亚北部高中校园安全空间内开展的校本生活技能培训——单独实施或与数字素养培训相结合——能否改善青春期女性的教育、健康和经济成果。干预实施12个月后，受干预学校的女生在与课程相关的知识方面表现出适度提升，但在学业保留率或社会情感技能方面未见效果。在中期已全面实施数字素养培训的州，增加该培训改善了数字技能，凸显了实施保真度的重要作用。这些发现既揭示了综合性校本干预的前景，也揭示了其局限性，并强调了在资源匮乏环境中推广技术类项目时交付能力的重要性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Educational attainment among adolescent girls in Nigeria remains low, particularly in the northern states where early marriage and limited schooling constrain women's economic opportunities. This study evaluates whether school-based life skills training delivered in safe spaces within senior secondary schools, alone or combined with digital literacy training, can improve educational, health, and economic outcomes for adolescent girls in northern Nigeria. Twelve months post-intervention, girls in treated schools show modest improvements in curriculum-related knowledge, with no effects on school retention or socio-emotional skills. Adding digital literacy training improved digital skills in the state where it had been fully implemented by midline, underscoring the role of implementation fidelity. These findings highlight both the promise and limitations of integrated school-based interventions and underscore the importance of delivery capacity for scaling technology-based programs in low-resource settings.
