<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

尽管关于最低工资的文献大多是回顾性的，分析过去政策变化的影响，但本文具有独特的前瞻性，预测了乌克兰一项拟议改革将如何影响未来的就业、收入和社保缴费。该分析通过模拟一项提案来预测这些效应，该提案提议在戒严结束后将最低工资标准设定为前一年平均工资的40%，并每年将最低工资提高两个百分点，直至达到前一年平均工资的50%。分析基于乌克兰养老基金2023年工人层面数据的10%随机样本，涵盖私营部门和非军事公共部门中所有缴纳养老金的职位。结果表明，在该提案下，到五年过渡期结束时，最低工资将比仅按工资通胀调整（作为反事实情景）所达到的水平高出45%。预计就业损失将较为温和（0.51%–1.03%），而总收益将增长至多3.24%。对社保缴费和税收的影响与对收益的影响相同，因为在乌克兰，这些费用是收益的固定百分比。本文所开发的方法应用于覆盖所有正规部门工人的行政数据集，为政策制定者预判正在考虑的最低工资改革可能产生的影响提供了有用工具。本文最后讨论了政策启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

While the literature on the minimum wage is largely retrospective, analyzing impacts of past policy changes, this paper is uniquely forward-looking, forecasting how a proposed reform in Ukraine would affect future employment, earnings, and social contributions. The analysis simulates these effects by mimicking a proposal that would set a minimum wage floor equal to 40 percent of the previous year’s average wage once martial law is over and increasing the minimum wage by two percentage points annually until reaching 50 percent of the previous year’s average wage. The analysis draws on a 10 percent random sample of 2023 worker-level data from the Pension Fund of Ukraine, covering all jobs with pension contributions in the private sector and nonmilitary public sector. The results indicate that, under the proposal, by the end of the five-year transition period, the minimum wage would be 45 percent above the level it would have reached if adjusted only for wage inflation, which serves as the counterfactual. Projected job losses would be modest (0.51–1.03 percent), while total earnings would increase by up to 3.24 percent. The effect on social contributions and taxes would be the same as the effect on earnings since they are a fixed percentage of earnings in Ukraine. Applied to an administrative data set covering all formal sector workers, the methodology developed in the paper offers a useful tool for policy makers to anticipate the likely effects of the minimum wage reforms that are being considered. The paper concludes by discussing policy implications.
