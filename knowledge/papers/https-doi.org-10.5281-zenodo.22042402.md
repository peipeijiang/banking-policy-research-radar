战略能力对于在动态且高度竞争环境中运营的商业银行的绩效和竞争力日益重要。本研究考察了肯尼亚商业银行战略能力与绩效之间的关系，重点关注网络能力、服务质量能力、人力资本能力和市场感知能力。本研究采用桌面研究方法，对相关理论和实证文献进行系统回顾与综合。理论回顾以资源基础观和动态能力理论为基础，并辅以平衡计分卡和SERVQUAL模型。研究回顾了来自肯尼亚及其他地理和组织背景的实证研究，以确定所识别的战略能力与组织绩效之间的关系。综述表明，战略能力为商业银行提供了重要的组织资源和能力，以应对不断变化的客户需求、加强服务交付、获取外部资源、发展员工专业知识以及识别新兴市场机会。所回顾的实证证据普遍表明，四种战略能力与组织绩效之间存在正相关关系，尽管肯尼亚商业银行业在情境和概念层面仍存在空白。研究得出结论，商业银行可以通过发展和有效整合战略网络、服务质量、人力资本和市场感知能力来提升其绩效。本研究通过整合肯尼亚商业银行业背景下这些能力的证据，为战略管理文献做出贡献，并为未来关于能力发展和持续组织绩效的实证研究及管理决策提供基础。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Strategic capabilities are increasingly important to the performance and competitiveness of commercial banks operating in dynamic and highly competitive environments. This study examined the relationship between strategic capabilities and the performance of commercial banks in Kenya, focusing on network capability, service quality capability, human capital capability, and market sensing capability. The study adopted a desktop research methodology, involving a systematic review and synthesis of relevant theoretical and empirical literature. The theoretical review was anchored on the Resource-Based View and Dynamic Capabilities Theory, complemented by the Balanced Scorecard and SERVQUAL models. Empirical studies from Kenya and other geographical and organisational contexts were reviewed to establish the relationship between the identified strategic capabilities and organisational performance. The review indicates that strategic capabilities provide commercial banks with important organisational resources and competencies for responding to changing customer needs, strengthening service delivery, accessing external resources, developing employee expertise and identifying emerging market opportunities. The reviewed empirical evidence generally indicates positive associations between the four strategic capabilities and organisational performance, although contextual and conceptual gaps remain within the Kenyan commercial banking sector. The study concludes that commercial banks can enhance their performance by developing and effectively integrating strategic networking, service quality, human capital and market sensing capabilities. The study contributes to strategic management literature by consolidating evidence on these capabilities within the Kenyan commercial banking context and provides a basis for future empirical research and managerial decision-making on capability development and sustained organisational performance.
