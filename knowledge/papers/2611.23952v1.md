<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究如何利用月度已实现股票波动率的共性来进行高维协方差预测和投资组合选择。我们将潜在因子模型与在预先指定的股票组内汇集动态参数的ARMA模型进行比较。我们证明，汇集ARMA模型具有精确的动态因子表示，其中包含组特定动态和股票特定载荷。隐含的静态因子表示涉及组特定因子，而这些因子不必是普遍存在的。将波动率预测与已实现动态条件相关性和非线性收缩相结合，我们评估了标普500成分股的样本外最小方差投资组合。在投资组合约束和模型设定下，基于汇集的方法始终优于替代方案。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how commonalities in monthly realized stock volatilities can be exploited for high-dimensional covariance forecasting and portfolio selection. We compare latent factor models with ARMA models that pool dynamic parameters within pre-specified groups of stocks. We show that pooled ARMA models admit an exact dynamic factor representation with group-specific dynamics and stock-specific loadings. The implied static factor representation involves group-specific factors that need not be pervasive. Combining volatility forecasts with realized dynamic conditional correlations and nonlinear shrinkage, we evaluate out-of-sample minimum variance portfolios of S&P 500 constituents. Pooling-based approaches consistently outperform the alternatives across portfolio constraints and model specifications.
