<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提供了关于国际硕士教育与创业之间关系的首个全球性实证证据。利用来自LinkedIn/Revelio的大规模纵向数据，我们记录到，在全球主要国际学生目的地，国际硕士毕业生系统性地比国内毕业生更倾向于创办企业。我们进一步展示了这种创业优势如何因研究领域、性别、来源国和目的地国而异。此外，我们记录了毕业后企业创建的地理分布，突出了前国际学生中创业活动的国际流动性。综合来看，这些结果为国际学生流动的经济影响以及国际硕士毕业生对全球创业、创新和商业活力的贡献提供了新的全球视角。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We provide the first global empirical evidence on the relationship between international master’s education and entrepreneurship. Using large-scale longitudinal data from LinkedIn/Revelio, we document that international master’s graduates are systematically more likely than domestic graduates to create firms across the world’s leading destinations for international students. We further show how this entrepreneurial advantage varies across fields of study, gender, origin and destination countries. In addition, we document the geography of post-graduation firm creation, highlighting the international mobility of entrepreneurial activity among former international students. Taken together, the results offer a new global perspective on the economic impact of international student mobility and the contribution of international master’s graduates to entrepreneurship, innovation, and business dynamism worldwide.
