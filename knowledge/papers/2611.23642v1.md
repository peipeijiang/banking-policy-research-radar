<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出一种基于大组内间隔的重复测量模型中非参数识别的尾部几何方法。差分消除了共同的潜在成分，而在大间隔条件下，特质误差被推向相反的尾部。在正则的薄于高斯尾部条件下，特质成分集中于一个确定性的、依赖于间隔的位置附近。因此，相应的观测测量在重新中心化后，揭示了共同潜在成分在位置上的分布。随后，特质分布可通过去卷积恢复。该原理既适用于经典的两测量Kotlarski模型，也适用于仅观测到次序统计量的情形。应用包括拍卖和工资报价模型，无论所有报价均被观测到还是仅可获得排名最高的报价。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a tail-geometric approach to nonparametric identification in repeated-measurement models based on large within-unit gaps. Differencing eliminates the common latent component, while conditioning on a large gap pushes the idiosyncratic errors into opposite tails. Under a regular thinner-than-Gaussian tail condition, an idiosyncratic component concentrates around a deterministic, gap-dependent location. Consequently, the corresponding observed measurement, after recentering, reveals the distribution of the common latent component up to location. The idiosyncratic distribution can then be recovered through deconvolution. This principle applies both to the classical two-measurement Kotlarski model and to settings where only order statistics are observed. Applications include auctions and wage-offer models, whether all offers are observed or only the top-ranked ones are available.

</details>
