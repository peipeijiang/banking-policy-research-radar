<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当稿件的生产成本变得低廉，而专家评审依然稀缺时，投稿权便具有了价值。一种不可转让的作者配额将投稿转变为一种显示优先级的决策：作者仅使用序数信息提名其最优项目，且在排名具有信息量的情况下，增加候选项目不会使被选中的项目组合变差。其代价是在作者之间以及已实现项目之间分配评审能力时缺乏灵活性。在一个易于处理的基准模型中，我推导出了精确的等负荷条件，在该条件下，配额对异质性费用敏感性和基数校准误差的稳健性超过了上述代价。在项目价值有界的情况下，充裕可以使等负荷费用转变为一种准入筛选机制，而配额则保留了作者内部的筛选功能。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When manuscripts become cheap to produce but expert evaluation stays scarce, submission rights acquire value. A non-transferable author quota turns submission into a revealed-priority decision: authors nominate their top projects using only ordinal information, and, with informative rankings, adding candidates cannot worsen the selected portfolio. The cost is inflexibility in allocating review capacity across authors and realised projects. In a tractable benchmark, I derive the exact equal-load condition under which the quota's robustness to heterogeneous fee sensitivity and cardinal miscalibration outweighs that cost. With bounded project values, abundance can turn an equal-load fee into an access screen, while the quota preserves within-author curation.

</details>
