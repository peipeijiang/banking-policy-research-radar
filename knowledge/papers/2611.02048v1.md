# Minimax-Optimal Static Estimators Under Interference

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02048v1)

## 一句话结论

> 在存在干扰的随机实验中，采用极小极大框架推导对干扰结构稳健的静态处理效应估计量，发现在大干扰下最优估计量为零估计量或半零估计量。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了在干扰下随机实验中估计全局处理效应的问题，其中某个单元的结果可能依赖于其他单元的处理分配。我们采用极小极大框架，允许对手从满足有界结果和有界干扰的函数类中选择结果函数。我们推导出对广泛干扰结构具有鲁棒性的极小极大最优静态估计器，这些干扰结构由标量$\delta$参数化，该标量控制一个单元的处理对另一个单元结果的最大影响。在大干扰下，我们观察到两种情形。当处理组和对照组大小相等时，极小极大最优静态估计器为零估计器。当两组中有一组较大时，极小极大最优静态估计器为“半零”估计器，其中较小一组的结果被忽略，估计器仅考虑较大一组的结果。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the problem of estimating the global treatment effect in randomized experiments under interference, where a unit's outcome may depend on the treatment assignments of other units. We adopt a minimax framework, allowing an adversary to select an outcome function from a class of functions that satisfy bounded outcomes and bounded interference. We derive minimax-optimal static estimators that are robust to a wide range of interference structures, parameterized by a scalar $δ$ controlling the maximum influence of one unit's treatment on another's outcome. Under large interference, we observe two regimes. When the treatment and control groups are equally sized, the minimax-optimal static estimator is the zero estimator. When one of the two groups is larger, the minimax-optimal static estimator is a "half-zero" estimator, in which the outcomes of the smaller group are ignored, and the estimator only considers the outcomes of the larger group.

</details>
