# The Transaction-Driven Performance of SSE Composite Index from year 2022 to 2024

[查看原文](https://doi.org/10.70114/ahmer.2026.5.1.p43)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文利用2022-2024年数据，通过回归分析发现股市交易额对上证综指有正向影响，M1无显著影响，工业增加值有负向影响，表明后疫情时期股市由交易活动主导，货币政策传导减弱。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文探讨了2022年5月至2024年12月期间中国股票市场与若干主要经济指标之间的动态关联。研究结果表明，股票交易周转率对上证综合指数价格具有显著的正向影响，M1货币供应量对上证指数无显著影响，而工业增加值增速与上证指数呈负相关关系。研究发现，在后疫情时期的波动环境下，中国股票市场表现主要由市场交易活动主导，货币政策传导关联有所弱化，且与实体经济呈现暂时的反向关联。本研究可为政策制定者和投资者提供实证参考。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper explores the dynamic linkage between the China’s stock market and some major economic indicators from May 2022 to December 2024. The results show that stock trading turnover significantly and positively affects SSE Composite Index prices, M1 has no effect on SSE, while the industrial value-added growth is negatively correlated with SSE. The findings reveal that during the volatile post-COVID period, China’s stock market performance has been dominated by the market transaction activities, with a weakened monetary policy transmission linkage and a temporary reverse connection with the real economy. This study could provide empirical references for policy makers and investors.
