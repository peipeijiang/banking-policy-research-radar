<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关税威胁可以在政策正式实施之前就改变家庭信念，然而其快速变化的语言却难以通过传统调查进行研究。我们构建了一个多智能体系统，将密歇根消费者调查中的300户家庭转化为持久的大型语言模型智能体，并在模拟的几个月内使其接触社交媒体信息。经过校准的智能体再现了在“解放日”关税公告后收集的人类调查数据中的部分分布性和人口统计学模式。模拟实验表明，即时性、税率显著性、语义递进、信息复杂度、叙事方式以及发送者身份共同塑造了通胀预期、失业预期及其离散程度。开放式回答将这些影响追溯到注意力、模糊性、可信度及因果叙事。第二项实验发现，央行解释能够协调信念，尽管其对平均预期的影响取决于信息内容。该框架支持对政策沟通进行有纪律的探索，但需以人类验证为前提，而非替代人类验证。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Tariff threats can move household beliefs before policy is enacted, yet their rapidly changing language is difficult to study with conventional surveys. We build a multi-agent system that turns 300 households from the Michigan Surveys of Consumers into persistent large-language-model agents exposed to social-media information over several simulated months. Calibrated agents reproduce some distributional and demographic patterns in human survey data collected after the announcement of Liberation Day tariffs. Simulated experiments indicate that immediacy, rate salience, semantic progression, message complexity, narrative, and sender identity jointly shape inflation and unemployment expectations and their dispersion. Open-ended responses trace these effects to attention, ambiguity, credibility, and causal narratives. A second experiment finds that central-bank explanations can coordinate beliefs, although their effects on average expectations depend on message content. The framework supports disciplined exploration of policy communication, subject to human validation rather than as a substitute for it.
