局部家庭扰动何时会推动信贷价格？我构建了一个社会网络上的金融加权传播算子。在一个条件化的家庭—中介均衡中，其投影决定了超额债券溢价中的家庭需求成分。由于响应在不同网络模式和渠道下以不同速率衰减，一个地点的相对金融重要性通常会随期限而变化，因此没有任何单一的集中度指标能够概括跨期限的暴露。当家庭测量独立测得的需​​求敏感性张成该空间时，这些测量能够精确恢复该投影。在实证上，社会层面对受风暴破坏县的暴露与数周后在记录足迹之外的县的支出呈负相关。在模型扩散律下对观测网络进行的模拟中，该算子对留出风暴的传播信贷溢价成分的预测误差比直接社会暴露低约30%。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When can local household disturbances move credit prices? I develop a financially weighted propagation operator on the social network. In a conditional household-intermediary equilibrium, its projection determines the household-demand component of the excess bond premium. Because responses decay at different rates across network patterns and channels, a location's relative financial importance generically changes with the horizon, so no single centrality measure summarizes exposure across horizons. Household measurements recover this projection exactly when they span independently measured demand sensitivities. Empirically, social exposure to storm-damaged counties is negatively associated with spending weeks later in counties outside the recorded footprints. In simulations on the observed network under the model's diffusion law, the operator predicts the propagated credit-premium component of held-out storms with about 30 percent less error than direct social exposure.
