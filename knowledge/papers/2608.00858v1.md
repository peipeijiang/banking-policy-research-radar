<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们引入了区分提供流动性与需求流动性策略的高频交易（HFT）的数据驱动度量指标。我们在一个包含已观测HFT活动的专有数据集上训练机器学习模型，随后将这些模型应用于公开的日内数据，以生成2010—2023年间所有美国股票的HFT度量。我们的度量指标优于传统代理变量，后者难以捕捉HFT的时间动态特征。与理论一致，我们的度量指标对一项准外生的减速带引入和数据源升级做出了响应。这些度量有助于揭示HFT对信息获取的差异化影响。提供流动性的HFT改善了盈余公告期间的价格信息含量，而需求流动性的HFT则阻碍了这一过程。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce data-driven measures of high-frequency trading (HFT) that distinguish between liquidity-supplying and liquidity-demanding strategies. We train machine learning models on a proprietary dataset with observed HFT activity, then apply these models to public intraday data to generate HFT measures across all U.S. stocks during 2010-2023. Our measures outperform conventional proxies, which struggle to capture the temporal dynamics of HFT. Consistent with theory, our measures respond to a quasi-exogenous speed bump introduction and a data feed upgrade. The measures help uncover the differential impact of HFT on information acquisition. Liquidity-supplying HFT improves price informativeness around earnings announcements, while liquidity-demanding HFT impedes it.
