# Making Experiment Results Comparable through Sitewide Impact Estimation

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18411v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出用覆盖因子将A/B实验提升缩放为全站影响估计量，并针对均值差指标和比率指标给出两种估计方法，实证表明实验提升与全站影响排序相关性低（Spearman=0.20），需用全站影响进行跨场景决策。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Roblox 涵盖多个产品界面——发现、虚拟经济、创作者工具——这些界面在覆盖范围以及受影响人群对任何全站指标的贡献程度上差异显著。随机实验表明某项处理对其样本中的用户是否有效，但该实验提升值（experiment lift）在不同界面之间不可比较，因为各界面在覆盖范围和其对全站指标的贡献上存在差异。战略决策所需的是一个反事实量——即在测量期内，如果该处理触达其目标人群的100%（该目标人群可能仅为全站人群的一部分），某个聚合全站指标（例如平台总游玩时长）将变动多少。我们将其称为全站影响（sitewide impact）。其缩放因子——我们称之为覆盖因子（coverage factor）——综合了覆盖范围与目标人群在无处理条件下对全站指标的贡献。因此，一个覆盖范围较低但其用户承载了较大指标份额的界面，其排名可能高于一个覆盖范围较高但用户贡献较小的界面。在此框架下，我们开发了两种估计量：一种用于均值差指标，另一种用于变体级比率指标（定义为两个总体聚合量之比）。实证表明，实验提升值是全站影响的糟糕代理指标——在139个对游玩时长具有统计显著性的实验中，两者之间的Spearman秩相关系数为0.20，且实验提升值最大的10个实验中有5个位列全站影响最大的10个之中。我们通过A/A测试和长期留出验证对该估计量进行了验证，并将其应用于跨领域优先级排序以及将同比增长归因于已交付的创新。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Roblox spans many product surfaces-discovery, the virtual economy, creator tools-that differ sharply in reach and in how much the affected population contributes to any sitewide metric. A randomized experiment shows whether a treatment works for the users in its sample, but this experiment lift is not comparable across surfaces, because surfaces differ in reach and in their contribution to sitewide metrics. The quantity a strategic decision needs is a counterfactual-how much an aggregated sitewide metric (e.g., total platform play time) would move if the treatment reached 100% of its target population, which may be only part of the sitewide population, during the measurement period. We call this sitewide impact. Its scaling factor, which we call the coverage factor, combines the reach and the target population's contribution to the sitewide metric under no treatment. Hence a low-reach surface whose users carry much of the metric could outrank a high-reach surface whose users carry little. We develop two estimators under this framing: one for difference-in-means metrics and one for variant-level ratio metrics (a metric defined as a ratio of two population aggregates). Empirically, experiment lift is a poor proxy for sitewide impact-across 139 experiments that are statistically significant on time spent, the Spearman rank correlation between the two is 0.20, and 5 of the 10 largest experiment lifts are among the 10 largest sitewide impacts. We validate the estimator with A/A tests and against a long-term holdout, and apply it to cross-domain prioritization and attributing year-over-year growth to shipped innovations.
