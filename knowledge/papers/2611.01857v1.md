# Inference for conditional Fréchet means via Riemannian Robust Bias Correction

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一个内在框架，用于在预测变量和响应变量取值于有限维黎曼流形时对条件Fréchet均值进行推断。我们推导了平滑偏差的二阶展开，通过对数映射在切空间中表示，并表明该偏差会影响一阶推断。我们提出了黎曼稳健偏差校正（Riemannian robust bias correction, RRBC），该方法在响应切空间中利用局部二次回归估计主导平滑偏差，并通过指数映射对估计量进行校正。我们的留一法（leave-one-out, LOO）协方差估计器利用全样本与LOO RRBC估计之间的内在位移，考虑了估计偏差校正的变异性及其与局部线性估计量的协方差。我们建立了无需欠平滑的渐近有效逐点置信集和同时置信管。在球面和对称正定矩阵上的模拟表明，当平滑偏差不可忽略时，RRBC可以改善覆盖概率。心血管和洋流应用展示了同时假设检验以及对光滑泛函的推断。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop an intrinsic framework for inference on conditional Fréchet means with predictors and responses taking values in finite-dimensional Riemannian manifolds. We derive a second-order expansion of the smoothing bias, represented in the tangent space via the logarithm map, and show that this bias can affect first-order inference. We propose Riemannian robust bias correction (RRBC), which estimates the leading smoothing bias using a local quadratic regression in the response tangent space and corrects the estimator through the exponential map. Our leave-one-out (LOO) covariance estimator uses intrinsic displacements between the full-sample and LOO RRBC estimates, accounting for the variability of the estimated bias correction and its covariance with the local linear estimator. We establish asymptotically valid pointwise confidence sets and simultaneous confidence tubes without undersmoothing. Simulations on spheres and symmetric positive-definite matrices show that RRBC can improve coverage when smoothing bias is nonnegligible. Cardiovascular and ocean-current applications illustrate simultaneous hypothesis testing and inference for smooth functionals.
