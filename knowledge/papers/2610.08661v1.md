# No Welfare Ties Between Pure-Strategy Nash Equilibria

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08661v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文证明在足够丰富的C^2博弈类中，不同的纯策略纳什均衡通常具有不同的功利主义福利值，从而福利最大化均衡若存在则唯一，并扩展到更一般的福利聚合器和扰动族。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们证明，在具有开策略集的足够丰富的$C^2$博弈类中，不同的纯策略纳什均衡通常具有不同的功利主义福利值。这里，“通常”既指通有意义下，也指有限普遍意义下；而“丰富”是指该类允许一个有限维扰动族，这些扰动可以在任意两个不同策略组合处独立地沿任意方向移动支付值和一阶导数（例如，次数至多为三的多项式扰动）。因此，如果存在一个福利最大化均衡，则它是唯一的。该结果可推广到每个固定的$C^1$支付聚合器，只要其关于支付向量的导数处处非零。在完整的$C^2$效用空间中，我们还处理依赖于策略、支付和支付导数的标量准则，但需满足在支付水平方向或跨参与者导数方向上的非消失条件。最后，从某个合适的有限维空间中附加任意小的扰动，会使得福利分离在增广族中成为典型的，并且对每个固定基础博弈的几乎所有扰动都成立分离。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We show that distinct pure-strategy Nash equilibria typically have different utilitarian welfare values in sufficiently rich classes of $C^2$ games with open strategy sets. Here, typical means both generic and finitely prevalent, while rich means that the class admits a finite-dimensional family of perturbations that can independently move payoff values and first derivatives in any direction at any two distinct strategy profiles (for example, polynomial perturbations of degree at most three). Consequently, a welfare-maximizing equilibrium, if one exists, is unique. The result extends to each fixed $C^1$ payoff aggregator whose derivative with respect to the payoff vector is everywhere nonzero. In the full $C^2$ utility space, we also treat scalar criteria depending on strategies, payoffs, and payoff derivatives, subject to a nonvanishing condition in payoff-level or cross-player derivative directions. Finally, adjoining arbitrarily small perturbations from a suitable finite-dimensional space makes welfare separation typical in the augmented family, with separation holding for almost every perturbation of each fixed base game.

</details>
