<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

各司法管辖区竞相吸引移动设施，但公用事业公司可能从辖区外的客户那里回收支持性资本成本。我构建了一个辖区竞标模型，其中各选址在生产性盈余、地方收益、基础设施成本以及当地居民承担这些成本的比例上存在差异。成本社会化提高了辖区获胜的意愿，并可能将项目引向福利水平较低的选址。即使区域福利下降，企业和主办辖区仍可能获益。其对现金补贴的影响是不确定的，因为接入费和有竞争力的投标也会发生变化。现金上限会促使支持方式转向实物支持，并可能使选址结果恶化。向企业收取净增量成本，或要求主办辖区承担施加于外部客户的成本，在政治收益普遍存在的情况下可恢复效率排序。在负荷不确定的情况下，更广泛的成本分担会提高承诺容量和未使用容量。数值模拟显示了这些扭曲在何处最为严重，以及补贴上限何时会适得其反。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Jurisdictions compete for mobile facilities, but utilities may recover supporting capital costs from customers outside the host. I develop a model of jurisdictional bidding in which sites differ in productive surplus, local benefits, infrastructure costs, and how much of those costs local residents bear. Cost socialization raises a jurisdiction’s willingness to win and can direct the project to a lower-welfare site. The firm and host may gain even as regional welfare falls. Its effect on cash subsidies is ambiguous because connection charges and rival bids also change. Cash caps induce substitution toward in-kind support and can worsen site selection. Charging the firm for net incremental costs, or assessing the host for costs imposed on outside customers, restores the efficient ranking when political benefits are common. With uncertain load, broader cost sharing raises committed and unused capacity. Numerical exercises show where these distortions are largest and when subsidy caps backfire.

</details>
