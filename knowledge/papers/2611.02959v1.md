<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

劳动力市场状况如何塑造家庭内部及家庭之间的教育不平等？越来越多的证据记录了世界各地劳动力市场中教育回报的凸性特征。我们构建了一个理论模型，证明收益凸性可能增加或减少兄弟姐妹之间在投资和结果上的差异，具体取决于父母不平等厌恶参数。我们通过一项高激励的实地实验室实验，恢复了这一结构性参数的分布。该实验以墨西哥圣路易斯波托西的246名家长为对象，其中代币分配决定儿童获得AI课程的机会。家长在六种情境下做出分配决策，这些情境在收益函数的凸性和禀赋-投资替代性上有所不同。我们识别出三类家长：罗尔斯主义型家长表现出完全的不平等厌恶（12.6%），中间型家长具有中等程度的不平等容忍度（76.8%），功利主义型家长则最大化总收益（10.6%）。尽管这三类群体对收益凸性的反应方式各异，但平均而言，更大的凸性增加了分配给高禀赋子女的份额，从而加剧了家庭内部的不平等。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How do labor market conditions shape educational inequality within- and between families? A growing body of evidence documents convex wage returns to education in labor markets across the world. We develop a theoretical model that demonstrates that earnings convexity can increase or decrease between-sibling differences in investments and outcomes depending on the parameter of parental inequality aversion. We recover the distribution of this structural parameter from a highly-incentivized lab-in-the-field experiment with 246 parents in San Luis Potosí, Mexico, in which token allocations determine children's access to an AI course. Parents make allocation decisions across six scenarios that vary convexity in the payoff function and endowment-investment substitutability. We identify three types of parents: Rawlsian parents display perfect inequality aversion (12.6%), interior parents that are moderately inequality tolerant (76.8%), and Utilitarian parents who maximize total payoffs (10.6%). While these three groups respond to earnings convexity in heterogeneous ways, on average, greater convexity increases the share allocated to the high-endowed child, thereby widening within-family inequality.
