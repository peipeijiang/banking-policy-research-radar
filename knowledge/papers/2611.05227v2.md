<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

对于随机成本向量为 $c$ 的随机规划 $\min_{z\in Z} c^T z + h(z)$，遗憾值（regret），即完全信息的期望值，通常通过逐个情景重新求解问题来计算。我们证明，遗憾值由成本向量与最优决策 $π^s(c)$ 之间的协方差决定。该恒等式是精确的，且无需正则性假设即可成立，只需将协方差沿从均值到每个情景的路径进行积分。令 $c_s=E[c]+s(c-E[c])$，则 $Reg=-\int_0^1 s^{-1} Cov(c_s,π^s(c_s))\,ds$。由此可得三个推论。第一，普通协方差始终是一个上界：$0\le Reg\le-Cov(c,π^s(c))$。第二，当最优决策规则为仿射时——如无约束和等式约束（例如预算约束的 Markowitz）二次规划——遗憾值恰好等于协方差的一半。对于纯线性目标函数，仿射规则迫使遗憾值为零。第三，遗憾值与协方差之比位于 $[0,1]$ 区间内，并受协方差沿路径的剖面形状所支配。该剖面首次变为正时的噪声水平是一个决策稳定性边际，它收紧了上述界。该恒等式对任意预测成本也逐情景成立，因此适用于具有不连续决策规则的线性规划和组合规划。我们给出了 plug-in 估计量的无维集中不等式和中心极限定理。在 7 个问题类别、8 种成本分布以及 Elmachtoub 和 Grigas（2022）的最短路径基准测试中（每种配置 $10^6$ 次抽样），各恒等式在求积误差范围内成立，且协方差界在每个情景中均成立。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

For a stochastic program $\min_{z\in Z} c^T z + h(z)$ with a random cost vector $c$, regret, or the expected value of perfect information, is usually computed by re-solving the problem scenario by scenario. We show that regret is determined by the covariance between the cost vector and the optimal decision $π^s(c)$. The identity is exact and holds without regularity assumptions once the covariance is integrated along the path from the mean to each scenario. With $c_s=E[c]+s(c-E[c])$, $Reg=-\int_0^1 s^{-1} Cov(c_s,π^s(c_s))\,ds$. Three consequences follow. First, the plain covariance is always an upper bound: $0\le Reg\le-Cov(c,π^s(c))$. Second, when the optimal decision rule is affine, as in unconstrained and equality-constrained (e.g., budget-constrained Markowitz) quadratic programs, regret equals exactly one half of the covariance. For purely linear objectives, an affine rule forces zero regret. Third, the ratio of regret to covariance lies in $[0,1]$ and is governed by the shape of the covariance profile along the path. The noise level at which this profile first becomes positive is a decision-stability margin that tightens the bound. The identity also holds scenario by scenario for any predicted cost, so it applies to linear and combinatorial programs with discontinuous decision rules. We give dimension-free concentration bounds and a central limit theorem for the plug-in estimator. Across 7 problem classes, 8 cost distributions, and the shortest-path benchmark of Elmachtoub and Grigas (2022) with $10^6$ draws per configuration, the identities hold to within quadrature error, and the covariance bound holds in every scenario.
