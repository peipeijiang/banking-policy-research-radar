# Unconfoundedness as an Information Constraint

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文在允许内生选择的一般模型类中，提供了无混淆性（unconfoundedness）的信息论刻画。我们施加一个条件互信息（conditional mutual information, CMI）限制，要求潜在结果中所包含的关于处理的信息，在给定协变量的条件下，不超过其潜在异质性中所包含的信息。在保持的模型条件下，该限制等价于将处理概率乘性分解为潜在异质性和协变量的独立函数。尽管CMI限制允许内生选择，但协变量取值间处理概率的充分丰富变异使其等价于相应潜在结果的无混淆性。对于二值处理，任何非常数倾向得分均足够；对于多值处理，倾向得分向量二阶矩矩阵的满秩条件即足够。状态特定限制界定了残余选择，并在边界支撑条件下蕴含个体处理状态的外生性。扩展涵盖了连续处理和工具外生性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper provides an information-theoretic characterization of unconfoundedness within a general class of models allowing for endogenous selection. We impose a conditional mutual information (CMI) restriction requiring that the information about treatment contained in a potential outcome, conditional on covariates, does not exceed that contained in its latent heterogeneity. Under the maintained model conditions, this restriction is equivalent to a multiplicative factorization of treatment probabilities into separate functions of latent heterogeneity and covariates. Although the CMI restriction permits endogenous selection, sufficiently rich variation in treatment probabilities across covariate values makes it equivalent to unconfoundedness for the corresponding potential outcome. For binary treatments, any nonconstant propensity score suffices; for multiple treatments, a full-rank condition on the second-moment matrix of the propensity-score vector is sufficient. State-specific restrictions bound residual selection and imply exogeneity for individual treatment states under boundary-support conditions. Extensions cover continuous treatments and instrument exogeneity.
