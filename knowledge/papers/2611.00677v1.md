<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

面板局部投影（panel local projections，panel LPs）因其灵活性和稳健性而广受重视，被广泛用于从微观数据中估计对总量冲击的异质性响应。然而，本文强调了面板向量自回归（panel vector autoregressions，panel VARs）的潜力，并刻画了panel VARs的效率与panel LPs的稳健性之间的权衡关系。与时间序列文献中的已有结果不同，我们证明：在正确设定下，异质性响应的panel VAR估计量以$(NT)^{-1/2}$的速率收敛，而panel LP估计量以$T^{-1/2}$的速率收敛。当异质性响应函数为非参数形式时，panel VAR会产生非参数速率损失，而panel LP在适当的平滑性和面板增长条件下仍保持其参数$T^{-1/2}$速率。我们提供了一种Hausman型检验以在二者之间进行选择，该检验对两种形式的panel VAR误设定具有检验功效。我们将panel LPs和panel VARs应用于土耳其行政企业层面数据，以估计企业对货币政策冲击的异质性响应。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Panel local projections (panel LPs), valued for their flexibility and robustness, are widely used to estimate heterogeneous responses to aggregate shocks from micro data. This paper, however, highlights the potential of panel vector autoregressions (panel VARs) and characterizes the trade-off between the efficiency of panel VARs and the robustness of panel LPs. In contrast to existing results in the time-series literature, we show that under correct specification, panel VAR estimators of the heterogeneous responses converge at rate $(NT)^{-1/2}$, whereas panel LP estimators converge at rate $T^{-1/2}$. When the heterogeneous response function is nonparametric, the panel VAR incurs a nonparametric rate loss, whereas the panel LP retains its parametric $T^{-1/2}$ rate under suitable smoothness and panel-growth conditions. We provide a Hausman-type test for choosing between them, with power against two forms of panel VAR misspecification. We apply panel LPs and panel VARs to Turkish administrative firm-level data to estimate heterogeneous firm responses to monetary policy shocks.
