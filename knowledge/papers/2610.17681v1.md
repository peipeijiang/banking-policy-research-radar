<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

预期短缺（Expected Shortfall, ES）衡量尾部损失的平均规模。其序贯回测通常还需要一个风险价值（Value-at-Risk, VaR）预测。我们开发了两种仅使用观测损失和ES预测的e-回测方法：一种仅适用于独立同分布损失，另一种适用于AR-GARCH条件VaR结构下的时间序列。在所述假设下，两者均能在任意停时控制错误拒绝。对于时间序列方法，有效性要求一个固定的参数——分位数对来代表条件VaR路径，以及一个正的报告边际。时间序列统计量对可能的参数和VaR值取下确界。我们通过最大化在候选参数上平均的经验增长率来选择投注过程$(λ_t)$。我们通过在密集参数网格上进行连续VaR剖面计算来得到统计量。对于网格近似，我们证明了当网格在固定监测期内细化时，统计量收敛于连续下确界，同时错误拒绝概率也收敛。参数网格上VaR下确界的下界和上界控制了数值剖面计算的精度。模拟实验（包括从过度报告转变为低报告的ES报告）以及一个应用于纳斯达克日损失的实证案例，提供了与VaR-ES e-过程和固定样本ES回测的数值比较，评估了检测频率和拒绝时机。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Expected Shortfall (ES) measures the average size of tail losses. Its sequential backtests typically also require a Value-at-Risk (VaR) forecast. We develop two e-backtesting methods using only observed losses and ES forecasts: one for iid losses only and the other for time series under an AR-GARCH conditional VaR structure. Under the stated assumptions, both control false rejections at any stopping time. For the time-series method, validity requires a fixed parameter--quantile pair representing the conditional VaR path and a positive report margin. The time-series statistic takes an infimum over possible parameter and VaR values. We choose the betting process $(λ_t)$ by maximizing an empirical growth rate averaged across candidate parameters. We compute the statistic by continuous VaR profiling on a dense parameter grid. For grid approximations, we prove convergence to the continuous infimum, together with convergence of the probability of false rejection, as the grid is refined over a fixed monitoring period. Lower and upper bounds for the VaR infimum on the parameter grid control the accuracy of the numerical profiling. Simulations, including ES reports that change from over-reporting to under-reporting, and an application to daily NASDAQ losses provide numerical comparisons with VaR--ES e-processes and fixed-sample ES backtests, assessing detection frequencies and the timing of rejections.

</details>
