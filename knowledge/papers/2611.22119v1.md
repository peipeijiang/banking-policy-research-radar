<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

优化确定性等价（Optimized Certainty Equivalents, OCEs）是优化与风险管理中的一类重要风险度量，包括均值—方差准则（Mean–Variance criterion）、预期短缺（Expected Shortfall）、熵风险度量（Entropic Risk Measure）以及期望。尽管其重要性不言而喻，OCEs 尚未获得经济公理的系统刻画。我们提出了一条新公理——公平硬币补偿（Fair-Coin Compensation），并将其与强风险厌恶（Strong Risk Aversion）和归一化（Normalization）相结合，共同刻画了 OCEs。我们通过掷硬币公平分配两个损失的情境来解释公平硬币补偿：参与者之间的转移可以消除随机分配所产生的额外风险。加入更多公理可获得更精细的刻画。单调性（Monotonicity）导出标准 OCEs；正齐次性（Positive Homogeneity）与审慎性（Prudence）共同导出预期短缺；公平硬币独立性（Fair-Coin Independence）导出熵风险度量。我们还利用新公理——等均值随机化（Equal-Mean Randomization）——对均值—方差准则进行了公理化刻画，该公理表明：对两个具有相同期望值的损失进行随机分配，所产生的风险度量不应高于二者风险度量的平均值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Optimized Certainty Equivalents (OCEs) are an important class of risk measures for optimization and risk management that includes the Mean--Variance criterion, Expected Shortfall, the Entropic Risk Measure, and expectation. Despite their importance, economic axioms have not characterized OCEs. We propose a new axiom called Fair-Coin Compensation, which characterizes OCEs together with Strong Risk Aversion and Normalization. We interpret Fair-Coin Compensation through the fair assignment of two losses by a coin toss: transfers between two participants can remove the additional risk created by the random assignment. Adding further axioms yields sharper characterizations. Monotonicity yields standard OCEs; Positive Homogeneity, together with Prudence, yields Expected Shortfall; and Fair-Coin Independence yields the Entropic Risk Measure. We also axiomatize the Mean--Variance criterion using our novel axiom of Equal-Mean Randomization, which states that random assignment of two losses with the same expected value does not produce a risk measure above the average of their risk measures.
