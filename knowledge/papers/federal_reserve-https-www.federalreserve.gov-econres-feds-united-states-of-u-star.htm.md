<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文由Hie Joo Ahn和Jeremy Rudd撰写，基于不同失业持续期内失业流入与流出的趋势成分，估算了州级趋势性失业率（趋势U-star）。估计得到的趋势U-star，尤其是其中可归因于长期失业的部分，其水平与动态在各州之间存在显著差异。在农业州，长期失业对趋势U-star的贡献相对较小，而在“锈带”州（Rust Belt）及“阳光地带”（Sun Belt）的若干州，其占比则更高。尽管更高的教育程度和人口老龄化对U-star构成下行压力，但产业结构与劳动力市场僵化的影响则呈现混合效应。利用州级趋势U-star的估计值，我们重新审视了以往关于州级菲利普斯曲线的研究，发现前瞻性通胀方程的估计斜率主要由趋势U-star的变动所驱动，而非失业率的周期性波动。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Hie Joo Ahn and Jeremy Rudd This paper estimates state-level trend unemployment rates (trend U-star) based on the trend components of unemployment inflows and outflows across unemployment durations. The estimated trend U-stars, particularly the portions attributable to long-termunemployment, have levels and dynamics that vary substantially across states. Long-termunemployment makes a relatively small contribution to trend U-star in agricultural states, but takes a larger share for Rust Belt states and several states in the Sun Belt. While higher educational attainment and population aging put downward pressure on U-star, the effects of industrial structure and labor-market rigidities are mixed. Using the estimates of state-level trend U-star, we revisit previous work on state-level Phillips curves and find that the estimated slopes of forward-looking inflation equations are driven bymovements in trend U-star rather than by cyclical variation in the unemployment rate.
