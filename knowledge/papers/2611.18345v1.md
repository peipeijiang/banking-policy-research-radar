# Exploitation, class income and welfare in a Sraffian economy with consumption preferences

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18345v1)

## 一句话结论

> 在斯拉法简单再生产模型中引入阶级特定消费偏好，证明利润率上升未必单调提高剩余价值、剥削率、总利润和资本家福利，因为相对价格变化可能降低资本家的实际消费与福利。

<details open>
<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文构建了一个简单再生产的斯拉法（Sraffian）模型，其中生产价格和功能性收入分配保持古典决定方式，而阶级消费则在给定价格和收入下实现最优选择。随后本文表明，一旦工人和资本家根据阶级特定偏好选择消费束，而非消费固定篮子，剩余价值、剥削率、总利润和资本家福利便不必随利润率单调上升。该框架定义了分配恩格尔曲线（distributive Engel curves），刻画消费、利润、劳动剥削和福利如何沿工资—利润边界变化。更高的利润率可能扩大资本家对剩余的名义索取权，却以改变相对价格的方式减少其实际消费和福利。这一结果对基于工资—利润曲线的标准马克思（Marxian）和斯拉法关于净产品分配的论述构成了限定。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops a Sraffian model of simple reproduction in which production prices and functional income distribution remain classically determined, while class consumption is chosen optimally at those prices and incomes. it then shows that once workers and capitalists choose consumption bundles according to class-specific preferences, rather than consuming fixed baskets, surplus value, exploitation rates, total profits, and capitalist welfare need not rise monotonically with the profit rate. The framework defines distributive Engel curves, tracing how consumption, profits, labor exploitation, and welfare vary along the wage-profit frontier. Higher profitability may expand capitalists' nominal claim on surplus, yet alter relative prices in ways that reduce their real consumption and welfare. The result qualifies standard Marxian and Sraffian accounts of net product distribution based on the wage-profit curve.

</details>
