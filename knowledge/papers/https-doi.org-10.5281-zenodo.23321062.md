2026年上半年，加密货币市场延续了下行趋势。1月份的短暂增长并未改变整体格局：市场在2月和6月大幅下跌，一度跌幅高达37%。尽管如此，截至9月21日，市场已几乎回升至年初水平。比特币已接近其1月份的价格水平，而大多数主要山寨币则未能如此。全年来看，Hyperliquid的治理代币（HYPE）和隐私类加密货币（尤其是Zcash）表现出积极势头。价格受到通胀预期和美国联邦储备系统（FRS）决策的影响。关键监管进展包括欧盟MiCA过渡期结束、俄罗斯新法出台，以及美国CLARITY法案的搁置。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In H1 2026, cryptocurrency market continued its downward trend. A brief growth in January did not alter the overall picture: the market fell significantly in February and again in June, dropping by as much as 37% at one point. Nevertheless, by September 21, the market had nearly returned to its levels from the beginning of the year. Bitcoin has approached its January price level, which cannot be said about the most major altcoins. Throughout the year, positive momentum was seen in Hyperliquid’s governance token (HYPE) and privacy-focused cryptocurrencies, particularly Zcash. Prices were influenced by inflation expectations and decisions made by the US FRS. Key regulatory developments included conclusion of the MiCA transition period in the European Union, introduction of a new law in Russia, and halting the CLARITY Act in the United States.
