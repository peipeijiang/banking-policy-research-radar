人工智能正在通过算法和计算能力的进步，使投资者和中介机构能够从日益丰富的数据中提取更多信息，从而改变金融市场。我们讨论了人工智能如何改变信息生产与决策、劳动力需求以及金融中介，特别侧重于证券市场。随后，我们考察了这一变革对市场效率、竞争、信息摩擦和金融稳定的影响。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial intelligence is transforming financial markets by enabling investors and intermediaries to extract more information from increasingly abundant data through advances in algorithms and computing power. We discuss how AI changes information production and decision-making, labor demand, and financial intermediation, with a particular emphasis on securities markets. We then survey the implications of this transformation for market efficiency, competition, informational frictions, and financial stability.
