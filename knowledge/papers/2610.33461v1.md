<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

弱相依的异质性误差在均值因子模型中是标准设定，但分位数因子模型仅在独立性假设下被分析过。均值情形下弱相依的定义——误差协方差矩阵特征值的界——并不适用：我证明它既不蕴含也不被控制分位数估计的性质所蕴含，因为不相关的误差可以共享一个普遍存在的分位数成分。我转而通过对异质性阵列的集中条件来定义近似分位数因子模型。在该条件下，分位数因子分析（QFA）估计量是一致的，其收敛速率因一个相依常数的平方根而变慢，且该速率是极小极大最优的，因子个数也能被一致估计。相依性改变了渐近方差，为此我提供了HAC估计量和阈值化估计量。蒙特卡洛实验证实了这些预测。在FRED-QD和FRED-MD中，忽略相依性的标准误低估了不确定性，且在尾部最为严重。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Weakly dependent idiosyncratic errors are standard in mean factor models, but quantile factor models have been analyzed only under independence. The mean-case definition of weak dependence, a bound on the eigenvalues of the error covariance matrix, does not carry over: I show that it neither implies nor is implied by the property that controls quantile estimation, since uncorrelated errors can share a pervasive quantile component. I instead define approximate quantile factor models through a concentration condition on the idiosyncratic array. Under it, the quantile factor analysis (QFA) estimator is consistent at a rate that deteriorates by the square root of a dependence constant, which is minimax optimal, and the number of factors is consistently estimated. Dependence changes the asymptotic variance, for which I provide HAC and thresholded estimators. Monte Carlo experiments confirm these predictions. In FRED-QD and FRED-MD, standard errors ignoring dependence understate uncertainty, most strongly in the tails.

</details>
