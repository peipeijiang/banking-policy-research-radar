# Cordon Tolls and Urban Traffic Volumes: Evidence from NYC's 2025 Congestion Relief Zone

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34818v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用TWFE-DiD比较纽约拥堵收费区与未收费路段的小时交通量，发现收费使收费区对数小时流量下降40-45%，东河桥梁流量上升约25%，体现路线替代。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

2025年1月5日，纽约市成为美国首个实施永久性区域拥堵收费的城市，对驶入曼哈顿中央商务区（拥堵缓解区，Congestion Relief Zone，简称CRZ）的车辆收费。本研究采用双向固定效应双重差分（two-way fixed-effects difference-in-differences，TWFE-DiD）策略，将受处理的CRZ路段与未受处理的曼哈顿60街以北路段进行比较，以估计该收费对每小时交通量的短期因果效应。实证分析以MTA收费计量系统的小时进入计数（486万条十分钟区块记录）作为主要描述性面板数据，并以纽约市交通局（NYC DOT）高频自动交通记录仪（Automated Traffic Recorder，ATR）数据作为路段内识别面板。主要TWFE-DiD点估计表明，受处理CRZ地点的对数小时交通量下降了40%至45%，效应方向在九项预注册的替代设定中保持一致，且集中于高峰时段。平衡面板中的东河桥梁在收费后期间交通量出现统计显著的25%增长，与路线替代行为一致。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

On January 5, 2025, New York City became the first U.S. city to operate a permanent cordon-based congestion toll, charging vehicles entering Manhattan's Central Business District (the Congestion Relief Zone, or CRZ). This study estimates the short-run causal effect of the toll on hourly traffic volumes using a two-way fixed-effects difference in-differences (TWFE-DiD) strategy that compares treated CRZ street segments to untreated Manhattan segments north of 60th Street. The empirical analysis exploits hourly entry counts from the MTA's toll-meter system (4.86 million ten-minute-block records) as the primary descriptive panel and high-frequency NYC DOT Automated Traffic Recorder (ATR) data as the within-segment identification panel. The headline TWFE-DiD point estimate indicates a 40 to 45 percent reduction in log hourly volume at treated CRZ locations, with the effect direction consistent across nine pre-registered alternative specifications and concentrated in peak hours. East River bridges in the balanced panel exhibit a statistically significant 25 percent post-period increase in volume, consistent with route substitution.
