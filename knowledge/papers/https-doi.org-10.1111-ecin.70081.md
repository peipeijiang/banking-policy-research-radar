<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察了正式采用通胀目标制（IT）对发达经济体及新兴市场和发展中经济体的通胀、增长及通胀预期锚定的影响。我们发现，尽管早期采用通胀目标制（2000年前）的国家在采用后通胀率有所下降，但自那时起采用通胀目标制的国家中，仅有约半数案例取得了此类成功。我们基于国家层面的分析——采用合成控制法——表明，在约三分之一的案例中，采用通胀目标制带来了显著的通胀改善。对于采用通胀目标制会系统性地导致增长表现恶化的担忧，我们发现的证据支持有限。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Abstract We examine the impact of formal adoption of inflation targeting (IT) on inflation, growth and anchoring of inflation expectations in advanced economies and emerging markets and developing economies. We find that while the early adopters of IT (pre‐2000) saw declines in inflation rates following adoption, IT adopters since then have enjoyed such success in only about half the cases. Our country‐level analysis—using the synthetic control method—shows that IT adoption delivers significant inflation gains in about a third of the cases. We find limited support for the concern that adoption of IT systematically leads to poorer growth outcomes.
