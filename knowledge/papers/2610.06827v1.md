<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

与自雇或绩效工资相比，固定工资降低了收入不确定性，但容易通过道德风险和逆向选择导致市场扭曲。利用不对称信息下工资合约的模型，我证明这些扭曲映射到边际处理效应（MTE）框架中的边际处理响应函数。我将这一框架应用于一项实地实验，实验中数据录入工人在随机设定的固定工资和计件工资之间进行选择。我发现了道德风险和逆向选择两者的证据：相对于均值，固定工资使工人生产率估计降低8.74%。同时，工资报价提高10%会吸引一名边际工人，其生产率比均值高1.90%。使用半参数MTE估计，我计算了与不对称信息相关的福利损失，以及旨在挽回这一损失的政策中公共资金的边际价值（MVPFs）。我发现，对计件工资征税可以缓解进入固定工资的逆向选择，以低至每美元0.88美元的社会成本产生税收收入。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Compared to self-employment or performance-based pay, fixed wages reduce earnings uncertainty but are prone to market distortions through moral hazard and adverse selection. Using a model of wage contracts under asymmetric information, I show that these distortions map to marginal treatment response functions in a marginal treatment effects (MTE) framework. I apply this framework to a field experiment in which data-entry workers choose between a randomized fixed wage and a piece rate. I find evidence of both moral hazard and adverse selection: Fixed wages reduce worker productivity by an estimated 8.74 percent relative to the mean. Meanwhile, a 10 percent increase in the wage offer attracts a marginal worker whose productivity is higher by 1.90 percent of the mean. Using semi-parametric MTE estimation, I calculate the welfare loss associated with asymmetric information and the marginal values of public funds (MVPFs) for policies aimed at recovering this loss. I find that a tax on piece rates can mitigate adverse selection into fixed wages, generating tax revenue at social costs as low as $0.88 per dollar.
