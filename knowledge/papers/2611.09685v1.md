<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当不可分割的物品（如传家宝或体育赛事门票）在具有平等权利的代理人之间无法均匀分配时，某些代理人必然比其他代理人获得更多物品。先挑选能否补偿那些获得较少物品的人？我们提出两条补偿公平原则。获得较少物品的代理人不得嫉妒一个更大 bundle 在其移除任意一件物品后的结果，且拥有相同数量物品的代理人之间的优先权优势必须交替而非累积。非浪费性、中立性以及一种物品层面的 Maskin 单调性形式刻画了选秀规则（draft rules），即代理人按固定顺序挑选。加入上述两条原则以及“至多一件物品的免嫉妒性”（envy-freeness up to one object），则唯一确定了区块-蛇形选秀（block-snake draft），其中获得较少物品的代理人在每一轮首先挑选，且各组之间的顺序在轮次间反转。在选秀规则中，补偿与策略防伪性（strategy-proofness）相容，但“至多一件物品的免嫉妒性”则不然：一旦至少两个代理人获得超过一件物品，任何满足该性质的选秀规则甚至都不是弱策略防伪的；而当存在两个这样的代理人时，这一冲突扩展到所有非浪费且中立的规则。当代理人可能对物品无差异时，将每个平局按照有利于最早的后序严格偏好挑选者的方式解决，可保持选秀规则的公平性保证，并确保成对支配（pairwise dominance）下的效率。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When indivisible objects, such as heirlooms or tickets to sports events, do not divide evenly among agents with equal claims, some agents must receive more objects than others. Can picking first compensate those with fewer objects? We propose two principles of compensatory fairness. An agent with fewer objects must not envy a larger bundle with any one of its objects removed, and priority advantages among agents with equally many objects must alternate rather than accumulate. Non-wastefulness, neutrality and an object-wise form of Maskin monotonicity characterize the draft rules, in which agents pick in a fixed sequence. Adding the two principles and envy-freeness up to one object singles out the block--snake draft, in which agents with fewer objects pick first in every round and each group's order reverses between rounds. Among draft rules, compensation is compatible with strategy-proofness, but envy-freeness up to one object is not: once at least two agents receive more than one object, no draft that satisfies it is even weakly strategy-proof, and with two such agents the conflict extends to all non-wasteful and neutral rules. When agents may be indifferent between objects, resolving each tie in favor of the earliest later picker who strictly cares preserves the draft's fairness guarantees and ensures efficiency under pairwise dominance.

</details>
