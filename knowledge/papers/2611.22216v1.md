<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在Babaioff等人（2013）提出的开创性Bertrand网络博弈中，每个卖家向专属买家以及与其相邻卖家共享的市场发布单一价格，卖家无法在不同市场之间进行价格歧视。尽管均衡的存在性以及在树结构上计算均衡的多项式时间算法（Babaioff等人（2013））已被已知，但对于一般图是否存在高效算法来计算均衡，一直是一个重要的开放问题。

本文通过证明在Bertrand网络中计算任意常数误差的近似Nash均衡是$\PPAD$-完全的，对该开放问题给出了否定回答。这一困难性即使在最大度为五的稀疏二部图上、且市场权重为常数整数时仍然成立。作为对困难性结果的补充，当最大度和拥有专属买家的卖家数量均有界时，我们设计了一种高效多项式时间近似方案（EPTAS）。

除时间复杂度之外，由于精确均衡需要对连续区间上的价格进行随机化，Babaioff等人（2013）留下的另一个基本问题是：精确均衡是否总能以有限数量的价格区间来表示，即其结构复杂度是否为有限的。我们通过证明每一个可实现的均衡收益剖面都可以由一个结构复杂度为$O(2^n)$的均衡来实现，对该问题给出了肯定回答，并由此得到一个运行时间为双指数时间的精确算法。我们进一步证明该界是紧的：存在一些实例，其实现目标收益剖面的唯一均衡具有结构复杂度$Ω(2^n)$。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In the seminal Bertrand network game introduced by Babaioff et al. (2013), each seller posts a single price to captive buyers and to the markets it shares with neighboring sellers, where sellers cannot price discriminate across the markets. Although the existence of equilibria and polynomial-time algorithms to compute them in trees (Babaioff et al. (2013)) have been known, it has been a major open problem whether there exist efficient algorithms to compute them for general graphs.
  In this paper, we resolve this open question negatively by proving that computing any constant-error approximate Nash equilibrium in a Bertrand network is $\PPAD$-complete. This hardness holds even on sparse bipartite graphs of maximum degree five with constant integer market weights. Complementing our hardness result, we design an efficient polynomial-time approximation scheme (EPTAS) when both the maximum degree and the number of sellers with captive buyers are bounded.
  Beyond time complexity, since exact equilibria require randomizing prices over continuous intervals, another fundamental question left open by Babaioff et al. (2013) is whether an exact equilibrium can always be represented with a finite number of price intervals, \ie whether its structural complexity is finite. We resolve this question affirmatively by proving that every achievable equilibrium revenue profile can be realized by an equilibrium with structural complexity $O(2^n)$, which leads to an exact algorithm running in doubly exponential time. We further show that this bound is tight: there exist instances for which the unique equilibrium realizing target revenue profiles has structural complexity $Ω(2^n)$.
