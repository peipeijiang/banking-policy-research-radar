<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

最惠国（MFN）关税的提高不仅仅会抬高贸易成本。通过改变竞争性出口国之间的相对关税待遇，最惠国关税的提高增加了现有贸易优惠的价值，重塑了相对市场准入和贸易配置。尽管提高关税的贸易削减效应已得到充分证实，但人们对这一机制的了解却少得多。我们研究最惠国关税的提高是否会放大优惠伙伴的竞争优势，从而产生贸易转移。利用国内和国际贸易数据，我们估计了一个结构引力模型，其中包含与CES一致的双边关税 margins，并以近期美国贸易政策的变化作为实证应用加以考察。我们发现，最惠国保护水平的提高同时减少了进口并将贸易转向优惠出口国。这些效应在各部门和各地区之间高度异质，最大的损失集中在中间品和资本品上，凸显了最惠国关税在重塑相对市场准入和全球贸易格局中的作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Most-favored-nation (MFN) tariff increases do more than raise trade costs. By changing the relative tariff treatment of competing exporters, they increase the value of existing trade preferences, reshaping relative market access and trade allocation. While the trade-reducing effects of higher tariffs are well established, much less is known about this mechanism. We investigate whether higher MFN tariffs amplify the competitive advantage of preferential partners, generating trade diversion. Using domestic and international trade data, we estimate a structural gravity model with CES-consistent bilateral tariff margins and examine recent changes in US trade policy as an empirical application. We find that higher MFN protection simultaneously reduces imports and redirects trade toward preferential exporters. These effects are highly heterogeneous across sectors and regions, with the largest losses concentrated in intermediate and capital goods, highlighting the role of MFN tariffs in reshaping relative market access and global trade patterns.
