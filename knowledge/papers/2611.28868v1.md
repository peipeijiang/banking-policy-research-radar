# Risk aversion with two risks for state-dependent utilities

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28868v1)

## 一句话结论

> 在状态依赖的双属性效用框架下定义并刻画存在背景风险时的Arrow-Pratt风险厌恶，给出风险厌恶保持的条件并引入一致风险厌恶概念，应用于公平保险合同与两期最优预防问题。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文在双属性、状态依赖效用框架下引入Arrow-Pratt风险厌恶概念，其中主要风险伴随背景风险。在这一设定下，我们研究在存在另一种风险时对一种风险的风险厌恶、状态依赖双属性效用函数的结构以及两种风险之间的相关程度之间的相互关系。更具体地说，我们在考虑两种风险之间各种相关结构的情况下，建立了状态依赖双属性效用函数的条件，在这些条件下，当涉及背景风险时，对主要风险的风险厌恶得以保持。此外，还引入了一个新概念，即在存在背景风险情况下的一致风险厌恶。我们证明，上述建立的状态依赖双属性效用函数条件等价于在存在背景风险时对主要风险的一致风险厌恶。这些发现为相关风险的状态依赖偏好提供了新的见解。在公平保险合同和两期模型中最优预防两个背景下的应用，说明了在状态依赖效用下所提出的双风险风险厌恶的实际有用性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper, we introduce the concept of Arrow-Pratt risk aversion within a two-attribute, state-dependent utility framework, where a primary risk is accompanied by a background risk. Within this setting, we investigate the interrelationships among risk aversion toward one risk in the presence of another risk, the structure of state-dependent two-attribute utility functions, and the degree of correlation between the two risks. More specifically, we establish conditions on the state-dependent two-attribute utility function under which risk aversion toward primary risk is preserved when a background risk is involved, considering various correlation structures between the two types of risks. Additionally, a new concept, uniform risk aversion in the presence of a background risk, is introduced. We show that the above established conditions on the state-dependent two-attribute utility function are equivalent to uniform risk aversion toward primary risk in presence of a background risk. These findings offer new insights into state-dependent preferences for correlated risks. Two applications in the contexts of fair insurance contract and optimal prevention within a two-period model illustrate the practical usefulness of the proposed risk aversion with two risks under state-dependent utilities.
