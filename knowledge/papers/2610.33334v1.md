<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在AI辅助服务系统中，服务器可以选择为每个任务投入多少处理努力，我们称之为服务深度。更深的推理会提高输出质量，但会占用服务器更长时间并加剧拥塞，且实际处理时间仍然是随机的，因为AI模型具有概率性且任务复杂度各异。我们研究单服务器队列中服务深度与优先级的联合设计问题，其中客户在延迟敏感性上存在差异，目标是最大化社会福利。对于每种客户类型，决策者选择一个优先级和一个服务深度，服务时间服从指数分布，其均值等于所选深度。
在两种客户类型和线性服务价值的设定下，我们刻画了集中式最优解。反直觉的是，当耐心型客户足够普遍时，最优解会优先考虑耐心型客户并给予其较小的服务深度。然而，当延迟敏感性为私有信息时，激励相容性要求不耐烦型客户获得弱更短的逗留时间，因此最优价格菜单永远不会优先考虑耐心型客户。 consequently，集中式决策者的最优控制策略与最优分散定价机制之间可能存在福利差距。在连续类型设定下，集中式最优解是单调的：延迟敏感性更高的客户获得弱更小的深度和更高的优先级，因此最优解可以通过价格菜单实现而无福利损失。允许随机化深度同样可以消除两种类型模型中的差距。最后，我们使用DeepSWE已发表的编码智能体试验数据校准一个智能体AI服务系统，并数值检验优先级反转、联合设计的价值以及个性化带来的福利增益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In AI-assisted service systems, the server can choose how much processing effort to spend on each job, which we call the service depth. Deeper reasoning improves output quality but occupies the server longer and increases congestion, and the realized processing time remains random because AI models are probabilistic and tasks vary in complexity. We study the joint design of service depth and priority in a single-server queue whose customers differ in delay sensitivity, with the goal of maximizing social welfare. For each customer type, the decision maker chooses a priority level and a service depth, and service times are exponential with mean equal to the chosen depth.
With two customer types and linear service values, we characterize the centralized optimum. Counterintuitively, it prioritizes the patient type and gives it a smaller service depth when patient customers are sufficiently prevalent. When delay sensitivity is private, however, incentive compatibility requires the impatient type to receive a weakly shorter sojourn time, so the optimal price menu never prioritizes the patient type. Consequently, there can be a welfare gap between the centralized decision maker's optimal control policy and the optimal decentralized pricing mechanism. With a continuum of types, the centralized optimum is monotone: more delay-sensitive customers receive weakly smaller depth and higher priority, so the optimum can be implemented by a price menu with no welfare loss. Allowing randomized depths similarly closes the gap in the two-type model. Finally, we calibrate an agentic AI service system using published coding-agent trials from DeepSWE and numerically examine priority inversion, the value of joint design, and the welfare gain from personalization.
