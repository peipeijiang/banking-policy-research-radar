<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究带有放弃的随机动态匹配问题，这是一个基本模型，其中不同类别的代理随时间到达，可能在等待时放弃市场，并且可以成对匹配以获得取决于其类别的奖励。没有任何在线策略能够保证超过全知基准的$0.5$，而全知基准知道所有到达和离开时间。这一保证是否可实现一直是一个核心未决问题，即使在特殊情况下也是如此。我们通过证明在每一个实例上，一种简单的静态优先级策略——可在多项式时间内计算——至少获得全知基准的$0.5$，从而解决了这一问题。这确立了最优竞争比恰好为$0.5$，改进了一般模型此前的最佳保证0.192 [Li et al. 2026; Collina et al. 2020]。

我们的分析依赖于一个关键的随机结果，即对来自给定类别集合的某个代理可用的概率的一个新下界。基于这一下界，我们发展了两种方法：一种原始方法，从一种新颖线性规划的最优解中读取优先级；一种对偶方法，为每个类别分配一个“价值”，并优先选择具有高“剩余”（奖励减去涉及代理的价值）的匹配。这些价值是由线性规划对偶性导出的不动点方程的唯一解。我们还表明，这两种方法密切相关。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study stochastic dynamic matching with abandonment, a fundamental model in which agents of different types arrive over time, may abandon the market while waiting, and can be matched in pairs for rewards that depend on their types. No online policy can guarantee more than $0.5$ of the omniscient benchmark, which knows all arrival and departure times. Whether this guarantee is achievable has been a central open problem, even in special cases. We resolve this problem by showing that, on every instance, a simple static priority policy, which is computable in polynomial time, earns at least $0.5$ of the omniscient benchmark. This establishes that the optimal competitive ratio is exactly $0.5$, improving the previous best guarantee of 0.192 for the general model [Li et al. 2026; Collina et al. 2020]
  Our analysis rests on a key stochastic result, which is a new lower bound on the probability that some agent from a given set of types is available. Building on this bound, we develop two approaches: a primal approach that reads the priorities from an optimal solution of a novel linear program, and a dual approach that assigns a ``value'' to each type, and prioritizes matches with high ``surplus'' (reward minus value of involved agents). These values are the unique solution of a fixed-point equation derived from linear programming duality. We also show that the two approaches are closely connected.

</details>
