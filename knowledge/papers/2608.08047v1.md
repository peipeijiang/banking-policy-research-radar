<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在交错双重差分（DiD）设计中，各单元在不同日历时间进入处理状态，因此处理效应并非单一数值，而是一组“队列平均处理效应”（Cohort-Average Treatment effects on the Treated, CATTs），每个队列-时间单元格对应一个效应。标准完全灵活估计器将每个CATT作为独立参数进行估计，虽然无偏，但在某些效应实际上相等时效率较低；而将所有效应合并为一个双向固定效应（TWFE）系数则效率较高，但只要异质性真实存在，该估计器对个体效应便会产生偏误。我们将这两种极端之间的选择问题框架化为队列-时间单元格上的划分选择问题，并采用狄利克雷过程（Dirichlet Process, DP）混合先验对CATTs进行建模。该模型倾向于简约的分组结构，且无需预先固定组数；通过折叠吉布斯采样器，可获得点估计、边缘化未知划分的置信区间，以及每对CATT的共聚类概率。在误差方差固定且对划分施加成对惩罚的条件下，最大后验（MAP）划分可简化为$\ell_0$惩罚回归，从而将贝叶斯公式与同质性追踪文献联系起来。在校准模拟中，相对于完全灵活估计器，该模型将队列-时间效应的抽样方差降低了26%–52%，且不引入合并估计器的偏误，前提是不同效应之间的差异足够大以便被识别；同时，通过对未知划分进行平均，后验分布提供了接近名义水平的置信区间覆盖率。在两个实际应用中，该方法在一个案例中恢复了能提升精度的部分同质性结构（其中队列-时间效应确实存在异质性），并在另一个案例中报告完全合并是充分的（其中效应不存在异质性）。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In staggered difference-in-differences (DiD) designs, units enter treatment at different calendar times, so the treatment effect is not a single number but a set of Cohort-Average Treatment effects on the Treated (CATTs), one per cohort-time cell. Estimating every CATT as its own parameter, as the standard fully flexible estimator does, is unbiased but inefficient when some of these effects are in fact equal, whereas pooling them all into a single two-way fixed effects (TWFE) coefficient is efficient but, whenever the heterogeneity is genuine, biased for the individual effects. We frame the choice between these extremes as a partition-selection problem on the cohort-time cells and address it with a Dirichlet Process (DP) mixture prior on the CATTs. The model favors parsimonious groupings without fixing their number, and a collapsed Gibbs sampler delivers point estimates, credible intervals that marginalize the unknown partition, and co-clustering probabilities for every pair of CATTs. With the error variance held fixed and a pairwise penalty placed on the partition, a maximum a posteriori (MAP) partition reduces to an $\ell_0$-penalized regression, connecting the Bayesian formulation to the homogeneity-pursuit literature. In a calibrated simulation, the model cuts the sampling variance of the cohort-time effects by 26--52\% relative to the fully flexible estimator, without the pooled estimator's bias, provided the distinct effects are separated enough to be recovered, and the posterior delivers near-nominal confidence-interval coverage by averaging over the unknown partition. In two applications the method recovers a precision-improving partial-homogeneity structure in one, where the cohort-time effects are genuinely heterogeneous, and reports that full pooling is adequate in the other, where they are not.
