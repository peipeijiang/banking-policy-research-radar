# A Game of Throngs: How Spotlight Tourism Reshapes Local Economies

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21364v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用《权力的游戏》取景地带来的外生旅游关注冲击，结合行政数据与合成控制法估计其对地方经济的影响，发现旅游活动激增但总就业无净效应，并伴随住房市场过热与人口结构变化。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

2017年7月，位于巴斯克地区的一座朴素小教堂圣胡安·德·加斯特卢加切（San Juan de Gaztelugatxe）以“龙石岛”之名出现在《权力的游戏》中，使这一原本在当地小有名气、旅游活动有限的景点一夜之间成为全球旅游目的地。我们利用这一外生的聚光灯冲击，使用新颖的行政数据集和合成控制法（Synthetic Control）估计其对地方经济的因果效应。我们发现旅游活动急剧增加，远超文献中记录的效应。尽管出现了这一激增，旅游相关部门的就业增长在很大程度上被其他领域的就业损失所抵消，对总就业没有净效应。我们还记录了住房市场过热的迹象，房产交易量和价格均有所上升。我们没有发现该冲击导致当地人口流出的证据。相反，它使境外游客到达量增加了约两倍。综合来看，这些发现表明，旅游活动的大幅增加并不必然转化为广泛的本地经济收益。相反，旅游主导的增长可能伴随显著的部门再配置和住房市场压力，凸显了在评估其更广泛经济效应时不能仅关注旅游活动本身的重要性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In July 2017, San Juan de Gaztelugatxe, a modest chapel in the Basque Country, appeared as "Dragonstone" in Game of Thrones, transforming a locally known site with limited tourism activity into a global tourist destination overnight. We exploit this exogenous spotlight shock to estimate its causal effects on the local economy using a novel administrative dataset and a Synthetic Control approach. We find an abrupt increase in tourism activity, far exceeding the effects documented in the literature. Despite this surge, employment gains in tourism-related sectors were largely offset by losses elsewhere, resulting in no net effect on total employment. We also document signs of housing-market overheating, with increases in both property transactions and prices. We find no evidence that the shock displaced the local population. Instead, it led to a roughly twofold increase in arrivals from abroad. Taken together, these findings suggest that a substantial increase in tourism activity does not necessarily translate into broad-based local economic gains. Instead, tourism-led growth may entail significant sectoral reallocation and housing-market pressures, underscoring the importance of looking beyond tourism activity alone when assessing its wider economic effects.
