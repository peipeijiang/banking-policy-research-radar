<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人们普遍认为，风能和太阳能会增加电价的波动性。本文指出，现有文献混淆了两种不同类型的价格变动：跨交付时间的价格差异与跨交易时间的价格波动。在同一日前拍卖中同时确定的、针对不同刻钟时段交付的电力价格存在差异。我将这种价格离散称为“变异性”（variability），其根源在于电力不可储存。该拍卖结束后，交易在日内市场继续进行，直至不平衡结算。同一交付时段的价格会随着新信息的出现（如天气预报）而变化。这种价格“波动性”（volatility）最终由不确定性引起。本文构建了一个关于日前市场和不平衡市场的简单代数模型，为变异性和波动性提供了形式化定义，允许进行分解，并推导出基本驱动因素——不确定性和不可储存性。随后，本文提供了德国过去25年价格变异性和波动性的实证数据，在此期间，风能和太阳能的发电占比从零增长到近50%。波动性通过不平衡价格、日内价格指数和日内交易数据进行衡量。变异性呈U形轨迹，从2000年代到2010年代下降，但此后持续上升。相比之下，波动性则随时间推移而下降。在24个欧洲竞价区域中，几乎所有区域的变异性自2010年代末以来均有所上升，且随太阳能发电占比的增加而增加。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

It is widely held that wind and solar power increase the volatility of electricity prices. This paper argues that the literature conflates two distinct types of price variation: across delivery time and across trading time. Determined simultaneously in the same day-ahead auction, prices for power to be delivered in distinct quarter-hours differ. This price dispersion, which I call "variability", is rooted in non-storability. After that auction, trading continues in intraday markets until imbalance settlement. The price for the same delivery period changes as new information arises, e.g., weather forecasts. Such price "volatility" is ultimately caused by uncertainty. This paper develops a simple algebraic model of day-ahead and imbalance markets that provides a formal definition of variability and volatility, allows for a decomposition and derives the fundamental drivers, uncertainty and non-storability. It then provides empirical data on price variability and volatility for the past 25 years in Germany, during which the share of wind and solar energy increased from zero to nearly 50 % of generation. Volatility is measured with imbalance prices, intraday price indices and intraday transaction data. Variability followed a U-shaped trajectory, falling from the 2000s to the 2010s but increasing since then. Volatility, in contrast, declined over time. Across 24 European bidding zones, variability has risen since the late 2010s in almost all zones and increases with the share of solar power.
