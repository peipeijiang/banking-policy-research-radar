# Higher-Order Marginal Likelihood Approximation for Nonlinear State Space Models

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

非线性状态空间模型的贝叶斯比较需要对不断增长的潜在状态路径和低维参数向量进行积分。我们提出一种边际似然近似方法：先用数值或基于模拟的评估器将状态积分掉，再对静态参数上的积分施加二阶校正的拉普拉斯近似（Laplace approximation）。该校正通过完全数据对数密度的平滑累积量（smoothing cumulants）表达，并在导数增长与后验集中条件下，将对数边际似然误差从 $O(T^{-1})$ 降至 $O(T^{-2})$。在随机波动率实验中，该方法以远低于基于模拟的估计器的成本消除了 64\% 至 99\% 的拉普拉斯误差。应用于双变量汇率波动率模型以及一个包含 $T = 19{,}266$ 个五分钟股票收益率的样本时，发现对重尾观测和暂时性波动率因子的支持最强。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Bayesian comparison of nonlinear state space models requires integrating over a growing latent state path and a low-dimensional parameter vector. We propose a marginal likelihood approximation that integrates out the states with a numerical or simulation-based evaluator and applies a second-order corrected Laplace approximation to the integral over the static parameters. The correction is expressed through smoothing cumulants of the complete-data log density and, under derivative-growth and posterior-concentration conditions, reduces the log marginal likelihood error from $O(T^{-1})$ to $O(T^{-2})$. In stochastic volatility experiments, it removes 64\% to 99\% of the Laplace error at far lower cost than simulation-based estimators. Applications to bivariate exchange-rate volatility models and to a sample of $T = 19{,}266$ five-minute stock returns find the strongest support for heavy-tailed observations and for a transitory volatility factor.

</details>
