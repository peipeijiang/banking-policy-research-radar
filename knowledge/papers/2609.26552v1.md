<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

指数二阶期望效用（Exponential Second-Order Expected Utility, SOEU）是处理模型不确定性的熵方法的理论基础。本文探讨该函数形式在何种意义上具有本质性。在错误设定稳健的平滑模糊性准则中，令单一参数同时控制模型层面的稳健性和跨模型的模糊厌恶聚合（Cerreia-Vioglio, Hansen, Maccheroni, and Marinacci, 2026）。对于每个紧模型集和每个二阶先验，该两层准则等于指数SOEU，其中贝叶斯预测测度作为基准。主要结果是逆命题。在聚合器方面，匹配的曲率是\emph{必要的}：在固定曲率下，没有其他连续、严格递增的聚合器能够实现该归约；而在曲率不匹配时，存在一个模型集和一个先验，使得在任何曲率和任何基准下，没有任何单层熵值能够复现该准则。在成本方面，在幂散度族（包含卡方散度和反向Kullback--Leibler（KL）散度）中，只有KL具有对数-求和-指数形式的对偶，而其他成员在第三累积量处首次偏离该形式。最后，与指数SOEU对偶的值是一个稳健控制值：它由理性疏忽（Rational Inattention）所驱动，但关于固定行为的任何贝叶斯合理的信息获取问题都无法生成它。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Exponential Second-Order Expected Utility (SOEU) underlies the entropic approach to model uncertainty. This paper explores in what sense that functional form is essential. In the misspecification-robust Smooth Ambiguity criterion, let a single parameter govern both the model-level robustness and the ambiguity-averse aggregation across models Cerreia-Vioglio, Hansen, Maccheroni, and Marinacci (2026). The two-layer criterion then equals Exponential SOEU for every compact set of models and every second-order prior, with the Bayesian predictive measure as the baseline. The main results are converses. On the aggregator side, matched curvature is \emph{necessary}: at a fixed curvature no other continuous, strictly increasing aggregator delivers the reduction, and with mismatched curvature there are a model set and a prior for which no single-layer entropic value, at any curvature and any baseline, reproduces the criterion. On the cost side, within the power-divergence family, which contains chi-squared and reverse Kullback--Leibler (KL), only KL has a dual of the log-sum-exp form, and the other members first depart from it at the third cumulant. Finally, the value dual to Exponential SOEU is a robust-control value: it is motivated by Rational Inattention, but no Bayes-plausible information-acquisition problem about a fixed act generates it.
