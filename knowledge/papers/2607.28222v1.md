<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究人工智能自动化能否通过降低信息收集过程中的方差来改善组织结果。我们开展了一项大规模自然实地实验，将70,000名求职者随机分配至由人类招聘人员或AI语音代理进行面试。在两种条件下，均由人类招聘人员评估面试并做出录用决定。由AI代理面试的求职者获得工作邀请的可能性高出12%，且这些收益转化为更高的入职率和员工留存率，同时被录用员工的生产力并未下降。对面试记录的分析显示，AI语音代理实现了受控方差：其面试更具结构性和一致性，同时仍能对个体求职者做出回应，这与收集到更多与招聘相关的信息相关联。这些结果表明，利用AI自动化信息收集可通过标准化提升决策质量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies whether AI automation can improve organizational outcomes by reducing variance when collecting information. We conducted a large-scale natural field experiment in which 70,000 job applicants were randomly assigned to be interviewed by human recruiters or AI voice agents. In both conditions, human recruiters evaluate the interviews and make hiring decisions. Applicants interviewed by AI agents are 12% more likely to receive job offers, and these gains translate into higher job starts and worker retention, with no decline in the productivity of hired workers. Analyzing interview transcripts reveals that AI voice agents achieve controlled variance: their interviews are more structured and consistent while remaining responsive to individual applicants, which is associated with more hiring-relevant information collected. These results demonstrate that automating information collection with AI can enhance decision quality through standardization.
