# Central Limit Theorems for Optimal Transport Functionals

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们建立了关于二次最优传输映射和势函数的标量泛函的经验插入估计量的中心极限定理，其中包括 Wasserstein 代价以及由基于传输的多元分位数构造的若干风险度量。我们研究单样本问题，其中参考分布已知，且观测值来自目标分布的独立同分布样本。一种自然的方法是将经验传输映射的极限定理与泛函 delta 方法相结合。然而我们证明，在维数至少为二时，经验传输映射误差在任何归一化尺度下于 $L^2$ 中都不存在非零弱极限。相反，我们直接对目标分布中的泛函进行线性化，得到影响函数展开，其余项由传输映射误差控制。影响函数由一个线性椭圆偏微分方程刻画。在正则性条件下，我们利用该展开在低维情形下证明中心极限定理，并建立经验 bootstrap 的有效性。我们还推导了半参数效率界，并证明插入估计量在相同条件下达到这些界。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We establish central limit theorems for empirical plug-in estimators of scalar functionals of quadratic optimal transport maps and potentials, including Wasserstein costs and several risk measures constructed from transport-based multivariate quantiles. We study the one-sample problem with a known reference distribution and i.i.d. observations from the target distribution. A natural approach is to combine a limit theorem for the empirical transport map with the functional delta method. We show, however, that in dimensions at least two, empirical transport-map errors admit no nonzero weak limit in $L^2$ under any normalizing scale. Instead, we linearize the functional directly in the target distribution, obtaining an influence-function expansion with a remainder controlled by the transport-map error. The influence function is characterized by a linear elliptic partial differential equation. Under regularity conditions, we use this expansion to prove central limit theorems in low dimensions and establish validity of the empirical bootstrap. We also derive semiparametric efficiency bounds and show that the plug-in estimators attain them under the same conditions.
