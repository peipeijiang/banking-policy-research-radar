<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

大语言模型（LLMs）使得生成合成调查受访者日益可行，但其经验有用性无法仅凭单一的准确性概念来评判。合成样本可能在受访者层面表现较弱，但仍能保留与社会学分析相关的分布性或实质性关系。我们开发了一个依赖于用途的多维验证框架，区分预测效度、分布效度和实质性效度，并利用关于人工智能态度的欧洲晴雨表数据加以说明。四个现成的大语言模型根据观测到的受访者画像生成回答，而监督机器学习则为解释其表现提供了预测性参照。结果揭示了不同验证维度之间的显著差异：一些大语言模型生成的样本尽管在受访者层面的一致性不完美，但仍保留了有意义的边际异质性、人口学排序、跨国差异和预测变量结构。表现因模型和结果变量而异，但总体图景是谨慎建设性的。因此，合成样本应依据其预期用途所需的经验属性进行评估。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Large language models (LLMs) make it increasingly feasible to generate synthetic survey respondents, but their empirical usefulness cannot be judged by a single notion of accuracy. A synthetic sample may perform weakly at the respondent level while still preserving distributional or substantive relationships relevant to social-science analysis. We develop a use-dependent, multi-dimensional validation framework distinguishing predictive, distributional, and substantive validity, and illustrate it using Eurobarometer data on attitudes toward artificial intelligence. Four off-the-shelf LLMs generate responses conditional on observed respondent profiles, while supervised machine learning provides a predictive reference for interpreting their performance. The results reveal substantial differences across validation dimensions: some LLM-generated samples preserve meaningful marginal heterogeneity, demographic ordering, cross-country variation, and predictor structure despite imperfect respondent-level correspondence. Performance varies across models and outcomes, but the broader picture is cautiously constructive. Synthetic samples should therefore be evaluated against the empirical properties required by their intended use.
