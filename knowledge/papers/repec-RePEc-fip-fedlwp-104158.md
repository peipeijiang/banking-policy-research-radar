# Doing Aggregative Economics with Disaggregate Data

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

应用经济学家使用子总量变异来估计宏观经济效应，而未检验这些关系是否具有一致性加总的性质。我们构建了一个加总一致的暴露框架。对于线性模型，两个要求唯一地导出了一个双斜率暴露设定：联盟响应必须与联盟内部处理分配呈反向变动关系，且对联盟平均处理单位增加量的响应必须随联盟规模线性变化。传统的时间固定效应回归识别的是相对暴露斜率；加总暴露斜率则捕捉对共同暴露增加的响应。将该框架应用于碳税、各州对石油供给冲击的暴露以及联邦所得税冲击，结果表明相对效应与加总效应可能存在显著差异。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Applied economists use subaggregate variation to estimate macroeconomic effects without establishing whether relationships aggregate consistently. We develop an aggregation-consistent exposure framework. For linear models, two requirements uniquely yield a two-slope exposure specification: coalition responses must be in variant to within-coalition treatment allocation, and the response to a unit increase in a coalition’s average treatment must vary linearly with coalition size. Conventional time fixed-effects regressions identify the relative-exposure slope; the aggregate-exposure slope captures responses to a common exposure increase. Applications to carbon taxes, state exposure to oil supply shocks, and federal income-tax shocks show that relative and aggregate effects can differ substantially.
