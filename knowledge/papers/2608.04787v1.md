<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

成功的合作可能会摧毁识别其自身失败所需的实践。我研究一个不断发展的社会，其中一种通用技术提高了产出，同时也增加了掠夺性体制的风险，而一种由文化维系的外部实践则提供了纠正所需的反事实与运作能力。核心对象是以崩溃风险时间衡量的累积性实践侵蚀。若侵蚀对规模的敏感度低于崩溃，则预期纠正是有限的；若其敏感度更高，则平均延迟是无限的；而弹性相等时则产生一个精确的系数边界。然而，除非政治消除了外部实践，否则纠正几乎必然最终发生。

同一机制造成了大众—份额之间的楔子：增长可以增加怀疑群体的人数和诊断证据，而怀疑者的政治份额却趋于消失。于是，任何固定的非一致同意的人口阈值都会在有限时间内被跨越。文化是一种认知资本的存量，而非不可替代的意识形态。一个充分资助的试点项目若能维持完整的外部实践，便可复制该实践，但同时也产生了一项资本化的执行与重建负债。在外部承诺下，一笔滚动履约保证金在项目被取消时被没收，用以资助该负债，从而阻止机会主义的废除，同时允许那些价值足以替代所失去实验的改革得以进行。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Successful cooperation can destroy the practice needed to recognize its own failure. I study a growing society in which a common technology raises output and the hazard of an appropriative regime, while a culturally sustained outside practice supplies the counterfactual and operating capacity needed for correction. The key object is cumulative practice erosion measured in breakdown-hazard time. If erosion is less scale-sensitive than breakdown, expected correction is finite; if it is more sensitive, the mean delay is infinite; and equal elasticities produce an exact coefficient boundary. Correction nevertheless remains almost surely eventual unless politics eliminates the outside practice.
  The same mechanism creates a mass--share wedge: growth can increase skeptical mass and diagnostic evidence while the skeptics' political share vanishes. Every fixed nonunanimous population threshold is then crossed in finite time. Culture is a stock of epistemic capital rather than an irreplaceable ideology. A fully funded pilot that maintains the complete outside practice can replicate it, but creates a capitalized enforcement and rebuilding liability. Under external commitment, a rolling performance bond forfeited upon cancellation funds that liability, blocking opportunistic repeal while permitting reforms valuable enough to replace the lost experiment.
