<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

更高的永久劳动收入（PLI）不平等会提高储蓄，因为高PLI家庭储蓄更多；资产价格必须随之调整。我们研究这如何反过来影响财富不平等。如果储蓄为资本融资，利率下降、工资上升，从而涓滴惠及穷人。如果储蓄推高资产价格，资本利得则归于富人。在一个具有非位似财富偏好和不完全竞争的异质性主体模型中，加成定价削弱了第一条渠道，并使第二条渠道在转型过程中占据主导。在我们的美国校准中，这些价格效应在1970—2020年间将前0.1%人群的平均财富提高了9%，将平均财富提高了0.2%。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Higher permanent labor income (PLI) inequality raises saving, since high-PLI households save more; asset prices must adjust. We ask how this feeds back on wealth inequality. If the saving finances capital, interest rates fall and wages rise, trickling down to the poor. If it bids up asset prices, capital gains accrue to the rich. In a heterogeneous-agent model with non-homothetic wealth preferences and imperfect competition, markups weaken the first channel and let the second dominate along the transition. In our U.S. calibration, these price effects raised the average wealth of the top 0.1% by 9% over 1970-2020, and average wealth by 0.2%.

</details>
