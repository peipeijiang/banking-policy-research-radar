<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

具有共同目标和实施日期的政策可能在细节或背景上有所不同。我们将总体平均处理效应（ATT）与按实施队列、辖区、时期或政策类型定义的子总体ATT区分开来。UN-DID和DID-INT是两种构建辖区-时间效应的双重差分估计量，它们在与聚合相匹配的平行趋势假设下估计这些ATT。在CPS安慰剂法模拟中，随机化推断通常具有良好的大小控制，尽管某些辖区特定检验偏保守。刀切法对于子总体ATT可能未定义；当有定义时，在少数处理组或对照组辖区的情况下会过度拒绝。估计目标与推断方法应与政策问题和实施背景相匹配。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Policies with a common objective and implementation date may differ in details or context. We distinguish the aggregate average treatment effect on the treated (ATT) from sub-aggregate ATTs defined by implementation cohort, jurisdiction, period, or policy type. UN-DID and DID-INT, two DiD estimators that construct jurisdiction-by-time effects, estimate these ATTs under parallel-trends conditions matched to the aggregation. In CPS placebo-law simulations, randomization inference is generally well-sized, though some jurisdiction-specific tests are conservative. The jackknife can be undefined for sub-aggregate ATTs; when defined, it over-rejects with few treated or comparison jurisdictions. Estimands and inference methods should match the policy question and implementation setting.

</details>
