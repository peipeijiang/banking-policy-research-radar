<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

AI智能体（AI agents）从委托人（principals）的选择中学习委托人的偏好。我们提出了一个标准，用以判断委托人在AI可能持有的每一种信念下的决策是否已构成AI所需的全部信息。对于行动而言，确实如此。谨慎的行动——例如将决策权交给人类——通常会将委托人的行动划分为若干区块；而对于选择无成本证据而言，当同一证据在每个区块中都是最优时，决策即已充分。否则，AI会按照其委托人的方式行动，却可能收集到错误的证据，而更强的能力会将这一错误转化为不正确的行动。两种错误之间的选择揭示了决策所遗漏的信息。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

AI agents learn what principals want from what principals choose. We offer a criterion for when a principal's decisions, at every belief an AI might hold, are all the AI needs. For acting, they are. Cautious actions, such as deferring to a person, typically divide the principal's actions into blocks, and for choosing costless evidence, decisions are enough exactly when the same evidence is best in every block. Otherwise, the AI acts as its principal would yet can gather the wrong evidence, and greater capability can turn that error into incorrect actions. Choices between two mistakes reveal what decisions miss.

</details>
