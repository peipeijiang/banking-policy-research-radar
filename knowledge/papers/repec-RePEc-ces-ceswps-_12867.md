<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

健康预期寿命传统上被衡量为一种人口统计指标，即对同一年龄的所有个体赋予相同的预期健康剩余寿命。我们通过将预测的健康轨迹与基于个体观察到的虚弱程度的生存概率相结合，将健康预期寿命发展为一个个体层面的概念。利用健康与退休研究的纵向数据，我们表明，在控制个体永久异质性后，虚弱指数的对数随年龄近似线性增长，这意味着缺陷积累呈指数规律。结合按性别区分的冈珀茨死亡率模型，这一规律为计算个体健康预期寿命提供了一个简洁的框架。由此得到的指标揭示出，同一年龄个体之间的健康剩余寿命存在显著异质性。尽管性别、教育、地理以及种族和民族与系统性差异相关，但大部分变异发生在这些群体内部，而非群体之间。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Healthy life expectancy is traditionally measured as a population statistic that assigns the same expected healthy remaining lifetime to all individuals of the same age. We develop healthy life expectancy as an individual concept by combining projected health trajectories with survival probabilities conditional on an individual's observed frailty. Using longitudinal data from the Health and Retirement Study, we show that the logarithm of the frailty index increases approximately linearly with age after controlling for permanent individual heterogeneity, implying an exponential law of deficit accumulation. Together with a sex-specific Gompertz mortality model, this regularity provides a simple framework for computing individual healthy life expectancy. The resulting measure reveals substantial heterogeneity in healthy remaining lifetime among individuals of the same age. Although sex, education, geography, and race and ethnicity are associated with systematic differences, most variation occurs within rather than between these groups.

</details>
