<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

远程办公是数字基础设施所支撑的一种常见工作场所灵活性实践，但其对企业人才构成与组织结构的影响仍未得到充分探讨。这一问题对于初创企业尤为重要，因为初创企业往往在通过本地劳动力市场获取人才方面面临约束。我们聚焦于技能互补性——这一对初创企业早期发展至关重要的人才构成维度。通过整合领英（LinkedIn）职位发布、员工技能与员工流动数据，我们研究了远程办公对初创企业技能互补性的影响。研究发现，较高的远程办公强度能够提升企业层面的技能互补性，其主要机制在于帮助初创企业招聘到技能更契合企业用人需求、且与现有员工技能组合更具差异化的新员工。我们进一步表明，这种劳动力构成的转变会带来组织层面的后果：远程办公通过提升技能互补性，与更深的组织层级结构相关联。这些发现表明，对于初创企业而言，远程办公不仅是一种灵活性政策，更是一种招聘机制，帮助年轻企业在资源约束下组建互补性人才团队。与此同时，这可能推动初创企业走向更加层级化的组织结构，以正式化的协调机制有效实现更具互补性劳动力队伍的价值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Remote work is a common practice of workplace flexibility enabled by digital infrastructure, yet its implications for firms' talent composition and organizational structure remain underexplored. This question is especially important for startups, which often operate with constraints in accessing talent through local labor markets. We focus on skill complementarity, a dimension of talent composition that is critical to startups' early-stage development. By integrating LinkedIn job postings, worker skills, and worker mobility data, we study the impact of remote work on skill complementarity in startups. We find that higher remote work intensity increases firm-level skill complementarity, primarily by helping startups recruit new workers whose skills better match the firms' hiring needs and diversify from the skill sets of existing employees. We further show that this shift in workforce composition carries organizational consequences: remote work is associated with greater organizational hierarchy through increased skill complementarity. These findings suggest that, for startups, remote work is not merely a flexibility policy but a hiring mechanism that helps young firms assemble complementary talent despite resource constraints. At the same time, this may push startups toward more hierarchical structures, which serve as formalized coordination mechanisms to effectively achieve the value of a more complementary workforce.
