# From Commercial Banking to Rural Banking: Regulatory Challenges and Legal Consequences in Indonesia’s Banking Sector

[查看原文](https://doi.org/10.30649/ph.v26i2.656)

## 一句话结论

> 论文采用规范法学方法，分析印度尼西亚传统商业银行变更为农村信贷银行（BPR）所引发的监管挑战与法律后果，包括业务运营限制和法人实体变更。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

常规商业银行的增资旨在强化资本并巩固银行业。所采用的研究方法为规范法学方法，采取的研究路径为立法路径与概念路径。研究结果表明，常规商业银行变更为农村信用银行，其营业执照的变更也将带来业务经营方面的变化，包括对活期存款（giro deposits）、参与清算、保险业务及外汇等活动的限制/禁止。其法律实体由开放型转变为封闭型，可中止农村银行（Rural Banks）所不允许的业务活动。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The capital increase for conventional commercial banks aims to strengthen capital and consolidate banking. The research method used is normative juridical, and the approaches taken are legislative and conceptual approaches. The research results indicate that the change of license from a conventional commercial bank to a rural credit bank will also bring changes in business operations, including restrictions/prohibitions on activities such as giro deposits, participating in clearing, insurance activities, and foreign exchange. The change in its legal entity from being open to closed can halt business activities that are not permitted for Rural Banks.
