<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一个拟合良好的理论之所以预测得好，可能是因为其所承诺的结构，也可能是因为它是在被评估的数据上拟合的。我们提出一些度量方法，将二者区分开来，其做法是将理论操作化为一个函数形式、一个关于行为的支撑限制以及一个参数分布，且不针对评估样本拟合任何参数。概率完备性是指从理论中抽取的规则在样本外所捕获的可预测变异的份额。概率限制性是指此类规则排除了多少可想象的行为；它可分解为函数形式所能达到的范围以及参数分布在该范围上的集中程度。参数分布可以被解释为关于代表性主体的不确定性，也可以被解释为异质性主体之间参数的分布，而这些度量通过评分规则、基线和上限的选择来适应这两种解释。一个范围层面对理论所主张的领域进行定价，并跨领域比较理论。我们将这些度量应用于期望效用、累积前景理论和失望厌恶在二元彩票确定性等价上的表现。函数形式几乎不排除任何可想象的行为，而文献中的参数分布则排除了这些形式所留出的大部分空间。从文献先验中抽取的规则保留了拟合前景理论三分之二的完备性、期望效用的大部分完备性，以及失望厌恶的零完备性。一旦各理论以其文献先验来持有，函数形式所呈现的灵活性—拟合权衡便消失了。若将其解读为总体，失望厌恶和期望效用无法产生受试者所表现出的离散程度，且文献先验在损失情形下的定位不如在收益情形下那样好。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A fitted theory that predicts well may do so because of the structure it commits to or because it was fitted to the data it is evaluated on. We propose measures that separate the two by operationalising a theory as a functional form, a support restriction on behavior and a distribution over the parameters, with no parameter fitted to the evaluation sample. Probabilistic completeness is the share of the predictable variation that a rule drawn from the theory captures out of sample. Probabilistic restrictiveness is how much conceivable behavior such a rule excludes; it separates into what the functional form can reach and how the parameter distribution concentrates on it. The parameter distribution can be interpreted as uncertainty about a representative agent or as the distribution of parameters across heterogeneous agents, and the measures adapt to both interpretations through the choice of scoring rule, baseline and ceiling. A scope layer prices the domains a theory claims and compares theories across domains. We apply the measures to expected utility, cumulative prospect theory and disappointment aversion on certainty equivalents for binary lotteries. The functional forms exclude little conceivable behavior, while the literature's parameter distributions exclude most of what the forms leave open. A rule drawn from a literature-informed prior keeps two thirds of fitted prospect theory's completeness, most of expected utility's and none of disappointment aversion's. The flexibility-fit trade-off found for functional forms disappears once the theories are held with their literature priors. Read as populations, disappointment aversion and expected utility cannot produce the dispersion the subjects show, and the literature priors are less well placed for losses than for gains.
