<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们建立了在高频率、长跨度采样下，对离散观测的多变量遍历扩散过程的精确最大似然估计量（MLE）的渐近理论，解决了 \citet{YCW2019} 和 \citet{Li2013} 提出的一个开放问题。漂移项可以线性增长，波动率有正下界，并且要么有界，要么与 $1+|x|$ 成正比。当采样区间多项式衰减时，精确 MLE 是一致的、渐近正态且有效的，漂移参数的收敛速率为 $\sqrt{nΔ_n}$，扩散参数的收敛速率为 $\sqrt n$。在关于遍历平均的附加条件下，并且当时间跨度 $nΔ_n$ 也多项式增长时，其矩也收敛。证明从未直接分析精确似然：它表明，一个足够高阶的闭式密度展开在每个观测转移处按相对意义一致精确，使得两个对数似然在参数上一致地相差 $o_P(1)$。同样的论证给出了近似似然与精确似然渐近等价的精度条件；特别地，Yang、Chen 和 Wan 的近似 MLE 收敛到真实参数。对于固定阶数 $K\ge1$ 的 delta 展开估计量，我们显式推导了主导偏差。它表明，当偏差常数非零时，$nΔ_n^{K+1}\to0$ 不能减弱，并且当 $K$ 为偶数时偏差落在漂移参数上，当 $K$ 为奇数时偏差落在扩散参数上。蒙特卡罗实验证实了预测的偏差。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We establish the asymptotic theory of the exact maximum likelihood estimator (MLE) for discretely observed multivariate ergodic diffusions under high-frequency, long-span sampling, solving an open problem posed by \citet{YCW2019} and \citet{Li2013}. The drift may grow linearly, and the volatility is bounded away from zero and either bounded or proportional to $1+|x|$. When the sampling interval decays polynomially, the exact MLE is consistent, asymptotically normal and efficient, with rates $\sqrt{nΔ_n}$ for drift and $\sqrt n$ for diffusion parameters. Under an additional condition on ergodic averages, and when the time span $nΔ_n$ also grows polynomially, its moments converge as well. The proof never analyzes the exact likelihood directly: it shows that a closed-form density expansion of sufficiently high order is uniformly accurate in relative terms at every observed transition, so that the two log-likelihoods differ by $o_P(1)$ uniformly in the parameter. The same argument yields accuracy conditions under which an approximate likelihood is asymptotically equivalent to the exact one; in particular, the approximate MLE of Yang, Chen and Wan converges to the true parameter. For the delta-expansion estimator of fixed order $K\ge1$, we derive the leading bias explicitly. It shows that $nΔ_n^{K+1}\to0$ cannot be weakened when the bias constant is nonzero, and that the bias falls on the drift parameters for even $K$ and on the diffusion parameters for odd $K$. Monte Carlo experiments confirm the predicted biases.
