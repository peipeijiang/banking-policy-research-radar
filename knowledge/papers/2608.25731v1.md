<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

不可知序贯均衡（agnostic sequential equilibrium, ASE）是对序贯均衡的一种精炼，它不强迫博弈者接受单一、任意的信念系统。此外，序贯均衡假定博弈者的信念是完全一致的（这一概念基于策略的扰动），而ASE则采用了一种新颖、更简单且局部的强一致性概念，用于连接策略组合与均衡外信念，该概念适用于一大类动态博弈，包括具有连续行动空间的博弈。在这一点上，新的解概念类似于完美贝叶斯均衡。研究表明，在具有完美回忆的不完美信息扩展式博弈中，一个策略组合是ASE，当且仅当它在每一个完全一致的信念系统下都是序贯均衡。ASE被集值解概念——不可知序贯多均衡（agnostic sequential polyequilibrium）——所推广，后者允许在某些信息集中对博弈者的行动部分或完全不作规定。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Agnostic sequential equilibrium (ASE) is a refinement of sequential equilibrium that does not force on the players a single, arbitrary belief system. In addition, whereas sequential equilibrium assumes the players' beliefs to be fully consistent (a notion that is based on perturbations of strategies), ASE employs a novel, simpler and local concept of strong consistency between strategy profiles and off-equilibrium beliefs, which is applicable to a large class of dynamic games, including games with a continuum of actions. In the last respect, the new solution concept is similar to perfect Bayesian equilibrium. It is shown that a strategy profile in an imperfect-information extensive-form game with perfect recall is an ASE precisely when it is a sequential equilibrium with every fully consistent belief system. ASE is generalized by the set-valued solution concept of agnostic sequential polyequilibrium, which allows leaving the players' actions in some information sets partially or completely unspecified.

</details>
