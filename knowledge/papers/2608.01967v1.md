<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Monderer和Shapley（1996）的研究表明，一个博弈是精确势博弈，当且仅当参与者的交叉差分两两一致，这是关于任意两个参与者的激励如何相互锁定的一种对称条件。本文探讨的是，当对称性失效时，能够从这些特征中构建出什么。由此得到的MS-势是基于表示博弈共同利益元素的二阶差分构建的。MS-势在可分离支付项的意义下是唯一的，并且它恰好在一个高阶MS-条件成立时存在。在精确势博弈的类别中，它能够恢复出除去参与者个体主效应之外的势。随后，一种最小二乘构造将MS-势推广到所有有限博弈的类别。该构造引出了MS-分解：每个有限博弈都可以分解为一个共同利益的MS-势博弈和一个吸收所有参与者个体支付的残差项。
本文的核心结果是一个恒等式：对于所有参与者拥有相同数量策略的博弈，MS-势的一个扩展形式——在加法常数意义下——与Candogan、Menache、Ozdaglar和Parrilo（2011）提出的势完全一致。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Monderer and Shapley (1996) showed that a game is an exact potential game exactly when the players' cross-differences agree pair by pair, a symmetry condition on how any two players' incentives interlock. This paper asks what can be built from these characteristics when symmetry fails. The resulting MS-potential is constructed from the second differences that represent the game's common-interest elements. The MS-potential is unique up to separable payoff terms and it exists precisely when a higher-order MS-condition holds. On the class of exact potential games it recovers the potential up to the players' individualistic main effects. A least-squares construction subsequently extends the MS-potential to the class of all finite games. The construction induces the MS-decomposition: every finite game splits into a common-interest MS-potential game and a residual that absorbs every player's individualistic payoffs.
  The paper's central result is an identity: for games in which all players have equally many actions, an augmentation of the MS-potential coincides --- up to the additive constant --- with the potential of Candogan, Menache, Ozdaglar and Parrilo (2011).

</details>
