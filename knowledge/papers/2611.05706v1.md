<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Hopfield神经网络与非合作博弈为理解集体结果如何从局部交互中涌现提供了看似不同的途径。在神经网络中，个体单元根据局部信号调整其状态；在博弈中，参与者选择行动以最大化个体收益。我建立了对称二值Hopfield网络与允许二次势函数的二值行动博弈之间的精确等价关系。该等价关系将稳定的网络构型与纯策略Nash均衡相对应，并将异步神经更新与最优反应动态相对应。更广泛地说，神经网络中记忆的存储与提取分别对应于博弈中均衡的设计与选择。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Hopfield neural networks and noncooperative games provide seemingly different approaches to understanding how collective outcomes emerge from local interactions. In a neural network, individual units adjust their states according to local signals; in a game, players choose actions to maximise individual payoffs. I establish an exact equivalence between symmetric binary Hopfield networks and binary-action games that admit a quadratic potential. The equivalence identifies stable network configurations with pure-strategy Nash equilibria, and asynchronous neural updating with best-response dynamics. More broadly, the storage and retrieval of memories in neural networks correspond, respectively, to the design and selection of equilibria in games.
