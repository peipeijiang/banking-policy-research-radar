# Minimally rational reallocation of objects

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28174v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文证明在对象再分配问题中，个体理性、配对理性和策略防伪性可以刻画最高交易循环（TTC），并且三个弱理性公理加上策略防伪性唯一决定TTC；当交换循环规模有上限时，弱理性要求会排除策略防伪实施。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

匹配理论的发展主要围绕两条经典规则：婚姻问题中的延迟接受（DA）与物品再分配问题中的最高交易循环（TTC）。尽管这两条规则通过不同的程序运作，我们证明它们建立在共同的公理化基础之上。在婚姻问题中，稳定性分解为个体理性与配对理性，而一个经典结果通过这两条公理连同提议方的策略防伪性刻画了DA。我们证明，个体理性、配对理性与策略防伪性同样刻画了TTC。更强地，关于个体与配对的三个弱理性公理，连同策略防伪性，唯一地确定了TTC。因此，即使是对交换循环规模设有上限时的弱理性要求，也会排除策略防伪的实现。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Matching theory has largely evolved around two canonical rules: deferred acceptance (DA) in the marriage problem and top trading cycles (TTC) in the object reallocation problem. Although the two rules operate through different procedures, we show that they rest on a common axiomatic foundation. In the marriage problem, stability decomposes into individual rationality and pair rationality, and a classic result characterizes DA by these two axioms together with strategy-proofness for the proposing side. We show that individual rationality, pair rationality, and strategy-proofness likewise characterize TTC. More strongly, three weak rationality axioms concerning individuals and pairs, together with strategy-proofness, uniquely determine TTC. Even weak rationality requirements therefore rule out strategy-proof implementation when there is a cap on the size of exchange cycles.
