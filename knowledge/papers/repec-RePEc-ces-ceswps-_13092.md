# When Parents Stay Home: Work-from-Home and Sorting Across Public, Private, and Home Schools

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13092.html)

> 论文利用美国县级远程办公潜力差异构建双重差分，研究COVID-19期间远程办公对公立、私立和家庭学校入学选择的影响，发现公立入学总体无下降但存在城乡异质性，私立入学下降且家庭学校暂时增加，并讨论了对学校财政的启示。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在COVID-19疫情期间，美国公立学校入学率出现了急剧且持续的下降，同时在家上学（homeschooling）人数激增。本文考察了居家办公（WFH）在推动这些变化中的作用。居家办公可能通过更大的灵活性和减少通勤来降低在家上学的成本，但生产力中断以及向私立学校教育的转移可能会抵消这些效应。我们将公立和私立入学人数的全国数据与新汇编的学区级在家上学人数数据相结合。利用地方劳动力市场中远程办公潜力的差异，在双重差分框架下，我们发现公立学校入学人数总体上没有下降，但私立学校入学人数下降，在家上学人数相应出现暂时性增加。公立学校入学人数的总体结果掩盖了异质性：城市公立学校入学人数下降，部分被更偏远地区的增长所抵消。这些入学变化对学校财政至关重要，因为它们直接影响州拨款公式，进而影响地方学区支出。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The U.S. saw a sharp and persistent decline in public school enrollment alongside a surge in homeschooling during the COVID-19 pandemic. This paper examines the role of work-from-home (WFH) in driving these changes. WFH may lower the cost of homeschooling through greater flexibility and reduced commuting, but productivity disruptions and shifts toward private schooling may offset these effects. We combine national data on public and private enrollments with newly assembled district-level homeschooling counts. Using variation in telework potential across local labor markets in a difference-in-differences framework, we find no aggregate decline in public school enrollment, but declines in private enrollment and corresponding temporary increases in homeschooling. The aggregate public school result masks heterogeneity: urban public schools experience declines, partially offset by gains in more remote areas. These enrollment shifts are critical for school finance, as they directly affect state aid formulas and therefore local district spending.

</details>
