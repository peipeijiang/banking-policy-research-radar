<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

保形预测传统上用于量化预测不确定性。我们将这种不确定性用于第二个用途，将75%的保形区间与分数凯利准则相结合，以确定投资组合的头寸规模：当区间变宽时，我们缩减头寸；当区间变窄时，我们增加头寸。在六年的开发窗口期（2016-2021年）内，考虑交易成本和严格的杠杆上限，该策略实现了28.5%的年化净对数增长，夏普比率为1.34，最大回撤为27.7%，而同期标普500指数的收益为15.9%，同等杠杆下的被动投资组合收益为21-22%。

我们在开发窗口期的主要发现与文献中关于时间序列保形预测的建议相悖。每一个使区间更快适应市场条件的调整，都会导致年化增长损失0.7至5.3个百分点；而胜出的方法是最简单的：缓慢、不加权、逐资产的滚动分位数。当区间用于确定头寸规模而非描述单一预测时，宽度的稳定性优于局部的尖锐性。在匹配杠杆下，该方法还比教科书式的标准差方法高出2.1个百分点。我们还实施了一项风险控制：当区间在下行方向的失配频率远超其历史水平时，我们削减杠杆。在开发窗口期内，这一措施将最大回撤从27.7%降至20.3%，同时提高了夏普比率，优于所有40个安慰剂时点（基于秩的p = 1/41）。

这些数字来自一个自主的LLM代理对200种配置的搜索，因此我们封存了2022年及以后的所有数据，并在一次评估之前预先注册了配置、基准和解释规则。校准性得以保持（覆盖率为0.745，目标为0.750，2022年期间最弱）；但增长未能保持：两种配置的年化收益分别为8.5%和7.0%，低于被动基准，而一个预先注册的事后基准在原始增长上超过了它们，但承担了46%的回撤。所有结果均按预先注册的方式报告。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Conformal prediction has traditionally been used to quantify prediction uncertainty. We put that uncertainty to a second use, combining a 75% conformal interval with fractional Kelly to size portfolio positions: as the range widens we shrink the position, and as it narrows we grow it. On a six-year development window (2016-2021), with trading costs and strict leverage caps, this compounds at 28.5% annualised net log growth with a Sharpe ratio of 1.34 and a 27.7% maximum drawdown, versus 15.9% for holding the S&P 500 and 21-22% for passive portfolios at the same leverage.
  Our main development-window finding runs against the literature's advice for conformal prediction on time series. Every tweak that adapts the interval faster to market conditions costs 0.7 to 5.3 points of annual growth; the winner is the simplest method: slow, unweighted, per-asset rolling quantiles. When an interval sizes a position rather than describing one forecast, width stability beats local sharpness. It also beats the textbook standard deviation by 2.1 points at matched leverage. We also implement a risk control: when the intervals miss on the downside far more than their historical rate, we cut leverage. On the development window this cut maximum drawdown from 27.7% to 20.3% while raising the Sharpe ratio, beating all 40 placebo timings (rank-based p = 1/41).
  These numbers came from an autonomous LLM-agent search over 200 configurations, so we sealed all data from 2022 onward and pre-registered configurations, benchmarks, and interpretation rules before one evaluation. Calibration held (0.745 coverage against 0.750, weakest through 2022); growth did not: the two configurations earned 8.5% and 7.0% per year, below the passive benchmarks, and a pre-registered hindsight benchmark beat them on raw growth while taking a 46% drawdown. All outcomes are reported as pre-registered.
