<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在本注记中，我提供了一个直接且自包含的Koray（2000）定理证明，该定理表明：一个中立且一致的社会选择函数是普遍自选择的，当且仅当它是独裁的。Koray（2000）的原始证明依赖于Sen（1970）和Houthakker（1950）的结果，为每个备选集合构造一个社会福利函数。他随后证明这些函数满足帕累托原则且独立于无关备选项，从而使他能够应用Arrow（1950，1951）的不可能性定理。相反，我构造了一个循环偏好剖面，并证明将所选备选项排在首位的投票者在每个有限非空备选集合上都是独裁者。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this note, I provide a direct and self-contained proof of Koray's (2000) theorem, by which a neutral and unanimous social choice function is universally self-selective if and only if it is dictatorial. Koray's (2000) original proof relies on results by Sen (1970) and Houthakker (1950) to construct a social welfare function for each alternative set. He then shows that these functions are Paretian and independent of irrelevant alternatives, allowing him to apply Arrow's (1950, 1951) impossibility theorem. Instead, I construct a cyclic preference profile and show that the voter who ranks the chosen alternative first is the dictator on every finite nonempty alternative set.
