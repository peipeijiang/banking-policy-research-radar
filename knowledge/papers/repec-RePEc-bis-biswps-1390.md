<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

美国官方季度GDP估计始于1947年；更早的季度历史数据必须利用年度基准和历史指标进行重构。为探究这些重构中的不确定性，本文系统比较了文献中用于重构1919—1946年季度GDP的各类设定。该比较在未公开的战后季度上评估预测效果，并将不同设定间的变异与模型内系数不确定性区分开来。重构选择确有影响，但其效应因时期而异。在58种重构设定中，大萧条（Great Depression）估计峰谷降幅的四分位距为30.9%—31.6%，而1937—1938年为6.6%—7.8%。在Balke and Gordon（1986）生产回归方法的基础上，结合Ramey and Zubairy（2018）所使用的Denton分配法（Denton allocation）进行扩展的重构方案，具有最低的预测误差。工业生产提供了关于季度波动的信息，而年度核算约束了由此生成的路径。更丰富的指标面板、额外的支出细节以及更灵活的关系设定，均未能改善该重构方案的预测精度。其估计的1937—1938年降幅为7.07%，比已发表重构方案中约10.5%—10.6%的降幅更为温和。该重构方案与全部六次NBER衰退相匹配，但将第一次收缩的起始时间提前了两个季度，即从1920Q1至1921Q3调整为1919Q3至1921Q1；其峰值是定年窗口所允许的最早时点。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Official quarterly US GDP estimates begin in 1947; earlier quarterly histories must be reconstructed using annual benchmarks and historical indicators. To investigate uncertainty in these reconstructions, this paper systematically compares specifications drawn from the literature for reconstructing quarterly GDP during 1919–1946. The comparison evaluates predictions on concealed postwar quarters and separates variation across specifications from coefficient uncertainty within a model. Reconstruction choices matter, but their effects differ across episodes. Across 58 reconstruction specifications, the interquartile range of estimated peak-to-trough declines is 30.9–31.6% for the Great Depression, compared with 6.6–7.8% for 1937–1938. A reconstruction that augments the production-regression approach of Balke and Gordon (1986) with the Denton allocation used by Ramey and Zubairy (2018) has the lowest prediction error. Industrial production supplies information about quarterly movements, while annual accounts constrain the resulting path. Richer indicator panels, additional expenditure detail and more flexible relation ships do not improve on this reconstruction’s predictive accuracy. Its estimated 7.07% decline in 1937–1938 is milder than the approximately 10.5–10.6% declines in those published reconstructions. The reconstruction matches all six NBER recessions, but dates the first contraction two quarters earlier, from 1919Q3 to 1921Q1 rather than 1920Q1 to 1921Q3; its peak is the earliest allowed by the dating window.
