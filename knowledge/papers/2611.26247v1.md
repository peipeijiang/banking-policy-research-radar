<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

标准索赔问题通常将索赔视为给定条件。本文研究当一项经认证的索赔可以通过一个私下知晓的、验证成本高昂的组成部分加以夸大时，规则选择将如何变化。在二元环境中，同等对待同等者、索赔有界性和效率这三个条件对夸大所得的收益施加了一个严格下界，从而也对使真实报告成为弱占优策略所需的预期审计施加了严格下界。受限均等分配规则（Constrained Equal Awards, CEA）通过一种相关性审计策略，在每一个报告组合下都达到了这一下界。反之，每一个逐组合审计最小化规则都是CEA。这一逐点结果还使CEA成为在真实索赔的任意先验分布下最小化预期验证成本的唯一可容许规则。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Standard claims problems take claims as given. We ask how rule selection changes when a certified claim can be augmented by a privately known component that is costly to verify. In a binary environment, equal treatment of equals, claim boundedness, and efficiency impose a sharp lower bound on the gain from exaggeration and hence on the expected auditing needed for truthful reporting to be weakly dominant. The constrained equal awards (CEA) rule attains this bound at every report profile through a correlated audit policy. Conversely, every profilewise audit-minimal rule is CEA. The pointwise result also makes CEA the unique admissible rule that minimizes expected verification cost under every prior over true claims.
