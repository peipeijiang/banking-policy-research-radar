<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

求职者对再就业的预期日益被用于研究求职行为，但其偏差所揭示的潜在信念与偏好仍不明确。我们结合新的调查数据、结构建模和机器学习，揭示这些预期所包含的信息内容，并展示如何利用它们改进就业支持的精准施策。利用一组与行政记录关联的法国求职者主观预期面板数据，我们表明，再就业预期偏差与求职两大基本要素——工作机会到达率和工资分布——的信念偏差密切相关，并对其进行了概括。这些潜在偏差具有异质性，但彼此高度正相关，因此其对求职行为的影响相互叠加。随后，我们估计了一个包含多种偏差信念来源的结构性求职模型，并表明纠正这些偏差有助于悲观的求职者，但可能削弱乐观求职者的积极性并对其造成损害，这为精准施策提供了依据。最后，我们开发了一种机器学习分层方法，仅凭易于获取的再就业预期即可识别出具有不同偏差信念和行为模式的政策相关群体。这为就业服务机构提供了一种简单的工具，以实施针对性的信息干预。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Job seekers' expectations about reemployment are increasingly used to study job search, but what their biases reveal about underlying beliefs and preferences is ambiguous. We combine new survey data, structural modeling, and machine learning to uncover the informational content of these expectations and show how they can be used to improve the targeting of employment support. Using a new panel of French job seekers' subjective expectations linked to administrative records, we show that reemployment expectation biases are strongly associated with, and summarize, biases in beliefs about the two fundamentals of search, job offer arrival rates and the wage distribution. These underlying biases are heterogeneous but strongly positively correlated, so their effects on search compound. We then estimate a structural job search model with multiple sources of biased beliefs and show that correcting them helps pessimistic job seekers but can demotivate and hurt optimistic ones, providing a rationale for targeting. Finally, we develop a machine-learning stratification that recovers policy-relevant groups, with distinct patterns of biased beliefs and behaviors, from easily elicited reemployment expectations alone. This gives employment services a simple tool to target informational interventions.
