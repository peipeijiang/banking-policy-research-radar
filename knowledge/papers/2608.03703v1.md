# Preying on Leveraged ETFs

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们认为，2026年韩国市场的极端波动性是由套利者利用杠杆交易所交易基金（LETFs）的收盘再平衡机制进行掠夺性交易所致。LETFs必须在收盘时（该收盘价同时用于衡量当日涨跌幅）按照当日走势方向进行交易，因此其需求随价格上涨而增加，而提前买入的套利者通过在成交价上交易放大了订单规模，随后将所制造的涨幅抛售给市场。正如预测所示，盘前美国消息在三星电子和SK海力士产品上市后一天内出现反转，而对照组中则无此现象。在测得的参数下，该循环机制贡献了四十七个百分点的年化波动率，并在八周内从散户持有者手中转移了四万亿韩元。韩国选择的应对方案——将再平衡分散至整个交易时段——只是移动了订单，却未改变决定订单规模的参考价，反而可能提高代价。改变参考价则行之有效：取两次成交价的平均值，可使任一偏移所能制造的涨幅减半。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We argue that the extreme volatility of the Korean market in 2026 was driven by arbitrageurs preying on the closing rebalance of leveraged exchange-traded funds (LETFs). An LETF must trade in the direction of the day's move at a close that also measures it, so its demand rises in price, and arbitrageurs buying ahead of it enlarge the order by trading at the print, then unload into what they manufactured. As predicted, pre-open U.S. news is reversed one day later in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix once their products list, and in no control group. At measured parameters the loop added forty-seven percentage points of annualized volatility and transferred KRW 4 trillion from retail holders in eight weeks. Dispersing the rebalance through the session, Korea's chosen remedy, moves the order but not the reference that sizes it and can raise the toll. Changing the reference works instead: an average of two prints halves what displacing either can manufacture.
