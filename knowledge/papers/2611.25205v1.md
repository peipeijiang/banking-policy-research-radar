<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关于某一策略的好消息未必能转化为演化上的成功。我们研究信息与策略暴露之间的交互如何塑造演化动态与均衡预测。我们从一个简单的修正过程推导出相关的种群动态，在该过程中，个体以状态依赖的速率遇到候选策略，并在接收到有利的收益信号后采纳这些策略。我们区分了信号的单调似然比性质（MLRP）与有效MLRP，后者考虑了相对于种群份额的暴露程度，并决定表现更好的策略是否增长得更快。随机种群相遇保留了MLRP并产生收益单调的演化。当暴露足够有利于稀有策略时，有效MLRP在严格纯纳什均衡附近被违反，使其变得不稳定。相反，当暴露足够有利于流行策略时，有效MLRP在非纳什纯状态附近被违反，使这些状态能够保持局部稳定。因此，即使信号满足MLRP，策略暴露的差异也可能逆转其演化含义并改变均衡预测。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Good news about a strategy need not translate into evolutionary success. We study how the interaction between information and strategy exposure shapes evolutionary dynamics and equilibrium predictions. We derive the associated population dynamic from a simple revision process in which agents encounter candidate strategies at state-dependent rates and adopt them after receiving favorable payoff signals. We distinguish the monotone likelihood ratio property (MLRP) of signals from effective MLRP, which accounts for exposure relative to population shares and determines whether better-performing strategies grow faster. Random population encounters preserve MLRP and generate payoff-monotone evolution. When exposure sufficiently favors rare strategies, effective MLRP is violated near strict pure Nash equilibria, rendering them unstable. Conversely, when exposure sufficiently favors popular strategies, effective MLRP is violated near non-Nash pure states, allowing them to remain locally stable. Thus, even when signals satisfy MLRP, differences in strategy exposure can reverse their evolutionary implications and alter equilibrium predictions.
