<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

政府通过信号传达政策意图。有些信号是清晰的；另一些则存在争议、复杂或刻意模糊。本文考察政策信号的质量是否因地区而异，如果是，又如何不同。我们聚焦中国的气候政策，中央政府通过国家媒体向31个省份传达信息，这些省份在经济结构和制度能力上存在显著差异。我们以中国气候政策不确定性（CCPU）指数作为政策信号模糊性的度量，并运用时变自回归框架测量持续性，发现存在显著的省级异质性。在国家政策辩论中处于核心地位的省份——沿海经济强省和政治中心——持续性更高且更具波动性，而边缘内陆省份则较低且更稳定。大量省份在2020年之后持续性急剧上升，这与新冠疫情（COVID-19）和地缘政治扰动相吻合。研究结果表明，政策信号质量并非全国性的常量，而是由经济和制度背景塑造的地方性现象。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Governments communicate policy intentions through signals. Some signals are clear; others are contested, complex, or deliberately vague. This paper examines whether the quality of policy signals varies across space, and if so, how. We focus on China's climate policy, where the central government communicates through national media to 31 provinces that differ markedly in economic structure and institutional capacity. Using the China Climate Policy Uncertainty (CCPU) index as a measure of policy signal ambiguity, and applying a time-varying autoregressive framework to measure persistence, we find substantial provincial heterogeneity. Persistence is higher and more variable in provinces central to national policy debates - the coastal economic powerhouses and political centres - and lower and more stable in peripheral inland provinces. A large number of provinces show a sharp increase in persistence after 2020, coinciding with the COVID-19 pandemic and geopolitical disruptions. The findings suggest that policy signal quality is not a national constant but a local phenomenon shaped by economic and institutional context.
