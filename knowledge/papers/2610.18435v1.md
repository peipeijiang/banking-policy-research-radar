# How Well Do LLMs Perform in Peer Review? Evidence from Finance and Accounting

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18435v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文让GPT-4o等LLM对2024年AFA和NBER金融会议论文进行同行评审，并与随后两年顶刊发表结果比较，发现模型对正确性和表述的判断能预测发表，而对原创性和重要性的判断无预测力，说明LLM可做技术性核查但无法判断研究贡献。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

审稿人越来越多地使用大语言模型（LLMs），而金融与会计期刊对于是否允许这样做存在分歧。我们探究这些模型能够承担审稿人工作中的哪些部分。GPT-4o的训练数据截止于2024年会议季之前，我们让它对2024年AFA和NBER金融会议上展示的论文进行审稿，并将其评判结果与这些论文随后两年内在三大金融期刊上的发表情况进行比较。在控制作者声誉后，模型评分高出一个标准差的论文发表概率高出44%。这一结果并非由前视偏差所致，因为模型并不能回忆起这些结果。该信息来源于模型对正确性和阐述的评判。其对原创性和重要性的评判则不携带任何信息，而一份强调重要性的审稿指南也无济于事。正确性与贡献之间的这种分化在会计学论文以及更新的模型GPT-5上同样成立。大语言模型可以检查研究是否正确，但无法判断其是否重要。技术性审查可以交给模型，而对贡献的判断则仍应留给人。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Referees increasingly use large language models (LLMs), while finance and accounting journals disagree about whether to allow it. We ask which part of a referee's job the models can do. GPT-4o, whose training data end before the 2024 conference season, referees the papers presented at the 2024 AFA and NBER finance meetings, and we compare its verdicts with publication in the top-three finance journals over the next two years. Papers the model scores one standard deviation higher are 44\% more likely to be published, net of author reputation. The result is not driven by look-ahead bias, as the model does not recall the outcomes. The information comes from its judgements of correctness and exposition. Its judgements of originality and significance carry none, and a referee guide that stresses importance does not help. The split between correctness and contribution holds for accounting papers and for a newer model, GPT-5. LLMs can check whether research is right, but not whether it matters. The technical check can go to the model, while the judgement of contribution stays with people.
