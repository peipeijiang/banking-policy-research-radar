# Manipulation Testing in Boundary Discontinuity Designs

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00350v1)

## 一句话结论

> 提出首个适用于一般边界形状的边界断点回归设计的操纵检验，基于边界附近观测在治疗组和对照组间的平衡性，通过k-means聚类和多项二项检验实现，并验证了渐近有效性和有限样本表现。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了首个针对具有一般边界形状的边界断点设计（BDDs）的操纵检验。BDD是回归断点设计（RDD）的多维扩展，其中处理分配由多维运行变量是否跨越低维边界集决定。该检验避免了多元密度估计，并基于以下观察：在没有操纵的情况下，边界附近的观测值在由其在边界上的投影定义的任意组内，应在处理组和对照组之间大致均匀分布。我们通过使用k均值聚类形成的组，对边界附近的观测值进行一系列二项式平衡检验来验证这一含义。我们在适当的正则条件下建立了该检验的渐近有效性。我们还通过蒙特卡洛模拟评估了有限样本性能，并在三个实证应用中展示了该检验。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose the first manipulation test designed for boundary discontinuity designs (BDDs) with general boundary shapes. A BDD is a multidimensional extension of the regression discontinuity design (RDD) in which treatment assignment is determined by whether the multidimensional running variable crosses a lower-dimensional boundary set. The test avoids multivariate density estimation and builds on the observation that, in the absence of manipulation, observations near the boundary should be approximately evenly split between treatment and control within arbitrary groups defined by their projections onto the boundary. We test this implication using a collection of binomial balance tests on observations near the boundary, with groups formed by k-means clustering. We establish the asymptotic validity of the test under suitable regularity conditions. We also evaluate finite-sample performance through Monte Carlo simulations and illustrate the test in three empirical applications.

</details>
