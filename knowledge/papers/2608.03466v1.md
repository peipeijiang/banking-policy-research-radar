<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们重新审视“均值坍缩”现象，该现象指的是某些温和的结构条件（如局部线性）会迫使定义在有限均值随机变量上的律不变泛函$\ph$仅依赖于其自变量$X$的期望，而不依赖于任何其他分布特征。从凸序的一个简洁刻画出发，我们提出的简化方法统一并推广了现有结果，且无需假设该泛函在几乎必然序下是凸的或单调的，同时厘清了“均值坍缩”现象的概念基础。此外，我们为拟星形泛函建立了一个新的“对偶坍缩”结果。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We revisit the ``collapse to the mean'' phenomenon, which refers to mild structural conditions, such as local linearity, that force a law-invariant functional $\ph$ defined on finite-mean random variables to depend solely on the expectation of its argument $X$, and not on any other distributional feature. Starting from a concise characterisation of the convex order, our simplified approach unifies and extends existing results without assuming the functional to be convex or monotone in the almost-sure order, and clarifies the conceptual foundations of the ``collapse to the mean" phenomenon. In addition, we establish a new ``dual collapse'' result for quasi-star-shaped functionals.

</details>
