# Moving Out: Geoeconomic Policy and the Relocation of Foreign Affiliates

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

各国政府越来越多地运用贸易与产业政策来重塑跨国公司的生产布局，然而衡量这种地缘经济压力的标准指标是总量性的，无法识别针对特定来源国的歧视。我们构建了一个双边、方向性的相对保护主义指数，用以衡量某一目的地对特定来源国的限制程度。我们将该指数与双边地缘政治风险指数及2010至2021年期间的全球股权关联数据相结合，并估计了一个带有三维固定效应的引力型泊松伪最大似然（Poisson-Pseudo Maximum Likelihood）模型。研究结果表明，双边保护主义程度越高，外国子公司的存在显著越少：该指数每上升1个百分点，该来源国—目的地组合的子公司数量比其原本应有的水平减少1.3%。其主要机制通过运营敌意渠道（operational hostility channel）发挥作用（例如歧视性补贴、采购排斥），该渠道的影响超过了起抵消作用的关税跳跃渠道（tariff-jumping channel），并且集中体现在不可贸易部门——在这些部门中，服务当地市场需要本地存在。估计结果未显示预期效应的证据，且对更长滞后和样本拆分均保持稳健。双边地缘政治风险以相同的负号进入模型，在地缘经济政策效应之上叠加了一层较小但具有互补性的碎片化风险。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Governments increasingly use trade and industrial policy to reshape where multinationals produce, yet standard measures of this geoeconomic pressure are aggregate and cannot detect origin-specific discrimination. We build a bilateral, directional index of relative protectionism that measures how restrictive a destination is toward a given origin. We combine it with bilateral geopolitical risk indices and global ownership links over the period 2010-21, and estimate a gravity-type Poisson-Pseudo Maximum Likelihood model with three-way fixed effects. Our results show that where bilateral protectionism is greater, foreign-affiliate presence is markedly smaller: a 1 percentage point increase in the index is associated with 1.3% fewer subsidiaries than that origin-destination pair would otherwise have. The main mechanism runs through an operational hostility channel (e.g. discriminatory subsidies, procurement exclusion), which outweighs an offsetting tariff-jumping channel and concentrates in non-tradable sectors, where serving the market requires local presence. The estimates show no evidence of anticipation and are robust to longer lags and sample splits. Bilateral geopolitical risk enters with the same negative sign, adding a smaller but complementary layer of fragmentation risk to the effect of geoeconomic policy.
