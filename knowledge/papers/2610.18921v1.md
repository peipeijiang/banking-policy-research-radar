# Creative destruction: Human-AI competition in an experimental market

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18921v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过实验市场研究人类与AI在生产中的竞争，发现AI设计的游戏优于人类，且消费者不偏好人类设计的产品。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了两种假设，探讨在AI能力不断增强的情况下，人类在生产中的角色如何得以延续。一种假设是，人类可能在品味方面拥有持久优势，即使AI代理自动化了常规任务，人类仍能设计出更好的产品；另一种假设是，消费者可能天生偏好让人类参与生产。在我们的实验中，参与者既作为生产者，与AI编码代理一起设计电脑游戏，也作为消费者购买这些游戏。在三项研究（N = 1,025）中，我们没有发现支持上述任一假设的证据。由AI独立设计的游戏表现优于人类生产者设计的游戏，这些人类生产者既包括业余爱好者，也包括专业创意人士。人类生产者未能预见到AI的优势。揭示一款游戏是否由人类设计，并不会提高消费者为该产品付费的意愿。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study two hypotheses for how a human role in production could persist despite growing AI capabilities. Humans might have a durable advantage in taste, designing better products even as AI agents automate routine tasks, or consumers might intrinsically prefer that humans have a hand in production. In our experiment, participants act as producers, designing computer games with an AI coding agent, and as consumers who buy them. Across three studies (N = 1,025), we find no evidence for either hypothesis. Games designed by an AI on its own outperform those of human producers, including both amateurs and professional creatives. Human producers do not anticipate the AI's advantage. Revealing whether a game is human-designed does not raise consumers' willingness to pay for the product.

</details>
