<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个双边同配匹配市场，其中代理人通过 costly investment（ costly investment，高成本投资）和 strategic risk taking（策略性冒险）来吸引伴侣。每个代理人选择一个投资水平以及一个任意的非负质量分布，该分布是投资水平的均值保持扩散。我们允许人口规模不相等以及不同人群之间投资成本存在差异。有限市场具有唯一的非平凡半对称均衡，其投资低于帕累托前沿。我们证明，当经济以固定的人口比例增长时，这种无效率会消失，并刻画其收敛速度。该极限通过从连续统经济的无穷多个均衡中选取一个唯一有效均衡，为大型匹配市场的分析提供了基础；这些均衡具有有效投资但不同的冒险特征。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a two-sided assortative matching market in which agents attract partners through costly investment and strategic risk taking. Each agent chooses an investment level and an arbitrary distribution of nonnegative quality that is a mean-preserving spread of investment. We allow for unequal populations and different investment costs across populations. Finite markets have a unique nontrivial semi-symmetric equilibrium, with investment below the Pareto frontier. We show that this inefficiency disappears as the economy grows with the population ratio held fixed and characterise its rate of convergence. The limit provides a foundation for the analysis of large matching markets, by selecting a unique efficient equilibrium from the continuum economy's infinitely many equilibria, which feature efficient investments but different risk-taking profiles.
