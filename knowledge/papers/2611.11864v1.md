<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究效用向量的不完全社会评价，以刻画社会中关于不平等厌恶程度存在分歧的情形。我们利用效率、公平、公正、可分离性以及一个称为连续可除完成条件（continuous divisible completion condition）的条件，刻画了多优先主义准则（multi-prioritarian criteria）。我们还给出了唯一性结果，并研究了比较不平等厌恶。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper, we study incomplete social evaluation of utility vectors to capture situations in which there is disagreement in society about the degree of inequality aversion. We characterize multi-prioritarian criteria using efficiency, equity, impartiality, separability, and a condition called continuous divisible completion condition. We also provide uniqueness results and investigate comparative inequality aversion.
