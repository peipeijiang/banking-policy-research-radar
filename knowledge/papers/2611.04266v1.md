# Prizes, Shared Hazard, and the Speed of Frontier AI

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04266v1)

## 一句话结论

> 使用一个校准的起飞模型，论文表明前沿AI实验室之间的竞赛（每个实验室只承担其加速所增加危险的一部分）解释了快速AI发展，而共同研究份额规则或理想开源会大幅减缓速度，且仅靠安全审计无法减缓竞赛。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

前沿人工智能实验室在争夺商业领导地位的同时，也共同承担着灾难性风险。每家实验室都能从更早到达中获益，却只承担其速度所增加的部分危险——本文称之为“危险外部性”（hazard externality）。在一个用于说明的校准起飞模型中，三家竞速实验室在18个月内度过起飞期，而一项关于研究份额的共同规则会将其拉长至69个月。单靠任何一种力量都无法解释如此之快的竞速。当全速运行时的灾难概率为75%时，首先移除竞赛因素可弥合竞速与共同规则之间灾难概率差距的90%以上；当该概率为25%时，这一比例为69%；对两种移除顺序取平均，竞赛因素分别解释了65%和44%。更多实验室会使每家实验室竞速更激烈，而在共同研究份额下行业危险并不取决于实验室数量。在其理想形式的开源模式下，每家实验室都立即公布其能力发现，实验室会放缓至协调一致的步伐，因为没有实验室能够抢先，且每家实验室承担其研究所增加的全部危险。一家实验室的克制所达到的效果远不及一项共同规则。2026年9月的《白宫超级智能协议》（White House Accord on Super Intelligence）对安全性进行审计，但未对速度设定限制。在模型中，它并未减缓竞速，因为每家实验室仍能从加速中获得同样多的收益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Frontier AI labs compete in a contest for commercial leadership while sharing the risk of catastrophe. Each lab gains from arriving earlier but bears only part of the hazard its speed adds, which this paper calls the hazard externality. In an illustrative calibrated takeoff model, three racing labs get through the takeoff period in 18 months, while a common rule on research shares would stretch it to 69 months. Neither force alone explains racing that fast. Removing the contest first closes over ninety percent of the gap in catastrophe probability between racing and a common rule when the catastrophe probability at full speed is 75 percent, and 69 percent when it is 25 percent; averaged over both orders of removal, the contest explains 65 and 44 percent. More labs make each lab race harder, when industry hazard at a common research share does not depend on the number of labs. Under open source in its ideal form, where every lab publishes its capability findings at once, labs slow to the coordinated pace, because no lab can get ahead and each bears all the hazard its research adds. One lab's restraint achieves much less than a common rule. The White House Accord on Super Intelligence of September 2026 audits safety but sets no limit on speed. In the model it does not slow the race, because each lab still gains as much from speeding up.

</details>
