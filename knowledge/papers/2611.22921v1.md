<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

物流领域的规范性分析项目（如离线强化学习（offline reinforcement learning）和次优行动推荐系统（next-best-action systems））往往在任何人验证现有历史日志能否支持改进决策策略之前就已获得资助。我们通过行动—结果耦合（action-outcome coupling）将这一投资前可行性问题形式化：该耦合是一个总效应方差指数，衡量预期结果随记录行动的变动程度。耦合提供了任何策略可实现价值的算法无关上界，并以每决策货币单位表示，适用于单步目标和折扣序贯目标。我们还分析了一种无效工作流的渐近错误许可风险：在同一数据上拟合价值模型并评估该模型的贪婪策略。错误许可率主要由执行选择的状态单元数量驱动，从单单元时的名义检验水平上升至几十个单元时的接近确定性，而重叠的影响较小。随后我们提出一种审计方法，将日志分配至四个决策支持层级，分别针对固定策略和情境策略，并返回明确的停止理由。在一个随机化电子商务日志上，该审计认可一项固定推荐策略，认证其收益等于理论上限的30%；剩余差距反映了80行动设计下界的松弛性。两个公开物流日志在不同检查点失败：一个是因为目标是行动的会计恒等式，另一个是因为在27个聚类下，估计耦合始终低于该设计的可检测阈值，尽管一个保守的离线学习器在30个种子中的29个上认证了改进。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Prescriptive analytics initiatives in logistics, such as offline reinforcement learning and next-best-action systems, are often funded before anyone verifies that the available historical logs can support an improved decision policy. We formalise this pre-investment feasibility question via action-outcome coupling: a total-effect variance index measuring how much expected outcomes change with the logged action. Coupling provides an algorithm-independent upper bound on any policy's achievable value, expressed in monetary units per decision for both single-step and discounted sequential objectives. We also analyse the asymptotic false-licensing risk of an invalid workflow: fitting a value model and evaluating the model's greedy policy on the same data. The false-licence rate is driven mainly by the number of state cells over which selection is performed, rising from the nominal test level with one cell to near certainty with a few dozen, while overlap has a smaller effect. We then propose an audit that assigns logs to four tiers of decision support, separately for fixed and contextual policies, and returns an explicit stopping rationale. On a randomised e-commerce log, the audit endorses a fixed recommendation policy, certifying gains equal to 30 per cent of the theoretical ceiling; the remaining gap reflects bound looseness under an 80-action design. Two public logistics logs fail at different checkpoints: one because the target is an accounting identity of the action, and another because, with 27 clusters, estimated coupling stays below the design's detectability threshold even though a conservative offline learner certifies an improvement in 29 of 30 seeds.
