<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

宏观经济政策日益要求对风险进行评估，而非仅仅依赖点预测。本文构建了一个框架，用于计算情景概率的脉冲响应以及宏观经济结果的完整预测分布。该方法将制度依赖的混合VAR（regime-dependent mixture VAR）与内生状态概率和异方差冲击识别相结合，并辅以稀疏符号约束和叙事约束。将该框架应用于欧元区和美国，本文回应了2026年伊朗战争爆发后的一个核心政策问题：地缘政治冲击是否意味着回归俄罗斯入侵乌克兰后所观察到的风险特征，还是说这次有所不同？结果表明，这些冲击不仅在对预期结果的影响上存在差异，而且在如何重塑情景概率、尾部风险以及向高波动状态的转变方面也各不相同。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Macroeconomic policy increasingly requires assessments of risk rather than point forecasts alone. This paper develops a framework to compute impulse responses for scenario probabilities and the full predictive distribution of macroeconomic outcomes. The approach combines a regime-dependent mixture VAR with endogenous state probabilities and heteroskedastic shock identification, complemented by sparse sign and narrative restrictions. Applied to the euro area and U.S., the framework addresses a central policy question following the outbreak of the 2026 Iran war: do geopolitical shocks imply a return to the risk profile observed after Russia’s invasion of Ukraine, or is this time different? Results show that shocks differ not only in their effects on expected outcomes, but also in how they reshape scenario probabilities, tail risks, and transitions into high-volatility states.
