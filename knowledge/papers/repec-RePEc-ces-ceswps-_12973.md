# Natural Resources, Democracy, and Labour-Market Outcomes in the Public Sector

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

资源丰富的国家的政府可能会利用其地下财富中相当大的一部分收入，通过公共部门就业来购买支持。这种情况既出现在民主政府（需要连任）中，也出现在专制政府（必须防范骚乱和政权更迭）中。利用世界银行提供的全球官僚体系指标数据集，我们分析了2000年至2018年自然资源对发展中国家公共就业的影响。我们发现，资源租金可能对公共部门特征产生影响。然而，在揭示不同类型自然资源的异质性效应时，我们发现石油和天然气财富可能会增加非完全民主国家的公共就业，而森林财富对所有民主程度水平的国家都具有积极影响。同时，公共部门的工资溢价在非民主国家受到石油和森林资源的正向影响，而在民主程度非常高的国家则受到天然气资源的正向影响。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Governments in resource-rich countries may use a sizable share of the revenue from their underground wealth to buy support through public employment. This occurs in both democratic governments (which need to be re-elected) and autocratic governments (which have to prevent riots and regime change). Using the Worldwide Bureaucracy Indicators dataset provided by the World Bank, we analyse the effect of natural resources on public employment in developing countries from 2000 to 2018. We find that resource rents may have an effect on public sector characteristics. However, uncovering the heterogeneous effects of different types of natural resources, we find that oil and gas wealth may increase public employment in countries that are not fully democratic, while forest wealth has a positive impact for all values of democracy. At the same time, the public sector wage premium is positively affected by oil and forest in non-democratic countries and by gas when the level of democracy is very high.

</details>
