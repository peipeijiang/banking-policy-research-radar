<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究在\textit{最小购买}行为下的单位需求定价问题，其中顾客购买能够产生非负效用的最低价格商品。与准线性模型相比，我们表明在最小购买行为下，统一定价变得显著更有效。首先，我们研究对称环境。我们表明，对于具有独立同分布估值的最小购买顾客，对所有商品采用统一价格是最优的。然后，我们将这一最优性结果推广到所有来自可交换且相关联分布的估值。然而，统一定价并非在所有对称环境中都是最优的：对于最小购买顾客，我们构造了一个可交换且非关联的估值分布，使得统一定价与最优商品定价之间的差距随商品数量呈对数增长。这一差距是紧的，并且与准线性设置中的最坏情况差距相匹配。接下来，我们考虑估值独立但不同分布的非对称环境。当这些估值是正则的且顾客为最小购买型时，我们表明统一价格能够保证达到最优商品定价收入的约69.86\%，并且这一界是紧的。总体而言，我们的结果突出表明，在更保守的需求模型下，非统一定价的价值显著降低，并且往往完全消失，在这些模型中顾客会采取诸如最小购买之类的满足化行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the unit-demand pricing problem under \textit{min-buying} behavior, where customers purchase the lowest-priced item that yields non-negative utility. In contrast to the quasi-linear model, we show that uniform pricing becomes substantially more effective under min-buying. First, we study symmetric environments. We show that for min-buying customers with independent and identically distributed valuations, a uniform price across items is optimal. We then extend this optimality result to all valuations drawn from exchangeable and affiliated distributions. However, uniform pricing is not optimal in all symmetric environments: for min-buying customers we construct an exchangeable and non-affiliated valuation distribution for which the gap between uniform pricing and optimal item pricing is logarithmic in the number of items. This gap is tight and matches the worst-case gap in the quasi-linear setting. Next, we consider asymmetric environments in which valuations are independent but not identically distributed. When these valuations are regular and customers are min-buying, we show that a uniform price guarantees approximately 69.86\% of the optimal item-pricing revenue and this is tight. Overall, our results highlight that the value of non-uniform pricing substantially diminishes, and often disappears entirely, under more conservative demand models in which customers engage in satisficing behaviors such as min-buying.
