# Credibility in school choice

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29597v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究集中式学校选择中机制的可信度，通过理论模型比较不同机制的稳健性，发现延迟接受机制更可信。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在集中式学校选择中，公布机制的机制设计者可能会偏离该机制以偏袒某些学生而不被发现。若一种机制不存在此类偏离，则称其为可信的。当学生不知道他人的报告且可能仅观察到部分匹配结果时，我们研究这一可信性问题。可信性要求很高：在常见的学校选择机制中，只有延迟接受机制是可信的，且仅当学生观察到足够多的匹配结果时如此。因此，我们按可信性对机制进行排序。无论学生观察到多少匹配结果，延迟接受机制都比任何其他稳定机制更可信，并且严格比波士顿机制更可信。其相对于顶级交易循环机制和效率调整的延迟接受机制的排序取决于信息披露程度：当学生观察到整个匹配结果时，延迟接受机制严格更可信；而当学生仅观察到自己的匹配结果时，排序则发生逆转。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In centralized school choice, a designer who announces a mechanism may deviate from it to favor some students without being detected. A mechanism is credible if it admits no such deviation. We study this credibility problem when students do not know others' reports and may observe only part of the assignment. Credibility is demanding: among common school choice mechanisms, only deferred acceptance is credible, and only when students observe enough of the assignment. We therefore rank mechanisms by their credibility. Deferred acceptance is more credible than any other stable mechanism and strictly more credible than the Boston mechanism, regardless of how much of the assignment students observe. Its ranking relative to top trading cycles and efficiency-adjusted deferred acceptance depends on disclosure: deferred acceptance is strictly more credible when students observe the entire assignment, whereas the ranking reverses when students observe only their own assignment.
