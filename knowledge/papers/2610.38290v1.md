# When an effort-maximizing team architecture is unstable

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38290v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文研究努力最大化团队架构在成员可组建替代团队时的稳定性，证明严格努力最优往往自带阻塞证书、稳定最优仅存在于努力不变的平台区，并在线性与二次基准中刻画稳定性与全局最优的重叠条件。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

组织选择团队结构以加剧竞争，但其成员可能倾向于重新组织。我们探究的问题是：当参赛者可以从现有名册中组建替换团队时，一个能够最大化总努力的组织架构能否保持稳定。当一个联盟从相同的团队中抽取成员并复制其类型时，其成员与设计者对重组的方向评价截然相反：联盟恰好在总努力下降时获益。因此，一个严格的努力最优解可能自身就提供了阻断证明，而一个稳定的最优解只能存在于一个平台上——在该平台上，此类每一种可达的重组都使努力保持不变。在线性基准模型中，我们刻画了针对每一种可行单团队重组的稳定性，以及针对每一种可行划分的全局努力最大化。除配对容量情形外，这两个区域不相交；在配对容量情形下，它们仅在一个边界处相遇。这一排序关系在具有严格凸努力成本的匹配二次基准模型中依然成立，且更广泛的一步偏离权利无法恢复重叠。在标准化满容量设计中，唯一稳定的努力最优解使用最大可行名册，并将激励集中度设定在其阻断边界上。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Organizations choose team structures to intensify competition, yet their members may prefer to reorganize. We ask whether an architecture that maximizes aggregate effort can remain stable when contestants may form a replacement team from incumbent rosters. When a coalition draws from identical teams and reproduces their type, its members and the designer rank the reorganization in opposite directions: the coalition gains precisely when total effort falls. A strict effort optimum can therefore supply its own blocking certificate, while a stable optimum can survive only on a plateau where every reachable reorganization of this kind leaves effort unchanged. In a linear benchmark, we characterize stability against every feasible one-team reorganization and global effort maximization over every feasible partition. Except under pair capacity, the two regions do not overlap; with pair capacity, they meet only at one boundary. The ordering persists in a matched quadratic benchmark with strictly convex effort cost, and broader one-step deviation rights cannot restore overlap. Among standardized full-cap designs, the unique stable-effort optimum uses the largest feasible roster and sets incentive concentration at its blocking boundary.
