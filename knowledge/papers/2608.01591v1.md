<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

按展示计费的拍卖长期以来一直是在线广告中的分配机制。我们认为，在“放手驾驶”（HOTW）市场中——即广告主声明预算和“投资回报率”（ROI）目标，而交易平台的机器学习模型预测点击价值——拍卖已不再必要：最优定价所需的全部信息已为交易平台所知，该平台处于垄断者面对向下倾斜需求曲线进行定价的位置。HOTW市场是一个Fisher市场，其竞争均衡可通过Eisenberg和Gale的凸规划计算得出，从而产生同时满足所有预算和ROI约束的市场出清价格与分配。这一竞争均衡价格在交易平台所有统一价格机制中实现了收入最优：避免了典型统一价格多单位拍卖中的需求缩减问题。此外，所得的竞争均衡在结果上等价于带节奏的序贯第一价格拍卖，其中节奏乘数可由交易平台事前计算。这种一次性方法用单个凸规划取代了数百万次独立拍卖，不仅在操作上比动态演化的竞价策略更简单，而且对交易平台而言收入最优，同时产生相同的均衡结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Per-impression auctions have long served as the allocation mechanism in online advertising. We argue that in ``hands-off-the-wheel'' (HOTW) markets, where advertisers declare budgets and ``return-on-investment'' (ROI) targets and the exchange's ML models predict click values, auctions are no longer necessary: all information required for optimal pricing is already known to the exchange, which occupies the position of a monopolist pricing against a downward-sloping demand curve. A HOTW market is a Fisher market, whose competitive equilibrium can be computed via the convex program of Eisenberg and Gale, yielding market-clearing prices and allocations satisfying all budget and ROI constraints simultaneously. This competitive-equilibrium price is revenue-optimal for the exchange among all uniform-price mechanisms: avoiding the demand reduction problem in typical uniform-price multi-unit auctions. The resulting competitive equilibrium is moreover outcome-equivalent to sequential first-price auctions with pacing, with pacing multipliers computable ex-ante by the exchange. This one-shot approach replaces millions of individual auctions with one convex program, which is not only operationally simpler than dynamically evolving bidding strategies, but revenue-optimal for the exchange, while delivering the same equilibrium outcome.
