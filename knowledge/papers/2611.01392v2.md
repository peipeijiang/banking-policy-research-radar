<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了杠杆ETF（LETFs）的成本与收益，重点关注业绩的五个决定因素：波动率拖累、费用、融资成本、产品发行和市场择时。宽基股票指数多头LETFs为投资者带来了超过1000亿美元的收益，其中相对于投资于标的资产的反事实投资而言超过400亿美元，这归因于温和的结构性逆风和上涨的市场。然而，单一股票LETFs是在波动率和历史收益分布接近顶部的股票上发行的。这迎合了散户对类彩票收益的需求，但也造成了更大的波动率拖累、更高的融资成本以及更大的市场择时失误导致损失的敞口。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the costs and benefits of leveraged ETFs (LETFs), focusing on five determinants of performance: volatility drag, fees, financing costs, product launches, and market timing. Long broad-equity-index LETFs generated more than 100 billion US dollars in investor gains, including over 40 billion US dollars relative to counterfactual investments in the underlying assets, due to modest structural headwinds and rising markets. Single-stock LETFs, however, are launched on stocks near the top of the volatility and past-return distributions. This caters to retail demand for lottery-like payoffs, but it also creates larger volatility drag, higher financing costs, and greater exposure to losses from poor market timing.
