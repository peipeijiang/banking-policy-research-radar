<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

尽管义务教育（CE）法律拓宽了受教育机会，并对经济、社会及健康结果产生了深远影响，但其是否能减少长期健康不平等仍不明确。我们利用1810年至2000年间66个国家的身高基尼系数，研究了义务教育政策对健康不平等的影响，并通过交错双重差分设计估计了意向治疗效应。结果表明，义务教育的实施降低了身高基尼系数。这一减少不平等的效应似乎主要通过增加受教育年限和降低生育率来实现。该效应在不同地区间并不一致，在欧洲最为显著，且主要由义务教育年限的后续延长所驱动，而非仅凭初始实施。总体而言，研究结果表明，教育政策能够随着时间推移显著缩小健康差距，但其有效性取决于政策的覆盖范围及实施时机。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Although compulsory education (CE) laws have widened access to schooling and shaped economic, social, and health outcomes, it remains unclear whether they reduce long-run health inequalities. We study the effect of CE policies on health inequality using height Gini indices for 66 countries between 1810 and 2000, estimating intent-to-treat effects through a staggered difference-in-differences design. Our results indicate that CE exposure reduces the height Gini. The inequality-reducing effect appears to operate mainly through increased years of schooling and reduced fertility. The effect is not uniform across regions, being largest in Europe, and it is driven largely by later extensions of CE duration rather than initial adoption alone. Overall, the findings indicate that education policy can meaningfully narrow health disparities over time, but its effectiveness depends on the scale of the mandate and the timing of adoption.
