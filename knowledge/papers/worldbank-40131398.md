# Returns to Private Debt in Emerging and Developing Economies

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

私人债务是新兴市场企业融资的重要形式，但其投资者回报尚未得到充分理解。本文利用国际金融公司（International Finance Corporation）的贷款层面现金流数据，首次系统性地提供了涵盖20多年间4，109笔贷款、总额约1，150亿美元、遍布127个国家的已实现回报证据。全球金融危机后的贷款表现优于摩根大通企业新兴市场债券指数（JPMorgan Corporate Emerging Market Bond Index）和美国国债，其公开市场等价回报率分别为1.02和1.10，在最近五年分别升至1.05和1.14。在2002至2024年期间，相应的公开市场等价回报率分别为0.98和1.07，其中全球金融危机时期的年份相对于摩根大通企业新兴市场债券指数表现较弱。总体而言，回报与公开市场具有竞争力。本文还更具体地评估了最具挑战性市场中的财务表现：极低收入国家以及脆弱和受冲突影响的国家。尽管这些市场的违约率较高，但相对于公开市场替代方案的回报在统计上与整体投资组合的回报无法区分，这表明国际金融公司即使在这些市场中也对违约风险进行了合理定价。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Private debt is an important form of corporate financing in emerging markets, but its investor returns are not well understood. This paper uses loan-level cash flow data from the International Finance Corporation to provide the first systematic evidence on realized returns across 4,109 loans over more than 20 years, totaling about US$115 billion in 127 countries. Post–global financial crisis loans outperform both the JPMorgan Corporate Emerging Market Bond Index and U.S. Treasuries, with public market equivalents of 1.02 and 1.10, respectively, rising to 1.05 and 1.14 over the latest five years. Over 2002–24, the corresponding public market equivalents are 0.98 and 1.07, respectively, with global financial crisis–era vintages posting weaker performance relative to the JPMorgan Corporate Emerging Market Bond Index. Overall, returns are competitive with public markets. The paper also assesses financial performance more specifically within the most challenging markets: very low-income countries and fragile and conflict-affected states. Although default rates are higher in these markets, returns relative to public-market alternatives are statistically indistinguishable from those of the overall portfolio, suggesting that the International Finance Corporation priced default risk appropriately even in these markets.

</details>
