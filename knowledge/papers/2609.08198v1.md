<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

布沙尔和法默（2023）认为，自我实现的信念能够产生现实的财富分布。他们报告的基尼系数、分位数和帕累托指数是基于250个日期上王朝层面财富的平均值计算得出的，而非基于截面数据。修正估计目标后，其定性结果得到强化：当王朝数量N=1,000,000时，期末截面基尼系数为0.883而非0.7，中位财富为平均财富的0.0052而非0.39。因此，该模型产生了更大的不平等，但并未达到所报告的定量拟合程度。附录F分析了一个不同的过程，并未确立所声称的帕累托尾部或指数。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Bouchaud and Farmer (2023) argue that self-fulfilling beliefs generate a realistic wealth distribution. Their reported Gini coefficient, quantiles, and Pareto exponent are computed from dynasty-level wealth averaged over 250 dates, not from a cross section. Correcting the estimand strengthens their qualitative result: with $N=1{,}000{,}000$ dynasties, the terminal cross-sectional Gini is 0.883 rather than 0.7, and median wealth is 0.0052 rather than 0.39 of average wealth. The model therefore generates more inequality, but not the reported quantitative fit. Appendix F analyzes a different process and does not establish the claimed Pareto tail or exponent.

</details>
