<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

假设一个经济沿着一条基本面的闭路径连续移动，并恰好回到其初始状态。沿着该路径连续追踪的一个正则瓦尔拉斯均衡价格未必回到自身。对于每一种商品数量 $L\ge3$，我们构造了一个纯交换经济的闭单参数族，其中所有偏好固定，只有三个个体的禀赋变化，且总资源保持不变。沿该环路，两个正则均衡价格分支发生互换，而第三个分支回到自身；最终，每一个经济基本要素都恰好与初始时相同。该构造至多使用 $2L+2$ 个消费者。我们还提供了一个至多使用 $2L$ 个消费者的精确实现，其中所有禀赋固定，只有一个偏好变化；这两个族生成相同的总超额需求。关键的实现问题是参数化的：经典的总量实现结果保证在每个固定参数值处存在一个经济，但不保证存在一个连续的实现基本要素环路。我们明确构造了这样一个环路。由此产生的单值化在足够小的 $C^1$ 扰动下是结构稳定的。对于一种或两种商品这是不可能的，这使得三种商品成为尖锐的阈值。因此，局部正则性保证了局部延拓，但不保证均衡分支具有全局路径无关的同一性，这对多重性下的全局比较静态以及基于延拓的均衡选择具有启示意义。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Suppose an economy moves continuously along a closed path of fundamentals and returns exactly to its initial state. A regular Walrasian equilibrium price followed continuously along that path need not return to itself. For every number of commodities $L\ge3$, we construct a closed one-parameter family of pure-exchange economies in which all preferences are fixed, only three individual endowments vary, and aggregate resources remain constant. Along the loop, two regular equilibrium-price branches are exchanged while a third returns to itself; at the end, every economic primitive is exactly as it was initially. The construction uses at most $2L+2$ consumers. We also provide an exact realization with at most $2L$ consumers in which all endowments are fixed and only one preference varies; the two families generate identical aggregate excess demand. The key realization problem is parametric: classical aggregate-realization results guarantee an economy at each fixed parameter value, but not a continuous loop of realizing primitives. We construct such a loop explicitly. The resulting monodromy is structurally stable under sufficiently small $C^1$ perturbations. It is impossible with one or two commodities, making three commodities the sharp threshold. Thus local regularity guarantees local continuation but not a globally path-independent identity of an equilibrium branch, with implications for global comparative statics under multiplicity and for equilibrium selection based on continuation.

</details>
