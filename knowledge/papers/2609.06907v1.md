<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

供给曲线和需求曲线斜率的符号约束常被用于结构向量自回归中的历史分解识别。本文表明，这些约束的识别力既取决于简化式参数，也取决于已实现的预测误差。因此，与许多其他结构对象不同，识别的强度无法仅从简化式参数中评估。在实证中，美国通胀历史分解的识别集合通常在很大程度上缺乏信息量，无论是在总体层面还是在大多数支出类别中。因此，现有的通胀分解对用于在观测等价模型中进行选择的辅助假设十分敏感。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Sign restrictions on the slopes of supply and demand curves are often used to identify historical decompositions in structural vector autoregressions. I show that the identifying power of these restrictions depends on both reduced-form parameters and realised forecast errors. Consequently, unlike many other structural objects, the strength of identification cannot be assessed from reduced-form parameters alone. Empirically, identified sets for historical decompositions of US inflation are typically largely uninformative, both in aggregate and in most expenditure categories. Existing inflation decompositions are therefore sensitive to auxiliary assumptions used to select among observationally equivalent models.
