<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究多元\(t\)-分布的二次型。本文推导了多个二次型的联合分布与密度，并确立了用有限级数逼近累积分布函数（CDF）的可能性。本文还给出了所有存在的混合矩的闭式表达式。此外，还考虑了二次型的逆以及二次型之比。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper concerns quadratic forms of multivariate $t$-distributions. This paper derives the joint distribution and density of multiple quadratic forms, and establishes the possibility of a finite series approximation to the CDF. The paper also provides closed-form expressions for all the mixed moments that exist. Inverses of quadratic forms and ratios of quadratic forms are also considered.
