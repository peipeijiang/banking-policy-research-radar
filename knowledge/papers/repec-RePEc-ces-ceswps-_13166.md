<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们将刚性工资的经典新凯恩斯模型扩展至允许受约束有效（constrained-efficient）可变薪酬合同，并给出一个中性结果：劳动力市场势力（labor market power）与工作时长和通胀的动态无关。基本工资（base pay）负责在企业间配置工人，而可变薪酬（variable pay）负责在一个匹配内配置工作时长并响应总体冲击。市场势力影响前者，但不影响后者。在固定工资合同（flat-wage contracts）的通常限制下，市场势力对经济周期动态的影响取决于工作时长是由供给决定还是由需求决定。我们运用这些洞见重新解释了文献中的若干结果。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We extend the canonical New Keynesian model with rigid wages to allow for constrained-efficient variable-pay contracts, and provide a neutrality result: labor market power is irrelevant for the dynamics of hours worked and inflation. Base pay allocates workers across firms, while variable pay allocates hours within a match and in response to aggregate shocks. Market power affects the former, but not the latter. Under the usual restriction of flat-wage contracts, the effect of market power on business-cycle dynamics depends on whether hours are supply-determined or demand-determined. We use these insights to reinterpret several results from the literature.

</details>
