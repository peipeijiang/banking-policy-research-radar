本教学笔记通过一个引理及简短的线性代数证明，将关于通过异方差性（heteroskedasticity）、波动率机制（volatility regimes）、GARCH、随机波动率（stochastic volatility）以及协变量驱动的方差（covariate-driven variances）进行识别的已知结果统一起来。一个冲击（shock）在符号和标签意义下可被识别，当且仅当没有其他冲击与其共享方差路径；而共享同一路径的冲击则在旋转意义下可被识别。结构系数矩阵的行求解一个广义特征值问题，该问题嵌套了Rigobon（2003）的两机制解。对误差二阶矩的秩检验最多计数线性无关的中心化方差路径，而非已识别的冲击。一个模拟示例说明了上述各点。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This teaching note unifies known results on identification through heteroskedasticity, under volatility regimes, GARCH, stochastic volatility and covariate-driven variances, in one lemma with a short linear-algebra proof. A shock is identified up to sign and labelling if and only if no other shock shares its variance path, and shocks that share a path are identified up to a rotation. The rows of the structural coefficient matrix solve one generalised eigenvalue problem that nests Rigobon's (2003) two-regime solution. Rank tests on second moments of the errors count at most linearly independent centred variance paths, not identified shocks. A simulation illustrates each point.
