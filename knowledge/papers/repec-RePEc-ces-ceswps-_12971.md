# Minimum Wages and Race Disparities

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_12971.html)

## 一句话结论

> 论文全面分析最低工资对黑人就业和收入的负面影响，发现提高最低工资并未缩小种族工资差距，反而对黑人（尤其是男性）造成更明显的就业和收入损失，且这种负面影响在黑人人口比例高的地区更严重。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提供了一项综合分析，考察最低工资对黑人的影响，以及对黑人与白人相对影响的差异。我们不仅研究青少年——这是最低工资-就业文献的主要关注对象——还研究更广泛的低技能群体。我们没有发现证据表明更高的最低工资能够缩小工资差距。首先，我们的许多证据表明，更高最低工资带来的失业效应对黑人更为明显——对黑人男性尤为如此——但在白人群体中无法检测到。其次，在我们的许多分析中，最低工资的影响大到足以对黑人的收入（以及相对收入）产生不利影响。第三，鉴于美国存在严重的种族居住隔离，最低工资对黑人更为不利的影响会集中在黑人人口占比较高的地区，而这一点似乎因以下证据而更加突出：无论个人种族如何，在黑人占总人口比例极高的地区，最低工资的影响更为不利。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We provide a comprehensive analysis of the effects of minimum wages on blacks, and on the relative impacts on blacks vs. whites. We study not only teenagers – the focus of much of the minimum wage-employment literature – but also broader low-skill groups. We find no evidence that higher minimum wages reduce wage disparities. First, much of our evidence points to job loss effects from higher minimum wages are more evident for blacks – and more so for black men – but not detectable for whites. Second, in many of our analyses the effects of minimum wages are large enough to generate adverse effects on earnings (and relative earnings) of blacks. Third, given strong residential segregation by race in the United States, more adverse effects of minimum wages on blacks would fall on areas with a high black population share, and this appears to be accentuated by evidence that minimum wage effects are more adverse in areas where blacks are a very high share of the population, regardless of individual race.

</details>
