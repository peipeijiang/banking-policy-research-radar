<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究两阶段拍卖，其中投标人私下知道其估值先验和学习成本，而卖方联合设计信息获取、延续分配和支付。收入最大化需要在满足跨先验激励的同时筛选学习成本，这可能会扭曲实验和延续分配。我们推导出一个涉及分配后验价值、虚拟学习成本和依赖于先验的租金的收入表示，并通过差分不等式刻画跨先验激励。我们证明了对于有限实验菜单和任意先验分布，最优机制的存在性。在先验支撑有限的情况下，有界有限类别线性规划以线性速率逼近最优收入，同时保持精确的动态激励相容和 interim 个体理性。其对偶产生了实验、分配和支付的联合最优性条件。一个双先验示例表明，有效的延续分配可能在收入上次优，而依赖于成本的实验可以严格增加收入。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study two-stage auctions in which bidders privately know their valuation priors and learning costs, while the seller jointly designs information acquisition, continuation allocation, and payments. Revenue maximization requires screening learning costs while satisfying cross-prior incentives, potentially distorting both experimentation and continuation allocation. We derive a revenue representation involving allocated posterior values, virtual learning costs, and prior-dependent rents, and characterize cross-prior incentives through difference inequalities. We establish existence of optimal mechanisms for finite experiment menus and arbitrary prior distributions. With finite prior support, bounded finite-category linear programs approximate optimal revenue at a linear rate while preserving exact dynamic incentive compatibility and interim individual rationality. Their dual yields joint optimality conditions for experimentation, allocation, and payments. A two-prior example demonstrates that efficient continuation can be revenue-suboptimal and that cost-contingent experimentation can strictly increase revenue.
