# AI at Work: Use, Complementary Technologies, and Task Reallocation

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13094.html)

## 一句话结论

> 基于2025年西班牙个人调查数据，描述工作场所AI使用率（约16%）及其在人口群体和职业间的异质性，发现AI使用与其他现代数字技术使用高度相关，并报告了在抽象认知任务上的生产率提升和有限的任务时间重新分配。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们基于2025年在西班牙开展的一项关于工作场所使用人工智能（AI）及其他现代数字技术的调查提供了证据。AI的使用已具有经济意义，但远未普及，约16%的劳动者报告使用AI。其采用在劳动者和工作岗位之间高度不均衡，在教育程度、年龄、收入以及企业规模和范围方面呈现明显梯度，同时职业内部也存在相当大的异质性。AI的使用与其他现代技术的使用密切相关，后者所解释的变异份额大于任何单独考察的劳动者或工作特征，包括职业暴露度量。AI使用的目的在不同人口群体和工作岗位之间也差异显著。使用者报告在抽象认知任务中主要产生积极的生产率效应，同时伴随有限但系统性的跨任务时间再分配。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We provide evidence from a 2025 survey of individuals in Spain on workplace use of artificial intelligence (AI) and other modern digital technologies. AI use is already economically meaningful but far from pervasive, with about 16% of workers reporting AI use. Its adoption is highly uneven across workers and jobs, with strong gradients by education, age, earnings, and firm size and scope, as well as substantial within-occupation heterogeneity. AI use is strongly correlated with the use of other modern technologies, which accounts for a larger share of variation than any worker or job characteristic considered separately, including occupational exposure measures. The purposes of AI use also vary markedly across demographic groups and jobs. Users report predominantly positive productivity effects in abstract cognitive tasks, together with limited but systematic time reallocation across tasks.
