<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了当机构的选择函数满足强可观察替代性（strong OS）时，累积报价机制（COM）的性质。首先，我们证明了COM的以下每个性质都刻画了强OS：IR单调性、弱Maskin单调性以及丢弃单调性。丢弃单调性是一个新条件，它弱于弱Maskin单调性，并且可以被解释为策略防范性和非专断性的一种弱化。其次，我们表明，当选择函数是强OS时，COM的弱群体策略防范性可归结为个体策略防范性。然而，强OS对于这种归结既不是必要的，也不是“几乎必要的”。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the properties of the cumulative offer mechanism (COM) when institutions' choice functions satisfy strong observable substitutability (strong OS). First, we show that each of the following properties of the COM characterizes strong OS: IR monotonicity, weak Maskin monotonicity, and dropping monotonicity. Dropping monotonicity is a new condition weaker than weak Maskin monotonicity, and it is interpretable as a weakening of strategy-proofness and non-bossiness. Second, we show that when choice functions are strongly OS, weak group strategy-proofness of the COM reduces to individual strategy-proofness. However, strong OS is neither necessary nor "almost necessary" for this reduction.
