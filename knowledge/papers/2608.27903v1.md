<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

对立方的辩护者可能无法捏造证据，但可以选择呈现哪些真实的观察结果。我们研究法院、编辑或平台应如何在偏好更高决策的辩护者与偏好更低决策的辩护者之间分配潜在的曝光度。每位辩护者控制一个独立的证据池，曝光在状态和证据已知之前承诺，且一个对选择不设防的接收者会对所展示的观察结果取平均值。在基准线性偏好和实现池为共同知识的条件下，每个有限池中的所有纳什均衡都会引发相同的行动和共同的披露门槛：向上偏好的辩护者披露高于该门槛的观察结果，而向下偏好的辩护者披露低于该门槛的观察结果。因此，注意力不仅改变了权重，也改变了选择。在共同证据法则下，更多的曝光会使辩护者更少但更极端地发言，同时将决策推向其偏好的方向。在大型池中，能够复现完整证据决策的唯一状态依存的曝光份额，平衡的是辩护者的方向性尾部矩，而非其实际发言。由于该份额通常依赖于未知状态，我们刻画了最优的事前折中方案，并求解了一个具有唯一次优政策的原始两状态经济模型。对于有限池，我们推导出精确的风险最小化调整，将选择引发的截断偏差、接收者的固定基准以及抽样方差分离开来。注意力对截断门槛的反馈在空槽位部分加权时依然存在，并在完全插补时消失。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Opposing advocates may be unable to fabricate evidence but can choose which true observations to present. We study how a court, editor, or platform should divide potential exposure between an advocate who prefers a higher decision and one who prefers a lower decision. Each advocate controls a separate evidence pool, exposure is committed before the state and evidence are known, and a selection-naive receiver averages displayed observations. Under baseline linear preferences and common knowledge of the realized pools, all Nash equilibria in every finite pool induce the same action and common disclosure bar: the upward advocate reveals observations above it and the downward advocate those below it. Attention therefore changes selection as well as weight. Under a common evidence law, more exposure makes an advocate speak less often and more extremely while moving the decision in its preferred direction. In large pools, the unique state-contingent exposure share that reproduces the complete-evidence decision balances the advocates' directional tail moments, not their observed speech. Because this share generally depends on the unknown state, we characterize the optimal ex ante compromise and solve a primitive two-state economy with a unique second-best policy. For finite pools, we derive the exact risk-minimizing adjustment, separating selection-induced cutoff bias, the receiver's fixed benchmark, and sampling variance. The attention-to-cutoff feedback survives partial weighting of empty slots and vanishes at full imputation.
