# Who Is the Regression About? Auditing AI-Labeled Treatments When Effects Differ

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21207v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文指出当 LLM 标注的处理变量存在与结果相关的选择性误分类时，OLS 会低估异质性处理效应，并提出分层盲审方法进行校正，以 LinkedIn 远程工作薪资差距为例说明偏差可达近 40%。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

研究者越来越多地将结果变量对由大语言模型标注的处理变量进行回归。当效应存在差异时，OLS将处理组效应（ATT）与未处理组效应（ATU）进行平均，并对较小群体赋予更大权重。此时标签偏差包含两部分：由误分类导致的经典衰减效应，以及当误差与结果变量相关时产生的选择性项。在LinkedIn职位发布数据中，模型遗漏了薪酬更高的远程职位：精确度检验通过，但远程薪酬差距被低估了近40%，这一偏差无法通过增加数据量消除。对两个标注组进行分层盲审可以纠正该偏差，并恢复两种效应。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Researchers increasingly regress outcomes on treatments labeled by large language models. When effects differ, OLS averages the effect on the treated (ATT) and on the untreated (ATU), weighting the smaller group more. A label's bias then has two parts: classical attenuation from misclassification, and a selective term when its errors are correlated with the outcome. In LinkedIn postings, the model misses better-paid remote jobs: a precision check passes, yet the remote pay gap is understated by nearly 40 percent, a bias that more data cannot remove. A stratified, blind audit of both labeled groups corrects it and recovers both effects.

</details>
