<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

投资组合的气候对齐受到资产管理人行业分类与气候情景行业分类之间不匹配的制约。本文提出一种基于范围1和范围2温室气体排放重要性、而非语义相似性的NGFS-GICS映射。该映射为行业脱碳方法（Sectoral Decarbonization Approach）提供输入，将NGFS轨迹降尺度为投资组合层面的GICS行业路径，并根据区域敞口进行调整。将其应用于两个示例性ETF指数，展示了行业构成和区域轨迹如何塑造投资组合的融资排放路径。该框架具有透明性、与资产类别无关，并可用于气候压力测试等内部练习。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Portfolio climate alignment is limited by the mismatch between asset managers' sector classifications and those of climate scenarios. This article proposes an NGFS-GICS mapping based on the materiality of Scope 1 and 2 GHG emissions rather than on semantic similarity. The mapping feeds a Sectoral Decarbonization Approach that down-scales NGFS trajectories into GICS sector pathways at portfolio level, adjusted for regional exposure. Applied to two illustrative ETF indices, it shows how sectoral composition and regional trajectories shape a portfolio's financed emissions pathway. The framework is transparent, asset-class agnostic and usable for internal exercises such as climate stress tests.
