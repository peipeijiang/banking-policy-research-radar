<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文在一个易于处理的、基于TANK（两类代理人）的新凯恩斯主义框架下，重新评估了货币政策传导机制，该框架包含“手到口”（hand-to-mouth）家庭和动物精神（animal spirits）。在模型中，动物精神通过行为性工资预期引入：感知到的宏观经济风险会影响预期的未来工资，进而影响总消费。该框架允许优化型家庭和“手到口”家庭对货币政策作出不同反应，这反映了直接利率渠道和工资收入传导在不同家庭类型中所扮演的截然不同的角色。分析表明，家庭构成对货币政策传导的强度具有重要影响。“手到口”家庭占比越高，总需求对当前工资收入的依赖程度就越大，而直接利率渠道则会被削弱。当优化型家庭在经济中占较大比重时，跨期替代和动物精神渠道变得更加重要。福利与稳健性检验表明，在异质性家庭的各类设定中，将当前工资效应与动物精神相结合，相较于仅基于当前工资收入传导的设定，能够降低福利损失，尤其是在“手到口”家庭占比较高的情况下。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper reassesses monetary policy transmission in a tractable TANK-based New Keynesian setting with hand-to-mouth households and animal spirits. In the model, animal spirits are introduced through behavioral wage expectations: perceived macroeconomic risk affects expected future wages and thereby aggregate consumption. The framework allows optimizing and hand-to-mouth households to respond differently to monetary policy, reflecting the distinct roles of the direct interest-rate channel and wage-income transmission across household types. The analysis shows that household composition matters for the strength of monetary transmission. A higher share of hand-to-mouth households makes aggregate demand more dependent on current wage income and weakens the direct interest-rate channel. When optimizing households account for a larger share of the economy, intertemporal substitution and the animal-spirit channel become more important. The welfare and robustness checks suggest that, among the heterogeneous-household specifications, combining current wage effects with animal spirits can reduce welfare losses relative to specifications based only on current wage-income transmission, especially when the hand-to-mouth share is high.
