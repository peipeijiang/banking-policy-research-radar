<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究卖方如何从截断的历史出价数据中学习第二价格拍卖的保留价。由于低于过去保留价的估值无法被观测到，即使拥有无限数据也可能无法揭示哪个保留价能带来最高收益。在单调风险率（MHR）假设下，我们精确刻画了最优保留价何时可被识别——即使估值分布本身可能无法识别：要么收益在最低历史保留价处尚未开始下降，从而最优保留价位于观测到的出价之中；要么截断比例达到MHR所允许的上限，从而确定整个分布。当识别失败时，我们推导了在与数据一致的分布上最小化最坏情况收益遗憾的保留价，并证明这一唯一的极小极大保留价及其遗憾仅依赖于两个可观测变量：截断比例和最低历史保留价之上的风险率。当有$N$个历史竞标者时，若最优保留价位于观测出价之中，则以期望最坏情况遗憾$O(N^{-1/2})$学习到最优保留价；若截断比例达到MHR所允许的上限且最优值位于最低保留价处或以下，则仅从截断计数即可获得$O(N^{-1})$的遗憾。在不可识别情形下，将这两个量的估计值代入极小极大保留价，在局部密度光滑性下可得到与总体最小值相比在$O(N^{-2/5})$以内的期望最坏情况遗憾，并在光滑类上具有匹配的极小极大下界。在MHR之外，我们求解了正则分布和无限制分布下的极小极大问题，并在已知边界参数界的条件下，证明了正则不可识别情形下的$O(N^{-2/5})$学习界。在合成数据和eBay数据上的实验表明，利用MHR可带来收益，且当参数模型设定错误时稳健规则具有优势。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how a seller can learn a reserve price for a second-price auction from censored historical bid data. Because values below past reserves are unobserved, even unlimited data may not reveal which reserve earns the most revenue. Under a monotone hazard rate (MHR) assumption, we characterize exactly when an optimal reserve is identified, even though the value distribution may not be: either revenue has not yet begun to fall at the lowest historical reserve, so the best reserve lies among the observed bids, or the censored fraction is as large as MHR allows, which pins down the entire distribution. When identification fails, we derive the reserve that minimizes worst-case revenue regret over distributions consistent with the data, and prove that this unique minimax reserve and its regret depend on just two observable quantities: the censored fraction and the hazard just above the lowest historical reserve. With $N$ historical bidders, the optimal reserve is learned with expected worst-case regret $O(N^{-1/2})$ when it lies among the observed bids, and $O(N^{-1})$ from the censored count alone when the censored fraction is as large as MHR allows and the optimum lies at or below the lowest reserve. In the nonidentifiable regime, plugging estimates of the two quantities into the minimax reserve gives expected worst-case regret within $O(N^{-2/5})$ of the population minimum under local density smoothness, with a matching minimax lower bound on a smooth class. Beyond MHR, we solve the minimax problem for regular and unrestricted distributions and prove an $O(N^{-2/5})$ learning bound in the regular nonidentifiable regime, given known bounds on the boundary parameters. Experiments on synthetic and eBay data show gains from exploiting MHR and the benefits of the robust rule when parametric models are misspecified.
