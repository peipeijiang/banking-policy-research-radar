<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文探讨的是，激励相容机制中的支付端是否可以被修复，从而在保持分配以及在最坏情况评估下所有逐报告比较结果不变的同时，使每一个已实现的结算恰好平衡。在报告之前，设计者承诺采用一组支付规则和一个与报告无关的选择协议。此时，一个逐点保持条件即可延续占优策略激励。在全支持共同参照下，若原始规则本身已平衡，则一项精确已知的标签法则才允许修复；而即使仅有一个非最坏标签存在频率不确定性，也会使所有非赤字规则均可修复。在共同剩余模糊性基准下，在正盈余状态下，任何保持性的、恰好平衡的有限菜单，随着认证频率信息变得更加精确，都需要更大的转移容量。在典型菜单中，固定转移上限会使可实现的最佳平衡在模糊性消失时向原始盈余恶化。当仅认证可能的标签时，两个标签即已足够。适当平衡的划分还可以在非负支付下保留原始上限，并在每个已实现规则下维持个体理性。当每个代理的保持分布具有全支持时，这一最终保证在正收入维克里状态下是不可能实现的。因此，频率信息以不同方式改变了可行性、流动性和已实现的参与情况。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper asks whether the payment side of an incentive-compatible mechanism can be repaired so that every realized settlement balances exactly while the allocation and all report-by-report comparisons under the maintained worst-case evaluation remain unchanged. Before reports, the designer commits to a menu of payment rules and a report-blind selection protocol. A pointwise preservation condition then carries over dominant-strategy incentives. Under a full-support common reference, a precisely known label law permits repair only if the original rule already balances, whereas frequency uncertainty about even one non-worst label makes every non-deficit rule repairable. Under the common residual-ambiguity benchmark, every preserving, exactly balanced finite menu at a positive-surplus state requires more transfer capacity as certified frequency information becomes tighter. In the canonical menu, fixing the transfer cap makes the best attainable balance deteriorate toward the original surplus as ambiguity vanishes. When only the possible labels are certified, two labels suffice. Suitably balanced partitions can also retain the original cap for nonnegative payments and preserve individual rationality under every realized rule. This last guarantee is impossible in positive-revenue Vickrey states when each agent's preserving distribution has full support. Frequency information therefore changes feasibility, liquidity, and realized participation in distinct ways.

</details>
