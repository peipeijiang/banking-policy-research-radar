<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

近期有研究提出，用具有比标准布朗运动更粗糙的连续样本路径的连续时间过程来刻画价格中的微观结构噪声。本文提出一个针对逐笔价格变动的微观结构模型，该模型明确区分了永久性价格变动与由噪声引起的短暂价格变动。我们展示了该模型如何收敛到一个标准的半鞅模型，用于描述永久价格过程，同时附加一个源自宏观尺度上短暂价格变动的粗糙噪声项。这为粗糙噪声模型提供了微观结构基础。随后，我们开发了一种适用于逐笔数据的广义矩估计（GMM）方法，并配套提出了一个正式的粗糙噪声检验。通过模拟研究，我们证明了该估计量和检验在有限样本中的有效性，并将其应用于2024年道琼斯工业平均指数成分股的逐笔数据。由于我们的估计量专为逐笔数据设计，我们在日度层面估计粗糙度，揭示了显著的日间波动。我们发现，粗糙噪声虽然存在，但并非普遍现象：即使在检测到的情况下，粗糙度指数通常也接近于零，且在最明显的日子里，其主导特征为短期价格反转。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Recently, it has been proposed to model the microstructure noise in prices by a continuous-time process with continuous sample paths that are rougher than those of a standard Brownian motion. In this paper, we propose a microstructural model for the tick-by-tick price changes that explicitly separates the permanent price changes from the fleeting price changes due to noise. We show how this model converges to a standard semimartingale model for the permanent price process, plus a rough noise term originating from the fleeting price changes on the macro scale. This provides a microstructural foundation for the rough-noise model. We then develop a GMM estimation method applicable to tick-by-tick data, together with a formal test for rough noise. We show that the estimator and test work in finite samples through a simulation study, and apply them to tick-by-tick data on Dow Jones Industrial Average constituents in 2024. Because our estimator is designed for tick-by-tick data, we estimate roughness at the daily level, revealing substantial day-to-day variation. We find that rough noise, while present, is not universal: even when detected, the roughness index is typically close to zero, and it is most pronounced on days dominated by short-run price reversals.
