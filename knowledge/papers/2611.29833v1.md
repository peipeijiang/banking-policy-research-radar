<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

混合logit估计在样本量和偏好异质性维度增加时，计算需求会显著上升。本研究考察了导数计算方式、处理器选择以及浮点精度如何在不同问题规模下影响估计时间和参数恢复。本文提供了一个受控基准测试，通过蒙特卡洛实验比较了有限差分（FD）与自动微分（AD）、中央处理器（CPU）执行与图形处理器（GPU）执行、以及双精度（FP64）与单精度（FP32）算术运算，实验中数据生成过程、模拟抽样、优化器、起始值和目标函数在各策略间保持一致。

结果表明，AD是加速的主要来源。将AD与FP32算术运算结合可带来进一步收益，而将AD、GPU执行和FP32算术运算三者结合则实现了最大的速度提升，尤其是在样本量和模型复杂度增加时。我们的发现表明，在所考虑的模拟设定中，计算效率获得了显著提升，而参数恢复性能总体相当。一个健康偏好的实证应用展示了当研究者能够估计更丰富的相关偏好设定时，可以获得哪些额外的实证证据以及模型拟合方面的统计收益。

我们提供了一个开源原型，用于采用AD-FP32策略工作流的模型估计，该工作流可自动检测硬件并提供最快的估计速度。更快的估计可以使更丰富的随机系数设定、更大的模拟抽样次数、自助法推断、多起点优化和稳健性检验在应用选择建模研究中更加可行。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Mixed logit estimation can become computationally demanding as sample size and the dimension of preference heterogeneity increase. This study examines how derivative computation, processor choice and floating-point precision affect estimation time and parameter recovery across problem sizes. This paper provides a controlled benchmark, comparing finite differences (FD) with automatic differentiation (AD), central processing unit (CPU) execution with graphics processing unit (GPU) execution, and double-precision (FP64) with single-precision (FP32) arithmetic using Monte Carlo experiments that hold the data-generating process, simulation draws, optimiser, starting values, and objective function fixed across strategies.
  The results show that AD is the dominant source of acceleration. Combining AD with FP32 arithmetic provides further gains, while combining AD, GPU execution, and FP32 arithmetic delivers the largest speed improvements, particularly as sample size and model complexity increase. Our findings indicate substantial computational gains with broadly comparable parameter-recovery performance in the simulated settings considered.An empirical application in health preferences illustrates what extra empirical evidence and statistical benefit on model fit could be achieved, when researchers are made possible to estimate a richer, correlated preference specification.
  We provide an open sourced prototype to for model estimation with AD-FP32 strategy workflow that automatically detect hardware and provide the fastest estimation. Faster estimation can make richer random-coefficient specifications, larger simulation draw counts, bootstrap inference, multi-start optimisation, and robustness checks more feasible in applied choice modelling research.
