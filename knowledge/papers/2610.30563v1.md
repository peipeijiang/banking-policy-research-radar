# Selling Evidence Bit by Bit

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30563v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究向竞争性买家出售一项资产的问题。在出售之前，卖方可以从中间人处购买关于该资产的硬信息，中间人设计并定价一个测试，以最大化其在各种均衡下的收益保证。该测试由一个或多个信号组成，卖方私下选择购买哪个信号子集，并向买家选择性披露其实现值。我们证明，中间人受益于将信息拆分为多个信号，并将其最大收益保证刻画为资产价值分布的累积熵。该保证通过一系列具有二元、独立同分布信号的“从顶部撇取”测试以及加性定价来实现。此外，任何能够实现最大保证的测试序列都会在极限中揭示资产的价值。对于二元价值，我们还刻画了任意给定信号数量下的收益保证，并证明它由一个对应于具有决定性好消息的“瑞士奶酪”失效模型的测试来实现。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the sale of an asset to competitive buyers. Before the sale, the seller can buy hard information about the asset from an intermediary who designs and prices a test to maximize his revenue guarantee across equilibria. The test consists of one or more signals, and the seller privately chooses which subset of signals to buy and selectively discloses their realization to the buyers. We show that the intermediary benefits from splitting information into multiple signals and characterize his maximal revenue guarantee as the cumulative entropy of the asset value distribution. This guarantee is achieved by a sequence of ``skim from the top'' tests with binary, i.i.d.\ signals and additive pricing. Furthermore, any sequence of tests that achieves the maximal guarantee reveals the asset's value in the limit. For binary values, we also characterize the revenue guarantee for any given number of signals, and show that it is achieved by a test that corresponds to a ``Swiss cheese" model of failure with conclusive good news.

</details>
