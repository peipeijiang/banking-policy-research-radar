<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人工智能使专业知识的利用成本降低，但其对专业服务企业的影响取决于客户如何重新分配工作以及供应商如何应对。我们构建了一个框架，将个体自我供给、企业自制或外购决策以及专业公司的定价联系起来。随着AI改进低价替代方案，客户可能在不将工作内部化的情况下，在不同外部供应商之间重新分配工作。如果流失的工作比留存的工作对价格更敏感，高端企业即使工作量份额下降，也可能提高其最优加成率。我们在美国法律服务领域考察了这些边际变化。在89个联邦司法区中，双重差分分析发现，截至2025财年，非囚犯自我代理相对于囚犯申诉增加了6.6个百分点，在调整既有趋势后仍然显著。相比之下，律师就业在2022年后没有明显向客户企业转移的迹象。法律AI基准测试揭示了在可靠完成复杂、多步骤工作方面持续存在的局限性，这提供了一种可能的解释。与此同时，最大的律师事务所在2025年报告了13%的收入增长，尽管计费工时几乎持平，而低价律所则经历了更强的需求增长。报告称AI整合程度在每律师收入较低的律所中也更强。这些模式凸显了工作分配变化和供应商竞争的重要性，尽管 underlying 再分配的大部分发生在生成式AI之前。这些发现说明了为什么更廉价的专业知识未必会导致广泛的内制化或高端专业服务价格的下降。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial intelligence makes specialized knowledge cheaper to use, but its effects on professional-service firms depend on how clients reallocate work and how suppliers respond. We develop a framework linking individual self-provision, enterprise make-or-buy decisions, and pricing by specialist firms. As AI improves lower-priced alternatives, clients may reallocate work across outside providers without bringing it in-house. If the departing work is more price-sensitive than the work that remains, premium firms may raise their optimal markups even as their share of work declines. We examine these margins in U.S. legal services. Across 89 federal districts, a difference-in-differences analysis finds a 6.6-percentage-point increase in non-prisoner self-representation relative to prisoner petitions by fiscal 2025, remaining substantial after adjusting for pre-existing trends. In contrast, lawyer employment shows no clear post-2022 shift toward client firms. Legal-AI benchmarks reveal continuing limitations in reliably completing complex, multi-step work, offering one possible explanation. Meanwhile, the largest law firms reported 13% revenue growth in 2025 despite nearly flat billable hours, while lower-priced firms experienced stronger demand growth. Reported AI integration is also stronger among firms with lower revenue per lawyer. These patterns highlight the importance of changes in work allocation and supplier competition, even as much of the underlying reallocation predates generative AI. The findings illustrate why cheaper expertise need not produce either widespread internalization or declining prices for premium professional services.
