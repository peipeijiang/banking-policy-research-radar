<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文介绍并分析了一种用于（实）斜对称数据的因子模型，此类数据在有向流以及用于排名的成对比较中自然出现。在信号加噪声框架下，我们分析了谱推断，并建立了信号强度中的BBP型相变。在高斯噪声下，我们推导了联合特征值涨落的极限。在分离阈值之上，只要总体信号强度具有重数一，估计的信号强度就是渐近正态的。对于一般的重数\(k\)，阈值之上相应样本特征值的联合极限涨落由缩放后的\(k\times k\)高斯酉系综矩阵的特征值给出。斜对称结构诱导出成对的特征空间，我们刻画了总体特征空间与样本特征空间之间的典范角。在阈值之下，相应的最大样本特征值收敛于半圆律的边缘，其涨落由Tracy--Widom \(F_2\)分布支配，这与实对称模型中常见的\(F_1\)律形成对比。该方法通过金融和人口学应用进行了说明。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The paper introduces and analyzes a factor model for (real) skew-symmetric data, which arise naturally in directed flows and in pairwise comparisons used for rankings. Working within a signal-plus-noise framework, we analyze spectral inference and establish a BBP-type phase transition in signal strength. Under Gaussian noise, we derive joint eigenvalue fluctuation limits. Above the separation threshold, the estimated signal strength is asymptotically normal whenever the population strength has multiplicity one. For a general multiplicity $k$, the joint limiting fluctuations of the corresponding sample eigenvalues above the threshold are given by the eigenvalues of a scaled $k\times k$ Gaussian unitary ensemble matrix. The skew-symmetric structure induces paired eigenspaces, and we characterize the canonical angles between the population and sample eigenspaces. Below the threshold, the corresponding largest sample eigenvalue converges to the edge of the semicircle law, with fluctuations governed by the Tracy--Widom $F_2$ distribution, in contrast to the $F_1$ law familiar from real symmetric models. The approach is illustrated through financial and demographic applications.
