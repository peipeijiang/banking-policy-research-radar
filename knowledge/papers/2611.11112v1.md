# The Effect of the U.S. Federal Production Tax Credit on Negative Energy Prices

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11112v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用2000-2023年美国东部互联电网批发电力市场数据，首次因果识别联邦生产税收抵免（PTC）对负电价概率的影响，发现PTC合格风电发电量每增加1%使负电价概率上升3.4个百分点，但PTC仅能解释约10%的可归因负电价。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

负电价在电力市场中频繁出现，生产补贴通常被认为是其主要成因。利用2000—2023年美国东部互联电网（Eastern Interconnection）批发电力市场的数据，我们发现，在所有出现负能源价格的小时中，至少有18%不能仅归因于联邦生产税收抵免（production tax credit, PTC）——该政策是研究期内一项规模庞大且持续存在的生产补贴。其次，我们提供了PTC导致负能源价格的首个因果证据。平均而言，符合PTC资格的风力发电量每增加1%，负能源价格出现的概率增加3.4个百分点。然而，不同电力市场之间存在显著的异质性，部分市场所显示的效果高出一个数量级。此外，对于观测到的、可能归因于PTC的负电价，PTC仅能解释其中的10%。最后，我们利用该因果估计值计算了在能源价格为负时PTC补贴所产生的环境效益的合理价值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Negative prices frequently arise in electricity markets and production subsidies are commonly cited as a leading cause. Using data from the U.S. Eastern Interconnection wholesale electricity markets for the period 2000-2023, we document that at least 18 percent of the total hours with negative energy prices cannot solely be attributed to the federal production tax credit (PTC), a large, persistent production subsidy during the period of study. Next, we provide the first causal evidence that the PTC leads to negative energy prices. On average, increasing PTC-eligible wind generation by 1 percent increases the probability of a negative energy price by 3.4 percentage points. However, significant heterogeneity exists across electricity markets, and some markets show an effect that is an order of magnitude larger. Furthermore, the PTC explains only 10 percent of the observed negative prices that could potentially be attributable to it. Finally, we use the causal estimate to calculate a plausible value for the environmental benefits accrued from the PTC subsidy when energy prices are negative.
