<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究双方均存在无差异、且企业对工人集合的偏好为公开已知的有限多对一匹配市场。一种标准方法是均匀随机地打破平局并运行延迟接受算法，该算法对工人具有策略防操纵性，但可能选择一个在代理人原始偏好下被帕累托支配的弱稳定匹配。对于每家企业偏好均允许总替代（gross substitutes, GS）表示的广泛市场类别，我们构造了一种确定性广义延迟接受机制，该机制具有弱稳定性、对双方均帕累托有效、工人弱群体策略防操纵性，并且在给定GS表示的值预言机时可在强多项式时间内实现。该领域包括非响应性企业偏好，例如拟阵约束下的加性得分和分配值。我们的分析将带无差异的序数匹配与不完全可转移效用下的竞争均衡联系起来。尽管该机制仅使用序数比较，但它返回一个分层辅助市场的卖方最优均衡匹配，而该市场的均衡结构产生了稳定性、效率和激励保证。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study finite many-to-one matching markets with indifferences on both sides and publicly known firm preferences over sets of workers. A standard approach breaks ties uniformly at random and runs deferred acceptance, which is strategy-proof for workers but may select a weakly stable matching that is Pareto dominated under the agents' original preferences. For the broad class of markets in which each firm's preferences admit a gross substitutes (GS) representation, we construct a deterministic generalized deferred acceptance mechanism that is weakly stable, Pareto efficient with respect to both sides, worker weakly group strategy-proof, and implementable in strongly polynomial time given value oracles for the GS representations. This domain includes nonresponsive firm preferences, such as additive scores under matroid constraints and assignment values. Our analysis bridges ordinal matching with indifferences and competitive equilibria under imperfectly transferable utility. Although the mechanism uses only ordinal comparisons, it returns a seller-optimal equilibrium matching of a tiered auxiliary market, and the equilibrium structure of this market yields the stability, efficiency, and incentive guarantees.
