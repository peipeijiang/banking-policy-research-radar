<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种异方差稳健且维度自适应的检验方法，用于稀疏高维线性回归模型中的多重线性约束。该方法将去稀疏化LASSO估计与岭正则化二次型统计量以及适应约束维度的临界值相结合。在所述正则条件下，我们的方法在固定或发散数量的约束下渐近有效，而无需预先指定渐近机制。我们的随机设计理论允许具有边际次Weibull尾部的独立非同分布协变量行，以及满足有限多项式矩的异方差误差。蒙特卡洛模拟表明，相对于传统Wald检验，该方法在有限样本下具有更好的规模控制，尤其是当约束数量增加时。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a heteroskedasticity-robust and dimention-adaptive test for multiple linear restric- tions in sparse high-dimensional linear regression models. It combines desparsified LASSO estimation with a ridge-regularized quadratic form statistic and critical values that adapt to the restriction dimension. Under stated regularity conditions, our approach is asymptotically valid under a fixed or diverging number of restrictions without specifying the asymptotic regime in advance. Our random-design theory allows independent non-identically distributed covari- ate rows with marginal sub-Weibull tails and heteroskedastic errors satisfying finite polynomial moments. Monte Carlo simulations demonstrate improved finite-sample size control relative to conventional Wald tests, particularly as the number of restrictions increases.

</details>
