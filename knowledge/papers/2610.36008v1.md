<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

生成式人工智能改变了不同形式人力资本的稀缺性。它使编码化知识在生产环节可以低成本获取，而通过实践获得的判断力则仍然与劳动者更为紧密地绑定。我们追问这对工资结构中两个熟悉的价格——教育回报和经验回报——会产生什么影响。在短期框架下，机器提供的编码化知识直接替代劳动者人力资本中的编码化成分，从而降低教育梯度。如果编码化知识与基于实践的知识是互补的，那么在实践中学习最快的领域，经验回报会上升；技术特定经验也可能变得过时，从而使经验曲线发生旋转而非均匀平移。我们使用2018年至2026年6月的美国当前人口调查（Current Population Survey）收入数据，比较ChatGPT发布前Felten-Raj-Seamans暴露指数最高四分位与最低四分位中的职业。在ChatGPT公开发布后，暴露职业中每一年学校教育的回报下降0.55个对数点。每一年经验的回报在潜在经验为三年时上升0.26个对数点，在十五年时不变，在二十五年时下降0.12个对数点；在基准二次设定下，该效应在18.1年处过零。这些断点随发布而出现，在疫情安慰剂检验下消失，在完整样本采用统一删失规则后仍然存在，并且在允许每个职业拥有自身冲击前教育梯度和经验梯度时依然成立。这一模式是暴露职业内部人力资本的重新定价：可编码知识变得不那么稀缺，而经验的价值越来越取决于它是否能迁移到新技术。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Generative AI changes the scarcity of different forms of human capital. It makes codified knowledge cheaply available at the point of production, while judgment acquired through practice remains tied more closely to the worker. We ask what this does to two familiar prices in the wage structure: the return to schooling and the return to experience. In a short-run framework, machine-supplied codified knowledge substitutes directly for the codified component of worker human capital, lowering the schooling gradient. If codified and practice-based knowledge are complements, the return to experience rises where learning through practice is fastest; technology-specific experience can also become obsolete, rotating the experience profile rather than shifting it uniformly. Using US Current Population Survey earnings data from 2018 through June 2026, we compare occupations in the top and bottom quartiles of the pre-ChatGPT Felten-Raj-Seamans exposure index. After the public release of ChatGPT, the return to a year of schooling falls by 0.55 log points in exposed occupations. The return to a year of experience rises by 0.26 log points at three years of potential experience, is unchanged at fifteen, and falls by 0.12 log points at twenty-five; under the baseline quadratic, the effect crosses zero at 18.1 years. These breaks appear with the release, disappear under a pandemic placebo, survive a common censoring rule across the full sample, and remain when each occupation is allowed its own pre-shock schooling and experience gradients. The pattern is a repricing of human capital inside exposed occupations: codifiable knowledge becomes less scarce, while the value of experience depends increasingly on whether it transfers to the new technology.
