<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

大多数关于人工智能与就业的分析都在追问哪些岗位会受到影响。本文追问的是生产专家的管道会发生什么。本文将Caosun和Aral（2026）的模型从单一劳动者扩展到拥有两个 cohort（初级和高级）的企业，研究当可编码的工作被自动化后，专业知识（expertise）的形成如何变化。由于初级工作是最可编码的，对初级员工的需求最先下降；高级员工的存量随后下降，但降幅更大，因为管道停止补充退休的专家。在基准校准中，初级需求在触底时比2022年水平低约五分之一，随后恢复了大部分失地，而高级存量下降四分之一，之后减半。由于专业知识通过在没有AI辅助的情况下对AI无法完成的工作进行实践而形成，从内部培养一名专家所需的时间从约五年上升到九年。成本落在与决定采用AI的不同一方身上：一位具有三年视野的管理者设定AI使用以最大化近期产出，将工资支出削减21%，而企业在AI无法完成的工作上的能力下降五分之二，且一旦管道变得稀薄，其在该类工作上的产出将无人服务。两个关键参数尚未被测量，因此结果是情景而非预测。本文提出组织可以追踪的三个指标：初级员工在AI无法完成的工作上获得的无人辅助实践、高级员工在此类工作上花费的时间，以及培养一名专家所需的年数。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Most analyses of AI and employment ask which jobs are exposed. This paper asks what happens to the pipeline that produces experts. It extends the model of Caosun and Aral (2026) from a single worker to a firm with two cohorts, juniors and seniors, and studies how expertise formation changes when codified work is automated. Because junior work is the most codified, demand for juniors falls first; the stock of seniors falls later but further, as the pipeline stops replacing the experts who retire. In the baseline calibration, junior demand bottoms out about a fifth below its 2022 level before recovering most of that ground, while the senior stock falls by a quarter and later halves. Because expertise forms through unassisted practice on work that AI cannot do, the time needed to develop an expert from within rises from about five years to nine. The costs fall on a different party from the one that decides on adoption: a manager with a three-year horizon sets AI use to maximise near-term output, cutting the wage bill by 21 percent while the capacity of the firm on work AI cannot do falls by two-fifths and its output on that work goes unserved once the pipeline runs thin. Two key parameters are not yet measured, so the results are scenarios rather than forecasts. The paper proposes three indicators organisations can track: the unaided practice juniors get on work AI cannot do, senior time spent on such work, and the years it takes to develop an expert.
