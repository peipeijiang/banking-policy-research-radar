<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

生成式人工智能重新定位了自动化的固定成本。模型提供方支付费用以训练前沿系统，而下游企业按使用量租用能力；同一企业必须持续承担工资支出，以提供人工增强型服务。我们在一个具有家庭预算和市场出清工资的本地服务经济中研究这种不对称性。人工增强从额外客户中获取更大的剩余，而自动化则具有更低的盈亏平衡规模。工资支出支撑着跨部门的需求。工资调整对这一反馈产生抑制作用，但通常不会完全抵消它：当工资—收入效应超过留存工人工资总额的下降时，生产模式便成为战略互补。此时，经济可能同时存在高需求的人工增强均衡和低需求的自动化均衡。前者是局部最优结果，即使弹性工资阻止了企业利润的排序。在具有前瞻性企业和交错修订机会的情况下，相同的既有就业结构可以支撑自动化级联或增强型复苏；后期采纳者的预期路径验证了先行者的选择。在Frankel和Pauzner的正则性与边界条件下，一个公共总体冲击选择唯一的状态依赖路径，而摩擦消失极限则根据风险占优进行选择。一个以专业服务收入与工资支出比率锚定的透明参数化示例，展示了高自主性用途如何进入协调区域，以及工资调整如何压缩该区域。最优政策将需求溢出所产生的采纳楔子与低状态局部自我维持时的临时桥梁相结合。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Generative AI relocates the fixed cost of automation. A model provider pays to train a frontier system, while a downstream firm rents capability by usage; the same firm must carry a continuing payroll to supply a human-augmented service. We study this asymmetry in a local service economy with household budgets and a market-clearing wage. Human augmentation earns a larger surplus from an additional customer, whereas automation has the lower break-even scale. Payroll supports demand across sectors. Wage adjustment works against this feedback but does not generally undo it: when the wage-income effect dominates the fall in the wage bill per retained worker, production modes are strategic complements. The economy can then possess both a high-demand human-augmented equilibrium and a low-demand automated equilibrium. The former is the local first best, even when flexible wages prevent a firm-profit ranking. With forward-looking firms and staggered revision opportunities, the same inherited employment structure can support an automation cascade or an augmentation recovery; the anticipated path of later adopters validates the first movers' choices. Under the regularity and boundary conditions of Frankel and Pauzner, a public aggregate shock selects a unique state-contingent path, while the vanishing-friction limit selects according to risk dominance. A transparent parameterization anchored to professional-services revenue-to-payroll ratios illustrates how high-autonomy uses can enter the coordination region and how wage adjustment compresses that region. Optimal policy combines the adoption wedge created by demand spillovers with a temporary bridge when the low state is locally self-sustaining.

</details>
