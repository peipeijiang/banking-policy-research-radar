# Changing inflation perception and the propagation of news shocks

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

摘要 基于先前建立的基于文本数据的指标——通胀感知指标（IPI），我们分析了与通胀相关的媒体报道所包含的信息内容。本文认为，媒体并非以恒定或系统性的方式报道通胀，而是对特定事件作出反应，这意味着报道具有状态依赖性。通过使用潜在狄利克雷分配（LDA）主题建模方法的动态扩展，我们考察了2001年至2024年间德国主要报纸发表的50,000篇文章中通胀的显著性与框架。该方法允许将IPI分解为动态的、特定主题的子指标。为分析这些新构建指标的信息内容，我们基于新凯恩斯宏观经济模型中的变量，并设定两种通胀机制，估计了一个阈值贝叶斯向量自回归（BVAR）模型。我们的结果表明，特定主题的IPI子指标包含关于价格变动的不同信息。此外，我们发现了部分证据表明，在高通胀和低通胀机制下，家庭在调整其通胀预期时对这些信息的利用方式有所不同。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Abstract Based on a previously established text data-based indicator, the Inflation Perception Indicator (IPI), we analyze the information content of inflation-related media coverage. It is argued that media do not report on inflation in a constant or systematic manner but instead react to specific events, which means that reporting is state-dependent. Using a dynamic extension of the Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) topic modeling approach we examine the salience and framing of inflation in a corpus of 50,000 articles published by major German newspapers between 2001 and 2024. This approach allows to decompose the IPI into dynamic topic-specific subindicators. To analyze the information content of these newly constructed indicators, we estimate a threshold Bayesian Vector AutoRegressive (BVAR) model for variables from a New Keynesian macroeconomic model with two inflation regimes. Our results suggest that the topic-specific IPI subindicators contain different information about price developments. Moreover, we find some evidence that households make different use of this information in high- and low-inflation regimes when adjusting their inflation expectations.
