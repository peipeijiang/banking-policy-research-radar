<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

非政府组织在为社会成果赋予相似价值的同时，常常争夺资金。筹款可能会扩大对某一事业的总捐赠额，也可能将既定的资金池重新导向焦点组织。本文研究跨组织的宗旨一致性如何改变这种竞争的构成。分析将筹款效应分为两种：对总资金的边际\emph{提升}，以及资金在组织之间的边际\emph{转移}。不完全一致性会在非政府组织的边际使命回报与总边际使命回报之间形成一个楔子，其大小与转移减提升成正比。当非政府组织在筹款技术之间进行选择时，更高的宗旨一致性会将努力转向相对更偏向提升的技术。相对于总使命基准，不完全一致的非政府组织会选择过于偏向转移的组合。对筹款施加统一限制可能会改变总努力，但通常无法纠正这种构成扭曲；纠正该组合需要改变不同技术之间的相对激励。一个简单的微观基础将事业推广解释为提升，将组织推广解释为转移。生产率差异在重新配置提高使命产出时仍会保留这种重新配置，而组织特定收益即使在完全一致下也会恢复竞争性。因此，该模型预测了共同宗旨如何改变竞争的形式，即使其对竞争水平的影响并不明确。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Non-governmental organizations often compete for funding while valuing similar social outcomes. Fundraising may expand total giving to a cause or redirect a given funding pool toward the focal organization. This paper studies how cross-organizational purpose alignment changes the composition of such competition. The analysis separates two effects of fundraising: marginal \emph{lift} in aggregate funding and marginal \emph{shift} of funding between organizations. Incomplete alignment creates a wedge between an NGO's marginal mission return and the aggregate mission return that is proportional to shift minus lift. When NGOs choose among fundraising technologies, greater purpose alignment shifts effort toward the relatively lift-intensive technology. Relative to the aggregate mission benchmark, incompletely aligned NGOs choose portfolios that are too shift-intensive. A uniform restriction on fundraising may change total effort but cannot generally correct this composition distortion; correcting the portfolio requires changing relative incentives across technologies. A simple microfoundation interprets cause promotion as lift and organization promotion as shift. Productivity differences preserve reallocation when it raises mission output, while organization-specific benefits restore rivalry even under complete alignment. The model therefore predicts how a common purpose changes the form of competition even when its effect on the level of competition is ambiguous.

</details>
