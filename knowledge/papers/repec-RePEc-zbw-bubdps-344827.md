<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文基于2008年以来MiFID I和MiFID II交易报告，构建了德国国债（Bunds，欧元区基准安全资产）的非匿名交易级数据集。我们表明，一个集中的交易商核心通过持久的交易商-客户关系对这一安全资产进行中介。交易在投资者部门之间呈现分割特征，订单流分散于双边OTC和电子交易平台。识别出的压力流显示，外国官方账户在价格因抢筹现金而下跌或因避险需求而上涨时均供给德国国债。对冲基金在两个情形中均吸收德国国债，而一级交易商在新冠疫情期间吸收抛售但在财政供给消息后分销德国国债。对冲基金和官方部门账户承载了最具永久性价格相关的订单流，但支付中等有效半价差，而零售家庭支付的有效半价差远高于其他参与者。执行成本并不随订单流的价格含量单调递增。我们评估了针对这一结构的两项改革：强制中央清算将使交易商在德国国债中的净结算义务减少35%，低于对美国国债和英国金边债券的可比估计。在基准结构校准中，平台集中化降低了福利，因为在交易商竞争不完美的情况下，更广泛交易商准入带来的匹配收益无法抵消损失的关系盈余。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper constructs a non-anonymous transaction-level dataset for German Bunds, the euro area's benchmark safe asset, from MiFID I and MiFID II transaction reports since 2008. We show that a concentrated dealer core intermediates this safe asset through persis- tent dealer-client relationships. Trading is segmented across investor sectors, with order flow fragmented across bilateral OTC and electronic venues. Identified stress flows show foreign official accounts supplying Bunds whether prices fall in a dash for cash or rise in a flight to quality. Hedge funds absorb Bunds in both episodes, while primary dealers absorb Covid selling but distribute Bunds after fiscal supply news. Hedge funds and official sector accounts carry the most permanent price-relevant flow yet pay middling effective half-spreads, while retail households pay by far the highest. Execution costs do not increase monotonically with the price content of flow. We evaluate two reforms targeting this structure: mandatory central clearing would cut dealers' net settlement obligations in Bunds by 35%, less than comparable estimates for U.S. Treasuries and U.K. Gilts. In the benchmark structural cali- bration, platform centralization lowers welfare because matching gains from broader dealer access do not offset lost relationship surplus under imperfect dealer competition.
