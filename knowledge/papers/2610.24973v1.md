# Improved Approximation and Tight Competition for Simple Auctions

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究针对一个加性估值买家的简单拍卖，该买家对 $m$ 件物品的估值相互独立。记 $\mathrm{REV}$ 为最优收入，$\mathrm{SREV}$ 和 $\mathrm{BREV}$ 分别为分开销售和大捆绑销售的收入，$\mathrm{BSPA}_2$ 为两个独立竞标者下大捆绑第二价格拍卖的收入。对于独立的规则物品，我们证明 $\mathrm{REV}\le\mathrm{BSPA}_2\le2\mathrm{BREV}$。这建立了规则性条件下捆绑销售的紧近似保证和竞争保证，并回答了 Beyhaghi 等人（EC 2025）提出的一个开放问题。对于一般独立物品，我们证明 $\mathrm{REV} \le \mathrm{SREV} + \frac{π^2}{6}\mathrm{BREV} \le 2.645 \max\{\mathrm{SREV}, \mathrm{BREV}\}$。这改进了 Babaioff、Immorlica、Lucier 和 Weinberg（FOCS 2014, JACM 2020）此前的近似因子 $6$ 以及 Ma 和 Simchi-Levi（AISTATS 2021）的 $5.2$。在下界方面，我们构造了一个实例，其中三个不相交捆绑包的价格所获收入超过 $2.11\max\{\mathrm{SREV},\mathrm{BREV}\}$，改进了 Rubinstein（ITCS 2016）此前的下界 $2$。我们还证明，在最坏情况下，通过分开销售和捆绑销售中较优者来精确恢复一个买家的最优收入需要 $Θ(\log m)$ 个竞标者。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study simple auctions for one additive buyer whose values for $m$ items are independent. Write $\mathrm{REV}$ for optimal revenue, $\mathrm{SREV}$ and $\mathrm{BREV}$ for the revenues from separate selling and grand bundling, and $\mathrm{BSPA}_2$ for grand-bundle second-price revenue with two independent bidders. For independent regular items, we prove $\mathrm{REV}\le\mathrm{BSPA}_2\le2\mathrm{BREV}$. This establishes tight approximation and competition guarantees for bundling under regularity and answers an open question from Beyhaghi et al. (EC 2025). For general independent items, we prove $\mathrm{REV} \le \mathrm{SREV} + \frac{π^2}{6}\mathrm{BREV} \le 2.645 \max\{\mathrm{SREV}, \mathrm{BREV}\}$. This improves the previous approximation factors of $6$ from Babaioff, Immorlica, Lucier, and Weinberg (FOCS 2014, JACM 2020) and $5.2$ from Ma and Simchi-Levi (AISTATS 2021). On the lower bound side, we construct an instance in which prices for three disjoint bundles earn more than $2.11\max\{\mathrm{SREV},\mathrm{BREV}\}$, improving the previous lower bound of $2$ from Rubinstein (ITCS 2016). We also show that exact recovery of one buyer's optimal revenue by the better of separate selling and bundling requires $Θ(\log m)$ bidders in the worst case.
