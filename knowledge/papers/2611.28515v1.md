<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究具有有限参与者和行动的非对称纯协调博弈中的均衡选择问题。一个对数正则化从均匀策略剖面出发，定义了一条内部均衡的追踪型路径。我们将均衡条件约化为单峰标量函数，并证明当某一关联收益指数唯一最小时，从均匀剖面出发的连通均衡分支收敛到相应的协调结果。在两人博弈情形下，该条件等价于最大化平方根收益之和，从而将已知的两行动结果推广至有限多行动。一个三人示例表明，平方根规则一般不能推广到更多参与者。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study equilibrium selection in asymmetric pure coordination games with finitely many players and actions. A logarithmic regularization defines a tracing-type path of interior equilibria from the uniform strategy profile. We reduce the equilibrium conditions to single-peaked scalar functions and show that, when one associated payoff index is uniquely minimal, the connected equilibrium branch from the uniform profile converges to the corresponding coordinated outcome. With two players, this condition is equivalent to maximizing the sum of square-root payoffs, extending the known two-action result to finitely many actions. A three-player example shows that the square-root rule does not generally extend to more players.
