<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当个体自行决定其活动与合作者时，团队便会自发形成。我构建并检验了一个理论，其中团队在共识基础上形成，互动反映团队构成，收益具有外部性和多种形式的拥塞效应。稳定性约束涉及群体互动，并要求每位团队成员愿意留在团队中，而不是退出或独自开展项目。对美联储理事会经济学家之间研究合作的实证分析表明：(i) 这些约束有助于解释观察到的团队，其作用超出合作收益的影响；(ii) 估计的违规情况指向大型新进入者团队，这些团队似乎是被指派形成的；(iii) 一种补偿机制缓解了政策工作量变化的影响，而政策工作量似乎对团队构成的影响大于对总研究产出的影响；(iv) 将工作量转移给经验丰富的经济学家会增加新进入者的研究产出与合作，代价是经验丰富的经济学家产出下降，他们通过与新进入者增加互动来补偿这一政策工作量的转移。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Teams emerge organically when individuals decide on their own their activities and collaborators. I develop and test a theory in which teams form with consent, interactions reflect team composition, and payoffs feature externalities and several forms of congestion. Stability constraints concern group interactions and require each team member to be willing to remain rather than withdraw or pursue a project alone. Empirical analysis of research collaborations among economists at the Federal Reserve Board suggests that: (i.) these constraints help explain observed teams beyond the payoffs to collaboration; (ii.) estimated violations point to large teams of entrants that appear to be assigned; (iii.) a compensatory mechanism mitigates the effects of variation in policy workload, which appears to affect team composition more than total research output; and (iv.) shifting workload toward experienced economists increases entrants' research output and collaboration, at the cost of lower output among experienced economists who compensate for this shift in policy workload with increased interactions with entrants.
