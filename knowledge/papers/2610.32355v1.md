<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个模型，其中人工智能降低写作成本的速度快于降低阅读成本的速度。作者在声誉上存在差异，而时间受限的读者（如审稿人）在忽略、验证和信任论文之间进行选择。随着写作成本下降，被验证论文的范围缩小：外部作者的论文不再被阅读，知名作者的论文也不再被核查。在“无成本写作极限”下，被验证论文的份额消失，论文的影响力仅取决于其作者的声誉。读者可以通过让AI对文献进行综述来逃避洪流。只有当这些综述的错误足够独立时，它们才能可靠地聚合论文。一旦使用同一AI撰写的论文份额超过阈值，综述就会继承该AI的错误，而AI审稿会过滤掉那些本可以纠正这些错误的原创研究。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a model in which artificial intelligence lowers the cost of writing faster than the cost of reading. Authors differ in reputation, and time-constrained readers, such as referees, choose between ignoring, verifying, and trusting papers. As writing costs fall, the band of verified papers shrinks: outsiders are no longer read, and established authors are no longer checked. In the ``costless writing limit,'' the share of verified papers vanishes, and a paper's impact depends on its author's reputation alone. Readers can escape the flood by asking AI to summarize the literature. These summaries aggregate papers reliably only if their errors are sufficiently independent. Once the share of papers written with the same AI exceeds a threshold, summaries inherit the AI's errors, and AI review filters out the original research that could correct them.
