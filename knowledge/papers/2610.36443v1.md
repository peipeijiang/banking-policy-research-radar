<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们针对未平滑的固定效应分位数回归估计量，建立了一个大$n$、大$T$的理论，与Hahn和Newey（2004）针对平滑非线性面板模型的工作相平行。基于剖面准则的一个精确秩得分恒等式（rank-score identity）以及凸性论证，我们以闭式形式给出了斜率估计量的主导附带参数偏误（incidental parameter bias），其阶数为$T^{-1}$。当$n$和$T$以相同速率增长时，该估计量是渐近正态的，但一般不是中心化的，且传统区间无效。$n$相对于$T$的容许增长范围，至多可达$n=o(T^3)$，取决于条件误差密度的正则性。分样本面板刀切法（split-panel jackknife）可在整个范围内消除主导偏误，并给出有效的推断。在依赖性按几何速率或多项式速率衰减的序列相关面板中，偏误获得一个来自不同时期回归变量与误差之间依赖性的项，刀切法在连续但非交错的半面板下仍然有效，聚类标准误也是有效的。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a large-$n$, large-$T$ theory for the unsmoothed fixed-effects quantile regression estimator, paralleling Hahn and Newey (2004) for smooth nonlinear panel models. An exact rank-score identity for the profiled criterion, with a convexity argument, delivers the leading incidental parameter bias of the slope estimator, of order $T^{-1}$, in closed form. When $n$ and $T$ grow at the same rate, the estimator is asymptotically normal but in general not centred, and conventional intervals are invalid. Admissible growth of $n$ relative to $T$, up to $n=o(T^3)$, depends on the regularity of the conditional error densities. A split-panel jackknife removes the leading bias throughout this range and yields valid inference. In serially dependent panels whose dependence decays geometrically or polynomially, the bias acquires a term from the dependence between regressors and errors of different periods, the jackknife remains valid with contiguous but not with interleaved half-panels, and clustered standard errors are valid.

</details>
