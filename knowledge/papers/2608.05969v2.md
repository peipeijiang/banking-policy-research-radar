<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

问题定义。当生成式AI产出的专家级成果使客户无法将其与合格服务提供商的成果区分开来时，经典的基于成本的质量信号便失效，唯有基于结果的或有承诺才能区分不同服务类型。此类承诺所认证的是一种内生的、易损耗的资产：企业通过让员工持续参与AI所处理的案例而构建的人力后备能力，这种能力若不加以维持便会逐渐退化。既有研究未阐明该机制在何种条件下成立。我们探究的是，在不同职业与责任制度下，这一机制在哪些情境中仍具有信息传递作用。

方法/结果。我们在企业所设定的责任上限与承载信息量的留存风险敞口之间引入了一个制度性楔子，该楔子由四项法律要素生成：费用承担规则、惩罚性条款的可执行性、国家责任或共同赔偿对私人责任的替代，以及对合同责任的强制性限制。该楔子将可分离的类型空间压缩为一个信号窗口，其上界由偿付能力与惩罚原则决定，下界则受制于标准条款控制下责任上限低于某一阈值即告无效的规则。我们基于公开证据对五种职业和七个法域进行了校准。在普通法系的约定损害赔偿渠道中，当可验证性低于1/m时，可证明性加价机制便不可用，从而将合同问题转化为运营问题。可验证性投资在责任上限约束生效时拓宽信号窗口，但在责任下限约束生效时反而收窄该窗口。在基于主体的市场模拟中，在所测试的政策类别内，所有空窗口单元均收敛至零参与度与技能崩溃。

管理启示。责任制度不仅是风险分配工具，更是劳动力能力管理手段。企业应针对各法域中的约束边际精准施策；责任下限与共同赔偿机制均会抑制维持后备能力所需的信号。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Problem definition. When generative AI produces expert artifacts clients cannot distinguish from a competent provider's, the classical cost-based quality signal collapses and only outcome-contingent commitments can separate types. Such a commitment certifies an endogenous, perishable asset: the human fallback capability a firm builds by keeping staff engaged with cases the AI handles, eroding otherwise. Prior work is silent on where that mechanism holds. We ask where, across occupations and liability institutions, it remains informative.
  Methodology/results. We introduce an institutional wedge between the liability cap a firm posts and the retained exposure that carries information, generated by four legal primitives: the cost rule, the enforceability of penalty clauses, the displacement of private liability by state liability or pooled indemnity, and mandatory limits on contractual liability. The wedge compresses the separating type space into a signaling window, bounded above by solvency and the penalty doctrine and below where standard-terms control voids caps beneath a threshold. We calibrate five occupations and seven jurisdictions on published evidence. In common-law agreed-damages channels the provability gross-up is unavailable whenever verifiability falls below 1/m, turning a contracting problem into an operational one. Verifiability investment widens the window where the ceiling binds but narrows it where the cap floor binds. In an agent-based market, within the tested policy class, every empty-window cell converges to zero engagement and skill collapse.
  Managerial implications. Liability institutions are a workforce-capability instrument, not merely a risk-allocation device. Firms should target the binding margin in each jurisdiction; cap floors and pooled indemnity each suppress the signal sustaining fallback capacity.
