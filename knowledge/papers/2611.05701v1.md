<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

信息设计者（information designer），例如大语言模型和在线平台，往往不知道其接收者或用户的主观信念。我们构建了学习算法，使设计者能够通过重复交互学习策略性接收者的信念。我们的学习算法对接收者针对设计者学习过程的策略性操纵具有鲁棒性。我们研究相对于两个基准的遗憾（regret），以衡量算法的性能。静态基准是设计者在已知信念下单期最优值的$T$倍。动态基准更强，刻画了设计者在已知信念下的全局动态最优性。我们的学习算法使设计者能够以$O(\frac{\log^2 T}{T})$的快速收敛速度对两个基准均实现无遗憾，显著快于文献中的其他方法。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Information designers, such as large language models and online platforms, often do not know the subjective beliefs of their receivers or users. We construct learning algorithms enabling the designer to learn the belief of a strategic receiver through repeated interactions. Our learning algorithms are robust to the receiver's strategic manipulation of the learning process of the designer. We study regret relative to two benchmarks to measure the performance of the algorithms. The static benchmark is $T$ times the single-period optimum for the designer under a known belief. The dynamic benchmark, which is stronger, characterizes global dynamic optimality for the designer under a known belief. Our learning algorithms allow the designer to achieve no regret against both benchmarks at fast convergence speeds of $O(\frac{\log^2 T}{T})$, significantly faster than other approaches in the literature.
