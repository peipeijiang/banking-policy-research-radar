<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们在早前的一篇论文中（Getty和Tkachenko 2025b），根据94个国家在1980—2021年间报告的净储蓄和市值资本数据提出，在全球尺度上，净储蓄对资本增长几乎没有影响，甚至没有影响。在此，我们将研究向前追溯至每个国家有报告或可重建这些数据的最早年份。我们发现，在集体尺度上，净储蓄对资本增长作出贡献的可能性边际更小（1.6%对6.4%）。我们还提出并论证了对资本增长的另一种解释，并建议在經濟分析中赋予现金流（cash flow）概念更大的作用。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We argued in an earlier paper (Getty and Tkachenko 2025b), from data reported for net saving and market-value capital by 94 nations over the period 1980-2021, that net saving has had little or no effect on capital growth at the global scale. Here we extend the study backward to the earliest year, in each country, for which those data have been reported or reconstructed. We find a still smaller margin of possibility (1.6% versus 6.4%) of contribution of net saving to capital growth at the collective scale. We also propose and defend an alternative explanation of capital growth, and suggest a larger role for the cash flow concept in economic analysis.
