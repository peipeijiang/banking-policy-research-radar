<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究了具有连续内生变量和工具变量的可加可分三角模型的非参数识别与估计，并允许第一阶段方程不可分离。在工具变量与不可观测项独立的假设下，我们证明了结果函数具有作为条件累积分布函数泛函的闭式表达式。由此得到的代入估计量无需正则化，并以$n^{-m/(2m+1)}$的速率收敛，其中$m$为模型所施加的光滑阶数。此外，任何结果函数的估计量都无法以更快的速率收敛。我们使用经验自助法构造了一个均匀置信带，该置信带能同时覆盖紧集上每一点的结果函数。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies the nonparametric identification and estimation of additively separable triangular models with continuous endogenous and instrumental variables, allowing for a nonseparable first-stage equation. Under the independence of instrumental variables and unobservables, we show that the outcome function possesses a closed-form expression as a functional of conditional cumulative distribution functions. The resulting plug-in estimators require no regularization and converge at the rate $n^{-m/(2m+1)}$, where $m$ is the order of smoothness the model imposes. Also, no estimator of the outcome function converges faster. We use the empirical bootstrap to construct a uniform confidence band that covers the outcome function at every point of a compact set simultaneously.
