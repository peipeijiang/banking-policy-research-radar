<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

大型足球赛事会带动经济信心吗？国家队是否参赛又是否重要？我们将欧盟委员会1991年至2025年间对27个欧盟成员国的统一企业与消费者调查，与17届世界杯和欧洲杯的数据相结合。赛事月份的经济信心指标（Economic Sentiment Indicator）比同一日历年其他月份高出约2.3点，但在赛事前两个月也同样偏高；消费者信心则未呈现稳健的模式。由于赛事日历对所有国家相同，且几乎所有赛事都落在偶数年，这一关联仅基于17项赛事，无法与情绪中更广泛的两年周期节奏相区分；相对于随机抽取的年份，这一关联在2020年之前不寻常，而在全样本中仅为边际显著（p = 0.08至0.14）。参赛情况因国家而异，可在较弱假设下通过完整时间固定效应进行检验。我们在基准设定中未发现统计显著的参赛溢价，且在任何设定中均未发现开赛时刻参赛特有的跳跃；较大的溢价难以与数据相吻合，较小的溢价则仍有可能。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Do major football tournaments move economic sentiment, and does it matter whether a country's national team takes part? We combine the European Commission's harmonized business and consumer surveys for the 27 EU member states from 1991 to 2025 with 17 World Cups and European Championships. The Economic Sentiment Indicator is about 2.3 points higher in tournament months than in other months of the same calendar year, but it is similarly elevated in the two months before; consumer confidence shows no robust pattern. Because the tournament calendar is common to all countries and almost all tournaments fall in even years, this association rests on 17 events and cannot be separated from a broader two-year rhythm in sentiment; relative to randomly drawn years, it is unusual before 2020 and marginal in the full sample (p = 0.08 to 0.14). Participation varies across countries and can be tested with full time fixed effects, under weaker assumptions. We find no statistically significant participation premium in the baseline specification and no participation-specific jump at kick-off in any specification; large premia are hard to reconcile with the data, smaller ones remain possible.
