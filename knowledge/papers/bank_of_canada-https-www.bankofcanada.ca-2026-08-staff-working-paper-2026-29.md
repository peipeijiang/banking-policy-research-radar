# Time Use and Consumption Expenditures

[查看原文](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/08/staff-working-paper-2026-29/)

## 一句话结论

> 本文发现家庭在衰退期间将更多时间重新分配到某些消费活动，而这些活动的支出下降更大，并通过两部门新凯恩斯模型表明这种时间-支出替代解释了货币政策冲击下消费响应的约40%。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper shows that consumption activities toward which households reallocate more time in recessions see larger expenditure declines, revealing a systematic link between time-use and expenditure cyclicality. A two-sector New Keynesian model shows this time-expenditure substitution explains roughly forty percent of consumption's response to monetary policy shocks.
