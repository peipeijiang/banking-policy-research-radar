<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文实证研究了印度哈里亚纳邦区域商业市场中数字银行采用与金融普惠的关键决定因素。在“数字印度”等国家倡议以及统一支付接口（Unified Payments Interface, UPI）基础设施扩展的推动下，金融生态系统正经历快速的结构性数字化。然而，大都市与半城市化或农村商业部门之间仍存在显著的采用差距。本研究以技术接受模型（Technology Acceptance Model, TAM）和整合型技术接受与使用理论（Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology, UTAUT）为理论基础，评估数字金融素养与感知交易安全对整体采用频率的联合预测影响。研究在罗塔克地区对N = 200名零售消费者、本地商户和商业银行客户进行了分层抽样的结构化定量调查。多元线性回归分析显示，数字金融素养（β = 0.485，p < 0.001）与感知安全性（β = 0.312，p < 0.001）共同解释了数字银行采用方差的61.0%（R² = 0.610）。研究结果表明，功能性操作能力是交易频率的主要驱动因素，而网络安全担忧则构成关键的次要调节因素。本文为寻求提升客户留存率和深化区域金融普惠的银行机构、商业企业和监管政策制定者提供了建议。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper empirically investigates the key determinants of digital banking adoption and financial inclusion within regional commercial markets in Haryana, India. Driven by national initiatives such as Digital India and the expansion of Unified Payments Interface (UPI) infrastructure, financial ecosystems are undergoing rapid structural digitization. However, significant adoption disparities persist between metropolitan and semi-urban or rural commerce sectors. Anchored in the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) and the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT), this study evaluates the joint predictive influence of digital financial literacy and perceived transaction security on overall adoption frequency. A structured quantitative survey was administered across a stratified sample of N = 200 retail consumers, local merchants, and commercial banking clients in the Rohtak region. Multiple linear regression analysis revealed that digital financial literacy (β = 0.485, p < 0.001) and perceived security (β = 0.312, p < 0.001) together account for 61.0% (R² = 0.610) of the variance in digital banking adoption. The findings demonstrate that functional operational capability serves as the primary catalyst for transaction frequency, while cybersecurity concerns act as a critical secondary moderator. Recommendations are provided for banking institutions, commercial enterprises, and regulatory policymakers seeking to enhance customer retention and deepen regional financial inclusion.
