# Grokipedia stretches the US political spectrum

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32420v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文比较 Grokipedia 与 Wikipedia 上 1,614 名美国政治人物传记的意识形态倾向，发现 Grokipedia 将政治光谱两端进一步拉伸，且该不对称可由政治人物意识形态解释。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

语言模型如今正在撰写人们和其他AI系统查阅的参考工具书。由xAI的模型撰写的Grokipedia，将自己定位为比众包编辑的维基百科偏见更少的替代品。我们比较了2026年2月和11月两部百科全书上1，614位现任及前任民主党和共和党政客的传记，涵盖从安德鲁·杰克逊到现任国会议员的人物。对于十分之九的共和党人，Grokipedia上的传记读起来仿佛出自一位比维基百科更保守的作者之手。而对于民主党人，这种情况仅在一半左右成立。我们发现政客的意识形态解释了这种不对称性。对于1，022位拥有国会唱名投票记录的政客，作者显得有多保守随政客的意识形态呈线性上升，且在Grokipedia上的上升斜率约为维基百科的两倍。在埃隆·马斯克的America PAC投入资金的竞选中，共和党人并未表现出额外的偏见。Grokipedia并非偏向某一政党，而是拉伸了政治光谱，将最自由派政客的传记进一步推向左侧，将最保守派政客的传记进一步推向右侧。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Language models now write reference works that people and other AI systems consult. Grokipedia, written by xAI's model, presents itself as a less biased alternative to crowd-edited Wikipedia. We compare the biographies of 1,614 Democratic and Republican current and former politicians, from Andrew Jackson to members of the current Congress, on both encyclopedias in February and November 2026. For nine in ten Republicans, Grokipedia's biography reads as if written by a more conservative author than Wikipedia's. For Democrats, this holds only about half the time. We find the politicians' ideology explains this asymmetry. For 1,022 politicians with Congressional roll call voting records, how conservative the author seems rises linearly with the politician's ideology and about twice as steeply on Grokipedia as on Wikipedia. Republicans in races where Elon Musk's America PAC spent money show no extra bias. Rather than favoring a party, Grokipedia stretches the political spectrum, moving the biographies of the most liberal politicians further left and of the most conservative politicians further right.
