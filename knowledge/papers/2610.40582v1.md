# Optimal Policy With Noisy Sufficient Statistics

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究政策制定者应如何设定$k$个政策工具以最优地实现$m$个（可能是动态的）目标，分析框架为线性二次模型，其中政策的因果效应存在统计不确定性。将估计效应代入“充分统计量”最优政策公式——正如文献中常见做法——可能导致无限风险。事实上，当$m>1$时，无论数据如何都维持现状是唯一的最小最大最优策略；当$m,k>1$时，维持现状是唯一的最小最大后悔最优策略。尽管如此，我们建议政策制定者采用流行代入规则的一个简单、阻尼版本，如Fisher（1962）和Brainard（1967）所述。这一谨慎规则不仅在大多数参数空间上优于无所作为，其最坏情况风险在经验相关模型中也接近最小最大值。政策制定者应使用来自无偏但分散的估计量（如局部投影，local projection）的因果效应估计，而非来自适度有偏但精确的估计量（如向量自回归，vector autoregression）。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how policy-makers should set $k$ policy instruments so as to best hit $m$ (perhaps dynamic) targets, in a linear-quadratic model where there is statistical uncertainty about the causal effects of the policies. Plugging the estimated effects into "sufficient statistics" optimal policy formulas, as commonly done in the literature, can result in infinite risk. In fact, maintaining the status quo regardless of the data is uniquely minimax-optimal when $m>1$, and uniquely minimax-regret-optimal when $m,k>1$. Nevertheless, we recommend that policy-makers adopt a simple, dampened version of the popular plug-in rule, as in Fisher (1962) and Brainard (1967). Not only does this cautious rule outperform doing nothing over most of the parameter space, its worst-case risk is also close to the minimax value in empirically relevant models. Policy-makers should use causal effect estimates from an unbiased but dispersed estimator (such as a local projection) rather than from a moderately biased but precise estimator (such as a vector autoregression).

</details>
