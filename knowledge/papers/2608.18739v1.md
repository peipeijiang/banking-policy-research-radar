<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究单峰域上区间值中位数对应的联盟操纵问题。在悲观扩展下，群体策略防护排除了任何真正集值的选择。相比之下，在乐观扩展和最优—最差扩展下，群体策略防护刻画了目标集对应。此外，至多由两名选民组成的联盟足以同时得出不可能性结果和刻画结果。因此，联盟稳健性严格限制了集值灵活性，但并未完全消除它。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study coalitional manipulation of interval-valued median correspondences on the single-peaked domain. Under the pessimistic extension, group strategy-proofness rules out any genuinely set-valued choice. Under the optimistic and best--worst extensions, by contrast, group strategy-proofness characterizes target set correspondences. Moreover, coalitions of at most two voters suffice for both the impossibility and characterization results. Thus, coalitional robustness sharply restricts, but need not eliminate, set-valued flexibility.

</details>
