# Asymptotic Equivalence of Immediate and Deferred Acceptance

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

即时接受机制（Immediate Acceptance, IA，亦称波士顿机制）常用于将学生分配到学校，因为若家长如实报告对学校的偏好，该机制能产生帕累托有效的匹配结果，而学生提议的延迟接受机制（Deferred Acceptance, DA）则无法实现这一点。本文探讨的问题是：在如实报告偏好的条件下，IA机制是否比DA机制产生显著更优的平均排名？我们证明，在独立同分布的一对一随机市场中，IA机制的预期平均排名渐近于$\log n$，与DA机制相同。因此，IA机制的帕累托效率并未转化为预期平均排名的一阶改进。这一结论同样适用于IA机制的变体以及多对一市场。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Immediate Acceptance (IA, also known as the Boston mechanism) is commonly used to assign students to schools because it produces a Pareto-efficient matching if parents report their preferences over schools truthfully, unlike student-proposing Deferred Acceptance (DA). In this paper, we ask: does IA produce meaningfully better average ranks than DA, conditional on truth-telling? We show that, in i.i.d. one-to-one random markets, IA's expected average rank is asymptotically $\log n$, just like DA's. Therefore, IA's Pareto efficiency does not translate into a first-order improvement in expected average rank. This conclusion extends to variations of IA as well as to many-to-one markets.

</details>
