<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

作为现有贝叶斯说服框架在信息机制不完善情形下的扩展，我们研究当发送者仅以概率$ρ$受已安装信息策略约束、约束实现状态不可观测、且接收者无法观察持久性结构环境时的直接推荐问题。接收者首先观察一个与收益无关、不可操纵的校准样本，随后面对一次全新的、未经认证的部署交互。在一般情况下，校准法则仅识别面向接收者的简化形式，而无法识别潜在的约束核与自由裁量核。我们刻画了类型层面的$ρ$-可实现性，构建了接收者关于完整部署节点的后验分布，并证明了一个静态直接跟随实施定理。在每一个通过后验预测服从性检验的校准历史之后，部署评估构成一个精确的完美贝叶斯均衡：贝叶斯一致性、接收者序贯理性、发送者序贯理性以及偏离路径的完备性均得到验证。在有限类型分离、共同推荐支撑集以及正服从边际的条件下，该检验以高概率激活此类均衡。我们的结果将统计失败概率与均衡近似严格区分开来。最后，我们将原始稳健值前沿、支撑集线性规划算法以及二元行动分数背包特例嵌入该实施框架之中。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

As an extension of existing Bayesian persuasion framework with inadequate message mechanism, we study direct recommendation when a sender is bound by an installed information policy only with probability $ρ$, the realization of binding is hidden, and the receiver does not observe the persistent structural environment. The receiver first sees a payoff-neutral, nonmanipulable calibration sample and then faces a fresh, non-certified deployment interaction. In common, the calibration law identifies only the receiver-facing reduced form, not the latent binding and discretionary kernels. We characterize type-wise $ρ$-implementability, construct the receiver's posterior over the full deployment node, and prove a static direct-following implementation theorem. After every calibration history that passes a posterior-predictive obedience test, the deployment assessment is an exact perfect Bayesian equilibrium: Bayes consistency, receiver sequential rationality, sender sequential rationality, and off-path completion are all verified. Under finite-type separation, common recommendation support, and a positive obedience margin, the test activates such an equilibrium with high probability. Our results keep statistical failure probability distinct from equilibrium approximation. Finally, we embed the original robust value frontier, support-wise linear-programming algorithm, and binary-action fractional-knapsack specialization into this implementation framework
