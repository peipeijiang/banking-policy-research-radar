<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文探讨了有限数量参数之间符号一致性的检验问题。该问题出现在多种实证情境中，例如检测不同子组、结果或时间段中处理效应符号相反的情况，以及检验局部平均处理效应的工具变量有效性。针对参数要么全部非负、要么全部非正的零假设，本文提出了两种新颖的检验方法：最不利检验（least favorable test）和条件检验（conditional test）。最不利检验使用最坏情况下的零假设临界值，而条件检验首先筛选出具有较大正估计或负估计的分量，然后在筛选事件条件下对剩余符号未决的分量进行检验。与现有的符号一致性检验不同，这两种方法均能适应估计量之间任意的依赖关系；在估计量相互独立的特殊情形下，临界值仅依赖于维度和检验水平。我们证明，这两种检验在一大类非参数分布上能够一致地控制渐近检验水平。局部渐近功效分析揭示了一种权衡：最不利检验在符号约束起作用的边界配置附近功效更高，而条件检验在部分分量远离零时功效更高。模拟证据在有限样本中支持了这些理论预测。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This article considers the problem of testing sign agreement among a finite number of parameters. This problem arises in empirical settings such as detecting treatment effects with opposite signs across subgroups, outcomes, or time periods, and testing instrument validity for local average treatment effects. For the null hypothesis that the parameters are either all non-negative or all non-positive, I propose two novel tests: a least favorable test and a conditional test. The least favorable test uses a worst-case null critical value, while the conditional test first screens components with large positive or negative estimates and then tests the remaining sign-unresolved components conditional on the screening event. Unlike existing sign agreement tests, both procedures accommodate arbitrary dependence among estimators; in the special case of independent estimators, the critical values depend only on the dimension and testing levels. We show that both tests control asymptotic size uniformly over a large class of nonparametric distributions. Local asymptotic power analysis reveals a tradeoff: the least favorable test is more powerful near boundary configurations where sign restrictions bind, whereas the conditional test is more powerful when some components are well separated from zero. Simulation evidence supports these theoretical predictions in finite samples.
