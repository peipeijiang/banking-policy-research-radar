<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

付费竞拍在分配前向参与者收费，因此使得失败方的支付容易受到卖家干预的影响。本文引入了目标损失暴露度（targeted-loss exposure），这是一种压力测试，在保持某一指定竞拍者类型分布不变的情况下，记录当其一个对手的均衡报价被固定为任意高水平时该竞拍者的支付金额。在非负支付以及赢家支付至少不低于输家支付的排序条件下，收益等价性得出了一个由沉默消耗战（silent war of attrition）实现的两竞拍者严格上界。对于任意数量的竞拍者，同一格式在支付排序的秩局部规则（rank-local rules）中达到上界。证明将支付位置表述为一个线性规划问题，并构造了一个对偶概率测度。当竞拍者超过两人时，互补松弛性几乎处处决定了最优输家支付方案。赢家支付拍卖（winner-pay auctions）的暴露度为零，标准全支付拍卖（all-pay auctions）严格低于前沿，且前沿暴露度随竞拍者数量增加而下降。一个仅输家支付规则（loser-only rule）表明，取消支付排序会使暴露度变得无界。一个共同冲击扩展还表明，预期干预会改变报价，但保持标准格式的排序。这些结果将收益等价性所固定的总期中支付与支付所依附的赢家或输家状态区分开来。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Pay-to-bid auctions charge participants before allocation and therefore make losing-side payments vulnerable to seller intervention. This paper introduces targeted-loss exposure, a stress test that records a designated bidder's payment when one rival's equilibrium report is fixed at an arbitrarily high level while her type distribution is preserved. Under nonnegative payments and an ordering in which a winner pays at least as much as a loser, revenue equivalence yields a sharp two-bidder bound attained by the silent war of attrition. For any number of bidders, the same format attains the upper bound among payment-ordered rank-local rules. The proof formulates payment location as a linear program and constructs a dual probability measure. With more than two bidders, complementary slackness determines the optimal loser-payment schedule almost everywhere. Winner-pay auctions have zero exposure, standard all-pay auctions lie strictly below the frontier, and frontier exposure decreases as the number of bidders rises. A loser-only rule shows that removing payment ordering can make exposure unbounded. A common-shock extension also shows that anticipating intervention changes bids but preserves the ordering of standard formats. The results separate the total interim payment fixed by revenue equivalence from the winning or losing state to which that payment is attached.

</details>
