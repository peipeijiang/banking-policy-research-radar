<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

多元化与专业化之间的张力是经济学的核心主题，塑造了从国际贸易、投资组合理论到企业范围等不同领域的讨论。本文在研究策略领域考察这一权衡。利用包含约6万名经济学家的RePEc面板数据库，我们证明，追求多元化的研究组合在引用量和发表量方面产生更优的表现，同时研究流失率更低。这一优势既适用于资深研究者，也与传统观点相反，同样适用于早期职业研究者。与新合作者的联合研究显著促进了这一策略。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The tension between diversification and specialization is a central theme in economics, shaping discourse in fields as disparate as international trade, portfolio theory, and corporate scope. This paper examines this trade-off within the realm of research strategy. Using the RePEc panel database of approximately 60,000 economists, we demonstrate that the pursuit of a diversified research portfolio yields superior performance as measured by citations and publications as well as lower research attrition rates. This advantage applies to both senior and, in contrast to conventional wisdom, early-career researchers. This strategy is significantly facilitated by joint work with new co-authors.
