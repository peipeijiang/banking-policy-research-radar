<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察帕累托原则下人类能力的分布，重点关注智力能力、学业成就、运动表现、音乐、艺术、领导力和决策等方面的个体差异。研究旨在理解为何部分个体能够将其能力发展至卓越水平，而大多数人的能力和成就则因个体与社会因素的影响而呈现不同程度的差异。本研究以帕累托原则作为分析框架，考察能力与结果的集中程度，并将帕累托原则与正态分布（Normal Distribution）加以区分——后者是一种用于描述数据围绕均值分布的统计模型。研究还探讨了专门能力、多维能力与人类潜能发展机会之间的关系。文章进一步引入泰国商业银行体系中的存款结构作为经济资源集中的比较案例研究。此外，研究运用作者提出的社会相对论（Social Relativity Theory, SRT）来解释社会质量、结构及其相互作用所产生的力量之间的关系，为理解影响人类能力发展与成就的社会条件提供了额外视角。概念分析表明，人类能力无法通过单一指标加以评估，高能力并不自动带来成功或卓越。成就的分布还与社会结构及个体发展潜能的条件相关。因此，人类发展应强调识别个体禀赋、提升专门能力、创造学习机会，以及在智力发展的同时培养道德品格。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This article examines the distribution of human capabilities under the Pareto Principle, focusing on individual differences in intellectual ability, academic achievement, athletic performance, music, art, leadership and decision-making. It aims to understand why some individuals develop their abilities to a level of excellence, while the majority demonstrate varying levels of ability and achievement influenced by individual and social factors. The study applies the Pareto Principle as an analytical framework for examining the concentration of capabilities and outcomes. It distinguishes the Pareto Principle from the Normal Distribution, a statistical model used to describe the distribution of data around the mean. It also considers the relationships among specialized abilities, multidimensional capabilities and opportunities for human potential development. The article further introduces the structure of deposits in Thailand’s commercial banking system as a comparative case study of economic resource concentration. In addition, it applies the Social Relativity Theory (SRT), developed by the author, to explain the relationship between social mass, structure and the force arising from their interaction. This framework provides an additional perspective on the social conditions influencing human capability development and achievement. The conceptual analysis suggests that human capabilities cannot be assessed through a single indicator and that high ability does not automatically lead to success or excellence. The distribution of achievement is also associated with social structures and the conditions under which individuals develop their potential. Human development should therefore emphasize the identification of individual aptitudes, the enhancement of specialized abilities, the creation of learning opportunities and the cultivation of moral character alongside intellectual development.
