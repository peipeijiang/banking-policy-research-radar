<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究规划者能否在保持真实报告的同时，偏向于学生在其排名较高的学校。延迟接受（deferred acceptance, DA）算法的标准实现通常不是解决方案。在内点截断值下，带多重并列打破的延迟接受（DA with multiple tie-breaking, DA-MTB）当且仅当规划者从学校分配中获得的收益与学生偏好无关时，才能解决规划者的优化问题。通常情况下并非如此，而DA-MTB下的严格激励相容性为通过依赖偏好的优先级调整来改进留下了空间。我们在两所学校模型中刻画了最优的依赖于排名的截断值调整，并表明在多学校环境中，可以以较小的支撑集（因此每种类型的学生可能只就读于少量学校）选择接近最优的近似激励相容分配。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study whether a planner can favor students at schools they rank more highly while preserving truthful reporting. The standard implementation of the deferred acceptance (DA) algorithm is typically not the solution. With interior cutoffs, DA with multiple tie-breaking (DA-MTB) solves the planner's optimization problem if and only if the planner's payoff from a school assignment is independent of the students' preferences. This is typically not the case, and strict incentive compatibility under DA-MTB leaves room for improvement via preference-dependent priority adjustments. We characterize optimal rank-dependent cutoff adjustments in a two-school model and show that, in a many-school environment, near-optimal approximately incentive-compatible assignments can be chosen with small support (so each type of student may only attend a small number of schools).
