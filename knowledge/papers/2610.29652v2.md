<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

最坏情况风险评价是金融与运筹学的核心问题，然而现有的分析结果仍较为零散，且大多局限于具有特定结构的不确定性集合。本文以谱风险度量作为基础类别，建立了一套凸变分理论，将上述分析统一起来，并将其推广至更丰富的不确定性模型。该理论基于一个表示原则：选用能够保持整个不确定性集合凸性的分位数表述或分布表述。在这两种表述下，所得最优性条件为识别最坏情况分布律并确定其风险值提供了系统化途径。该方法恢复了经典的中心矩界，并针对具有实际动机的模型给出了闭式或半闭式解，这些模型涉及带尾部压力的Wasserstein不确定性、双边期望短缺（ES）带、非对称部分矩、几何矩界以及指数参考或高斯参考的Kullback–Leibler（KL）邻域。除上述分析结果外，同一理论在给定假设下还可导出具有固定网格收敛性的基于投影的算法。在一个高维投资组合应用中，我们针对高斯参考下的均值锚定联合KL不确定性建立了一个精确投影恒等式。由此得到的标准化最坏情况ES系数适用于每一个投资组合，从而在单次校准后将稳健投资组合选择化归为一个精确的二阶锥规划。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Worst-case risk evaluation is central to finance and operations research, yet existing analytical results remain fragmented and largely confined to ambiguity sets with specific structures. Using spectral risk measures as the underlying class, we develop a convex variational theory that unifies these analyses and extends them to richer ambiguity models. The theory rests on a representation principle: use the quantile or distribution formulation that preserves convexity of the full ambiguity set. In both representations, the resulting optimality conditions provide a systematic route to identify the worst-case law and determine its risk value. This approach recovers classical central-moment bounds and yields closed-form or semi-closed-form solutions for practically motivated models involving Wasserstein ambiguity with tail stress, two-sided expected shortfall (ES) bands, asymmetric partial moments, geometric-moment bounds, and exponential- or Gaussian-reference Kullback--Leibler (KL) neighborhoods. Beyond these analytical results, the same theory yields projection-based algorithms with fixed-grid convergence under the stated assumptions. In a high-dimensional portfolio application, we establish an exact projection identity for mean-anchored joint KL ambiguity under a Gaussian reference. The resulting standardized worst-case ES coefficient applies to every portfolio, reducing robust portfolio selection to an exact second-order cone program after a single calibration.
