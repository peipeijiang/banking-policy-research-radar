<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

识别时间偏好冲击具有挑战性：足以改变调查所引出的陈述性不耐烦程度的冲击，同时也会收紧财务资源，从而混淆对陈述性时间偏好的解读。联合考察陈述性偏好与显示性偏好可以解决这一识别问题：不耐烦冲击与财务资源减少会产生相反的消费反应，尽管二者都会提高陈述性不耐烦程度。我们将识别框架应用于2009年拉奎拉（L'Aquila）地震，将面板数据与空间上精细的地震和损失数据相结合，采用双重差分设计，将经历了地震的未受损家庭与距震中距离相近的空间对照组进行比较。处理组家庭报告了更强的不耐烦程度，且相对于对照组，其非耐用品消费增加了11%。效应与心理压力渠道一致，集中在最容易受到压力影响的人群中，并随着家庭随时间消耗其流动性财富缓冲而持续存在。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Identifying time preference shocks is challenging: shocks large enough to shift survey-elicited stated impatience also tighten financial resources, confounding the interpretation of stated time preference. Jointly examining stated and revealed preferences resolves this identification problem: impatience shocks and reductions in financial resources generate opposite consumption responses, even though both raise stated impatience. Applying our identification framework to the 2009 L'Aquila earthquake, we combine panel data with spatially granular seismic and damage data in a difference-in-differences design that compares undamaged households that experienced the earthquake with a spatial control group at similar distances from the epicenter. Treated households report greater impatience and increase nondurable consumption by 11 percent relative to controls. Effects are consistent with a psychological stress channel, concentrated among the most vulnerable to stress, and persist as households deplete their liquid wealth buffer over time.

</details>
