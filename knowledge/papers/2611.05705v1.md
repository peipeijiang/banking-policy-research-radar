<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文比较了两种迭代占优程序：一种基于剔除混合策略，另一种基于剔除纯策略。研究表明，在紧致且连续的博弈中，混合策略程序的剔除轮数最多比纯策略程序多一轮：若纯策略程序在第 $M$ 轮终止，则混合策略程序在第 $M$ 轮或第 $M+1$ 轮终止，且该上界是紧的。本文还给出了混合策略程序的两种刻画，这两种刻画与该程序逐轮等价，并且只需检验相对于对手纯策略的改进。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This note compares two iterated dominance procedures, one based on eliminating mixed strategies and one based on eliminating pure strategies. It shows that, in compact and continuous games, the mixed-strategy procedure can trail the pure-strategy procedure by at most one round: If the pure-strategy procedure terminates at round $M$, the mixed-strategy procedure terminates at either $M$ or $M+1$, and this bound is tight. It also provides two characterizations of the mixed-strategy procedure that are round-for-round equivalent to it and that require checking for improvements only against opponents' pure strategies.
