<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究当目标函数和约束条件均为估计值时线性规划（linear program, LP）最优值的推断问题。利用LP对偶性，我们通过反转线性不等式系统可行性的检验来构造单侧和双侧置信区间。现有检验方法可得到渐近有效的置信区间，但检验功效可能较差。我们提出一种修正方法，通过引入估计的“箱约束”（box constraints）来增广这些系统。由此得到的程序对 \(N^{-1/2}\)-局部备择假设具有检验功效，并可得到阶为 \(N^{-1/2}\) 的置信区间。这些结果仅要求LP最优值有限，不要求可行域具有紧性，也不要求满足标准约束品性（constraint qualifications）。在多种约束品性下，我们获得了更强的保证，并且当已知确定性约束界定了原始变量和/或对偶变量时，在大类数据生成过程（DGPs）上建立了均匀有效性。模拟结果展示了良好的有限样本性能。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies inference on the value of a linear program (LP) when the objective and constraints are estimated. Using LP duality, we construct one- and two-sided confidence intervals by inverting tests of the feasibility of systems of linear inequalities. Existing tests yield asymptotically valid confidence intervals but can have poor power. We propose a modification that augments these systems with estimated ``box constraints.'' The resulting procedure has power against $N^{-1/2}$-local alternatives and yields confidence intervals of order $N^{-1/2}$. These results require only that the LP value be finite, and do not require compactness of the feasible region nor standard constraint qualifications. We obtain stronger guarantees under various constraint qualifications and establish uniform validity over large classes of DGPs when known deterministic constraints bound the primal and/or dual variables. Simulations demonstrate good finite-sample performance.
