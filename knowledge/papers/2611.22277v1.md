<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们能否即使在事后解释资产价格变动？本文将Roll（1988）美国金融学会主席演讲“$R^2$”重新解释为一项现代实证研究，表明传统的宏观经济、现金流、预期和流量变量在样本外只能解释不到10%的美国股票月度收益变动（通常$R^2$为负），且缺乏日度观测数据。我们使用生成式人工智能从《华尔街日报》市场摘要中提取所述原因，同时过滤掉与价格相关的语言。这些原因在样本外可解释约30-40%的日度和月度收益变动。其内容涵盖可解释的主题，包括公司新闻、宏观经济状况、货币政策、市场情绪和地缘政治。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Can we explain asset price movements even with hindsight? Reinterpreting Roll's (1988) AFA Presidential Address, "$R^2$," as a modern empirical exercise, we show that conventional macroeconomic, cash-flow, expectations, and flow variables explain less than 10% (often with negative $R^2$) of monthly U.S. equity return variation out of sample and lack daily observations. We use generative AI to extract reasons stated in Wall Street Journal market summaries while filtering out price-related language. These reasons explain around 30-40% of daily and monthly return variation out of sample. Their content spans interpretable themes, including corporate news, macroeconomic conditions, monetary policy, sentiment, and geopolitics.

</details>
