# Sequential Candidate Entry: A Counterexample to the Osborne--Kats Conjecture

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10591v1)

## 一句话结论

> 构建有限候选人序贯选择选举位置的博弈模型，通过构造12名玩家情形下的子博弈完美纳什均衡，反驳了Osborne-Kats关于唯一均衡结果的猜想。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

有限个潜在候选人依次在区间[0,1]上选择一个竞选位置或选择不参与，并观察所有先前选择。选民在同一区间上具有均匀分布的理想位置，并非策略性地投票给距离最近的候选人；位于同一位置的候选人平分其选票。得票份额最大的候选人平分单位收益，落选者获得负收益，不参与则收益为零。20世纪80年代中期，Osborne和Kats猜想：对于任意玩家数量$n\geq 2$，唯一的子博弈完美纳什均衡（SPNE）结果是只有第一位和最后一位玩家在中位数位置参与。我们通过确立12名玩家存在不同的SPNE结果来反驳该猜想。从de Vries先前提出的前两个进入位置出发，我们选择一个SPNE策略组合，使得在前两个位置1/2和1/10之后，第二位玩家的收益至少为1/10。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Finitely many potential candidates sequentially choose an electoral position on the interval [0,1] or stay out, observing all prior choices. Voters have a uniform distribution of ideal positions on the same interval and vote non-strategically for a nearest candidate; candidates at the same position divide its votes. Candidates with the largest vote share divide a unit payoff equally, losing entrants receive a negative payoff, and staying out yields zero. In the mid-1980s, Osborne and Kats conjectured that, for every number $n\geq 2$ of players, the unique subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium (SPNE) outcome has only the first and last players entering at the median. We refute the conjecture by establishing the existence of a different SPNE outcome for 12 players. Starting from the first two entries proposed earlier by de Vries, we select an SPNE strategy profile after the first two locations 1/2 and 1/10 that gives the second player payoff at least 1/10.
