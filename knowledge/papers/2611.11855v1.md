<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个理性疏忽模型，其中决策者在面对关于状态先验分布的不确定性（模糊性）时选择随机选择规则。模糊性通过分布鲁棒公式建模，其中先验属于由一般phi散度定义的不确定性集合。我们的主要结果建立了所得最大最小问题的精确拉格朗日对偶表示，即一个有限维凹优化问题。这一刻画给出了鲁棒考虑集出现的充分必要条件，并为Cressie-Read散度族提供了一个可处理的一维对偶表示。我们进一步将该模型与鲁棒风险度量联系起来：平均风险价值（Average Value-at-Risk）源于一种特定的非光滑散度，而Kullback-Leibler模糊性则通过拉格朗日对偶与熵风险乘数表示相联系。随后我们发展了两个扩展。第一个扩展允许注意力成本技术和先验模糊性由不同的phi散度控制，第二个扩展在决策者的基线先验之上引入了更深层次的模糊性。两个扩展均保持了模型的有限维结构，并将标准的Kullback-Leibler鲁棒理性疏忽设定作为特例或相应的乘数情形纳入其中。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a rational inattention model in which a decision maker chooses a stochastic choice rule while facing ambiguity about the prior distribution over states. Ambiguity is modeled through a distributionally robust formulation in which the prior belongs to an uncertainty set defined by a general phi-divergence. Our main result establishes an exact Lagrangian dual representation of the resulting max-min problem as a finite-dimensional concave optimization problem. This characterization yields necessary and sufficient conditions for the emergence of robust consideration sets and a tractable one-dimensional dual representation for the Cressie-Read family of divergences. We further connect the model to robust risk measures: Average Value-at-Risk arises from a particular non-smooth divergence, while Kullback-Leibler ambiguity is linked through Lagrangian duality to the entropic-risk multiplier representation. We then develop two extensions. The first allows the attention-cost technology and prior ambiguity to be governed by distinct phi-divergences, while the second introduces a deeper layer of ambiguity over the decision maker's baseline prior. Both extensions preserve the finite-dimensional structure of the model and encompass standard Kullback-Leibler robust rational inattention specifications as special or corresponding multiplier cases.
