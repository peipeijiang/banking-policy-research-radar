<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文提出了关系首要性一般理论（General Theory of Relational Primacy, GTRP），这是一个用于理解复杂系统中稳定性、危机与转型的概念性与形式化框架。其核心论点是：系统并非由其变量所定义，而是由组织这些变量的关系所定义。变量仅是深层关系机制的晚期表现。危机源于这些关系的无声重组，而非变量本身的变化。我们形式化了三条公理（关系首要性、关系先行性、耦合普遍性），并由此推导出一个完整的假设-演绎框架（假设H1-H5、有效性条件C1-C6以及可观测参数beta的属性P1-P5）。我们证明了一个基础性定理，确立了每个复杂系统都拥有一个不可还原的关系层级，引入了一个类似于能量守恒的理论不变量，并提出了危机、关系因果性与关系信息的普适性定义。参数beta(t) = d log y / d log x，通过对数-对数空间中的时变卡尔曼滤波器进行估计，以O(delta theta)的灵敏度测量这种重组，其速度比经典O((delta theta)^2)信号快一个数量级。实证证据（NASA AIRS数据，2002-2026年）以及在六个模拟系统上的验证表明，beta比经典预警信号提前14至24个月或39至153个时间步长。该理论旨在具有普适性，可应用于气候、生物学、经济学、人工智能以及系统的主权性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper presents the General Theory of Relational Primacy (GTRP), a conceptual and formal framework for understanding stability, crisis, and transition in complex systems. The central thesis is that systems are not defined by their variables but by the relations that organize them. Variables are merely late manifestations of deep relational mechanisms. Crises arise from the silent reorganization of these relations, not from changes in the variables themselves. We formalize three axioms (relational primacy, antecedence of relations, and universality of coupling) and derive from them a complete hypothetico-deductive framework (hypotheses H1-H5, validity conditions C1-C6, and properties P1-P5 of the observable beta). We prove a foundational theorem establishing that every complex system possesses an irreducible relational level, introduce an invariant of the theory analogous to the conservation of energy, and propose universal definitions of crisis, relational causality, and relational information. The parameter beta(t) = d log y / d log x, estimated via a time-varying Kalman filter in the log-log space, measures this reorganization with an O(delta theta) sensitivity, one order of magnitude faster than classical O((delta theta)^2) signals. The empirical evidence (NASA AIRS data, 2002-2026) and validations on six simulated systems confirm that beta precedes classical early warning signals by 14 to 24 months and 39 to 153 time steps. The theory is designed to be universal, applicable to climate, biology, economics, artificial intelligence, and sovereignty of systems.
