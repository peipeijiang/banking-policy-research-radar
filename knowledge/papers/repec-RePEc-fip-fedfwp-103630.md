<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们基于一个包含异质性企业、企业面临特有生产率及可变加成率的一般均衡框架，研究人工智能如何影响市场竞争。企业选择采用人工智能技术需承担固定成本，而人工智能生产需要数据与能源投入。我们的模型预测，人工智能的扩散与行业集中度之间存在非单调关系。当人工智能使用率从初始较低水平上升时，大型在位使用者将获得更大市场份额。当人工智能使用率达到充分扩散后，新进入者及较小规模采用者的加入会侵蚀在位者的市场份额，从而降低行业集中度。当企业能够以自身数据补充人工智能时，上述非单调关系依然稳健。我们的校准模型预测，若人工智能采用率相对于当前水平有所提升，行业集中度很可能会下降。相比之下，人工智能与平均加成率之间的关系则取决于人工智能使用率的增长是由需求因素还是供给因素驱动。我们的模型还预测，对人工智能采用者收入给予约3%的适度补贴可最大化社会福利，这反映了与人工智能使用相关的总体生产率与平均加成率之间的权衡。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how AI affects market competition based on a general equilibrium framework with heterogeneous firms facing idiosyncratic productivity and variable markups. Firms choose the AI technology subject to fixed costs, where AI production requires data and energy inputs. Our model predicts a non-monotonic relation of AI diffusion with industry concentration. As AI usage rises from an initially low level, large incumbent users gain market share. When AI usage is sufficiently diffused, entry of new and smaller adopters erodes the market share of incumbents, reducing industry concentration. The non-monotonic relations are robust when firms can complement AI with their own data. Our calibrated model predicts that industry concentration is likely to fall if AI adoption increases relative to the current level. In comparison, the relation of AI with the average markup depends on whether increased AI usage is driven by demand or supply factors. Our model also predicts that a modest subsidy of about 3 percent for AI adopter revenues maximizes social welfare, reflecting a tradeoff between aggregate productivity and the average markup associated with AI usage.
