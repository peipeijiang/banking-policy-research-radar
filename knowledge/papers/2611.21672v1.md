# Who sees the sea? Sea-view potential by façade and floor from cadastral LoD1 models on the Costa del Sol

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

景观是住宅最有价值却又最少被测量的属性之一。我们提出一种开放数据方法，利用带有楼层数的地籍轮廓图和国家地形模型，估算每栋住宅建筑每个自由立面在三层（底层、中层和顶层；其他楼层取最接近者）的海景潜力。该方法在西班牙太阳海岸21个市镇的158，060栋建筑上运行，双核耗时不到一小时，发现52%的建筑从顶层能看到部分海景，36%拥有开阔视野（20°或以上）。在试点中沿LiDAR表面追踪，评分与模型在地籍轮廓内的结果一致（20°阈值下96.7%的视点属于同一类别），但地籍未记录的结构使从顶层看到的海景平均降低6.1--9.0°。与93套在售住宅的照片进行对比——由两位开发商的AI模型盲标，分歧由作者裁定——评分的AUC为0.80（95%CI 0.70--0.89；对第一轮标签为0.81）；在20°阈值下敏感（0.90）但不特异（0.45）。住宅层面的数据取决于住宅如何分配到立面和楼层：在主规则下42%的住宅位于具有开阔视野潜力的楼层，而在所测试的分配、几何和植被情景中为12--45%。作为应用，较高收入的普查区在各市镇内往往具有更高的建模视野潜力，但效应大小不确定且推断脆弱（约两个有效聚类）；仅基于地形时差异基本消失。代码和立面级数据可复现主要结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Views are among the most valued and least measured attributes of a home. We present an open-data method that estimates the sea-view potential of every free façade of every residential building at three floors (ground, middle and top; other floors take the nearest of them), from cadastral footprints with floor counts and a national terrain model. Run for 158,060 buildings of 21 municipalities on Spain's Costa del Sol in under an hour on two cores, it finds that 52 % of buildings see some sea from their top floor and 36 % have an open view (20° or more). Traced over a LiDAR surface in a pilot, scores agree with the model's inside cadastral footprints (96.7 % of viewpoints in the same class at 20°), but structures the cadastre does not record lower the sea seen from the top floor by 6.1--9.0° on average. Against the photographs of 93 homes for sale, labelled blind by AI models of two developers with ties decided by the author, the score has an AUC of 0.80 (95 % CI 0.70--0.89; 0.81 against the first-round labels); at 20° it is sensitive (0.90) but not specific (0.45). Dwelling figures depend on how dwellings are allocated to façades and floors: 42 % of dwellings are on a floor with open-view potential under the main rule, and 12--45 % across the allocation, geometry and vegetation scenarios tested. As an application, higher-income census sections tend to have more modelled view potential within municipalities, with uncertain size and fragile inference (about two effective clusters); on terrain alone the difference largely disappears. Code and façade-level data reproduce the main results.
