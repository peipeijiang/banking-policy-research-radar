<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在经历了数十年的小额信贷扩张之后，尽管小额信贷机构（MFIs）收取更高的利率，放贷人依然与小额信贷机构并存。我们构建了一个包含事后道德风险的动态模型，其中借款人将小额信贷机构的贷款用于生产，并可在遭受冲击后依赖放贷人提供过桥贷款，以偿还小额信贷机构并保留未来获得小额信贷机构信贷的机会。随后，我们研究了小额信贷机构竞争如何塑造小额信贷机构与放贷人之间的共存关系。竞争对小额信贷机构信贷的影响是不确定的：它可能削弱还款激励，也可能取代放贷人作为过桥贷款提供者的角色。相比之下，竞争无疑会减少放贷人的信贷。在孟加拉国一项以基线小额信贷机构存在情况为分层依据的村级随机扩张中，每增加一家小额信贷机构，两到三年后放贷人贷款数量大约减少30%至40%，而对小额信贷机构借贷或总体借贷没有可检测到的影响。这表明，在小额信贷机构主要依靠排斥威胁来诱导还款、且放贷人提供应急融资的市场中，新增小额信贷机构进入所带来的收益在饱和度较低的地区可能更大。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

After decades of microfinance expansion and despite charging higher interest rates, moneylenders continue to exist alongside microfinance institutions (MFIs). We develop a dynamic model with ex post moral hazard, in which borrowers use MFI loans for production and can rely on moneylenders for bridge loans after shocks to repay MFIs and preserve future access to MFI credit. We then study how MFI competition shapes the coexistence between MFIs and moneylenders. The effect of competition on MFI credit is ambiguous: it may weaken repayment incentives or replace moneylenders as bridge lenders. By contrast, competition unambiguously reduces moneylender credit. In a village-level randomized expansion in Bangladesh, stratified by baseline MFI presence, one additional MFI reduces the number of moneylender loans by roughly 30-40% two to three years later, with no detectable effect on MFI or overall borrowing. This suggests that, in markets where MFIs rely primarily on the threat of exclusion to induce repayment and moneylenders provide emergency finance, gains from additional MFI entry may be greater in less saturated areas.

</details>
