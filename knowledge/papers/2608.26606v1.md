<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

近期研究鼓励政治学者从仅使用事后测量设计转向受试者内设计，以通过重复测量提高估计精度。我们为两期受试者内设计形式化了一个潜在结果框架，该框架允许非均衡分配以及异质性处理效应和遗留效应。我们刻画了合并估计量的性质，并评估了用于证明合并合理性的遗留效应检验。我们的发现如下：第一，仅当两种处理序列的平均遗留效应之差为零时，合并估计才能识别平均处理效应。合并估计量的单位聚类标准误与其基于设计的标准误完全相同。第二，在温和条件下，遗留效应检验的检验力严格低于仅使用事后数据时的平均处理效应检验。由此产生的两步程序——仅在检验未拒绝后才进行合并——所产生的置信区间通常覆盖不足。当遗留效应之差为零时，覆盖不足发生当且仅当合并比仅事后分析更有效，而这正是受试者内设计具有价值的情形。当遗留效应之差非零时，除非差值或样本量较大，否则覆盖不足是典型现象。第三，我们推导了敏感性分析，并发现已发表的结论对合理的遗留效应差值具有稳健性。因此，我们赞同使用受试者内设计，但建议在实质性论证中说明遗留效应差值为零的合理性，并报告对偏离该假设的敏感性分析结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Recent work encourages political scientists to move from post-only toward within-subject designs for improved precision from repeated measurements. We formalize a potential-outcomes framework for two-period within-subject designs that allows for unequal allocation and heterogeneous treatment and carryover effects. We characterize the pooled estimator and evaluate the carryover test used to justify pooling. We find: first, pooling identifies the average treatment effect only when the gap in the average carryover effects is zero across the two treatment sequences. The unit-clustered standard error for the pooled estimator is identical to its design-based counterpart. Second, under mild conditions, the carryover test has strictly less power than the average-treatment-effect test with post-only data. The resulting two-step procedure, which pools only after a nonrejected test, produces confidence intervals that typically undercover. When the gap is zero, undercoverage occurs if and only if pooling is more efficient than post-only analysis, precisely when the within-subject design is worthwhile. When the gap is nonzero, undercoverage is typical unless the gap or sample size is large. Third, we derive a sensitivity analysis and find published conclusions robust to plausible carryover gaps. We therefore endorse within-subject designs but recommend justifying a zero carryover gap substantively and reporting sensitivity to departures.
