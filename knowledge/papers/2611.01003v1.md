我们研究了在参考分布周围的Wasserstein球中，具有给定均值和方差的对称分布上的最坏情况扭曲风险度量。我们的关键贡献是构造了一个依赖于参考分布的辅助扭曲，该扭曲编码了Wasserstein约束，同时在凹包络操作下保持对称性。在适当的正则性和非退化条件下，该构造对有界变差的扭曲给出了尖锐上界，允许非单调性和内部不连续性，并在上界可达时识别出极值分布。一个精确保持对称性和给定矩的近似表明，对于严格高于可行性阈值的预算，对称化扭曲的上半连续松弛使上确界保持不变。在附加单峰性的情况下，对称性将分位数的凹-凸转变固定在中点，从而对具有平方可积导数的绝对连续扭曲，通过向固定闭凸锥上的$L^2$-投影给出刻画。针对ES、RVaR和VaR的数值示例说明了形状约束和参考分布如何影响可行性与最坏情况风险。使用数据依赖的Wasserstein半径进行的模拟考察了在正确设定和错误设定形状约束下的最坏情况ES和可行性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study worst-case distortion riskmetrics over symmetric distributions with prescribed mean and variance in a Wasserstein ball around a reference distribution. Our key contribution is the construction of a reference-dependent auxiliary distortion that encodes the Wasserstein constraint while preserving symmetry under the concave-envelope operation. Under suitable regularity and nondegeneracy conditions, this construction yields sharp upper bounds for distortions of bounded variation, allowing nonmonotonicity and interior discontinuities, and identifies extremal distributions when the bounds are attained. An approximation preserving symmetry and the prescribed moments exactly shows that upper semicontinuous relaxation of the symmetrized distortion leaves the supremum unchanged for budgets strictly above the feasibility threshold. With additional unimodality, symmetry fixes the quantile's concavity-convexity transition at the midpoint, yielding a characterization via $L^2$-projection onto a fixed closed convex cone for absolutely continuous distortions with square-integrable derivatives. Numerical examples for ES, RVaR, and VaR illustrate how shape restrictions and the reference distribution affect feasibility and worst-case risk. Simulations with data-dependent Wasserstein radii examine worst-case ES and feasibility under correctly specified and misspecified shape restrictions.
