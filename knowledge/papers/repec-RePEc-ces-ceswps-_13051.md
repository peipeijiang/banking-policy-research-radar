# Like a Good Neighbor: Childhood Neighbors Influence Occupation Choice

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究童年时期的邻居如何影响职业选择和晚年收入。利用超过1000万名儿童的关联人口普查记录和历史人口普查表微观地理数据，我们发现，与同一条街上的其他儿童相比，成长过程中与从事某一职业的人为邻，会使成年后从事该职业的概率提高10%。当接触强度更大、社区联系更紧密以及家庭之间更为相似时，这种传递效应更强。接触高收入或高学历邻居会产生持久的经济影响，带来成年后收入和教育的显著提升。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how childhood neighbors influence occupational choices and later-life earnings. Using linked census records for over 10 million children and historic census sheet microgeography, we find that growing up next door to someone in a given occupation increases the probability of being in that occupation as an adult by 10 percent relative to other children on the same street. The transmission effect is higher with more intense exposure, more connected neighborhoods, and between more similar families. Exposure to high-income or highly educated neighbors has lasting economic effects, leading to significant gains in adult income and education.
