<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关于大语言模型（LLM）交易智能体和阿尔法挖掘的论文所报告的夏普比率，若置于实证金融的其他任何领域都会显得非同寻常。我们对这些数字背后的证据进行了审计。在编写任何一篇论文的编码之前，我们预先注册了一个由LLM交易研究精选语料库中91篇论文构成的总体、一套包含十个字段的编码细则、一个分析脚本和五个方向性假设，并将所有这些提交至版本控制系统。每个编码均由一段逐字引文作为依据，脚本会将引文与论文原文进行比对核查，另有一名盲编码员对随机抽取的20%进行了重新编码。在75篇包含交易测试的论文中，51%未扣除任何交易成本，仅9%从数据中建模成本；测试窗口的中位数为12个月，四分之一的窗口为六个月或更短。在51篇测试窗口未知是否位于模型训练截止日期之后的论文中，98%既未在截止日期之后进行测试，也未进行实盘评估，也未使用时间上一致的模型。仅9%报告了多重性调整后的结果，只有五篇论文同时具备扣除成本、泄漏控制和报告试验次数三项要素。五个假设中有两个通过（短窗口；未控制的前视偏差）。三个未通过：报告试验次数的论文数量多于我们的预测，尽管其中大多数计数是重复的随机种子而非所搜索的策略数量；而头条夏普比率与测试窗口长度之间、以及头条夏普比率与成本之间的预期关联，在13篇于摘要或结论中给出头条夏普比率的论文中无法检测到。我们公开了编码、引文和脚本，并提出了一项六行报告最低标准。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Papers on large-language-model (LLM) trading agents and alpha mining report Sharpe ratios that would be remarkable in any other part of empirical finance. We audit the evidence behind those numbers. Before coding a single paper we pre-registered a population of 91 papers from a curated corpus of LLM trading research, a ten-field coding rubric, an analysis script and five directional hypotheses, and committed all of them to version control. Every code is backed by a verbatim quote that a script checks against the paper's text, and a second, blind coder recoded a random 20%. Of the 75 papers with a trading test, 51% deduct no trading costs and only 9% model costs from data; the median test window is 12 months, and a quarter of windows are six months or shorter. Among the 51 papers whose test window is not known to lie after the model's training cutoff, 98% neither test after the cutoff, nor evaluate live, nor use a chronologically consistent model. Only 9% report a multiplicity-adjusted result, and just five papers combine deducted costs, a leakage control and a reported trial count. Two of the five hypotheses pass (short windows; uncontrolled look-ahead). Three fail: more papers report a trial count than we predicted, though most of those counts are repeated random seeds rather than the number of strategies searched; and the predicted links between headline Sharpe and test-window length, and between headline Sharpe and costs, are not detectable in the 13 papers that state a headline Sharpe in their abstract or conclusion. We release the codes, quotes and script, and propose a six-line reporting minimum.
