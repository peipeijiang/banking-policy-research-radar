<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

实证研究通常依赖于对随机变量分布的假设（如潜在结果或潜变量），以识别某些感兴趣的参数。我们引入了一个统一框架，用于评估目标估计量在给定人群比例下对这些分布假设放松的敏感性。我们推导了当该比例在0到1之间变化时估计量可能取值的界，并利用可处理的有限维规划计算这些界。我们提出了界的插入估计量并刻画了其大样本性质。我们还证明了我们的界在不增加额外计算成本的情况下恢复了精确的非参数锐界，并引入了将误设定不确定性（misspecification uncertainty）与抽样不确定性分离的汇总度量，并按稳健性对研究进行排序。我们将该框架应用于评估以下方面的稳健性：(i) 带有流失（attrition）的随机实验中的平均处理效应，(ii) 结构模型中的反事实预测，以及(iii) 公式工具变量（formula instruments）。我们提供了实现所有方法的R和Python软件包。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Empirical work often relies on assumptions on the distribution of random variables, such as potential outcomes or latent variables, to identify some parameter of interest. We introduce a unified framework for assessing the sensitivity of a target estimand to relaxing these distributional assumptions for a given share of the population. We derive bounds on the values the estimand can take as this share varies between 0 and 1, and compute the bounds using tractable finite-dimensional programs. We propose plug-in estimators for the bounds and characterize their large sample properties. We also show that our bounds recover exact nonparametric sharp bounds at no additional computational cost, and introduce summary measures that separate misspecification uncertainty from sampling uncertainty and rank studies by their robustness. We apply our framework to assess the robustness of (i) average treatment effects in randomized experiments with attrition, (ii) counterfactual predictions in structural models, and (iii) formula instruments. We provide R and Python packages implementing all our methods.
