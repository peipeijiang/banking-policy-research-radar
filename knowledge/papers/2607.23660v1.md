<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文批判了一套根深蒂固的方法论惯例，这些惯例共同导致临床决策统计研究中的系统性功能障碍。具体包括：(1) 普遍使用假设检验来比较治疗方案，(2) 用于评估患者结局预测准确性的方法与患者护理脱节，(3) 荟萃分析（meta-analysis）在整合多项研究结果时的不良实践，以及(4) 以难以置信的确信度进行广泛研究。这种功能障碍似乎由三个因素维持：(i) 医学生和住院医师接受的统计方法论基础教育不足，(ii) 临床研究者依赖咨询生物统计学家（biostatisticians），后者在评估基金申请和论文投稿时充当统计把关人，以及(iii) 研究资助机构、医学期刊及监管医疗实践的政府机构的制度性惯例。我推测，必须进行系统性变革才能打破现有僵局，推动统计研究迈向更优均衡。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I critique a set of entrenched methodological conventions that collectively create systemic dysfunction in statistical research for clinical decisions. These include: (1) the prevalent use of hypothesis tests to compare treatments, (2) remoteness from patient care of the methods used to evaluate the accuracy of predictions of patient outcomes, (3) poor practice of meta-analysis to combine findings across studies, and (4) widespread research with incredible certitude. It appears that the dysfunction is held in place by three factors: (i) rudimentary instruction in statistical methodology received by medical students and residents, (ii) reliance of clinical researchers on consulting biostatisticians, wo act as statistical gatekeepers in evaluation of grant proposals and paper submissions, and (iii) institutional practices of research funding agencies, medical journals, and governmental bodies that regulate medical treatment. I conjecture that systemic changes are necessary to break the existing impasse, moving statistical research to a better equilibrium.
