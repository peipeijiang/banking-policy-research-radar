<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们基于巴西选举法院的选区级记录，逐分钟重建了巴西2026年总统决选公开计票结果，并将其与Kalshi和Polymarket上的每一笔交易进行了比较。当晚实时发布的一项预测从19:15起、在仅统计了1.6%的选区时，就给最终获胜者卢拉（Lula）赋予了0.998的概率。而市场在20:24、已统计46%时才达到0.9。若将该预测解读为其中心估计加上更宽的不确定性，则市场价格隐含的最终差距标准差约为实时预测的四倍，也约为基于2018年决选（此前误差最大的夜晚）校准的选区级预测的两倍。按这些预测估值，计票恢复后买方对卢拉的购买在扣除费用后每份Kalshi合约的预期利润为4至18美分；若按计票结果的州级外推估值，则为零。在计票暂停的两小时内，市场给卢拉的概率为0.25至0.44，低于我们此后根据当时已公开的选区数据计算出的每一个读数，而这些数据在实时中均不可得。在首轮投票之夜，当头条计票结果从其第一个文件起就显示最终领先者处于领先时，同样的市场比我们的预测更快。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We rebuild the public count of Brazil's 2026 presidential runoff minute by minute from the electoral court's section-level records and compare it with every trade on Kalshi and Polymarket. A projection published live on the night gave the eventual winner, Lula, a probability of 0.998 from 19:15, with 1.6% of sections counted. The markets reached 0.9 at 20:24, with 46% counted. Read as the projection's central estimate with a wider uncertainty, their prices imply a standard deviation of the final margin about four times the live projection's, and about twice that of a section-level projection calibrated on the 2018 runoff, the earlier night with the largest errors. Valued at these projections, takers' purchases of Lula after the count resumed had an expected profit of 4 to 18 cents per Kalshi contract after fees; valued at a state-level extrapolation of the count, it was zero. During a two-hour suspension of the count the markets gave Lula 0.25 to 0.44, below every reading we compute afterwards from the sections then public, none of which was available live. On the first-round night, when the headline count showed the eventual leader ahead from its first file, the same markets were faster than our projection.
