<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当彩票的概率结构能够独立于其最终奖金影响价值时的风险偏好。我们的第一个结果刻画了扰动期望效用表示，其中彩票价值分解为对奖金的期望效用和一个仅依赖于概率树的加性扰动项。关键的行为约束是分支消去性（branch cancellation），它将奖金评估与概率结构分离；以及树内占优性（within-tree dominance），它刻画了同一概率树内的评估是简单的且遵循占优关系。将分支消去性强化为彩票消去性（lottery cancellation），即把不变性要求从共同奖金扩展到共同子彩票，刻画了扰动在各分支间的加性可分离性。我们还刻画了依赖树的扰动项仅依赖于不同可能奖金数量的情形：在较弱的连续性要求下，加入同支撑独立性（same-support independence）可得到非退化彩票的支撑大小表示，而确定性等价条件将其扩展到退化彩票。最后，凹性赋予扰动项以复杂性成本（complexity cost）的解释。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study preferences under risk when the probability structure of a lottery can affect value separately from its final prizes. Our first result characterizes perturbed expected utility representation in which lottery value separates into expected utility over prizes and an additive perturbation term depending only on the probability tree. The key behavioral restrictions are branch cancellation, which separates prize evaluation from probability structure, and within-tree dominance, which captures that evaluations within the same probability tree are simple and follow dominance. Strengthening branch cancellation to lottery cancellation, which extends the invariance requirement from common prizes to common sublotteries, characterizes additive separability of the perturbation across branches. We also characterize when the tree dependent perturbation term depends only on the number of distinct possible prizes: under weaker continuity requirements, adding same-support independence yields a support size representation for nondegenerate lotteries, and a certainty equivalent condition extends it to degenerate lotteries. Finally, concavity gives the perturbation term its interpretation as a complexity cost.
