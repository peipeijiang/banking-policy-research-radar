<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本数据论文描述了一项匿名化发布的数据集，该数据集源自于在Ellsberg型瓮任务中针对风险（已知概率）和模糊不确定性（部分指定或未知概率）下决策行为的受控行为实验。该数据集包含来自两种实施方式（实验室和在线）的246名成人参与者的4,486条决策记录。在每项任务中，参与者报告其参与后续抽奖的最大投入金额；这些投入金额响应可被解释为在所实施的激励机制中支付意愿的代理指标。在部分任务中，参与者还选择了其希望下注的颜色。在线实施方式还额外包含了参与者对情境中感知不确定性的事后评估。该存储库进一步提供了详细的博弈定义、已实现的抽奖结果、参与者说明以及变量数据字典。原始导出数据包含4,544条决策记录；其中属于5名未满18岁参与者的58条记录因缺乏监护人同意公开数据发布的书面许可而被排除在本发布之外。该数据集支持复制研究、模糊态度模型的比较分析，以及重复参与情境下行为稳定性的分析。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This data paper describes an anonymized release derived from controlled behavioral experiments on decision-making under risk (known probabilities) and ambiguous uncertainty (partially specified or unknown probabilities) in Ellsberg-type urn tasks. The release contains 4,486 decision records from 246 adult participant records across two implementations (laboratory and online). In each task, participants reported their maximum stake to enter a subsequent lottery; these stake responses can be interpreted as proxies for willingness to pay within the implemented incentive mechanism. In a subset of tasks, participants also selected the color they wished to bet on. The online implementation additionally includes ex-post evaluations of perceived uncertainty across situations. The repository further provides detailed game definitions, realized lottery outcomes, participant instructions, and a data dictionary of variables. The source export contained 4,544 decision records; 58 records belonging to five participants younger than 18 were excluded from this release because documentation of guardian permission for unrestricted public data publication was not available. The dataset supports replication studies, comparisons of ambiguity-attitude models, and analyses of behavioral stability under repeated participation.
