<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一个统一框架，用于分析一类证据博弈。在这类博弈中，发送者寻求提高后验期望（例如市场价格），并选择是否披露或保留其信息。我们的模型涵盖了若干经典模型，包括依赖于类型的披露成本或收益、信息禀赋不确定以及柠檬市场。摩擦被刻画为随机披露成本，该成本为发送者私人所知，并被接收者带噪声地推断。我们给出了均衡的一个简单刻画，并表明其特征是：当摩擦较低或基本面不确定性较大时，披露增加。此外，在不确定性较高或摩擦较低时，关于披露成本的更大不确定性会降低披露的可能性。正成本并不必然排除向完全披露的 unraveling，尤其是当结果具有重尾或披露成本非常数时。最后，我们证明该模型可以用一个简单的 probit 模型进行估计，这表明成本不确定性能够描述现实世界中的行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a unified framework for a class of evidence games in which a sender, seeking to increase posterior expectations (e.g., a market price), chooses whether to reveal or withhold her information. Our model nests several classic models, including type-dependent disclosure costs or benefits, uncertain information endowment, and the market for lemons. Frictions are captured as random disclosure costs that are privately known to the sender and noisily inferred by the receiver. We derive a simple characterization of the equilibrium and show that it features increasing disclosure for lower frictions or greater fundamental uncertainty. Further, with high uncertainty or low frictions, greater uncertainty about disclosure costs decreases the likelihood of disclosure. Positive costs do not necessarily rule out unraveling to full disclosure, especially when outcomes have heavy tails or when disclosure costs are not constant. Finally, we demonstrate that the model can be estimated with a simple probit, suggesting that cost uncertainty is descriptive of real-world behavior.
