# AI Events, Persistence, and Volatility in US Utility and AI-Exposed Stocks: The AI-Power Channel

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13001.html)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过事件研究法考察2022-2025年四个AI事件对14只美国股票价格持续性和波动性的影响，发现事件主要影响波动性而非持续性，且只有与AI公司签订电力购买协议的公用事业公司（Constellation和Vistra）出现持续性变化，表明AI新闻通过AI-电力合同渠道影响股价。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察了2022年至2025年间发生的四起人工智能事件——ChatGPT、GPT-4、欧盟人工智能法案（EU AI Act）投票以及DeepSeek——是否改变了十四只美国上市股票的行为。分析的重点是两家与大型人工智能公司签署了电力购买协议（power purchase agreement）的公用事业公司——Constellation Energy和Vistra，并将其与未签署此类协议的公用事业公司进行比较。本文估计了每只股票在各事件前后时期的价格持续程度（degree of persistence），以确定其是否发生了变化。同时，本文还研究了波动率的变化以及市场反应在各事件之间是增强还是减弱。结果表明，所考虑的事件并未影响价格的持续程度；然而，这些事件几乎对每只股票的波动率都产生了影响。在公用事业公司中，只有Constellation和Vistra表现出持续性的转变，且该转变在经更广泛市场走势调整后依然显著，而未签署电力购买协议的公用事业公司则没有表现出这种转变。此外，市场反应从一次事件到下一次事件呈递增趋势。本文识别出一个特定渠道：人工智能新闻主要在公用事业公司与人工智能公司签订了售电合同时才会推动其股价变动。然而，这一证据具有提示性而非结论性。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines whether four AI events between 2022 and 2025 - ChatGPT, GPT-4, the EU AI Act vote, and DeepSeek - changed the behaviour of fourteen US-listed stocks. The focus of the analysis is on two utilities that signed power purchase agreements with large AI companies, Constellation Energy and Vistra, which are compared with utilities that did not. The degree of persistence of the price of each stock is estimated in the periods around the events to establish whether it has changed. Volatility changes and whether the market reaction grows or fades across the events are also investigated. The results suggest that the events considered do not affect the degree of persistence of prices; however, they have an impact on their volatility for almost every stock. Among the utilities, only Constellation and Vistra exhibit a persistence shift that survives an adjustment for developments in the wider market, whilst utilities without power purchase agreements do not. Further, the response increases from one event to the next. A specific channel is identified: AI news moves a utility's price mainly when the firm has a contract to sell power to an AI company. However, this evidence is suggestive rather than conclusive.
