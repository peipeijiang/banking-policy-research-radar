<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当评估会影响组织内后续策略性沟通时的最优反馈设计。一家公司在员工向经理沟通关于项目特定情况的私人信息之前，设计关于员工潜力的公开反馈。虽然信息量更高的反馈有助于经理做出更好的决策，但它也可能加剧偏好冲突并削弱后续沟通的可信度。我们刻画了最优反馈政策。第一，完全披露要么对所有先验信念都是最优的，要么对所有先验信念都严格次优。特别地，当偏好冲突足够小或足够大时，完全披露是最优的。第二，当完全披露次优时，不披露对某些先验信念是最优的，而部分披露对某些先验信念严格最优，当且仅当均衡沟通中的消息数量随后验信念而变化。第三，当高潜力罕见时，最优部分披露可以确凿地识别低潜力员工，而当高潜力常见时，则可以确凿地识别高潜力员工。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study optimal feedback design when evaluations affect subsequent strategic communication within organizations. A firm designs public feedback about an employee's potential before the employee communicates private information about project-specific circumstances to a manager. While more informative feedback helps the manager make better decisions, it can also intensify preference conflicts and undermine the credibility of subsequent communication. We characterize the optimal feedback policy. First, full disclosure is either optimal for all prior beliefs or strictly suboptimal for all of them. In particular, it is optimal when preference conflicts are sufficiently small or sufficiently large. Second, when full disclosure is suboptimal, no disclosure is optimal for some priors, while partial disclosure is strictly optimal for some priors if and only if the number of messages in equilibrium communication varies with posterior beliefs. Third, optimal partial disclosure can conclusively identify low-potential employees when high potential is rare, but high-potential employees when it is common.

</details>
