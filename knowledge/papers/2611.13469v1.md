<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们引入了一种非显然可操纵性的群体版本，该版本强于Troyan和Morrill（2020）的原始定义，弱于群体策略防篡改性（group strategy-proofness），并研究了其在Shapley-Scarf住房市场中的含义。我们利用这一新条件给出了最高交易循环机制（Top Trading Cycles mechanism）的若干刻画。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce a group version of non-obvious manipulability, which is stronger than the original definition by Troyan and Morrill (2020) and weaker than group strategy-proofness, and study its implications in Shapley-Scarf housing markets. We provide several characterizations of the Top Trading Cycles mechanism using the new condition.
