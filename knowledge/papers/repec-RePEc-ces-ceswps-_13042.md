# Will Fixed Book Prices Fix It? Common Agency and Vertical Integration of Publishers and Bookstores

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13042.html)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过共同代理与纵向整合模型研究固定书价制度对图书零售价格和福利的影响，发现纵向整合会削弱固定书价降低零售价的效果，使其更可能有利于出版商而非消费者。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

图书市场的特点是竞争性书店之间的共同分销以及出版商与连锁书店之间的纵向一体化，在许多国家，出版商被允许在书店设定固定书价（FBP）。在纵向分离的情况下，当出版商具有足够强的议价能力时，FBP可以降低零售价格，因为双重边际化的消除超过了品牌内竞争的损失。我们表明，纵向一体化从根本上改变了这种权衡。它消除了连锁内部的双重边际化，从而大幅缩小了FBP降低零售价格的空间。但由于FBP消除了同一书名之间的零售价格差异，实施FBP时配置扭曲减少，消费者福利和总福利不再一致。我们发现，在存在纵向一体化的情况下，与纵向分离的行业相比，引入FBP更不可能使消费者受益，而更可能使出版商受益。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Book markets are characterised by common distribution through competing bookstores and vertical integration between publishers and bookstore chains, and in many countries publishers are allowed to set fixed book prices (FBP) in the bookstores. Under vertical separation, FBP can reduce retail prices when publishers have sufficiently strong bargaining power, as the elimination of double marginalisation outweighs the loss of intra-brand competition. We show that vertical integration fundamentally changes this trade-off. It eliminates double marginalisation inside the chain, thereby substantially reducing the scope for FBP to lower retail prices. But since FBP eliminates retail price differences on the same title, the allocative distortions are reduced when imposing FBP and consumer welfare and total welfare are no longer aligned. We find that, in the presence of vertical integration, the introduction of FBP is less likely to benefit consumers and more likely to benefit publishers than in an industry with vertical separation.

</details>
