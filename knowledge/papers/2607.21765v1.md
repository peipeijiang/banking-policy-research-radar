# Screening with Product Mismatch

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21765v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一个垄断者销售一条产品线，其变体从买方视角来看是水平差异化的，但按生产成本排序。买方私下知晓自己的理想产品，其支付意愿可能与水平需求相关。卖方通过产品错配来筛选买方，而卖方必须筛选的内容决定了错配是增加还是减少信息租金。当买方仅在水平需求上存在差异时，错配会产生租金：卖方会诱导较少的错配，使所服务的买方获得比第一最优（first best）情况下更接近其理想的产品。当支付意愿与水平需求相关时，错配反而会减少租金：卖方会诱导更多的错配，将基础产品销售给那些有效产品为高级变体的买方，同时排除与之匹配度更高的买方，而更强的水平差异化可以扩大覆盖范围并提高利润。由于错配具有类型特异性，最优配置由个体理性（individual rationality）而非仅由激励相容（incentive compatibility）决定。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A monopolist sells a product line whose variants are horizontally differentiated from the buyers' perspective but ordered by production cost. Buyers privately know their ideal product, and willingness to pay may be correlated with horizontal need. The seller screens buyers through product mismatch, and what she must screen determines whether mismatch creates or reduces information rent. When buyers differ only in horizontal need, mismatch creates rent: the seller induces less mismatch, assigning served buyers products closer to their ideals than under the first best. When willingness to pay is correlated with horizontal need, mismatch instead reduces rent: the seller induces more mismatch, sells the basic product to buyers whose efficient products are advanced variants while excluding buyers better matched to it, and stronger horizontal differentiation can expand coverage and raise profit. Because mismatch is type-specific, optimal allocations are determined by individual rationality rather than by incentive compatibility alone.

</details>
