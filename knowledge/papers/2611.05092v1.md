<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究连续度量图上随机设施选址的策略防伪（strategyproof, SP）机制的自动化设计，目标是最小化总距离。我们开发了用于计算给定图和代理数量下SP机制可达到的最优近似比的上界和下界的方法。
  对于下界，我们提出了一种保成本的归约到有限域的方法，通过线性规划对偶证书为无限制SP机制产生下界。
  对于上界，我们引入了机制的有限表示，称为分段线性归属（piecewise-linear attributions, PL attributions），其在报告空间的三角剖分上使用仿射权重混合分段线性确定性机制。我们证明，对于由PL归属表示的机制，检查SP和近似保证归结为在紧致多胞体上的有限多个二次非负性检验。通过精确算术和比较，每次验证要么证明所提出的性质，要么返回其失败的位置。此外，我们引入了激励概念的强化版本，称为强策略防伪性（strong strategyproofness）。我们证明，对于固定的组件机制和PL归属的区域，强策略防伪性要求可以表示为关于归属系数的有限多个线性约束。利用这些理论结果，我们提出了一种启发式过程来计算近似比的上界。
  我们在若干度量图上计算了经过认证的上界和下界，并对所提出的搜索过程进行了数值评估。
  最后，我们证明图上的确定性SP满射（onto）机制是强策略防伪的，并且允许PL归属。因此，将注意力限制在允许PL归属的强策略防伪机制上不会丢失任何确定性SP满射机制。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study automated design of strategyproof (SP) mechanisms for randomized facility location on continuous metric graphs, with the objective of minimizing total distance. We develop methods for computing upper and lower bounds on the optimal approximation ratio achievable by SP mechanisms for a given graph and number of agents.
  For the lower bound, we propose a cost-preserving reduction to a finite domain that yields lower bounds for unrestricted SP mechanisms through linear programming dual certificates.
  For the upper bound, we introduce finite representations of mechanisms, called piecewise-linear attributions (PL attributions), that mix piecewise-linear deterministic mechanisms using affine weights on a triangulation of the report space. We show that for mechanisms represented by PL attributions, checking SP and an approximation guarantee reduces to finitely many quadratic nonnegativity tests over compact polytopes. With exact arithmetic and comparisons, each verification certifies the proposed property or returns locations where it fails. In addition, we introduce a strengthening of the incentive notion called strong strategyproofness. We show that for fixed component mechanisms and regions of a PL attribution, the strong strategyproofness requirement can be expressed by a finite number of linear constraints on the attribution coefficients. Using these theoretical results, we propose a heuristic procedure for computing an upper bound on the approximation ratio.
  We compute certified upper and lower bounds on several metric graphs and numerically evaluate the proposed search procedure.
  Lastly, we show that deterministic SP onto mechanisms on graphs are strongly strategyproof and admit PL attributions. Thus restricting attention to strongly strategyproof mechanisms admitting PL attributions loses no deterministic SP onto mechanisms.
