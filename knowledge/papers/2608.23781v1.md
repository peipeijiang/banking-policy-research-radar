<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当同一决策者在一次深思熟虑干预前后被观察时的确定性选择，该干预使得两个固定序数后果维度中的一个更加显著。两种选择模式都是路径独立的。普通选择尊重二维支配关系，而在更充分的深思熟虑下，任何二元反转都必须偏向在被强调维度上严格更优的备选方案。我们精确刻画了允许的普通排序和全视野排序。该刻画产生了一个受保护偏序、尖锐的成对和菜单级识别、对菜单级选择变化的符号限制，以及对不完整观测的无环性检验。对于有序的深思熟虑模式序列，从任何固定菜单中的选择形成一条单调权衡路径：每次切换都改善被强调维度、恶化另一维度，且被放弃的备选方案不会重新出现。当被强调的后果顺序是严格时，允许的排序具有等价的Kemeny-Kendall表示。该框架是序数的：它既不要求基数权衡，也不要求词典式优先，并且适用于深思熟虑对两个固定后果维度之一给予更大强调的任何情形。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study deterministic choice when the same decision maker is observed before and after a deliberative intervention that makes one of two fixed ordinal consequence dimensions more salient. Both choice modes are path independent. Ordinary choice respects two-dimensional dominance, while any binary reversal under fuller deliberation must favor the alternative that is strictly better on the emphasized dimension. We characterize exactly the admissible ordinary and full-horizon rankings. The characterization yields a protected partial order, sharp pairwise and menu-level identification, a signed restriction on menu-level choice changes, and an acyclicity test for incomplete observations. With an ordered sequence of deliberative modes, choices from any fixed menu form a monotone tradeoff path: every switch improves the emphasized dimension, worsens the other dimension, and an abandoned alternative cannot reappear. When the emphasized consequence order is strict, the admissible rankings have an equivalent Kemeny-Kendall representation. The framework is ordinal: it requires neither a cardinal tradeoff nor lexicographic priority, and it applies whenever deliberation gives greater emphasis to one of two fixed consequence dimensions.
