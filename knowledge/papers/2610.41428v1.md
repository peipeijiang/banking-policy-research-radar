<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

面板局部投影（LP）结合固定效应（FE）估计被广泛用于评估经济冲击在各国或各地区的动态效应。然而，面板LP模型的固定效应估计会受到Nickell偏误的影响，该偏误源于LP设定中的预测结构，即使回归变量中不包含滞后因变量也是如此。这种偏误使基于传统t统计量的标准推断失效。我们引入xtlp，这是一个新的Stata命令，实现了带有分面板刀切法（split-panel jackknife, SPJ）偏误校正的面板LP。它消除了Nickell偏误并恢复了标准统计推断的有效性。该命令支持多种设定，包括因变量的不同变换、额外控制变量以及灵活的方差估计量。我们通过考察金融危机对产出影响的实证应用来展示xtlp的功能。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Panel local projection (LP) estimated with fixed effects (FEs) is widely used to evaluate the dynamic effects of economic shocks across countries or regions. However, FE estimation of panel LP models is subject to Nickell bias arising from the predictive structure of LP specifications, even when lagged dependent variables are not included in the regressors. This bias invalidates standard inference based on conventional t-statistics. We introduce xtlp, a new Stata command that implements panel LP with split-panel jackknife (SPJ) bias correction. It removes the Nickell bias and restores the validity of standard statistical inference. The command accommodates a broad range of specifications, including different transformations of the dependent variable, additional control variables, and flexible variance estimators. We illustrate xtlp through empirical applications that examine the effects of financial crises on output.
