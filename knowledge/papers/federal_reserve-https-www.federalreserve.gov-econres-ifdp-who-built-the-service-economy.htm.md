<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Immo Schott和Vladimir Smirnyagin 我们从实证上表明，美国就业从制造业向服务业的再配置是由大型现有服务企业推动的，这些企业通过开设更多分支机构实现扩张。在这些事实的引导下，我们构建了一个两部门企业动态的一般均衡模型，其中现有企业可以通过运营多个分支机构来实现规模扩张。在定量上，制造业生产率的相对提高和复制成本——即开设和管理额外分支机构的成本——的下降，共同解释了观察到的部门再配置的一半以上。生产率渠道将就业转向服务业，但企业规模分布保持不变。相比之下，较低的复制成本导致大型多分支机构服务企业不成比例地扩张，并且其自身大约解释了两股力量共同产生的再配置的三分之一。我们的结果确定了企业动态——具体而言，现有企业通过新分支机构实现的扩张——是结构转型背后一个在定量上重要的力量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Immo Schott and Vladimir Smirnyagin We show empirically that the reallocation of employment from manufacturing to services in the U.S. was driven by large incumbent service firms, which expanded by opening additional establishments. Guided by these facts, we develop a two-sector general equilibrium model of firm dynamics in which incumbent firms can scale by operating multiple establishments. Quantitatively, a relative increase in manufacturing productivity and a decline in replication costs—the cost of opening and managing additional establishments—jointly account for more than half of the observed sectoral reallocation. The productivity channel shifts employment toward services but leaves the firm-size distribution unchanged. Lower replication costs, by contrast, generate the disproportionate expansion of large, multi-establishment service firms and, on their own, account for roughly one third of the reallocation generated by the two forces jointly. Our results identify firm dynamics—specifically, incumbent expansion through new establishments—as a quantitatively important force behind structural transformation.
