# AI and the Two Margins of Work: Why Job Losses Show Up Before Job Creation

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17530v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

技术变革在两个边际上作用于劳动力需求：既有活动内部的调整，以及额外活动的创造（通常由新企业完成）。工资统计能及时记录前者，而对后者的记录则较为滞后。在计算时代，1978年或之后进入的企业到1998年已占工资总额的42%，且新进入企业群体在五年后保留了约80%的工资总额；在受自动化影响最深的行业中，现有企业在进入者积累之前便已收缩，这一序列在该时期早期表现精确，到末期则仅有提示性。我们构建了一个模型，其中一项AI能力降低了既有生产中可接受产出的全部成本，并使额外项目值得尝试，创始人努力与雇佣工时相分离。如果运营产品即时重新缩放，自动化不会取代任何总量工时；如果运营产品需要时间调整，工时先下降，随后随着现有企业重新扩张和新产品出现而恢复，长期方向由收入效应决定。截至2026年第一季度的机构就业流动数据显示，在语言模型暴露度上两个边际均无梯度，这既是模型在观测到的采用路径下所预测的结果，也是工资无任何影响时会呈现的样子；而以创始任务暴露度为研究对象的研究发现进入率上升了10%至20%。我们提出了净恢复比率（net reinstatement ratio），并证明那些对新工作记录滞后的统计数据会在数年内高估替代效应。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Technological change acts on labor demand at two margins: adjustment within established activities and the creation of additional activities, often by new firms. Payroll statistics record the first promptly and the second late. In the computing era, firms that entered in 1978 or later held 42 percent of payroll by 1998 and entering cohorts kept about 80 percent of their payroll after five years; in the industries most exposed to automation, incumbents contracted before entrants accumulated, a sequence that is precise early in the period and suggestive by its end. We develop a model in which one AI capability lowers the full cost of acceptable output in existing production and makes additional projects worth attempting, with founder effort separate from hired hours. If operating products re-scale instantly, automation displaces no aggregate hours; if they take time, hours fall first and recover as incumbents re-expand and new products arrive, with the long-run sign set by income effects. Establishment job flows through the first quarter of 2026 show no gradient on language-model exposure on either margin, which is what the model predicts under the observed adoption path and also what no effect on payroll would look like; studies with founding-task exposure find entry rising 10 to 20 percent. We propose a net reinstatement ratio and show that statistics which record new work late overstate displacement for years.

</details>
