我们介绍了fastC-GC和fastC-GC$^*$，这是利用由Reichenbach共同原因原则（RCCP）启发的双检验规则以及因果化Granger因果性（c-GC和c-GC$^*$）的条件集，进行时间序列图估计的计算高效方法。这些方法使用标准化数据、岭残差相关以及通过傅里叶变换评估的完全循环移位枚举。共享精度计算和Schur降阶更新避免了重复的回归拟合。在单线程基准测试中，fastC-GC比所比较的c-GC实现快68-277倍，fastC-GC$^*$比c-GC$^*$快192-886倍。在四个带有参考图的测试套件中，聚合平均精确率-召回率曲线下面积（AUPRC）与相应基线的差异最多为0.019，但在每个套件中所选阈值下的F1均较低。因此，计算收益伴随着估计器和所选图的变化。在八种方法中，Bagged PCMCI+在稀疏VAR、自相关结构过程和DREAM3上具有最高的平均AUPRC；CUTS+在Lorenz-96上领先。风洞和河流流量应用展示了推断图之间的差异，但未确立因果准确性。我们证明了fastC-GC$^*$的计算恒等式和一个受限的总体邻接结果。因果解释需要所述的图假设；所实施检验的有效性额外需要合适的平移不变零律。经验零拒绝率指导了条件阈值$β= 0.005$的选择，但未确立一般检验有效性或全图误差控制。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce fastC-GC and fastC-GC$^*$, computationally efficient methods for time-series graph estimation using the two-test rule motivated by Reichenbach's common cause principle (RCCP) and the conditioning sets of causalised Granger causality (c-GC and c-GC$^*$). The methods use standardized data, ridge-residual correlation, and complete circular shift enumeration evaluated by Fourier transforms. Shared precision calculations and Schur downdates avoid repeated regression fits. On a single-threaded benchmark, fastC-GC was 68-277 times faster than the compared c-GC implementation, and fastC-GC$^*$ was 192-886 times faster than c-GC$^*$. Across four suites with reference graphs, aggregate mean area under the precision-recall curve (AUPRC) differed from the corresponding baseline by at most 0.019, but F1 was lower at the selected thresholds in every suite. The computational gains therefore accompany a change in estimator and selected graphs. Among eight methods, Bagged PCMCI+ had the highest mean AUPRC on sparse VAR, an autocorrelated structural process, and DREAM3; CUTS+ led on Lorenz-96. Wind-tunnel and river-discharge applications illustrate differences between inferred graphs without establishing causal accuracy. We prove the computational identities and a restricted population adjacency result for fastC-GC$^*$. Causal interpretation requires the stated graphical assumptions; validity of the implemented tests additionally requires a suitable shift-invariant null law. Empirical null rejection rates informed the conditional threshold $β= 0.005$, without establishing general test validity or graph-wide error control.
