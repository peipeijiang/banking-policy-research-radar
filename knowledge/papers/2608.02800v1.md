# Raising Rivals' Costs on Hybrid Platforms: The Complementarity of Fees and Self-Preferencing

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02800v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文通过模型分析混合平台如何通过收费和自我优待提高竞争对手成本，并发现这两种工具是战略互补的，单一工具监管即可有效，但会损害消费者福利。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

混合平台通过平台费用和自我优待使第三方卖家处于不利地位，而监管机构一直担心对其中任一手段的限制可能会加剧另一手段的影响。我们构建了一个同时选择这两种手段的平台模型，并发现了相反的结果：单一手段监管是有效的，因为这两种手段具有战略互补性，对任一手段的监管都会抑制另一种手段。我们还发现，这两种手段实现了与垄断相同的效果，即提高价格并降低消费者福利，同时却能够通过所有常规反垄断测试。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Hybrid platforms disadvantage third-party sellers through the platform fee and self-preferencing, and regulators have worried that constraining either instrument may intensify the other. We model a platform that chooses both instruments and find the opposite: single-instrument regulation is effective because the instruments are strategic complements, and regulating either instrument curbs the other. We also find that the two instruments achieve what monopolization achieves, higher prices and reduced consumer welfare, while passing every conventional antitrust test.

</details>
