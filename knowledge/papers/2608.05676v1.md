<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们利用一个具有随机波动性的动态因子模型，刻画了美国一百多个宏观经济与金融变量的非对称尾部风险。一个统一的机制将增长风险、通胀风险以及部门风险的异质性联系起来：共同因子及其波动性同步变动，而异质性载荷则将由此产生的非对称性不均等地传导至各变量。我们发现，非对称尾部风险普遍存在，但具有异质性。这种异质性具有系统性：因子暴露，尤其是对金融状况和通胀的暴露，解释了各变量尾部非对称性截面差异的一半以上。这些暴露决定了经济中脆弱性集中于何处，以及尾部风险的平衡如何随时间推移而转变。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We characterize asymmetric tail risk across over one hundred U.S. macroeconomic and financial variables using a dynamic factor model with stochastic volatility. A single mechanism unifies growth-at-risk, inflation-at-risk, and sectoral risk heterogeneity: common factors and their volatilities move together, while heterogeneous loadings transmit the resulting asymmetry unevenly across variables. We find that asymmetric tail risk is pervasive but heterogeneous. The heterogeneity is systematic: factor exposures, especially to financial conditions and inflation, explain over half of the cross-sectional variation in tail asymmetry across variables. These exposures determine where in the economy vulnerabilities concentrate and how the balance of tail risks shifts over time.
