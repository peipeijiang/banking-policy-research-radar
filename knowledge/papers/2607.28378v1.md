# Do Crises Increase Parochial Behavior? Evidence from Donations During Covid

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人们在危机期间是否会更倾向于善待自己的内群体？我们以地理来界定内群体与外群体，并研究了新冠疫情期间人们向本地慈善机构与非本地慈善机构的捐赠情况。证据表明，地方主义倾向有所增强：我们记录了向本地慈善机构捐赠的相对增加，并显示疫情严重地区的捐赠者增加了对本地捐赠，同时对国际灾难的响应度有所降低。本地捐赠的增加在疫情第一年最为显著。我们的解释是，捐赠中更强的地方主义倾向，源于在地方问题凸显之际，人们对新冠疫情的直接影响更为担忧。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Do people behave more favorably towards their in-group during a crisis? Defining in/out-groups by geography, we study donations to local versus non-local charities during the Covid pandemic. The evidence points to increased parochialism: We document a relative increase in donations to local charities and show that donors in high-Covid areas increased their local giving and became less responsive to international disasters. Increased local giving was most pronounced during the first year of the pandemic. Our interpretation is that greater parochialism in donations was due to heightened concern about the immediate impact of Covid at a time when localities were salient.
