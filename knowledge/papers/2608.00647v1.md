<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

实物支撑的杠杆事件头寸需要真实信用：若抵押品C获得杠杆率L，协议需提供(L-1)C，并将合并后的金额用于获取已确认的事件敞口。本文为该资本层构建了一种与场所无关的链上信用架构，并建立了其资本市场的内生模型。该模型将交易者、优先信贷流动性提供者、做市商、清算人和清算兜底提供者区分开来；形式化了资金池与债务份额、基于利用率和风险敏感性的利息、抵押锁定头寸账户、场所能力、做市商承诺、提款队列、隔离资金池、不可赎回准备金以及确定性损失瀑布；并对内生参与者参与、杠杆需求、流动性提款、清算人进入、准备金补充、挤兑及共同因子传染进行了建模。形式化结果确立了平衡的真实单位与整数单位记账、结算确认的债务优先级、交易者剩余所有权、幂等部分结算、非稀释性份额发行、次级优先于优先级的减值顺序、损失分担的提款队列、利用率均衡条件，以及损失分配与传染边界。该版本保留了28个精确测试夹具和31,082项确定性检查，并新增了固定随机种子的基于智能体的实验，包含70,207,488次标量不变量评估且零失败。实验表明，分层保护能够降低但无法消除优先级损失；5倍杠杆会显著加大资本压力；若做市商准入扩张过快，其容量反而可能提高总体缺口。研究结果为作者指定行为下的合成机制比较，而非对年化收益率、违约率、场所流动性或生产安全性的预测。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A physically backed leveraged event position requires real credit: if collateral C receives leverage L, the protocol supplies (L-1)C and uses the combined amount to acquire recognized event exposure. This paper develops a venue-agnostic on-chain credit architecture for that capital layer and an endogenous model of its capital market. It separates traders, Senior Credit LPs, market makers, liquidators, and Liquidation Backstop Providers; formalizes pool and debt shares, utilization- and risk-sensitive interest, collateral-locked position accounts, venue capabilities, market-maker commitments, withdrawal queues, isolated pools, non-redeemable reserves, and a deterministic loss waterfall; and models endogenous provider participation, leverage demand, liquidity withdrawal, liquidator entry, reserve replenishment, runs, and common-factor contagion. Formal results establish balanced real- and integer-unit accounting, settlement-confirmed debt priority, trader residual ownership, idempotent partial settlement, non-dilutive share issuance, junior-before-Senior impairment, loss-participating withdrawal queues, utilization-equilibrium conditions, and loss-allocation and contagion bounds. The release preserves 28 exact fixtures and 31,082 deterministic checks and adds fixed-seed agent-based experiments with 70,207,488 scalar invariant evaluations and zero failures. The experiments show that layered protection reduces but does not eliminate Senior loss, that 5x leverage materially increases capital pressure, and that market-maker capacity can raise aggregate shortfall if admission expands too quickly. Results are synthetic mechanism comparisons under author-specified behavior, not forecasts of APY, defaults, venue liquidity, or production safety.
