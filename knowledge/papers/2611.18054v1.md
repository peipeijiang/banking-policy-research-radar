<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个单中心城市，其中有有限多个家庭，每个家庭可以持有任意可测的土地集合，并承担取决于其持有土地空间分布的通勤成本。在均衡中，每一地块都是一个区间，且地块按宗地规模从中心向外排序。在任何位置调整土地持有量的自由将支撑租金限制在竞争性投标租金与所分配家庭的投标租金之间的一个区间内。该区间排除了在宗地限制为区间时支撑同一配置的租金，包括Berliant和Fujita（1992）所构造的租金，并使得支撑租金的存在性即使在家户相同的情况下也成为一个实质性问题。我们给出了局部支撑租金存在的充分条件，该租金满足每个家庭的预算约束并阻止所有小偏离。我们还证明，任何均衡都最小化达到均衡效用的实际资源成本，并在单一交叉条件下将排序结果推广到异质性家庭。最后，我们讨论了与特征价格模型的关系，区分了整宗地的价格与支配宗地边界变化和碎片化成本的位置特定租金。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a monocentric city with finitely many households, each of which may hold any measurable set of land and incurs commuting costs that depend on the spatial distribution of its holdings. In equilibrium, every parcel is an interval and parcels are ordered by lot size from the center outward. The freedom to adjust land holdings at any location restricts supporting rents to a band between the competing bid rents and the assigned household's bid rent. This band excludes rents that support the same allocation when lots are restricted to intervals, including the one constructed by Berliant and Fujita (1992), and makes the existence of a supporting rent a substantive question even with identical households. We give sufficient conditions for the existence of a locally supporting rent that meets every household's budget and deters all small deviations. We also show that any equilibrium minimizes the real resource cost of attaining equilibrium utilities and extend the sorting result to heterogeneous households under a single-crossing condition. Finally, we discuss the relation to hedonic models, distinguishing the prices of whole parcels from location-specific rents that govern the cost of changes in parcel boundaries and fragmentation.
