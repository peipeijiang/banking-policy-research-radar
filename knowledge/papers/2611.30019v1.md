<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

鉴于电网级电池的高昂成本，发展和低收入地区需要其他储能方案。在这些地区，大容量蓄水通常比电池更具经济可行性。本文针对一种此类储能配置——埃塞俄比亚干旱的阿法尔地区的太阳能-电网混合供水系统——提出了双框架建模方法。该系统利用一座1，900立方米的高架水库作为“水力电池”，替代传统的电化学储能，从而显著降低资本支出。研究使用Python进行高分辨率技术模拟，并使用Xendee进行经济优化，结果显示太阳能保证率为55.1%，内部收益率（IRR）为10%。结果证实，采用水力储能的混合系统为能源受限地区的水资源获取提供了一种经济可行且可靠的解决方案。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Given the high cost of grid-scale batteries, other energy storage approaches are needed for developing and low-income regions. Elevated volumes of water are often more economically feasible energy storage options than batteries in these contexts. This paper presents a dual-framework modeling approach for one such energy storage setup: a solar-grid hybrid water supply system in the arid Afar region of Ethiopia. By utilizing an elevated 1,900 m3 water reservoir as a "hydraulic battery," the system replaces traditional electrochemical storage, significantly reducing capital expenditures. Using Python for high-resolution technical simulation and Xendee for economic optimization, the study shows a 55.1% solar fraction and a 10% Internal Rate of Return (IRR). The results confirm that hybrid systems with hydraulic storage offer a financially viable and reliable solution for water access in energy-constrained regions.
