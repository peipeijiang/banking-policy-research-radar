# Pricing the Unprovable: Ambiguity, Turnstile Runs, and the Verifiability Discount in Crypto Protocols

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21540v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出“可验证性折价”概念，用旋转门模型和VDISC框架分析Zcash Orchard等加密协议在漏洞修复后因无法证明历史是否被利用而产生的市场价值折价，发现市场反应方向与预测一致但仅为方向性证据。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

隐私保护协议可以在不确认漏洞是否曾被利用的情况下修复漏洞，即使在无人报告被盗且总供给受到保护时也是如此。我们将这一缺口所导致的潜在价值减损称为可验证性折价。一个转门模型展示了早期退出如何将可能的短缺转移给剩余持有者，以及为何可信证据会随着风险敞口的增长而变得更有价值。我们提出VDISC，一个可复现框架，分别记录已报告损失、暴露价值、公开证据、收益和资金池流动。它结合了Orchard的分阶段披露与重建的链上资金流、此前的Zcash案例以及一个包含40个事件的安全面板。Orchard遵循了预测方向：在修复和供给保证之后，市场反应较为平淡，而最大的负收益和聚合屏蔽池净流出则与承认无法排除过去滥用行为的声明相一致。这些模式是方向性的，而非因果性的。在各事件中，按市值标准化的已报告损失与未解决风险敞口在38个具有公开损失数字的事件中存在重叠（Pearson 0.86，Spearman 0.56），部分原因是风险敞口往往根据损失本身进行编码，因此仅凭收益无法区分已报告损失与未解决历史。这些发现引出了三个候选设计优先事项：生成关于过去状态的隐私兼容证据，限制单一缺陷所暴露的状态，并明确披露残余不确定性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Privacy-preserving protocols can repair a vulnerability without establishing whether it was exploited beforehand, even when no theft is reported and aggregate supply is protected. We call the potential value reduction from this gap the verifiability discount. A turnstile model shows how early withdrawal can shift a possible shortfall to remaining holders and why credible evidence can become more valuable as exposure grows. We introduce VDISC, a reproducible framework that records reported loss, exposed value, public evidence, returns, and pool flows separately. It combines Orchard's staged disclosures with reconstructed on-chain flows, earlier Zcash cases, and a 40-event security panel. Orchard follows the predicted direction: market responses were muted after repair and supply assurance, while the largest negative return and aggregate shielded-pool net outflow aligned with the acknowledgment that past abuse could not be ruled out. These patterns are directional, not causal. Across events, market-cap-normalized reported loss and unresolved exposure overlap among the 38 events with a public loss figure (Pearson 0.86, Spearman 0.56), partly because exposure is often coded from the loss itself, so returns alone cannot distinguish reported loss from unresolved history. The findings motivate three candidate design priorities: produce privacy-compatible evidence about past states, limit the state exposed by a single flaw, and disclose residual uncertainty explicitly.
