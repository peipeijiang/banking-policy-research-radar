本文开发了用于检验离散选择随机效用模型（RUM）的计算算法。满足RUM一致性的选择概率集合构成一个多面体，现有的计算方法依赖于其顶点表示。我们利用替代的半空间表示（Block--Marschak不等式）和网络流表示，开发了两种方法，分别基于交替方向乘子法和基于流的列生成算法。与当前基于顶点的方法相比，我们的算法更适合GPU实现。我们为所有三种算法建立了收敛性保证并提供了最优性证书。所得算法相较于现有方法实现了$100$--$600\times$的加速。基于我们方法的求解器已在Github上公开可用，地址为\href{https://github.com/mingxiz/d-rum-testing-gpu}{\texttt{d-rum-testing-gpu}}。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops computational algorithms for testing discrete-choice random utility models (RUM). The set of RUM-consistent choice probabilities forms a polytope, and existing computational methods rely on its vertex representation. We exploit alternative half-space (Block--Marschak inequalities) and network-flow representations to develop two methods based on the alternating direction method of multipliers and a flow-based column-generation algorithm. Compared with current vertex-based methods, our algorithms are better suited to GPU implementation. We establish convergence guarantees and provide optimality certificates for all three algorithms. The resulting algorithms achieve $100$--$600\times$ speedups over existing methods. The solver based on our methods is publicly available on Github at \href{https://github.com/mingxiz/d-rum-testing-gpu}{\texttt{d-rum-testing-gpu}}.
