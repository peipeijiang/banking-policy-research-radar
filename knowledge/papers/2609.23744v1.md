<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

组织在激励机制、治理结构、绩效测量、分析工具以及反复重组等内部机制上投入巨大。然而，尤其是在大型成熟组织中，实际实现的绩效提升往往微弱或难以持续。标准解释强调由激励错位、信息问题或有限理性所导致的配置无效率。尽管这些解释具有相关性，但它们难以说明这样一类环境：其中努力程度很高、优化能力精湛，而总体绩效仍远低于显而易见的潜力。本文发展了一种关于组织内部结构性无效率的理论。其核心机制是内部外部性（internal externalities）的存在：组织机制同时作用于多个绩效目标时所产生的未定价交叉效应。当此类效应符号混杂时，组织努力会在内部被部分抵消，从而对实际绩效形成结构性上限。本文引入了对组织基本要素与关键绩效指标的一种简单表示，并推导出一条实际绩效定律（realized-performance law），其中内部冲突作为一个乘数折扣因子进入。在较弱且现实的条件下，结构性无效率主导配置无效率，从而解释了为什么在组织一致性恢复之前，优化往往失败。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Organizations invest heavily in internal mechanisms such as incentive systems, governance structures, performance measurement, analytics, and repeated reorganizations. Yet realized performance gains are often weak or short-lived, particularly in large and mature organizations. Standard explanations emphasize allocative inefficiency arising from incentive misalignment, information problems, or bounded rationality. While relevant, these explanations struggle to account for environments in which effort is high, optimization capabilities are sophisticated, and aggregate performance remains far below apparent potential. This paper develops a theory of structural inefficiency inside organizations. The central mechanism is the presence of internal externalities: unpriced cross-effects of organizational mechanisms acting simultaneously on multiple performance objectives. When such effects are mixed in sign, organizational effort is partially canceled internally, creating a structural ceiling on realized performance. The paper introduces a simple representation of organizational primitives and key performance indicators and derives a realized-performance law in which internal conflict enters as a multiplicative discount factor. Under weak and realistic conditions, structural inefficiency dominates allocative inefficiency, explaining why optimization often fails before organizational coherence is restored.
