<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

生成式人工智能可以降低生产精美申请材料的成本，却无法提升申请者胜任工作的能力。我们研究一位雇主，该雇主观察到由可观测质量所概括的资质和工作经历，但必须从申请者可以操纵的报告中推断其他与工作相关的属性，而操纵成本取决于私人已知的操纵能力。雇主在容量约束下承诺遵守某一招聘规则，我们以扣除申请者操纵成本后的匹配价值来评估这些规则。我们证明，随着具有相同可观测质量的申请者之间操纵能力日益分散，社会最优招聘收敛于仅依据可观测质量进行选择，我们将这一现象称为策略性特征坍缩（strategic feature collapse）。为捕捉报告所带来的剩余福利增益，我们构造了一个简单的双轨机制，直接录取具有高可观测质量的申请者，并为部分原本被排除的申请者提供一条基于报告的录取途径。尽管多维筛选具有复杂性，该机制在附加正则性条件下是渐近最优的，并通过二阶项匹配无约束最优情形下报告所带来的福利增益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Generative AI can lower the cost of producing polished application materials without improving applicants' abilities to do the job. We study an employer who observes qualifications and work history, summarized by observable quality, but must infer other job-relevant attributes from reports that applicants can manipulate at costs that depend on privately known manipulation ability. The employer commits to a hiring rule under a capacity constraint, and we evaluate rules by match value net of applicants' manipulation costs. We show that, as manipulation ability becomes increasingly dispersed among applicants with the same observable quality, socially optimal hiring converges to selection on observable quality alone, a phenomenon we call strategic feature collapse. To capture the remaining welfare gain from reports, we construct a simple dual-track mechanism that directly admits applicants with high observable quality and offers some otherwise excluded applicants a report-based route to admission. Despite the complexity of multidimensional screening, this mechanism is asymptotically optimal under additional regularity, matching the unrestricted optimum's welfare gain from reports through second order.
