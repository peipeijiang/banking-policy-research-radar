<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们引入并分析了一种循环赛同时资源分配竞赛，其动机来源于体育联赛以及其他竞争环境，在这些环境中，代理人必须在多次对抗中分配有限资源。参与者在与所有对手的比赛前预先分配资源，比赛结果由相对分配量决定。我们研究了两种收益方案：总积分制，即参与者每赢得一场比赛便累积奖励；赢家通吃制，即只有锦标赛冠军获得奖励。对于三名参与者，我们提供了两种方案下完整的均衡刻画。对于四名或更多参与者，我们证明总积分收益存在Blotto型均衡，而赢家通吃收益则需要数值和算法分析。为解决这一问题，我们开发了一种带有随机惯性的最佳反应动态算法，并用其近似四名和五名参与者博弈中的均衡。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce and analyze a round-robin simultaneous resource allocation contest, motivated by sports leagues and other competitive environments where agents must distribute limited resources across multiple encounters. Players allocate resources in advance to matches against all opponents, and match outcomes are determined by relative allocations. We study two payoff schemes: total points, where players accumulate rewards for each win, and winner-takes-all, where only the tournament winner is rewarded. For three players, we provide complete equilibrium characterizations under both schemes. For four or more players, we show that the total points payoff admits Blotto-type equilibria, while the winner-takes-all payoff requires numerical and algorithmic analysis. To address this, we develop a best-response dynamics algorithm with stochastic inertia and use it to approximate equilibria in games with four and five players.
