# Recommendations with Costly Verification

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01195v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文构建一个推荐者面对具有异质验证成本的消费者的信息设计模型，刻画降低验证成本（透明度）在承诺与不承诺下对虚假推荐和消费者福利的影响，发现承诺情形下透明度可能同时恶化推荐质量与福利，其充要条件是消费者成本类型分布的对数弹性递减。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

推荐方面临着能够验证推荐的消费者，但这些消费者在注意力成本或采纳选择的显著性上存在差异。我们刻画了在何种条件下，一项通过降低验证产品质量成本来提高透明度的技术会增加或减少虚假推荐。在没有承诺的情况下，降低验证成本从不会增加虚假推荐，但当推荐方能够承诺某一政策时，透明度可能使推荐质量和消费者福利双双恶化。其充要条件是消费者成本类型分布的对数弹性递减。当该分布具有足够厚的尾部，或混合了成熟型与天真型消费者时，该条件可以成立。我们的结果可应用于平台推荐列表、安全评级和审计。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A recommender faces consumers who can verify recommendations, but differ in their attention costs or in the salience of their choice of adoption. We characterize the conditions under which a technology that increases transparency by lowering the cost of verifying a product's quality will increase or decrease false recommendations. Without commitment, reducing verification costs never increases false recommendations, but when the recommender can commit to a policy, transparency can worsen both the quality of recommendations and consumer welfare. The necessary and sufficient condition is that the log-elasticity of the distribution of consumer cost types is decreasing. It can hold when the distribution is sufficiently fat-tailed, or when it mixes sophisticated and naive consumers. Our results can be applied to platform-recommended listings, security ratings, and audits.

</details>
