# PanelGP: A Probabilistic Factor Model for Asset-Return Panels

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04040v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出基于高斯过程先验的非参数条件因子模型 PanelGP，用稀疏变分推断联合估计载荷与因子，可同时预测股票收益及其协方差矩阵，并在 S&P 500 数据上显示非线性载荷与更优组合表现。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了PanelGP，一个针对大规模股票收益面板数据的条件因子模型，其中公司特征决定因子载荷，经济状况决定预期因子。两个关系均采用高斯过程先验进行非参数估计。核函数的选择嵌套了工具化主成分分析（instrumented principal component analysis, IPCA）以及平滑非线性映射。资产特定的载荷新息和共同因子新息将特质风险与系统性风险分离，因此一个估计模型可以同时预测预期收益及其协方差矩阵。估计采用稀疏变分推断，对载荷和因子施加联合后验分布。由于每个预测收益在高斯状态下是双线性的，预测协方差矩阵及任意投资组合的累积量均具有闭式解。在模拟中，估计量能够恢复非线性映射和方差参数。对于1995-2024年间的标普500成分股，非线性载荷在股票排序上优于仿射载荷，并改善了高斯对数得分。相对于IPCA，由此构建的投资组合具有更高的夏普比率（扣除交易成本后亦然），且最小方差组合具有更低的波动率。预测方差能够按风险对股票和月份进行排序，但过于压缩。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop PanelGP, a conditional factor model for large panels of stock returns in which firm characteristics determine the factor loadings and economic conditions determine the expected factors. Both relations are estimated nonparametrically with Gaussian-process priors. The kernel choice nests instrumented principal component analysis (IPCA) as well as smooth nonlinear maps. Asset-specific loading innovations and common factor innovations separate idiosyncratic from systematic risk, so one estimated model forecasts both expected returns and their covariance matrix. Estimation uses sparse variational inference with a joint posterior over loadings and factors. Because each predicted return is bilinear in a Gaussian state, the predictive covariance matrix and the cumulants of any portfolio have closed forms. In simulations, the estimator recovers the nonlinear maps and the variance parameters. For S&P 500 constituents over 1995-2024, nonlinear loadings rank stocks better than affine loadings and improve the Gaussian log score. Relative to IPCA, the resulting portfolios have higher Sharpe ratios, also net of trading costs, and the minimum-variance portfolio has lower volatility. The predicted variances rank stocks and months by risk but are too compressed.
