<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一级方程式赛车的成绩反映了车手与车队（constructor）的共同贡献，但这些贡献无法直接观测，且随时间变化。我们提出一种贝叶斯状态空间模型，利用两个可观测结果——最快排位赛单圈时间（fastest qualifying lap times）和比赛排名（race rankings）——来分离动态的车手能力与车队能力。这两个结果均共同依赖于在大奖赛（Grand Prix）层面演变的潜在车手状态和车队状态，而比赛方程还额外考虑了发车位置（starting-grid position）的影响。这种分解通过将车手和车队能力中心化于零的约束条件，以及车手—车队组合随时间的变化来得以实现。贝叶斯推断采用无U型采样器（No-U-Turn sampler）进行，在弱信息先验（weakly informative priors）下对称地处理车手与车队能力。将该模型应用于2014年至2021年的一级方程式混合动力时代（hybrid era），我们发现车手和车队能力均存在显著的异质性。车手能力随时间通常更为稳定，而车队能力则表现出更大的波动，且对于许多车手—车队组合而言，车队能力对观测到的表现贡献更为显著。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Formula One outcomes reflect the joint contributions of drivers and constructors, but these contributions are unobserved and vary over time. We propose a Bayesian state-space model that disentangles dynamic driver and constructor abilities using two observed outcomes: fastest qualifying lap times and race rankings. Both outcomes depend jointly on latent driver and constructor states that evolve at the Grand Prix level, while the race equation additionally accounts for starting-grid position. The decomposition is supported by constraints that center the driver and constructor abilities at zero, together with variation in driver-constructor assignments over time. Bayesian inference is performed using the No-U-Turn sampler under weakly informative priors that treat driver and constructor abilities symmetrically. Applying the model to the Formula One hybrid era from 2014 to 2021, we find substantial heterogeneity in both driver and constructor abilities. Driver abilities are generally more stable over time, whereas constructor abilities exhibit greater variation and, for many driver--constructor combinations, contribute more strongly to observed performance.
