引言 本研究探讨了数字伦理治理与数据保护策略如何通过结构性机制影响商业银行部门中员工的信任与适应性绩效。方法 采用横截面定量设计，收集了越南402名银行员工的数据。使用偏最小二乘结构方程模型（PLS-SEM）和PLSpredict来检验假设并评估预测能力。结果 结果显示，数字伦理治理和数据保护显著增强了组织公正感。组织公正感作为关键中介变量，对员工信任产生重要影响。此外，数字化转型强度和组织文化支持直接促进员工信任，进而驱动适应性行为。讨论 研究结果表明，若无强有力的伦理治理和结构性支持，仅靠技术采纳是不够的。银行必须将伦理保障与文化及公正机制相结合，以培养信任和适应性绩效。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Introduction This study examines the structural mechanisms through which digital ethics governance and data protection strategies influence employee trust and adaptive performance in the commercial banking sector. Methods A cross-sectional quantitative design was employed, collecting data from 402 bank employees in Vietnam. Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) and PLSpredict were used to test the hypotheses and evaluate predictive capabilities. Results Results reveal that digital ethics governance and data protection significantly enhance organizational justice. Organizational justice acts as a critical mediator toward employee trust. Furthermore, digital transformation intensity and organizational culture support directly promote employee trust, which in turn drives adaptive behavior. Discussion The findings highlight that technological adoption alone is insufficient without strong ethical governance and structural support. Banks must integrate ethical safeguards with culture and justice mechanisms to foster trust and adaptive performance.
