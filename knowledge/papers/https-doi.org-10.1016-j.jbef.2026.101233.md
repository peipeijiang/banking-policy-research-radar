# Framing effects in inflation expectation surveys: Evidence from a randomized field experiment

2022年，新西兰储备银行将其家庭预期调查中关于通胀预期的问题从区间式改为开放式。这一变化的影响尚未得到探讨。为弥补这一空白，我们开展了一项实地实验，采用具有全国代表性的800名受访者样本，随机分配至两种格式之一。我们估计平均处理效应为2.90个百分点，接触开放式格式的受访者所报告的一年期通胀预期显著高于获得预设区间的受访者。为评估该效应是否超出均值层面，我们估计了第50百分位数的分位数回归。结果显示，中位数差异为1.44个百分点，具有统计显著性，表明框架效应在分布中心同样显著，并非仅由极端值驱动。这些发现证实了通胀预期对调查设计的敏感性，并对如何解读新西兰储备银行2022年后的数据具有直接启示意义。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In 2022, the Reserve Bank of New Zealand switched from range-based to open-ended questions about inflation expectations in its household expectations survey. The effects of this change remain unexplored. To address this gap, we conducted a field experiment using a nationally representative sample of 800 respondents, randomly assigned to either format. We estimate the average treatment effect to be 2.90 percentage points, with respondents exposed to the open-ended format reporting significantly higher one-year inflation expectations than those given predefined ranges. To assess whether this effect was present beyond the mean, we estimated quantile regression at the 50th percentile. The results reveal a statistically significant median difference of 1.44 percentage points, indicating that framing effects are also evident at the center of the distribution and are not solely driven by outliers. These findings confirm the sensitivity of inflation expectations to survey design and carry direct implications for how post-2022 data from the Reserve Bank of New Zealand should be interpreted.
