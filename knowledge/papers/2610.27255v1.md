本文研究了随机特征模型学习比金融收益的简单负自相关更复杂模式的能力。理论洞见强调，随机特征岭回归的学习能力由信噪比（SNR）水平与正则化之间的相互作用决定。我们通过推导一个特定阈值——SNR前沿——来刻画不同的学习机制。在实践中，我们的结果能够确定在估计过程中引入的最小惩罚水平，以防止在实施阶段SNR下降。我们将该方法实证应用于股权溢价预测，并利用输出构建均值-方差有效投资组合。由此产生的投资组合相较于基于短期反转策略的投资组合构建方案产生了可观的经济价值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper investigates the ability of random feature models to learn more complex patterns than the simple negative autocorrelation of financial returns. Theoretical insights highlight that the learning capacity of random features ridge regression is determined by the interplay between signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) level and regularization. We characterize the different learning regimes through the derivation of a particular threshold: the SNR frontier. Practically, our results permit to determine the minimum penalty level to be introduced into the estimation process in order to guard against a drop in the SNR during the implementation phase. We empirically apply this methodology to equity premium forecasting and use the output to build mean-variance efficient portfolios. The resulting portfolios yield substantial economic value over a portfolio construction scheme based on a short-term reversal strategy.
