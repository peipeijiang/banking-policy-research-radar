<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一个通过解决不可能性所需信息来评估实施不可能性的框架。域覆盖（domain covers）描述用于选择机制而不限制可容许偏好的信息。粗化覆盖会削弱所需信息。在具有现状点的准线性公共决策问题中，我们研究了占优策略实施的两类不可能性：将效率与个体理性同预算可行性或预算平衡性相结合。在基于最大社会剩余边界的区间覆盖下，我们刻画了在占优策略中可实施且满足第一组公理的社会选择规则。在基于精确剩余水平的剩余水平覆盖下，我们获得了第二组公理对应的刻画。两种覆盖都是最小的：在各自要求下，不存在严格更粗的覆盖允许实施。这些具有经济可解释性的最小覆盖加深了我们对每种不可能性的信息严重程度的理解。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a framework for assessing implementation impossibilities through the information required to resolve them. Domain covers describe information used to select a mechanism without restricting admissible preferences. Coarsening a cover weakens the required information. In quasi-linear public decision problems with a status quo, we study two impossibilities of dominant-strategy implementation: combining efficiency and individual rationality with either budget feasibility or budget balancedness. Under an interval cover based on bounds on maximal social surplus, we characterize the social choice rules implementable in dominant strategies and satisfying the first set of axioms. Under a surplus-level cover based on exact surplus levels, we obtain the corresponding characterization for the second set of axioms. Both covers are minimal: no strictly coarser cover permits implementation under the respective requirements. These economically interpretable minimal covers deepen our understanding of the informational severity of each impossibility.

</details>
