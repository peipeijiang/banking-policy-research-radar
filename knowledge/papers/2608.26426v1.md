<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文为意大利所有城市构建了市政公平与可持续福祉指数（MESWI），通过49项指标将涵盖12个领域的BES框架扩展至地方层面。研究结果显示，区域及省级统计可能掩盖显著的领土异质性。南北分化仍是主导性地理格局，其强度远大于内部地区与非内部地区之间的差异。与此同时，同一区域和领土类别内的城市在福祉特征上表现出显著差异。偏远地区与社会服务及社会经济机会的薄弱相关，但环境绩效却更为突出。MESWI提供了多维福祉的精细化图景，并为基于地方的政策制定、监测与评估提供了潜在的信息基础。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops the Municipal Equitable and Sustainable Well-being Index (MESWI) for all Italian municipalities, extending the 12-domain BES framework to the local level through 49 indicators. Results reveal substantial territorial heterogeneity that regional and provincial statistics may conceal. The North-South divide remains the dominant geographical pattern and is considerably stronger than the difference between inner and non-inner areas. At the same time, municipalities within the same regions and territorial categories display markedly different well-being profiles. Remoteness is associated with weaker services and socio-economic opportunities but also with stronger environmental performance. The MESWI provides a fine-grained picture of multidimensional well-being and a potential information base for the programming, monitoring and evaluation of place-based policies.
