<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

分布双重差分研究通常假定处理组与对照组之间的政策后差距是政策前差距的混合。这一历史迁移假设可能以单一统计量检验所遗漏的方式失效。当政策事件被合并时，平均反事实可以是一个有效的分布，尽管没有任何单个事件的反事实是有效的，且合并边界可能排除真实平均效应。过去差距的混合可能对结果赋予负概率，且逐截断点边界可能不相容。我们刻画了锐边界并给出了崩溃半径、一个无网格置信区域，以及有实验和无实验情况下的检验。这些检验在全国支持工作数据中拒绝了迁移假设，并揭示了合并最低工资边界背后不可能的状态反事实。对于1993年劳动所得税抵免扩展，这些检验通过至1995年，此时边界将单身母亲相对于无子女女性的就业增益定在5至7个百分点；在剔除早期福利豁免州后，该增益仍为正。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Distributional difference-in-differences studies often assume that the post-policy gap between treated and comparison groups is a mixture of pre-policy gaps. This historical-transport assumption can fail in ways that one-statistic checks miss. When policy events are pooled, the average counterfactual can be a valid distribution although no event's counterfactual is, and pooled bounds can exclude the true average effect. Mixtures of past gaps can assign negative probability to outcomes, and cutoff-by-cutoff bounds can be incompatible. We characterize sharp bounds and give breakdown radii, a grid-free confidence region, and tests with and without an experiment. The tests reject transport in the National Supported Work data and expose impossible state counterfactuals behind a pooled minimum-wage bound. For the 1993 Earned Income Tax Credit expansion they pass through 1995, when bounds put single mothers' employment gain relative to childless women at 5 to 7 points; the gain stays positive after dropping early welfare-waiver states.
