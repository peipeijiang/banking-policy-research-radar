<details open>
<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们探讨了单调性原则在音乐流媒体平台订阅收入在内容提供者之间分配问题中的含义。我们将这一原则的各种形式形式化为流行度指数的若干公理，这些公理在该情境下生成分配规则。我们证明，当这些公理与其他一些基本公理（如可加性、对称性或两两齐次性）相结合时，它们为该问题中的焦点指数以及包含这些指数的更一般族提供了公理化刻画。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We explore the implications of the principle of monotonicity in the problem of sharing the revenues raised from subscriptions to music streaming platforms among content providers. We formalize various forms of this principle as several axioms for popularity indices, which generate sharing rules in this setting. We show that, combined with some other basic axioms (such as additivity, symmetry, or pairwise homogeneity), they provide axiomatic characterizations of focal indices for this problem, as well as more general families comprising them.

</details>
