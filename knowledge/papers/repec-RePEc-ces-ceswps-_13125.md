# Heterogeneous CEO Death Effects

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13125.html)

## 一句话结论

> 利用CEO死亡事件作为外生冲击，通过事件研究考察股价反应及其与CEO和企业特征的关系，并检验市场反应能否预测后续经营绩效变化，发现CEO重要但许多CEO损害股东价值。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文分析了CEO死亡引起的公司价值、绩效和行为的变化。许多CEO的死亡引发了股价的大幅变动——股东认为某些CEO为股东增加了价值，而另一些CEO则被认为未能实现最优匹配或薪酬过高。这些价值变化与CEO和公司特征相关（例如，年长CEO的死亡往往增加价值），但大部分变异仍无法解释。市场反应预测了经营绩效的变化，表明股东知道哪些CEO能提升绩效、哪些不能。证据表明CEO很重要，但同时也表明许多CEO降低了股东价值。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper analyzes changes in firm value, performance, and behavior caused by CEO deaths. Many deaths trigger large stock price changes—shareholders believe that some CEOs add shareholder value, but others are seen as not optimally matched or overpaid. These value changes are correlated with CEO and firm characteristics (e.g., deaths of old CEOs tend to add value), but much of the variation remains unexplained. The market reactions predict changes in operating performance, indicating that shareholders know which CEOs improve performance and which do not. The evidence suggests that CEOs are important, but also that many reduce shareholder value.

</details>
