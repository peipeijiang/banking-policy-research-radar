<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们通过引入关于在位者私人类型的外生公共信号，重新审视经典的连锁店悖论（chain-store paradox），这些信号在有限期限内每期到达。我们以随时间递增的声誉临界值序列来完整刻画完美贝叶斯均衡（perfect Bayesian equilibria）：进入者在低于其临界值时进入，而正常在位者在低于其临界值时混合策略、在高于其临界值时进行斗争。均衡结果在一般意义上是唯一的。信息的到达抑制了建立声誉的激励，因此即使期限任意延长，完全威慑也未必出现，即使在位者非常有耐心。我们精确刻画了极限情形下何时存在完全威慑，以及不存在完全威慑时的极限均衡。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We revisit the classic chain-store paradox by introducing exogenous public signals about the incumbent's private type that arrive each period over a finite horizon. We fully characterize perfect Bayesian equilibria in terms of sequences of reputation cutoffs that increase over time: entrants enter below their cutoff, while the normal incumbent mixes below its cutoff and fights above it. The equilibrium outcome is generically unique. Information arrival dampens the incentive to build a reputation, so full deterrence need not emerge as the horizon grows arbitrarily long, even when the incumbent is very patient. We characterize exactly when there is full deterrence in the limit, along with the limiting equilibrium when there is not.

</details>
