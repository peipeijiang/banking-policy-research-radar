<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当少数聚类主导观测加权估计方程时，聚类规模异质性可能使传统的聚类稳健推断失效。本文针对独立聚类、任意实现的聚类规模以及无限制的簇内相依性情形，建立了聚类平衡\(Z\)-估计量的渐近理论。基于Chiang, Sasaki & Wang (2025)的规模调整重加权方法，本文的分析区分了目标定义、识别与推断三个层面。一个共同的聚类水平零点——或作为识别条件施加，或由个体水平外生性或基于设计的机制导出——使结构目标对确定性聚类重加权保持不变；重加权后总体矩的分离则确保了识别性。逐点结果在允许协方差序列沿聚类阵列变化的同时建立了渐近正态性。关于泛函参数的结果建立了弱收敛性以及用于一致推断的聚类乘子自助法（cluster multiplier bootstrap）的有效性。该框架涵盖光滑与非光滑得分函数，并以分布回归、分位数回归和期望分位数回归作为具体应用。蒙特卡洛实验展示了异质聚类规模下的逐点推断与一致推断，补充的实证应用则说明了聚类加权在市政学校数据中的后果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Cluster-size heterogeneity can invalidate conventional cluster-robust inference when a small number of clusters dominate observation-weighted estimating equations. This paper develops asymptotic theory for cluster-balanced \(Z\)-estimators under independent clusters, arbitrary realised cluster sizes, and unrestricted within-cluster dependence. Building on the size-adjusted reweighting of Chiang, Sasaki & Wang (2025), the analysis distinguishes target definition, identification, and inference. A common cluster-level zero, imposed as an identifying condition or derived from individual-level exogeneity or design-based mechanisms, makes the structural target invariant to deterministic cluster reweighting; separation of the reweighted population moments then ensures identification. The pointwise results establish asymptotic normality while allowing the covariance sequence to vary along the cluster array. The results for functional parameters establish weak convergence and validity of a cluster multiplier bootstrap for uniform inference. The framework covers smooth and non-smooth scores with distribution, quantile, and expectile regressions as concrete applications. Monte Carlo experiments illustrate pointwise and uniform inference under heterogeneous cluster sizes, and a supplementary empirical application illustrates the consequences of cluster weighting in municipal-school data.
