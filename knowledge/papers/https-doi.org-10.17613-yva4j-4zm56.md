纠错冻结问题（Correction Freeze Problem, CFP）由Quan-Hoang Vuong教授提出并形式化，用以解释动态系统如何丧失纠正感知状态与实际状态之间差异的能力。本研究对CFP的第四条路径——信息限制——进行了两项独立的严苛检验，分别涉及通胀预期和气候变化信念，随后探讨了气候变化怀疑态度的潜在滞后现象，并提出一个新的跨学科研究领域。通胀检验整合了美国月度通胀预期与官方价格数据（2000—2026年）。在汽油价格大幅冲击期间，在预期变化非零的月份中，有94%的月份预期与汽油价格同向变动，而其他月份这一比例为62%。相反，与渐进性通胀变化的方向一致性仅为51.2%，接近随机水平。气候检验将“美国人心中的气候变化”调查（2008—2025年）中的37,417名受访者与NOAA区域气温数据相关联。长期区域变暖与“全球变暖正在发生”的信念呈负相关但在统计上不显著（，），而近期温度距平则显示出较小的、统计上不确定的正相关（，）。两项检验均未触发预设的证伪标准，表明第四条路径通过了独立的严苛检验。重要的是，长期变暖与气候变化信念之间的负相关关系提出了滞后动态的可能性，并可能被政治和制度反馈所强化。基于这些发现，我们提出“纠错冻结研究”（Correction Freeze Studies）作为一个跨学科研究领域，将CFP定位为关于动态系统如何丧失纠错能力的一般性理论。该拟议领域旨在识别纠错冻结机制、考察滞后现象与隐性俘获，并研究恢复纠错能力的条件，以哲学探究和严谨的实证检验为支撑。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The Correction Freeze Problem (CFP), proposed and formalized by professor Quan-Hoang Vuong, explains how dynamic systems lose their capability to correct discrepancies between perceived and actual states. This study subjects CFP’s fourth route—the information limit—to two independent severe tests involving inflation expectations and climate-change beliefs, before exploring the potential hysteresis of climate-change disbelief and proposing a new interdisciplinary research field. The inflation test integrates U.S. monthly inflation expectations and official price data (2000–2026). During substantial gasoline-price shocks, expectations moved in the same direction as gasoline prices in 94% of months with nonzero expectation changes, compared with 62% in other months. Conversely, directional agreement with gradual inflation changes was only 51.2%, close to chance. The climate test links 37,417 respondents from the Climate Change in the American Mind survey (2008–2025) to NOAA regional temperatures. Long-term regional warming exhibited a negative but statistically insignificant association with belief that global warming is happening (, ), while recent temperature departures showed a small, statistically uncertain positive association (, ). Neither test triggered the predefined falsification criteria, suggesting that Route 4 survived independent severe tests. Importantly, the negative association between long-term warming and climate-change belief raises the possibility of hysteretic dynamics, potentially reinforced by political and institutional feedback. Building on these findings, we propose Correction Freeze Studies as an interdisciplinary research field, positioning CFP as a general theory of how dynamic systems lose their corrective capabilities. The proposed field aims to identify correction-freeze mechanisms, examine hysteresis and hidden capture, and investigate conditions for restoring corrective capabilities, supported by philosophical inquiry and rigorous empirical testing.
