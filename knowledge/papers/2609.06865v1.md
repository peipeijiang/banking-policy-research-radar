# School Choice with Appeals

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06865v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究学校选择中的申诉机制如何影响家长偏好报告和配置效率，通过理论模型和预注册实验发现申诉能提高IA机制的防操纵性并扩大其效率优势。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

每年，英格兰大约有五万个家庭通过一个集中化且研究不足的程序对学校分配结果提出申诉，该程序改善了约五分之一的申诉者的安置情况。本文表明，申诉并非制度上的事后补充：它们会改变家长向教育主管部门报告偏好的方式，以及该程序所产生的安置质量。一旦将申诉阶段纳入考量，即时接受机制（IA）的可操纵性降低，并且能够支持帕累托占优的均衡结果，这些结果优于真实报告下的延迟接受机制（DA）的结果，从而逆转了DA与IA之间的福利比较。在一项预注册实验中，我们发现了与理论预测一致的结果：申诉提高了IA机制下的真实报告率，而对DA机制几乎没有影响，并扩大了IA与DA之间的效率差距。约60%的IA效率提升出现在任何申诉被支持之前，因为预期到潜在申诉可能性的受试者会以不同方式对学校进行排序。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Roughly fifty thousand families in England appeal their school assignments each year through a centralized and understudied procedure that improves the placement of about one in five claimants. This paper shows that appeals are not an institutional afterthought: they change how parents report preferences to the education authority and the quality of placements it generates. Once the appeal stage is incorporated, Immediate Acceptance (IA) becomes less manipulable and can support equilibrium outcomes that Pareto dominate those of Deferred Acceptance (DA) under truth-telling, reversing the DA-IA welfare comparison. In a preregistered experiment, we find results consistent with the theoretical predictions: appeals increase truth-telling in IA, leaving DA essentially unchanged, and widen the IA-DA efficiency gap. Around 60% of the IA efficiency gain appears before any appeal is upheld, because subjects anticipating the possibility of a potential appeal rank schools differently.
