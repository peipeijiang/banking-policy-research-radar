<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

合成控制法（Synthetic Control, SC）将处理单元的处理前轨迹与供体单元的加权组合进行匹配。我们研究了谱合成控制法（Spectral SC），该方法在由供体面板的主要时间奇异向量定义的坐标中对处理单元进行匹配，并提出了一种混合估计量，该估计量对保留方向和丢弃方向分别赋予可调节的权重，并将原始路径SC和截断谱SC作为端点。我们证明，该族估计量在满秩时精确退化为原始路径SC；当供体数量 \(N_0 > K+1\) 时，在 \(K\) 个保留维度上实现精确平衡是欠定的，其仿射解集的维度为 \(N_0 - K - 1\)；并且谱不平衡通过有限样本最佳线性预测分解映射为处理效应偏差。我们使用每个数据生成机制下400次重复实验以及仅基于供体的安慰剂验证来选择正则化参数和混合权重，在11种数据生成机制下评估了这些估计量。截断谱SC在所有机制下的均方根误差（RMSE）均显著高于经过调优的原始路径SC，配对差异相当于4到11个蒙特卡洛标准误。混合估计量在大多数重复实验中选择原始路径匹配，并且在大多数机制下与调优后的SC在统计上无显著差异。该结果对预处理高度敏感：使用原始输入时，性能差距较大；而根据我们理论边界背后的假设，在谱分解前去除单位和时间固定效应后，这一差距几乎消失，且安慰剂验证开始倾向于截断。我们将这些发现视为诊断性结论，而非认为谱SC应取代原始路径SC。基估计噪声、平衡欠定性以及固定效应污染共同决定了谱匹配何时能够发挥作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Synthetic control (SC) matches a treated unit's pre-treatment trajectory to a weighted combination of donor units. We study Spectral SC, which instead matches the treated unit in coordinates defined by the leading temporal singular vectors of the donor panel, and a hybrid estimator that places separately tunable weight on retained and discarded directions, nesting raw-path SC and truncated Spectral SC as endpoints. We prove that the family reduces exactly to raw-path SC at full rank, that exact balance on $K$ retained dimensions with $N_0$ donors is underdetermined whenever $N_0>K+1$, with an affine solution set of dimension $N_0-K-1$, and that spectral imbalance maps to treatment-effect bias through a finite-sample best-linear-predictor decomposition. We evaluate the estimators across eleven data-generating regimes, using $400$ replications per regime and donor-only placebo validation to select regularization and the mixing weight. Truncated Spectral SC has significantly higher RMSE than tuned raw-path SC in every regime, with paired differences equal to $4$ to $11$ Monte Carlo standard errors. The hybrid estimator selects raw-path matching in most replications and is statistically indistinguishable from tuned SC in most regimes. The result is highly sensitive to preprocessing. With raw inputs, the performance gap is large; after removing unit and time fixed effects before spectral decomposition, as suggested by the assumptions behind our bound, the gap nearly disappears and placebo validation begins to favor truncation. We interpret these findings diagnostically rather than as evidence that Spectral SC should replace raw-path SC. Basis-estimation noise, balancing underdetermination, and fixed-effects contamination determine when spectral matching can help.
