# Who Leads and Who Collects:Algorithmic Collusion in Markets of Heterogeneous Language Models

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11256v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过让四个不同供应商的最廉价大语言模型在无沟通的四企业对数伯特兰市场中重复定价，检验异质性算法代理之间的算法合谋程度、混合市场效应和租金分配，发现合谋是模型属性，混合不一定降低合谋，且价格领导者获得最少租金。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

定价算法合谋的证据来自每个卖家都运行相同算法的市场。我们追问：当卖家运行不同算法时会发生什么？来自四家提供商的四个语言模型，各自处于其最便宜的层级，在无沟通的四企业logit Bertrand市场中进行定价，涵盖所有同质、两两组合和完全混合的构成（11个单元格，20次运行，200个周期）。合谋是模型的一种属性：Claude和Gemini市场达到垄断租金的72%至79%，DeepSeek市场为24%，GPT市场则没有，尽管GPT的价格漂移至垄断水平之上而非趋向竞争。混合本身并不减少合谋。包含Gemini的市场——Gemini以最高价格开局并最终稳定在最高水平——比构成它们的同质市场更具合谋性；包含Claude的市场——Claude以较低价格开局并跟随竞争对手降价——则合谋性较低；只有完全混合的市场显著低于同质市场的平均合谋水平。稳定性取决于最不稳定的参与者：两个GPT企业就足以阻止任何市场收敛。在混合市场内部，租金按传递性顺序分配，即DeepSeek高于Claude，Claude高于Gemini，Gemini高于GPT，这一顺序与锚定排名相反。提价最多的模型收取的租金最少，正如价格领导模型所预测的那样。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Evidence that pricing algorithms collude comes from markets in which every seller runs the same algorithm. We ask what happens when they do not. Four language models from four providers, each at its cheapest tier, price in a four-firm logit Bertrand market without communication, in every homogeneous, two-by-two and fully mixed composi- tion (11 cells, 20 runs, 200 periods). Collusion is a property of the model: Claude and Gemini markets reach 72 to 79 percent of the monopoly rent, DeepSeek markets 24 percent, and GPT markets none, though GPT prices drift above the monopoly level rather than toward competition. Mixing does not reduce collusion by itself. Markets containing Gemini, which opens at the highest price and settles highest, are more col- lusive than the homogeneous markets they are built from; markets containing Claude, which opens lower and follows its rivals down, are less so; and only the fully mixed market is significantly less collusive than the average homogeneous one. Stability de- pends on the least stable participant: two GPT firms suffice to keep any market from converging. Inside mixed markets the rent is shared in a transitive order, DeepSeek over Claude over Gemini over GPT, that inverts the anchor ranking. The model that raises the price collects the least of the rent, as the price-leadership model predicts.

</details>
