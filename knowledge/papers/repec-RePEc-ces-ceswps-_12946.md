<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文探讨了如何在经历结构性转型的经济体中衡量与福利相关的增长，在此类经济体中，相对价格和支出模式的持续变化可能导致标准产出衡量指标出现加总偏差。我们在一个包含非齐次偏好、部门特定生产率趋势以及投资特定技术变革的连续时间动态一般均衡模型中处理这一问题。我们比较了两种基于福利的实际收入增长衡量指标：一种是以当前为基期的等价变差指标，另一种是链式费雪-希尔指数。链式费雪-希尔指数与链式迪维西亚指数一致，为国民账户核算方法提供了坚实的理论基础，并且仅需主要支出组成部分的增长率及其名义支出份额即可作为福利导向产出指标的充分统计量。相比之下，以当前为基期的指标对信息的要求更高，需要系统性修正，并引入依赖于基期的偏差，从而扭曲对过去增长的评估。我们的定量分析表明，这些偏差显著且随时间推移而增大，并与结构性转型文献中的关键典型化事实相冲突。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines how to measure welfare-relevant growth in economies undergoing structural transformation, where persistent changes in relative prices and expenditure patterns can lead to aggregation biases in standard output measures. We address this issue within a continuous-time dynamic general equilibrium model featuring non-homothetic preferences, sector-specific productivity trends, and investment-specific technical change. We compare two welfare-based measures of real income growth: a current-base equivalent variation measure and a chained Fisher-Shell index. The chained Fisher-Shell index coincides with the chained Divisia index, providing strong theoretical foundations for national accounting methodology and requiring the growth rates and nominal expenditure shares of major expenditure components as sufficient statistics for a welfare-based output index. In contrast, the current-base index is more informationally demanding, requires systematic revisions, and introduces base-dependent biases that distort the evaluation of past growth. Our quantitative analysis reveals that these biases are substantial, increase over time, and conflict with key stylized facts in the structural transformation literature.
