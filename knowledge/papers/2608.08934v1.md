# Political Power-Sharing, Firm Entry, and Economic Growth: Evidence from Multiple Elected Representatives

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察了政治权力共享对地方经济活动的影响。这一影响取决于不受制约的权力所伴随的风险与制衡机制可能带来的效率增益或损失之间的相对重要性。我们的研究设计利用了一种地理不连续性设计，该设计基于选举边界与行政边界偶然重叠所产生的准随机变异，即相邻地区由不同数量的政治家治理。我们进一步利用一次选举划界事件来补充这一设计，从而能够利用区域内政治家数量的变异。我们发现，增加治理某一地区的政治家数量可以促进新企业创建、降低失业率并提升实际经济活动水平。我们的结果表明，非结盟的多位政治家通过相互施加制衡机制提升了国家效率，从而降低了监管障碍，减少了裙带关系，并改善了公共基础设施的供给，为企业创建营造了有利的经济环境。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We examine the effect of political power-sharing on local economic activity. This effect depends on the relative importance of the risks associated with unchecked power and the potential efficiency gains or losses arising from checks and balances. Our research design exploits a geographic discontinuity design due to the haphazard overlap of electoral and administrative boundaries that generates quasi-random variation in the number of politicians governing adjacent regions. We supplement this design using an episode of electoral delimitation that allows us to exploit within-region variation in the number of politicians. We find increasing the number of politicians governing an area can lead to new firm creation, lower unemployment, and greater real economic activity. Our results suggest that non-aligned multiple politicians enhance state efficiency by imposing checks and balances on each other, leading to lower regulatory obstacles, less cronyism, and improved provision of public infrastructure, creating an economically favorable environment for firm creation.
