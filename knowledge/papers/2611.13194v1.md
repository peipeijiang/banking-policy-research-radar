<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多决策依赖于专家评审团，而其成员与某些候选人存在关联，偏袒行为很难与共同品味或更充分的信息区分开来。利用来自花样滑冰的丰富数据集，我们发现了战略性同胞溢价（compatriot premium）以及对同胞近邻竞争对手的有针对性惩罚的证据。该溢价沿随机抽取的出场顺序而变化，并且在2022/23赛季之前，当近邻竞争对手有自己的评委在评审团中时，该溢价会上升。该惩罚随同胞获得名次的机会而增大，一旦她退出竞争便逐渐消失。反事实分析表明，这些偏差可以影响势均力敌的竞争。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many decisions rest on expert panels whose members have ties to some candidates, and favoritism is hard to tell from shared taste or better information. Using a rich dataset from figure skating, we find evidence of a strategic compatriot premium and a targeted penalty on the compatriot's close rivals. The premium falls along a randomly drawn start order and, before 2022/23, rises when close rivals have their own judges on the panel. The penalty grows with the compatriot's placement chances and fades once she is out of contention. A counterfactual analysis shows that these biases can shape close competitions.
