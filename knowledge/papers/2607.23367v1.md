<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究严格正的边际价值能否恢复不可分割物品中“至多一个物品的嫉妒自由”（EF1）与“帕累托最优”（PO）的兼容性。对于两个智能体，我们确定了物品数量的确切阈值。每个包含至多七个物品且具有严格递增估值的实例，在不需任何子模性假设的情况下，均存在同时满足EF1和PO的分配。相反，我们构造了一个包含八个物品的实例，其估值具有归一化、整数值、严格递增且子模的性质，在该实例中每个EF1分配都严格被帕累托支配。因此，八个物品是构造两个智能体反例的充分必要条件。最后，我们强化了Chandramouleeswaran和Nimbhorkar（2026）关于三个智能体的NP困难性结果：即使当零边际值仅限于涉及单个智能体的八个固定智能体-物品对时，对于归一化、整数值、单调子模估值，判断是否存在EF1和PO分配仍然是NP困难的。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study whether strictly positive marginal values restore the compatibility of envy-freeness up to one good (EF1) and Pareto optimality (PO) for indivisible goods. For two agents, we identify the exact threshold in the number of goods. Every instance with at most seven goods and strictly increasing valuations admits an allocation that is both EF1 and PO, without any submodularity assumption. In contrast, we construct an eight-good instance with normalized, integer-valued, strictly increasing, submodular valuations in which every EF1 allocation is strictly Pareto dominated. Thus, eight goods are necessary and sufficient for a two-agent counterexample. Finally, we strengthen the three-agent NP-hardness result of Chandramouleeswaran and Nimbhorkar (2026): deciding whether an EF1 and PO allocation exists remains NP-hard for normalized, integer-valued, monotone submodular valuations even when zero marginals are confined to eight fixed agent-good pairs, all involving a single agent.

</details>
