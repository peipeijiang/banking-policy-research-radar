<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

货币政策在影响金融市场流动性、利率、信贷可得性及投资者情绪方面发挥着重要作用。在印度储备银行（RBI）所采用的多种货币政策工具中，回购利率（Repo Rate）是调节货币供应量和维持宏观经济稳定的最重要工具之一。回购利率的变动直接影响商业银行的借贷成本，并间接影响股票市场表现，尤其是在银行等利率敏感型行业中尤为显著。本研究考察了回购利率公告对Bank Nifty（代表印度银行业的主要行业指数之一）收益率波动性及其收益的影响。研究分析了2018年至2020年期间，在3天、6天和9天多个事件窗口下，Bank Nifty收益率的公告前后波动模式。本研究采用描述性研究设计，使用从印度储备银行和印度国家证券交易所官方来源收集的二手数据。研究采用均值收益率分析、方差分析和F检验等统计工具，以评估收益率及波动性变化的显著性。研究结果显示，回购利率公告在3天和6天的短期窗口内并未对Bank Nifty收益率产生即时且统计上显著的影响。然而，在部分9天公告后窗口中观察到了适度的长期影响证据，表明市场对货币政策变化的反应存在滞后性。尽管回购利率的下调通常会产生积极的市场预期并改善投资者情绪，但诸如COVID-19疫情等外部宏观经济冲击显著改变了市场行为，并削弱了货币政策向股票收益传导的有效性。研究得出结论：仅凭回购利率公告不足以解释Bank Nifty的短期波动性，投资者应避免仅依据货币政策公告做出即时交易决策。相反，投资决策应考虑更广泛的宏观经济指标和更长的观察期。本研究丰富了货币政策传导的相关文献，并为投资者、政策制定者和金融分析师理解印度银行业指数的行为提供了实践启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Monetary policy plays a significant role in influencing liquidity, interest rates, credit availability and investor sentiment within financial markets. Among the various monetary policy instruments adopted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Repo Rate serves as one of the most important tools for regulating money supply and maintaining macroeconomic stability. Changes in Repo Rate directly influence the borrowing cost of commercial banks and indirectly affect stock market performance, particularly in interest-sensitive sectors such as banking. The present study examines the impact of Repo Rate announcements on the volatility and returns of Bank Nifty, one of the major sectoral indices representing the Indian banking sector. The study investigates the pre- and post-announcement volatility patterns of Bank Nifty returns across multiple event windows of 3 days, 6 days, and 9 days during the period from 2018 to 2020. The study adopts a descriptive research design and uses secondary data collected from the official sources of the Reserve Bank of India and National Stock Exchange. Statistical tools including Mean Return Analysis, Variance Analysis, and F-Static Test were employed to evaluate the significance of changes in returns and volatility. The findings reveal that Repo Rate announcements do not create immediate statistically significant changes in Bank Nifty returns in short-term windows of 3 and 6 days. However, evidence of moderate long-run influence was observed in selected 9-day post-announcement windows, indicating delayed market reaction to monetary policy changes. Although reductions in Repo Rate generally created positive market expectations and improved investor sentiment, external macroeconomic shocks such as the COVID-19 pandemic significantly altered market behavior and reduced the effectiveness of monetary transmission to stock returns. The study concludes that Repo Rate announcements alone are insufficient to explain short-term Bank Nifty volatility and that investors should avoid making immediate trading decisions solely based on monetary policy announcements. Instead, investment decisions should consider broader macroeconomic indicators and longer observation periods. The study contributes to the growing literature on monetary policy transmission and provides practical implications for investors, policymakers, and financial analysts in understanding the behavior of banking sector indices in India.
