# Extremal Perfect Monotone Equilibria in Bayesian Games with Strategic Complementarities

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当参与者可能犯错时，贝叶斯博弈中均衡行为的尖锐界限。在策略互补性下，行为完美均衡的集合具有最大元和最小元，且均为纯策略。我们追问这些界限何时是单调的，以及它们如何依赖于犯错的方式。对于单调超模博弈，我们考虑递增扰动下均衡的极限，其中条件颤抖分布（tremble distributions）具有满支撑并随类型随机递增。我们证明最大和最小完美均衡存在，且为纯策略且单调。在一般完美性概念下，当行动集有限时，极端完美均衡仍然是单调的。当行动集无限时，极端完美均衡可能非单调，且完美单调均衡的集合甚至可能缺乏极值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study sharp bounds on equilibrium behavior in Bayesian games when players may make mistakes. Under strategic complementarities, the set of behavioral perfect equilibria has a largest and a smallest element, both in pure strategies. We ask when these bounds are monotone and how they depend on the way mistakes are made. For monotone supermodular games, we consider limits of equilibria under increasing perturbations, in the sense that conditional tremble distributions have full support and increase stochastically with type. We show that the largest and smallest perfect equilibria exist, which are pure and monotone. Under the general perfection notion, the extremal perfect equilibria continue to be monotone in the setting with finite actions. With infinite actions, the extremal perfect equilibria can be nonmonotone, and the set of perfect monotone equilibria can even lack extrema.
