# Assessing Financial Preparedness During Old Age in Japan

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ris/adbewp/023941.html)

## 一句话结论

> 利用日本大规模综合生活状况调查数据，评估临近法定退休年龄人群的退休后收入（含公共养老金）是否足以维持退休前消费，发现约五分之四准备充足、五分之一不足需继续工作，并揭示性别、婚姻状况等异质性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究通过考察公共养老金（public pension）给付收入及其他来源收入，评估日本老年人的财务准备情况。研究检验这些退休后收入是否足以在预期退休生涯中维持退休前消费水平。我们使用来自国民生活基础调查（Comprehensive Survey of Living Conditions）的大规模数据。研究发现，接近法定退休年龄的人群中，五分之四拥有充足的收入资源，而其余五分之一则收入不足，需要继续工作。研究结果还凸显了财务准备状况的多样性，反映了性别、婚姻状况及其他个体特征的差异——这一发现对公共养老金及其他政策的目标设定具有相关性。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study evaluates old-age financial preparedness in Japan by looking at incomes from public pension benefits as well as other sources. It examines whether these post-retirement incomes are adequate to maintain pre-retirement consumption throughout the expected retirement life. We use large-scale data obtained from the Comprehensive Survey of Living Conditions. We find that four-fifths of individuals close to the statutory retirement age have adequate income resources, while the remaining one-fifth fall short and are required to continue working. Our results also highlight diversity in financial preparedness, reflecting differences in sex, marital status, and other individual attributes—a finding relevant to the targets of public pension and other policies.

</details>
