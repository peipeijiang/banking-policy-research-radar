<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Blotto上校博弈（Colonel Blotto）是一种基本的资源分配博弈，其中两名玩家将各自的预算分配到各个战场上，在每个战场上分配额较高的一方赢得该战场的价值。对于具有任意预算且各战场价值可能不同但对双方玩家相同的经典连续版本，高效计算均衡的问题一直悬而未决。我们给出了一个完全多项式时间近似方案，该方案在输入长度和$1/\varepsilon$的多项式时间内计算出一个$\varepsilon$-Nash均衡，并且对于固定的$\varepsilon$，其时间复杂度关于输入长度接近线性。对于足够均衡的预算和战场价值，我们还能够在线性时间内计算出精确均衡。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Colonel Blotto is a fundamental resource-allocation game in which two players divide their budgets across battlefields, and the higher allocation on each battlefield wins its value. The problem of computing an equilibrium efficiently has remained unresolved for its classical continuous version with arbitrary budgets and with values that may vary across battlefields but are the same for both players. We give a fully polynomial-time approximation scheme that computes an $\varepsilon$-Nash equilibrium in time polynomial in the input length and $1/\varepsilon$, and nearly linear in the input length for fixed $\varepsilon$. For sufficiently balanced budgets and battlefield values, we also compute exact equilibria in linear time.
