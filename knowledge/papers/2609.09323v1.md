先买后付（Buy Now, Pay Later, BNPL）在短短几年内已从一种新奇产品发展成为主流消费金融工具。2020年之前，BNPL只是在线时尚商家结账时的一种小众选择。此后，BNPL经历了大幅增长（Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, 2022; Salem & Udis, 2025），这与2020年新冠疫情爆发以来在线购物的增加相吻合。如今，BNPL已成为一种普遍存在的支付选项。到2026年，你可以使用BNPL来延迟和分期支付从披萨到房租乃至介于两者之间的许多商品。随着市场的发展，学术文献也在不断增加。目前已有足够多的关于BNPL的研究，使我们能够对已有发现进行梳理和回顾。在本文中，我们总结了来自经济学、金融学和市场营销学学术文献的见解。尽管在过去几年中我们对BNPL有了大量了解，我们也讨论了许多尚待解决的开放性问题以及当前影响政策决策的关切。我们首先解释BNPL产品是什么以及BNPL贷款机构的商业模式。然后，我们总结了一些消费者使用这些产品背后的经济学和心理学原理。接着，我们描述了使用BNPL的客户群体。我们展示了BNPL对消费者的影响，这是一个具有重大政策意义的研究领域。另一个政策关注的领域是BNPL贷款是否应被纳入消费者的信用报告。我们解释了为什么BNPL通常不出现在信用报告中及其影响。最后，我们提出了一些总结性思考。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) has moved from a novelty product to mainstream consumer finance in the space of a few years. Before 2020, BNPL was a niche offering at the checkouts of online fashion merchants. BNPL then experienced substantial growth (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, 2022; Salem & Udis, 2025), coinciding with increased online shopping since the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020. BNPL is now a pervasive payment option. In 2026, you can use BNPL to delay and split up payments for anything ranging from a pizza to your rent, and many items in between. As the market has grown, so has the academic literature. There is now sufficient research into BNPL to take stock and review what we have learned. In this article, we summarize insights from the economics, finance, and marketing academic literature. While we have learned a great deal about BNPL in the last few years, we also discuss many remaining open questions and current concerns impacting policy decisions. We start by explaining what BNPL products are and the business models of BNPL lenders. Then, we summarize the economics and psychology behind why some consumers use these products. Next, we describe the customer base that uses BNPL. We show the effects of BNPL on consumers, an area of research with substantial policy interest. Another area of policy interest is whether BNPL loans should be reported in a consumer's credit report. We explain why BNPL is typically not on credit reports and its implications. Finally, we offer some concluding thoughts.
