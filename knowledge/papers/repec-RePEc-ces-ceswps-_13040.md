# Access to Jobs and Individual Labor Market Outcomes

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了城市内部就业可达性对就业的影响。为克服居住区位选择的内生性，我们利用德国五个城市保障性住房申请者在不同住房单元之间的准随机分配，并提出了一种新的工具变量策略。我们的就业可达性指标综合了地理空间和技能空间中与就业岗位的接近程度，同时考虑了来自其他劳动者的潜在竞争。我们编制了一套独特的行政数据集，涵盖迁入保障性住房个体的完整劳动力市场履历。就业可达性每提高一个标准差，低技能劳动者的失业风险降低3.4个百分点。我们未在高技能劳动者中发现类似效应，他们更可能拥有汽车，因而受地理位置的约束较小。模拟结果显示，完全隔离——即将所有低技能劳动者集中分配至中心社区——将使样本中最大城市慕尼黑的全市失业率降低1.3个百分点。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We investigate the employment effects of job access within cities. To overcome the endogeneity of residential location choices, we exploit the quasi-random allocation of applicants across affordable housing units in five German cities and propose a novel IV strategy. Our job access measure combines proximity to jobs in both geographical and skill space, taking into account potential competition from other workers. We compile a unique administrative dataset comprising the complete labor market biographies of individuals who moved into affordable housing. A one-standard-deviation increase in job access reduces the unemployment hazard of unskilled workers by 3.4 percentage points. We do not find similar effects for skilled workers, who are more likely to own a car and are therefore less geographically constrained. Simulations show that full segregation, with all unskilled allocated to central neighborhoods, would reduce the city-wide unemployment rate of the largest city in our sample, Munich, by 1.3 percentage points.
