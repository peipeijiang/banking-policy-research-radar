# Does fare communication format matter? Evidence from a randomised experiment in public transport

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12117v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过瑞士随机陈述偏好实验（n=1082）检验单程票价沟通格式是否影响出行时间调整，发现总体上无显著效应，但百分比格式对低票价和浅折扣（约47%以下）会放大价格敏感性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

公共交通需求的高度集中给基础设施带来压力并降低服务质量。定价工具——例如非高峰时段单程票折扣——是一种被广泛使用的跨时间重新分配需求的工具。然而，尚无研究直接考察单程票的价格沟通格式是否会影响出行者在出发时间重新安排方面的出行行为。为填补这一空白，我们利用在瑞士开展的一项基于参与者实际出行（n = 1,082）的随机陈述偏好调查，将参与者分配到三种价格格式之一：绝对金额、绝对偏差或相对于其当前票价的百分比偏差。通过估计包含价格格式交互项的多项Logit模型（multinomial logit model），我们发现沟通格式对总体价格敏感性没有显著影响。然而，对于低票价出行，基于百分比的沟通相对于绝对金额或绝对偏差显著放大了价格敏感性。此外，当价格以百分比偏差形式呈现时，价格敏感性在小价格差时增加、在大价格差时降低，其交叉点对应于约47%的折扣深度。总之，这些发现表明，基于百分比的价格沟通有可能强化非高峰折扣的需求转移效应，尤其是对于低票价产品以及折扣浅于约47%的情形。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Highly concentrated demand in public transport places pressure on infrastructure and reduces service quality. Pricing instruments-such as discounted off-peak single fare tickets-are a widely used tool for rescheduling demand across time. Yet no study has directly examined whether the price communication format of single fare tickets affects travel behaviour in terms of departure-time rescheduling. To fill this gap, we exploit a randomised stated preference survey conducted in Switzerland based on participants' actual journeys (n = 1,082), assigning participants to one of three price formats: absolute amounts, absolute deviations, or percentage deviations from their current fare. Estimating a multinomial logit model with price-format interaction terms, we find no significant effect of communication format on aggregate price sensitivity. However, for low-fare trips, percentage-based communication substantially amplifies price sensitivity relative to absolute amounts or absolute deviations. Moreover, when prices are shown as percentage deviations, price sensitivity increases for small price spreads and decreases for large price spreads, with a crossover point corresponding to a discount depth of approximately 47%. In summary, these findings suggest that percentage-based price communication has the potential to reinforce the demand-shifting effect of off-peak discounts, particularly for low-fare products and for discounts shallower than approximately 47%.
