<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我研究具有共同价值的单物品首价拍卖的粗收益保证（coarse revenue guarantee, CRG）。CRG是在所有信息结构和所有贝叶斯粗相关均衡（Bayes coarse correlated equilibria, BCCE）下的最小期望收益，而BCCE是无遗憾学习所收敛到的均衡概念。所有竞标者提交相同出价的同一性博弈（identical play）达到了最坏情况收益，这一归约产生了闭式刻画。粗保证为正，且随竞标者数量$N$的增加而增加。在每一个有限$n$处，它严格低于Bergemann、Brooks和Morris（2017）的收益保证，且两者之间的差距以$\log N / N$的速率收敛。我将分析扩展到保留价格、对称与非对称先验、其他标准拍卖以及未知价值分布。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I study the coarse revenue guarantee (CRG) of the single-unit first-price auction with common values. The CRG is the minimum expected revenue over all information structures and all Bayes coarse correlated equilibria (BCCE), the equilibrium notion to which no-regret learning leads. Identical play, in which all bidders submit the same bid, attains the worst-case revenue, and this reduction yields a closed-form characterization. The coarse guarantee is positive and increases with the number of bidders $N$. It lies strictly below the revenue guarantee of Bergemann, Brooks and Morris (2017) at every finite $n$, and the gap between the two closes at rate $\log N / N$. I extend the analysis to reserve prices, symmetric and asymmetric priors, other standard auctions, and an unknown value distribution.

</details>
