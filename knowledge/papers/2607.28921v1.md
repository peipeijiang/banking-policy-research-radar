<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究网络结构如何影响社会学习环境中信息聚合的效率。我们考虑一个模型，其中理性主体基于私人信号以及网络中邻居行为的观察，依次选择行动。在给定有限时期内的期望收益比较中，我们证明存在一种信息结构，使得星型网络实现的期望收益严格高于任何其他网络；同时存在另一种信息结构，使得完全网络实现的期望收益严格高于任何其他网络。综合来看，这些结果表明，没有任何网络在所有信息结构下都是统一最优的。我们的分析凸显了响应效应（responsiveness effect）与颠覆效应（overturning effect）之间的权衡：不连通的网络保持了行动对私人信号的响应性，而高度连通的网络则促进了能够颠覆公共信念的极端信息的聚合。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies how network structures affect the efficiency of information aggregation in social learning environments. We consider a model in which rational agents sequentially choose actions based on private signals and observations of their neighbors' actions in a network. Focusing on comparisons of expected payoffs at a given finite period, we show that there exists an information structure under which the star network achieves a strictly higher expected payoff than any other network, and another information structure under which the complete network achieves a strictly higher expected payoff than any other network. Taken together, these results imply that no network is uniformly optimal across all information structures. Our analysis highlights a trade-off between the responsiveness effect and the overturning effect: disconnected networks preserve responsiveness of actions to private signals, whereas highly connected networks facilitate the aggregation of extreme information that overturns public beliefs.
