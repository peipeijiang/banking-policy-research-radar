<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出一个框架，用于在风险偏好通过广义失真风险度量建模时进行偏好稳健决策。与分布稳健性不同，我们的方法处理的是风险泛函本身的不确定性。我们利用Wasserstein距离和Bregman散度在失真（权重）函数上构造模糊集，并推导出最坏和最佳情形失真风险度量的闭式表达式。我们进一步将该框架扩展到秩依赖效用，从而得到偏好稳健的行为模型。特别地，秩依赖效用表现为期望效用模型的稳健化形式，为处理阿莱悖论提供了一种新颖的途径。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce a framework for preference-robust decision making when preferences over risk are modelled through generalised distortion risk measures. Unlike distributional robustness, our approach addresses ambiguity in the risk functional itself. We construct ambiguity sets on distortion (weight) functions using the Wasserstein distance and Bregman divergences, and derive closed-form expressions for the worst- and best-case distortion risk measures. We further extend the framework to rank-dependent utility, yielding preference-robust behavioural models. In particular, rank-dependent utility appears as a robustification of the expected utility model, yielding a novel way to address the Allais paradox.
