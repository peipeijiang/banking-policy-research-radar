<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我对Berk-Nash均衡（Berk-Nash equilibrium）在可能存在模型误设的决策问题中进行了公理化。我明确了一个规则——即在某个问题中选择一组行动-信念对的规则——应当如何比较正确预测与错误预测、如何对模型的移除作出反应，以及如何容纳无法区分各模型的额外观测。结合关于对随机化与收益态度的公理以及一对正则性条件，这些公理刻画了Berk-Nash均衡。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I axiomatize Berk-Nash equilibrium in decision problems with possibly misspecified models. I specify how a rule--which selects a set of action-belief pairs in a problem--ought to compare a correct forecast with an incorrect one, react to the removal of models, and accommodate additional observations that do not distinguish among the models. Together with axioms concerning attitudes toward randomization and payoffs, and a pair of regularity conditions, these axioms characterize Berk-Nash equilibrium.

</details>
