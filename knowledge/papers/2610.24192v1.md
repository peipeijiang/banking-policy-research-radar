<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在先前的研究中，我们采用基于主体建模的模拟方法，对伊恩·赖特（Ian Wright）提出的资本主义经济模型进行了研究，结果表明财富与收入分配的不平等程度在很大程度上由一个关键参数决定：人均平均财富与平均工资之比，记为 \(R\)。该参数刻画了累积资产相对于劳动收入的相对重要性。在模型中，\(R\) 值越大，以基尼系数（Gini coefficient）衡量的不平等程度系统性地越高。

在本研究中，我们考察这一关系在现实经济中是否同样成立。利用世界不平等数据库（World Inequality Database, WID）和经济合作与发展组织（Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development, OECD）的经验数据，我们构建了 \(R\) 的经验估计值，并将其与收入不平等和财富不平等的标准测度指标进行比较。我们发现，在多种经验设定下，\(R\) 与不平等之间均存在正相关关系，其中在成熟发达经济体中观察到的关系最为显著。这些结果为财富-工资比作为与 economic inequality 相关联的简单宏观指标的作用提供了经验支持，同时也凸显了各国及各时期在数据定义和测量方法上的差异所带来的局限性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In previous studies, using agent-based simulations of a model of a capitalist economy proposed by Ian Wright, we showed that inequality in both wealth and income distributions is largely governed by a single key parameter: the ratio between average wealth per capita and the mean salary, denoted by $R$. This parameter captures the relative importance of accumulated assets compared with labor income. In the model, larger values of $R$ are systematically associated with higher levels of inequality, as measured by the Gini coefficient.
  In the present work, we investigate whether this relationship is also observed in real-world economies. Using empirical data from the World Inequality Database (WID) and the Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), we construct empirical estimates of $R$ and compare them with standard measures of income and wealth inequality. We find a positive relation between $R$ and inequality across several empirical settings, with the strongest relationships observed for mature and advanced economies. These results provide empirical support for the role of the wealth-to-salary ratio as a simple macroscopic indicator associated with economic inequality, while also highlighting the limitations imposed by differences in data definitions and measurement across countries and periods.
