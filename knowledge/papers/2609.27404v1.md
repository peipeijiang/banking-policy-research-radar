# When Trust Attracts Fraud: AI and Trust Arbitrage

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27404v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过两市场信号模型表明，生成式AI降低伪造与验证成本后，信任会延迟验证并引发跨市场的‘信任套利’，使欺诈暂时从低质量市场转向高质量市场，随后又因验证被触发而自我限制。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

信任在延迟验证时会招致欺诈。我们构建了一个双市场信号模型，其中生成式人工智能（generative AI）降低了伪造、验证和定向的成本。当伪造在验证之前变得有利可图时，声明的可信度先下降后恢复。跨市场而言，较高的先验质量可能延迟验证，从而形成一个只有低质量市场进行核查的区间。如果定向在该区间内变得有利可图，欺骗性卖家便进入质量较高但警惕性较低的市场，其进入最初可能逆转该市场的可靠性优势。这种流入也会触发验证并阻止进一步进入。我们将这一自我限制机制称为信任套利（trust arbitrage）。在生成式人工智能时代，信任因此可能制造出一种内生但暂时的保护缺口，将欺骗重新导向不同市场。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Trust can attract fraud when it delays verification. We develop a two-market signaling model in which generative AI lowers fabrication, verification, and targeting costs. When fabrication becomes profitable before verification, claim credibility first falls and later recovers. Across markets, higher prior quality can delay verification, creating an interval in which only the lower-quality market checks. If targeting becomes profitable in this interval, deceptive sellers enter the higher-quality but less vigilant market, and their entry can initially reverse its reliability advantage. The inflow also triggers verification and deters further entry. We call this self-limiting mechanism trust arbitrage. In the age of generative AI, trust can thus create an endogenous but temporary protection gap that redirects deception across markets.

</details>
