# Loss Choice or Model Choice? The Role of Forecast Level in Cryptocurrency Volatility Forecasting

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

波动率预测在金融风险管理中发挥着核心作用，因为其整体水平和逐日变动会影响下游决策。大多数研究在保持训练损失固定的情况下比较预测模型。然而，不同损失强调不同的误差，并可能针对未来波动率的不同属性，因此直接比较可能将持续的预测水平差异与逐日预测变动差异混在一起。这使得损失选择的重要性究竟主要来自其所针对的预测水平，还是来自水平调整后仍然存在的差异，仍未得到解决。我们通过比较主要加密货币上的七种损失和五种模型来填补这一空白。基于验证的对齐在原始预测和对齐后预测使用统计评分和单日风险价值（Value-at-Risk）进行评估之前，先调整预测水平。对齐前，边际评分在不同损失之间的变异更大。对齐后，在完整的五模型比较中，模型选择成为更大的变异来源，而不同损失之间在VaR突破率上的差异则大幅缩小。我们的贡献是对损失和模型选择进行的全面评估，表明为什么损失在直接比较中可能显得如此具有影响力，以及当明确考虑预测水平和下游风险时，这种解释会如何变化。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Volatility forecasts play a central role in financial risk management because their overall level and day-to-day movements affect downstream decisions. Most studies compare forecasting models while keeping the training loss fixed. Yet losses emphasise different errors and can target different properties of future volatility, so raw comparisons may combine persistent forecast-level differences with differences in daily forecast movements. This leaves unresolved whether the importance of loss choice comes mainly from the forecast level it targets or from differences that remain after level adjustment. We address this gap through a comparison of seven losses and five models across major cryptocurrencies. Validation-based alignment adjusts the forecast level before the raw and aligned forecasts are evaluated using statistical scores and one-day Value-at-Risk. Before alignment, marginal score variation is greater across losses. After alignment, model choice becomes the larger source of variation in the full five-model comparison, while cross-loss differences in VaR breach rates narrow substantially. Our contribution is a comprehensive evaluation of loss and model choice that shows why losses can appear so influential in raw comparisons and how this interpretation changes when forecast level and downstream risk are considered explicitly.

</details>
