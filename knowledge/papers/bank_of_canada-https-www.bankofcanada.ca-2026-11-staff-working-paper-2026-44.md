<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们开发了一种基于新闻的加拿大感知金融压力测量方法，使用了自1977年以来的《环球邮报》（Globe and Mail）文章。我们表明，新闻冲击主要影响无担保家庭信贷，这与家庭预防性行为相一致。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a news-based measure of perceived financial stress for Canada using Globe and Mail articles since 1977. We show that news shocks primarily impact unsecured households credit, consistent with household precautionary behavior.
