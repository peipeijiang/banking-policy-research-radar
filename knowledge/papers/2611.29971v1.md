<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究用于改进预测模型的补充训练数据集的销售问题。买方不仅对其自身数据集拥有私有信息，还可以选择合并这两个数据集的方式。这种灵活性对菜单施加了新的约束，要求买方的总净效用在其私有数据集的Blackwell序上具有单调性。在此基础上，我们证明，在假设检验中，在微分虚拟价值条件下，对完全信息数据集设定单一标价是最优的。该结果可推广至多类分类问题，其中私有数据集共享一个干净成分，但在标签受污染的方式上存在差异。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies the sale of supplementary training dataset to improve predictive model. The buyer not only has private knowledge of its own dataset, but also can choose the way to merge these two datasets. This flexibility imposes a new constraint on the menu, requiring the buyer's total net utility be monotonic in the Blackwell order on private datasets. Given that, we show that a single posted price for a fully informative dataset is optimal in hypothesis testing, under differential virtual-value condition. The result extends to multi-class classification in which private datasets share a clean component but differ in how their labels are contaminated.
