<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Kabir Dasgupta、Marco Francesconi、Jonathan James、Linda Kirkpatrick 和 Alexander Plum 本文估计了酒驾定罪对劳动力市场结果的因果效应。利用关联行政数据和首次定罪时间上的差异，采用交错双重差分设计（staggered difference-in-differences design），我们将定罪前后的个体与较晚定罪的个体进行比较。我们发现就业率下降6至7个百分点，月收入下降约2%。这些效应主要由就业进入减少而非失业驱动。就业效应在雇主更可能筛查申请人犯罪定罪的行业中最为显著。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Kabir Dasgupta , Marco Francesconi, Jonathan James Linda Kirkpatrick, and Alexander Plum This paper estimates the causal effect of a drink-driving conviction on labour market outcomes. Using linked administrative data and variation in the timing of first convictions in a staggered difference-in-differences design, we compare individuals before and after conviction to those convicted later. We find employment falls by 6–7 percentage points and monthly earnings decline by around 2 percent. The effects are driven primarily by reduced job entry as opposed to job loss. Employment effects are largest in industries where employers are more likely to screen applicants for criminal convictions.
