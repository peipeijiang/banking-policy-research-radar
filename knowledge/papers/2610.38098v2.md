<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我证明了两层嵌套logit模型恰好源自一个序贯选择过程，其中组、子组和产品冲击分阶段揭示。该结果可推广至任意层数，并且对任何小于1的嵌套参数均成立，不受随机效用最大化所要求的边界和排序条件的限制。应用研究中因违反标准排序或下界而被丢弃的参数估计，与序贯选择是一致的。线性反演和福利公式仍然成立。当标准排序不成立时，改进某一产品可以提高其子组中竞争对手的市场份额，即使在单单位需求下也是如此。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I show that the two-level nested logit model arises exactly from a sequential choice process in which group, subgroup, and product shocks are revealed in stages. The result extends to any number of levels and holds for any nesting parameters below one, without the bounds and ordering that random utility maximization requires. Parameter estimates that applied work discards for violating the standard ordering or the lower bound are consistent with sequential choice. The linear inversion and welfare formula hold. When the standard ordering fails, improving one product can raise a rival's market share in its subgroup, even with single-unit demand.

</details>
