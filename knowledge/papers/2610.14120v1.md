<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

设定受益养老金计划的安全性取决于其发起人，然而相对于计划侧建模，发起人困境的建模工具明显不足。我们提出了一个完全基于公开数据的透明、可复现的早期预警流程。发起人脆弱性指数（Sponsor Vulnerability Index, SVI）是一个梯度提升分类器，基于时点化的SEC EDGAR资产负债表基本面数据以及两个宏观序列，提前一到两个季度预测基于申报文件的困境状态。在五个行业组的36家美国发起人（1，418个公司-季度，2015年第一季度至2025年第三季度）上，在留一公司法交叉验证下，其样本外AUC-ROC达到0.887，PR-AUC达到0.711（基准率为11.9%）。带有风格化转移压力叠加的蒙特卡洛VaR（Monte Carlo VaR）表明，气候类压力对多元化计划而言可忽略不计，但当22.5%的资产持有于发起人证券时，会使一年期95% VaR增加20-43%。一个动态自我投资上限将SVI映射为一个限额，该限额在严重困境时从8%降至1%以下，并随发起人状况恢复而回升，我们以PG&E 2019年破产和波音持续脆弱性为例进行了说明。我们在报告方法结果的同时也报告了其失效模式（账面权益标签也会对分拆等非信用事件触发、正样本集中于单一公司、以及风格化压力参数），并以概念DOI发布了代码、数据和结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A defined-benefit pension is only as safe as its sponsor, yet sponsor distress is under-instrumented relative to plan-side modelling. We present a transparent, reproducible early-warning pipeline built entirely on public data. A Sponsor Vulnerability Index (SVI), a gradient-boosted classifier over point-in-time SEC EDGAR balance-sheet fundamentals and two macro series, predicts a filings-based distress state one to two quarters ahead. On 36 U.S. sponsors in five sector groups (1,418 firm-quarters, 2015Q1-2025Q3) it reaches an out-of-fold AUC-ROC of 0.887 and PR-AUC of 0.711 (base rate 11.9%) under leave-one-firm-out cross-validation. A Monte Carlo VaR with a stylized transition-stress overlay shows that climate-type stress is negligible for a diversified plan but adds 20-43% to one-year 95% VaR when 22.5% of assets are held in sponsor securities. A dynamic self-investment cap maps the SVI to a limit that falls from 8% to below 1% in acute distress and recovers with the sponsor, which we illustrate on PG&E's 2019 bankruptcy and Boeing's persistent fragility. We report the method's failure modes alongside its results (book-equity labels that also fire on non-credit events such as spin-offs, positives concentrated in one firm, and stylized stress parameters) and release code, data and results under a concept DOI.

</details>
