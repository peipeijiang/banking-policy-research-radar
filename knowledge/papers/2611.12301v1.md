# Inequality-Aware Regulation

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我研究当监管者关注剩余在异质性消费者与企业之间的分配时，对一家筛选垄断者的监管问题。消费者私下知道自身对质量的支付意愿，企业私下知道自身的生产成本。监管合同规定了企业必须提供的质量—价格选项。我证明，当监管者赋予支付意愿较低的消费者更大权重时，基准监管是最优的：监管者要求提供可负担的基本选项，但让企业自由设计和定价高端选项。在强烈的消费者侧再分配关切下，单一强制性的基本选项即已足够。监管者利用企业从高端买家抽取剩余的动机，来支撑基本选项的低监管价格。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I study regulation of a screening monopolist when the regulator cares about the distribution of surplus across heterogeneous consumers and the firm. Consumers privately know their willingness to pay for quality, and the firm privately knows its production costs. Regulatory contracts specify quality-price options that the firm must offer. I show that when the regulator gives greater weight to consumers with lower willingness to pay, baseline regulation is optimal: the regulator requires affordable basic options but leaves the firm free to design and price premium options. Under strong consumer-side redistributive concerns, a single required basic option suffices. The regulator uses the firm's incentive to extract surplus from premium buyers to support low regulated prices for basic options.

</details>
