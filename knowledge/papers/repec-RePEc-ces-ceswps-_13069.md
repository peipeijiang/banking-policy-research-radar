<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多新兴经济体往往相当依赖碳密集型产业，因而面临潜在的转型风险。本文量化了这种风险的影响，以及各种政策和政策设计在缓解该风险方面的作用。为此，本文构建了一个两国两部门环境动态随机一般均衡模型，其中每个“国家”分别代表发达经济体或新兴经济体的一个集团。该模型纳入了现实世界的特征，如金融摩擦以及跨集团之间的金融和贸易联系。利用该模型，我们模拟了发达经济体以碳税和关税形式实施的气候政策，以及围绕全球最低碳价格的国际协调。本文识别并量化了新兴经济体在发达经济体实施气候政策面前的重要脆弱性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many emerging economies tend to rely considerably on carbon-intensive sectors, exposing themselves to potential transition risk. This paper quantifies the impact of this risk and the role of various policies and policy designs in mitigating it. It does so with a two-country two-sector environmental dynamic stochastic general equilibrium model, where each “country” represents a bloc of advanced or emerging economies, respectively. The model includes real-world features such as financial frictions and financial and trade linkages across blocs. With it, we simulate climate policy in advanced economies, in the form of carbon taxes and tariffs, and international coordination around a minimum global carbon price. This paper identifies and quantifies important vulnerabilities among emerging economies to the implementation of climate policy in advanced economies.
