# Too Late to Prune: Generative AI, the Screen, and Team Creative Decisions

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14984v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过预注册实验研究生成式AI在团队创意任务中的访问时机，发现将AI访问从搜索阶段移到筛选阶段会降低专家评定的创意质量，机制在于晚期选项绕过筛选并增加低质量提案。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

生成式人工智能（Generative AI）使选项变得廉价，并将创造性工作的瓶颈从生成选项转移到筛选选项。我们认为，人工智能的价值取决于其选项是在团队的筛选之前还是之后到达。在我们的模型中，早期选项竞争组合中的位置；晚期选项在该竞争之后到达，并且未经筛选即被保留。在一项预注册实验中，我们将148个三人团队随机分配到五种访问同一语言模型的日程安排中，任务为具有定时搜索和选择阶段的产品设计任务。将同样的15分钟访问时间从搜索阶段移到选择阶段，使专家评定的创造力降低了0.62个标准差，尽管各团队使用该模型的频率相似。平台日志将差异定位在筛选环节。早期访问使想法池扩大了五倍，而团队对其进行了修剪。晚期访问的团队带着与无人工智能团队相同的小规模想法池进入筛选阶段，然后再次搜索，将其选择阶段查询的29%用于生成新选项，而全程访问的团队这一比例为11%。晚期访问增加了广度，而非质量：这些团队提交的方案比无人工智能团队多73%。语言模型评判者检测到了访问带来的收益，但未检测到晚期时机带来的成本。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Generative AI makes options cheap and shifts the bottleneck of creative work from generating options to screening them. We argue that AI's value depends on whether its options arrive before or after the team's screen. In our model, early options compete for a place in the portfolio; late options arrive after that competition and are kept unscreened. In a preregistered experiment, we randomly assigned 148 three-person teams to five schedules of access to the same language model during a product-design task with timed search and selection stages. Moving the same 15 minutes of access from search to selection lowered expert-rated creativity by 0.62 standard deviations, although teams used the model at similar rates. Platform logs locate the difference at the screen. Early access enlarged the pool of ideas fivefold, and teams pruned it. Late-access teams reached the screen with the same small pool as teams without AI and then searched again, devoting 29% of their selection-stage queries to generating new options, compared with 11% for teams with access throughout. Late access added breadth, not quality: these teams submitted 73% more proposals than did teams without AI. Language-model judges detected the benefit of access but not the cost of late timing.

</details>
