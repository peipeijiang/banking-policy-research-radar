<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

从2021年初到2024年初，美国经历了前所未有的无证移民潮，且各地方经济体之间存在显著异质性。利用行政微观数据，我们构建了全国和地方无证移民流动的新测度指标，并通过多种实证设计利用外生变异。无证移民劳动力流动使地方就业几乎一比一地增加，但工资压低的证据不一。这些流动还推高了房价和租金，却未扩大住房供给。最后，这些流动降低了人均劳动收入和政府转移支付。这些发现有助于为围绕无证移民如何影响美国劳动力市场、住房市场和公共财政的争论提供依据。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

From early 2021 through early 2024, the U.S. experienced an unprecedented boom in unauthorized immigration, with substantial heterogeneity across local economies. Using administrative microdata, we construct new measures of national and local unauthorized immigration flows and exploit exogenous variation using multiple empirical designs. Unauthorized immigrant worker flows increased local employment approximately one-for-one, with mixed evidence of wage depression. They also raised house prices and rents without expanding housing supply. Finally, these flows reduced labor income per capita and government transfers. These findings help inform debates surrounding how unauthorized immigration impacts labor and housing markets and public finances in the U.S.
