<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文推导了聚类稳健推断中方差线性化估计量偏差的精确表达式，该表达式推广了Bell和McCaffrey（2002）此前推导的结果。文中讨论了连续处理和二元处理两种情形。在前一种情形下，研究表明线性化方差估计量及其刀切变体的偏差相对于真实方差为$G^{-1}$阶，其中$G$表示聚类总数。随后提出了一种偏差校正方差估计量（BCCV），并证明其相对偏差为$G^{-2}$阶。在具有二元处理的模型情形下，标准估计量和所提出的偏差校正估计量的偏差阶数分别为$\max (G_{1}^{-1},G_{0}^{-1})$和$\max (G_{1}^{-2},G_{0}^{-2})$，其中$G_{1}$和$G_{0}=G-G_{1}$分别为处理组和未处理组的聚类数。文中还提出了对使用偏差校正标准误计算的$t$统计量进行尺度和自由度调整的方法。通过蒙特卡洛实验研究了所提出的BCCV检验的小样本性质。结果表明，所提出的检验通常优于基于传统聚类标准误和刀切聚类标准误的检验，并与wild bootstrap推断以及\cite{hansen2026jackknife}近期提出的刀切分布调整检验程序表现相当。最后，通过文献中的三个实证应用说明了所提出检验的实用性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper derives an exact expression for the bias of the linearized estimator of the variance for cluster-robust inference, which generalizes an earlier result derived by Bell and McCaffrey (2002). Both cases of continuous and binary treatments are discussed. Under the former it is shown that the bias of the linearized variance estimator and its jackknife variant are of order $G^{-1}$ relative to the true variance, where $G$ denotes the total number of clusters. A bias-corrected variance estimator (BCCV) is then proposed and shown to have a relative bias of order $G^{-2}$. In the case of models with binary treatments the orders of the bias of standard estimators and the proposed bias-corrected one are shown to be $\max (G_{1}^{-1},G_{0}^{-1})$ and $\max (G_{1}^{-2},G_{0}^{-2})$, where $G_{1}$ and $G_{0}=G-G_{1}$ are the numbers of treated and untreated clusters. Scale and degrees of freedom adjustments to the $t$-statistic computed using bias-corrected standard errors are also proposed. Small sample properties of the proposed BCCV test are investigated using Monte Carlo experiments. It is shown that the proposed test generally outperforms tests based on conventional and jackknife cluster standard errors, and performs comparably to wild bootstrap inference and the recent jackknife distribution-adjusted testing procedure by \cite{hansen2026jackknife}. The utility of the proposed test is illustrated\ using three empirical applications from the literature.
