<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了在具有相互依赖估值的简单双边贸易机制中，最坏情况下的均衡贸易收益。此类设定中的均衡通常很复杂，因为提议的价格可以传递提议者所掌握的私人信息。这种均衡会因筛选所引入的低效率（如在独立私人价值设定中）以及逆向选择而遭受贸易收益损失。我们识别出了广泛的条件，在这些条件下，序贯均衡仍被保证能够实现第一最优贸易收益的固定比例。我们的界量化了稀有悲观卖方信号和稀有乐观买方信号的核心作用。这些结果适用于卖方提议、买方提议和随机提议机制。我们分析中的关键思想是将均衡贸易收益与一个具有独立私人价值的辅助市场中的提议者利润联系起来。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the worst-case equilibrium gains from trade in simple bilateral trade mechanisms with interdependent valuations. Equilibria in such settings are typically complex, as proposed prices can convey private information held by the proposer. Such equilibria suffer lost gains from trade both due to the inefficiency introduced by screening (as in independent private value settings) and due to adverse selection. We identify broad conditions under which, nevertheless, sequential equilibria are guaranteed to achieve a constant fraction of the first-best gains from trade. Our bounds quantify the central role of rare pessimistic seller signals and rare optimistic buyer signals. The results apply to seller-proposer, buyer-proposer, and random-proposer mechanisms. The key idea in our analysis is to relate equilibrium gains from trade to proposer profits in an auxiliary market with independent private values.
