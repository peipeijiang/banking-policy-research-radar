<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

公交运营本质上是不稳定的——初始以相等发车间隔调度的公交车最终不可避免地会发生串车。该问题加剧了乘客等待时间，造成公交车之间的载荷失衡，并削弱了服务可靠性。强化学习（RL）已被证明在缓解公交串车方面非常有效，并且优于传统的基于模型的方法。然而，现有的RL方法需要智能体与环境持续交互，这可能在有效策略学到之前就干扰公交系统。本文提出了一种基于离线图的多智能体演员-评论家方法（Offline Graph-based Multi-agent Actor-Critic, Offline-GMAC），该方法仅使用在正常无控制运营期间收集的现成GPS日志数据来训练公交驻站策略，从而消除了破坏性在线交互或探索的需要。该方法采用通用图神经网络进行多智能体信用分配。我们方法的一个关键创新是一种数据增强方案，该方案从历史无控制公交轨迹中合成未执行的驻站动作。该方案利用了随机路段行程时间延迟与有意驻站干预之间的等价性。在温和假设下，我们证明了合成状态转移分布与目标状态转移分布之间的1-Wasserstein距离上界可由一个与路段行程时间标准差成线性关系并加上一个可调的依赖于合成的项来界定。使用真实世界公交数据的评估表明，所提出的离线方法达到了与其在线对应方法相当的性能，在某些情况下甚至更好。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Bus operations are inherently unstable---buses initially dispatched at equal headways will inevitably end up bunching up. This issue exacerbates passenger waiting times, creates load imbalances between buses, and undermines service reliability. Reinforcement learning (RL) has proven highly effective for mitigating bus bunching and outperforms traditional model-based approaches. However, existing RL approaches require agents to continuously interact with the environment, which potentially disrupts the bus system before effective policies are learned. This paper proposes an Offline Graph-based Multi-agent Actor-Critic (Offline-GMAC) method that trains bus holding policies using only readily available logged GPS data collected during normal uncontrolled operations, thereby eliminating the need for disruptive online interaction or exploration. The method employs general graph neural networks for multi-agent credit assignment. A key innovation of our method is a data augmentation scheme that synthesizes unperformed holding actions from historical uncontrolled bus trajectories. The scheme exploits the equivalence between stochastic link travel time delays and deliberate holding interventions. Under mild assumptions, we prove that the 1-Wasserstein distance between synthesized and target state transition distributions is bounded above by a term that scales linearly with the standard deviation of link travel times plus a tunable synthesis-dependent term. Evaluations using real-world bus data demonstrate that the proposed offline method achieves performance comparable to, and in some cases, even better than its online counterpart.
