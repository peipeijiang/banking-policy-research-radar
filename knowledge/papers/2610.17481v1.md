<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在短而相关的定价面板中，估计误差主要由轨迹离散度而非抽样噪声主导，从而造成识别不足，而只有独立分配的变异才能解决这一问题。我们提出一种引导式迭代方法，以决定应生成多少变异、在何处生成以及持续多久：一种在风险收入预算下的周期性价格扰动方案，被表述为针对设计特定误差协方差的方差最小化问题，类似于投资组合选择。我们建立了四项关键结果：（1）一个闭式最小方差组合规则；（2）证明强度的凸成本会产生内部且分散的分配，而在凹的可容忍交易量损失成本下，将强度集中于更少扰动只有在候选异质、固定成本已计入价格，或扰动超过成本曲线拐点时才有收益；（3）一个折现递归，产生依赖于数据的退出实验区时间表；（4）将其应用于Dominick's零售数据，其中约五个设计独立单元即可匹配锚定类别中约700个被动等价单元的精度，而跨十个类别的快速代理相对于被动面板实现了97%的中位方差缩减。十六项比较（其中十一项为预先指定）支持该理论：在五十多个可穷尽验证的实例中，贪婪选择达到全局最优的90-93%，并且该递归比预测提前两个周期达到稳态基准的90%，尽管参数自助法表明其delta方法区间低估了右尾风险。边界条件表明，正则化协方差估计器将即插即用噪声容忍度从29%扩展到超过50%，子模性在明确的曲率阈值内成立，而共同冲击则施加了基本的精度下限。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Estimation error in short, correlated pricing panels is dominated by trajectory dispersion rather than sampling noise, creating an identification shortfall that only independently assigned variation can resolve. We propose a guided iteration methodology to decide how much variation to generate, where, and for how long: a cyclic price-perturbation scheme under a revenue-at-risk budget, framed as variance minimization over design-specific error covariances, analogous to portfolio selection. We establish four key results: (1) a closed-form minimum-variance combination rule; (2) proof that a convex cost of intensity yields an interior, spread-out allocation, whereas under a concave tolerable-volume-loss cost, concentrating intensity into fewer perturbations pays off only with heterogeneous candidates, priced-in fixed costs, or perturbations beyond the cost curve's inflection point; (3) a discounted recursion yielding a data-dependent exit timeline from the experimentation zone; and (4) an application to Dominick's retail data, in which about five designed independent units match the precision of approximately 700 passive equivalents in the anchor category, while a fast proxy across ten categories achieves a median 97% variance reduction relative to passive panels. Sixteen comparisons (eleven pre-specified) support the theory: greedy selection achieves 90-93% of the global optimum across more than fifty exhaustively verifiable instances, and the recursion reaches its predicted 90%-of-steady-state benchmark two cycles early, though a parametric bootstrap indicates that its delta-method interval understates the right-tail risk. Boundary conditions show that regularized covariance estimators extend plug-in noise tolerance from 29% to beyond 50%, submodularity holds up to a clear curvature threshold, and a shared shock imposes a fundamental precision floor.
