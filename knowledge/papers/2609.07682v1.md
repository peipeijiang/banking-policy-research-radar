<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究了匹配市场中优先级违规的强度问题。若某代理人偏好另一代理人的匹配结果而非自身所得，并且其对该对象的优先级优势超过$k$个位次，则称其具有“非常合理的嫉妒”（very justified envy）。研究表明，除非$k$值过大而难以实际应用，否则帕累托效率与消除非常合理的嫉妒无法兼得。本文提出了一种对延迟接受（Deferred Acceptance）与即时接受（Immediate Acceptance）的简单推广，并证明该机制能够对任意$k$值消除非常合理的嫉妒。在随机匹配市场的模拟中，我发现对合理嫉妒的适度放宽能够显著提升匹配位次。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies the intensity of priority violations in matching markets. An agent is said to have very justified envy if she prefers another agent's match to her own and her priority advantage for the object exceeds $k$ ranks. I show that unless $k$ is prohibitively large, Pareto efficiency is incompatible with the elimination of very justified envy. A simple generalization of Deferred Acceptance and Immediate Acceptance is proposed and shown to eliminate very justified envy for any $k$. In simulations of random matching markets, I find that modest relaxations to justified envy can yield significant improvements in assignment ranks.
