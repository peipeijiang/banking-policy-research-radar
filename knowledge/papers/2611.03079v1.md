# When Covariates Mask Pre-Trends

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03079v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文指出在协变量调整的DiD设计中，若协变量在placebo窗口之后测量，会吸收结果变量的前期冲击从而掩盖真实预趋势，导致置信区间过窄；作者提出可估计和检验该扭曲，并发现90篇近期发表研究中77篇至少有一个时变协变量测量过晚。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

诚实的双重差分法（difference-in-differences）通过处理前平行趋势的失败程度来判断处理后平行趋势可能失败的程度，这一点由安慰剂估计（placebo estimates）所体现。当研究设计对协变量（covariates）进行调整时，安慰剂估计也会被调整。我们证明，只有当协变量在最早的安慰剂窗口之前被测量时，这一基准才是有效的。较晚测量的协变量已经反映了安慰剂期间对结果的冲击，因此对其调整会掩盖部分前趋势。这样一来，安慰剂估计看起来比真实趋势更平坦，由此得到的置信集（confidence sets）过窄。当协变量在早期时点也被观测到时，这种扭曲可以被估计和检验。在最近发表的90项对时变协变量进行调整的研究中，有77项至少有一个协变量的测量时间过晚。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Honest difference-in-differences methods judge how far parallel trends may fail after treatment by how far it failed before treatment, as shown by placebo estimates. When the design adjusts for covariates, the placebo estimates are adjusted too. We show that this benchmark is valid only if the covariates are measured before the earliest placebo window. A covariate measured later already reflects shocks to the outcome during the placebo period, so adjusting for it hides part of the pre-trend. The placebos then look flatter than the true trend and the resulting confidence sets are too narrow. When the covariate is also observed at an early date, the distortion can be estimated and tested. Of 90 recent published studies that adjust for time-varying covariates, 77 measure at least one covariate too late.

</details>
