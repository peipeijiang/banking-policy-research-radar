<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究非参数面板回归 \(G_{it}=g(\alpha_i,\gamma_t)+\varepsilon_{it}\) 中双向不可观测异质性的识别问题，其中潜在类型的识别可归结为构造可识别的、**单射**的代理变量。为此，我们考虑二元回归函数 \(g(\alpha,\gamma)\) 在乘积域 \(\Omega_\alpha\times\Omega_\gamma\) 上的奇异值分解（SVD），其左奇异函数 \(\{u_r\}\) 作为不可观测异质性参数 \(\alpha\) 的代理变量。对于 \(\{v_r\}\) 与 \(\gamma\) 的关系，论证是对称的。我们在**观测等价简化**下工作：若两个 \(\alpha\) 值诱导相同的条件响应 \(g(\alpha,\cdot)\)，则它们被视为可识别的，因此响应映射 \(\alpha\mapsto g(\alpha,\cdot)\) 在构造上即为单射。我们证明两点。第一，这一简化**等价于**全部左奇异特征函数集合的单射性，因此无需对无限集合 \(\{u_r\}_{r\ge1}\) 施加额外条件。第二，在单一附加的**局部单射性**条件下，有限个前导特征函数构成的集合 \(U_R=(u_1^{\top},\dots,u_R^{\top})^{\top}\) 对所有足够大的 \(R\) 都是单射的。证明将全局单值性问题归结为局部一阶条件加上拓扑紧致性论证，从而绕过了通常所需的全局雅可比条件。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study identification of two-way unobserved heterogeneity in the nonparametric panel regression $G_{it}=g(α_i,γ_t)+\varepsilon_{it}$, where identification of the latent types reduces to constructing identified, \emph{injective} proxies for them. To this end we consider the singular value decomposition (SVD) of the bivariate regression function $g(α,γ)$ on a product domain $Ω_α\timesΩ_γ$, whose left singular functions $\{u_r\}$ serve as proxies for the unobserved heterogeneity parameter $α$. The arguments are symmetric for $\{v_r\}$ vis-à-vis $γ$. We work under an \emph{observational-equivalence simplification}: two values of $α$ that induce the same conditional response $g(α,\cdot)$ are identified, so that the response map $α\mapsto g(α,\cdot)$ is injective by construction. We show two things. First, this reduction is \emph{equivalent} to injectivity of the full collection of left singular eigenfunctions, so no further condition is needed over the infinite collection $\{u_r\}_{r\ge1}$. Second, under a single additional \emph{local injectivity} condition, a finite collection of leading eigenfunctions $U_R=(u_1^{\top},\dots,u_R^{\top})^{\top}$ is injective for all sufficiently large $R$. The proof reduces a global univalence question to a local first-order condition plus a topological compactness argument, bypassing the global Jacobian conditions usually required.
