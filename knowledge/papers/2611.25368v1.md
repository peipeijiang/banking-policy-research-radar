# Cognitive Constraints and Misspecification Concern

## 一句话结论

> 研究认知受限且担心模型误设的代理人在重复决策中如何加权候选模型并评价选择，发现更多数据未必克服有限认知能力，简单但统计上较差的模型可长期保持相关性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一类重复决策问题：其中决策主体具有认知约束，并担心其模型可能存在设定偏误。她首先利用数据对候选模型进行加权，但受制于其所能处理的复杂度的认知容量约束；随后，她根据这些模型下各选择的表现来评估选择。更多的数据未必能克服有限容量：一个统计上较差但更简单的模型在长期内仍可能具有相关性。当约束起作用时，按模型复杂度成比例地偏向某些模型的证据可能使当前行为保持不变，同时仍会影响后续对数据的反应。当简单模型倾向于安全时，该约束消除了风险行动与安全行动之间的循环；放松该约束可能使风险行为在初始阶段更具吸引力，但长期来看频率更低。该约束还可以通过抑制那些会揭示其错误的决策来维持设定偏误的模型类别。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study repeated decision problems in which an agent has cognitive constraints and is concerned that her models might be misspecified. She first uses data to weight candidate models, subject to a cognitive-capacity constraint on the complexity she can process, and then she evaluates choices according to how well they perform under those models. More data need not overcome limited capacity: a statistically inferior but simpler model can remain relevant in the long run. When the constraint binds, evidence that favors models in proportion to their complexity can leave current behavior unchanged while still affecting later responses to data. This constraint eliminates cycles between risky and safe actions when simple models favor safety, and relaxing it can make risky play more attractive initially yet less frequent in the long run. It can also sustain misspecified model classes by suppressing choices that would reveal their errors.
