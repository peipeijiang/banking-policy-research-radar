<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在实证科学中，显著性阈值往往决定研究结果是否被视为效应存在的证据。本文研究了在相同样本量的重复实验中，恰好达到常规显著性阈值的研究结果有多大可能性仍保持显著。为回答这一问题，我们基于实验经济学、心理学和社会科学领域已发表研究，估计了在给定p值条件下预期的重复实验成功概率。我们通过展示该指标能准确预测实际重复实验结果（优于预测市场）来验证其有效性。p值为0.05的研究结果，其预期重复实验成功概率在各领域中介于0.10至0.25之间。低可重复性反映了原始研究中的低统计功效，而非发表偏倚。随后，我们开发了一种非参数估计方法，并将其应用于使用更大样本的经济学文献，发现重复实验成功概率有所提高，但仍处于较低水平。这些结果表明，单一研究中的统计显著性仅能提供效应存在的提示性证据，更可靠的结论需要累积证据的支持。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In the empirical sciences, significance thresholds often determine whether findings are treated as evidence of an effect. This paper studies how likely findings that just meet conventional significance thresholds are to remain significant in replications of the same sample size. To answer this question, we estimate the expected replication probability conditional on a given p-value among published studies for experimental economics, psychology, and social science. We validate this measure by showing it accurately predicts actual replication outcomes, outperforming prediction markets. A finding with a p-value of 0.05 has an expected replication probability ranging from 0.10 to 0.25 across fields. Low replicability reflects low power in original studies rather than publication bias. We then develop a nonparametric estimator and apply it to economics literatures that use larger samples, finding higher but still low replication probabilities. These results indicate that statistical significance in a single study provides only suggestive evidence of an effect. Stronger conclusions require cumulative evidence.
