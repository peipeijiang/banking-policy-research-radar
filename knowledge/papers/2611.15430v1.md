# Factors Influencing Rural Female Workforce Participation in India, 2017- 2025: An Econometric Analysis Using District-Level Panel Data

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15430v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用2017-2025年印度约500个地区的面板数据，实证分析农村女性劳动参与率的决定因素，发现幼儿存在和家庭收入上升降低参与率，而农村非正规工资上升提高参与率但存在阈值效应。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

印度农村女性劳动参与率（Rural Female Workforce Participation Rate, RFWPR）在1993-94年至2017-18年间呈下降趋势。2017-18年之后，趋势发生逆转，RFWPR从2017-18年的23.7%上升至2025年的44.9%。本文利用约500个地区九年（2017-18年至2025年）的地区级面板数据，对近期印度RFWPR的一些关键决定因素进行了计量经济学分析。我们发现，家庭中幼童的存在倾向于降低RFWPR，这与以往多项研究的发现一致。我们的计量模型结果表明，家庭实际收入的增加——以按消费者价格指数平减的人均月消费支出作为代理变量——倾向于降低RFWPR。农村地区临时工实际工资率的上升——可能反映了此类劳动力（尤其是农业领域）的短缺——倾向于提高RFWPR。然而，工资上涨对RFWPR的正向效应仅在农村实际工资达到某一特定水平之前持续存在。超过该工资水平后，工资对RFWPR的正向效应减弱，甚至可能转为负向，即农村工资上涨倾向于降低RFWPR。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Rural female workforce participation rate (RFWPR) in India declined between 1993-94 and 2017-18. After 2017-18, the trend reversed, and RFWPR rose from 23.7% in 2017-18 to 44.9% in 2025. Using district-level panel data for about 500 districts for nine years, 2017-18 to 2025, the paper analyzes econometrically some key determinants of RFWPR in India in the recent period. We find that the presence of young children in the family tends to reduce RFWPR, consistent with findings from several previous studies. Our econometric model results indicate that an increase in household real income, proxied by the monthly per capita consumption expenditure deflated by the consumer price index, tends to reduce RFWPR. Increases in the real wage rate of casual workers in rural areas, likely reflecting a shortage of such labour, particularly in agriculture, tend to raise RFWPR. However, the positive effect of wage hikes on RFWPR continues only until real rural wages reach a particular level. Beyond that wage rate, the positive effect of wages on RFWPR diminishes or could even turn negative, i.e., rural wage hikes tending to reduce RFWPR.
