<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关于人们为何向开源软件（open source software, OSS）及相关开放协作平台贡献的研究已持续二十余年，但研究结论存在冲突，且相关研究往往将人们所贡献的工作与其持续贡献的意愿视为可互换的概念。基于自我决定理论（self-determination theory）和动机挤出理论（motivation crowding theory），我们对41项研究进行了元分析。主分析汇集了来自29个独立样本（N = 9,632）的178个相关系数，涉及八种前因变量与贡献努力及持续意愿之间的关系。七种前因变量与努力呈正相关，全部八种前因变量与意愿呈正相关，且每种前因变量与意愿的关联至少与其与努力的关联同样强。与一种常见假设相反，内在动机与努力的关联并不比外在动机更强，但其在意愿与努力之间的差距更大。声誉主要在设有声誉系统的平台上与努力相关，且我们未发现金钱奖励会挤出享受感的迹象。研究结果为平台如何将贡献者的意愿转化为实际工作提供了启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Research on why people contribute to open source software (OSS) and related open collaboration platforms spans more than two decades, but its findings conflict, and studies often treat the work people contribute and their intention to keep contributing as interchangeable. Drawing on self-determination theory and motivation crowding theory, we meta-analyze 41 studies. The main analysis pools 178 correlations of eight antecedents with contribution effort and continuance intention from 29 independent samples (N = 9,632). Seven antecedents relate positively to effort, all eight relate positively to intention, and each relates at least as strongly to intention as to effort. Contrary to a common assumption, intrinsic motivation is not more strongly related to effort than extrinsic motivation, but its gap between intention and effort is larger. Reputation relates to effort mainly on platforms with a reputation system, and we find no sign that monetary rewards crowd out enjoyment. The findings inform how platforms can turn contributors' intentions into work.
