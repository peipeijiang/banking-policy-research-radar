RePEc是一个由经济学家于1997年为经济学家创建的倡议项目。它主要作为一个文献目录工具，但也收集了关于经济学家、其所属机构及其影响力的各种关联元数据。因此，劳动经济学家可能会在文献检索和追踪之外对RePEc产生兴趣——即将其作为一个描述该职业、其成员及其成果的数据集。本文介绍了RePEc的起源与历史、其数据的多个层面以及如何获取这些数据。文中还提供了一些利用RePEc数据进行学术研究的示例，这些研究可能引起劳动经济学家的兴趣，并引导他们发现RePEc数据的其他用途。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

RePEc is an initiative created by economists for economists in 1997. It functions primarily as a bibliographic tool, but it also collects various linked metadata about economists, their institutions, and their impact. Thus, a labor economist may be interested in RePEc beyond literature searches and tracking—namely, as a dataset describing a profession, its members, and their output. This essay describes RePEc’s origins and history, the many facets of its data and how the data can be accessed. It also provides some examples of scholarly studies leveraging RePEc data that could be of interest to labor economists and lead them to other uses of RePEc data.
