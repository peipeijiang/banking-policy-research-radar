<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

国际气候合作的设计面临着一个权衡：分配效率与对因退出、重新谈判和政治更迭所导致的制度侵蚀的稳健性。我们在一个风格化的联盟形成博弈中形式化了这一权衡，该博弈的成员资格由两个基于市场的渠道驱动——成员溢价与外部者流失——并利用稳健控制的工具，在有限的一组制度扰动下保持稳定。每个成员面临的搭便车差距源自经济基本要素，即碳成本差异与出口的排放强度，并与特定架构的渠道相分离，这使得模型可解且具有可比性。该模型是双稳态的：一个小规模残余俱乐部与一个近乎普遍的联盟之间由临界质量分隔。对于碳货币这一新提出的基于数量的体系，其排放权每期重新发放、使用后即灭失，并在边境强制执行，已建立的联盟能够承受的扰动大约是点火阶段可承受扰动的十二倍，且该临界翻转在异质性达到约七倍成员溢价的临界无序度之前依然成立。两个稳健性坐标——点火成本与崩溃阈值——将每种进入架构置于一个包含三种状态的地图中：自点火型、依赖创始型与依赖持续支持型。碳货币属于自点火型；边境调整属于依赖创始型；出口退税属于依赖持续支持型。缺乏外部者流失机制的架构，例如清洁证书与排放交易体系链接，构成一个独立类别，它们能在联盟内部提升效率，却无法推动联盟的形成。稳健性决定了合作能否形成并持续；效率则仅决定已建立的联盟能带来多少成果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Designs for international climate cooperation face a trade-off between allocative efficiency and robustness to the institutional erosion by defection, renegotiation, and political turnover. We formalize this trade-off in a stylized coalition-formation game whose membership is driven by two market-based channels, a membership premium and an outsider drain, and is stabilized against a bounded set of institutional perturbations using the apparatus of robust control. The free-rider gap each member faces is derived from economic primitives, the carbon cost differential and the emission intensity of exports, and separated from the architecture-specific channels, which makes the model solvable and comparative. The model is bistable: a small remnant club and a near-universal coalition are separated by a critical mass. For the carbon currency, a newly proposed quantity-based system whose emission rights are reissued each period, extinguished upon use, and enforced at the border, the established coalition withstands roughly twelve times the perturbation admissible at ignition, and the tipping survives heterogeneity up to a critical disorder of about seven times the membership premium. Two robustness coordinates, an ignition cost and a collapse threshold, place each entry architecture in a map with three regimes: self-igniting, founding-dependent, and permanent-support-dependent. The carbon currency is self-igniting; the border adjustment is founding-dependent; the export rebate is permanentsupport- dependent. Architectures without an outsider drain, such as clean-up certificates and emissions-trading-system linking, form a separate class that improves efficiency within a coalition but cannot drive its formation. Robustness governs whether cooperation forms and endures; efficiency decides only how much an established coalition delivers.
