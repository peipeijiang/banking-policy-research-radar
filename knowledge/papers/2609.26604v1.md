# The network advantage: benefits of interregional scientific knowledge spillovers on AI patenting

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26604v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

知识溢出在很大程度上被研究为通过地理邻近性扩散的非预期外部性，而有意的跨区域网络则大多通过共同发明人关系来考察，而非通过共同署名论文所承载的科学层面。本研究追问：来自跨区域人工智能相关共同发表的溢出是否提升了区域人工智能专利产出，以及区域研发的所有权构成是否塑造了利用这些溢出的能力。我们采用网络计量方法，对2006年至2020年中国26个省级区域进行分析，发现共同发表溢出促进了人工智能专利产出，但其回报几乎完全集中于民营企业主导研发的区域，从而加剧了区域分化。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Knowledge spillovers have largely been studied as unintended externalities diffusing through geographic proximity, while deliberate interregional networks have been examined mostly through co-inventor ties rather than the scientific layer carried by co-authored publications. This study asks whether spillovers from interregional AI-related co-publications increase regional AI patenting, and whether the ownership composition of regional R&D shapes the capacity to capitalize on them. Using a network-econometric approach across 26 Chinese provincial-level regions from 2006 to 2020, we find that co-publication spillovers foster AI patenting, but the returns concentrate almost entirely in regions where privately owned enterprises dominate R&D, reinforcing regional divergence.
