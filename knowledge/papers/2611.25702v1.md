<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

去偏机器学习（debiased machine learning）消除了冗余参数估计的一阶效应，但当冗余函数难以学习时，残余的二阶偏差会使推断失效。二阶正交矩（second-order orthogonal moments）以额外方差为代价消除这一偏差。我们将这一代价刻画为：为抵消矩对冗余误差的二阶敏感性所需的修正的最小方差，并证明只有当该代价有限时才能实现精确修正。由于该代价因矩而异，一阶有效矩未必能最小化总方差。因此，我们联合选择矩及其修正，并推导出由此得到的最小方差，即半参数效率界（semiparametric efficiency bound）的二阶类比。最优矩可能以一阶效率换取更低的修正代价。在二元选择 logit 模型中，它仅在样本充足的协变量单元中使用条件似然之外的信息；在含连续协变量的双重差分（difference-in-differences）中，精确修正放弃了排除限制（exclusion restrictions）带来的效率增益。在含连续控制变量的部分线性模型（partially linear model）中，每个矩的代价都是无穷大，因此只能消除部分偏差。针对此类情形，我们提出一种数据驱动的自适应规则，在方差膨胀的界限内选择消除多少偏差，并证明选择之后推断仍然有效。在部分线性模型的模拟中，名义 95\% 区间的覆盖率在所有设计中都从 32--80\% 上升到 94--97\%，但压力测试除外。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Debiased machine learning removes the first-order effect of nuisance estimation, but the remaining quadratic bias can invalidate inference when nuisance functions are hard to learn. Second-order orthogonal moments remove this bias at the cost of additional variance. We characterize this cost as the minimum variance of a correction that offsets the quadratic sensitivity of the moment to nuisance errors, and show that exact correction is possible only when this cost is finite. Because the cost varies across moments, the first-order efficient moment need not minimize total variance. We therefore choose the moment and its correction jointly, and derive the resulting minimum variance, a second-order analogue of the semiparametric efficiency bound. The optimal moment may trade first-order efficiency for a lower correction cost. In a binary choice logit model, it uses information beyond the conditional likelihood only in well-populated covariate cells; in difference-in-differences with continuous covariates, exact correction forgoes the efficiency gains from exclusion restrictions. In the partially linear model with continuous controls, the cost is infinite for every moment, so that only part of the bias can be removed. For such cases, we propose a data-driven adaptive rule that chooses how much bias to remove within a bound on variance inflation, and we show that inference remains valid after selection. In simulations for the partially linear model, the coverage of nominal 95\% intervals rises from 32--80\% to 94--97\% in all designs but a stress test.
