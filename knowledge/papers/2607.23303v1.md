# Ranking-based competitive balance measures in Formula One

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在竞技体育中，竞争力可通过比较同一赛事内的起跑与完赛排名，以及同一赛季中不同赛事的起跑与完赛排名来衡量。由于位置变化的重要性并非均匀分布，且排名顶端的变化比底端更受关注，我们提出采用加权距离（weighted distances）进行分析。为此，我们应用了两种加权方案，并将其与标准凯梅尼距离（Kemeny distance）进行对比，以分析1950年至2024年间的一级方程式赛车（Formula One）数据。令人意外的是，竞争平衡的演变对权重选择具有稳健性，但若聚焦于排名前列，竞争则更为激烈。过去二十年间，竞争平衡状况比以往任何时候都更为不利。统计检验揭示了竞争平衡中存在的三个结构性断点（structural breaks），这些断点与规则变更密切相关，凸显了决策者在竞争力演变过程中的关键作用。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Competitiveness in racing sports can be measured by comparing the start and finish rankings within races, as well as the start and finish rankings across races in a season. Since the importance of position changes is non-uniform and variance at the top of the ranking is more interesting than at the bottom of the ranking, we propose using weighted distances for this purpose. Therefore, two weighting schemes are applied and compared to the standard Kemeny distance to analyse the Formula One between 1950 and 2024. The evolution of competitive balance is unexpectedly robust to the weights, but competition is more intense if one focuses on the top positions. Competitive balance has been more unfavourable in the last two decades than ever before. Statistical tests uncover three structural breaks in competitive balance that are closely related to regulatory changes, highlighting the role of decision-makers in the evolution of competitiveness.

</details>
