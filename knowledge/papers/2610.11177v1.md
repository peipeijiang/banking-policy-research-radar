<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文构建了一个面向人工智能代理的动态机制与信息设计的一般性框架，涵盖最优合约设计，并将不断演化的隐藏信息与隐藏行动同部分验证、技术授权以及多代理互动整合在一起。该框架嵌套了传统的动态筛选、隐藏行动合约以及条件动态信息设计，同时允许报告、行动和信息使用决策通过一个共同的延续机制相互作用。我们建立了一个动态显示原理以及可实施性的递归刻画，其中真实报告与报告后服从通过延续激励被联合决定。在拟线性最优合约环境中，动态包络与积分单调性方法产生了一个经验证调整的动态虚拟剩余表示，并识别出三种不同的边际：动态信息租金、验证租金以及隐藏行动的实施成本。这些边际产生了一个分支层级，将传统动态筛选、隐藏行动合约及其联合问题区分开来。我们还研究了监测、信息使用与激励的联合设计：信息量更大的监测能力扩展了设计者的机会集合，但完全披露未必最优，且信息使用本身可以改变隐藏行动激励。在多个代理的情况下，相关信息、同伴约束、耦合激励、联合可行性以及延续机会会创造额外的策略互动。尽管该框架受人工智能代理环境启发，但它更广泛地适用于具有类似经济特征的动态代理情境。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops a general framework for dynamic mechanism and information design with AI agents, encompassing optimal contracting and integrating evolving hidden information and hidden action with partial verification, technological authorization, and multi-agent interaction. It nests conventional dynamic screening, hidden-action contracting, and conditional dynamic information design, while allowing reporting, action, and information-use decisions to interact through a common continuation mechanism. We establish a dynamic revelation principle and a recursive characterization of implementability in which truthful reporting and post-report obedience are jointly determined through continuation incentives. In quasilinear optimal-contracting environments, dynamic envelope and integral-monotonicity methods yield a verification-adjusted dynamic virtual-surplus representation and identify three distinct margins: dynamic information rents, verification rents, and the implementation cost of hidden action. These margins generate a branched hierarchy separating conventional dynamic screening, hidden-action contracting, and their joint problem. We also study the joint design of monitoring, information use, and incentives: more informative monitoring capacity expands the designer's opportunity set, but full disclosure need not be optimal, and information use can itself alter hidden-action incentives. With multiple agents, correlated information, peer discipline, coupled incentives, joint feasibility, and continuation opportunities create additional strategic interactions. Although motivated by AI-agent environments, the framework applies more broadly to dynamic agency settings with similar economic features.
