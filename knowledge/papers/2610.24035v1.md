<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人工智能（AI）使得产出研究成果变得前所未有的容易。紧迫的问题在于，研究者应当如何利用这项技术来组织自己的工作。这个问题并不新鲜：软件工程和安全研究早已研究了人与机器组成的团队如何对由众多贡献者共享的工作体保持控制。我们从这些文献所报告的实践中提炼原则，并将其迁移到AI辅助研究中。这些原则构成了TRACE：追踪记录（Track records）、对照来源审查（Review against sources）、独立验收（Accept independently）、控制交接（Control transitions）、评估修正（Evaluate corrections）。这五项原则共同为研究者提供了一个共同基础，使其在将AI整合进工作流程的同时保持控制。通过界定工作单元并关注每个单元如何被塑造，这些原则帮助研究者按照人类的注意力节奏来安排AI辅助工作，而不是按照AI产出内容的速度。我们还介绍了TRACE-OS，一个用于AI辅助研究项目的开源操作系统。它通过共享指令、工具和记录将原则付诸实践，并契合研究者现有的工作流程。这一共享标准有助于建立信任，从而使团队能够委派工作并实现AI所承诺的生产力。更广泛地说，我们旨在开启一场关于治理原则的对话，借助这些原则，研究共同体能够从AI中获益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial intelligence (AI) makes it easier than ever to produce research outputs. The pressing question is how researchers should organize their work with this technology. The question is not new: software engineering and security research have long studied how teams of people and machines keep control of a shared body of work with many contributors. We distil principles from the practices these literatures report and transpose them to AI-assisted research. The principles form TRACE: Track records, Review against sources, Accept independently, Control transitions, and Evaluate corrections. Together, the five principles give researchers a common basis for remaining in control while integrating AI into their workflows. By bounding units of work and attending to how each is shaped, they help researchers pace AI-assisted work to human attention rather than to the speed at which AI produces content. We also introduce TRACE-OS, an open source operating system for AI-assisted research projects. It puts the principles into practice through shared instructions, tools, and records that fit researchers' existing workflows. The shared standard can contribute to building trust, which allows teams to delegate and realize the productivity that AI promises. More broadly, we aim to open a conversation about the governing principles through which the research community can benefit from AI.
