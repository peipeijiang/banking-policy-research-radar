<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

凹性消费函数意味着边际消费倾向随财富增加而下降。本文刻画了在有限期最优储蓄问题中，当存在随机贴现、收益、收入及借贷约束时，能够保证这一性质的效用函数。在条件性缺乏耐心——即条件期望贴现总收益不超过1——的情况下，消费始终为凹函数当且仅当逆绝对谨慎度，$-u''/u'''$，为凹函数。若缺乏条件性耐心，则双曲绝对风险厌恶（HARA）是保证一致凹性的充分必要条件。一个确定性的单期例子表明，违反条件性耐心如何使非HARA效用下的消费变为严格凸函数。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Concave consumption functions imply a marginal propensity to consume that falls with wealth. I characterize the utility functions that guarantee this property in finite-horizon optimal saving problems with stochastic discounting, returns, income, and borrowing limits. Under conditional impatience---the conditional expected discounted gross return does not exceed one---consumption is always concave if and only if inverse absolute prudence, $-u''/u'''$, is concave. Without conditional impatience, hyperbolic absolute risk aversion (HARA) is necessary and sufficient for uniform concavity. A deterministic one-period example shows how violating conditional impatience can make consumption strictly convex for a non-HARA utility.
