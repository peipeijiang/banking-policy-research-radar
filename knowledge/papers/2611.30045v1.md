# The Entangled Veil: Why Redistribution Requires a New Language

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

罗尔斯的无知之幕要求在选择正义原则时，不知道自已将占据何种位置——时间的、空间的、社会的或个人的。我们认为，这一对象无法在标准经济模型中得到忠实表征。一个忠实的表征必须满足三项要求：必须对称地对待所有世代，捕捉位置之间不可分离的相关性，并允许流动性成本随地点和职业而变化。我们表明，标准最优税收模型至少违反其中一项要求，而满足全部三项要求的最小框架是一个改编自弦理论的几何框架，其中无知之幕是跨越可能生活情境空间的一个纠缠态。我们推导出完全均等化作为第一最优基准，当相关性奖励区分时分离簇的出现，代内公平与代际公平之间的对称性，以及一种通过相关性降低制度壁垒的隧穿机制。该模型生成四种不平等剖面和四种不平等类型。估计结果在数量上具有重要意义：该框架改变了再分配政策的排序，而忽略其所识别的边际会导致系统性的政策评估偏差。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The Rawlsian veil of ignorance requires choosing principles of justice without knowing which position-temporal, spatial, social, or personal one will occupy. We argue that this object cannot be represented faithfully within standard economic models. A faithful representation must satisfy three requirements: it must treat all generations symmetrically, capture non-separable correlations among positions, and allow mobility costs to vary across locations and occupations. We show that standard optimal-tax models violate at least one requirement, and that the minimal framework satisfying all three is a geometric framework adapted from string theory, in which the veil is an entangled state over a space of possible life situations. We derive full equalization as a first-best benchmark, the emergence of separated clusters when correlations reward distinction, a symmetry between intraand inter-generational equity, and a tunneling mechanism by which correlations reduce institutional barriers. The model generates four inequality profiles and four inequality types. The estimates are quantitatively important: the framework changes the ranking of redistributive policies, and ignoring the margins it identifies leads to systematically biased policy evaluation.
