# Administrative Boundary Reforms and Firm Pollution in China: Institutional Path Divergence and Spatial Reallocation of Pollution

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06374v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文利用1998-2013年中国企业污染面板和边界配对设计，发现县改区（集权）降低本地NOx强度但导致同市邻县污染上升，县改市（赋权）则通过脏增长显著增加污染，表明行政边界改革重塑污染的空间配置而非净减排。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

中国的行政边界改革改变了地方权力配置和增长激励，但并未改变自然地理格局。我们研究了两项制度内容相反的改革——县改区（集权化：将县并入地级市成为市辖区）和县改市（赋权：将县升级为县级市）——使用企业-年度污染面板数据（1998—2013年）以及比较同一县边界两侧企业的边界配对研究设计。集权化改革通过清洁生产使地方NOx排放强度降低14.8%，且未缩减产出或就业；赋权改革则通过肮脏增长使COD、粉尘和废水水平上升87%—95%。地方减排并非净减排：同一地级市相邻县的NOx上升（+0.77，p<0.01），污染再配置遵循行政边界而非地理距离，这一点在卫星环境数据中得到证实，且并非由风或水的传输所驱动。这种再配置的运作机制是相邻县现有企业变得更脏，而非企业搬迁或新进入。一项粗略的福利计算显示，唯一显著的减排（NOx，每年4700万日元，置信区间跨零）带来的福利收益在统计上不显著，而县改市改革造成的损害为每年-23亿日元。这些发现表明，边界改革重塑的是污染跨越行政边界的空间配置，而非减少污染。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Administrative boundary reforms in China alter the assignment of local authority and growth incentives without changing physical geography. We study two reforms with opposite institutional content---county-to-district (centralization: a county absorbed into a municipality as an urban district) and county-to-city (empowerment: a county upgraded to a county-level city)---using a firm-year pollution panel (1998--2013) and a border-pair research design that compares firms on opposite sides of the same county border. Centralizing reform lowers local NOx intensity by 14.8% through cleaner production, without shrinking output or employment; empowering reform raises COD, dust, and wastewater levels by 87--95\% through dirty growth. The local abatement is not net abatement: NOx rises in same-prefecture neighbor counties (+0.77, p<0.01), reallocation follows administrative boundaries rather than geographic distance, is confirmed in satellite ambient data, and is not driven by wind or water transport. The reallocation operates through incumbent neighbor firms becoming dirtier, not through firm relocation or new entry. A back-of-the-envelope welfare calculation yields a statistically insignificant welfare gain from the only significant abatement (NOx, \textyen 47 million/yr, CI crossing zero) and a damage of -\textyen 2.3 billion/yr from county-to-city reform. These findings show that boundary reforms reshape the spatial configuration of pollution across administrative boundaries rather than reducing it.

</details>
