# The Role of Monetary Policy in Influencing Interbank Rate, Inflation, and Gross Domestic Product in Malaysia

[查看原文](http://ir.unimas.my/id/eprint/53791/1/ARBMSV44_N1_PP260_284.pdf)

## 一句话结论

> 论文使用1984-2024年马来西亚时间序列数据，通过Granger因果、VECM、协整和方差分解检验OPR与货币总量对银行间利率、通胀和GDP的短期与长期影响，发现短期关系不显著而长期关系显著，货币政策传导主要通过信贷、流动性和汇率渠道逐步实现。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

货币政策在调控马来西亚经济方面的有效性如何？货币政策是否对经济产生了积极影响？尽管以往的研究主要关注马来西亚货币供应量、利率和汇率之间的关系，但更近期的研究表明，隔夜政策利率（OPR）、银行信贷、流动性状况和汇率之间的相互作用能更好地理解货币政策传导机制。具体而言，货币政策冲击似乎通过信贷渠道和汇率渠道传导至通货膨胀和经济活动。本研究探讨货币政策如何影响马来西亚的银行间利率、通货膨胀和GDP增长，重点关注货币总量（M1、M2和M3）以及隔夜政策利率（OPR）。尽管该问题具有重要意义，但与发达国家相比，针对马来西亚货币政策机制精确效果的研究仍然有限。本研究利用1984年至2024年的二手数据，采用定量研究方法以填补这一空白。研究运用时间序列计量经济学技术考察货币政策工具与经济指标之间的关系，包括格兰杰因果检验、向量误差修正模型（VECM）、协整检验和方差分解。这些技术能够对短期和长期动态进行全面考察。研究结果表明，货币政策工具与宏观经济指标之间存在不显著的短期关系，但存在显著的长期关系。总之，货币政策工具对马来西亚的通货膨胀率、银行间利率和国内生产总值（GDP）增长具有部分显著的影响。结果还表明，马来西亚的货币政策在长期比短期更为有效，其效果通过信贷、流动性和汇率渠道逐步传导。货币政策在宏观经济稳定和长期经济增长中发挥了积极作用，但其对不同指标的影响各有差异，且无法单独调控经济。因此，马来西亚货币政策的有效性可视为中等水平，其成效取决于外部经济条件、金融市场反应以及银行、企业和家庭的行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How effective is monetary policy in regulating the economy in Malaysia? Has monetary policy positively influenced the economy? While previous studies have focused on the relationships between Malaysia's money supply, interest rates, and exchange rates, more recent research indicates that the interaction among the OPR, bank credit, liquidity conditions, and exchange rates provides a better understanding of monetary policy transmission. Specifically, it appears that monetary policy shocks are transmitted to inflation and economic activity through the credit and exchange rate channels. This study investigates how monetary policy affects interbank rates, inflation, and GDP growth in Malaysia, focusing on monetary aggregates (M1, M2, and M3) and the Overnight Policy Rate (OPR). Despite its significance, limited research has been conducted on the precise effects of Malaysia's monetary policy mechanisms compared with those of developed nations. Utilising secondary data from 1984 to 2024, this study employs a quantitative research methodology to address this gap. The relationships between monetary policy tools and economic indicators are examined using time-series econometric techniques, such as Granger causality tests, the Vector Error Correction Model (VECM), cointegration tests, and variance decomposition. These techniques enable a thorough examination of both short- and long-term dynamics. The findings of this study indicate an insignificant short-term relationship but a significant long-term relationship between monetary policy instruments and macroeconomic indicators. In conclusion, monetary policy instruments have partially significant effects on inflation rates, interbank rates, and Gross Domestic Product (GDP) growth in Malaysia. The results also show that monetary policy in Malaysia is more effective in the long term than in the short term, with its effects transmitted gradually through credit, liquidity, and exchange rate channels. Monetary policy has played a positive role in macroeconomic stability and long-term economic growth, but its impacts on different indicators vary, and it cannot regulate the economy alone. The effectiveness of Malaysia's monetary policy can thus be considered moderate, with its success depending on external economic conditions, financial market reactions, and the behaviour of banks, firms, and households.
