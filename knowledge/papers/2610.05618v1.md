# Causal Lag Structure Discovery in Confounded Time Series via Orthogonalized Adaptive Estimation

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05618v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在多元时间序列中找出哪些变量导致哪些其他变量，以及在什么滞后阶数上导致，是科学和政策的中心问题，然而现有方法迫使研究者在灵活的混杂因素调整、数据驱动的滞后选择和能够控制错误发现率（FDR）的推断之间做出选择。ORACLE-VARX在一个流程中同时实现了这三点。首先，双重/去偏机器学习（DML）从结果变量和滞后序列中去除非线性混杂效应。其次，自适应因果滞后估计（ACLE）通过序贯显著性检验在每个时间步选择滞后阶数，从而追踪机制变化。第三，逐项$z$检验结合Benjamini--Hochberg校正，在目标FDR下选择有向边。我们证明，在每个滚动窗口中，去偏系数渐近服从以窗口平均目标为中心的正态分布，因此其$z$检验渐近有效。在具有时变结构和非线性混杂的合成基准上，ORACLE-VARX（LightGBM）对真实滞后阶数的追踪效果最好（RMSE $0.96$ 对比 $1.1$--$1.5$），边FDR为$0.047$，接近PCMCI（$0.045$），低于VAR（$0.129$）和VAR-LiNGAM（$0.187$），并且预测效果优于这三者。在九个带有宏观经济混杂因素的美国行业ETF上，它产生了可解释的因果图，其滞后阶数在高波动机制下上升。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Finding which variables cause which others in multivariate time series, and at what lags, is central to science and policy, yet existing methods force a choice between flexible confounder adjustment, data-driven lag selection, and inference that controls the false discovery rate (FDR). ORACLE-VARX does all three in one pipeline. First, double/debiased machine learning (DML) removes nonlinear confounder effects from the outcomes and the lagged series. Second, adaptive causal lag estimation (ACLE) picks the lag order at each time step by sequential significance tests, tracking regime changes. Third, entry-wise $z$-tests with Benjamini--Hochberg correction select directed edges at a target FDR. We prove that in each rolling window, the debiased coefficients are asymptotically normal around a window-averaged target, so their $z$-tests are asymptotically valid. On a synthetic benchmark with time-varying structure and nonlinear confounding, ORACLE-VARX (LightGBM) tracks the true lag order best (RMSE $0.96$ vs $1.1$--$1.5$), has edge FDR $0.047$, close to PCMCI ($0.045$) and below VAR ($0.129$) and VAR-LiNGAM ($0.187$), and forecasts better than all three. On nine U.S. sector ETFs with macroeconomic confounders, it yields interpretable causal graphs whose lag order rises in high-volatility regimes.
