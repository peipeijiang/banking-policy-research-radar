<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们定义了一个策略博弈的范畴，其中态射是由玩家集合之间的映射和策略剖面集合之间具有特定性质的映射所组成的对，并证明该范畴是良定义的。对于策略剖面集合之间的映射，我们考虑了三种情形：它们可以关于每个玩家的偏好关系是保序的、逆序的或序嵌入的。我们定义了一个从博弈范畴到集合范畴取值的预层，该预层将每个策略博弈映射到策略剖面集合，并给出了该预层为良定义的等价条件。对于集合范畴中的态射，我们考虑了两种情形：它们可以是关系或映射。我们定义了一个将每个策略博弈映射到纳什均衡（分别地，帕累托有效策略剖面）集合的预层，并证明该预层是良定义的当且仅当策略剖面集合之间的映射是逆序的或序嵌入的（分别地，策略剖面集合之间的映射是序嵌入的），且集合范畴中的态射是关系。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We define a category of strategic games in which a morphism is a pair of a map between sets of players and a map between sets of strategy profiles with specific properties, and we show that this category is well-defined. Three cases are considered for the maps between sets of strategy profiles: they may be order-preserving, order-reflecting or order-embedding with respect to each player's preference relation. We define a presheaf on the category of games valued in a category of sets that sends each strategic game to a set of strategy profiles, and we present equivalent conditions for this presheaf to be well-defined. Two cases are considered for the morphisms in the category of sets: they may be relations or maps. We define a presheaf that sends each strategic game to the set of Nash equilibria (resp.\ Pareto efficient strategy profiles), and we show that this presheaf is well-defined if and only if the maps between sets of strategy profiles are order-reflecting or order-embedding (resp.\ the maps between sets of strategy profiles are order-embedding), and the morphisms in the category of sets are relations.
