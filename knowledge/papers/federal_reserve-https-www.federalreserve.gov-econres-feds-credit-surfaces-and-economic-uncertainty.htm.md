<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

约翰·吉纳科普洛斯与大卫·E·拉波波特 杠杆维度上的信用曲面将债券利差表示为贷款价值比的函数。实证上，我们表明不确定性冲击通常会扩大利差并使信用曲面变得更加陡峭，从而深刻影响信贷供给。理论上，我们推导出信用曲面凸性的充分必要条件，以及预期抵押品价格分布变化导致信用曲面陡峭化的条件。最后，我们证明信用曲面本身完全揭示了抵押品价格的整个分布，从而为描述不确定性和随机序提供了一种新的、生动的语言。信用曲面陡峭化本身是一种新的随机序，可能更好地捕捉我们对“更多不确定性”的直观概念。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

John Geanakoplos and David E. Rappoport The Credit Surface along the leverage dimension gives the bond spread as a function of the loan-to-value ratio. Empirically, we show that uncertainty shocks typically increase spreads and steepen the credit surface, profoundly affecting the supply of credit. Theoretically, we derive necessary and sufficient conditions for the convexity of the credit surface, and for changes in the anticipated distribution of collateral prices that lead to steepening of the credit surface. Finally, we show that the credit surface itself fully reveals the entire distribution of collateral prices, thus providing a new and vivid language with which to describe uncertainty and stochastic orders. Credit surface steepening itself is a new stochastic order that may better capture our intuitive notion of more uncertainty.
