# The Hidden Cost of Resource Nationalism

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文探讨关键矿产出口限制对外国勘探投资的影响，勘探是决定未来资源基础的上游活动。我们构建了一个涵盖国家、矿产和年份的三维面板数据，将OECD关键原材料出口限制清单与S&P Global 2009年至2024年的勘探预算相结合，并采用适应限制措施交错实施的局部投影双重差分估计方法，发现出口限制的引入对外国勘探投资具有统计和经济意义上显著的负向影响：每对国家-矿产组合每年约1100万美元（不变美元），约占典型金矿项目勘探和开发成本的9%。该效应具有不对称性，因为取消限制并不能恢复投资。直接出口禁令驱动了这一结果，而出口税和配额对外国勘探没有可辨别的影响。关键结果主要由腐败控制较弱的国家驱动，这表明在政策可信度较低时，贸易限制的信号效应具有普遍性。最后，我们验证了限制措施在实践中确实具有约束力：限制措施引入后出口值下降约15%，其中禁令和配额导致的降幅最大。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper explores the effect of export restrictions on critical minerals on foreign investment in exploration, the upstream activity that determines the future resource base. Using a three-way panel of countries, minerals and years that combines the OECD inventory of export restrictions on critical raw materials with S&P Global exploration budgets over the period 2009 to 2024, and a local-projections difference-in-differences estimator that accommodates the staggered adoption of restrictions, we find that the introduction of an export restriction has a statistically and economically significant negative effect on foreign exploration investment: about US$11 million (constant dollars) per country-mineral pair per year, or roughly 9% of the cost of exploring and developing a typical gold project. The effect is asymmetric, as removing a restriction does not restore investment. Outright export bans drive the result, whereas export taxes and quotas have no discernible effect on foreign exploration. The key results are driven by countries with weaker control of corruption, suggesting that the signalling effect of trade restrictions is pervasive when there is poor policy credibility. Finally, we verify that restrictions bind in practice: export values fall by about 15% after a restriction is introduced, with the largest declines for bans and quotas.
