<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多经济参数依赖于估计的条件分布。我们通过表示这些参数的第一步导数，并将表示子在协变量条件下进行中心化，为这些参数构造局部稳健矩。我们发展了互补的条件测度与CDF线性化方法，并推导了针对控制函数、反事实泛函、条件秩-秩回归以及条件\(L\)-泛函变换的修正。在CDF线性化下，Riesz回归准则自动估计修正项；其分析将表示子估计与对估计条件律的积分区分开来。我们在连续性与余项条件下，为交叉拟合估计量建立了渐近神谕等价性、渐近正态性和一致方差估计，且不要求第一步估计量的泛函弱收敛。精确线性表示带来了双重稳健性；对于秩-秩回归，该性质是逐分量成立的。模拟结果表明，相对于代入法估计，偏差和RMSE均有降低，而双重交叉拟合进一步改善了有限样本性能。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many economic parameters depend on estimated conditional distributions. We construct locally robust moments for these parameters by representing their first-step derivative and centering the representer conditionally on the covariates. We develop complementary conditional-measure and CDF linearizations, and derive corrections for control functions, counterfactual functionals, conditional rank-rank regression, and transformations of conditional $L$-functionals. Under the CDF linearization, a Riesz-regression criterion estimates the correction automatically; its analysis distinguishes representer estimation from integration against the estimated conditional law. We establish asymptotic oracle equivalence, asymptotic normality, and consistent variance estimation for cross-fitted estimators under continuity and remainder conditions, without requiring functional weak convergence of the first-step estimators. An exact linear representation yields double robustness; for rank-rank regression this property is componentwise. Simulations report reductions in bias and RMSE relative to plug-in estimation, with double cross-fitting improving the finite-sample performance.
