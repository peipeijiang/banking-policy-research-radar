<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究考察了人工智能（Artificial Intelligence, AI）的扩散如何影响宏观经济冲击向资产价格的传导。通过结合AI专利、企业信息披露中的AI含量以及职业组合的AI暴露度，构建了美国行业AI强度的新指数，并依据关于AI的调查证据对其进行了验证。面板局部投影估计表明，面对供给冲击和技术冲击，AI密集型行业获得显著更高的超额收益并享有更高的估值，但对需求冲击并未表现出不同的反应。对前一类冲击的反应在经济意义上很大，在多种独立的供给侧识别下均稳健，并且在控制对标准风险因子的暴露后依然成立。该反应由使用AI的行业而非生产AI的行业驱动，伴随着同一期限内估值的同期上升，并且集中于高波动状态。总体而言，结果表明技术暴露是总体冲击如何影响所考察行业横截面的重要决定因素。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study investigates how the diffusion of Artificial Intelligence (AI) affects the transmission of macroeconomic shocks to asset prices. A new index of AI intensity for US industries is constructed by combining AI patents, the AI content of corporate disclosures, and the AI exposure of occupational mixes; this is then validated against survey evidence on AI. Panel local projection estimates indicate that AI-intensive industries earn significantly higher excess returns and command higher valuations in response to supply and technology shocks, but do not exhibit different responses to demand shocks. The response to the former type of shocks is economically large, robust across multiple independent supply-side identifications, and survives controlling for exposure to standard risk factors. It is driven by the industries that use AI rather than those that produce it, it is accompanied by a contemporaneous rise in valuations over the same horizon, and it is concentrated in high-volatility states. On the whole the results suggest that technological exposure is an important determinant of how aggregate shocks affect the cross-section of industries examined.
