# Systematic Bias in Green Patent Classification: Silent Green and False Green

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

基于合作专利分类Y02标签构建的绿色专利指标广泛应用于研究、政策与投资领域，然而其构念效度尚未在语料库规模上接受审计。我们评估Y02是否存在系统性偏差，以及该偏差是否可能加剧ESG创新脱节现象。我们引入“误差即信号”框架，将行政标签与独立模型之间的不一致视为测量误差的诊断性证据。利用微调领域模型对9,075,421项美国专利商标局授权专利（1962—2024年）进行Y02筛查，共识别出517,772项不一致案例。两个独立的开放权重大语言模型随后通过共识裁决，判断每项被标记的发明是否具有直接的气候减缓或适应功能。我们识别出180,384项行政性第一类错误（“虚假绿色”），集中于数字数据处理、无线网络、数字通信和半导体领域；以及29,465项第二类错误（“沉默绿色”），集中于分离工艺、催化、排气控制、热泵、电力系统和电池领域。修正共识归因错误后，测得的绿色专利总量减少25.5%（从592,387项降至441,468项；敏感性区间为390,540—508,126项），其中ICT能效类（Y02D）下降67.6%。遗漏与技术非典型性呈倒U型关系，使得复杂且非常规的发明尤其容易被漏标。事件检验显示，当绿色分类受到关注时，误分类并未出现离散性跃升；2013年CPC体系推出后，绿色框架表述仅出现小幅上升。该偏差主要反映分类能力不足而非申请人策略性行为，并在结构上对重工业领域识别不足。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Green-patent indicators built on Cooperative Patent Classification Y02 tags are widely used in research, policy, and investment, yet their construct validity has not been audited at corpus scale. We assess whether Y02 is systematically biased and whether that bias may reinforce the ESG innovation disconnect. We introduce an Error-as-Signal framework that treats disagreement between an administrative label and an independent model as diagnostic evidence of measurement error. Screening 9,075,421 USPTO granted patents (1962-2024) with a fine-tuned domain model against Y02 yields 517,772 disagreements. Two independent open-weight large language models then judge by consensus whether each flagged invention has a direct climate-mitigation or adaptation function. We identify 180,384 administrative Type I errors (False Green), concentrated in digital data processing, wireless networks, digital communications, and semiconductors, and 29,465 Type II errors (Silent Green), concentrated in separation processes, catalysis, exhaust control, heat pumps, power systems, and batteries. Correcting consensus-attributed errors reduces the measured green-patent population by 25.5% (592,387 to 441,468 patents; sensitivity bounds 390,540-508,126), with ICT energy efficiency (Y02D) falling 67.6%. Omission follows an inverted-U relationship with technological atypicality, making complex and unconventional inventions especially likely to go unlabelled. Event tests show no discrete jump in misclassification when green classification became salient and only a small rise in green framing after the 2013 CPC launch. The bias primarily reflects classification capacity rather than applicant strategy and structurally under-recognizes heavy industry.
