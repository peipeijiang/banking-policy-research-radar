# Linear Demand Systems are Consistent with Discrete Choice

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13785v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

聚合线性需求系统可以通过由进行离散选择的异质性消费者总体来建立微观基础。它们也可以被扩展以纳入收入效应。我证明了这些结果，并展示了如何仅使用市场层面数据对这一类模型进行结构估计。消费者偏好的分布仅通过用聚合项约束角点解来恢复。这一方法在保留线性需求的分析与计算优势的同时，允许达到Berry、Levinsohn和Pakes（1995）[BLP]那样的丰富性：它不需要外生固定市场规模，允许选择集存在异质性，并产生一个灵活的替代矩阵，该矩阵会随着不同消费者进入/退出市场而内生变化。离散选择微观基础促进了福利分析以及对加成和成本传导的灵活测度，并可应用于广泛的其他用途。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Aggregate linear demand systems can be micro-founded by a population of heterogeneous consumers making discrete choices. They can also be extended to incorporate income effects. I prove these results and show how to structurally estimate this class of models using only market-level data. The distribution of consumer preferences is recovered by disciplining the corner solutions with aggregate terms only. This approach allows for the same richness of Berry, Levinsohn, and Pakes (1995) [BLP] while retaining the analytical and computational advantages of linear demand: it does not require exogenously fixing market size, allows for heterogeneity in choice sets, and results in a flexible substitution matrix which varies endogenously as different consumers enter/exit the market. The discrete-choice microfoundation facilitates welfare analysis and the flexible measurement of markups an cost pass-throughs, among a wide range of other applications.

</details>
