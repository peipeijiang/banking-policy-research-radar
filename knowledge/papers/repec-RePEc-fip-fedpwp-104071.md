<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

长期以来，人类创造了大量非结构化文本数据档案，而当代研究者在很大程度上仍无法获取这些数据。光学字符识别（OCR）、全文搜索和大语言模型（LLMs）的进步，使得从历史原始材料中挖掘洞见在技术和经济上变得可行，从而提供了前所未有的机遇。我们提出了一个框架，用于考量处理包含非结构化文本的大型图像语料库时不同方法的各种成本与权衡。我们通过一个案例研究来说明该框架并展示性能统计数据，该案例研究识别了1910年至1965年间510万张费城房产契约图像中的种族限制条款。我们发现8，395张契约图像包含种族限制条款，据我们估计，这覆盖了费城所有地块的6%以上。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Over time, humans have created vast archives of unstructured textual data that remain largely inaccessible to contemporary researchers. Advances in optical character recognition (OCR), full-text search, and large language models (LLMs) offer unprecedented opportunities to unlock insights from historical source material by making it technically and economically feasible to analyze these data. We present a framework to consider the various costs and trade-offs of different methods for working with a large corpus of images containing unstructured text. We illustrate this framework and present performance statistics via a case study identifying racial restrictions in 5.1 million images of Philadelphia property deeds from 1910–1965. We find 8,395 deed images containing a racial restriction, which we estimate to cover more than 6% of all parcels in Philadelphia.
