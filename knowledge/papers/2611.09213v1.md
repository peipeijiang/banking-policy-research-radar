<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

车险理赔管理日益面临双重目标：在控制理赔成本的同时，减少与车辆维修决策相关的温室气体排放。本文提出一个双变量离散时间建模框架，受BINAR(1)依赖结构启发，将财务理赔动态与维修相关碳足迹相联系，允许时间持续性、交叉依赖以及两个维度之间的正/负相关关系。利用外部来源的排放因子和替换零部件的成本信息，我们在两种维修路径下操作化理赔的碳足迹：新零部件与循环经济再使用零部件（PIEC）。随后，我们进行情景分析，其中再使用零部件的接受率发生变化，并量化对预期货币支出和CO₂当量排放的联合影响。结果表明，更高的PIEC接受率可以同时降低预期排放和平均理赔成本，且联合效益的大小取决于BINAR(1)过程所捕捉的依赖结构和持续性。所提出的框架为保险公司提供了一个透明、可复现的工具，用于评估维修政策杠杆，并使理赔运营与脱碳目标保持一致，同时保留明确的随机风险视角。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Motor insurance claims management increasingly faces a dual objective: controlling claim costs while reducing greenhouse-gas emissions associated with vehicle repair decisions. This paper proposes a bivariate, discrete-time modelling framework that links the dynamics of financial claims and repair-related carbon footprint inspired by a BINAR(1) dependence structure, allowing for temporal persistence, cross-dependence and a positive/negative correlation between the two dimensions. Using externally sourced emission factors and cost information for replacement parts, we operationalise the carbon footprint of claims under two repair pathways: new parts versus circular-economy reused parts (PIEC). We then conduct scenario analyses in which the acceptance rate of reused parts varies, and we quantify the joint implications for expected monetary outlays and CO 2 -equivalent emissions. Results show that higher PIEC acceptance can simultaneously reduce expected emissions and average claim cost, and the magnitude of the joint benefit depends on the dependence structure and persistence captured by the BINAR(1) process. The proposed framework provides a transparent, reproducible tool for insurers to evaluate repair-policy levers and to align claims operations with decarbonisation targets while retaining an explicit stochastic risk perspective.
