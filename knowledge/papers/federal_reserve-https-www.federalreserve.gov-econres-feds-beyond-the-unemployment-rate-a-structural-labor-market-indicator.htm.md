<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

Isabel Cairó、Hess Chung、Francesco Ferrante、Cristina Fuentes-Albero、Camilo Morales-Jimenez 和 Damjan Pfajfar 的研究指出，劳动力市场变量在反映松弛或紧张程度时常常发出相互矛盾的信号，这往往使政策评估在关键节点上变得复杂。本文为美国经济构建了一个结构性劳动力市场指标（Structural Labor Market Indicator, SLMI），以弥补现有单变量指标或非理论性统计方法的不足——这些方法虽能综合多个劳动力市场指标，却无法区分供给与需求的驱动因素。我们利用一个包含搜索与匹配摩擦、内生劳动力参与率以及可变工作时间的中等规模新凯恩斯主义DSGE模型来构建SLMI。该模型受到一个综合数据集的约束，该数据集不仅包括劳动力市场变量，还涵盖其他宏观经济总量。SLMI通过主成分分析综合了模型隐含的多个劳动力市场维度缺口。我们表明，GDP增长和通胀提供了超出劳动力市场变量本身所包含的大量关于劳动力市场松弛的信息，从而验证了我们多变量结构性方法的有效性。与其他替代指标相比，SLMI往往能在衰退初期更早发出状况恶化的预警，但在扩张期间恢复则更为缓慢。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Isabel Cairó , Hess Chung , Francesco Ferrante , Cristina Fuentes-Albero , Camilo Morales-Jimenez , and Damjan Pfajfar Labor market variables frequently send conflicting signals about the degree of slack or tightness, often complicating policy assessments at critical junctures. This paper develops a Structural Labor Market Indicator (SLMI) for the U.S. economy that addresses the limitations of existing univariate measures or atheoretical statistical approaches that synthesize multiple labor market indicators but cannot distinguish between supply and demand driving forces. We construct the SLMI using a medium scale New Keynesian DSGE model featuring search and matching frictions, endogenous labor force participation, and variable hours. The model is disciplined by a comprehensive dataset that includes not only labor market variables but also other macroeconomic aggregates. The SLMI synthesizes model-implied gaps across multiple labor market dimensions using principal component analysis. We show that GDP growth and inflation provide substantial information about labor market slack beyond what labor market variables contain, validating our multi-variable structural approach. Relative to alternative measures, the SLMI often provides earlier warnings of deteriorating conditions at recession onset but recovers more gradually during expansions.

</details>
