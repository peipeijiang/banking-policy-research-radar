# The Role of Inflation Targeting in Placement of Household Savings in Nominal Contracts

[查看原文](https://doi.org/10.18288/1994-5124-2026-5-142-173)

## 一句话结论

> 论文分析俄罗斯家庭大量持有名义卢布资产面临的通胀风险，论证通胀目标制（尤其2015年引入后）是维持储蓄购买力的关键制度，并主张对一次性通胀冲击应提高政策利率以防储蓄购买力被累积侵蚀。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

俄罗斯家庭的一个显著特征是，其金融资产中有很大比例以名义卢布合约（或资产）的形式持有，即名义支付固定为卢布的合约：银行存款、债券以及现金。以此类资产持有的储蓄，其购买力面临通货膨胀风险。作者得出若干关键结论和政策建议，旨在维持家庭储蓄的购买力。第一，鉴于通胀冲击幅度大且历史上频繁出现不对称性（向上偏倚），8.5%的名义利率可被视为为长期储蓄提供充分激励的最低长期门槛。第二，通胀目标制构成了将储蓄保持在名义合约中的重要激励组成部分。俄罗斯于2015年引入通胀目标制，使得系统性对抗累积储蓄购买力长期下降成为可能。第三，鉴于长期宏观经济稳定取决于维持储蓄的购买力，所提出的通胀目标制框架意味着，无论通胀预期是否得到良好锚定，政策利率都应对一次性通胀冲击作出上调反应。允许货币政策适应暂时性价格水平变化的通胀目标制方法，可能导致储蓄购买力遭受永久性（累积性）侵蚀。本文的结论是，支撑家庭储蓄的关键因素并非通胀目标制本身，而是维护俄罗斯在过去十年中已确立的通胀目标制方法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

One distinctive feature of Russian households is that they hold a large proportion of their financial assets in nominal ruble contracts (or assets), that is, contracts whose nominal payment is fixed in rubles: bank deposits and bonds as well as cash. The purchasing power of savings held in such assets is subject to inflation risk. The author arrives at several key conclusions and policy recommendations aimed at maintaining the purchasing power of household savings. First, a nominal interest rate of 8.5% may be regarded as the minimum long-term threshold that provides sufficient incentives for long-term saving given large and historically frequent asymmetric (upward biased) inflationary shocks. Second, inflation targeting constitutes an important part of the incentive for keeping savings in nominal contracts. The introduction of inflation targeting in Russia in 2015 made it possible to systematically counteract the long-standing decline in the purchasing power of accumulated savings. Third, insofar as long-term macroeconomic stability depends on preserving the purchasing power of savings, the proposed inflation targeting regime entails that the policy rate should be increased in response to one-off inflationary shocks whether or not inflation expectations are well anchored. An inflation targeting approach which allows monetary policy to accommodate temporary price-level changes may cause permanent (cumulative) erosion of the purchasing power of savings. The paper concludes that the key factor that would support household savings is not inflation targeting per se, but rather the preservation of the inflation targeting approach that has been established in Russia over the past decade.
