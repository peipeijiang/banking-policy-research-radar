# The Fragility of Social Learning with Noisy Messages

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18283v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究在噪声中继下社会学习的脆弱性，发现即使有大量独立信源，对突变率的微小不确定性也会使长链学习失败。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察了当信息从原始来源经过噪声中继后才到达个体时，个体如何学习。接收者能够学习，当且仅当他们能够获取足够多的噪声中继链，并且完全理解噪声过程。然而，即使对消息突变率存在轻微的不确定性，也会使得从长链条中学习变得不可能，无论获取多少独立来源。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We examine how agents learn when information from original sources only reaches them after noisy relay. A receiver learns if and only if they have access to sufficiently many chains of noisy relay and they perfectly understand the noise process. However, even slight uncertainty over message mutation rates makes learning from long chains impossible, no matter how many independent sources are accessed.
