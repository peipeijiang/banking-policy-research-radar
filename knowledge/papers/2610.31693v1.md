# When AI Gets Cheaper, Do Prices Follow? Information Delays and Algorithmic Pricing

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31693v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过模拟双寡头市场与Q学习实验，检验AI运行成本下降是否传导至更低价格，以及信息延迟和调价频率对消费者剩余的影响，发现成本节省未必完全转嫁给消费者。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察运行AI模型的成本降低是否会导致价格下降，以及观察竞争对手价格的延迟和对价格变化的限制是否会影响消费者获益的程度。我们研究一个模拟市场，其中有两个差异化卖家、logit需求（logit demand）和表格型Q学习（tabular Q learning）。一个析因实验改变了竞争对手价格信息的时效、卖家修改价格的频率、生产成本是否下降，以及企业质量和成本的差异。我们在16种条件中的每一种下，对基于60个配对市场种子训练的策略进行评估，共960次训练运行，并测量学习结束后的预期消费者剩余。
  在企业和成本相同且成本下降的主要设定中，信息延迟与重新定价频率对消费者剩余的估计交互作用为-0.00025标准化单位，95%配对种子自助法区间为[-0.0402, 0.0393]。该区间包含零，检验该交互作用是否随成本 regime 或企业间差异而变化的区间也同样包含零。在最短信息延迟且能够每期修改价格的条件下，两家企业的平均降价幅度分别相当于所施加成本削减的55.6%和44.0%。在所有16种条件下，学习策略下的平均消费者剩余均低于相应的静态网格纳什基准（static grid Nash benchmark）。
  研究结果表明，模拟的成本节约未必会完全传递给买方。这些结果并未确立合谋，也未描述实际AI提供商的定价行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We examine whether lower costs of running AI models lead to lower prices, and whether delays in observing competitors' prices and limits on price changes affect how much consumers benefit. We study a simulated market with two differentiated sellers, logit demand, and tabular Q learning. A factorial experiment varies the age of rival price information, how often sellers can revise prices, whether production costs fall, and differences in firm quality and costs. We evaluate policies trained on 60 paired market seeds in each of 16 conditions, for 960 training runs, and measure expected consumer surplus after learning ends.
  In the main setting with identical firms and falling costs, the estimated interaction between information delay and repricing frequency on consumer surplus is -0.00025 normalized units, with a 95% paired seed bootstrap interval of [-0.0402, 0.0393]. The interval includes zero, as do intervals for tests of whether the interaction changes with the cost regime or differences between firms. With the shortest information delay and the ability to revise prices every period, average price reductions equal 55.6% and 44.0% of the imposed cost reduction for the two firms. Mean consumer surplus under the learned policies is below the corresponding static grid Nash benchmark in all 16 conditions.
  The findings show that simulated cost savings need not be fully passed on to buyers. They do not establish collusion or describe pricing by actual AI providers.
