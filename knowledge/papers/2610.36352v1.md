# Many Hands Make Light Work: Long-Term Effect Inference for the Experiment Reservoir

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

替代指标方法（surrogate index method）是利用短期结果估计设计实验中长期效应的现代标准方法。该方法依赖于替代性假设和可比性假设，而这些假设在实际中可能被违反，从而导致估计偏差。我们提出了一种名为实验储备替代（Experiment Reservoir Surrogate, ERS）的新方法，该方法利用大规模实验平台中常见的历史长期实验储备。ERS通过直接预测长期平均处理效应来避免替代性假设，并通过匹配相似的历史实验来放宽可比性假设，而不要求储备中的每个实验都与目标实验可比。此外，ERS具有内存高效的特点，因为它仅需要每个时间段的聚合数据，而非原始个体层面数据。我们从理论上证明了ERS效率的提升，并在DoorDash在线平台的实验储备上实证展示了其改进效果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The surrogate index method is the modern standard for estimation of long-term effects in a designed experiment using only short-term outcomes. This method relies on the surrogacy and comparability assumptions which may be violated in practice leading to estimation bias. We propose a new method called Experiment Reservoir Surrogate (ERS) which leverages a reservoir of historical long-term experiments that is common in large-scale experimentation platforms. ERS avoids the surrogacy assumption by predicting the long-term average treatment effect directly, and relaxes the comparability assumption by matching to similar historical experiments, without requiring every experiment in the reservoir to be comparable to the target experiment. Moreover, ERS is memory-efficient, as it only requires aggregate data for each time period, instead of raw individual-level data. We theoretically justify the improved efficiency of ERS and demonstrate the improvement empirically on a reservoir of experiments from the online platform DoorDash.
