<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了在共同预期贴现预算约束下，跨异质性垄断市场的最优投资补贴设计。每个生产者面临一个规则的一维需求扩散过程，并通过一个奇异随机控制问题选择不可逆的产能扩张。监管者对加权消费者剩余、生产者利润以及装机容量带来的额外收益进行估值，同时考虑筹集公共资金的超额成本。我们通过自由边界刻画生产者的最优投资响应，并将监管者的随机优化问题重新表述为关于投资边界的确定性优化问题。随后，一个积分表示恢复了实现所得产能政策的最优补贴安排。在扩散过程的温和条件下，我们建立了最优安排的存在性以及所诱导投资政策的唯一性。最后，我们刻画了补贴安排并提供了数值示例。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the optimal design of investment subsidies across heterogeneous monopolistic markets subject to a common expected discounted budget. Each producer faces a regular one-dimensional demand diffusion and chooses irreversible capacity expansion through a singular stochastic control problem. The regulator values weighted consumer surplus, producer profits, and an additional benefit from installed capacity, while accounting for the excess cost of raising public funds. We characterize producers' optimal investment responses through free boundaries and reformulate the regulator's stochastic optimization problem as a deterministic optimization over investment boundaries. An integral representation then recovers the optimal subsidy schedules that implement the resulting capacity policies. Under mild conditions on the diffusions, we establish existence of optimal schedules and uniqueness of the induced investment policies. Finally, we characterize the subsidy schedule and provide numerical illustrations.
