<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

面板局部投影（panel local projections）在政策分析中被广泛用于估计脉冲响应。本文考察了面板局部投影中固定效应估计量及各种偏差校正方法的性质，重点关注带有自回归控制变量的情形。我们在允许目标变量和控制变量均存在持续性的条件下，分析了这些估计量的偏差与方差。理论结果表明，半面板刀切法（half-panel jackknife）在平稳设定之外也具有理想的偏差缩减性质。对于实证研究者，我们建议使用经偏差校正调整的标准误，以考虑与半面板刀切估计量相关的抽样不确定性，无论数据的时间序列维度如何。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Panel local projections are widely used to estimate impulse responses in policy analysis. This paper examines the properties of the fixed-effect estimator and various bias-correction methods in panel local projections, focusing on settings with autoregressive control variables. We analyze the bias and variance of these estimators while allowing for persistence in both the target and control variables. Our theoretical findings indicate that the half-panel jackknife has desirable bias-reducing properties beyond the stationary setup. For empirical researchers, we recommend using bias-correction-adjusted standard errors to account for the sampling uncertainty associated with the half-panel jackknife estimator, regardless of the time-series dimension of the data.
