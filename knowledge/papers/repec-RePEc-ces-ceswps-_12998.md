<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究美国 inbound 航运路线中断的宏观经济与贸易政策含义。标准模型将其视为冰山成本冲击，从而将冲击本身与对冲击的响应混为一谈。利用卫星船舶追踪数据，我们构建了2016年至2025年所有驶往美国的集装箱船在航线层面的潜在运力与有效运力指标。近期中断中的利用率损失达到20至40个百分点，并且在港口拥堵变得可见之前数月就已开始。我们将这些指标嵌入一个一般均衡模型，其中企业重新配置一支共同船队，但并未内部化其造成的拥堵，并且定价高于边际成本，而进口商的采购则对航线盈利能力作出反应。中断所引发的重新配置因此具有一阶福利效应，而航线的 Domar 权重并不是其福利成本的充分统计量。2021年西海岸危机和2023—2024年红海袭击分别造成产出的0.69%和0.35%的成本。对红海航运的海军保护以0.02%的财政成本产生了0.04—0.08%产出的收益。关税会缓解其征税航线的拥堵，从而抵消甚至逆转其常规福利成本。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the macroeconomic and trade-policy implications of disruptions to U.S.-bound shipping routes. Standard models treat them as iceberg-cost shocks, conflating the shock with the response to it. Using satellite vessel-tracking data, we construct route-level measures of potential and effective capacity for all U.S.-bound container ships from 2016 to 2025. Uti-lization losses in recent disruptions ran 20 to 40 percentage points, and began months before port congestion became visible. We embed these measures in a general equilibrium model in which firms reallocate a common fleet without internalizing the congestion they create and price above marginal cost, while importers’ sourcing responds to route profitability. The reallocation triggered by a disruption then has first-order welfare effects, and the route’s Do-mar weight is not a sufficient statistic for its welfare cost. The 2021 West Coast crisis and the 2023–2024 Red Sea attacks cost 0.69% and 0.35% of output. Naval protection of Red Sea shipping generated benefits of 0.04–0.08% of output at a fiscal cost of 0.02%. Tariffs decongest the routes they tax, offsetting or even reversing their conventional welfare cost.
