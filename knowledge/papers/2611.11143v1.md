<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一个高效且稳定的拍卖可能使卖方无法分享剩余。我们研究如何在尊重联盟稳定性的前提下，使组合拍卖的收益在卖方和买方之间尽可能平等地分配。我们证明，当买方为替代品时，核心由对买方收益的个体上限所刻画，并包含一个唯一的洛伦兹占优收益向量。每个买方获得以边际贡献为上限的卖方收益。因此，卖方至少获得总剩余中相等的份额，同时不损害效率或稳定性。随后，我们构建了一个完全信息议价博弈，其中卖方提出剩余分配方案，拒绝的买方获得退出支付。在没有贴现的情况下，该博弈在纯策略子博弈完美均衡中完全实施洛伦兹占优向量：均衡存在，且每一个此类均衡都恰好产生这一分配。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

An efficient and stable auction can leave the seller with no share of the surplus. We study how the gains from a combinatorial auction can be shared as equally as possible between the seller and buyers while respecting coalitional stability. We show that, when buyers are substitutes, the core is described by individual caps on the buyers' payoffs and contains a unique Lorenz-dominant payoff vector. Each buyer receives the seller's payoff capped at her marginal contribution. The seller consequently receives at least an equal share of the total surplus, without compromising efficiency or stability. We then construct a complete-information bargaining game in which the seller proposes a division of the surplus and rejecting buyers receive exit payments. Without discounting, the game fully implements the Lorenz-dominant vector in pure-strategy subgame-perfect equilibrium: an equilibrium exists, and every such equilibrium yields precisely this division.

</details>
