# Effort without Evidence

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03719v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究隐藏努力如何影响公共证据和决策者间的沟通，发现动机过强会导致信息无法识别，并探讨了纠正性支付的设计。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

行动不仅决定收益，还决定能够学到什么。我研究在隐藏努力支配公共证据、且发送者在低估努力成本的情况下激励其继任者时，连续决策者之间的沟通。常见的发送者排序可能迫使每一条信息都落入贝叶斯不可避免的不可识别区域。如果实验是有成本的，强烈的激励可能使说服性鼓励变得不可信，并在混杂失败后导致不作为。如果最大努力在技术上已达到饱和，同样的偏差可能在糟糕的混合历史之后产生普遍的过度努力，而这同样不具信息量。在这些节点上，崩溃是单调的：一旦强烈的激励消除了可识别的行动，强化激励无法恢复信息。而适度的激励则能保留因果区分。在一个阈值应用中，更强的激励提高了可观测的成功率，却降低了福利，因为社会未能认识到较少的努力就足够了。Kullback-Leibler分解表明，在进入固定的递归不可识别区域后，认证本身不再增加任何信息。认证是否能阻止进入取决于归因与选择的对齐：认证诱导的后验是否保留了可识别的行动？矫正性行动支付在不同技术下符号相反——当代理人做得太少时补贴努力，但当最大努力饱和时补贴克制。激励选择的是代价高昂的行动，而非信息丰富的行动。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Actions determine not only payoffs but what can be learned. I study communication across successive decision makers when hidden effort governs public evidence and a sender motivates her successor while underweighting effort cost. Common sender rankings can force every message into a Bayes-unavoidable nonidentifying region. If experimentation is costly, strong motivation can make persuasive encouragement incredible and produce inactivity after a confounded failure. If maximal effort is technologically saturated, the same bias can produce universal overexertion after a bad pooled history, which is equally uninformative. At such nodes collapse is monotone: once strong motivation eliminates the identifying action, strengthening it cannot restore information. Moderate motivation instead preserves causal distinctions. In a threshold application, stronger motivation raises measured success yet lowers welfare because society fails to learn that less effort would suffice. A Kullback--Leibler decomposition shows that certification directly adds no information after entry into a fixed recursively nonidentifying region. Whether it prevents entry depends on attribution--selection alignment: do certificate-induced posteriors preserve the identifying action? The corrective action payment changes sign across technologies---subsidize effort when agents do too little, but subsidize restraint when maximal effort is saturated. Motivation selects the costly action, not the informative one.

</details>
