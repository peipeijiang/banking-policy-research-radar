# Resource Allocation with Midterm Review

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15786v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文研究委托人在两期项目中利用中期评审信号进行资源分配的最优机制，发现最优机制会将评审信息前置用于激励真实报告，导致高质量项目初始获得更多资金但坏评审后削减更大。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

委托人分两个阶段资助代理人的项目，中期审查提供关于项目质量的公开信号。代理人私下知道项目质量，并且总是偏好更多资金，而委托人希望使资金与质量相匹配。一个自然的基准是仅在审查结果到来之后才使用它，以更新信念并改进第二阶段的配置。最优机制则相反，将审查的信息价值前置：它还将审查用作激励工具，以在事前诱导真实报告，从而严格改善委托人在第一阶段的预期收益。在最优机制中，较高质量的项目最初获得更多资金，但在差评后面临更大的削减，最终获得的资金少于较低质量的项目。好评会增加资金，但由此产生的配置未必保持项目最初的排名，并且最终会随质量下降而下降。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A principal funds an agent's project over two periods, with a midterm review providing a public signal about project quality. The agent privately knows the project's quality and always prefers more funding, while the principal wants to match funding to quality. A natural benchmark uses the review only after it arrives, to update beliefs and improve the second-period allocation. The optimal mechanism instead frontloads the review's information value: it also uses the review as an incentive device to elicit truthful reporting up front, thereby strictly improving the principal's first-period expected payoff. In the optimal mechanism, higher-quality projects receive more funding initially but face larger cuts after a bad review, leaving them with less funding than lower-quality projects. A good review increases funding, but the resulting allocation need not preserve the initial ranking of projects and eventually declines with quality.

</details>
