<details open>
<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关于具有有限p阶矩的律的每一个统计量，若其在独立风险间具有可加性，且在均值保留展形下单调，则当p严格小于2时，该统计量仅依赖于均值的某个可加函数；当p不小于2时，仅依赖于这样的函数以及方差的某个非负倍数。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Every statistic on laws with finite pth moment that is additive across independent risks and monotone in mean-preserving spreads depends only on an additive function of the mean when p is strictly less than 2, and only on such a function and a nonnegative multiple of the variance when p is no less than 2.

</details>
