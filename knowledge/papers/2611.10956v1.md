# Risk-Sensitive Reinforcement Learning for an Options Market Maker: An Empirical Study with Real S&P 500 Options

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10956v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文构建基于1996-2017年真实S&P 500期权数据的经验回放环境，训练分布式TD3强化学习做市商对冲代理，并在2018-2023年样本外检验其相对delta对冲和模拟环境训练代理的尾部风险表现，发现经验训练可将CVaR尾部损失降低3.1%（波动下跌市达8.9%），但在上涨市反转为7-10%的成本，且策略呈现状态依赖的净delta倾斜。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

期权做市商吸收客户订单流，并必须在交易摩擦下对冲由此产生的库存风险。我们研究在历史市场数据上训练的期权对冲强化学习（RL）智能体在尾部风险保护方面是否优于三种替代方案：（i）传统delta对冲，（ii）简单的对冲比率规则，（iii）在模拟市场中训练的同一智能体。大多数RL对冲智能体在模型生成的市场中训练，这些市场无法捕捉真实数据中存在的崩盘和波动率状态。我们引入一个使用1996年至2017年标普500期权数据的经验回放环境，并与匹配的几何布朗运动（GBM）环境、随机波动率（SABR）模拟器以及用Black-Scholes对历史路径进行估值的混合环境进行比较，所有这些环境共享相同的投资组合机制、随机到达、成本和测试期。我们训练一个分布式双延迟深度确定性策略梯度（TD3）智能体，并在2018-2023年期间进行样本外评估。在经验数据上训练的智能体相比delta对冲将条件风险价值（CVaR）尾部损失降低了3.1%，并优于训练期间选择的每一条对冲比率规则以及每一种替代训练环境。在波动和下跌市场中，降幅达到8.9%，但在上涨市场中逆转为7-10%的成本。经验训练诱导出一种状态依赖的净空头delta：该策略对负delta头寸对冲不足，在价格下跌后最为明显。这种倾斜在GBM训练中消失，在SABR或混合训练中较弱。这些发现表明，接触历史市场动态可以实质性地改变尾部风险对冲策略，尽管证据基于单一测试路径且优势具有状态条件性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Options market makers absorb client order flow and must hedge the resulting inventory risk under trading frictions. We study whether an option hedging reinforcement learning (RL) agent trained on historical market data outperforms three alternatives in tail-risk protection: (i) traditional delta hedging, (ii) simple hedge-ratio rules, and (iii) the same agent trained in simulated markets. Most RL hedging agents are trained in model-generated markets that do not capture the crashes and volatility regimes present in real data. We introduce an empirical replay environment using S&P 500 option data from 1996 to 2017 and compare against a matched geometric Brownian motion (GBM) environment, a stochastic-volatility (SABR) simulator, and a hybrid that values historical paths with Black-Scholes, all sharing the same portfolio mechanics, stochastic arrivals, costs, and test period. We train a distributional Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (TD3) agent and evaluate it out of sample over 2018-2023. The agent trained on empirical data reduces Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) tail loss by 3.1% compared to delta hedging and outperforms every hedge-ratio rule chosen during the training period and every alternative training environment. The reduction reaches 8.9% in volatile and falling markets, but reverses to a cost of 7-10% in rising markets. Empirical training induces a state-dependent short net delta: the policy under-hedges negative-delta books, most strongly after prices have fallen. This tilt disappears with GBM training and is weaker with SABR or hybrid training. These findings suggest that exposure to historical market dynamics can materially change a tail-risk hedging policy, although the evidence rests on a single test path and the edge is regime-conditional.
