<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出网络自适应市场假说（Network Adaptive Market Hypothesis, NAMH），这是自适应市场框架的一种新颖拓展，将市场效率概念化为一种依赖于网络均衡的状态，该均衡源于长记忆可预测性和跨市场信息传导。利用2001—2026年G20股票指数和五种商品期货数据，我们识别出日本是核心信息枢纽，而美国主要充当信息广播者。与传统预期相反，系统性危机并未表现出更高的网络谱半径，这挑战了危机相关脆弱性必然通过增强网络放大效应来体现的假设。相反，危机相关的谱贡献主要由澳大利亚和美国主导。重要的是，方差分解关联性与网络模块性的关联比与谱半径的关联更为密切，危机时期的增长主要由跨集群溢出驱动。值得注意的是，与地理和资产类别相比，基于效率的市场分组对网络关联性的解释仅边际显著，而中等效率层级始终表现出最强的特质行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose the Network Adaptive Market Hypothesis (NAMH), a novel extension of the adaptive markets framework that conceptualizes market efficiency as a network-dependent equilibrium emerging from long-memory predictability and cross-market information transmission. Using G20 equity indices and five commodity futures over 2001-2026, we identify Japan as the central information hub, while the United States primarily acts as an information broadcaster. Contrary to conventional expectations, systemic crises do not exhibit higher network spectral radii, challenging the assumption that crisis-related fragility necessarily manifests through increased network amplification. Instead, crisis-related spectral contributions are dominated by Australia and the United States. Importantly, variance-decomposition connectedness is more closely associated with network modularity than with spectral radius, with crisis-period increases driven primarily by cross-cluster spillovers. Notably, efficiency-based market groupings explain network connectedness only marginally compared with geography and asset class, while the middle efficiency tier consistently exhibits the greatest idiosyncratic behaviour.
