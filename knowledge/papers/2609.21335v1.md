<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多分配规则优先考虑满足特定优惠条件的申请人，包括残疾、无家可归和退伍军人身份。由于这些条件无法直接观测，按特征对申请人进行排序的规则必须首先确定所提交的证明文件能够证明什么。本文研究了具有硬性但部分证据的分配问题：一份残疾证明可以证明残疾，但无法证明不存在导致不合格的租赁记录。当且仅当每一位意向申请人都拥有一份可行的证明文件，能够排除所有非目标申请人时，该主张才是可证明的。链式规则——评分、字典序和标量排名——要求对每一个优先级截断点进行认证，而保留规则要求对每一个资格类别进行认证。因此，分配规则选择了需要被认证的主张族。当认证失败时，标准修复方法将不支持链式优先级的申请人降级，并删除不支持的保留标签。非空保留资格类别可以表示为单一链的上轮廓，当且仅当它们是嵌套的。本文以一个住房申请案例说明了这些后果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many allocation rules give priority to applicants satisfying favored conditions, including disability, homelessness, and veteran status. Because these conditions are not directly observed, a rule ranking applicants by characteristics must first determine what submitted certificates prove. This paper studies allocation with hard but partial evidence: a disability document may prove disability without proving the absence of a disqualifying tenancy record. A claim is certifiable exactly when every intended claimant has a feasible certificate excluding all outsiders. Chain rules---scores, lexicographic orders, and scalar rankings---require certification of every priority cutoff, whereas reserve rules require certification of each eligibility class. Allocation rules thus select the claim family to be certified. When certification fails, canonical repairs demote unsupported chain priorities and delete unsupported reserve labels. Nonempty reserve eligibility classes can be represented as upper contours of a single chain if and only if they are nested. A housing application illustrates the consequences.
