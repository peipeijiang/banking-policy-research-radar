<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

财务杠杆代表了一项关键的企业融资决策，因为债务既能提升股东回报，同时也增加了财务风险，尤其是在宏观经济和金融状况波动较大的新兴经济体中。本研究考察了七个新兴经济体（巴基斯坦、印度、孟加拉国、斯里兰卡、马来西亚、土耳其和埃及）非金融企业的财务杠杆与股票市场回报之间的关系。分析采用了2013年至2022年间55家上市非金融企业的平衡面板数据。财务杠杆通过债务权益比（debt-to-equity ratio）、债务资产比（debt-to-assets ratio）和利息保障倍数（interest coverage ratio）来衡量，同时将资产回报率（return on assets）、每股收益（earnings per share）和企业规模作为企业层面的控制变量。经济增长、政策利率、通货膨胀和汇率也被纳入模型，以考量宏观经济因素对股票表现的影响。实证研究结果显示，债务权益比和债务资产比对股票市场回报均具有正向且统计显著的影响，表明在控制企业特征和宏观经济因素后，较高的债务使用与较高的股东回报相关联。相比之下，利息保障倍数对股票回报未表现出统计显著的独立影响。盈利能力持续改善股票市场表现，而每股收益和企业规模的影响则因模型设定不同而有所差异。在宏观经济因素中，经济增长对股票回报具有正向影响，而较高的政策利率则产生显著的负向效应。通货膨胀与股票回报呈正相关，而汇率变动在统计上不显著。国家和行业层面的比较进一步揭示了杠杆结构、盈利能力、偿债能力和股票表现方面存在显著的异质性。本研究通过将多种杠杆度量指标、基于市场的绩效指标、企业特征和宏观经济条件整合在一个统一的面板框架内，为新兴市场公司金融文献作出了贡献，为不同新兴经济体之间杠杆与股票回报之间的关系提供了更广泛的证据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Financial leverage represents a critical corporate financing decision because debt can enhance shareholder returns while simultaneously increasing financial exposure, particularly in emerging economies characterized by volatile macroeconomic and financial conditions. This study investigates the relationship between financial leverage and stock market returns among non-financial firms across seven emerging economies: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Turkey, and Egypt. The analysis employs a balanced panel of 55 listed non-financial firms observed from 2013 to 2022. Financial leverage is measured through the debt-to-equity ratio, debt-to-assets ratio, and interest coverage ratio, while return on assets, earnings per share, and firm size are incorporated as firm-specific controls. Economic growth, policy interest rate, inflation, and exchange rate are included to account for macroeconomic influences on equity performance. The empirical findings reveal that both the debt-to-equity ratio and debt-to-assets ratio have positive and statistically significant effects on stock market returns, indicating that greater debt utilization is associated with higher shareholder returns after controlling for firm and macroeconomic characteristics. In contrast, interest coverage does not exert a statistically significant independent effect on stock returns. Profitability consistently improves stock market performance, whereas the influence of earnings per share and firm size varies across model specifications. Among macroeconomic factors, economic growth positively affects stock returns, while higher policy interest rates exert a significant negative effect. Inflation displays a positive association with stock returns, whereas exchange-rate movements remain statistically insignificant. Country- and sector-level comparisons further reveal considerable heterogeneity in leverage structures, profitability, debt-servicing capacity, and equity performance. The study contributes to emerging-market corporate finance literature by combining multiple leverage measures, market-based performance, firm characteristics, and macroeconomic conditions within a unified cross-country panel framework, providing broader evidence on the leverage–stock return relationship across heterogeneous emerging economies.
