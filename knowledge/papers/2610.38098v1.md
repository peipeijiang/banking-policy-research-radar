<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我证明，两层次嵌套logit需求模型恰好源于一个序贯选择过程，其中群体、子群体和产品口味冲击逐阶段揭示。该结果可推广至任意层数，并对任何小于1的嵌套参数成立，无需随机效用最大化所要求的界和排序条件。因此，应用研究中因违反这些界而被丢弃的估计结果与序贯选择是一致的。标准的线性反演和福利公式仍然有效。即使需求为单单位，向一个嵌套中添加产品也可能提高该嵌套中另一产品的份额。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I show that the two-level nested logit demand model arises exactly from a sequential choice process in which group, subgroup, and product taste shocks are revealed stage by stage. The result extends to any number of levels and holds for any nesting parameters below one, without the bounds and ordering that random utility maximization requires. Estimates that applied work discards for violating these bounds are therefore consistent with sequential choice. The standard linear inversion and welfare formula remain valid. Adding a product to a nest can raise another product's share in that nest, even with single-unit demand.

</details>
