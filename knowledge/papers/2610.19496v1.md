# Who Keeps a Bubble Alive? Skin in the Game and the Survival of Rational Bubbles

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究保留所有权如何支撑一种不支付股息、仅用于转售的资产的价值。发起人选择隐藏的维护水平，该维护决定市场存续，承担其全部成本，并且仅获取其保留代币所获得的收益。正稳态均衡存在，当且仅当保留比例达到一个内生最低要求。在额外的曲率限制下，高于该阈值的保留比例支撑两个正稳态均衡。在存续条件下，低于上均衡的价格会收敛到下均衡，而上价格是最大可行的初始估值。更高的保留比例扩大了可行价格区间。可信的消息表明未来保留比例将低于最低要求时，会通过逆向拆解导致立即崩溃。该模型识别出一条治理渠道，通过该渠道，内部人解锁可以在不改变代币总供给或基本面现金流的情况下影响估值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how retained ownership supports the value of an asset that pays no dividends and is held only for resale. A sponsor chooses hidden maintenance that determines market survival, bears its full cost, and captures only the benefit accruing to its retained tokens. A positive stationary equilibrium exists if and only if retention meets an endogenous minimum. Under additional curvature restrictions, retention above this threshold supports two positive stationary equilibria. Conditional on survival, prices below the upper equilibrium converge to the lower one while the upper price is the largest feasible initial valuation. Greater retention expands the feasible price interval. Credible news that future retention will be below the minimum causes an immediate collapse through backward unraveling. The model identifies a governance channel through which insider unlocks can affect valuation without changing total token supply or fundamental cash flows.
