<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

增长奖励统一性。它也可能摧毁一个成功社会赖以识别自身失败的那种实践。我研究了一个信用经济，其中一种受保障的真实合约在主流通货网络之外结算，与之形成一种标签化对照，并训练那些维持该替代方案可用性的人们。这种对照告知社会应在多大程度上修订一个可能不健全的标准，因此证据具有福利价值，而不仅仅是统计意义上的解释。高效储备的绝对规模在增长，但作为人口份额却在缩小。这产生了一种“总量—份额”楔子：证据变得更有价值，而能够保护其来源的群体在政治上却变得无足轻重。私人合作仅能提供有限的准备，而廉价的演练会取代真实的对照，除非诊断被明确地赋予价值。文化投资与政治生存相互强化，产生了暴露型均衡与保护型均衡。我解释了为何以份额为指数的保护最终会失效，刻画了一种能够随增长存续的以服务为指数的章程，并推导出采购、学徒支持与安全真实抵押品的最低成本组合，用以在取消后为替代方案提供资金。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Growth rewards uniformity. It can also destroy the practice by which a successful society would recognize its own failure. I study a credit economy in which a safeguarded real contract settles outside the dominant monetary network, creates a labelled comparison with it, and trains the people who keep that alternative usable. The comparison informs how far society should revise a possibly unsound standard, so evidence has a welfare value rather than merely a statistical interpretation. The efficient reserve grows in absolute size but vanishes as a population share. This creates a mass--share wedge: the evidence becomes more valuable while the constituency able to protect its source becomes politically negligible. Private cooperation supplies only bounded readiness, and cheap drills displace live comparisons unless diagnosis is valued explicitly. Cultural investment and political survival are mutually reinforcing, producing exposed and protected equilibria. I show why share-indexed protection eventually fails, characterize a service-indexed charter that can survive growth, and derive the least-cost combination of procurement, apprenticeship support, and safe-real collateral that funds replacement after cancellation.
