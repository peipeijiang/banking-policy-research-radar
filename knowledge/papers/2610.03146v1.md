<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究这样一种机制：其中代理人的偏好既取决于物品分配，也取决于第三方对私人信息的信念。一个机制决定了分配、转移支付，并向售后市场发送信号。我们比较两种信息环境：（i）私人转移支付，即售后市场观察分配和信号；（ii）公开转移支付，即转移支付可被公开观察。我们的主要结果表明，任何在隐藏转移支付下可实施的均衡结果，都可以通过公开转移支付任意逼近。因此，转移支付的可观察性至多产生消失性的信息扭曲，并有助于合理化一个常见假设，即转移支付对售后市场保持隐藏。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study mechanisms in which agents' preferences depend both on an object allocation and on a third party's beliefs about private information. A mechanism determines an allocation, transfers, and sends signals to the aftermarket. We compare two informational environments: (i) private transfers, where the aftermarket observes allocations and signals, and (ii) public transfers, where transfers are publicly observable. Our main result shows that any equilibrium outcome implementable with hidden transfers can be approximated arbitrarily well with public transfers. Thus, transfer observability generates at most a vanishing informational distortion and helps rationalize the common assumption that transfers remain hidden from aftermarkets.

</details>
