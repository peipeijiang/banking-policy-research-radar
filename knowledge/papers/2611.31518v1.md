<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

用外国AI代理替代本地劳动的企业，每付费工时产出更多，但国内收入却更少。我们在一个出口商和本地服务业均使用代理的小型开放经济中研究这一权衡。一旦工资触及保留底线，在本地服务业扩大代理使用会在国外成本不变的情况下降低国内收入。如果供应商获取足够多的节约份额，不变价格增加值和消费也会下降。出口商有一个抵消因素：采用代理可以扩大生产并赚取支付代理所需的外汇。这一差异相当大。以巴西账户为参照，供应商议价份额高于约0.15即足以使进一步的本地服务采用即使在国外成本可忽略时也降低增加值。可贸易品部门的相应阈值约为0.66。我们推导了维持收入和消费所需的各自出口增长要求，并用巴西和墨西哥的历史账户加以说明。更便宜的代理放松了消费要求；更低的出口价格则提高了这一要求。随着时间的推移，国内能力的下降还可能使回归本地生产变得更加昂贵，从而使供应商在续约时能够收取更高费用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Firms that replace local work with foreign AI agents can produce more per paid hour while generating less income at home. We study this trade-off in a small open economy where exporters and local services both use agents. Once wages reach a reservation floor, extending agent use in local services lowers domestic income at unchanged foreign costs. Value added at constant prices and consumption also fall if suppliers capture enough of the saving. Exporters have an offset: adopting agents can expand production and earn the foreign exchange needed to pay for them. The difference is substantial. Using Brazilian accounts as a reference, a supplier bargaining share above about 0.15 is sufficient to make further local-service adoption reduce value added even at negligible foreign costs. The corresponding threshold in tradables is about 0.66. We derive separate export-growth requirements for preserving income and consumption and illustrate them using historical accounts for Brazil and Mexico. Cheaper agents ease the consumption requirement; lower export prices raise it. Over time, declining domestic capabilities can also make returning to local production more expensive, allowing suppliers to charge more at renewal.

</details>
