<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在涉及两个以上状态的序贯观察学习中，相同的信息结构和偏好可以导致在某些全支撑先验下发生学习，而在另一些全支撑先验下出现羊群行为。这否证了Kartik、Lee、Liu和Rappoport（2024，工作论文版）的一个猜想。在反例中，级联区域具有非空内部，然而对于其外的一般全支撑先验，学习仍然发生。聚焦于信息和偏好，我们引入了学习的两个必要条件。可逃脱性（escapability）要求信号能够提供任意强的证据，证明任何给定行动在事后是次优的；成对可逃脱性是其成对变体。若可逃脱性不成立，则对某些初始先验会出现羊群行为；若成对可逃脱性不成立，则对每个全支撑先验都会出现羊群行为。对于一类具有区间最优性和单调似然比的环境，可逃脱性完全刻画了学习与羊群行为，且与全支撑初始先验无关。对于位置移动信号（location-shift signals），该刻画简化为成对可区分性（pairwise distinguishability），即无界信念（unbounded beliefs）（Smith and Sørensen, 2000）的推广。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In sequential observational learning with more than two states, the same information structure and preferences can yield learning for some full-support priors and herding for others. This disproves a conjecture of Kartik, Lee, Liu, and Rappoport (2024, working-paper version). In the counterexample, the cascade region has nonempty interior, yet learning occurs for generic full-support priors outside it. Focusing on information and preferences, we introduce two necessary conditions for learning. Escapability requires that signals can provide arbitrarily strong evidence that any given action is ex post suboptimal; pairwise escapability is its pairwise variant. If escapability fails, herding occurs for some initial prior; if pairwise escapability fails, it occurs for every full-support prior. For a class of environments with interval optimality and monotone likelihood ratios, escapability completely characterizes learning and herding independently of the full-support initial prior. With location-shift signals, this characterization reduces to pairwise distinguishability, a generalization of unbounded beliefs (Smith and Sørensen, 2000).

</details>
