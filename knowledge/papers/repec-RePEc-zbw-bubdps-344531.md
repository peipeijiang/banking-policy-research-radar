# Safe haven status and international shock transmission

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/zbw/bubdps/344531.html)

## 一句话结论

> 论文构建两国DSGE模型，研究避险地位从美国转向欧元区如何缓冲避险冲击对欧洲总需求和金融条件的影响，发现2025年4月后的冲击可能被欧元区避险地位显著缓冲，但在2008-09式较小规模避险转移中缓冲作用有限。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

如果避险地位从美国转移到欧元区，会产生哪些宏观金融影响？在一个两块DSGE模型（dynamic stochastic general equilibrium model，动态随机一般均衡模型）中，欧元区的避险地位会削弱避险资金流动冲击对欧洲国内总需求和金融状况的影响。2025年4月2日之后的冲击，可能因欧元区当时的避险地位而在欧元区得到相当程度的缓冲。然而，在一次国际避险资金流动规模相对较小的危机中——如2008-09年——欧洲避险地位对冲击的衰减作用将是有限的。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

What are the macro-financial implications if the safe haven status shifts from the US to the euro area? In a two-block DSGE model, a safe haven status of the euro area dampens the effects of flight-to-safety shocks on European domestic aggregate demand and financial conditions. The shock after April 2, 2025, may have been considerably cushioned in the euro area by its safe haven status at the time. However, in a crisis with a relatively smaller international flight to safety, as in 2008-09, the shock attenuation by a European safe haven status would be limited.

</details>
