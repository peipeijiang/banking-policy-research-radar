<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们将近期的空间计量经济学文献置于历史背景中，并描绘当前趋势，进行了一次非穷尽性综述。重点将主要放在方法论层面。从经典的空间自回归模型出发，我们涵盖其诸多扩展和更丰富的参数化形式。我们讨论空间面板数据模型以及非线性模型和非参数模型研究的进展。空间计量模型的设定检验——其根源可追溯至Davidson和MacKinnon（1981）的\(J\)检验——也在讨论范围之内，空间相关稳健推断同样如此。最后，我们综述一般极限理论、受限因变量模型、空间权重矩阵估计与内生性方面的进展，近期将网络形成与空间文献统一起来的尝试，以及若干其他重要议题。我们评估当前范式，并批判性地评价空间文献对应用实践的影响。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We conduct a non-exhaustive survey of the recent spatial econometrics literature by placing it in historical context and mapping current trends. The focus will be primarily on methodological aspects. Starting from the canonical spatial autoregressive model, we cover its many extensions and richer parameterizations. We discuss spatial panel data models and advancements in the study of nonlinear and nonparametric models. Specification testing for spatial econometric models, with its roots in the $J$-test of Davidson and MacKinnon (1981), is also covered, as is spatial correlation robust inference. Finally, we review advancements in general limit theory, limited dependent variable models, spatial weight matrix estimation and endogeneity, recent attempts at uniting the network formation and spatial literature, and sundry important topics. We take stock of the current paradigm and critically evaluate the impact of the spatial literature on applied practice.

</details>
