研究者经常通过聚合结果来估计对抽象概念（如“犯罪”或“财富”）的处理效应。首先，我记录了主成分分析（PCA）、逆方差矩阵（IVM）加权和标准化平均（SA）是常用方法，但存在意想不到的缺陷。PCA可能赋予负权重，不一定能最大化精度，依赖于任意的符号归一化，并且可能具有非标准渐近性。与SA不同，IVM可能赋予负权重，但两者都不一定能最大化精度。其次，我开发了一种统计决策理论方法，利用均方误差来选择非负权重，同时考虑结果质量。我重新审视了一项关于社交距离的研究。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Researchers often aggregate outcomes to estimate treatment effects on abstract concepts, such as "crime" or "wealth." First, I document that principal component analysis (PCA), inverse variance matrix (IVM) weighting, and standardized averaging (SA) are commonly used but have unexpected shortcomings. PCA can assign negative weights, need not maximize precision, depends on an arbitrary sign normalization, and can have nonstandard asymptotics. Unlike SA, IVM can assign negative weights, but neither necessarily maximizes precision. Second, I develop a statistical decision-theoretic approach that uses mean-squared error to choose nonnegative weights while accounting for outcome quality. I revisit a study on social distancing.
