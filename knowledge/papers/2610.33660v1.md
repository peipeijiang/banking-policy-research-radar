<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考虑连续时间动态离散博弈中均衡的多重性与唯一性，其中参与者按照独立的泊松过程依次行动。当存在多重均衡时，结构模型是不完整的：简化形式和反事实预测取决于均衡是如何被选择的。我们为这类博弈中马尔可夫完美均衡（Markov perfect equilibrium）的唯一性提供了闭式充分条件。这些条件是半参数的，并且可以从模型原语中加以验证。为了衡量这些条件的覆盖范围并更广泛地刻画多重均衡，我们对一个进入与退出的动态双寡头模型进行了大规模的均衡数值搜索。尽管在我们的广泛搜索中唯一性是常态，但我们发现多重均衡集中在具有经济相关性的区域：市场无法有利可图地维持两家企业的情形、企业非常耐心的情形，以及平衡战略互动与承诺的中间决策频率。当存在多重均衡时，我们通常发现两个稳定的非对称均衡和一个不稳定的对称均衡。蒙特卡洛模拟和反事实模拟表明，这种结构对实证研究具有重要意义。假设唯一性的全解估计量（full-solution estimator）可能收敛到不稳定均衡，即使数据是由稳定均衡生成的；而两步估计量（two-step estimator）则能恢复正确的均衡。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We consider multiplicity and uniqueness of equilibria in continuous-time dynamic discrete games where players move sequentially according to independent Poisson processes. With multiple equilibria, the structural model is incomplete: the reduced form and counterfactual predictions depend on how equilibria are selected. We provide closed-form sufficient conditions for uniqueness of Markov perfect equilibrium in this class of games. The conditions are semiparametric and can be verified from model primitives. To measure the coverage of the conditions and characterize multiplicity more broadly, we carry out a large-scale numerical search for equilibria of a dynamic duopoly model of entry and exit. Although uniqueness is the norm in our broad search, we find that multiplicity concentrates in economically relevant regions: where the market cannot profitably sustain both firms, where firms are very patient, and at intermediate decision frequencies that balance strategic interaction and commitment. When multiplicity is present, we typically find two stable asymmetric equilibria and one unstable symmetric equilibrium. Monte Carlo and counterfactual simulations show this structure matters for empirical work. A full-solution estimator that assumes uniqueness can converge to the unstable equilibrium, even when the data were generated by a stable one, while a two-step estimator recovers the correct equilibrium.
