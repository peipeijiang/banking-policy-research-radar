<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们基于洛伦兹曲线（Lorenz curve）的曲率构建了一个连续的不平等与社会分层指数族。我们研究了该指数族的不平等公理，发现仅当所谓“分层厌恶参数”$α$设为0时，这些公理才能被满足——在所有其他情况下，这些约束条件无法在严格意义上成立。当$α=0$时，该指数具有非常简洁的闭式表达式，且其数值易于近似。我们利用世界银行（World Bank）的不平等数据研究了该指数的取值，并观察了随着$α$的变化，广泛选取的国家之间的排名如何改变。我们还计算并分析了该指数与其他常用指数之间的斯皮尔曼（Spearman）和肯德尔（Kendall）秩相关矩阵。我们发现，曲率指数始终与对比指数存在系统性偏差，尽管基于消费的国家比基于收入的国家偏差程度更小。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We construct a continuous family of inequality and social stratification indices based on the curvature of the Lorenz curve. We study the inequality axioms of the family and find that they are satisfied only with the so-called stratification-aversion parameter $α$ set to 0 -- in all other cases, these constraints cannot be satisfied in a strict sense. With $α= 0$, the index has a very simple closed form and its value can be easily approximated. We study the values of the index on World Bank inequality data and see how the rankings across a wide selection of countries change as $α$ is varied. Spearman and Kendall rank-correlation matrices with other common indices are also computed and analyzed. We find the curvature index to deviate consistently from the comparison indices, albeit less so for consumption- than income-based countries.
