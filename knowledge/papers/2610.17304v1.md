<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

乘法背景风险模型（Multiplicative background risk models, MBRMs）为描述相依风险提供了一个易于处理的框架。我们通过结合Marshall--Olkin指数风险因子结构和加性伽马风险因子结构，构建了一类具有Pareto边缘分布的MBRMs，每种结构均允许纳入特质性和系统性背景风险因子。我们的主要结果建立了：在风险因子系统满足容许性条件的情况下，联合生存函数可用Pareto生存函数的有限次混合表示，其权重为背景风险因子所诱导排序的Dirichlet概率，且权重之和至多为1。这为此类MBRMs提供了一种新的解释，其中相依性通过Pareto生存函数及其Dirichlet权重来表达。由此得到的分布族涵盖了许多已知的多元Pareto构造，在边缘化下封闭，并可刻画独立性以及在非退化条件下刻画其奇异部分和绝对连续部分。该分布族具有关联性，其协方差分解将两种风险因子结构的贡献及其交互作用分离开来，且其联合尾部沿射线是正则变化的。渐近尾相依性的存在以及联合超越概率的衰减速率仅由伽马风险因子决定；指数风险因子仅影响尾部常数。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Multiplicative background risk models (MBRMs) provide a tractable framework for describing dependent risks. We construct a class of MBRMs with Pareto margins by combining Marshall--Olkin exponential and additive gamma risk factor structures, each allowed to incorporate idiosyncratic and systemic background risk factors. Our main result establishes, under an admissibility condition on the risk factor systems, a finite sub-mixture representation of the joint survival function in terms of Pareto survival functions, whose weights are Dirichlet probabilities of orderings induced by the background risk factors and sum to at most one. This yields a new interpretation of such MBRMs, in which dependence is expressed through Pareto survival functions and their Dirichlet weights. The resulting family encompasses many known multivariate Pareto constructions, is closed under marginalization, and admits characterizations of independence and, under a non-degeneracy condition, of its singular and absolutely continuous parts. It is associated, its covariance decomposition separates the contributions of the two risk factor structures and their interaction, and its joint tails are regularly varying along rays. The presence of asymptotic tail dependence and the decay rates of joint exceedance probabilities are governed by the gamma risk factors alone; the exponential risk factors affect only the tail constants.
