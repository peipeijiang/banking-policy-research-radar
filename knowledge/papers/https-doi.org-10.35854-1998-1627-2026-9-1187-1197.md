# Import restriction as a means of maintaining macroeconomic stability: Evidence from the Japanese experience of monetary and financial regulation

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

目的：以日本经济高速增长时期的发展为例，揭示进口限制通过调节进出口业务在维持国家货币汇率稳定中的作用。目标：分析维持金融稳定的替代措施，包括进口限制，该措施能够有效管理汇率；考察日本在长时期内将国家货币汇率维持在持续高位水平的经验，该经验已证明其可行性和有效性。方法：运用一般科学认知方法、比较法以及分析经济过程的制度方法，考察通过关键利率管理和使用进口限制机制来维持国家货币汇率稳定的国家宏观经济指标调控政策，包括20世纪下半叶日本宏观经济调控的案例。结果：本文将维持国家货币汇率稳定视为一项旨在对抗通货膨胀的措施，以及作为大幅提高关键利率的一种痛苦较小的替代方案。通过对俄罗斯和日本在确保宏观经济稳定问题上的方法进行研究和比较，得以识别两国在金融领域公共管理问题上的国家特殊性差异，并形成对有助于在长时期内维持可持续汇率进程取得最有利结果的因素的理解。在这些因素中，除了现代俄罗斯积极实施的关键利率上调政策外，本文所展示的日本在调节进出口业务方面相当成功的实践也占据重要位置。与此同时，进口遏制政策可以成为确保宏观经济稳定和维持可持续国家货币汇率的关键要素。结论：通过配额和其他关税及非关税限制措施遏制进口，有助于稳定国家货币汇率，即使在对外币需求增加和通胀预期上升趋势的条件下，也能防止国家货币贬值。与货币措施不同——在货币措施中，浮动国家货币汇率是平衡进出口流量的主要杠杆之一，同时又受到剧烈波动的影响——行政性进口遏制方法能够实现国家货币汇率的稳定，并有助于将通货膨胀控制在可控范围内。因此，便有可能维持较低的关键利率，使国家经济能够在积极利用现有信贷资源的情况下动态发展。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Aim. To reveal the role of import restriction in maintaining a stable national currency exchange rate through the regulation of export-import operations, using Japan’s development during a period of high economic growth as an example. Objectives. To analyze alternative measures for maintaining financial stability, including import restriction, which allows for effective management of the exchange rate; to examine the Japanese experience of maintaining the national currency exchange rate at a consistently high level over a long period, which has proven its viability and effectiveness. Methods. Using general scientific methods of cognition, comparison, and an institutional approach to the analysis of economic processes, the policy of state regulation of macroeconomic indicators through key rate management and the use of import restriction mechanisms to maintain a stable national currency exchange rate is examined, including the example of macroeconomic regulation in Japan in the second half of the 20th century. Results. Maintaining a stable national currency exchange rate is considered in the article as a measure aimed at combating inflation and as a less painful alternative to a strong increase in the key rate. The study and comparison of approaches to the problem of ensuring macroeconomic stability in Russia and Japan made it possible to identify differences in the national specifics of public administration problems in the financial sphere and to form an understanding of the factors contributing to the most favorable results of the process of maintaining a sustainable exchange rate over a long period. An important place among these factors, in addition to the policy of raising the key rate actively implemented in modern Russia, is occupied by the quite successful, as shown in the article, Japanese practice of regulating export-import operations. At the same time, a policy of import containment can become a key element in ensuring macroeconomic stability and maintaining a sustainable national currency exchange rate. Conclusions. Containing imports through quotas and other tariff and non-tariff restrictive measures contributes to stabilizing the national currency exchange rate and prevents depreciation of the national currency even under conditions of increased demand for foreign currency and a tendency toward rising inflation expectations. Unlike monetary measures, in which a floating national currency exchange rate serves as one of the main levers for balancing exportimport flows and at the same time is subject to sharp fluctuations, the methodology of administrative import containment makes it possible to achieve stability of the national currency exchange rate and helps keep inflation under control. As a result, it becomes possible to have a low key rate, allowing the country’s economy to develop dynamically with the active use of available credit resources
