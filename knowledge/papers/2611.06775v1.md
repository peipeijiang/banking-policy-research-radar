<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一个约束加权网络形成的一般框架，其中参与者在联盟互动中分配资源，同时面临可行性约束。更准确地说，我们允许任意参与者联盟被赋予连续权重，并将联盟容量约束和个体预算约束同时纳入模型。该框架通过将连续互动强度与内生可行性限制相结合，扩展了Bich和Morhaim的加权网络形成模型。在此设定下，我们引入了一种新的稳定性概念——称为联盟稳定性（coalitionwise stability）——将成对稳定性扩展到受约束的网络博弈。在收益函数满足标准连续性和分量拟凹性假设的条件下，我们建立了联盟稳定网络的存在性。随后，我们研究了受Selten提出的完美均衡概念以及Bich和Teteryatnikova为网络博弈开发的完美成对稳定性概念启发的稳定性精炼。可行性约束的存在从根本上改变了可行集的几何结构，使其不再具有乘积结构。因此，颤抖手类型的精炼变得更加微妙，因为某些互动的扰动可能使其他互动变得不可行。尽管如此，我们引入了Bich和Teteryatnikova所考虑的颤抖手完美稳定性和扰动完美稳定性概念的约束类比，并研究了它们的存在性及其相互关系。特别地，我们在某些分量单调性假设下证明了这些颤抖手表述和扰动表述之间的等价性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce a general framework of constrained weighted network formation in which agents allocate resources across coalitional interactions while facing feasibility constraints. More precisely, we allow arbitrary coalitions of agents to be assigned a continuous weight, and we incorporate both coalitional capacity constraints and individual budget constraints into the model. This framework extends the weighted network formation model of Bich and Morhaim by combining continuous interaction intensities with endogenous feasibility restrictions. Within this setting, we introduce a new notion of stability -- called coalitionwise stability -- extending pairwise stability to constrained network games. Under standard continuity and componentwise quasiconcavity assumptions on payoff functions, we establish the existence of coalitionwise stable networks. We then investigate refinements of stability inspired by the notion of perfect equilibrium introduced by Selten, and by the notion of perfect pairwise stability developed by Bich and Teteryatnikova for network games. The presence of feasibility constraints fundamentally alters the geometry of the feasible set, which no longer possesses a product structure. As a consequence, trembling-hand type refinements become substantially more delicate, since perturbations of some interactions may render other interactions infeasible. Nevertheless, we introduce constrained analogues of both the trembling-hand and perturbative notions of perfect stability considered by Bich and Teteryatnikova, and study their existence and their relationship. In particular, we show the equivalence between these trembling-hand and perturbative formulations under some componentwise monotonicity assumption.
