<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考虑相关矩阵的Box-Cox变换，$\Phi_\alpha(C)=\mathrm{vecl}\{(C^\alpha-I)/\alpha\}$，其雅可比为$J_\alpha$。该族在$\alpha=1$时包含原始相关系数，在$\alpha=0$时包含广义Fisher变换（GFT）$C\mapsto\mathrm{vecl}(\log C)$，在$\alpha=-1$时包含取负的非对角集中坐标。对于每个$\alpha$，逆问题都是一个严格凸规划，因此每个成员都是单射。该映射是满射到$\mathbb{R}^{d}$（$d=n(n-1)/2$）当且仅当$\alpha\le0$，从而$\alpha=0$定义了无限制参数化的边界。对于$\alpha>0$，像是有界的。条件数满足界$\kappa(J_\alpha)\le\kappa(C)^{|1-\alpha|}$，其中$\kappa(C)=\lambda_{\max}(C)/\lambda_{\min}(C)$，且在满射范围上指数在$\alpha=0$处最小。在高斯采样下（$n\geq3$），变换后样本相关矩阵的渐近协方差在独立性处对每个成员均为单位矩阵，但只有$\alpha=0$在一阶相关系数下保持该单位性，而在独立性处领先的联合三阶累积量仅在$\alpha=1/3$时消失。最后，将该变换应用于$C$的对角重缩放（使结果成为空心矩阵）为现有的相关参数化增添了一个新族。对于每个$\alpha$，该重缩放求解一个严格凸问题且逆变换是显式的，并且缩放坐标是无限制的恰好当$\alpha=0$时，此时它们恢复了已知的全局正态坐标（以独立性为中心），用于相关矩阵的有效信息度量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We consider Box-Cox transformations of correlation matrices, $Φ_α(C)=\mathrm{vecl}\{(C^α-I)/α\}$, with Jacobian $J_α$. The family nests the raw correlations at $α=1$, the generalised Fisher transformation (GFT), $C\mapsto\mathrm{vecl}(\log C)$, at $α=0$, and the negated off-diagonal concentration coordinates at $α=-1$. The inverse problem is a strictly convex program for every $α$, so every member is injective. The map is onto $\mathbb{R}^{d}$, $d=n(n-1)/2$, if and only if $α\le0$, such that $α=0$ defines the boundary for unrestricted parametrizations. For $α>0$ the image is bounded. The conditioning obeys the bound $κ(J_α)\leκ(C)^{|1-α|}$, where $κ(C)=λ_{\max}(C)/λ_{\min}(C)$, and on the surjective range the exponent is smallest at $α=0$. Under Gaussian sampling with $n\geq3$, the asymptotic covariance of the transformed sample correlations is the identity at independence for every member, but only $α=0$ retains the identity to first order in the correlations, whereas the leading joint third cumulant at independence vanishes only at $α=1/3$. Finally, applying the transformation to the diagonal rescaling of $C$ that makes the result hollow adds a new family to the existing correlation parametrizations. For every $α$ the rescaling solves a strictly convex problem and the inverse is explicit, and the scaled coordinates are unrestricted exactly when $α=0$, where they recover the known global normal coordinates, centred at independence, for the efficient information metric of the correlations.
