<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

标准多项式probit（MNP）模型假设潜效用分布具有对称性，这意味着选择概率对相同幅度的正负协变量变动会作出对称的反应。这一限制在实证选择情境中往往缺乏合理性，并可能导致误导性的弹性与替代预测。我们提出了一种偏态多项式probit（SMNP）模型，通过为潜效用设定多元偏态正态分布来捕捉非对称的选择反应。该模型保留了MNP框架下灵活的替代模式，引入了备选方案特定的偏度参数，并在偏度为零时嵌套标准MNP模型。引入偏度会带来识别与计算上的挑战，因为偏度参数与MNP的尺度归一化相互作用，并破坏了贝叶斯MNP估计中使用的条件高斯更新结构。我们通过协方差重新参数化来应对这些挑战，该参数化通过构造确保识别性和正定性，在识别参数空间上设定可解释的先验，并提出一种双重数据增广方案，从而得到吉布斯采样器内的Metropolis-Hastings算法。数值实验及消费者选择数据的应用表明，SMNP能够恢复非对称的选择反应，提高概率预测精度，并在价格弹性和替代模式上产生具有经济意义的差异。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Standard multinomial probit (MNP) models specify symmetric latent utility distributions, implying that choice probabilities respond symmetrically to positive and negative covariate shifts of the same magnitude. This restriction is often implausible in empirical choice settings and can lead to misleading elasticity and substitution predictions. We propose a skewed multinomial probit (SMNP) model that captures asymmetric choice responses by specifying a multivariate skew-normal distribution for the latent utilities. The model preserves the flexible substitution patterns of the MNP framework, introduces alternative-specific skewness parameters, and nests the standard MNP model when skewness is zero. Introducing skewness creates identification and computational challenges because the skewness parameters interact with the MNP scale normalization and disrupt the conditional Gaussian updating structure used in Bayesian MNP estimation. We address these challenges through a covariance reparameterization that enforces identification and positive definiteness by construction, interpretable priors on the identified parameter space, and a double data-augmentation scheme that yields a Metropolis-Hastings within Gibbs sampler. Numerical experiments and applications to consumer choice data show that SMNP recovers asymmetric choice responses, improves probabilistic prediction, and produces economically meaningful differences in price elasticities and substitution patterns.
