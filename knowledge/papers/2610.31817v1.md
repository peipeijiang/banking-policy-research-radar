# Dynamic Test Design

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31817v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究在代理人可花费成本操纵证据的动态环境中，无法承诺的委托人如何设计测试以决定雇佣、解雇或继续试用，发现均衡测试具有单边性（只产生通过或只产生失败），并刻画了延迟决策与即时误配置之间的权衡。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究在操纵威胁下的动态测试设计。在每一时刻，一个无法承诺未来行动的主体（principal）选择是否雇用代理人、解雇他，或通过设计测试继续进行成本高昂的试用。测试提供关于代理人持久类型的信息，该类型要么是好的，要么是坏的。在观察到测试选择后，坏类型可以付出高昂的努力来操纵证据并模仿好类型。阻止操纵施加了一个内生信息预算，限制了主体能够以多密集的程度进行测试。我们表明，除了在无消息时保持不变的信念之外，均衡测试是单边的：它们只产生通过，从而触发雇用；或只产生失败，从而触发解雇。均衡结果要么是在代价高昂的延迟之后做出正确的雇用和解雇决策，要么是冒着错配风险的即时决策。在承诺下，主体反而在整个试用期内保持测试不变，即使她几乎确定代理人的类型。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study dynamic test design under the threat of manipulation. At each instant, a principal who cannot commit to future actions chooses whether to hire an agent, fire him, or continue costly probation by designing a test. The test provides information about the agent's persistent type, which is either good or bad. After observing the test choice, the bad type can exert costly effort to manipulate the evidence and mimic the good type. Deterring manipulation imposes an endogenous information budget that limits how intensively the principal can test. We show that, except at beliefs that remain unchanged in the absence of news, equilibrium tests are one-sided: they generate only passes, which trigger hiring, or only failures, which trigger firing. Equilibrium outcomes feature either correct hiring and firing decisions after costly delay or immediate decisions that risk misallocation. With commitment, the principal instead keeps the test constant throughout probation, even when she is nearly certain of the agent's type.
