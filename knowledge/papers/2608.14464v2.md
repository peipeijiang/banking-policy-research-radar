<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文提出了一种度量彩票复杂性的指标方法。首先观察到退化彩票是最简单的选择备选项，因此彩票的复杂性通过其与最接近的退化彩票之间的距离来评估。等价地，当一张彩票难以用单一结果近似时，它就被视为复杂的。给定结果空间上的一个度量，我们所考虑的复杂性指标是彩票与其最佳退化代理之间的最小平均距离。本文为该表示提供了公理化基础，研究了其主要性质，并与其他复杂性度量进行了比较。随后，该指标通过一类复杂性调整的期望效用偏好被应用于风险下的选择。在此模型内，我们研究了复杂性如何影响风险态度以及与随机占优的一致性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper proposes a metric approach to measuring the complexity of lotteries. Starting by observing that degenerate lotteries are the simplest choice alternatives, the complexity of a lottery is evaluated by its distance from the closest degenerate lottery. Equivalently, a lottery is complex when it is difficult to approximate it by a single outcome. Given a metric over outcomes, the complexity index we consider is the minimum average distance between the lottery and one of its best degenerate proxies. The paper provides an axiomatic foundation for this representation, studies its main properties, and compares it with other measures of complexity. It then applies the index to choice under risk through a class of complexity adjusted expected utility preferences. Within this model, we study how complexity affects risk attitudes and consistency with stochastic dominance.
