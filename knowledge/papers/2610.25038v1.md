<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

嵌套logit模型在运营需求模型中应用广泛，但联合估计其效用系数与嵌套相异性参数需要求解非凸优化问题。标准估计程序无法提供全局最优性证书，从而在嵌套logit需求下的全局优化决策与该需求模型的可靠估计之间形成了差距。据我们所知，我们开发了首个具有显式全局精度保证的全局收敛优化框架，用于嵌套logit联合估计。我们的核心复杂性结果将所有指数依赖性限制在自由嵌套相异性参数的数量上：计算量在观测数、备选项数和效用系数数量上均为多项式。因此，在所述紧致域与松弛误差条件下，固定嵌套数量即可得到加性多项式时间近似方案（PTAS）。该框架利用固定相异性下的精确指数锥表述，以及仅在自由相异性参数上进行搜索的空间分支定界算法。有效的似然界提供了任意时间证书，并可在任意规定的加性平均对数似然容差下有限终止。在八个基准数据集和240个估计实例上，该方法对每个报告的解均给出证书，并揭示了在求解器正常终止情况下仍存在的劣质局部估计。这些结果为全局认证的嵌套logit估计奠定了计算基础。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Nested logit is widely used in operational demand models, yet jointly estimating its utility coefficients and nest dissimilarity parameters requires nonconvex optimization. Standard estimation routines offer no certificate of global optimality, leaving a gap between globally optimizing decisions under nested-logit demand and reliably estimating that demand model. We develop, to our knowledge, the first globally convergent optimization framework with an explicit global-accuracy guarantee for joint nested-logit estimation. Our central complexity result confines all exponential dependence to the number of free nest dissimilarity parameters: computation is polynomial in the numbers of observations, alternatives, and utility coefficients. Consequently, under the stated compact-domain and relaxation-error conditions, fixing the number of nests yields an additive polynomial-time approximation scheme (PTAS). The framework exploits an exact exponential-cone formulation for fixed dissimilarities and a spatial branch-and-bound algorithm that searches only over the free dissimilarities. Valid likelihood bounds provide an anytime certificate and finite termination at any prescribed additive average-log-likelihood tolerance. Across eight benchmark datasets and 240 estimation instances, the method certifies every reported solution and exposes inferior local estimates despite normal solver termination. These results establish a computational foundation for globally certified nested-logit estimation.
