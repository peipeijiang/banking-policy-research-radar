<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究了一系列Friedkin-Johnsen（FJ）模型中的观点演化，这些模型串联连接，使得每个模型的最终观点作为下一个模型的初始观点。每个FJ模型之后进行一次投票，每个智能体在下一个模型中根据其观点与投票结果的接近程度而变得部分或完全固执。由于固执程度的状态依赖切换，动力学不保证具有收缩性。我们证明，在任何固定的强连通网络上，观点总是收敛的。提出了关于初始观点的充分条件，在该条件下共识以指数速度达成。对于完全图，我们进一步证明，如果智能体的固执程度差异不太大，则在某个FJ模型中部分固执的智能体在后续模型中永远不会变得完全固执：一旦它开始妥协，就会持续妥协。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper, we study opinion evolution in a sequence of Friedkin-Johnsen (FJ) models, concatenated so that the final opinions of each model serve as the initial opinions of the next. Each FJ model is followed by a vote, and each agent becomes partially or fully stubborn in the next model, depending on how close its opinion is to the voting result. With the state-dependent switching of stubbornness, the dynamics are not guaranteed to be contractive. We show that, over any fixed strongly connected network, the opinions always converge. A sufficient condition on the initial opinions is proposed, under which consensus is reached exponentially fast. For the complete graph, we further show that, if the agents' stubbornness levels do not differ too much, an agent that is partially stubborn in one FJ model can never become fully stubborn in a subsequent one: once it has started compromising, it keeps doing so.
