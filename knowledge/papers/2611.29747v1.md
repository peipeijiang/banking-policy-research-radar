<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

收益信号的经济价值通常通过基于这些信号构建的投资组合的夏普比率（Sharpe ratios）来评估。在现代资产定价中，收益信号往往依赖于许多特征，包括状态变量和公司特定特征，且机器学习方法越来越多地被用于估计这些信号。但是，我们对估计的夏普比率能有多大信心？我们发现，即使在大规模资产面板中，信号估计误差对推断仍然可能具有实质性影响。此外，当预测变量包含共同特征或表现出显著的截面依赖性时，跨资产汇集观测值并不一定能使这种不确定性变得可忽略。作为补救措施，我们开发了一种去偏夏普比率估计量（debiased Sharpe-ratio estimator），它考虑了信号估计误差，并提供了有效的置信区间和针对基准的检验。我们的目标是某一指定投资组合规则在给定特征下的真实条件期望收益处所评估的总体夏普比率。该框架涵盖市场择时、多空及其他基于信号的投资组合，允许资产数量随时间序列样本量增长并大幅超过其规模。该方法将正交投资组合矩与时间序列交叉拟合相结合，在机器学习预测的适当估计误差界下保持有效性。在实证方面，我们使用收益信号的机器学习估计量重新审视了Gu et al. (2020)的设定。相对于市场基准的最强多空夏普比率增益在去偏后仍然显著，为其特征集的经济价值提供了统计证据。常规和去偏夏普比率估计通常相似，而相关的置信区间和检验可能差异很大。这些差异因信号估计量和投资组合规则而异，且并不一致地削弱投资组合表现的证据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The economic value of return signals is often assessed using the Sharpe ratios of portfolios based on those signals. In modern asset pricing, return signals often depend on many features, including state variables and firm-specific characteristics, and machine-learning methods are increasingly used to estimate them. But how much confidence can we place in the estimated Sharpe ratios? We find that signal-estimation error can remain consequential for inference even in large asset panels. Moreover, pooling observations across assets does not necessarily make this uncertainty negligible when predictors contain common features or exhibit substantial cross-sectional dependence. As a remedy, we develop a debiased Sharpe-ratio estimator that accounts for signal-estimation error and provides valid confidence intervals and tests against benchmarks. Our target is the population Sharpe ratio of a specified portfolio rule evaluated at the true conditional expected returns given the features. The framework covers market-timing, long-short, and other signal-based portfolios, allowing the number of assets to grow with and greatly exceed the time-series sample size. The method combines orthogonal portfolio moments with time-series cross-fitting and remains valid under suitable estimation-error bounds for machine-learning forecasts. Empirically, we revisit the setting of Gu et al. (2020) using machine-learning estimators of the return signals. The strongest long-short Sharpe-ratio gains relative to market benchmarks remain significant after debiasing, providing statistical evidence of the economic value of their feature set. Conventional and debiased Sharpe-ratio estimates are generally similar, while the associated confidence intervals and tests can differ substantially. These differences vary across signal estimators and portfolio rules and do not uniformly weaken the evidence of portfolio performance.
