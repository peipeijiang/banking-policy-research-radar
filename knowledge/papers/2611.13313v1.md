<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多政策评估涉及多个无序处理。一种常见方法是将这些处理聚合成簇，并在处理簇层面应用工具变量（IV）方法：将簇视为二元处理，并在簇层面引入IV假设。本文表明，在未聚类处理层面的一组假设下，使用处理簇的IV识别的是局部平均处理效应（LATEs）的加权平均。当可获得未聚类处理选择的数据时，我们提出了使用处理簇的IV之因果解释的可检验必要条件。我们通过实证应用说明了我们的方法，这些应用涉及将研究领域聚合为STEM和非STEM簇或八个广泛研究方向后的因果效应。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many policy evaluations include multiple unordered treatments. A common approach is to aggregate these treatments into clusters and apply instrumental variable (IV) methods at the treatment cluster level: The clusters are treated as binary treatments and the IV assumptions are invoked at the cluster level. This paper shows that IV with treatment clusters identifies a weighted average of local average treatment effects (LATEs) under a set of assumptions at the unclustered treatment level. When data on unclustered treatment choice is available, we propose testable necessary conditions for the causal interpretation of IV with treatment clusters. We illustrate our methods with empirical applications to the causal effects of fields of study aggregated into STEM and non-STEM clusters or eight broad study directions.
