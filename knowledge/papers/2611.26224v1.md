<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究投票中明显的群体操纵，并将其刻画为仅从联盟的选项集即可识别出的操纵。我们的分析聚焦于两个相互重叠的类别：仅取决于最高选项的规则（tops-only rules）以及满足多数标准的规则，后者包括所有与孔多塞相容的规则（Condorcet-consistent rules）。在至少有三个备选方案的普遍域上，一个仅取决于最高选项的规则是群体不可明显操纵的（NOMG），当且仅当它是独裁的。对于满足多数标准的规则，当 \(n,m\geq3\) 时，NOMG 是不可能的，除非在四名选民和三个备选方案的情形下。我们量化了这些限制背后的联盟规模，得到了高效仅取决于最高选项规则的精确界，以及奇数选民多数标准规则的精确阈值。在例外配置中，我们刻画了一个包含 Borda 的 NOMG 评分规则区间，该规则在该情形下也与孔多塞相容。我们进一步刻画了两个备选方案下的 NOMG，并将我们的仅取决于最高选项刻画应用于广义中位选民方案和委员会投票。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study obvious group manipulation in voting and characterize it as manipulation identifiable from a coalition's option sets alone. Our analysis focuses on two overlapping classes: tops-only rules and rules satisfying the majority criterion, including all Condorcet-consistent rules. On the universal domain with at least three alternatives, a tops-only rule is not obviously manipulable by groups (NOMG) if and only if it is dictatorial. For rules satisfying the majority criterion, NOMG is impossible when $n,m\geq3$, except with four voters and three alternatives. We quantify the coalition sizes behind these restrictions, obtaining sharp bounds for efficient tops-only rules and an exact threshold for majority-criterion rules with odd electorates. In the exceptional configuration, we characterize an interval of NOMG scoring rules containing Borda, which is also Condorcet consistent there. We further characterize NOMG with two alternatives and apply our tops-only characterization to generalized median voter schemes and voting by committees.
