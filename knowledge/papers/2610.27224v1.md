<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

研究者经常观察到分组分位数而非分组均值，这引出了一个问题：仅凭这些汇总信息是否足以支持常规回归分析。我们针对仅披露分位数和混合披露情形下的分组均值线性模型，开发了估计与推断方法。我们建立了披露设计的一致性估计条件以及参数速率下的渐近正态性条件。在固定披露条件下，我们刻画了尖锐识别集并构建了联合置信域，阐明了信息量如何取决于所披露分位数的数量和位置。在教育、社会照护和医疗支出方面的应用展示了这些方法。R包qonlyreg实现了所提出的程序。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Researchers often observe group quantiles but not group means, raising the question of whether these summaries alone support conventional regression analysis. We develop estimation and inference methods for linear models of group means under quantile-only and mixed disclosure. We establish conditions on disclosure design for consistent estimation and asymptotic normality at the parametric rate. Under fixed disclosure, we characterize sharp identified sets and construct simultaneous confidence regions, clarifying how informativeness depends on the number and placement of disclosed quantiles. Applications to education, social care, and medical expenditure illustrate the methods. The R package qonlyreg implements the proposed procedures.
