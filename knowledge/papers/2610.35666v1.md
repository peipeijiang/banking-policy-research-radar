<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究完全随机实验中处理效应比值的回归调整。现有文献分别对分子和分母进行调整，但这种方法错过了效率目标，当使用更丰富或高维的协变量信息以及灵活或正则化的预测方法来追求进一步的效率提升时，这种效率损失更为重要。我们提出一种两步估计量，直接预测相关的变换后结果。利用交叉拟合，我们的方法能够容纳高维第一阶段预测，同时保持最终调整为固定维数，从而避免调整协变量数量增长所带来的不稳定性。我们进一步发展了基于持续性的LASSO理论，该理论不要求稀疏性，允许协变量数量多于观测数量，并允许在预测水平条件下进行交叉验证的惩罚参数选择。为了获得额外的效率提升，我们的增广两步估计量嵌套了基准调整空间，因此渐近效率不低于基准估计量，同时其可行的保守方差估计量满足相同的效率排序。模拟研究和实际数据分析表明，我们提出的估计量优于现有方法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study regression adjustment for ratios of treatment effects in completely randomized experiments. The existing literature adjusts the numerator and denominator separately, but this approach misses the efficiency target, loss of which matters more when richer or high-dimensional covariate information and flexible or regularized prediction methods are used to pursue further efficiency gains. We propose a two-step estimator that directly predicts the relevant transformed outcome. Using cross-fitting, our method accommodates high-dimensional first-stage prediction while keeping the final adjustment fixed-dimensional, thereby avoiding instability from a growing number of adjustment covariates. We further develop persistence-based LASSO theory that does not require sparsity, permits more covariates than observations, and allows cross-validated penalty selection under prediction-level conditions. For additional efficiency gains, our augmented two-step estimator nests the benchmark adjustment spaces and is therefore asymptotically no less efficient than the benchmark estimators, while its feasible conservative variance estimator satisfies the same efficiency ordering. Simulation studies and a real-data analysis demonstrate that our proposed estimator performs better than existing methods.
