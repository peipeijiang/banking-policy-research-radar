<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究模型多样性是否能在进入交易的选择过程中存活下来。在由三个语言模型家族以固定比例构成的人工市场中，新闻呈现方式改变了它们在提交订单中的代表性。在公告轮次中，Qwen在融资事件中提交订单的份额移动了48个百分点，而净订单数量几乎没有变化。在裁员事件中，Mistral的份额移动了40个百分点，同时净数量反号。同质化群体在其主动决策方向一致时消除了对冲流。一项解析分解表明，为什么即使在总需求敏感性不变的情况下，选择也可能提高或降低价格准确性。这些证据涉及呈现组合与提交流；更干净的复制和已知价值验证已被前瞻性地指定。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study whether model diversity survives selection into trading. In synthetic markets with a fixed mixture of three language-model families, news presentation changes their representation among submitted orders. At the announcement round, Qwen's share of submitted orders shifts by 48 percentage points in the financing event, with little change in net order counts. In the workforce-reduction event, Mistral's share shifts by 40 percentage points while net counts reverse sign. Homogeneous populations remove opposing flow when their active decisions share a direction. An analytical decomposition shows why selection can improve or worsen price accuracy even at unchanged aggregate demand sensitivity. The evidence concerns presentation bundles and submitted flow; cleaner replication and a known-value validation are specified prospectively.

</details>
