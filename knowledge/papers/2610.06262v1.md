<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在Komo、Kominers和Roughgarden的模型中，单一拍卖可以同时具有抗托均衡和非抗托均衡。我们给出了一个明确的单物品例子，其中包含三个投标人位置、公开可观察的行动、正的获胜价格以及严格规则的独立同分布（i.i.d.）价值分布。真实投标人在两个均衡中都具有严格激励。一个初始公开行动传递了关于最终位置是否真实的信息。由此产生的信念改变了中间投标人的反应，并逆转了第一个托儿在两个行动之间的严格偏好。我们验证了两个均衡并计算了它们的期望收益。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A single auction can have both a shill-proof equilibrium and a non-shill-proof equilibrium in the model of Komo, Kominers, and Roughgarden. We give an explicit single item example with three bidder positions, publicly observed actions, positive winning prices, and a strictly regular i.i.d.\ value distribution. Real bidders have strict incentives in both equilibria. An initial public action conveys information about whether the final position is real. The resulting belief changes the middle bidder's response and reverses the first shill's strict preference between two actions. We verify both equilibria and compute their expected revenues.
