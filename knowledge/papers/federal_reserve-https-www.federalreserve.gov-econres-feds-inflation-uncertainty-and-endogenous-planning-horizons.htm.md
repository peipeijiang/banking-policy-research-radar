<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个有限期界规划模型，在该模型中，企业在设定价格时自行选择提前规划的时间跨度。提前规划更久能够改善企业的定价决策，但需要付出认知努力。我们推导出企业最优规划期界的解析解，并表明大规模且持续的总需求或总供给冲击会促使企业延长规划期界，从而使通胀对冲击的反应更加敏感，并引发通胀不确定性的内生变动。在定量层面，我们证明该模型能够匹配数据中观察到的通胀预测修正幅度与通胀不确定性之间的正相关关系。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Christopher Gust , Edward Herbst , and David López-Salido We develop a finite-horizon planning model in which firms choose how far ahead to plan when setting prices. Planning further ahead improves a firm's pricing decision but requires cognitive effort. We derive analytical solutions for a firm's chosen planning horizon and show that large and persistent aggregate demand or supply disturbances induce firms to plan further ahead, making inflation more sensitive to shocks and generating endogenous movements in inflation uncertainty. Quantitatively, we show that the model matches the positive relationship between the size of inflation forecast revisions and inflation uncertainty observed in the data.
