<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当研究者不确定哪些处理前时期满足平行趋势假设时，对处理组平均处理效应的双重差分（difference-in-differences, DID）估计。Roth（2022）表明，预趋势检验可能引入偏差，这与关于后选择推断的统计文献相补充。我们提出模型平均双重差分（model averaged difference-in-differences, MADID）估计量，即候选 \(2\times2\) DID 估计量的加权平均。权重是候选残差平方和的归一化指数函数，因此实施不需要设定检验。我们在使无效比较可被检测为比至少一个有效比较更具变异性条件下，推导了 MADID 的渐近性质。在这些条件下，当平行趋势假设至少对一个处理前时期成立时，MADID 是一致的。尽管其在处理后时期上的联合极限分布通常是非高斯的，推断可以通过子抽样或模拟进行。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study difference-in-differences (DID) estimation of average treatment effects on the treated when the researcher is uncertain about which pre-treatment periods satisfy the parallel trends assumption. Roth (2022) shows that pre-trend testing can induce bias, complementing the statistical literature on post-selection inference. We propose the model averaged difference-in-differences (MADID) estimator, a weighted average of the candidate $2\times2$ DID estimators. The weights are normalized exponential functions of the candidate residual sums of squares, so implementation requires no specification test. We derive MADID's asymptotic properties under conditions that make invalid comparisons detectably more variable than at least one valid comparison. Under these conditions, MADID is consistent when the parallel trends assumption holds for at least one pre-treatment period. Although its joint limiting distribution across post-treatment periods is generally non-Gaussian, inference can be conducted by subsampling or simulation.

</details>
