<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们刻画了满足同等对待同等者、双边一致性、向下合成与向上合成的索赔问题的分配规则。这些规则恰好是单参数对数-指数族$\{r^θ\}_{θ\in[-\infty,+\infty]}$的成员，其端点为约束等额奖励（CEA）和约束等额损失（CEL）。对于有限的$θ$，$r^θ$是在$u_θ=\log\varphi_θ$下的等牺牲奖励规则，其中\[ \varphi_θ(x):=\frac{e^{θx}-1}θ\quad(θ\ne0), \qquad \varphi_0(x):=x, \] 同时，它也是对偶效用$u_{-θ}$下的等牺牲损失规则。比例规则是中点，即$θ=0$。规则的连续性并非假设，而是由其他公理推导得出的结果。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We characterise the division rules for claims problems that satisfy equal treatment of equals, bilateral consistency, composition down, and composition up. The rules are precisely the members of a one-parameter log-exponential family $\{r^θ\}_{θ\in[-\infty,+\infty]}$, with constrained equal awards (CEA) and constrained equal losses (CEL) as its endpoints. For finite $θ$, $r^θ$ is the equal-sacrifice rule in awards for $u_θ=\log\varphi_θ$, where \[ \varphi_θ(x):=\frac{e^{θx}-1}θ\quad(θ\ne0), \qquad \varphi_0(x):=x, \] and simultaneously the equal-sacrifice rule in losses for the dual utility $u_{-θ}$. The proportional rule is the midpoint, $θ=0$. Continuity of the rules are not assumed but a consequence of the other axioms.

</details>
