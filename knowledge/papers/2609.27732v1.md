# Do Drug Consumption Rooms Reduce Drug-Related Hospitalizations? Evidence from Switzerland

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27732v1)

## 一句话结论

> 本文利用瑞士城市1998-2022年间交错开设毒品消费室（DCR）的准自然实验，采用双重差分法估计DCR对毒品相关住院率的因果效应，发现DCR使5公里内毒品相关住院率降低约35%，主要将致命过量转化为非致命住院，支持DCR作为有效的减少伤害策略。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文估计了毒品消费室（drug consumption room, DCR）的开设对瑞士毒品相关住院的因果效应。我利用1998年至2022年间瑞士各城市DCR的错时引入，使用个体层面的医院记录和双重差分估计量。DCR的开设使设施5公里范围内的毒品相关住院率降低约35%。这一下降在致命性结局方面最为显著，降幅为35%，而麻醉品中毒住院则上升了相当的数量，这与DCR将潜在致命的过量用药转化为非致命住院的情况一致。精神和行为障碍住院下降32%，而肝炎和HIV的估计结果不精确。研究结果支持DCR作为一种有效减害策略的作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper estimates the causal effect of drug consumption room (DCR) openings on drug-related hospitalizations in Switzerland. I exploit the staggered introduction of DCRs across Swiss cities between 1998 and 2022, using individual-level hospital records and a difference-in-differences estimator. DCR openings reduce drug-related hospitalization rates by roughly 35\% within 5 km of a facility. The reduction is most pronounced for fatal outcomes, which fall by 35\% while narcotics poisoning hospitalizations rise by a comparable amount, consistent with DCRs converting potentially fatal overdoses into non-fatal hospitalizations. Mental and behavioral disorder hospitalizations decline by 32\% while estimates for Hepatitis and HIV are imprecise. The findings support the role of DCRs as an effective harm reduction strategy.
