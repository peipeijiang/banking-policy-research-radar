<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究具有转移成本的能力受限流网络中端节点之间的路径集分配问题。我们引入一种起点—终点（origin–destination，OD）投标语言，其中参与者对入口—出口—数量许可证的有界锥组合进行投标，机制通过一个网络流优化胜者确定问题来执行路由。随后我们研究OD机制中的支付规则。在一个受限模型中，即来源公开且统一定价，且投标人对天然气来源无差异时，Vickrey–Clarke–Groves（VCG）支付是核心稳定的；关键的结构性事实是，在该情形下，某些最优聚合流从不以两个方向使用同一物理管道，因此胜者确定问题简化为一个单商品网络流，其价值在活跃投标人集合上是子模的。如果来源准入被允许依赖于单个消费者——这是一个现实特征，例如当天然气交易嵌入双边政治谈判时——这种归约就会失效，VCG支付可能严格位于核心之外。对于这一一般模型，我们提出一种由流量守恒约束的影子价格导出的对偶（节点）定价规则，并证明它始终是核心稳定的，代价是失去占优策略激励相容性。每个结果均附有说明性例子。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the route-set allocation problem between end nodes in capacity-constrained flow networks with transfer costs. We introduce an origin--destination (OD) bidding language, in which a participant bids for bounded conic combinations of inlet--outlet--quantity licences and the mechanism performs the routing through a network-flow optimising winner-determination problem. We then study payment rules for the OD mechanism. Under a restricted model with openly and uniformly priced sources and bidders who are indifferent to the source of the gas, the Vickrey--Clarke--Groves (VCG) payment is core-stable; the key structural fact is that in this regime some optimal aggregate flows never use a physical pipe in both directions, so the winner-determination problem reduces to a single-commodity network flow whose value is submodular in the set of active bidders. If source access is allowed to depend on the individual consumer -- a realistic feature, e.g. when gas deals are embedded in bilateral political bargaining -- the reduction fails and VCG payments may lie strictly outside the core. For this general model we propose a dual (nodal) pricing rule derived from the shadow prices of the flow-conservation constraints, and prove it is always core-stable, at the cost of dominant-strategy incentive compatibility. Illustrative examples accompany each result.
