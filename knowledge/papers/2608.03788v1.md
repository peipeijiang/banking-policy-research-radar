# When Does Party Convergence Persist under Alienation-Based Abstention?

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在标准唐斯模型中，两个追求执政的政党会收敛于中位选民。然而，疏离的选民可能弃权，并且只会投票给处于其容忍半径范围内的政党。对于单峰选民分布而言，收敛依然存在，但会移至一个中心选民——即在收敛平台上参与投票的选民群体的中位数。然而，选民分布的单峰性在实证上是一个备受争议的假设。因此，我们首先刻画任意连续选民分布下的纯策略均衡。对于一般分布，纯策略均衡可能不存在或非唯一，并且均衡的存在性不一定会随着选民容忍半径的增大而持续。为解决这些问题，我们提出一个基本对象：\emph{向心}结构，该结构存在一个单一的锚定平台，竞争始终被拉向该平台。我们证明，在基于疏离的弃权机制下，该结构在均衡处产生收敛。我们的主要结果关注随着容忍半径增大，这一新结构的出现与持续性。尽管追求执政的政党的均衡本身可能随半径增大而消失并重新出现，但一旦向心结构出现，只要中点选民未被疏离，该结构就会持续存在。此外，向心结构总是会出现，并且当政党具有政策动机时，该结构能完全对均衡进行分类。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In the standard Downsian model, two office-seeking parties converge to the median voter. However alienated voters may abstain and turn out only for a party within their tolerance radius. For single-peaked voter distributions, convergence survives but relocates to a central voter, the median of the electorate that participates at the convergent platform. However single-peakedness of the voter distribution is an empirically contested assumption. So we first characterize pure-strategy equilibrium for any continuous voter distribution. For general distributions, pure-strategy equilibrium can fail to exist or be non-unique, and existence of equilibrium need not persist as the tolerance radius of the voters increases. In order to resolve these issues, we propose a fundamental object: \emph{centripetal} structure for which there is a single anchor platform toward which competition always pulls. We show this structure produces convergence at equilibrium under alienation based abstention. Our main result concerns the emergence and persistence of this new structure as the tolerance radius increases. Even though equilibria for office-seeking parties themselves can vanish and reappear as the radius grows, once centripetal structure emerges, it persists as long as the midpoint voter is not alienated. Moreover, the centripetal structure always emerges, and this structure classifies equilibrium completely when parties are policy motivated.

</details>
