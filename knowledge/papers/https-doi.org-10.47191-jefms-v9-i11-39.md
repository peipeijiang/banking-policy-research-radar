# To What Extent Did Interest Rate Hikes, Rather Than Supply-Side Normalisation, Bring Inflation Down in India and the Us Between 2022 and 2026?

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

2022年至2026年间，印度储备银行（RBI）和美联储在三十年的时间内实施了激进的货币紧缩政策。到2026年，美联储已将政策利率上调525个基点，而印度储备银行则将利率上调250个基点，以应对2022年6月美国高达9.1%的通胀率和2022年4月印度7.8%的通胀率。本文旨在探究随后出现的反通胀过程中，有多少可单独归因于货币紧缩，又有多少可视为全球供应链正常化、粮食供应和能源价格等因素独立于货币紧缩效应之外的影响。本文采用美国劳工统计局、印度储备银行（RBI）、纽约联邦储备银行全球供应链压力指数（GSCPI）、统计与计划执行部（MoSPI）以及美国能源信息署的公开数据，构建了两个经济体关键统计数据的对比时间线。文章运用Bernanke和Blanchard（2023）提出的需求-供给分解框架，并结合相关文献，主张以下立场：尽管货币政策紧缩对于控制通胀预期和调节需求敏感部门是必要的，但2022年至2024年观察到的反通胀的时机与幅度，以及2025-26年在能源和地缘政治冲击重新出现的情况下、尽管存在低利率或降息政策却出现的逆转，表明两个经济体反通胀过程的大部分归因于供给侧稳定过程。文章最后概述了对以利率机制为基础的通胀目标制框架的政策启示，尤其是在通胀主要由粮食和能源驱动的国家。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

From 2022 to 2026, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Federal Reserve carried out aggressive monetary tightening over a period of thirty years. By 2026, the Federal Reserve had increased the policy rates by 525 basis points while the RBI raised the rates by 250 basis points in response to inflation being as high as 9.1% in the U.S. in June 2022, and 7.8% in India in April 2022. This paper seeks to find out what part of disinflation that followed can be attributed to that monetary tightening solely and which parts can be regarded as the influence of such factors as global supply chain normalization, food supply, and energy prices, independent of the effects of monetary tightening. The paper employs publicly available data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Federal Reserve Bank of New York's Global Supply Chain Pressure Index (GSCPI), Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), and the U.S. Energy Information Administration to build a timeline comparing the key statistics of both economies. It utilizes the demand-supply breakdown suggested by Bernanke and Blanchard (2023) along with relevant literature in advocating the position that while tightening of monetary policy was required to control inflation expectations and to moderate demand-responsive segments, the timing and extent of disinflation that was observed from 2022 to 2024 and the turnabout in 2025-26 in the light of renewed energy and geopolitical shocks in spite of the presence of low or reduced interest rate policies demonstrate the fact that the majority of the disinflation process in both economies was due to the process of supply-side stabilization. The article concludes with outlining the policy implications for inflation targeting regime, which is based on the reliance on interest rate mechanisms, in countries where inflation is primarily food- and energy-driven.
