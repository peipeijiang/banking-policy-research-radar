<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种衡量外貌吸引力的新方法——相对视觉嵌入（relative vision embedding, RVE）得分。该得分将一张人脸映射到预训练深度视觉模型的嵌入空间中，并测量其与一组名人参考人脸的接近程度。该方法无需额外训练或微调，无需手工设计的特征，也无需人工评分者；它具有连续性、低成本、可扩展且完全可复现的特点。利用带有真人吸引力评分的公开照片，我们表明RVE得分与人工评分高度相关，且其排名在多个现成视觉模型中保持一致。我们将该指标应用于一个研究型经济学系的行政招聘数据，并辅以手工收集的求职者照片。较高的RVE得分与更好的就业市场结果相关，但这种关联在所有设定下并不稳健。相比之下，与推荐信长度和情感倾向的关联则强且稳健：RVE得分较高的候选人收到的推荐信更长、措辞更有利，且这种情感梯度对女性候选人更为陡峭。这一模式与外貌部分通过推荐信影响学术招聘的机制相一致。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce a new approach to measure physical attractiveness---the relative vision embedding (RVE) score. The score maps a face to a vector in the embedding space of a pre-trained deep vision model and measures its proximity to a reference set of celebrity faces. It requires no additional training or fine-tuning, no hand-crafted features, and no human raters; it is continuous, inexpensive, scalable, and fully reproducible. Using publicly available photographs with human attractiveness ratings, we show that the RVE scores correlate strongly with human ratings and that rankings are consistent across several off-the-shelf vision models. We apply the measure to administrative hiring data from a research-oriented economics department, complemented by hand-collected photographs of the job applicants. Higher RVE scores are associated with better job-market outcomes, though the association is not robust across all specifications. In contrast, the association with reference-letter length and sentiment is strong and robust: candidates with higher RVE scores receive longer letters written in more favorable language, and this sentiment gradient is steeper for female candidates. The pattern is consistent with appearance affecting academic hiring in part through letters of recommendation.
