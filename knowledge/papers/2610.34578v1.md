<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文重新审视了Wolinsky（1986）的消费者搜索与价格竞争模型。假设市场中有有限数量的企业，且匹配值分布函数和生存函数均为对数凹函数。那么，存在唯一的对称纯策略均衡价格，且消费者进行活跃搜索。本文讨论了两种应用情形。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper revisits the consumer search and price competition model of Wolinsky (1986). Suppose that the market has a finite number of firms, and the match value distribution function and survival function are both log-concave. Then, there is a unique symmetric pure-strategy equilibrium price with active consumer search. Two applications are discussed.
