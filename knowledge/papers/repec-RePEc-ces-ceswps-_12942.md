# The U.S.–China Trade War and the Geography of Global Production

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_12942.html)

## 一句话结论

> 利用跨国企业层面的数据，研究中美贸易战对第三国制造业活动的影响，发现跨国企业通过重新配置生产网络来应对关税冲击，总体净效应为负。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们利用一个涵盖50个主要经济体中数百万家工厂的新型工厂层面数据集（包括超过20万家跨国企业的附属机构），研究了中美贸易战如何影响第三国的制造业活动。通过将工厂层面数据与详细的关税信息相结合，我们估算了美国和中国加征的双边惩罚性产出关税与投入关税对各国、各行业及各生产阶段企业的销售额、就业和工厂数量的影响。研究发现，贸易战对第三国的影响具有高度异质性，且在很大程度上相互抵消，总体净效应为中等程度的负面。大部分调整由跨国企业在其附属网络内部重新配置活动所驱动，而国内企业的反应则要小得多。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how the U.S.–China trade war affected manufacturing activity in third countries using a novel plant-level dataset covering millions of establishments in 50 major economies, including affiliates of more than 200,000 multinational enterprises (MNEs). Combining establishment-level data with detailed tariff information, we estimate the effects of U.S. and Chinese punitive bilateral output and input tariffs on sales, employment, and establishments across countries, industries, and stages of production. We find that third-country effects of the trade war are highly heterogeneous and largely offsetting, yielding moderately negative net effects overall. Most of the adjustment is driven by multinational enterprises reallocating activity across affiliate networks, while domestic firms respond much less.
