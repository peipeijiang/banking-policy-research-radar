# Public salience mobilizes nationwide responses to violence against women

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02647v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用2017-2024年意大利案件级女性杀害数据，采用事件研究/比较案例分析，发现高关注度亲密伴侣女性杀害引发全国性求助与报警上升，但未增加致命暴力，表明公众关注可助力预防而不引发模仿效应。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

围绕女性杀害（femicides）的公众关注度究竟会动员社会回应，还是会通过模仿效应引发进一步伤害？利用新近整理的2017年至2024年意大利女性杀害案件级数据，我们研究了最受关注的案件对求助行为、报警和致命暴力的全国性影响。我们发现，高度关注的亲密伴侣女性杀害（intimate partner femicides, IPFs）在全国范围内引发了求助行为的增加，而一起特别受关注的亲密伴侣女性杀害案件还导致警方收到的针对女性的暴力报案显著上升。然而，我们没有发现女性杀害案件增加的证据——这一模式与模仿理论不一致。回应在关注度达到峰值期间最为强烈。对于关注度较低的亲密伴侣女性杀害案件、由家庭成员实施的关注度相似的女性杀害案件、高度关注的未被归类为女性杀害的女性被杀案件，或关注度较低的虚假案件，均未出现 comparable 的动员效应。综合这些比较表明，动员取决于关注强度、受害者-施害者关系以及暴力的性别性质，而非反映对受关注暴力犯罪的泛化回应。这些发现表明，公众关注度可以促进针对性别暴力的预防策略，而不会加剧致命暴力。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Does public salience around femicides mobilize societal responses or trigger further harm through imitation? Using newly assembled case-level data on femicides in Italy from 2017 to 2024, we study the nationwide consequences of the most salient cases for help-seeking, police reporting, and lethal violence. We find that highly salient intimate partner femicides (IPFs) generate nationwide increases in help-seeking, and that an exceptionally salient IPF also leads to a marked rise in police reports of violence against women. Yet we find no evidence of an increase in femicides-a pattern inconsistent with copycat theories. Responses are strongest during periods of peak salience. No comparable mobilization follows a lower-salience IPF, a similarly salient femicide committed by family members, a highly salient killing of a woman not classified as a femicide, or a lower-salience falsification case. Together, these comparisons indicate that mobilization depends on salience intensity, the victim-perpetrator relationship, and the gendered nature of the violence, rather than reflecting a generic response to salient violent crime. These findings suggest that public salience can contribute to prevention strategies against gender-based violence without amplifying lethal violence.
