<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

大多数住房研究依赖于单一市场模型，其中单一住房价格对应单一住房数量。我们通过引入收入不平等和住房质量来推广这一方法。研究住房供给（及需求）面临两个重要挑战：首先，不存在单一的住房供给函数，而是存在一个由质量索引的连续单位供给函数族。这意味着供给弹性估计值是局部平均处理效应（LATEs），而非结构性弹性。其次，空间环境中常见的溢出效应可能使估计值甚至无法被解释为LATE，而应被视为局部一般均衡对象。关键在于，这些问题无法通过使用土地价值份额来衡量供给约束来规避，因为在标准模型中，该指标甚至不能作为供给约束的诊断依据。我们认为，理解收入异质性和住房服务质量对于更全面地理解住房市场可能至关重要。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Most housing research relies on a single-margin model where a single housing price relates to a single housing quantity. We generalize this approach by introducing income inequality and housing quality. Two important challenges to studying housing supply (and demand) emerge: First, there is no singular housing supply function, instead there is a continuum of unit-supply functions indexed by quality. This implies that supply elasticity estimates are local average treatment effects (LATEs), not structural elasticities. Second, spillovers common in spatial settings may preclude even the LATE interpretation of estimates, which are instead local general equilibrium objects. Critically, these issues cannot be bypassed by using the land share of value to measure supply constraints as it is not even a diagnostic for supply constraints in the standard model. We suggest that understanding heterogeneity in incomes and housing services quality is likely critical to understanding housing markets more generally.

</details>
