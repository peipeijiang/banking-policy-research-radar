<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

投资者通过决策规则将信号转化为持仓：买入排名最高的十分位，持有高动量低波动的股票，剔除未通过筛选的股票。许多此类规则是关于信号排名的阈值陈述的布尔函数，以这种方式书写规则既能显示规则持有什么，也能显示其交易频率。Walsh分解将任何规则的收益拆分为对单个信号的暴露以及对信号间交互作用的暴露；例如，相同两个筛选条件的交集和并集具有相反的交互作用暴露。相同的系数连同底层陈述的持续性共同决定了换手率。交互作用比其组成部分的持续性更低，因此加载高阶交互作用的规则交易更频繁；而阈值会丢弃信息，因此在同等风险下，交互作用的阈值实现比平滑实现交易更频繁。在1973年至2024年的美国股票中，这些预测高度成立：预测持续性与实际持续性的相关系数至少为0.999，预测换手率与实际换手率的匹配斜率为1.02，且相同的交互作用作为排名乘积比作为阈值规则更具持续性。交易成本随阶数上升，而由于溢价随阶数下降，1995年至2024年间58%的单信号组合的绝对溢价高于其报价价差成本，而双信号和三信号交互作用组合的这一比例分别为24%和6%。在样本外，交互作用组合提高了单信号切点组合的夏普比率，且在1995年至2024年间，它们拒绝了六因子模型，而基于相同信号的传统排序则无法拒绝该模型。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Investors turn signals into holdings through decision rules: buy the top decile, hold stocks with high momentum and low volatility, exclude stocks that fail a screen. Many such rules are Boolean functions of threshold statements about signal ranks, and writing them this way shows both what a rule holds and how often it trades. A Walsh decomposition splits the return of any rule into exposures to individual signals and to interactions among signals; an intersection and a union of the same two screens, for example, have opposite interaction exposures. The same coefficients, together with the persistence of the underlying statements, determine turnover. Interactions are less persistent than their components, so rules that load on higher-order interactions trade more, and thresholds discard information, so at equal risk threshold implementations of interactions trade more than smooth ones. In U.S. stocks from 1973 to 2024, these predictions hold closely: predicted and realized persistence have correlations of at least 0.999, predicted turnover matches realized turnover with a slope of 1.02, and the same interactions are more persistent as products of ranks than as threshold rules. Trading costs rise with order, and because premia fall with order, 58% of single-signal portfolios have absolute premia above their quoted-spread costs over 1995-2024, against 24% of two-signal and 6% of three-signal interaction portfolios. Out of sample, interaction portfolios raise the Sharpe ratio of single-signal tangency portfolios, and over 1995-2024 they reject a six-factor model that conventional sorts on the same signals do not reject.
