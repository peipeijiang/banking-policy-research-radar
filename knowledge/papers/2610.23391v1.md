<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

结合线性规划的投入产出分析广泛应用于国民经济规划。然而，线性规划在可行集的顶点处达到最优，最优计划在某一方向上的微小偏差即会违反某一约束。迄今为止，鲜有研究关注寻找既保证经济供需平衡又保证给定国内生产总值（GDP）水平的生产计划集合，而球填充方法无法直接应用，因为平衡方程所定义的集合具有空内部。本文引入$(\varepsilon,δ)$-解集，其中GDP至多比其最优值低$\varepsilon$，供给与需求之差至多为$δ$，并通过求解线性规划问题找到内接于该集合的最大半径球。我们证明，对于正容差，最优半径为正；最优半径关于容差是非递减的、凹的且分段线性的；最优半径受最窄平衡约束的限制，而该约束由各部门的销售集中度指数决定。该方法应用于蒙古2010—2019年和2024年的官方投入产出表。经典最优计划的鲁棒性为零，而鲁棒计划保持了最大GDP的99.73%，且在蒙特卡洛模拟中能够容忍所有产出和最终需求的独立偏差高达$\pm$0.30%。鲁棒计划的裕度受限于销售集中的部门，主要是教育部门以及日益增加的出口导向型采矿业，这表明采矿业产出用户的多元化拓宽了鲁棒计划集合。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Input-output analysis with linear programming is widely used in the planning of national economies. However, linear programming attains its optimum at a vertex of the feasible set, and a small deviation of the optimal plan in some direction violates a constraint. So far, little attention has been paid to finding sets of production plans which guarantee both the supply-demand balance of an economy and a given level of its gross domestic product (GDP), and the sphere packing approach cannot be applied directly, since the balance equations define a set with an empty interior. In this paper, we introduce the set of $(\varepsilon,δ)$-solutions, in which GDP may be smaller than its optimal value by at most $\varepsilon$ and supply may differ from demand by at most $δ$, and we find a sphere with the maximum radius inscribed into this set by solving a linear programming problem. We prove that the optimal radius is positive for positive tolerances, that it is non-decreasing, concave and piecewise linear with respect to the tolerances, and that it is bounded by the narrowest balance constraint, which is determined by a sales-concentration index of sectors. The approach is applied to the official input-output tables of Mongolia for 2010-2019 and 2024. The classical optimal plan has zero robustness, while the robust plan keeps 99.73% of the maximal GDP and, in a Monte Carlo simulation, tolerates independent deviations of all outputs and final demands of up to $\pm$0.30%. The margin of robust plans is limited by sectors with concentrated sales, mainly education and, increasingly, export-oriented mining, which indicates that a diversification of the users of mining output widens the set of robust plans.
