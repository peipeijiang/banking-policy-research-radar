<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

预期是资产定价和家庭金融领域的核心研究对象。资产价格取决于对未来现金流和贴现率的信念，而投资组合、债务选择和支出则取决于对回报、通胀、收入和利率的主观信念。然而，金融学和宏观经济学长期以来在研究这些决策时并未观测到做出决策的主体的信念。本文概述了一个将主观预期置于实证金融学中心的研究议程。我首先讨论为何基于调查的预期是有信息价值的经济对象，以及通胀预期是如何形成的。接着，我展示为何在通胀时期预期尤为重要——此时投资者必须评估实际收益、名义合约、货币政策以及估值效应。本文聚焦于关联调查、实验和行政金融记录的证据，表明信念具有异质性，随信息和心智模型而变化，并在高风险的现实金融决策中影响交易、支出、债务和投资组合选择。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Expectations are central objects in asset pricing and household finance. Asset prices depend on beliefs about future cash flows an​d discount rates, while portfolios, debt choices, and spending depend on subjective beliefs about returns, inflation, income, and interest rates. Yet finance and macroeconomics long studied these decisions without observing the beliefs of the agents making them. This article summarizes an age​nda that puts subjective expectations at the center of empirical finance. I first discuss why survey-based expectations are informative economic objects and how inflation expectations are formed. I then show why expectations are especially relevant during inflationary periods, when investors must assess real payoffs, nominal contracts, monetary policy, and valuation effects. The article focuses on evidence from linked surveys, experiments, and administrative financial records, showing that beliefs are heterogeneous, move with information and mental models, and affect trading, spending, debt, and portfolio choices in high-stakes real-world financial decisions.
