<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在考虑驯服强长期主义对当代的极端要求以及确保保护远未来世代福祉的可持续发展时，应如何平衡不同世代之间的利益？我们采用一种多元主义伦理方法，该方法仅通过考虑在整类普遍接受的分配规范中所有要素均一致同意的比较，来确定被稳健地视为道德上不可接受的代际分配集合。在一个简单技术环境的设定中，我们证明，任何道德上不可接受的流在道德标准意义上都被某个道德上可允许的流所支配。此外，我们表明，生产率提高和时间视野延长使当代为未来作出更大牺牲在道德上可允许——这与长期主义一致——同时并不迫使当代放弃平等的福祉份额。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How should the interests of different generations be balanced when considering both the issue of taming the extreme demands by strong longtermism on the present as well as ensuring a sustainable development that protects the well-being of generations in the far future? We adopt a pluralistic ethical approach which determines sets of intergenerational distributions that are robustly deemed morally unacceptable by considering only comparisons where there is unanimity among all elements in an entire class of commonly accepted distributional norms. In the setting of a simple technological environment we demonstrate that any morally unacceptable stream is dominated in terms of the moral criterion by some morally permissible stream. Furthermore, we show that increased productivity and an extended time horizon make it morally permissible for the present generation to incur greater sacrifices for future—in line with longtermism—while not obliging it to renounce an equal share of well-being.

</details>
