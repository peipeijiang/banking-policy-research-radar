<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当管理者私下了解项目生产率及其构建帝国收益，而总部缺乏可信先验时的资本预算问题。总部相对于知情投资最小化最大遗憾。在没有薪酬补偿的情况下，最优政策是一个支出上限，用以平衡构建帝国的过度投资与放弃的有利可图的投资。当构建帝国的激励较弱时，薪酬补偿会有所帮助。最优支付激励克制，随投资下降，并在支出上限处降至零，从而支持更大的管理者自由裁量权。当构建帝国的激励很强时，薪酬补偿无法区分生产性投资与有偏扩张；因此，仅靠支出上限是最优的。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study capital budgeting when a manager privately knows project productivity and her empire-building benefits, while headquarters lacks credible priors. Headquarters minimizes maximum regret relative to informed investment. Without compensation, the optimal policy is a spending cap balancing empire-building overinvestment against profitable investment forgone. Compensation helps when empire-building incentives are weak. The optimal payment incentivizes restraint, declines with investment, and reaches zero at the spending cap, supporting greater managerial discretion. When empire-building incentives are strong, compensation cannot distinguish productive investment from biased expansion; therefore, a spending cap alone is optimal.

</details>
