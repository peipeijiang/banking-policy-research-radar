<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

谁应当为AI安全研究让出算力：客户还是能力研究？由客户买单的安全不会延迟任何能力。由研究买单的安全会减缓前沿进展，这给知识更多时间积累，但推迟了完成后的前沿所能提供的东西。在一个前沿起飞模型中，给定分配下更便宜的来源由需求容量中的一个阈值决定。需求容量是前沿完成后客户可以使用的算力份额。只有当需求容量低于该阈值时，研究也才应当买单。阈值位于何处取决于客户服务的价值衰减得有多快。在标定中，它随服务预算上升，因为从客户那里拿走的每一个进一步单位都会让他们付出更多代价。由客户买单的安全应当尽早到来：除非安全生产力快速上升，否则其与客户算力的比率会在起飞过程中下降。在一个服务预算紧张的标定中，早期由客户资助的安全将灾难概率从75%降至51%。这与所找到的、可能也会减缓研究的最佳规则相差在一个百分点以内。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Who should give up compute for AI safety research: customers or capability research? Safety paid for by customers delays no capability. Safety paid for by research slows the frontier, which gives knowledge more time to accumulate but postpones what a finished frontier provides. In a frontier-takeoff model, the cheaper source at a given allocation is set by a threshold in demand capacity. Demand capacity is the share of compute customers can use once the frontier is finished. Research should pay too only when demand capacity lies below the threshold. Where the threshold sits depends on how fast the value of customer services diminishes. In the calibration it rises with the service budget, because each further unit taken from customers costs them more. Safety paid for by customers should come early: its ratio to customer compute falls over the takeoff unless safety productivity rises fast. In a calibration with a tight service budget, early customer-funded safety lowers the probability of catastrophe from 75 to 51 percent. That is within one point of the best rule found that may also slow research.
