<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

利用2005年至2021年间10个亚洲新兴市场经济体的企业-银行关联数据，我们研究了僵尸企业在国内及跨境层面的影响。我们得出三项主要发现。第一，过去15年间，亚洲新兴市场中的僵尸企业数量显著增加，这一现象由弱质银行的“续贷”行为所支撑。第二，僵尸企业在国内的普遍存在抑制了企业绩效，挤出了健康企业，并压低了通货膨胀与GDP增长。第三，僵尸企业的宏观经济效应跨越国界：亚洲新兴市场经济体中僵尸企业的暴露度显著降低了发达经济体的通胀与增长。这些溢出效应主要通过全球价值链（GVCs）及中间品进口价格渠道发挥作用，而跨境银行关联并未扮演重要角色。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Using firm-bank linked data for 10 Asian emerging market economies (EMEs) over 2005–2021, we study the domestic and cross-border implications of zombie firms. We document three main findings. First, the number of zombie firms in emerging Asia has increased significantly over the past 15 years, sustained by evergreening practices from weak banks. Second, zombie prevalence domestically depresses firm performance, crowds out healthy firms, and depresses inflation and GDP growth. Third, the macroeconomic effects of zombie firms extend across borders: expo sure to zombie firms in Asian EMEs significantly reduces inflation and growth in advanced economies. These spillover effects operate mainly through global value chains (GVCs) and an intermediate goods import price channel, while cross-border bank linkages do not play any major role.
