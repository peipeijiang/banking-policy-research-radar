# The Effect of Asset Allocation Policy, Interest Rates, Inflation, Mutual Fund Size, and Mutual Fund Age on Mutual Fund Performance in Indonesia

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究旨在考察资产配置政策、利率（BI Rate）、通货膨胀、基金规模和基金年龄对2021—2024年期间印度尼西亚传统共同基金业绩的影响。该研究的动因在于共同基金投资者数量不断增加，但行业资产净值（Net Asset Value, NAV）并未随之持续增长。研究采用定量关联方法，使用从金融服务管理局（OJK）、印度尼西亚银行、印度尼西亚统计局（BPS）、Bareksa以及共同基金产品说明书获取的二手数据。样本包括通过目的性抽样选取的32只传统共同基金。数据分析采用描述性统计、经典假设检验以及使用SPSS进行的多元线性回归。研究结果表明，回归模型满足经典假设，适合用于假设检验。各自变量同时影响共同基金业绩。在部分影响方面，BI Rate、通货膨胀、基金规模和基金年龄对共同基金业绩表现出不同程度的影响，而资产配置政策变量由于为常量而被排除在回归模型之外，无法进一步分析。这些发现表明，宏观经济状况和共同基金内部特征在决定印度尼西亚传统共同基金业绩方面发挥着重要作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study aims to examine the effect of asset allocation policy, interest rate (BI Rate), inflation, fund size, and fund age on the performance of conventional mutual funds in Indonesia during the 2021–2024 period. The study is motivated by the increasing number of mutual fund investors that was not accompanied by a consistent increase in the industry's Net Asset Value (NAV). A quantitative associative approach was employed using secondary data obtained from the Financial Services Authority (OJK), Bank Indonesia, Statistics Indonesia (BPS), Bareksa, and the mutual funds' fact sheets. The sample consisted of 32 conventional mutual funds selected through purposive sampling. Data were analyzed using descriptive statistics, classical assumption tests, and multiple linear regression with SPSS. The findings indicate that the regression model satisfied the classical assumptions and was appropriate for hypothesis testing. Simultaneously, the independent variables affected mutual fund performance. Partially, the BI Rate, inflation, fund size, and fund age showed varying effects on mutual fund performance, whereas the asset allocation policy variable could not be further analyzed because it was constant and excluded from the regression model. These findings indicate that macroeconomic conditions and internal mutual fund characteristics play an important role in determining the performance of conventional mutual funds in Indonesia.
