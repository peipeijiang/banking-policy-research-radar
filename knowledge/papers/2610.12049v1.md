<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

德国的雇主声称，集体协议限制了他们在工作时间方面的灵活性。我们利用2008年至2024年IAB企业面板数据，考察了退出集体谈判后的工作时间调整情况。通过固定效应模型和事件研究模型，我们发现退出集体谈判仅与每周工作时间约0.3%至0.6%的小幅增加相关，相当于每周增加八至十三分钟。这一调整发生在退出集体谈判覆盖后不久。我们进一步发现，没有证据表明退出后会更广泛地使用其他工作时间调整边际，例如周六或周日工作。与以往的调查证据相反，我们的研究结果对工作时间限制是退出集体谈判覆盖的主要驱动因素之一这一观点提出了质疑。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Employers in Germany argue that collective agreements restrict their flexibility concerning working hours. Using data from the IAB Establishment Panel for the years 2008 to 2024, we investigate working-time adjustments after exit from collective bargaining. We find that exit is associated with only a modest increase in weekly working hours of around 0.3 to 0.6 per cent using fixed-effects and event-study models, corresponding to eight to thirteen additional minutes per week. This adjustment occurs shortly after leaving collective bargaining coverage. We further find no evidence that other margins of working-time adjustment, such as Saturday or Sunday work, are used more extensively after exit. In contrast to previous survey evidence, our findings call into question that working-hours constraints are one of the main drivers of exit from collective bargaining coverage.
