# The Role of Precedence Order in Matching with Multi-Criteria Admissions

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29139v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文研究多标准录取中优先级顺序对结果的影响，通过理论推导和机制比较，发现反转最后两个区块的优先级会使每个标准偏好其区块靠后的结果，但在某些条件下可能反转。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

录取系统通常通过依次应用特定标准的排名来填补名额。我们推导了当排名可能任意不对齐，且每个标准通过响应性偏好评估整个录取集合时，关于优先顺序的标准-wise比较静态分析。对于固定的申请者集合，反转最后两个区块会使每个标准弱偏好其区块被置于较后位置的结果。如果两个区块之后还有另一个标准，这种比较可能会反转，即使在保留型排名下也是如此。在学生提议的延迟接受机制下，由于拒绝链会改变焦点学院的申请者集合，这种比较也可能在两个标准下反转；这种反馈是唯一可能的失败来源。在常规大规模市场中，失败几乎对所有学院消失，并且在足够厚度的条件下，跨学院一致消失。在波士顿机制下，最终录取阻止了反馈；因此，对于固定的提交排名顺序列表和标准无关的可接受性，该比较在每个有限市场中均成立。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Admission systems often fill seats by applying criterion-specific rankings sequentially. We derive criterion-wise comparative statics with respect to precedence order when rankings may be arbitrarily nonaligned, and each criterion evaluates the entire admitted set through a responsive preference. For a fixed applicant set, reversing the final two blocks makes each criterion weakly prefer the outcome in which its block is later. This comparison can reverse if two blocks are followed by another criterion, even under reserve-type rankings. Under student-proposing deferred acceptance, it can also reverse with two criteria because rejection chains change the focal college's applicant set; such feedback is the only possible source of failure. In regular large markets, failures vanish for almost all colleges and, under sufficient thickness, uniformly across colleges. Under the Boston mechanism, final acceptance prevents feedback; thus the comparison holds in every finite market for fixed submitted rank-order lists and criterion-independent acceptability.
