<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究双边多对多匹配市场，其中 agent 的偏好满足强可替代性（strong substitutability）和总需求法则（law of aggregate demand, LAD）。在该框架下，稳定匹配集合构成一个有限分配格，分位数稳定匹配（quantile stable matchings）存在并满足利益极化性质。基于 Bonifacio 等（2022）的环约简机制，我们给出了分位数稳定匹配的一个纯组合刻画，该刻画以偏好环及其诱导的优先序表述。具体而言，我们在环上定义了一个序论计数函数，并证明每个分位数稳定匹配对应于从企业最优稳定匹配出发，消去一个内生确定的闭环集合。该刻画将 Cheng（2008）基于轮换（rotation）的框架从一对一市场推广到多对多市场，并避免了在整个稳定集上进行穷举式结果枚举或伙伴排序的需要。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study two-sided many-to-many matching markets where agents' preferences satisfy strong substitutability and the law of aggregate demand (LAD). In this framework, the set of stable matchings forms a finite distributive lattice, and quantile stable matchings exist and satisfy the polarization of interests. Building on the cycle-reduction machinery of Bonifacio et al. (2022), we provide a purely combinatorial characterization of quantile stable matchings formulated in terms of preference cycles and their induced precedence order. Specifically, we define an order-theoretic counting function on cycles and prove that each quantile stable matching corresponds to the elimination of an endogenously determined closed set of cycles starting from the firm-optimal stable matching. This characterization extends the rotation-based framework of Cheng (2008) from one-to-one markets to many-to-many markets and bypasses the need for exhaustive outcome enumeration or partner sorting across the entire stable set.

</details>
