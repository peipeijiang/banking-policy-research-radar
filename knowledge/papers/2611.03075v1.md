<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

不完整的记录即使在没有任何一对结果被联合观测到的情况下，也能收紧对联合结果概率的界。我推导出了尖锐的逐点Fréchet–Hoeffding界，将已知的总体边际分布与选择性观测的记录相结合，且不对观测机制施加限制。我建立了识别问题到有限维优化的精确归约，给出了两个界的可计算刻画。涉及收入流动性、潜在结果和分卷问卷的示例展示了不完整记录如何相对于经典界改进识别。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Incomplete records can sharpen bounds on joint outcome probabilities even when no pair of outcomes is jointly observed. I derive sharp pointwise Fréchet--Hoeffding bounds combining known population marginals with selectively observed records, without restricting the observation mechanism. I establish an exact reduction of the identification problem to finite-dimensional optimization, yielding computable characterizations of both bounds. Illustrations involving income mobility, potential outcomes, and split questionnaires demonstrate how incomplete records can improve identification relative to the classical bounds.
