<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文提出了一个投影工具变量框架，该框架放宽了利用工具变量进行因果识别时的传统假设。基于将条件期望解释为希尔伯特空间中的正交投影，该框架用一般投影算子替代条件期望算子。我们基于Riesz表示定理提供了一种替代的逆问题表述。借助半参数效率理论，我们进一步发展了用于估计和推断的去偏自适应机器学习方法。所提出的框架将无效工具变量与工具化双重差分（instrumented difference-in-differences）联系起来。我们还考察了无效投影工具变量，并讨论了其与现有方法的关系。模拟研究和实际数据分析表明，所提出的方法与现有方法相比表现良好。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper, we develop a projected instrumental variable framework that relaxes conventional assumptions for causal identification with instrumental variables. Building on the interpretation of conditional expectation as an orthogonal projection in a Hilbert space, the framework replaces the conditional expectation operator with a general projection operator. We provide an alternative inverse problem formulation based on the Riesz representation. Drawing on semiparametric efficiency theory, we further develop debiased and adaptive machine learning methods for estimation and inference. The proposed framework connects invalid instrumental variables to instrumented difference-in-differences. We also examine invalid projected instrumental variables and discuss their relationship to existing methods. Simulation studies and real data analysis demonstrate that the proposed methods perform favorably compared with existing approaches.
