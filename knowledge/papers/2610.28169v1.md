<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文提出了一种基于得分的Tobit回归模型简易设定检验，通过计算高效的多乘数bootstrap（乘数自助法）实现。我们通过广义Tobit残差条件均值为零的条件矩限制来检验设定是否正确，并采用积分条件矩方法构建可行的基于得分的经验过程，以估计指数为条件，从而无需平滑或调节参数。我们推导了可行过程的一阶一致表示，展示了其极限行为如何受结构参数的最大似然估计影响，并获得了相应Kolmogorov--Smirnov统计量在原假设、固定备择假设和一列局部备择假设下的渐近性质。由于极限零分布非枢轴，$p$值通过模仿一阶表示的多乘数bootstrap获得，从而捕捉参数估计的不确定性。蒙特卡洛实验——包括未知尺度设计、指数可见/指数不可见的功效研究以及与现有设定检验的比较——证实了在一系列设计和协变量维度下具有准确的检验水平和有竞争力的功效。两个实证应用进一步展示了我们方法的实际效用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper, we propose a simple score-based specification test for the Tobit regression model, implemented through a computationally efficient multiplier bootstrap. We test correct specification through the conditional moment restriction that the generalized Tobit residual has zero conditional mean, and construct a feasible score-based empirical process using the integrated conditional moments approach, conditioning on the estimated index so that no smoothing or tuning parameter is required. We derive a first-order uniform representation of the feasible process, showing how its limiting behavior is affected by maximum-likelihood estimation of the structural parameter, and obtain the asymptotic properties of the corresponding Kolmogorov--Smirnov statistic under the null, fixed alternatives, and a sequence of local alternatives. Since the limiting null distribution is non-pivotal, $p$-values are obtained from a multiplier bootstrap that mimics the first-order representation and therefore captures the parameter estimation uncertainty. Monte Carlo experiments, including an unknown-scale design, an index-visible/index-invisible power study, and comparisons with existing specification tests, confirm accurate size and competitive power across a range of designs and covariate dimensions. Two empirical applications further demonstrate the practical utility of our approach.
