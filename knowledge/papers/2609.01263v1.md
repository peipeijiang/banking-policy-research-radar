<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人工智能（AI）正通过改变生产过程、劳动力市场以及企业与行业的组织结构，迅速重塑经济体系。AI不仅仅是一项技术创新，从根本上说，它更是一种重大的经济现象和一股新的创新浪潮，对生产率、就业、市场结构、公共政策、长期经济增长以及社会整体福利都具有重要意义。本文通过分析AI影响经济活动和社会福祉的多重渠道，对AI的经济学进行了探讨。文章认为，AI应同时被理解为一种资本形式、一种合成劳动力、一种通用技术，以及一种经济基础设施。分析表明，AI通过自动化、增强、优化、预测和创新等途径，成为生产率增长的多渠道引擎。文章指出，AI有潜力提高产出、降低成本、激发创业活力并加速经济增长，同时也会通过劳动力替代、劳动力互补和创造性破坏重塑劳动力市场。本文进一步考察了AI对竞争、市场集中度及创业活动的影响。最后，文章探讨了AI对消费者、生产者、劳动者、政府及整个社会的福利影响，并在结论中概述了旨在最大化AI收益、同时缓解其潜在不利后果的政策考量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is rapidly transforming economic systems by altering production processes, labor markets, and the structure of firms and industries. AI is not merely a technological innovation. It is fundamentally a major economic phenomenon and a new wave of innovation with important implications for productivity, employment, market structure, public policy, long-run economic growth, and collective welfare. This essay examines the economics of AI by analyzing the multiple channels through which AI influences economic activity and societal well-being. It argues that AI should be understood simultaneously as a form of capital, a form of synthetic labor, a general-purpose technology, as well as an economic infrastructure. The analysis shows that AI functions as a multi-channel engine of productivity growth through automation, augmentation, optimization, prediction, and innovation. It is argued that AI has the potential to increase output, reduce costs, stimulate entrepreneurship, and accelerate economic growth, while also reshaping labor markets through labor substitution, labor complementarity, and creative destruction. The essay further examines AI's effects on competition, market concentration, and entrepreneurship. Finally, it explores the welfare implications of AI for consumers, producers, workers, governments, and society as a whole, and concludes by outlining policy considerations aimed at maximizing the benefits of AI while mitigating its potential adverse consequences.
