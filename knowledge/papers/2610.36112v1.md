<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

同一批学生的不同授课教师之间评分标准的差异，构成了道德风险问题的证据，无论成绩是否被虚高。一门课程各教学班的最优评分标准应当相同，然而各班的成绩却存在很大差异。我们构建了一个委托—代理模型，其中了解最优标准的教师仍会偏离该标准，或宽松或严苛，取决于其激励。对偏离院系平均成绩的行为进行惩罚不会改变平均值，但会压缩评分方差，从而提高学生福利。相反，向学生披露相同信息则会增大评分方差。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Variation in grading standards across instructors who teach the same students is evidence of a moral hazard problem, whether or not grades are inflated. The optimal standard is the same for every section of a course, yet grades vary widely across sections. We present a principal-agent model in which instructors who know the optimal standard still depart from it, leniently or harshly, depending on their incentives. Penalizing deviations from the department average leaves the average unchanged but compresses grading variance, which raises student welfare. Disclosing the same information to students instead increases grading variance.

</details>
