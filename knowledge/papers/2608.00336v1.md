<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在许多实际应用中，研究者往往只能获得真实回归变量的含噪声代理指标，这通常被认为会引发衰减偏误。然而，在线性均值模型中，估计出的同伴效应却可能被放大，进而可能导致错误的阳性结论。本文证明，渐近偏误的方向取决于个体特征与网络连接之间的交互作用，并展示了网络结构如何在不依赖额外外部信息的情况下促进识别。基于这些识别结果，我们提出了易于实施且具有一致性的广义矩估计（GMM）与两阶段最小二乘（2SLS）估计量。我们通过蒙特卡洛模拟对上述结果进行了说明。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In many practical applications, only noisy proxies for the true regressors are available, which is commonly believed to induce an attenuation bias. In the linear-in-means model, however, estimated peer effects might be inflated, potentially leading to false positives. This paper shows that the asymptotic bias depends on the interplay between individual characteristics and network links and demonstrates how the network structure can facilitate identification without the need for additional external information. Based on these identification results, we present consistent GMM and 2SLS estimators that are easily implementable. Our results are illustrated by means of a Monte Carlo simulation.
