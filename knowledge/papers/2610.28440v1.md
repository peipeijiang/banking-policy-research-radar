本文在具有沉没成本、进入壁垒、企业特定技术进步和不可逆退出的双寡头博弈中，建立了直观精炼马尔可夫完美均衡（Markov-perfect equilibrium）的存在性与本质唯一性。我们开发了一种算法，通过求解有限序列辅助收缩映射（auxiliary contraction mappings）的不动点来快速计算均衡，我们将该方法命名为伪逆向归纳法（Fake Backward Induction）。我们通过对一个随机增长市场中竞争政策的定量分析，展示了该博弈的实用价值，该市场中企业通过老化过程进行异质性学习（learning by aging）。计算该示例的一个均衡在单个 Apple M5 Pro 核心上耗时 10.2 秒。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper establishes the existence and essential uniqueness of an intuitively refined Markov-perfect equilibrium in a duopoly game with sunk costs, barriers to entry, firm-specific technological progress, and irreversible exit. We develop an algorithm that quickly computes the equilibrium by solving for fixed points of a finite sequence of auxiliary contraction mappings, which we label Fake Backward Induction. We illustrate the game's practical value with a quantitative analysis of competition policy in a stochastically growing market with firms idiosyncratically learning by aging. Computing one equilibrium of this example takes 10.2 seconds on one Apple M5 Pro core.
