# Reducing Talent Misallocation Through Policy Signals: Theory and Evidence

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

长期以来，劳动力市场参与中的性别和种族障碍一直与人才和资本配置不当相关联，并对总体生产率产生显著影响。我们研究了2018年沙特阿拉伯解除女性驾车禁令的总体效应。我们发现，企业和劳动者对该政策公告的反应非常迅速，且该公告与就业增加及工作匹配质量提升相关。有证据表明，劳动者在企业间出现了显著的重新配置以响应这一政策，招聘（任何）女性的企业比例激增，男性和女性员工在企业间的流动也有所增加。我们构建了一个框架，考察取消驾驶限制的公告如何减少劳动力市场中的扭曲，促使企业开始雇用女性，提高企业效率，并在女性获准驾车之前就增加女性和男性的就业与工资。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Gender and race-based barriers to labor market participation have long been associated with misallocation of talent and capital, and notable impacts on aggregate productivity. We examine the aggregate effects of the 2018 lift of the women’s driving ban in Saudi Arabia. We find that firms and workers react to the announcement of the policy very quickly, and that the announcement is associated with increases in employment and the quality of job matches. There is evidence of significant reallocation of workers across firms in response to the policy, with a surge in the share of firms that hire (any) women and increased movement of male and female employees across firms. We develop a framework that examines how the announcement of the elimination of driving restrictions reduces distortions in the labor market, inducing firms to begin hiring women, raising firm efficiency, and increasing employment and wages for women and men, even before women were permitted to drive.
