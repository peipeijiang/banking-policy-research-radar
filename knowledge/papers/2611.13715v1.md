<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

总体结果可能掩盖网络配置，而网络配置会使家庭面临不可保险的风险并需要不同的公共服务。我们研究一个监管者在双群体网络博弈中对矫正性活动收费和信息制度的联合选择。尽管总体行为遵循精确的稳定规律，但不同的专业化均衡需要不同的针对性干预。在购买高斯监测的情况下，原始条件意味着在无购买信息的强监管与知情专业化之间存在不连续切换。监管者也可以选择获得签名认证，或通过由承诺的随机审计支持的报告来引出私人已知状态。我们刻画了三种制度的优化值，以及可信披露取代政府监测的成本阈值。制度选择也会改变最优监管：即使认证和真实报告揭示相同的状态，昂贵的审计也倾向于较少的专业化和更高的矫正性收费。这种排序以及审计成本中的单调性在明确的内部性条件下成立，而无需审计问题的全局凹性。制度比较在不存在预期政策反馈的基准情形中求解，而在附加正则条件下，政策切换和严格的制度排序允许局部持续性。结果取决于可执行的验证、数据访问限制以及明确受限的制度菜单。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Aggregate outcomes can conceal network configurations that expose households to uninsurable risk and require different public services. We study a regulator's joint choice of a corrective activity charge and an information institution in a two-population network game. Although aggregate behavior follows an exact stable law, distinct specialization equilibria require different targeted interventions. With purchased Gaussian monitoring, primitive conditions imply a discontinuous switch between strong regulation without purchased information and informed specialization. The regulator may alternatively obtain signed certification or elicit a privately known state through a report backed by committed random audits. We characterize the three institutions' optimized values and the cost thresholds at which credible disclosure replaces government monitoring. The institutional choice also changes optimal regulation: even when certification and truthful reporting reveal the same state, costly audits favor less specialization and a higher corrective charge. This ordering and monotonicity in audit costs hold under an explicit interiority condition without global concavity of the audit problem. The institutional comparison is solved in the benchmark without feedback from anticipated policy, while the policy switch and strict institutional rankings admit local persistence under additional regularity conditions. Results depend on enforceable verification, restrictions on data access, and an explicitly limited institutional menu.

</details>
