# Siting and Curtailment Depth Limit the Grid Headroom Available to New Large Loads

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00462v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该研究用覆盖美国16个区域的国家能源系统模型，对小时级发电与大型负荷削减进行协同优化，刻画数据中心等新增大型负荷可获得的电网余量；发现50 GW负荷虽可在年削减不足0.1%的条件下接入，但多数区域至少有一小时需削减超过90%，且已宣布的数据中心选址与建模余量不匹配，依赖现有发电机组还会推高CO2排放与批发电价。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

数据中心需求的增长速度超过了发电能力的建设速度，这促使人们提出在高压时段减少用电的条件下接入大型负荷的方案。关于通过灵活性可以增加多少负荷的估算依赖于简化的核算方式，且仅报告年度削减电量。本文采用一个全国能源系统模型，对全美16个地区的逐时发电与负荷削减进行协同优化，以刻画启用余量（headroom）所带来的灵活性和电力系统影响。结果表明，虽然可以在年用电量削减不足0.1%的情况下满足50 GW的负荷需求，但较小的年度削减量掩盖了潜在的严苛运行要求。大多数地区至少在某一个小时需要超过90%的削减深度，因此负荷必须断开或具备足够的现场资源以提供完全备用。此外，已公布的数据中心选址与模型计算的余量并不匹配，且依赖现有发电机组会增加CO2排放和批发电价。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Data center demand is increasing faster than generation can be built, encouraging proposals to interconnect large loads on condition that they reduce consumption during high-stress periods. Estimates of how much load can be added via flexibility rest on simplified accounting and report only annual curtailed electricity. Here we use a national energy systems model that co-optimizes hourly generation and load curtailment across 16 U.S. regions to characterize the flexibility and power system impacts of enabling headroom. While 50 GW can be served while curtailing less than 0.1% of its annual energy, small annual curtailment conceals potentially severe operating requirements. Most regions require curtailment deeper than 90% in at least one hour, so the load must disconnect or have sufficient on-site resources to provide full back up. Furthermore, announced data center locations are not aligned with modeled headroom and relying on existing generation fleets raises CO2 emissions and wholesale electricity prices.

</details>
