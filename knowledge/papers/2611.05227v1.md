# Regret-based Characterization, Information Limits, and the Sample Complexity of Auditing Adaptive Policies

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05227v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文证明有限期MDP中自适应策略与开环边际策略的性能差等于各期Q值与策略动作的协方差之和，据此给出由策略所用信息量界定的自适应差距上界，并推导审计该差距所需的样本复杂度下界，同时指出确定性非常数策略不可审计。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一项策略可以通过其平均定位或通过对信息作出反应来获得性能。对于有限时域MDP中的任意马尔可夫策略$π$，令$ν$为其开环边际分布，该分布在每一步给出$π$的平均动作分布。我们证明，在内生动态下，$J(π)-J(ν)$等于$\sum_t tr Cov(Q^ν_t,π_t)$。仅基于即时成本的审计可能出现符号错误。将协方差写成联合与乘积之差，可得到界$|J(π)-J(ν)|\le\sum_t span_t\sqrt{I(s_t;a_t)/2}$，且常数是紧的。因此，一个MDP的自适应间隙（adaptivity gap）由其最优策略所使用的信息所界定。从$π$自身的bandit反馈日志中估计$J(π)-J(ν)$需要$Θ(\barχ^2 \log(1/η)/δ^2)$轮，其中$\barχ^2$是平均$χ^2$-散度。同时，确定性的非恒定策略根本无法被审计。我们还为分幕式MDP提供了一种重要性采样审计器。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A policy can earn its performance by where it locates on average or by reacting to information. For any Markov policy $π$ in a finite-horizon MDP, let $ν$ be its open-loop marginal, which plays $π$'s average action distribution at each step. We show that $J(π)-J(ν)$ equals $\sum_t tr Cov(Q^ν_t,π_t)$ under endogenous dynamics. Audits based on immediate costs alone can have the wrong sign. Writing the covariance as a joint-versus-product gap gives the bound $|J(π)-J(ν)|\le\sum_t span_t\sqrt{I(s_t;a_t)/2}$, with a sharp constant. Hence, an MDP's adaptivity gap is bounded by the information that its optimal policy uses. Estimating $J(π)-J(ν)$ from $π$'s own bandit-feedback logs takes $Θ(\barχ^2 \log(1/η)/δ^2)$ rounds, where $\barχ^2$ is an average $χ^2$-divergence. At the same time, deterministic non-constant policies cannot be audited at all. We also provide an importance-sampling auditor for episodic MDPs.

</details>
