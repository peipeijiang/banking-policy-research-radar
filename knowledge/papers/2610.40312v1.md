<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文证明了一个刚性定理，该定理在一般决策论约束与弱洛伦兹支配（weak Lorenz dominance）下刻画了基尼型洛伦兹面积排序（Gini-type Lorenz-area ordering），从而解决了这一平等主义研究路线所追寻的刻画问题。当然，这并未使更广泛的探究走向终结。在某种意义上，它或许反而标志着我们试图通过共同的数学语言将社会选择理论、福利经济学与形式道德哲学相联结的尝试迈出了超越 infancy 的一步。主要证明策略最初由作者本人获得，尽管 5.6sol 和 Astra 在没有任何额外提示的情况下也能毫不费力地复现它。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper proves a rigidity theorem characterizing the Gini-type Lorenz-area ordering under general decision-theoretic constraints together with weak Lorenz dominance, thereby resolving the characterization problem pursued in this line of research on egalitarianism. This does not, of course, bring the broader inquiry to an end. In a certain sense, it may instead mark a step beyond the infancy of our attempt to connect social choice theory, welfare economics, and formal moral philosophy through a common mathematical language. The main proof strategy was originally obtained by the author's own hand, though 5.6sol and Astra could effortlessly reproduce it without any additional hint.
