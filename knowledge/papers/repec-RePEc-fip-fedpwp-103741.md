# How Do Large, Sophisticated Cryptocurrency Trades Impact Broader DeFi Market Dynamics?

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

利用比特币（BTC）、以太坊（ETH）和包装比特币（WBTC）的交易级数据，并与公开的“巨鲸警报”（Whale Alert）信号进行匹配，我们考察了大额加密货币交易如何塑造市场微观结构。具体而言，我们检验了巨鲸交易是否改变了活跃的非巨鲸参与者构成、是否引发了领导者—追随者式的羊群行为，以及是否抬高了短期回报波动率。我们记录到网络之间显著的差异：巨鲸警报通过调动先前不活跃的散户和机构投资者，大幅重塑了（原生）BTC的参与者结构，而在以太坊平台上，ETH和WBTC的参与者画像则保持高度稳定。此外，非巨鲸投资者在BTC市场中表现出强烈的领导者—追随者行为——与巨鲸方向同步买入和卖出。这一模式在以太坊生态系统中基本不存在，仅在最大的非巨鲸交易层级中有所体现。这些行为动态直接映射了市场稳定性：巨鲸警报引发BTC波动率在24小时内的短暂飙升（在WBTC警报后最为剧烈），但在以太坊平台上则伴随着波动率的压缩。此外，这些不对称的市场反应在以太坊从工作量证明（proof-of-work）向权益证明（proof-of-stake）的过渡期间持续存在。这些发现表明，数字资产市场中大型投资者与小型投资者之间存在着持续的信息和结构性不对称。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Using transaction-level data for Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and Wrapped Bitcoin (WBTC) matched to public “Whale Alert” signals, we examine how large-scale cryptocurrency trades shape market microstructure. Specifically, we test whether whale transactions alter the composition of active non-whale participants, trigger leader-follower herd behavior, and elevate short-term return volatility. We document a stark divergence between networks: Whale alerts heavily reshape (native) BTC participation by mobilizing previously inactive retail and institutional investors, whereas ETH and WBTC participant profiles remain highly stable on the Ethereum platform. Furthermore, non-whale investors exhibit strong leader-follower behavior in the BTC market — buying and selling in tandem with whale direction. This pattern is largely absent in the Ethereum ecosystem, except among the largest non-whale tranches. These behavioral dynamics directly mirror market stability: Whale alerts induce a brief 24-hour spike in BTC volatility (most acutely following WBTC alerts) but coincide with compressed volatility on the Ethereum platform. Additionally, these asymmetric market responses persisted across Ethereum’s transition from proof-of-work to proof-of-stake. These findings indicate persistent informational and structural asymmetries between large and small digital-asset investors.
