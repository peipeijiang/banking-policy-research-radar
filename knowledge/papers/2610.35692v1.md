<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究在大型匿名玩家群体中反复进行的博弈的稳态，这些玩家会收到关于其对手策略的部分事后反馈。与经典贝叶斯框架不同，玩家并不将其猜测视为对策略环境的正确描述，而是将其视为对仅被部分建模和部分观测的现实的近似。我们引入了带有反馈和不确定性态度的博弈，其中标准型结构被赋予一组反馈函数，并为每个玩家赋予一个偏好泛函，该泛函编码了对模型误设和模糊性的厌恶。由此诱导出的误设敏感准则将变分评估、最大最小评估和平滑模糊评估作为特例包含在内，并且当信念是明确的且玩家对误设持中性态度时，退化为期望效用。此类博弈的适当稳态概念是模型误设下的自确认均衡（Self-Confirming Equilibrium under Model Misspecification, SCEM），它要求每个玩家对一组结构化猜测做出最优反应，而均衡策略所产生的反馈无法证伪这些猜测。与经典理论及其模糊厌恶扩展相比，混合策略纳什均衡不必是自确认的。因此，我们发展了一个伴随概念——稳健纳什均衡（Robust Nash Equilibrium, R-NE），其中每个被支持的行动在变分评估下保持最优。稳健纳什均衡精炼了SCEM，只要统计距离连续即存在，从而给出SCEM的存在性，并在完全信息反馈下与后者一致。在观测一致的散度和可观测收益下，R-NE和SCEM分别退化为纳什均衡和模糊性下的自确认均衡（Self-Confirming Equilibrium under Ambiguity, SCEA），后者推广了平滑自确认均衡。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the steady states of games played recurrently within large anonymous populations of players who receive partial ex-post feedback about the play of their opponents. Departing from the classical Bayesian account, players do not trust their conjectures as a correct description of the strategic environment but consider them as approximations of a reality that is only partially modeled and partially observed. We introduce games with feedback and uncertainty attitudes, in which the normal-form structure is enriched with a profile of feedback functions and, for each player, a preference functional that encodes aversion to misspecification and to ambiguity. The induced misspecification sensitive criterion contains the variational, maxmin, and smooth-ambiguity evaluations as special cases and collapses to expected utility when beliefs are unambiguous and the player is neutral to misspecification. The appropriate steady-state notion for such games is the Self-Confirming Equilibrium under Model Misspecification (SCEM), which requires each player to best respond to a set of structured conjectures that the feedback generated by equilibrium play cannot falsify. In contrast with the classical theory and with its ambiguity-averse extensions, mixed-strategy Nash Equilibria need not be self-confirming. We therefore develop a companion notion of Robust Nash Equilibrium (R-NE), in which every supported action remains optimal under a variational evaluation. Robust Nash equilibria refine SCEM, exist whenever the statistical distances are continuous, thereby delivering existence of SCEM, and coincide with the latter under fully informative feedback. Under observationally consistent divergences and observable payoffs, R-NE and SCEM reduce, respectively, to Nash equilibrium and Self-Confirming Equilibrium under Ambiguity (SCEA), which generalizes smooth self-confirming equilibrium.
