# Randomized to Opportunity: Measuring Place Effects for Refugee Children

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13049.html)

## 一句话结论

> 利用丹麦1986-1998年难民分散安置的准随机分配，结合永久居民的mover-exposure设计，检验地区经济机会指标能否预测难民儿童的长期收入、就业、犯罪与青少年生育结果。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

区域层面的经济机会衡量指标越来越多地被用于指导基于地方的政策。我们研究这些指标是否能预测弱势群体的长期结果：超过12，000名难民儿童在丹麦1986-1998年分散安置政策下被准随机分配到各市镇。我们聚焦于儿童抵达时已有的预定机会衡量指标，并在永久居民中采用迁移者-暴露设计（mover-exposure design）对其进行验证。从一个低机会地区迁移到高机会地区（增加两个标准差），会使成年后收入排名提高3个百分位点（7.5%），就业率提高4个百分点。同时，刑事定罪减少14%，青少年生育减少19%，对教育 attainment 的影响则较为温和。经济机会对犯罪和青少年生育的预测能力强于其他地区特征，包括当地犯罪率。对于成年收入而言，地区层面的不平等是一个特别强的预测因素。这些发现表明，难民儿童成长地区的社会流动性对其长期福祉具有实质性影响。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Area-level measures of economic opportunity are increasingly used to guide place-based policies. We study whether these measures predict long-run outcomes for a vulnerable population: over 12,000 refugee children quasi-randomly assigned to municipalities under Denmark's 1986–1998 dispersal policy. We focus on predetermined measures of opportunity available when children arrive and validate them using a mover-exposure design among permanent residents. Moving from a low- to a high-opportunity area (a two-standard-deviation increase) raises adult income rank by 3 percentile points (7.5 percent) and employment by 4 percentage points. It also reduces criminal convictions by 14 percent and teen parenthood by 19 percent, with more modest effects on educational attainment. Economic opportunity predicts crime and teen parenthood more strongly than other area characteristics, including local crime rates. For adult income, area-level inequality is a particularly strong predictor. These findings suggest that social mobility in the areas where refugee children grow up meaningfully shapes their long-term wellbeing.

</details>
