# Determinants of Common Stock Prices: Evidence from NEPSE-Listed Commercial Banks in Nepal

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究探讨了在尼泊尔证券交易所（NEPSE）上市的尼泊尔商业银行普通股价格的影响因素。具体而言，研究考察了六个关键内部变量的影响：收益收益率（E/P）、每股股利（DPS）、每股账面价值（BVPS）、净资产收益率（ROE）、资产收益率（ROA）和企业规模（Ln_SIZE）。本研究采用因果效应研究设计。数据采用定量方法和演绎法进行分析以检验假设。实证主义哲学指导了对现实的判定。NEPSE所有上市商业银行均被作为研究总体和样本。研究采用2015年至2025年的横截面面板数据，数据提取自相关机构的经审计年度报告和NEPSE网站。使用SPSS-26，在分析描述性统计、相关性和多元回归之前，确认了正态性。结果表明，DPS、BVPS、ROE和ROA与MPS（每股市价）呈显著正相关。相反，E/P和SIZE与MPS呈负相关。回归结果表明，DPS、BVPS、ROE和ROA对股票价格具有统计上显著的正向影响（p ˂ 0.01）。相比之下，E/P和Ln_SIZE被发现有负向影响。结果表明，盈利能力和股利支付显著提升股票价格。另一方面，市值较大或收益收益率相对较高的银行会对股价产生下行压力。这些发现为投资者和学者提供了一个富有洞见的框架，有助于了解关键基本面因素如何显著引导尼泊尔商业银行部门普通股的市场价格。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study investigates the influencing factors of common stock prices among Nepalese commercial banks that are listed in NEPSE. Specifically, it examines the impacts of six key internal variables; earnings yield (E/P), dividend per share (DPS), book value per share (BVPS), return on equity (ROE), return on assets (ROA) and firm size (Ln_SIZE). The study employed the causal effect research design. Data analyzed by employing quantitative method and deductive approach to test hypotheses. Positivist philosophy guided to dictate the reality. All listed commercial banks in NEPSE were taken as the population as well as sample of the study. The study employed cross sectional panel data from 2015 to 2025, extracted from the audited annual report of concern institutions and NEPSE website. By using SPSS-26, before analyzing descriptive statistics, correlation and multiple regression, normality was confirmed. The results exhibit a significant positive association of DPS, BVPS, ROE and ROA with MPS. Conversely, E/P and SIZE were found to have negative association with MPS. Regression results show that DPS, BVPS, ROE and ROA had a statistically significant positive impact on stock prices (p ˂ 0.01). In contrast, E/P and Ln_SIZE were found to be negative elements. The results suggest that the profitability and dividend payout significantly appreciate stock prices. On the other hand, banks having larger capitalization or relatively higher earnings yield put pressure to downsize the stock prices. These findings offer an insightful framework for investors and academicians to know how key fundamentals elements significantly guide market price of common stocks of Nepalese commercial banking sectors.
