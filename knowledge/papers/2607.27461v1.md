<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出一个用于动态投资组合管理的简洁框架，其输入仅需每日价格、交易量和市值。其状态由三个固定大小的矩阵构成，这些矩阵均基于价格历史构建：收益相关性的距离矩阵，以及两个马尔可夫链的转移矩阵，这两个马尔可夫链分别按月对标准普尔500指数成分股按滞后收益和滞后波动率进行排序。这三个矩阵完全依赖于价格历史，与马科维茨均值-方差优化所依赖的信息相同，但用它们替代了后者的期望收益向量和协方差矩阵。我们的方法无需矩阵求逆，基于对异常值稳健的横截面排名运作，并且是动态的而非单期静态的。实证结果表明，波动率排名可提前一期预测，而收益排名则几乎不可预测。基于这些预测构建的投资组合——一个市场中性动量多空组合，叠加一个机会型纯多头部分——在两个不重叠的样本外测试区间（2022年1月至2024年12月，以及2025年1月至2026年7月）中均跑赢市场，其夏普比率分别为1.06和1.32，而同期市场为0.78和1.14，且已扣除五个基点的交易成本并按日盯市。该组合也优于经典的最小方差组合和最大分散化组合。通过残差距离对多头部分进行分散化处理，在两个区间内均进一步提升了表现，将夏普比率提升至1.08和1.44，年化收益率分别从18%提高至20%，以及从44%提高至56%。一个凸性信息领导者叠加层单独为市场中性部分提供保险，以较小的收益代价换取凸性和更浅的回撤，夏普比率保持不变。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We present a simple framework for dynamic portfolio management that uses nothing but daily prices, trading volumes, and market capitalizations. Its state is three fixed-size matrices built from the price history: the distance matrix of the return correlations and the transition matrices of two Markov chains that rank the S\&P 500 names monthly by trailing return and by trailing volatility. These three matrices rest on the price history alone, the same information Markowitz mean-variance optimization draws on, but they replace its expected-return vector and covariance matrix. Our method requires no matrix inversion, works on outlier-robust cross-sectional ranks, and is dynamic rather than single-period. Empirically the volatility rank is forecastable one step ahead while the return rank stays close to unforecastable. A portfolio built on the forecasts, a market-neutral momentum long-short blended with an opportunistic long-only sleeve, beats the market on two non-overlapping out-of-sample test sets, January 2022 to December 2024 and January 2025 to July 2026, at Sharpes of $1.06$ and $1.32$ against the market's $0.78$ and $1.14$, respectively, net of a five-basis-point trading cost and marked to market daily. It also outperforms the classical minimum-variance and maximum-diversification portfolios. Diversifying the long sleeve by residual distance adds a further edge on both periods, lifting the Sharpe to $1.08$ and $1.44$ and the annualized return from $18\%$ to $20\%$ and from $44\%$ to $56\%$, respectively. A convex information-leader overlay separately insures the market-neutral sleeve, buying convexity and a shallower drawdown at a small cost in return, the Sharpe unchanged.
