<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

面板局部投影（panel local projection）即使在缺失数据导致没有任何完整回归案例的情况下仍可保持可识别性。我们通过将基于因子的条件均值与来自观测对的异质性协方差相结合，在指定日期上恢复投影。在辅助面板中存在强因子、观测日期上因子变异充分以及所需配对观测足够多的条件下，二阶展开给出偏差校正矩和用于推断的联合得分。在附加依赖限制下，局部化乘数自助法（localized multiplier bootstrap）提供逐点置信区间和联立带。我们以更大的季度企业样本重新审视Caldara和Iacoviello（2022）研究的地缘政治风险投资响应，并将分析扩展到滚动三个月投资强度的月度投影。在月度分析中，随着历史数据不完整的企业进入样本，高暴露企业与低暴露企业之间六个月响应差异变得更加负向。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A panel local projection can remain identified even when missing data leave no complete regression case. We recover the projection over specified dates by combining factor-based conditional means with idiosyncratic covariances from observed pairs. Under strong factors in the auxiliary panel, sufficient factor variation at observed dates and enough observations of the required pairs, a second-order expansion yields bias-corrected moments and a joint score for inference. The localized multiplier bootstrap provides pointwise confidence intervals and simultaneous bands under additional dependence restrictions. We revisit the investment response to geopolitical risk studied by Caldara and Iacoviello (2022) with a larger quarterly firm sample and extend the analysis to monthly projections of trailing-three-month investment intensity. In the monthly analysis, the six-month response difference between high- and low-exposure firms becomes more negative as firms with incomplete histories enter the sample.
