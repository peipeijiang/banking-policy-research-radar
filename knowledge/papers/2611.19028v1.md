# Optimal Capital Injections in a Microlending Group under External Shocks

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

受小额信贷中小组违约静态模型的启发，我们研究一个动态问题：借贷小组的成员面临外部冲击（如干旱或洪水），共同应急基金的管理者在每次冲击发生时决定向受灾成员注入多少资金。冲击在随机时间到达，一次冲击所影响的成员集合可能取决于前一次冲击影响的成员，且损失可能溢出到未直接受灾的成员。在冲击间隔期间，成员的财富遵循一个线性随机微分方程，其中成员之间相互支持，基金通过利息和成员缴款增长，直至达到上限。成本在成员低于贫困线的短缺（一种福利度量，包含标准贫困度量作为特例）与资金注入的行政成本以及将资金保留在基金中的收益之间进行权衡。我们将该问题表述为嵌入冲击时刻的马尔可夫决策过程（Markov decision process），并证明嵌入过程是马尔可夫的且具有弱连续转移核。当折现率超过由财富动态决定的增长率时，我们证明在任意策略下期望总成本是有限的，有限期最优值以显式速率收敛于无限期最优值，且存在一个平稳最优注入策略，该策略可以选择为不依赖于日历时间。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Motivated by static models of group default in microlending, we study a dynamic problem in which the members of a lending group are exposed to external shocks, such as droughts or floods, and the manager of a common emergency fund decides at each shock how much capital to inject into the members who were hit. Shocks arrive at random times, the set of members that a shock hits may depend on which members the previous shock hit, and the losses may spill over to members who were not hit directly. Between shocks, the members' wealth follows a linear stochastic differential equation in which members support each other, and the fund grows with interest and member contributions up to a cap. The cost balances the members' shortfall below a poverty line, a welfare measure that contains a standard poverty measure as a special case, against the administrative cost of the injections and the benefit of keeping money in the fund. We formulate the problem as a Markov decision process embedded at the shock times and prove that the embedded process is Markov with a weakly continuous transition kernel. When the discount rate exceeds a growth rate determined by the wealth dynamics, we show that the expected total cost is finite under every policy, that the finite-horizon optimal values converge to the infinite-horizon one at an explicit rate, and that there exists a stationary optimal injection policy, which can be chosen independent of calendar time.

</details>
