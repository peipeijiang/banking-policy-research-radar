<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

短期观测定价面板通常包含大量观测值，但价格变动却很少。我们通过将估计误差分解为给定已实现价格轨迹条件下的不确定性以及不同轨迹之间的变异性，在合成数据生成过程中评估这种稀疏性对推断的影响。在基线模拟中，跨设计成分对梯度提升规格的估计误差方差贡献了97.6%，导致由设计特异性中心化误差驱动的覆盖率不足，而标准的面板内重抽样和聚类稳健方法无法捕捉这一误差。

三个主要结果构成了分析框架。第一，跨设计离散度遵循经验关系式 sigma_b ≈ 0.182 V^(-0.271)，其中 V = n_moves * magnitude^2，且 -0.271 被视为模拟规律性。第二，增加共享同一价格路径的区域可改善干扰项估计，但不会产生独立的价格轨迹；只有对具有独立设计误差的单位进行平均，才能以 sqrt(k) 的速率降低跨设计标准差。第三，在独立定价单位间估计的 Paule-Mandel 方差成分，在齐质性条件下将经验覆盖率从 0.469 提高到 0.931。总体而言，改进被动面板中的推断需要生成独立的识别变异性，例如通过受控的区域随机化。

最后，对扫描数据（Dominick's Finer Foods，软饮料）的应用证实了这些发现：名义价格区和产品在独立设计抽取中的行为仅相当于其数量的很小一部分，导致单位间的离散区间远宽于传统面板内自助法所得结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Short observational pricing panels often contain many observations but few distinct price movements. We evaluate the inferential consequences of this sparsity in a synthetic data-generating process by separating estimation error into uncertainty conditional on a realized price trajectory and variation across alternative trajectories. In baseline simulations, this across-design component accounts for 97.6% of estimation error variance for a gradient-boosted specification, causing coverage shortfalls driven by design-specific centering error that standard within-panel resampling and cluster-robust procedures fail to capture.
  Three main results organize the analysis. First, across-design dispersion follows the empirical relation sigma_b approx 0.182 V^(-0.271), where V = n_moves * magnitude^2, with -0.271 treated as a simulation regularity. Second, adding regions sharing a common price path improves nuisance estimation but creates no independent price trajectories; only averaging across units with independent design errors reduces across-design standard deviation at the sqrt(k) rate. Third, a Paule-Mandel variance component estimated across independently priced units increases empirical coverage under homogeneity from 0.469 to 0.931. Broadly, improving inference in passive panels requires generating independent identifying variation, such as through controlled regional randomization.
  Finally, an application to scanner data (Dominick's Finer Foods, Soft Drinks) confirms these findings: nominal price zones and products behave as a small fraction of their count in independent design draws, yielding between-unit dispersion intervals far wider than conventional within-panel bootstraps.
