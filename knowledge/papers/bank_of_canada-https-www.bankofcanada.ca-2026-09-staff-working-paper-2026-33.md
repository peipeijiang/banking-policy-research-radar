<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

收益率曲线的投资组合平衡模型历来难以估计。本文提出了一种简单的两步估计方法，可以通过标准的期限结构估计方法来实现。在我们对美国国债数据的应用中，我们的方法恢复了收益率曲线的具有经济可解释性的驱动因素。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Portfolio-balance models of the yield curve are notoriously hard to estimate. This paper offers a simple, two-step estimation approach that can be implemented with standard term structure estimation methods. In our application to U.S. Treasury data, our method recovers economically interpretable drivers of the yield curve.
