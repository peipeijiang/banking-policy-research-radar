# Smart charging without rebound peaks: Wholesale price feedback limits distribution grid peaks from price-responsive electric vehicles

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34133v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关于价格优化电动汽车充电的研究一致发现，常见的价格信号会使整个车队在最低价时段同步充电，并在低压电网中产生新的“反弹”负荷峰值。几乎所有这类研究都将批发电价视为外生变量，即不受电动汽车负荷转移的影响。本文认为，这一假设高估了问题的严重性：在低价时段充电会推高这些时段的批发电价，从而在均衡状态下将充电分散到不同时段。我基于根据德国2025年数据校准的实时电价，模拟了价格优化充电。即使在对电力需求增长进行调整之后，本地电网的同时进口峰值仍上升了260%。在加入考虑价格-数量反馈的均衡批发电价模型后，该增幅降至仅5%。在允许车辆到电网（vehicle-to-grid）的情况下，出口峰值分别上升115%和下降6%。该结果在多种车辆与充电参数、充电约束、可再生能源情景、网络费用、价格函数、峰值度量方法以及模拟假设下均成立。在车辆普及率领先于全国平均水平或充电功率较高的地区，反弹峰值仍有可能出现，但其幅度远小于文献共识所暗示的水平。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Studies on price-optimized EV charging consistently find that the common price signal synchronizes charging of the entire fleet of cars in the cheapest hours and creates new "rebound" load peaks in low-voltage grids. Almost all of these studies treat wholesale prices as exogenous, i.e., unaffected by the shifting EV load. This paper argues that this assumption overstates the problem: charging in low-price hours raises the wholesale price in those hours, which spreads charging across hours in equilibrium. I simulate price-optimized charging under real-time prices calibrated to German 2025 data. Even when adjusting for the increase in electricity demand, the coincident import peak of the local grid rises by 260%. After adding a model of equilibrium wholesale prices that takes the price-quantity feedback into account, the increase is reduced to a mere 5%. Allowing for vehicle-to-grid, the export peak rises by 115% and falls by 6%, respectively. The result holds across vehicle and charging parameters, charging constraints, renewable scenarios, network charges, price functions, peak measures, and simulation assumptions. Rebound peaks remain possible where vehicle adoption runs ahead of the national average or where charging power is high, but they are much smaller than the literature consensus suggests.

</details>
