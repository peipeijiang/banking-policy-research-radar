# Agentic AI in Automated Markets

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22522v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文用强化学习建模自动化市场中的套利者、操纵者和羊群交易者三类代理，基于加密货币数据发现羊群代理产生最频繁但最短的压力事件、操纵者产生最少但最持久的压力事件，并主张行为特定监管而非统一限制。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

自动化市场日益容纳自主的学习型交易智能体，并由公式化的自动做市商（AMMs）提供动力。我们研究异质性智能体行为如何塑造此类市场中的压力、风险调整后表现、流动性与波动率。三种行为原型被建模为智能体强化学习（RL）系统：理性套利者、操纵者和散户羊群跟随者。每种原型由其在共同的、资产无关的敞口控制环境中的奖励函数定义，且每种原型分别使用基于价值的深度Q网络（DQN）和采用学习基线的REINFORCE进行训练。
基于每小时加密货币数据（AAVE、ETC、SUI）的初步实验为假设提供了动机。羊群跟随者产生最频繁但最短的压力事件，操纵者产生最少但最持续的压力事件，而理性智能体尽管收益波动率更高，却产生最稳定的滚动夏普比率。SHAP归因分别将这些模式与回撤和跨资产信号、短期波动率和持仓状态以及动量联系起来。这些结果支持针对特定行为的监管，而非对波动率或换手率施加统一限制。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Automated markets increasingly host autonomous, learning trading agents and are powered by formulaic automated market makers (AMMs). We study how heterogeneous agent behavior shapes stress, risk-adjusted performance, liquidity, and volatility in these types of markets. Three behavioral archetypes are modeled as agentic reinforcement learning (RL) systems: a rational arbitrageur, a manipulator, and a retail herd-follower. Each archetype is defined by its reward function within a common, asset-agnostic exposure-control environment, and each is trained with a value-based Deep Q-Network (DQN) and with REINFORCE using a learned baseline.
  A pilot on hourly cryptocurrency data (AAVE, ETC, SUI) motivates the hypotheses. Herd-followers produce the most frequent but shortest stress episodes, manipulators the fewest but most persistent, and rational agents the most stable rolling Sharpe ratios despite higher return volatility. SHAP attributions tie these patterns to drawdown and cross-asset signals, short-term volatility and position state, and momentum, respectively. The results motivate behavior-specific regulation rather than uniform limits on volatility or turnover.
