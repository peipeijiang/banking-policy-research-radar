<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

贝叶斯说服框架（Bayesian persuasion framework）建模了发送者向一个或多个接收者进行策略性信息揭示的过程。我们研究多接收者说服设置中一个复杂的变体，其中通信层级构成一个森林——即某些接收者观察到比其他接收者更具信息量的信号，且接收者根据其所获信息形成的偏序关系由一个预先指定的有向森林表示。该模型自然地刻画了层级组织中的交互，并且它介于贝叶斯说服两个最常研究的版本之间：私有说服（private persuasion）和公开说服（public persuasion）。受经典私有说服模型的启发，我们聚焦于一个具有二元自然状态和二元行动异质接收者的设置，使得发送者的效用是采取某一特定合意行动的接收者集合的单调函数。

我们受最优传输理论（optimal transport theory）启发的方法论，将对称发送者效用下的计算可处理性结果从私有说服扩展到森林层级框架。我们进一步在发送者效用为模块化（modular）时提供了一个多项式时间算法。在负面结果方面，我们证明与私有说服不同，超模（supermodular）发送者效用函数的情形导致一个NP难优化问题，且除非P=NP，该问题不存在FPTAS；即使森林的每个树分量均为3个接收者的链，该结果仍然成立。为补充该硬度结果，我们提供了一个简单的近似算法，其近似比线性依赖于森林层级的深度。我们进一步为子模（submodular）效用情形提供了类似的算法。最后，在森林通信层级之外，我们排除了即使在对称效用情形下发送者最优效用存在FPTAS的可能性，除非P=NP。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The Bayesian persuasion framework models strategic information revelation by a sender to one or more receivers. We study an intricate version of the multi-receiver persuasion setting in which the communication hierarchy is a forest - i.e., some receivers observe more informative signals than others, and the partial ordering of the receivers according to the information they get is represented by a pre-specified directed forest. This model naturally captures interactions in hierarchical organizations, and it lies in between the two most commonly studied versions of Bayesian persuasion: private persuasion and public persuasion. Inspired by the classic model of private persuasion, we focus on a setting with a binary state of nature and binary-action heterogeneous receivers s.t. the sender's utility is a monotone function of the set of receivers taking a specific desirable action.
  We extend the computational tractability result for symmetric sender's utility from private persuasion to the forest hierarchy framework using methodology inspired by optimal transport theory. We further provide a polynomial-time algorithm when the sender's utility is modular. On the negative side, we show that, unlike in private persuasion, the case of a supermodular sender's utility function leads to an NP-hard optimization problem which further does not admit an FPTAS unless P=NP; this result holds even when each tree component of the forest is a chain of 3 receivers. To complement the hardness result, we provide a simple approximation algorithm with the approximation ratio depending linearly on the depth of the forest hierarchy. We further provide an analogous algorithm for the submodular utility case. Finally, beyond forest communication hierarchies, we rule out an FPTAS for the sender's optimal utility even in the symmetric utility case, unless P=NP.
