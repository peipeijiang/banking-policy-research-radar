<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了经典Cramér–Lundberg模型中破产时间的Laplace变换，其中索赔额服从形状参数为2的Erlang分布。我们推导出一个带有两个边界条件的积分微分方程。对于每个正贴现率，我们证明其特征多项式有一个负根和两个互异正根。有界性排除了增长指数项，并给出了该变换作为两个衰减指数之和的表示。我们构造了卷积链的有限维矩阵约化。对于一组模型参数，我们得到了破产概率、平均破产时间以及更高阶矩的精确表达式，包括零初始盈余下的三阶矩。我们证明了该表示关于贴现率的解析性，并验证了由此得到的矩级数。我们单独考虑将该表示解析延拓到负贴现率的情形。对于形状参数为任意正整数的Erlang索赔，我们建立了一个封闭线性系统，以及延续零贴现率下零根的根的渐近性质。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the Laplace transform of the time of ruin in the classical Cramér--Lundberg model with claims having an Erlang distribution of shape parameter 2. We derive an integro-differential equation with two boundary conditions. For every positive discount rate, we show that its characteristic polynomial has one negative root and two distinct positive roots. Boundedness excludes the growing exponential term and yields a representation of the transform as a sum of two decaying exponentials. We construct a finite-dimensional matrix reduction of the convolution chain. For a model parameter set, we obtain exact expressions for the ruin probability, the mean time of ruin, and higher moments, including the third moment at zero initial surplus. We prove analyticity in the discount rate and justify the resulting moment series. We consider the analytic continuation of the representation to negative discount rates separately. For Erlang claims with an arbitrary positive integer shape parameter, we establish a closed linear system and the asymptotics of the root continuing the zero root at zero discount.
