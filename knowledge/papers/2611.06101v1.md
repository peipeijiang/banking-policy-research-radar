# Optimality of Separate Selling with Finite-Support Values

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06101v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们刻画了在具有可加性买家以及独立、有限支撑估值的多物品拍卖中，分离销售何时恰好是收益最优的。对于每一件物品，估值分布在买家之间相同，但在不同物品之间可能不同。我们证明，只要至少有两件物品具有严格为正的非最高支撑值，那么对于任意数量的买家 $n \ge 2$，分离销售最优当且仅当最高销售——即每件物品仅按其最高可能价值出售——在单一买家情形下最优。该等价性在占优策略激励相容和贝叶斯激励相容下均成立。随后，我们通过一类特定对偶流的存在性，刻画了单买家问题中最高销售的最优性，并推导出其最优性的一个必要条件和若干关于价值分布的充分条件。这些条件揭示了一个共同的经济特征，我们称之为最高价值隔离（top-value isolation）：较高的非最高估值必须足够远地低于最高价值，或者相对于最高价值处的概率质量而言承载足够小的概率质量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We characterize when separate selling is exactly revenue-optimal in multi-item auctions with additive buyers and independent, finitely supported valuations. For each item, the valuation distribution is identical across buyers, but it may differ across items. We show that, provided at least two items have a strictly positive non-top support value, separate selling is optimal for any number of buyers $n \ge 2$ if and only if top selling, which sells each item only at its highest possible value, is optimal for a single buyer. This equivalence holds under both dominant-strategy incentive compatibility and Bayesian incentive compatibility. We then characterize the optimality of top selling in the one-buyer problem through the existence of a particular class of dual flows and derive a necessary condition and several sufficient conditions on the value distributions for its optimality. These conditions reveal a common economic feature, which we call top-value isolation: high non-top valuations must be sufficiently far below the highest value or carry sufficiently little probability mass relative to the probability mass at the highest value.
