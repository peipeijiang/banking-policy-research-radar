<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一个用于高维时间序列的因子模型，其中潜在因子的动态由多元得分驱动模型（score-driven model）生成，从而能够对条件均值和条件方差中的非线性和重尾性进行建模。估计分为两步：首先通过主成分分析（Principal Components）提取因子，然后基于估计的因子，通过准极大似然（Quasi Maximum Likelihood）估计得分驱动模型的参数。由于因子不可识别，我们首先在时间序列数量和样本量均趋于无穷大的情况下，为控制因子动态的估计参数建立了集合一致性和渐近正态性。然后，我们构建了有效的渐近预测区间，该区间不依赖于对因子所选择的识别约束。数值结果证实了我们估计器的良好性能。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a factor model for high-dimensional time series where the dynamics of the latent factors is generated by a multivariate score-driven model, thus allowing to model nonlinearities and heavy tails in both the conditional mean and the conditional variance. Estimation is in two steps: first the factors are extracted via Principal Components and then the parameters of the score-driven model are estimated via Quasi Maximum Likelihood based on the estimated factors. Since factors are not identified, we first establish set-consistency and asymptotic normality for the estimated parameters governing their dynamics, as both the number of time series and the sample size diverge to infinity. Then, we construct valid asymptotic prediction intervals, which do not depend on the chosen identifying constraints for the factors. Numerical results confirm the goodness of our estimator.

</details>
