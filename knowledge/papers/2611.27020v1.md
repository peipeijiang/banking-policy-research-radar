<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察社会信任是否通过影响投资者对与极端未来收益相关的乐观信息的信念，进而塑造类彩票股票的定价。我们认为，较高的社会信任会降低投资者对与极端未来收益相关的乐观信息的怀疑，增加对彩票型股票的需求，导致定价过高和后续收益降低。我们使用1996—2018年间美国49个州8，875家公司的样本，并以MAX作为类彩票特征的代理变量，结果表明MAX效应集中在信任度相对较高的地区，而在信任度相对较低的地区则较弱或不存在。我们进一步发现，这种信任调节效应在教育或收入水平较低的地区更强，这与当投资者获取私人信息的渠道较为有限时社会信任发挥相对更重要作用的观点一致。上述结果在替代彩票代理变量和一系列稳健性检验下均保持稳健。我们的研究发现将社会信任识别为类彩票股票定价的一个新决定因素，并提供证据表明社会资本可能放大高偏度收益资产中由信念驱动的错误定价。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We examine whether social trust shapes the pricing of lottery-like stocks by influencing investors' beliefs regarding optimistic information associated with extreme future payoffs. We argue that higher social trust reduces investors' skepticism toward optimistic information associated with extreme future payoffs, increasing demand for lottery-type stocks and leading to overpricing and lower subsequent returns. Using a sample of 8,875 firms across 49 US states over the period 1996-2018 and MAX as a proxy for lottery-like characteristics, we show that the MAX effect is concentrated in regions with relatively high trust and is weak or absent in regions with relatively low trust. We further find that this trust conditioning is stronger in regions with lower education or income levels, consistent with social trust playing a relatively more important role when investors have more limited access to private information. The results are robust to alternative lottery proxies and a range of robustness checks. Our findings identify social trust as a novel determinant of the pricing of lottery-like stocks and provide evidence that social capital may amplify belief-driven mispricing in assets with highly skewed payoffs.
