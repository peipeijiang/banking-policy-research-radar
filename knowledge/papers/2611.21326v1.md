<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

健康保险索赔呈现出时间依赖性，因为先前的索赔可能提供关于后续索赔的时间、类型和金额的信息。这种依赖性难以用基于汇总索赔次数的传统精算模型来捕捉。我们研究了一种神经标记点过程（neural marked point process），其中因果Transformer（causal Transformer）表示个体索赔历史并确定类型特定的条件强度，允许自激发和索赔类型之间的交互。该框架还纳入了条件严重程度分布、报告延迟和随机医疗成本膨胀。我们开发了一种基于似然的校准程序，该程序考虑了不完整的索赔报告，并使用模拟数据和实证数据对模型进行了评估。一项包含10，000份保险合同的模拟研究检验了已知索赔动态的恢复情况，而对MEPS衍生的医疗事件数据的应用评估了预测性能。拟合模型再现了索赔频率和严重程度的主要模式，尽管在分布尾部仍存在一些差异。在MEPS数据上，就索赔计数偏差和支出区分而言，该神经模型在预测性能上优于两个广义线性模型基准。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Health insurance claims exhibit temporal dependence, as previous claims may provide information about the timing, type and amount of subsequent claims. Such dependencies are difficult to capture with traditional actuarial models based on aggregated claim counts. We investigate a neural marked point process in which a causal Transformer represents individual claim histories and determines type-specific conditional intensities, allowing for self-excitation and interactions between claim types. The framework also incorporates conditional severity distributions, reporting delays and stochastic medical-cost inflation. We develop a likelihood-based calibration procedure that accounts for incomplete claim reporting and evaluate the model using both simulated and empirical data. A simulation study with 10,000 insurance contracts examines the recovery of known claim dynamics, while an application to MEPS-derived medical-event data assesses predictive performance. The fitted model reproduces the main patterns in claim frequencies and severities, although some discrepancies remain in the tails of the distributions. On the MEPS data, the neural model achieves better predictive performance than two generalized linear model benchmarks in terms of claim-count deviance and expenditure discrimination.
