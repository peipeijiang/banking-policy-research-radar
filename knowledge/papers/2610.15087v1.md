<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究具有类型无关偏好的知情发送者的广义可验证披露。我们首先将所有完美贝叶斯均衡刻画为发送者类型的个体理性划分，这些类型被划分为按收益排序的联盟。如果一个均衡满足我们的联盟防共谋准则，即没有任何类型群体能够有利可图地协调一项声明，使接收者将其精确理解为来自愿意加入该声明的类型，则该均衡得以存续。联盟防共谋均衡是贪婪划分：在每一步，剩余类型组成的联盟获得与均衡相一致的最高收益。鉴于我们设定的普遍性，联盟防共谋均衡可能不存在。我们在对偏好和证据的限制下建立存在性：具有消息完备性的拟凹性、中间性、具有消息完备性的廉价谈话，以及收益退化。对于一个具有随机证据的丰富披露博弈，我们证明发送者在联盟防共谋均衡中的事前收益是信念单纯形上的分段线性帐篷函数，类似于贝叶斯说服中的凹闭包和廉价谈话中的拟凹闭包。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study generalized verifiable disclosure by an informed sender with type-independent preferences. We first characterize all perfect Bayesian equilibria as individually rational partitions of the sender's types into coalitions ordered by payoff. An equilibrium survives our coalition-proofness criterion if no group of types can profitably coordinate an announcement that the receiver will interpret as coming precisely from the types willing to join it. Coalition-proof equilibria are partitions that are greedy: at each step, a coalition of the remaining types attains the highest payoff consistent with equilibrium. Given the generality of our setting, coalition-proof equilibria may fail to exist. We establish existence under restrictions on preferences and evidence: quasiconcavity with message completeness, betweenness, cheap talk with message completeness, and payoff degradation. For a rich disclosure game with stochastic evidence, we show that the sender's ex-ante payoff in a coalition-proof equilibrium is a piecewise linear tent over the belief simplex, analogous to the concave closure in Bayesian persuasion and the quasiconcave closure in cheap talk.
