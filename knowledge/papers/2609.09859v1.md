# How an Economy Shrinks in Space: Concavity-on-Jobs and Upward Consolidation under Demographic Decline

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09859v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文研究人口下降如何导致城市经济在空间上不均匀收缩：小城市因产业规模阈值集中而急剧失去产业和就业，产业向上整合至更大城市，工人为恢复收入向东京等顶端城市迁移，并用日本工人面板数据验证了迁移激励。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当一个国家的人口下降时，总体经济似乎在集约边际上收缩：产业多样性保持不变，每个产业都略微缩小。在区域层面，收缩是不均衡的，并采取广延形式：整个产业一个接一个地消失。相关的单位是城市：产业按规模嵌套——即产业区位的层级属性——每个产业只有在达到最低人口门槛时才能存续。必需品产业的阈值集中在低端，因此一个城市的产业数量——及其就业岗位——随规模呈急剧凹性（就业岗位的凹性）。适度的损失会一次性将一个小城市推到许多阈值之下；而一个大型核心城市则只会逐个失去少数专业化产业。失去的产业向上整合到下一个大到足以容纳它们的城市；对工人而言，这意味着降级到一份薪酬更低的本地工作。为了恢复那份收入，工人向上迁移到顶点——唯一容纳完整产业范围的城市。以日本为研究对象——领先OECD二十年，东京处于其顶点——使用承载迁移的年轻工人的工人层面面板数据，一个以实际工资（包含住房成本）进行的工资回归识别出了一种随来源地而变化的迁往东京的激励，其变化遵循就业岗位的凹性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When a country's population declines, the aggregate economy appears to contract on the intensive margin: industrial diversity intact, every industry a little smaller. At the regional level, contraction is uneven and takes the extensive form: entire industries disappear, one after another. The relevant unit is the city: industries are nested by size - the hierarchy property of industrial location - each viable only above a minimum population. Necessity industries' thresholds bunch at the low end, so a city's industry count - and its jobs - is sharply concave in size (concavity on jobs). A modest loss pushes a small city below many thresholds at once; a large core sheds a few specialized industries, one at a time. Lost industries consolidate upward to the next city large enough to host them; for the worker it means a step down to a lower-paid local job. To recover that income, workers move up to the apex - the only city hosting the full industry range. Studying Japan - two decades ahead of the OECD, Tokyo at its apex - with worker-level panel data on the young workers who carry the migration, a wage regression in real, housing-inclusive wages identifies a Tokyo-bound migration incentive that varies by origin, following concavity on jobs.

</details>
