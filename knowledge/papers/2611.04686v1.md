<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

AI助手越来越多地参与需要员工交换信息和协调决策的工作。我们研究当员工之间的利益以及员工与公司利益存在冲突时，公司应如何设计这种沟通。两名员工私下了解本地条件及其偏好的应对方式，而助手了解项目总体状况。公司在员工报告并选择各自行动之前披露项目信息。透明度改善了适应性，但可能损害偏好报告的真实性。当员工与公司在生产方向上一致时，在弱协调下粗化项目信息可以保持有用的偏好报告，但以预测精度为代价。在强协调下，公司反而偏好完全披露项目信息和事实性报告，因为偏好信息会引发对私人目标的高成本迁就。在弱协调下，公司最优政策可能使员工境况比直接交换更差；要求员工接受该政策可能需要进一步粗化信息。在报告之后发布项目背景信息可以维持早期发布所会阻止的事实性沟通。当交付决策相互独立且无法联合修订时，内生转发在弱意义上优于不变转发，改善公司偏好的绩效，并在某些环境中严格改善。数值比较表明，在所考虑的一致环境中，完全初始披露捕获了大部分收益，而当员工可能倾向于相反方向时，进一步设计的价值更大。研究结果区分了员工报告的可信度与其组织价值，并展示了披露时机如何影响二者。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

AI assistants increasingly participate in work that requires employees to exchange information and coordinate decisions. We study how a firm should design this communication when workers' interests conflict with each other's and the firm's. Two workers privately know local conditions and their preferred responses, while the assistant knows aggregate project conditions. The firm discloses project information before workers report and choose their own actions. Transparency improves adaptation but can undermine truthful preference reporting. When workers agree with the firm on the productive direction, coarsening project information under weak coordination can preserve useful preference reporting at a cost in forecast precision. Under strong coordination, the firm instead prefers full project disclosure and factual reports, because preference information induces costly accommodation of private objectives. Under weak coordination, a firm-optimal policy can leave workers worse off than direct exchange; requiring their acceptance can call for further coarsening. Releasing project context after reports can sustain factual communication that early release would prevent. With separate delivery decisions that cannot be revised jointly, endogenous forwarding weakly improves firm-preferred performance over unchanged forwarding and strictly improves it in some environments. Numerical comparisons show that full initial disclosure captures most gains in the aligned environments considered, while further design has greater value when workers can favor opposing directions. The results distinguish the credibility of employees' reports from their organizational value and show how disclosure timing affects both.

</details>
