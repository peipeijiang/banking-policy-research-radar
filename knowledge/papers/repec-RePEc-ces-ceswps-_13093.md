<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当政府将基础设施现代化至统一标准时，获得最大隐性升级的家庭是否受益最多？我们研究了尼泊尔的一项电力现代化推广项目，该项目安装了智能电表并提高了接电容量，以促进电力使用。我们发现，受处理家庭报告电力供应更加可靠，计费用电量平均上升了5%。然而，获得最大升级的家庭——其基线支出较低、电器拥有量较少——用电量并未增加，而其电费上涨了19%，逾期缴费也有所增加。已达到标准的家庭则在用电量增加的同时未出现相应的缴费压力，并从可靠性提升中受益。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When governments modernize infrastructure to a common standard, do households receiving the largest implicit upgrades benefit most? We study a Nepal electricity modernization rollout that installed smart meters and raised connection capacity to enable greater use of electricity. We find that treated households reported more reliable electricity and billed use rose 5 percent on average. However, households receiving the largest upgrades, who had lower expenditures and fewer appliances at baseline, showed no increase in use while their bills rose 19 percent and late payments increased. Households already at the standard increased use without comparable payment strain, benefiting from increased reliability.

</details>
