<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

众所周知，日股票收益率的波动率对消息的反应是非对称的，这由消息影响曲线（NIC）描述，该曲线将波动率与前一期收益率联系起来。实证研究表明：（i）波动率对负收益率的反应比对同等幅度的正收益率更强烈；（ii）非对称点，即NIC达到最小值的位置，为正；（iii）高于该点的收益率往往根本不会增加波动率。流行的GJR模型和A-GARCH模型各自排除了上述特征之一。我们研究平滑阈值GARCH（STGARCH）模型，该模型嵌套了上述两个模型，并通过结合估计的阈值（即非对称点）及其两侧各自的斜率，允许（i）-（iii）成立。尽管似然函数在阈值处并非二阶连续可微，我们证明拟最大似然估计量是一致的，并且无论（iii）是否成立，都以通常的$\sqrt{n}$速率收敛。若（iii）不成立，估计量是渐近正态的；而若（iii）成立，则阈值上方的斜率为零，该值位于参数空间的边界上，极限分布为非高斯分布。在这种情况下，（iii）的$t$统计量收敛于标准正态变量的正部，而GJR模型的$t$检验仍然有效。这些结果使我们能够在STGARCH模型框架内使用标准正态临界值来检验GJR模型和A-GARCH模型以及（i）-（iii）。对于五个主要股票指数的日收益率，我们对所有五个指数均拒绝了GJR模型，对其中三个指数拒绝了A-GARCH模型，表明STGARCH模型优于两者。当收益率高于其条件均值0.3-0.6个百分点时，波动率最低，这与（i）和（ii）一致。最后，对于五个指数中的两个，（iii）中的假设被拒绝。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

It is well established that the volatility of daily stock returns responds asymmetrically to news, as described by the news impact curve (NIC), which relates volatility to the previous return. Empirical studies suggest that (i) volatility reacts more strongly to negative returns than to positive returns of the same magnitude, (ii) the point of asymmetry, where the NIC attains its minimum, is positive, and (iii) returns above this point often do not increase volatility at all. The popular GJR and A-GARCH models each rule out one of these features. We study the Smooth Threshold GARCH (STGARCH) model, which nests both and allows for (i)-(iii) by combining an estimated threshold, the point of asymmetry, with separate slopes on either side of it. Although the likelihood is not twice continuously differentiable in the threshold, we show that the quasi maximum likelihood estimator is consistent and converges at the usual $\sqrt{n}$-rate, whether or not (iii) holds. If (iii) does not hold, the estimator is asymptotically normal, while if it holds, the slope above the threshold is zero, a value on the boundary of the parameter space, and the limit is non-Gaussian. In that case, the $t$-statistic for (iii) converges to the positive part of a standard normal variable, and the $t$-test of the GJR model remains valid. These results allow us to test the GJR and A-GARCH models, as well as (i)-(iii), within the STGARCH using standard normal critical values. For daily returns on five major stock indices, we reject the GJR model for all five and the A-GARCH model for three, suggesting that the STGARCH improves upon both. Volatility is lowest after returns 0.3-0.6 percentage points above their conditional mean, in line with (i) and (ii). Finally, the hypothesis in (iii) is rejected for two of the five indices.
