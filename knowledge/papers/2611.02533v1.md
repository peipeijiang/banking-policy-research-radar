<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察日本各市町村食品零售供给与常住人口之间的关系。我构建了一个局部均衡框架，其中本地食品零售提升消费便利性，人口密度支撑零售进入。该框架允许正的本地反馈，并将本地零售与邻近消费机会区分开来。利用日本2010年、2015年和2020年人口普查以及2012年、2016年和2021年经济普查数据，我估计了带有空间滞后解释变量的市町村固定效应模型。本地及邻近食品饮料零售供给（FRS）与常住人口呈正相关。相应系数分别为0.0893和0.1364，且在引入都道府县×调查轮次固定效应后仍保持为正。共同样本比较表明，本地FRS的标准化关联强于餐饮服务供给（FSS）。估计的本地FRS关系还随零售规模而变化，尽管这种非线性关系的幅度以及高设施数量下的负斜率对区域时间效应和估计时期较为敏感。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines the relationship between food retail supply and resident population across Japanese municipalities. I develop a partial-equilibrium framework in which local food retail improves consumption convenience and population density supports retail entry. The framework allows positive local feedback and distinguishes local retail from neighboring consumption opportunities. Using Japan's 2010, 2015, and 2020 Population Censuses and the 2012, 2016, and 2021 Economic Censuses, I estimate municipality fixed-effects specifications with spatially lagged explanatory variables. Local and neighboring food and beverage retail supply (FRS) are positively associated with resident population. The corresponding coefficients are 0.0893 and 0.1364, respectively, and remain positive when prefecture-by-wave fixed effects are introduced. A common-sample comparison shows that the standardized local FRS association is stronger than that of food-service supply (FSS). The estimated local FRS relationship also varies across retail scale, although the magnitude of this nonlinearity and the negative slope at high establishment counts are sensitive to regional time effects and the estimation period.
