<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我研究通过数量菜单向具有私人价值的买方出售K件异质物品的最优性问题：卖方为每种数量设定一个价格，买方选择一个数量，支付相应价格，并在不超过该数量的范围内选择任意物品。我提出了一种定价方法，该方法汇总了数量价值二阶差分（每个数量下可达到的最高价值）的临界值。当每个二阶差分的条件逆风险比仅依赖于该差分时，临界值可以分别确定。对于可加价值，我证明在显式的分布和支持条件下（包括跨物品标签的事前对称性），所得菜单在所有激励相容和个体理性机制中是最优的。另一项结果将相同的定价方法应用于非可加捆绑价值，在无事前对称性的情况下获得最优菜单，假设自由处置、数量价值向量与各捆绑相对于其相应数量价值的短缺向量之间的独立性，以及相应的分布和支持条件。对于任意数量的物品，应用结果产生了涉及数量折扣、稀疏数量选项或两部定价的显式最优菜单。这些结果不同于Haghpanah和Hartline（2021）中确立纯捆绑最优性的结果、Yang（2025）中嵌套捆绑的结果、Kwak（2026）中单独销售的结果，以及Zhong（2026）中核心-边缘捆绑的结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I study the optimality of selling K heterogeneous objects to a buyer with private values through a quantity menu: the seller posts a price for each number of objects, and the buyer chooses a quantity, pays its price, and selects any objects up to that quantity. I propose a pricing method that aggregates critical values for second differences of quantity values (the highest values attainable at each quantity). Critical values can be determined separately when each second difference's conditional inverse-hazard ratio depends only on that difference. For additive values, I show that the resulting menu is optimal among all incentive-compatible and individually rational mechanisms under explicit distributional and support conditions, including ex ante symmetry across object labels. A separate result applies the same pricing method to obtain an optimal menu for nonadditive bundle values without ex ante symmetry, assuming free disposal, independence between the quantity-value vector and the vector of bundle shortfalls from their respective quantity values, and corresponding distributional and support conditions. For an arbitrary number of objects, applications yield explicit optimal menus that involve quantity discounts, sparse quantity options, or two-part tariffs. These results differ from those establishing the optimality of pure bundling in Haghpanah and Hartline (2021), nested bundling in Yang (2025), separate sales in Kwak (2026), and core-peripheral bundling in Zhong (2026).

</details>
