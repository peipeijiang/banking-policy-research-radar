# On accessibility of the core in mutidimensional spatial majority voting situations

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10029v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考虑（多维）空间多数投票的情形。我们分析选民人数为偶数（大于或等于4）的情形，并假设每位选民对政策集的偏好是“欧几里得式”的：即每位选民都有一个最偏好的“理想”政策，且随着政策与理想政策之间的（欧几里得）距离增大，选民从政策中获得的收益递减。我们将关注范围限定于核心（core）具有唯一元素且该元素属于政策集内部（interior）的投票情形。已知在该文献通常的条件下，核心的这一元素一般不是孔多塞赢家（Condorcet winner）：即至少存在一个核心之外的政策，使得仅通过一轮多数支配无法从该政策到达核心政策。我们证明，然而，在此类条件下，从任何现行政策出发，通过有限序列的多数支配（我们隐含地假设真诚投票），最终都能到达核心中的政策。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper we consider situations of (multidimensional) spatial majority voting. We analyze such situations having an even number (greater than or equal to 4) of voters and assume that each voter's preference over the set of policies is ``Euclidean": i.e., each voter has a most preferred ``ideal" policy and the voter's pay-offs from policies decrease as the (Euclidean) distance of the policies from the ideal policy goes up. We confine attention to voting situations for which the core has a single element belonging to the interior of the policy set. It is known that under conditions usual in this literature, this element of the core, generally, is not a Condorcet winner: i.e., there is at least one policy outside the core such that the core-policy cannot be reached from it by just one round of majority domination. We demonstrate that, however, under such conditions, starting from any policy in place, by a finite sequence of majority-dominations (we assume, implicitly, sincere voting) the policy in the core is reached eventually.
