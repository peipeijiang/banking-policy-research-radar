<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

过去50年全球气温显著上升，但温度异常的持续性是否也随之改变仍是一个悬而未决的问题。本文对1850年至2025年的月度HadCRUT5全球温度序列应用时变自回归框架。为处理长期趋势，采用了两种方法：带确定性断点的Bai-Perron方法，以及LOESS——一种非参数灵活方法。研究结果表明，趋势处理方式的选择至关重要：使用Bai–Perron去趋势后的残差，发现持续性略有增加；使用LOESS残差，持续性保持稳定。原始数据中的持续性增幅最大。结果表明，在全球器测记录期间，全球温度持续性保持稳定，而去趋势方法的选择在持续性估计中至关重要。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Global temperatures have risen markedly over the past 50 years, but whether the persistence of temperature anomalies has also changed remains an open question. This paper applies a time-varying autoregressive framework to the monthly HadCRUT5 global temperature series from 1850 to 2025. To account for the long-run trend, two methods are used: Bai and Perron with deterministic breaks, and LOESS, a non-parametric flexible approach. The findings show that the choice of trend treatment matters: using residuals from Bai–Perron detrending, a modest increase in persistence is found; using LOESS residuals, persistence remains stable. Persistence in the raw data increases most strongly. The results suggest that global temperature persistence has remained stable over the instrumental record, and that the choice of detrending method is critical in persistence estimation.
