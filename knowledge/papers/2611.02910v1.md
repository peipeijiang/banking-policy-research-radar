# The Market for AI-Assisted Signaling

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究了一个市场，在该市场中，AI提供商选择用于传递能力信号的技术和价格。个体的采用行为不可观测。有利可图的辅助工具可能导致福利低于没有AI时的信号传递，即使能力仍然被完全揭示。更容易的模仿增加了展示能力所需的产出，而额外的信号传递可能超过生产节约。提供商可以通过改善对低能力发送者的辅助来获利，因为这增加了高能力发送者的购买。相对于保留现有工具，此类改进可能降低福利。提供相同技术的提供商之间的竞争支持完全信息，但开发成本可以维持隐藏能力的垄断供给。更强的验证能力也可能通过改变提供商所服务的客户而减少信息。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies a market in which AI providers choose technologies and prices for assistance used to signal ability. Individual adoption is unobserved. Profitable assistance can lower welfare relative to signaling without AI, even when ability remains fully revealed. Easier imitation increases the output required to demonstrate ability, and the extra signaling can outweigh production savings. Providers can profit from improving assistance for lower-ability senders because this increases higher-ability senders' purchases. Such improvements can lower welfare relative to retaining existing tools. Competition among providers offering the same technology supports full information, but development costs can sustain monopoly provision that conceals ability. Greater verification capacity can also reduce information by changing which customers providers serve.
