# Common Ownership and Intertemporal Price Discrimination

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13095.html)

## 一句话结论

> 论文构建理论模型分析共同所有权如何影响跨期价格歧视市场（如航空预售）的提前购买价格与现货价格，发现共同所有权具有反竞争效应但平均交易价格上升幅度小于统一定价情形，且当消费者提前信息不足时可能反而受益。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察共同所有权（common ownership）对具有跨期价格歧视特征的预购市场（如机票市场）的影响。共同所有权会提高预购价格，而现货价格可能下降。我们表明，共同所有权具有反竞争效应，但平均成交价格的上升幅度小于统一定价情形，尽管消费者会转向信息更充分、价格更高的购买方式。与统一定价相比，共同所有权对消费者的不利影响较小，当消费者在预购时对自身偏好足够不了解时，甚至可能使消费者受益。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper considers the effects of common ownership on advance purchase markets featuring intertemporal price discrimination (e.g. airline tickets). Common ownership increases advance prices, while spot prices can decrease. We show that common ownership is anticompetitive but the increase in average transacted prices is smaller than under uniform pricing, although consumers shift towards more informed, more expensive purchases. Common ownership is less detrimental for consumers than under uniform pricing and can even benefit consumers when consumers are sufficiently uninformed about their preferences in advance.

</details>
