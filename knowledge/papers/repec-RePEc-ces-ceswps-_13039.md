# Financial Inclusion: A Meta-analytic Evidence from Global Research

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

关于金融包容性决定因素的实证证据有多稳健？为什么不同研究报告的效应存在差异？我们对该文献进行了首个系统性元分析，综合了2013年至2024年间发表的100项研究中的5，172个估计值。我们使用经UWLS+3程序校正的偏相关系数对报告的估计值进行标准化，并使用精度加权FAT-PET回归检验发表选择偏误。我们采用贝叶斯模型平均法识别异质性来源。发表选择偏误显著存在，但在不同决定因素类别之间差异明显。在我们的主设定中，宏观经济状况、银行与金融深化、技术与基础设施以及金融包容性的持续性等方面，在超越发表偏误后仍存在显著效应，而人口特征、收入、教育与人力资本以及制度性决定因素的证据则较弱。除持续性外，校正后的关联通常较为温和。在可操作的决定因素中，技术与基础设施提供了最为一致的证据，而归于银行与金融深化的效应则远小于未经调整的文献所显示的程度。我们未发现p-hacking的系统性证据。测量方式、估计方法、样本特征和经济背景的差异解释了剩余异质性的大部分。总体而言，该文献包含具有经济意义的规律性，但传统估计往往高估其幅度和普遍性。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How robust is the empirical evidence on the determinants of financial inclusion, and why do reported effects differ across studies? We provide the first systematic meta-analysis of this literature, synthesizing 5,172 estimates from 100 studies published between 2013 and 2024. We standardize reported estimates using partial correlation coefficients corrected with the UWLS+3 procedure and examine publication selection using precision-weighted FAT-PET regressions. We employ Bayesian model averaging to identify sources of heterogeneity. Publication selection is substantial but differs markedly across determinant categories. In our main specification, significant effects beyond publication bias remain for macroeconomic conditions, banking and financial depth, technology and infrastructure, and the persistence of financial inclusion, whereas evidence for demographic, income, education and human capital, and institutional determinants is weaker. Except for persistence, the corrected associations are generally modest. Technology and infrastructure provide the most consistent evidence among actionable determinants, while the effect attributed to banking and financial depth is substantially smaller than the unadjusted literature suggests. We find no systematic evidence of p-hacking. Differences in measurement, estimation methods, sample characteristics, and economic context explain much of the remaining heterogeneity. Overall, the literature contains economically relevant regularities, but conventional estimates often overstate their magnitude and generality.
