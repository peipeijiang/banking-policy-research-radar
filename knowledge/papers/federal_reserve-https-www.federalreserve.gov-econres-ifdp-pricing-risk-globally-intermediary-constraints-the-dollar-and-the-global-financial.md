我们研究美国资产回报不确定性上升如何影响全球资产价格和汇率，采用一个包含中介资产负债表约束的两国模型。实证上，不确定性冲击扩大全球信用利差，推升美元，并增加货币风险溢价。在我们的模型中，更高的不确定性收紧中介约束并压低资产价格，扭转了无摩擦模型中与事实相反的资产价格上涨。由于约束使得净值在不利时期尤为珍贵，风险溢价对不确定性冲击反应强烈。这种相互作用使模型能够匹配数据中的信用利差、货币溢价和美元反应。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Ozge Akinci, Sebnem Kalemli-Ozcan, and Albert Queralto We study how increased uncertainty about U.S. asset returns affects global asset prices and exchange rates in a two-country model with intermediary balance-sheet constraints. Empirically, uncertainty shocks widen global credit spreads, appreciate the dollar, and increase currency risk premia. In our model, higher uncertainty tightens intermediary constraints and lowers asset prices, reversing the counterfactual asset price increase in frictionless models. Because constraints make net worth especially valuable in bad times, risk premia respond strongly to uncertainty shocks. This interaction allows the model to match the credit spread, currency premium, and dollar responses in the data.
