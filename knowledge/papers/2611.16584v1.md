# Uniform Inference for Two-Way Clustered Data

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16584v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出通过随机匹配样本均值平均来估计双向聚类数据总体均值，构造闭式标准误和置信区间，实现均匀渐近精确推断，并扩展到GMM参数的平滑函数。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们为双向聚类数据的总体均值建立了均匀渐近精确推断，允许数据生成过程随样本量变化。这一发现与 Menzel (2021) 著名的不可能性结果并不矛盾，后者对归一化全阵列均值的渐近分布的均匀估计设定了根本性限制。我们通过对随机匹配的样本均值进行平均来估计同一总体均值。闭式标准误使所得的置信区间易于实施。方差估计量是半正定的，且学生化匹配估计量是均匀渐近标准正态的。均匀有效性的代价是在退化情形下收敛速率较慢。我们将结果推广到 GMM 参数的平滑函数，允许不同的矩分量表现出不同的依赖机制。在模拟中，所提出的置信区间在所考虑的依赖机制下达到了接近名义水平的覆盖率。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We establish uniformly asymptotically exact inference for the population mean of two-way clustered data, allowing the data-generating process to vary with sample size. This finding does not contradict the well-known impossibility result of Menzel (2021), which places a fundamental limit on uniform estimation of the asymptotic distribution of the normalized full-array mean. We estimate the same population mean by averaging the sample means from random matchings. A closed-form standard error makes the resulting confidence interval straightforward to implement. The variance estimator is positive semidefinite and the studentized matching estimator is uniformly asymptotically standard normal. The cost of uniform validity is a slower convergence rate under degeneracy. We extend the result to smooth functions of GMM parameters, allowing the different moment components to exhibit different dependence regimes. In simulations, the proposed confidence intervals achieve coverage close to the nominal level across the dependence regimes considered.

</details>
