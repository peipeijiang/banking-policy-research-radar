# Do Modern Difference-in-Differences Estimators Change Published Findings in Business Research?

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11003v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文用大语言模型代理识别商学院期刊中交错采纳 DiD 设定，复现 TWFE 估计并用 Callaway-Sant'Anna 与 Borusyak 等异质性稳健估计量重新估计 355 个设定，发现约 18-25% 的系数符号改变、约 7-10% 显著且符号相反，表明现代 DiD 估计量会改变相当一部分已发表结论。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

交错采纳双重差分（DiD）设计是商学院期刊实证研究的主力方法。通常用于实现该类设计的双向固定效应（TWFE）估计量可能对异质性处理效应赋予负权重。我们通过重新分析具有期刊级公开复制档案的商学院期刊中的交错采纳设定，来检验负权重是否会改变已发表的研究结论。参照Hull（2026）的方法，我们使用大语言模型智能体识别符合条件的设定，复制已发表的TWFE估计值，并使用Callaway和Sant'Anna（2021）的DiD平均估计量以及Borusyak等人（2024）的插补估计量对每个设定进行重新估计。我们重新估计了来自40篇论文的355个设定。在通过Hull杠杆筛选的283个DiD平均比较和306个插补比较中（分别来自28篇和29篇论文），TWFE与现代估计值的相关系数分别为0.61和0.70，在25.1%和18.0%的比较中符号发生改变，在10.2%和7.2%的比较中存在显著差异且符号相反。现代标准误分别是TWFE标准误的1.61倍和1.30倍。纳入未通过筛选的比较后，这些数字基本保持不变。我们的结果表明，在我们重新估计的已发表交错设计中，TWFE与异质性稳健估计量存在不可忽视比例的不一致，且这一比例高于经济学领域。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Staggered-adoption difference-in-differences (DiD) designs are a workhorse of empirical research in business-school journals. The two-way fixed effects (TWFE) estimator that typically implements them can place negative weights on heterogeneous treatment effects. We ask whether negative weights change published findings by reanalyzing staggered-adoption specifications in business-school journals with journal-wide public replication archives. Following Hull (2026), we use large language model agents to identify eligible specifications, replicate the published TWFE estimate, and re-estimate each specification with the DiD-averaging estimator of Callaway and Sant'Anna (2021) and the imputation estimator of Borusyak et al. (2024). We re-estimate 355 specifications from 40 papers. Across the 283 DiD-averaging and 306 imputation comparisons that pass Hull's leverage screen, from 28 and 29 papers, TWFE and modern estimates have correlations of 0.61 and 0.70, change sign in 25.1 and 18.0 percent of comparisons, and differ significantly with opposite signs in 10.2 and 7.2 percent. Modern standard errors are 1.61 and 1.30 times those of TWFE. Including the comparisons that fail the screen leaves these figures essentially unchanged. Our results suggest that TWFE and heterogeneity-robust estimators disagree in a non-negligible share of the published staggered designs we re-estimate, and more often than in economics.
