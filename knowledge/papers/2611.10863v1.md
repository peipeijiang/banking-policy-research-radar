<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究带有学习型冗余参数（learned nuisances）的部分线性工具变量模型中的弱工具稳健推断。基于独立同分布（i.i.d.）均匀样本量基线，我们在所述抽样条件和冗余参数速率条件下，建立了聚类交叉拟合Anderson-Rubin推断和交叉拟合得分推断的一致渐近有效性。扩展部分确立了Anderson-Rubin有效性适用于调查加权目标律和折叠嵌套生成工具。一个支撑性总体基准表明，当处理冗余参数被正确设定时，预测最优工具残差化使集中度最大化。一个五设计诊断组合、一个已知真值蒙特卡洛以及一个探索性选择器考察了冗余参数设定和估计如何影响实现的强度和推断。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study weak-instrument-robust inference in partially linear instrumental-variables models with learned nuisances. Building on an i.i.d. uniform-size baseline, we establish uniform asymptotic validity of clustered cross-fitted Anderson-Rubin inference and cross-fitted score inference under the stated sampling and nuisance-rate conditions. Extensions establish Anderson-Rubin validity for survey-weighted target laws and fold-nested generated instruments. A supporting population benchmark shows that prediction-optimal instrument residualization maximizes concentration when the treatment nuisance is correctly specified. A five-design diagnostic portfolio, a known-truth Monte Carlo, and an exploratory selector examine how nuisance specification and estimation affect realized strength and inference.
