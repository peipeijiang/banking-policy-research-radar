# Perverse Trade Sanction Effects

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_12947.html)

## 一句话结论

> 利用包含国内贸易流的分解数据，估计全面贸易制裁的直接和域外效应，发现域外效应可能抵消直接效应，导致目标国总贸易反而上升。

<details open>
<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

利用既定的计量经济学技术以及包含国内贸易流量的分类数据，我们估算了全面贸易制裁对国际贸易的直接效应与域外效应。在各行业中，全面贸易制裁的直接效应为负，符合预期。然而，其域外效应主要为正，与直接效应方向相反。在极端情况下，全面贸易制裁的域外效应可能完全抵消其直接效应，导致一种反常情形：受制裁方的总体贸易在制裁后反而上升。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Using established econometric techniques and disaggregated data that include intra-national trade flows, we estimate the direct and extraterritorial effects of complete trade sanctions on international trade. Across sectors, the direct effects of complete trade sanctions are negative, as expected. However, their extraterritorial effects are predominantly positive, working in the opposite direction of the direct effects. At the extreme, the extraterritorial effects of complete trade sanctions may fully offset their direct effects and lead to a perverse scenario in which the target’s overall trade rises in the aftermath of sanctions.

</details>
