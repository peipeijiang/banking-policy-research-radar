<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

利用1980年至2023年的丹麦行政数据，我们分析了终身收入（包括劳动所得和净收入）不平等及其在不同队列间的演变。劳动所得不平等从中年起随年龄增长而上升，在低教育程度人群中最高，在中等教育程度人群中最低。就业依附差异——如非就业和兼职工作——解释了终身劳动所得离散度的30%至60%，且在低教育程度人群中尤为重要。弱就业依附是终身劳动所得不平等的关键驱动因素，并与失业和健康相关事件等不利冲击的暴露密切相关。在低教育程度人群中，终身劳动所得不平等在不同队列间有所上升，部分原因在于就业依附离散度的扩大。从劳动所得转向净收入时，转移支付对不平等缩减的贡献约为税收的五倍，但这种均等化效应随时间推移有所减弱。我们的结果凸显了就业动态和社会保险对终身不平等的作用。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Using Danish administrative data from 1980–2023, we analyze lifetime income (both earnings and net-income) inequality and its evolution across cohorts. Earnings inequality rises with age from midlife onward and is highest among the low educated and lowest among those with medium education. Differences in employment attachment — such as non-employment and part-time work — account for 30–60 percent of lifetime earnings dispersion, and are particularly important among the low educated. Weak employment attachment is a key driver of lifetime earnings inequality and is closely linked to exposure to adverse shocks such as unemployment and health-related events. Lifetime earnings inequality has risen across cohorts among the low educated, partly through growing dispersion in employment attachment. In moving from earnings to net-income, transfers account for roughly five times as much of the inequality reduction as taxes, but this equalizing effect has weakened over time. Our results highlight the role of employment dynamics and social insurance for lifetime inequality.

</details>
