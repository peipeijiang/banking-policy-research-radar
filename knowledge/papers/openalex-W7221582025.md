<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

通货膨胀通过在收入增长跟不上物价上涨时降低购买力，从而改变家庭福利。本概念性综述考察通货膨胀如何影响消费的构成与时机、储蓄能力以及金融资产的实际价值。它综合了关于消费理论、通胀预期和家庭资产负债表的文献。必需品成本上升可能挤出可自由支配支出和常规储蓄，对于收入和财务缓冲有限的家庭尤其如此。一些家庭动用储蓄或借贷以维持消费；另一些家庭在不确定性上升且资源允许时增加预防性储蓄。对物价上涨的预期可能使购买提前，但疲弱的收入前景和借贷成本可能抑制支出。影响因家庭消费篮子、年龄、收入保障、资产和债务合同而异。综述得出结论：价格稳定、有针对性的支持、可获得的储蓄产品和金融素养可以降低脆弱性。各表格呈现的是概念性比较，而非新收集的家庭观测数据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Inflation changes household welfare by reducing purchasing power when income growth does not keep pace with prices. This conceptual review examines how inflation affects the composition and timing of consumption, the capacity to save, and the real value of financial assets. It synthesizes the literature on consumption theory, inflation expectations and household balance sheets. Rising costs of necessities can crowd out discretionary spending and regular saving, especially for households with limited income and financial buffers. Some households draw down savings or borrow to maintain consumption; others increase precautionary saving when uncertainty rises and resources permit. Expectations of higher prices may bring purchases forward, but weak income prospects and borrowing costs can discourage expenditure. Effects differ with household consumption baskets, age, income security, assets and debt contracts. The review concludes that price stability, targeted support, accessible savings products and financial literacy can reduce vulnerability. The tables present conceptual comparisons rather than newly collected household observations.
