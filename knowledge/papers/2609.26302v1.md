<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

集体规则常常需要在受限边界上选择一个公共结果。我们刻画了在任意紧致、满维、严格凸体边界上的对角一致性真实选择，该凸体位于维数至少为二的有限维空间中。在线性支撑偏好的全定义域上，普通的个体策略防操纵性与对角一致性迫使规则在每一组偏好剖面下选择某一个固定参与者的最优点。总体为任意固定的有限非空集合。不假设帕累托条件、边界光滑性，也不假设规则的连续性或可测性。如果改为允许内部结果，则两个或更多参与者可以在同一类型定义域上使用其最优点的正固定加权平均，以实现真实的非独裁妥协。证明从两个代理人的激励不等式推导出正则性，将共同的支撑测度界跨报告进行传递，并使每个凸化可达菜单成为可行体的Minkowski和项（Minkowski summand）。边界生成随后迫使固定控制。该结果推广了球面公共品选址问题，并识别了边界值选择在消除真实妥协中的作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A collective rule must often choose a public outcome on a constrained boundary. We characterize truthful choice with diagonal unanimity on the entire boundary of any compact full-dimensional strictly convex body in finite dimension at least two. On the full domain of linear support preferences, ordinary individual strategy-proofness and diagonal unanimity force the rule to select one fixed participant's preferred point at every profile. The population is any fixed finite nonempty set. No Pareto condition, boundary smoothness, or continuity or measurability of the rule is assumed. If interior outcomes are allowed instead, two or more participants can use positive fixed weighted averages of preferred points for truthful non-dictatorial compromise on the same type domain. The proof derives regularity from the two agents' incentive inequalities, transports a common support-measure bound across reports, and makes every convexified attainable menu a Minkowski summand of the feasible body. Boundary generation then forces fixed control. The result extends the spherical public-good location question and identifies the role of boundary-valued choice in eliminating truthful compromise.
