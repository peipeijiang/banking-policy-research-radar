<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个包含跨区域入学流动性的本科高等教育市场均衡模型。个体选择就读的大学，力求最大化取决于教育质量和就读成本的效用。成本包括学费、通过入学考试所需的努力，以及从家乡到大学所在地区的迁移成本。每所大学录取入学考试成绩超过某一阈值的学生，我们将该阈值称为大学的合格分数线。在我们的模型中，每所大学的合格分数线作为在校生人数不得超过容量这一约束的影子价格而被内生决定。我们通过使用日本数据模拟均衡，对模型参数进行结构估计。基于实证结果和反事实实验，我们评估了由于各地区大学空间分布不均而导致的高等教育入学机会差异所带来的福利成本。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop an equilibrium model of the undergraduate higher education market that incorporates interregional mobility for enrollment. An individual chooses a college to attend, seeking to maximize utility depending on the quality of education and the costs of attendance. Costs include tuition fees, the effort required to pass the entrance exam, and moving costs from one's hometown to the region where the college is located. Each college admits students whose entrance exam scores exceed a certain threshold, which we call the college's qualifying score. In our model, the qualifying score of each college is determined endogenously as the shadow price of the constraint that the number of enrolled students must not exceed capacity. We structurally estimate the model's parameters by simulating equilibrium using Japanese data. Based on empirical results and counterfactual experiments, we evaluate the welfare costs of disparities in access to higher education due to uneven spatial distribution of colleges among regions.
