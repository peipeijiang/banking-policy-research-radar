# Universal Nonexistence of Monotone Equilibrium in Multiunit Auctions with Risk Aversion

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06644v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们建立了在具有风险厌恶投标者的多单位拍卖中单调纯策略均衡的普遍不存在性结果。固定任意大于一的投标者数量以及任意严格递增凹效用剖面，其中至少有一个严格风险厌恶的投标者。在统一价格定价下，对于至少三个单位的任意供给量，我们构造了独立且具有全支撑的类型分布，使得最低中标价定价和最高未中标价定价均不存在可容许的单调均衡。在歧视性定价下，我们构造了有限供给量以及独立且具有全支撑的类型分布，排除了单调纯策略均衡的存在。我们将这些结果推广至全支付拍卖（all-pay auctions）和有限报价网格。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We establish universal nonexistence results for monotone pure-strategy equilibrium in multiunit auctions with risk-averse bidders. Fix any number of bidders greater than one and any profile of strictly increasing concave utilities, with at least one strictly risk-averse bidder. Under uniform pricing, for every supply of at least three units, we construct independent, full-support type distributions under which neither lowest-winning-bid nor highest-losing-bid pricing admits an admissible monotone equilibrium. Under discriminatory pricing, we construct a finite supply and independent, full-support type distributions that preclude monotone pure-strategy equilibrium. We extend these results to all-pay auctions and finite bid grids.

</details>
