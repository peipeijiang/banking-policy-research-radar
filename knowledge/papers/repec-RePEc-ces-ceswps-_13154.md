<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

经济素养——对基本经济学概念的理解——对于经济预期和政策观点的形成有多重要？我们设计、实施并对一个调查模块进行了心理测量学验证，以在具有代表性的成年人群中测量经济素养。经济素养与教育水平和智力相关，但捕捉的是一种独特的技能。虽然与意识形态取向无关，但经济素养较高的个体(1)持有锚定更好的经济预期，(2)对实验提供的关于政策权衡的信息反应更灵敏，(3)在偏好、政策观点和政党支持之间表现出更高的一致性。这些结果与经济素养使选民能够更好地处理信息并形成与其潜在偏好更一致的政策观点相一致。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How important is economic literacy – the understanding of basic economic concepts – for the formation of economic expectations and policy views? We design, implement, and psychometrically validate a survey module to measure economic literacy in a representative adult population. Economic literacy is associated with education and intelligence but captures a distinct skill. While unrelated to ideological orientation, individuals with higher economic literacy (1) hold better-anchored economic expectations, (2) are more responsive to experimentally provided information on policy trade-offs, and (3) show a closer alignment between preferences, policy views, and party support. The results are consistent with economic literacy enabling voters to better process information and form policy views more consistent with their underlying preferences.
