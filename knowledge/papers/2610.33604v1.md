<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们审计了FLARE的评分流程——FLARE是PIXIU和FinBen背后的金融任务套件——发现在相当一部分单元格中，分数是答案抽取规则的属性，而非模型的属性。最清晰的证据已经发表：在已发布的34个Headlines分数中，有九个精确到小数点后四位等于基准自身代码在没有任何输出与Yes字节相等时返回的值，而只有四个超过了分层多数基线。FLARE将数据卡选项列表中任意位置出现的第一个条目计入生成结果；其分叉的lm-evaluation-harness则按位置取第一个匹配项；在相同的生成结果上，两者准确率相差最高达0.4216，且没有任何排行榜说明其数字由哪条规则产生。未指定的规则使分数仅被部分识别，而由于粗化源于多重性而非缺失性，区间宽度是被计数的而非有界的。在一个204单元格的因子实验和一个46单元格的诊断实验中，10个单元格被认证为对规则不变，其中4个仅因为几乎没有任何内容可解析，因此认证需与覆盖率配对报告。在Financial PhraseBank上，七个模型中有四个在一种已发布规则下低于0.5948的多数基线，而在另一种规则下超过该基线；在四个子串评分任务中的三个上，我们测试的所有开放权重模型均无法与退化预测器区分。解读FLARE分数需要三个它未报告的量——歧义率、覆盖率、基线——而floorbench从已生成的输出中计算全部三项。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We audit the scoring pipeline of FLARE, the financial task suite behind PIXIU and FinBen, and find that in a decisive fraction of cells the score is a property of the answer-extraction rule, not the model. The clearest evidence is already published: of the 34 released Headlines scores, nine equal to four decimals what the benchmark's own code returns when no output is byte-equal to Yes, and only four clear the per-stratum majority floor. FLARE credits the first entry of the dataset card's choices list found anywhere in a generation; the lm-evaluation-harness it forks takes the first match by position; on identical generations the two differ by up to 0.4216 accuracy, and no leaderboard states which rule produced its numbers. An unspecified rule leaves the score partially identified, and because the coarsening is multiplicity rather than missingness the interval's width is counted rather than bounded. Across a 204-cell factorial and a 46-cell diagnostic, 10 cells are certified invariant to the rule, 4 only because almost nothing parses, so certification is paired with coverage. On Financial PhraseBank four of seven models fall below the 0.5948 majority floor under one shipped rule and clear it under the other; on three of four substring-scored tasks no open-weight model we test is distinguishable from a degenerate predictor. Reading a FLARE score needs three quantities it does not report -- ambiguity rate, coverage, floor -- and floorbench computes all three from generations already produced.

</details>
