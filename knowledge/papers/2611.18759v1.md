<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一个吸收违约过程会对不同期限上的累积违约分布施加联合约束。我们刻画了这些约束在有限同质组合下的表现形式。对于混合分布函数至多交叉一次的 binomial 混合，整个违约计数的随机序等价于两个不等式：无违约概率必须非增，且全部违约的概率必须非减。在单因子 Gaussian 模型中，这两个不等式给出了可容许的后期相关性的精确区间，该区间由两个标量方程得到。随着组合规模增大，这些区间是嵌套的。若前期和后期违约概率分别为 $p_0<p_1$，且前期潜在相关性为 $ρ_0\in(0,1)$，则两个区间边界都以 $(\log n)^{-1/2}$ 的速率趋近于 $ρ_0$，并具有共同的主阶量 $ρ_0\sqrt{2(1-ρ_0)}[Φ^{-1}(p_1)-Φ^{-1}(p_0)]$。我们还给出了一个构造性的吸收耦合、一种精确的顺序修复、光滑期限曲线的微分条件，以及一个有限规模下的不相容性证书。对数域求积、区间求根和确定性数值实验用于评估这些区间，并验证对完整计数分布的约束。该框架为给定期限法则的可行性分析提供了工具。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

An absorbing default process imposes joint restrictions on cumulative default distributions across horizons. We characterize these restrictions for finite homogeneous portfolios. For binomial mixtures whose mixing distribution functions cross at most once, stochastic ordering of the entire default count is equivalent to two inequalities: the probability of no defaults must be nonincreasing and the probability of all defaults must be nondecreasing. In the one-factor Gaussian model, these inequalities give an exact interval of admissible later correlations, obtained from two scalar equations. The intervals are nested as portfolio size increases. If the earlier and later default probabilities are $p_0<p_1$ and the earlier latent correlation is $ρ_0\in(0,1)$, both interval boundaries approach $ρ_0$ at rate $(\log n)^{-1/2}$, with common leading magnitude $ρ_0\sqrt{2(1-ρ_0)}[Φ^{-1}(p_1)-Φ^{-1}(p_0)]$. We also give a constructive absorbing coupling, an exact sequential repair, differential conditions for smooth horizon curves, and a finite-size certificate of incompatibility. Log-domain quadrature, bracketed root finding, and deterministic numerical experiments evaluate the intervals and verify the restrictions on the full count distribution. The framework provides a feasibility analysis of prescribed horizon laws.
