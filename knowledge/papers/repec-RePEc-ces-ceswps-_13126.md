<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文综述了关于激励机制在并购中作用的文献。我们考察了并购决策中最重要的参与者的激励：收购方和目标方高管、收购方和目标方董事、股东、并购套利者以及投资银行家。我们重点关注影响价值最大化关注度的激励，无论是优先考虑还是偏离价值最大化的激励，并涵盖财务激励、私人收益、帝国构建、职业关注以及风险降低动机。我们发现，决策者之间存在一个由相互抵消和相互强化的激励构成的复杂网络，每个决策者都会预期其他人的激励，并且我们认为，收购市场本身会产生重要的激励来约束管理者。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This survey reviews the literature on the role of incentives in mergers and acquisitions. We examine the incentives of the most important players in M&A decisions: acquirer and target executives, acquirer and target directors, shareholders, merger arbitrageurs, and investment bankers. We concentrate on incentives that affect the focus on value maximization, whether they be incentives to prioritize or deviate from it, and we cover financial incentives, private benefits, empire building, career concerns, and risk-reduction motives. We find a complex web of offsetting and reinforcing incentives among the decision makers, each of whom anticipates the others’ incentives, and we argue that the acquisition market itself generates important incentives that discipline managers.

</details>
