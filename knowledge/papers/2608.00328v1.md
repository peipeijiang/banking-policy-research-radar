# Global coal trade is resilient to maritime chokepoints

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00328v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文分析全球煤炭贸易对海上咽喉要道的韧性，发现贸易可通过改道和替代供应商适应，成本影响有限，但南中国海和东中国海的限制可能对中国成本影响较大。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

海运咽喉要道及其对全球能源贸易的潜在干扰再次受到关注。我们分析了全球煤炭贸易，发现其对海运咽喉要道具有高度韧性。可行的咽喉要道并不会真正切断任何来自海运市场的供应。潜在的贸易流向重新调整，以及转向替代供应商或消费者，会缓和成本与收入方面的影响。我们评估认为，在大多数可行的咽喉要道关闭的情况下，进口商的成本增幅将低至每吨0.5美元或更少。例外情况是南海和东海的海上交通受限，这可能导致中国的成本每吨增加10美元，同时使该地区其他进口商的成本降低相近幅度。海运咽喉要道确实会造成区域市场的地理分隔，因此其对进口商成本和出口商收入的影响并非全球统一的。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

There is renewed attention for maritime chokepoints and their potential disruption of global trade in energy. We analyse global trade in coal, and find that it is highly resilient to maritime chokepoints. Feasible chokepoints do not truly sever any supply from the seaborne market. Potential re-routing of trade flows, and switching to alternative suppliers or consumers moderates effects on costs and revenues. We assess costs to importers would rise by as little as 0.5 \$/t or less in case of closures of most feasible chokepoints. The exception is a restriction to maritime traffic in the South and East China Sea, which could raise costs by 10 \$/t for China, whilst reducing costs for other importers in the region by similar levels. Maritime chokepoints do create geographical separation of regional markets, and therefore do not have a globally uniform effect on costs to importers and revenues to exporters.
