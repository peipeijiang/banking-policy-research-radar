<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究考察了发达市场和新兴市场低碳股票指数对全球市场不确定性、石油价格波动和通胀预期的敏感性。在分析中，我们采用了小波分位数相关方法，该方法能够在不同投资期限和市场条件下分解依赖结构，并通过滚动窗口方法对小波分位数相关框架以及由小波分位数相关导出的分位数贝塔测度进行动态扩展，从而超越静态相关分析。实证结果表明，发达市场低碳指数对全球市场不确定性表现出更强的负向依赖，尤其是在VIX渠道中，而OVX渠道中的发达市场-新兴市场（DM-EM）区分随时间推移则不那么系统。动态结果进一步表明，依赖性和暴露度在不同分位数和不同时间上存在差异，而T5YIE渠道则表现出更为异质的动态模式。总体而言，结果表明低碳资产的分散化收益因市场条件、投资期限和市场发展水平的不同而有所差异。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study examines the sensitivity of low-carbon equity indices in developed and emerging markets to global market uncertainty, oil price volatility, and inflation expectations. In the analysis, we employ the wavelet quantile correlation approach, which decomposes the dependence structure across investment horizons and market conditions, and we dynamically extend both the wavelet quantile correlation framework and the quantile beta measures derived from wavelet quantile correlation through a rolling-window methodology to move beyond static correlation analysis. The empirical findings reveal that developed market low-carbon indices exhibit stronger negative dependence on global market uncertainty, particularly in the VIX channel, whereas the DM-EM distinction in the OVX channel is less systematic over time. The dynamic results further show that dependence and exposure vary across quantiles and over time, while the T5YIE channel exhibits a more heterogeneous dynamic pattern. Overall, the results show that the diversification benefits of low-carbon assets vary depending on market conditions, investment horizon, and the level of market development.
