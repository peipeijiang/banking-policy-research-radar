<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在排他性限制、独立性和单调性假设下，Imbens和Angrist（1994）证明了Wald估计量等于局部平均处理效应（LATE）。然而，这些假设蕴含了额外的限制条件，我们利用这些限制条件对LATE进行推断。具体而言，我们提出了一种条件似然比检验，并证明该检验在一大类数据生成过程上一致地控制渐近 size。该检验的计算成本非常低：无需模拟，且最多涉及求解四个二次规划问题。蒙特卡洛模拟表明，与Wald检验和Anderson-Rubin检验相比，我们的检验能够提供显著的功效增益，包括在工具变量较弱的情形下。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Under the exclusion restriction, independence, and monotonicity assumptions, Imbens and Angrist (1994) show that the Wald estimand is equal to the local average treatment effect (LATE). However, these assumptions entail additional restrictions, which we exploit for inference on the LATE. Specifically, we propose a conditional likelihood ratio test that we show controls asymptotic size uniformly over a large class of data-generating processes. The test has very low computational cost: it requires no simulation and involves solving at most four quadratic programming problems. Monte Carlo simulations show that our test can offer substantial power gains over the Wald and Anderson-Rubin tests, including when the instrument is weak.
