# Testing for peer effects in short panels without identifying the network structure

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种基于工具的检验方法，用于在底层网络未知的短T面板中检验同伴效应。该检验通过聚合潜在同伴的协变量投影结果与残差化自身结果之间的两两比较，避免了对网络的估计。该统计量针对具有个体固定效应、异质性斜率、异方差性以及单元内序列相关的固定T、大N设定而设计。我们还开发了一种加权扩展，以考虑可加的公共时间效应。当结果的投影分量的范数有界远离零时，极限分布为标准高斯分布，但当其局部趋于零时可能为非标准分布。我们推导了一个条件高斯极限实验和一种可行的条件蒙特卡罗程序，以获得一致有效的临界值。蒙特卡罗证据表明，该条件程序在保持对潜在同伴效应备择假设的检验功效的同时，实现了准确的规模控制。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose an instrument-based test for peer effects in short-T panels when the underlying network is unknown. The test avoids estimating the network by aggregating pairwise comparisons between covariate-projected outcomes of potential peers and residualized own outcomes. The statistic is designed for fixed-T, large-N settings with individual fixed effects, heterogeneous slopes, heteroskedasticity, and serial dependence within units. We also develop a weighted extension that accounts for additive common time effects. The limiting distribution is standard Gaussian when the norm of the projected component of the outcomes is bounded away from zero, but can be nonstandard when it is local to zero. We derive a conditional Gaussian limit experiment and a feasible conditional Monte Carlo procedure to obtain uniformly valid critical values. Monte Carlo evidence shows that the conditional procedure leads to accurate size control while retaining power against latent peer-effect alternatives.
