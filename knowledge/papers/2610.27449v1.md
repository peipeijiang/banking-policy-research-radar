<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多经济问题在每个时期都有多个控制变量，它们的联合选择面临维度灾难。分阶段求解在计算上更为廉价，但没有一般性结果说明何时分阶段求解是精确的。我发展了一种可分离性理论，涵盖离散选择和非凹目标：若效用、转移和约束仅通过一个向前传递的状态依赖于较早的决策，则分阶段求解是精确的。对于每一种排序，最小的此类状态存在且在重新标记的意义下唯一。任何能使分阶段求解对所有关于后续决策和未来的偏好都保持精确的状态，必须承载最小状态为转移所承载的一切信息。对于仿射原始条件，最小状态由线性组合构成，例如手头现金（cash-on-hand），后续结果依赖于这些线性组合。其维度决定了最大阶段最小的排序。在一个六选择家庭模型中，每个阶段在一个三维状态上选择一个变量，而联合问题则涉及全部六个变量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many economic problems have several controls per period, whose joint choice is subject to the curse of dimensionality. Solving them in stages is computationally cheaper, but no general result says when staging is exact. I develop a separability theory, discrete choices and non-concave objectives included: staging is exact if utility, transitions, and constraints depend on earlier decisions only through a state passed forward. For each ordering the smallest such state exists and is unique up to relabeling. Any state that keeps staging exact for all preferences over later decisions and the future must carry everything the smallest one carries for the transition. For affine primitives, the smallest state consists of the linear combinations, such as cash-on-hand, on which later outcomes depend. Its dimensions pick the order whose largest stage is smallest. In a six-choice household model, each stage chooses one variable on a three-dimensional state, the joint problem all six.

</details>
