# The Introduction of Universal Child Care in New Mexico: Initial Evidence on Mothers' Labor Supply

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

2025年11月，新墨西哥州成为美国首个实施普惠性免费儿童保育补贴计划的州。本文通过估计双重差分模型和事件研究模型，评估普惠性儿童保育对母亲短期劳动供给调整的影响。利用月度当前人口调查（Current Population Survey）数据，我发现该改革对低技能和高技能母亲产生了不同的效应。对于低技能母亲，该政策在广延边际和集约边际上均提高了就业。相反，高技能母亲，尤其是已婚者，有偿工作时间减少，但部分被入学率上升所抵消。因此，普惠性儿童保育在缓解弱势家庭所面临的约束的同时，对优势家庭而言则起到了一种实物转移的作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In November 2025, New Mexico became the first U.S. state to implement a universal, no-cost child care subsidy program. This paper estimates difference-in-differences and event-study models to evaluate the impact of universal child care on mothers' short-run labor supply adjustments. Using monthly Current Population Survey data, I find that the reform produced different responses across lower- and higher-skilled mothers. For lower-skilled mothers, the policy increased employment along both the extensive and intensive margins. Conversely, higher-skilled mothers, particularly those who are married, experienced reductions in paid work, with partially offsetting increases in school enrollment. Thus, while universal child care relaxes a binding constraint among disadvantaged families, it acts as an in-kind transfer for their advantaged counterparts.
