<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我从基于设计的视角研究模糊双重差分设计（fuzzy difference-in-differences designs），其中潜在结果和处理决策是固定的，只有政策的分配是随机的，且分配概率具有异质性且可能是未知的。Wald-DiD设计估计量等于分配加权的依从者效应，加上来自转换者（switchers）、始终接受者效应变化以及未处理结果趋势的污染。我推导了估计量具有清晰因果解释的条件。这些条件弱于结果和处理的平行趋势假设。我们提出了一个Anderson--Rubin型检验，其采用在弱第一阶段下仍然有效的保守Neyman方差，还提出了依从者效应的敏感性分析，以及针对已知分配概率设计的估计量。对俄勒冈健康保险实验（Oregon Health Insurance Experiment）的应用说明了这些结果。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I study fuzzy difference-in-differences designs from a design-based perspective, in which potential outcomes and treatment decisions are fixed and only the assignment of a policy is random, with heterogeneous and possibly unknown assignment probabilities. The Wald-DiD design estimand equals an assignment-weighted complier effect plus contamination from switchers, from changes in always-takers' effects and from trends in untreated outcomes. I derive the conditions under which the estimand holds a clear causal interpretation. The conditions are weaker than parallel trends in both outcomes and treatments. We propose an Anderson--Rubin-type test with a conservative Neyman variance that remains valid under weak first stages, a sensitivity analysis for the complier effect, and estimators for designs with known assignment probabilities. An application to the Oregon Health Insurance Experiment illustrates the results.

</details>
