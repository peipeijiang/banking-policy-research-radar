<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

可再生能源现已主导英国发电结构，但很少占据边际定价位置，这引发了一个问题：哪些灵活技术能将富含可再生能源的系统转化为实时价格形成机制。本研究重建了2023年至2025年间50,684个半小时结算期内英国平衡机制中合格报价或要约堆栈的价格排序边缘，并将其归因于各个平衡机制单元，区分了长系统出价活跃与短系统报价活跃条件，并保留了共同边际联系。电池在出价活跃边际性中的占比从0.8%上升至36.2%，在报价活跃边际性中的占比从3.2%上升至26.9%，分别取代了联合循环燃气轮机在出价侧和抽水蓄能在报价侧的地位。按容量归一化的边际捕获率在两侧均有所上升，单元-季度固定效应显示，平衡机制活跃容量每增加100兆瓦，与季度出价侧和报价侧边际份额分别提高0.55和0.48个百分点相关。到2025年，电池行动比来自同一阶梯的容量匹配的非电池替代方案每兆瓦时优惠约9至10英镑，但在短系统时段价格最高的5%中，电池的代表性不足12.9个百分点。单个电池仍与价格接受者行为一致，而整个电池群体已内生性地参与常规平衡价格形成。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Renewables now dominate Great Britain's generation mix but rarely occupy the marginal price-setting position, which raises the question of which flexible technologies translate a renewable-rich system into real-time price formation. This study reconstructs the price-ranked edge of the eligible bid or offer stack in the GB Balancing Mechanism across 50,684 half-hourly Settlement Periods from 2023 to 2025 and attributes it to individual Balancing Mechanism Units, separating long-system bid-active from short-system offer-active conditions and retaining co-marginal ties. Batteries rose from 0.8% to 36.2% of bid-active and from 3.2% to 26.9% of offer-active marginality, displacing combined-cycle gas on the bid side and pumped storage on the offer side. Capacity-normalised marginal capture rose on both sides, and unit-quarter fixed effects show that an additional 100 MW of Balancing Mechanism active capacity was associated with a 0.55 and 0.48 percentage-point increase in quarterly bid- and offer-side marginal share. By 2025, battery actions were around £9 to 10/MWh more favourable than volume-matched non-battery alternatives drawn from the same ladder, yet batteries were under-represented by 12.9 percentage points in the highest-priced 5% of short-system periods. Individual batteries remain consistent with price-taking, while the fleet has become endogenous to routine balancing price formation.

</details>
