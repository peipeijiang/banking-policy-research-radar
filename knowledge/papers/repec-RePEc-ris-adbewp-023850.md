<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文识别了亚太沿海地区气候灾害造成的福祉损失范围，并综述了这些损失在政策与实践中的测量方式。研究基于对选定文献的针对性综述。综述识别出九个福祉损失领域：粮食安全、水和卫生设施、生计与就业、无偿劳动、流动性与流离失所、健康与心理社会福祉、安全与性别暴力、文化与社会福祉，以及生态系统损失。现有测量方法无法以全面、省时且具成本效益的方式捕捉这些福祉损失。关于孟加拉国气旋“雷马尔”（Cyclone Remal）的案例研究凸显了利用定制快速调查工具及时收集按性别分列数据的重要性，以实现有效且公平的灾害响应、恢复和韧性建设。本文提出了一个数据收集框架，并推荐了关键福祉指标。研究强调有必要将定制快速调查工具制度化纳入国家系统，并加强数据能力和协调，以支持性别包容的灾害响应。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper identifies the range of well-being losses caused by climatic hazards in coastal regions of Asia and the Pacific and reviews how these losses are measured for policy and practice. It draws on a targeted review of selected studies. The review identifies nine domains of well-being loss: food security, water and sanitation, livelihoods and employment, unpaid work, mobility and displacement, health and psychosocial well-being, safety and gender-based violence, cultural and social well-being, and ecosystem loss. Existing measurement approaches are not equipped to capture these well-being losses in a comprehensive, time-effective, and cost-effective manner. A case study on Cyclone Remal in Bangladesh highlights the importance of timely, sex-disaggregated data collection using customized rapid survey tools for effective and equitable disaster response, recovery, and resilience. The paper proposes a framework for data collection and recommends key well-being indicators. It emphasizes the need to institutionalize tailored rapid survey tools within national systems and strengthen data capacity and coordination to support gender-inclusive disaster response.
