<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一个拥有承诺能力的发送者向已经知情的接收者提供额外信息。最终后验均值在凸序意义下必须比初始均值更分散，但比状态本身更不分散。我们刻画了这两个合理性约束何时也是充分的，从而使得任何满足这些约束的分布都可以被诱导。如果状态具有正密度，那么它们恰好在区间划分信息下是充分的：接收者了解到状态落在某个划分的哪个区间内，仅此而已，其中区间可以是单点。否则，即使发送者观察到接收者的信息，满足这两个约束的一个非空开集分布也无法被诱导。我们利用充分情形推导出第二价格拍卖中的最优对称披露规则，其中竞标者最初知道估值区间。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A sender with commitment provides additional information to an already informed receiver. Final posterior means must be more dispersed than initial means and less dispersed than the state, in convex order. We characterize when these two plausibility constraints are also sufficient, so that every distribution satisfying them can be induced. If the state has a positive density, they are sufficient exactly under interval-partition information: the receiver learns which interval of a partition contains the state and nothing more, where an interval may be a single point. Otherwise, a nonempty open set of distributions satisfying both constraints cannot be induced, even when the sender observes the receiver's information. We use the sufficient case to derive an optimal symmetric disclosure rule in a second-price auction where bidders initially know valuation intervals.

</details>
