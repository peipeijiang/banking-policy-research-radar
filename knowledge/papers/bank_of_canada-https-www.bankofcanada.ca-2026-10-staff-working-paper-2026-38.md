# The Distributional Consequences of Commodity Booms

<details open>
<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

正向商品价格冲击会通过压缩技能溢价来降低商品出口经济体的劳动收入不平等，因为建筑等非熟练劳动密集型部门的扩张速度快于技能密集型服务业。一个多部门小型开放经济模型展示了将商品意外收益用于储蓄或支出如何强化或缓和这一不平等下降趋势。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Positive commodity price shocks reduce labor income inequality in commodity-exporting economies by compressing the skill premium, as unskilled-labor-intensive sectors such as construction expand faster than skill-intensive services. A multisector small-open-economy model shows how saving or spending commodity windfalls can reinforce or moderate this decline in inequality.

</details>
