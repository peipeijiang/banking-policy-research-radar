<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

交易成本可能成就或毁掉一个交易策略，尤其是在大宗商品和宏观市场的相对价值交易中，其利润空间仅有几个基点。价格冲击是交易成本的核心组成部分。价格冲击模型通常包含自身冲击（某一合约的交易会推动该合约自身价格变动），但忽略了两个已被充分证实的效应：交叉冲击（某一合约的交易也会推动相关合约的价格变动）和瞬态冲击（价格冲击随时间衰减，因此平仓可部分回收建仓成本）。未包含这些效应的模型会高估相对价值交易的影响，此类交易的相关腿在数日内构建并平仓，从而可能放弃原本有利可图的交易机会。本文通过一个凸二次成本函数对这两种效应进行建模。在每个时期，一个由波动率、成交量和相关性预测构建的半正定矩阵将各合约间的交易耦合起来；随后，一个幂律衰减核将各时期之间的交易耦合起来。由此产生的成本即使在规划期内流动性发生变化时也不会引发价格操纵。该模型在原油期货日历价差交易中围绕大宗商品指数展期进行了实证验证。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Transaction costs can make or break a trading strategy, particularly in relative-value trading of commodity and macro markets, where edges are a few basis points. Price impact is a central component of transaction cost. Price impact models usually include self-impact (a trade in a contract moves that contract's price) but omit two well-documented effects: cross-impact (a trade in one contract also moves the prices of related contracts) and transient impact (price impact decays over time, so an unwind recovers part of the entry cost). A model without these effects overprices the impact of relative-value trades, whose correlated legs are built and unwound over days, and so forgoes potentially profitable trades. This paper models both effects with a convex quadratic cost. In each period, a positive semidefinite matrix built from volatility, volume, and correlation forecasts couples trades across contracts. A power-law decay kernel then couples trades across periods. The resulting cost admits no price manipulation even when liquidity varies over the planning horizon. The model is demonstrated empirically on calendar spread trading of crude oil futures around the commodity index roll.

</details>
