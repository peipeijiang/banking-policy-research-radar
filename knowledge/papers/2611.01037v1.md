# Contracting for Experimentation with Private Findings

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01037v1)

## 一句话结论

> 构建动态委托代理模型研究代理人可私下获取并隐瞒或伪造有利发现时的最优合同，证明最优合同仅支付与时间挂钩的成功奖金而非基于报告，且奖金在研究阶段高且递增、在开发阶段降至低位恒定。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

委托人雇佣代理人为一个质量未知的项目工作。成功与否可缔约，但在成功发生之前，代理人可能私下获得有利的发现。例如，研发药物的科学家可能观察到有前景的显微镜读数。知情的代理人可能隐瞒该发现；不知情的代理人可能伪造发现。代理人的私人信息可以同时指导项目期限及其薪酬。然而，我证明最优合同只支付与时间挂钩的成功奖金，而不支付与报告发现挂钩的奖金，这与研究资助中常用的基于时间的支付安排相一致。动态到达的私人信息驱动了这种简单性：代理人可以隐瞒好消息以保留更高的奖金，因此遏制隐瞒就限制了不同报告历史之间的薪酬差异。在研究阶段，奖金较高且随时间上升，该阶段诱导学习；随后在开发阶段降至较低且恒定的水平，在该阶段只有知情的代理人继续工作。上升的奖金反映了一种新颖的力量：来自私人学习的租金激励努力，但随着剩余研究期减少而收缩。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A principal hires an agent to work on a project of unknown quality. Success is contractible, but before it occurs, the agent may privately obtain a favorable finding. For example, a scientist developing a drug may observe a promising microscope reading. An informed agent may conceal the finding; an uninformed agent may fake one. The agent's private information could guide both the project's duration and his pay. Yet I show that an optimal contract pays only success bonuses tied to time, not reported findings, consistent with time-based payment schedules often used in research funding. Dynamically arriving private information drives this simplicity: the agent can conceal good news to retain higher bonuses, so deterring concealment limits pay differences across report histories. Bonuses are high and rising in a research stage that induces learning, then fall to a low, constant level in a development stage where only an informed agent continues working. Rising bonuses reflect a novel force: rents from private learning motivate effort but shrink as fewer research periods remain.
