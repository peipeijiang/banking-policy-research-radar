# Trading Scope for Credibility in Difference-in-Differences

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16867v1)

## 一句话结论

> 提出一种在平行趋势假设部分失效时估计处理效应的方法，通过重新加权组-时间效应来识别可信子总体的局部ATT，并应用于页岩气繁荣对房价的影响，发现总体效应不显著。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当平行趋势假设对某些处理组群成立而对其他组群不成立时，处理组的平均处理效应（ATT）——即对所有组群的平均——恰恰成为难以恢复的目标。我们建议改变估计量而非为其辩护。可信子群体局部ATT（LATT）是平行趋势假设可信的组群子群体的效应，它仅在所选组群满足平行趋势的条件下即可点识别，这一条件比ATT所需的假设更弱，且在ATT假设失效时仍可能成立。该效应通过对标准组群-时间效应进行重新加权以聚焦于这些组群来估计，并配以诚实的敏感性界限，以界定事前趋势筛查无法排除的残余违背。模拟结果证实，该方法的优势随着事前趋势对事后违背的信息量增加而增大。在一项应用中，页岩气繁荣对当地房价影响的汇总估计显著为正，但事实证明该估计依赖于在冲击发生前已呈趋势的组群，而可信子群体分析则显示不存在效应。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When parallel trends fails for some treated cohorts but not others, the average treatment effect on the treated (ATT), an average over all of them, is exactly the target that becomes hard to recover. We propose changing the estimand rather than defending it. The credible-subpopulation local ATT (LATT) is the effect for the subpopulation of cohorts whose parallel trends is credible, and it is point-identified under parallel trends for the selected cohorts alone, a weaker requirement that can hold when the ATT's fails. It is estimated by reweighting standard group-time effects toward those cohorts, and paired with honest sensitivity bounds on the residual violation that a pre-trend screen cannot rule out. The method's advantage grows with how informative pre-trends are about post-treatment violations, as simulations confirm. In an application, a significantly positive pooled estimate of the shale boom's effect on local house prices proves to rest on cohorts already trending before onset, and the credible subpopulation reveals no effect.

</details>
