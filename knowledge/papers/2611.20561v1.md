# When Better Artificial Intelligence Widens the Adoption Gap in Health Care

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20561v1)

## 一句话结论

> 构建理论模型分析医疗服务提供者影响力如何影响AI采纳与审查要求两项政策选择，发现AI能力提升会使提供者从采纳中获益先升后降，进而反对高自主性AI，导致采纳不足与审查要求偏离患者最优。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人工智能（AI）可以协助医疗服务提供者进行临床实践，或替代他们获得报酬的服务。我们建模了提供者的影响力如何塑造两项政策选择。第一项是采用率，即将覆盖范围切换为使用AI提供护理的病例比例。第二项是审查要求，即在临床护理中使用AI时必须伴随的有偿审查数量。辅助功能可以降低提供者的成本并增加其利润，同时不影响有偿审查。然而，能力日益增强的AI可能支持自主使用，从而替代审查并减少提供者的利润。随着AI能力的提升，提供者从采用中获得的收益先上升后下降。因此，如果提供者因审查损失的收入超过更好辅助带来的节省，他们就会反对足够自主的AI。对于替代更多审查的AI，对AI采用的反对出现得更早。当提供者能够影响采用率但无法影响审查要求时，他们的反对会导致采用不足。随着AI的进步，边际上放弃的患者价值不断增大。在内部政策下，提供者对两项决策的影响力扩大了采用率，并将审查要求提高到高于患者最优水平。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial intelligence (AI) can assist healthcare providers in clinical practice or replace services for which they are paid. We model how provider influence shapes two policy choices. The first is adoption, the share of cases in which coverage is switched to care using AI. The second is the review requirement, the number of paid reviews that must accompany AI when it is used in clinical care. Assistance can lower providers' costs and increase their profits while leaving paid reviews unaffected. However, increasingly capable AI may support autonomous use that can replace reviews and reduce providers' profits. Provider gains from adoption first rise and subsequently fall as AI capability improves. Hence, providers oppose sufficiently autonomous AI if their lost income from reviews exceeds savings from better assistance. Opposition to AI adoption arises earlier for AI that displaces more reviews. When providers can influence adoption but not the review requirement, their opposition causes under-adoption. The patient value forgone at the margin grows as AI improves. At interior policies, provider influence over both decisions widens adoption and raises review requirements above the patient optimum.
