<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

乘法康德均衡要求行为主体评估他们是否更偏好所有参与者按一个共同比例因子改变行动。本文指出，这一反事实并不能仅由物质支付环境完全确定：还必须明确比例普遍化是在何种尺度上进行的。基于Sher-Roemer关于策略非不变性的讨论，我们刻画了行动的平滑单调重参数化如何改变乘法康德一阶条件。随后，我们用一个简单的两国减排问题说明该问题。将实际减排量普遍化与将减排支出普遍化都是“尽自己一份力”的自然解释，但尽管它们描述的是同一物质环境，却导致不同的康德结果。本文还识别了内部一阶条件具有表示不变性的特殊情形，包括共同指数幂变换以及相同变换下的对称剖面。结论具有建设性：将乘法康德均衡应用于经济环境，既需要明确康德准则的使用方式，也需要明确评估康德反事实时所采用的表示尺度。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Multiplicative Kantian equilibrium requires agents to evaluate whether they would prefer all players to change their actions by a common proportional factor. This note argues that such a counterfactual is not fully specified by the material payoff environment alone: one must also specify the scale in which proportional universalization is performed. Building on the Sher-Roemer discussion of strategic non-invariance, we characterize how a smooth monotone reparametrization of actions changes the multiplicative Kantian first-order condition. We then illustrate the issue in a simple two-country abatement problem. Universalizing physical abatement and universalizing abatement expenditures are both natural interpretations of "doing one's part," but they lead to different Kantian outcomes, even though they describe the same material environment. The note also identifies special cases in which the interior first-order condition is representation-invariant, including common-exponent power transformations and symmetric profiles under identical transformations. The conclusion is constructive: applying multiplicative Kantian equilibrium to an economic environment requires specifying both the use of the Kantian criterion and the represented scale in which the Kantian counterfactual is evaluated.

</details>
