<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

信用压力测试不仅需要宏观金融驱动因素的脉冲响应，还需要投资组合违约概率的脉冲响应。我们在一个模块化框架中推导了均值、分位数（风险违约概率，PD-at-Risk）以及预期短缺的闭式广义脉冲响应，该框架结合了贝叶斯向量自回归（Bayesian VAR）、高斯卫星模型以及巴塞尔内部评级法（Basel IRB）所依据的默顿-瓦西切克（Merton-Vasicek）模型。研究结果可推广至任何潜因子的probit-高斯映射。非线性使得响应依赖于条件均值和条件方差；插件式评估将预测违约概率水平低估了6-8%，并遗漏了尾部极值分位数。对于美国地缘政治风险冲击，99%分位数的响应比均值响应高出50%，且峰值响应在信贷周期中的变化幅度达4.6倍。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Credit stress testing requires impulse responses of portfolio default probabilities, not only macro-financial drivers. We derive closed-form generalized impulse responses for the mean, quantiles (PD-at-Risk), and expected shortfall in a modular framework combining a Bayesian VAR, a Gaussian satellite, and the Merton-Vasicek model underlying Basel IRB regulation. Results extend to any probit-Gaussian mapping of a latent factor. Nonlinearity makes responses depend on conditional means and variances; plug-in evaluations understate projected default probability levels by 6-8% and miss tail quantiles. For U.S. geopolitical risk shocks, 99%-quantile responses exceed mean responses by 50%, and peak responses vary 4.6-fold across the credit cycle.
