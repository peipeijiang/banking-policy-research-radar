# Entry Motivations Shape Preferences for Social Protection Among Platform Workers

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/iza/izadps/dp18967.html)

## 一句话结论

> 通过离散选择实验研究德国、意大利和波兰近800名平台工作者对带薪病假和带薪年假的支付意愿，发现'推入型'工作者的支付意愿显著高于'拉入型'工作者，表明社会保障扩展的福利影响取决于平台劳动力中经济需要驱动型工作者的比例。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了平台工作者对社会保护的偏好——以病假工资和带薪年假的形式——并区分了因经济需要而进入平台工作的劳动者（“推动型”）与因平台工作的灵活性和优势而被吸引的劳动者（“拉动型”）。通过对德国、意大利和波兰近800名出租车、配送和清洁平台工作者进行离散选择实验（discrete-choice experiment），我们发现，劳动者对病假工资保障的平均支付意愿为收入的3.7%，对带薪年假的平均支付意愿为4.6%。然而，偏好因动机不同而差异显著：推动型劳动者平均愿意为病假工资放弃收入的5.3%，为带薪年假放弃5.6%，而拉动型劳动者的估值则大幅较低（分别为3.1%和4.2%）。这一差异在无法获得这些福利的劳动者中最为明显，表明缺乏保护对那些在经济约束更大情况下进入平台工作的人尤为具有影响。研究结果表明，将社会保护扩展至平台工作者——如欧盟《平台工作指令》（EU Platform Work Directive）所提议的——其福利影响可能取决于平台劳动力中需求驱动型劳动者的比例。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study platform workers' preferences for social protection â€” in the form of sick pay and paid annual leave â€” distinguishing between workers who entered platform work out of economic necessity (the "pushed") and those attracted by its flexibility and advantages (the "pulled"). Using a discrete-choice experiment administered to nearly 800 taxi, delivery, and cleaning platform workers in Germany, Italy, and Poland, we find an average willingness to pay at 3.7% of earnings for sick pay coverage and 4.6% for paid annual leave. Preferences, however, differ sharply by motivation: pushed workers are willing to forgo on average 5.3% of earnings for sick pay, and 5.6% for paid annual leave, while pulled workers' valuations are substantially lower (3.1% and 4.2%, respectively). This difference is most pronounced among workers without access to these benefits, suggesting that the absence of protections is particularly consequential for those who entered platform work under greater economic constraint. The findings indicate that the welfare impact of extending social protections to platform workers â€” as proposed by the EU Platform Work Directive â€” might depend on the share of necessity-driven workers in the platform workforce.
