<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究不确定性下的二元选择问题，其中两个行为之间的比较可能依赖于它们已实现结果的联合分布。这引出了相关性概率精致性（correlation probabilistic sophistication, CPS），一种主观约简原则，它同时推广了概率精致性和Fishburn的非传递主观期望效用。我们展示了同时事件可交换性如何引出一个唯一的可解有限可加主观概率，并将行为的二元比较约简为诱导联合分布律。在凸值域下，诱导域与完整的有限联合分布律域重合，从而允许从风险情形转移相关性效用表示，并在完备性和强独立性下，得到具有斜对称比较核的相关性期望效用。在完备性和传递性下，CPS退化为普通的概率精致性。我们进一步将该框架扩展到多个小世界，允许相关性敏感性随不确定性来源而变化。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study binary choice under uncertainty when the comparison between two acts can depend on the joint distribution of their realized outcomes. This motivates correlation probabilistic sophistication (CPS), a subjective reduction principle that extends both probabilistic sophistication and Fishburn's nontransitive subjective expected utility. We show how simultaneous event exchangeability elicits a unique solvable finitely additive subjective probability and reduces binary comparisons of acts to induced joint laws. Under convex range, the induced domain coincides with the full finite joint-law domain, allowing the transfer of correlation-utility representations from risk and, under completeness and Strong Independence, correlation expected utility with a skew-symmetric comparison kernel. Under completeness and transitivity, CPS collapses to ordinary probabilistic sophistication. We further extend the framework to multiple small worlds, allowing correlation sensitivity to vary with the source of uncertainty.
