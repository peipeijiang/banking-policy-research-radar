<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文发展了用于处理效应异质性的免调优检验。通过将原假设刻画为期望分位数处理效应曲线的恒定性，我们以均值中心化样本期望分位数过程的连续泛函构造检验统计量。我们建立了一致Bahadur表示，推导了检验统计量在原假设和固定备择假设下的渐近性质，并证明了其对以$n^{-1/2}$速率逼近原假设的局部备择假设具有非平凡功效。对于可行推断，我们开发了一种快速乘子自助法，该方法能够一致地近似非枢轴原分布，同时捕捉估计未知公共偏移量的一阶效应。该过程免调优，无需密度或得分函数估计、带宽选择或重复拟合。该框架进一步纳入了逐点学生化精化以及一种协变量调整，后者在不改变总体差异的情况下减少渐近变异。与现有方法的蒙特卡洛比较表明，该方法通常具有可靠的 size 控制、有竞争力的功效以及显著的计算节省。对一项大规模节水实验的应用展示了所提出的检验方法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops tuning-free tests for treatment-effect heterogeneity. By characterizing the null hypothesis as constancy of the expectile treatment-effect curve, we construct test statistics as continuous functionals of the mean-centered sample expectile process. We establish a uniform Bahadur representation, derive the asymptotic properties of the test statistics under the null and fixed alternatives, and show nontrivial power against local alternatives approaching the null at the $n^{-1/2}$ rate. For feasible inference, we develop a fast multiplier bootstrap that consistently approximates the nonpivotal null distribution while capturing the first-order effect of estimating the unknown common shift. The procedure is tuning-free, requiring no density or score-function estimation, bandwidth selection, or repeated refitting. The framework further incorporates a pointwise-studentized refinement and a covariate adjustment that reduces asymptotic variation without altering the population discrepancy. Monte Carlo comparisons with existing methods show generally reliable size control, competitive power, and substantial computational savings. An application to a large-scale water-conservation experiment illustrates the proposed tests.
