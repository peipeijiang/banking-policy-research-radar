<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

重复博弈策略可以通过跨时间平衡受控奖励来施加全局收益关系。问题在于，当对手在观察每一轮后决定是否继续该关系时，还剩下什么。针对每一个具有有限期望持续时间的、依赖于历史记录的对手策略，一个累积线性恒等式是可执行的，当且仅当其期望阶段增量在每一个可达历史处为零。因此，策略性退出将全局控制转化为局部控制。在一个具有唯一局部均衡器的双行动博弈中，控制者必须在每一个可达历史处使用相同的混合行动，且对任一博弈方均无记忆限制。对于囚徒困境博弈，参数为$(T,R,P,S)=(5,3,1,0)$时，存续的恒等式为：
  $(1+p)U_X+(4-p)U_Y+5(p^2-3p-1)E[τ]=0$
  该持续时间系数在区间$[0,1]$上永不消失。因此，策略性双边退出留下了一个一维的收益-持续时间关系，同时排除了所有非平凡的纯收益累积恒等式。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Repeated-game strategies can impose global payoff relations by balancing controlled rewards across time. The question is what remains when the opponent decides, after observing each round, whether the relationship continues. Against every history-dependent opponent strategy with finite expected duration, a cumulative linear identity is enforceable if and only if its expected stage increment vanishes at every reachable history. Strategic exit therefore converts global control into local control. In a two-action game with a unique local equalizer, the controller must use the same mixed action at every reachable history, without any memory restriction on either player. For the Prisoner's Dilemma with $(T,R,P,S)=(5,3,1,0)$, the surviving identities are
  $(1+p)U_X+(4-p)U_Y+5(p^2-3p-1)E[τ]=0$
  The duration coefficient never vanishes on $[0,1]$. Hence strategic bilateral exit leaves a one-dimensional payoff-duration relation, while ruling out every nontrivial payoff-only cumulative identity.

</details>
