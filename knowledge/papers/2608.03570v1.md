<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文利用空间细化的冲突与人口数据，考察了西非政治暴力在城市群及其周边地区之间的分布情况。分析覆盖2012年至2025年中的十七个国家，结果显示，政治暴力与城市及其边缘地区密切相关，大多数暴力事件发生在城市群内部及其周边。大城市在行政边界内记录的暴力事件数量最多，而较小的城镇——通常更靠近边境——则在行政边界外经历的暴力水平更高。然而，以城市为中心的暴力自2012年以来也有所下降，表明冲突正逐步向农村地区扩散。这些发现为“冲突城市化”的叙事提供了重要的区域背景，表明西非叛乱运动在战略上同时利用城市与农村环境。研究还强调了边缘人口持续面临的脆弱性，以及在主要城市之外采取流动式和社区化安全手段的必要性。最后，研究凸显了加强边境基础设施、交通网络和公民机构建设作为发展与安全双重工具的重要性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines how political violence in West Africa is distributed between urban agglomerations and their surrounding areas using spatially disaggregated conflict and population data. Covering seventeen countries from 2012 to mid-2025, the analysis shows that political violence remains clearly tied to cities and their peripheries with the majority of violence occurring within and around urban agglomerations. Large cities record the highest number of incidents within their boundaries, while smaller towns, which are more frequently situated near borders, typically experience higher levels of violence outside their boundaries. However, urban-centric violence has also declined since 2012, indicating a progressive ruralization of conflict. These findings provide important regional context to the urbanization of conflict narrative by showing that West African insurgent movements use both urban and rural environments strategically. They also underscore the ongoing vulnerability of peripheral populations and the need for mobile and community-based security approaches outside major cities. Finally, they highlight the importance of strengthening border infrastructure, transport networks, and civic institutions as tools of both development and security.
