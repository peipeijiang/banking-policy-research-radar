<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文展示了研究人员如何在存在内生性、自相关或伪相关的情况下，仍能利用局部外生变异（locally exogeneous variation）。文章描述了基于县级数据、普查区级数据及类似区域平均数据的广义最小二乘估计的填充渐近性质（infill asymptotic properties），包括一致性、渐近正态性和有效推断的充分条件。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This article shows how researchers can exploit \textit{locally exogeneous variation} even in the presence of endogeneity, autocorrelation, or spurious correlation. It describes the infill asymptotic properties of generalized least squares estimation with county-level data, Census tract-level data, and similar regional averages, including sufficient conditions for consistency, asymptotic normality, and valid inference.
