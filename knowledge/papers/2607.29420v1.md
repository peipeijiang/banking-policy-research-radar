<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究最优拍卖问题，其中资源配置会对第三方执行者产生道德风险问题。执行者在竞得者确定之前选择努力水平。在常规的独立私人价值环境中，最优机制为影子评分拍卖。每位竞标者的虚拟价值通过利用该竞标者的配置状态来放松执行者激励约束的影子价值进行调整。与标准评分拍卖不同，该评分来源于非竞标者的道德风险约束，而非对质量的偏好。保留价格形式缺乏这一排序渠道，因为它们只能调整物品是否售出，却无法偏向那些更有助于激励执行努力的配置状态。本文还研究了评分表示失效的情形，此时最优机制变为受约束的影子配置。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies optimal auctions in which the allocation creates a moral hazard problem for a third-party executor. The executor chooses effort before the winner is known. In regular independent-private-values environments, the optimal mechanism is a shadow-score auction. Each bidder's virtual value is adjusted by the shadow value of relaxing the executor's incentive constraint using that bidder's allocation state. Unlike standard scoring auctions, the score is derived from a non-bidder's moral hazard constraint rather than from a preference for quality. Reserve formats miss this ranking channel, as they can adjust whether the object is sold, but cannot favor allocation states that are more useful for motivating execution effort. The paper also studies cases in which this scoring representation breaks down and the optimal mechanism becomes a constrained shadow allocation.
