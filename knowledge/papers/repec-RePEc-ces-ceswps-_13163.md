<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

为什么人工智能的进步尚未加速总体生产率增长？一个表面上稳定的平衡增长体制，是否可能掩盖着一场即将到来的相变——这场相变将引发继新石器革命和工业革命之后人类历史上的第三次大转型？本研究提出如下假说：人工智能革命将引发一场相变，在这场相变中，现行的平衡增长体制及其引力将消失，而一条急剧加快的增长轨迹将作为新的吸引子出现，带来增长过程的深刻蜕变。本研究提出一种人工智能革命的动态理论，其中，一个看似稳定的平衡增长体制会内生地生成自身消亡的条件。随着人工智能进步相对于技术前沿的收益递减使技术知识与人工智能能力的增长率趋于一致，总体生产率增长可能保持稳定。然而，绝对人工智能能力会无情地逼近认知阈值，一旦跨越该阈值便会释放出自主的人工智能驱动创新，从根本上改变创新引擎。递归式的人工智能自我改进推动经济要么走向一条大幅提高的平衡增长路径，要么走向一种持续加速增长的全新体制。在生产率稳定增长这一具有欺骗性的平静表象之下，变革性力量正在不可阻挡地积聚，注定要释放出对人类境况前所未有的彻底变革。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Why haven't advances in AI accelerated aggregate productivity growth? Could an apparently stable balanced-growth regime conceal an impending phase transition that would trigger the Third Great Transition in human history, following the Neolithic and Industrial Revolutions? This research advances the hypothesis that the AI Revolution will unleash a phase transition in which the prevailing balanced-growth regime and its gravitational pull will vanish, while a radically faster growth trajectory will emerge as a new attractor, bringing about a profound metamorphosis of the growth process. It advances a dynamic theory of the AI Revolution in which a deceptively stable balanced-growth regime endogenously generates the conditions for its own demise. As diminishing returns to AI advances relative to the technological frontier align the growth rates of technological knowledge and AI capability, aggregate productivity growth may remain stable. Yet absolute AI capability advances relentlessly toward the cognitive threshold that unleashes autonomous AI-driven innovation, fundamentally transforming the engine of innovation. Recursive AI improvement propels the economy toward either a substantially higher balanced-growth path or a regime of perpetually accelerating growth. Beneath the deceptive calm of stable productivity growth lies the inexorable buildup of transformative forces, destined to unleash an unprecedented transformation of the human condition.
