# The Triadic Stress Index in Financial Markets

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10788v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出并测试了一种基于网络拓扑的金融压力指数（TSI），在多个资产市场中与现有指标比较，发现其具有更清晰的警报和更好的检测性能，但并非针对特定政策领域。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

三元压力指数（Triadic Stress Index, TSI）采用了一种网络指标，其四个因子最初在土壤微生物共现网络中被观察到，并将其不加修改地应用于金融资产的相关性网络。

我们在五个市场上对其进行了测试，时间跨度涵盖2006年至2026年（包括银行业危机和人工智能板块的股票、加密货币、大宗商品、外汇及主权债务），针对三种独立的危机事件定义，在固定预警预算下，进行样本外测试，采用块自助法（block-bootstrap）区间，并在整个测试族中应用霍尔校正（Holm correction）。基准方法包括：吸收比率（Absorption Ratio），即MSCI和各国央行使用的行业标准；有效秩（effective rank）和Vendi分数，即现有最尖锐的谱度量方法；Ollivier-Ricci曲率；以及带符号相关网络的全局和局部平衡指数。

有三项比较结果有利于该指数。它具备逐节点分解功能，即diag(A^3)，无需选择任何参数即可指出哪个资产承载了集中度，其得分为0.97-0.99，而唯一已发表的逐节点替代方法的得分为0.33-0.84；相比之下，谱归因方法必须首先选择读取多少个分量，并且在标准但错误的选择下会失效。其预警信号是所有测试方法中最干净的，其中4.0%的预警没有对应的事件，而有效秩的这一比例为14.7%，吸收比率则约为59%。在样本外检测中，其F1分数比吸收比率高出0.273，p<0.0005。

其余的比较结果为平局。与有效秩和Vendi分数相比，该指数在每种方案和两个样本中均持平，而在最严格的标注下，其相对于吸收比率的优势有所缩小。在真实矩阵上，远为简单的节点度（node degree）即可复现该归因结果。领先-滞后分析显示峰值互相关出现在零滞后处：这表明该指数是一个同步状态指标，而非预测工具。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The Triadic Stress Index (TSI) takes a network index whose four factors were first observed in soil microbiome co-occurrence networks and applies it, without alteration, to the correlation network of financial assets.
  We test it on five markets spanning 2006-2026 (equities including banking crises and the AI sector, cryptocurrencies, commodities, foreign exchange and sovereign debt), against three independent definitions of a crisis episode, at a fixed alarm budget, out of sample, with block-bootstrap intervals and a Holm correction across the family of tests. The benchmarks are the Absorption Ratio, the industry standard used by MSCI and central banks; the effective rank and the Vendi score, the sharpest spectral measures available; Ollivier-Ricci curvature; and the global and local balance indices of signed correlation networks.
  Three comparisons favour the index. It carries a per-node decomposition, diag(A^3), naming which asset is carrying the concentration with no parameter to select, and scores 0.97-0.99 against 0.33-0.84 for the only published per-node alternative, whereas spectral attribution must first choose how many components to read and collapses under a standard but wrong choice. Its alarms are the cleanest of anything tested, 4.0% of them with no matching episode against 14.7% for the effective rank and roughly 59% for the Absorption Ratio. And it beats the Absorption Ratio on detection by 0.273 in F1 out of sample, p<0.0005.
  The remaining comparisons are ties. Against the effective rank and the Vendi score the index ties in every scheme and both samples, and the margin over the Absorption Ratio narrows under the strictest labelling. On real matrices the far simpler node degree reproduces the attribution. A lead-lag analysis puts the peak cross-correlation at zero lag: this is a coincident state index, not a forecast.

</details>
