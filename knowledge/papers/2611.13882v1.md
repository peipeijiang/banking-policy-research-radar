<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

预测评估越来越多地使用高维预测变量，但大型预测模型的估计与正则化可能会扭曲传统的回测。这一问题在预测下行风险时尤为重要，因为下行风险的信噪比较低。我们开发了样本外期望损失（ES）回测方法，能够消除估计高维风险价值（VaR）、ES以及评估工具所产生的大幅一阶偏差效应。在线性ES设定下，所提出的方法可以方便地实现为双重Lasso（Double Lasso），并以经尾部规模调整的得分作为生成因变量。更一般地，我们刻画了正交ES回测矩，并在时间序列相依性以及具有重叠和扩展训练窗口的重复估计下，建立了Oracle等价性和有效推断。模拟表明，传统回测可能对ES最优预测严重过度拒绝，而正交化改善了规模控制。将其应用于七国集团（G7）经济体的下行增长预测时发现，有证据反对仅基于美国增长的ES设定，尽管面向预测的正则化并未选择任何额外的金融预测变量。该应用还表明，考虑估计问题可以在任一方向上改变拒绝决策。这些发现凸显了预测变量选择与回测在高维尾部风险模型评估中的不同作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Forecast evaluation increasingly uses high-dimensional predictors, but estimation and regularization of large predictive models can distort conventional backtests. This issue is particularly important for forecasting downside risk, where the signal-to-noise ratio is low. We develop out-of-sample Expected Shortfall (ES) backtests that remove the large first-order bias effects of estimating high-dimensional Value-at-Risk, ES, and the evaluation instrument. In a linear ES specification, the proposed methods are easily implemented as Double Lasso with a tail-size-adjusted score as a generated dependent variable. More generally, we characterize orthogonal ES backtesting moments, and establish oracle equivalence and valid inference under time-series dependence and repeated estimation with overlapping and expanding training windows. Simulations show that conventional backtests can severely overreject ES optimal forecasts, whereas orthogonalization improves size control. An application to downside-growth forecasting for G7 economies finds evidence against a US growth-only ES specification, even though forecast-oriented regularization selects no additional financial predictors. The application also shows that accounting for estimation can change rejection decisions in either direction. These findings highlight the distinct roles of predictive variable selection and backtesting in the evaluation of high-dimensional tail-risk models.
