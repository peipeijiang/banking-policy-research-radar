<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

条件期望分位数风险价值（EVaR）因其良好的理论性质在金融风险管理中受到越来越多的关注。然而，现有的条件EVaR估计方法要么依赖于可能设定错误的参数模型，要么在非参数方法中面临维数灾难问题。为此，我们研究了误差分布未指定的非线性时间序列模型中条件EVaR的半参数估计与推断。具体而言，我们提出了一种两步条件EVaR估计量，该估计量达到了参数收敛速率。进一步地，我们推导了条件EVaR过程的泛函中心极限定理，并扩展研究了条件期望分位数期望损失和条件分布的估计。我们提出了一种残差自助法（residual bootstrap），以供非专家从业者进行统计推断。渐近结果及其应用通过数值研究和针对S\&P 500收益率的实证应用得到了说明。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Conditional expectile-based value-at-risk (EVaR) has attracted increasing attention in financial risk management owing to its desirable theoretical properties. However, existing estimation methods for conditional EVaR either rely on potentially misspecified parametric models or suffer from the curse of dimensionality in nonparametric approaches. To this end, we study semiparametric estimation and inference for conditional EVaR in nonlinear time series models with unspecified error distribution. Specifically, we propose a two-step conditional EVaR estimator that achieves the parametric convergence rate. Furthermore, we derive a functional central limit theorem for conditional EVaR process, and then extend to investigate estimating both conditional expectile-based expected shortfall and conditional distribution. A residual bootstrap procedure is proposed for non-expert practitioners to conduct statistical inference. The asymptotic results and their applications are illustrated by numerical studies and an empirical application to S\&P 500 returns.
