<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考虑当代理人之间可以进行转移支付时的谈判问题。此类情形通常被建模为具有可转移效用的联盟博弈。然而，该模型隐含了一个强假设：结果只能取决于每个代理人联盟所能实现的总剩余，而不能取决于联盟内部哪些代理人创造了该剩余。这一假设是否合理？我们定义了一个更丰富的模型，在该模型中，解可以依赖于谁创造了剩余。在此模型中，Shapley值的经典公理化不再成立：一个广泛的解族满足效率性、匿名性、哑元性质以及可加性。尽管如此，通过在原始公理基础上增加连续性和对哑元性质的强化，我们在更丰富的模型中获得了Shapley值的一个公理化刻画。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We consider the problem of bargaining when transfers between agents are possible. Such situations are typically modelled as coalitional games with transferable utilities. However this model makes a strong implicit assumption: the outcome can only depend on the total surplus that each coalition of agents can achieve, not on which agents within the coalition generate the surplus. Is this assumption justified? We define a richer model in which solutions may depend on who generates the surplus. In this model, the classical axiomatisation of the Shapley value fails: a broad family of solutions satisfy efficiency, anonymity, the dummy property, and additivity. Nevertheless, we obtain an axiomatisation of the Shapley value in the richer model by adding continuity and a strengthening of the dummy property to the original axioms.

</details>
