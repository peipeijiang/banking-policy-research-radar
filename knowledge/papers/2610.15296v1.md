<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察了循环神经网络及其扩展模型在预测自回归因果—非因果过程（autoregressive causal-noncausal processes）中的应用，这类过程具有局部爆炸性模式，如泡沫和尖峰。我们研究了循环神经网络（RNN）、长短期记忆网络（LSTM）、门控循环单元（GRU）、双向LSTM，以及一个简单的前馈网络（FNN），以揭示根据网络类型和训练集长度的不同，预测极端值所面临的困难。我们表明，在泡沫期间预测单变量非因果时间序列的极端值时，FNN优于其他网络。我们还考察了通过池化和修改后的损失函数对极端值点预测所带来的改进。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines the use of recurrent neural networks and their extensions for forecasting autoregressive causal-noncausal processes, characterized by local explosive patterns such as bubbles and spikes. We study the recurrent neural networks (RNN), long short-term memory networks (LSTM), gated recurrent unit (GRU), bidirectional LSTM, and also a simple feed-forward network (FNN) to reveal the difficulty of forecasting extreme values depending on the type of network and the length of the training set. We show that FNN outperforms other networks in predicting extreme values of a univariate noncausal time series during a bubble. We also examine the improvements of point predictions of extreme values resulting from pooling and a modified loss function.
