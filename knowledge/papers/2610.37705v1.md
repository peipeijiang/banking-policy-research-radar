<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

对话式AI智能体正在将财富管理转变为一个持续循环的过程，涵盖市场分析、资产配置、风险检查和执行，由少数基础模型（foundation models）为数百万客户运行。我们针对这一层面构建了一个多资产智能体基模型（agent-based model），依据实测的模型集中度、跨模型相关性、零售换手率以及33年真实股票回报数据进行校准，并探究该层面何时会放大市场下跌以及什么能够阻止这种放大。独立决策者的有效数量受限于该层面所服务的客户数量：在当今的消费者集中度下约为2.0。这种观点的一元化驱动了个股层面的拥挤交易，并设定了一个受损模型提供商的影响半径，但它并不会驱动全市场范围的清算。指数尾部损失随智能体采用率的平方根增长，在智能体架构下比在周期性机器人投顾架构下大1.47至1.90倍，且不随模型集中度上升而增长；同步化自动化（风险覆盖、回撤削减和注意力驱动的执行）才是驱动因素。一个由总流量、公开回报和冲击估计计算得出的脆弱性指数在样本外预测尾部清算（留出R^2为0.56），远优于集中度或相关性指标。去相关化自动化而非模型是有效的：分散覆盖参数、分阶段执行和参与断路器三者结合可将尾部清算减少63%，且对客户福利没有可测量的成本，而提供商多元化则没有效果。由于所需的统计量是求和量，它们可以在安全聚合（secure aggregation）下跨平台计算，并配合差分隐私（differential privacy），对于现实客户群体的成本可忽略不计。在历史回放中，一个持有10%市场份额的智能体层面会使2020年崩盘加深约858个基点。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Conversational AI agents are turning wealth management into a continuous loop of market analysis, allocation, risk checks and execution, run for millions of clients on a handful of foundation models. We build a multi-asset agent-based model of this layer, calibrated to measured model concentration, cross-model correlation, retail turnover and 33 years of real equity returns, and ask when the layer amplifies market declines and what prevents it. The effective number of independent decision-makers is bounded however many clients the layer serves: about 2.0 at today's consumer concentration. This monoculture of views drives crowding in individual securities and sets the blast radius of a compromised model provider, but it does not drive market-wide liquidation. Index tail losses rise with the square root of agentic adoption, are 1.47-1.90 times larger under an agentic than under a periodic robo-advice architecture, and do not rise with model concentration; synchronized automation (risk overlays, drawdown cuts and attention-driven execution) drives them. A fragility index computed from aggregate flow, public returns and an impact estimate forecasts tail liquidation out of sample (hold-out R^2 of 0.56) far better than concentration or correlation measures. Decorrelating the automation, not the models, works: dispersing overlay parameters, staging execution and a participation breaker together cut tail liquidation by 63% at no measurable cost to client welfare, whereas provider diversification has no effect. Because the statistics required are sums, they can be computed across platforms under secure aggregation with differential privacy at negligible cost for realistic client populations. In historical replays, an agentic layer holding 10% of the market would have deepened the 2020 crash by about 858 basis points.
