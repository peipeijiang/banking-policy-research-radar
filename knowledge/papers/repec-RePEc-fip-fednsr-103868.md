<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

相对于消费信贷中常见的持续拖欠现象，新冠疫情期间的住房抵押贷款延期还款（forbearance）却出乎意料地短暂。为理解其中原因，我们构建了一个丰富的结构模型，并在信贷面板微观数据上进行估计。随后，利用该模型和调查数据，我们识别出两个关键因素的作用：一是相对有耐心的家庭在抵押贷款中的自选择效应，二是预期收入损失未能实际发生。即使将自选择纳入考量，若收入损失确实发生，延期还款的参与本将是持续性的。因此，延期还款作为一种常见的宏观经济政策工具，其效力取决于借款人的自选择、冲击的实现情况以及在这些冲击实现之后预期的修正。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Relative to the persistent delinquency typically observed in consumer credit, mortgage forbearance during the COVID-19 pandemic was surprisingly short-lived. To understand why, we develop a rich structural model and estimate it on credit panel microdata. Next, using the model and survey data, we identify key roles for both selection into mortgages by relatively patient households and the failure of anticipated income losses to materialize. Even taking selection in account, had losses occurred, forbearance participation would have been persistent. Thus, the power of forbearance, a frequent macroeconomic policy, hinges on borrower selection, shock realizations, and revisions to expectations following those realizations.
