<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

针对不完美工具的敏感性分析通常以线性两阶段最小二乘系数为目标，然而许多应用具有二值结果和概率尺度上的目标参数。我们将“看似外生”框架扩展到控制函数Probit模型，以平均偏效应和交互效应为目标。我们推导了生成回归量的方差、并集区间和局部到零区间，以及在所述模型和校准条件下的渐近覆盖率。在错误的潜变量尺度上陈述的违背会以由内生性相关系数决定的因子使区间产生误校准。对天主教学校教育、兄弟姐妹性别构成和儒家宗族的应用比较了Probit和线性分析。分析附带Stata和R实现。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Sensitivity analysis for imperfect instruments usually targets a linear two-stage least squares coefficient, yet many applications have binary outcomes and probability-scale targets. We extend the plausibly exogenous framework to the control-function probit, targeting average partial and interaction effects. We derive the generated-regressor variance, union and local-to-zero intervals, and their asymptotic coverage under stated model and calibration conditions. A violation stated on the wrong latent scale can miscalibrate the interval by a factor set by the endogeneity correlation. Applications to Catholic schooling, sibling sex composition and Confucian clans compare probit and linear analyses. Stata and R implementations accompany the analysis.
