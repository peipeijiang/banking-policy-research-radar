<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

利他主义能否成为有效制度设计的行为基础？我们研究了伯奇均衡下的完全实施问题，这一解概念将策略性利他主义形式化：每个参与者的对手们共同最大化她的收益。尽管伯奇均衡解决了囚徒困境，并能消除社会困境中的合作失败，但我们表明，这种乐观主义在从个体行为转向制度设计时无法存续。首先，我们证明弱帕累托最优规则在伯奇均衡下不可实施。其次，在无限制的严格偏好域上，任何既满足弱帕累托效率又能在伯奇均衡下实施的社会选择规则都必须是独裁性的。最后，我们表明伯奇可实施性严格强于纳什可实施性：在策略性利他主义下可实现的所有社会目标，在自利行为下同样可实现，但反之则不然。正是使伯奇均衡在行为层面具有吸引力的特征——即对手们保护每个参与者的收益——恰恰在设计层面排除了高效、非独裁的制度。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Can altruism serve as the behavioral foundation for efficient institutional design? We study full implementation in Berge equilibrium, the solution concept that formalizes strategic altruism: each agent's opponents collectively maximize her payoff. While Berge equilibrium resolves the Prisoner's Dilemma and can eliminate cooperation failures in social dilemmas, we show that this optimism does not survive the move from individual behavior to institutional design. First, we show that the weak Pareto optimal rule is not implementable in Berge equilibrium. Second, on the unrestricted domain of strict preferences, any social choice rule that is both weakly Pareto efficient and implementable in Berge equilibrium must be dictatorial. Finally, we show that Berge implementability is strictly more demanding than Nash implementability: every social goal achievable under strategic altruism is also achievable under self-interest, but not vice versa. The very feature that makes Berge equilibrium appealing at the behavioral level, that is, opponents protecting each agent's payoff, is precisely what forecloses efficient, non-dictatorial institutions at the design level.
