本版提供了对V. N. Zasukhin 1941年短文《论多维平稳随机过程理论》的英文翻译与评注，该文原发表于《苏联科学院报告》，33(7-8)，435-437。该文陈述了Wold分解的多变量推广：分解为奇异分量与正则分量的正交分解、正则分量基于标准正交创新序列的单边移动平均表示，以及满秩正则性的谱准则。后者将谱函数的绝对连续性与谱密度矩阵行列式的正性及对数可积性相结合。Whittle（1953）明确将多变量推广归功于Zasukhin。原文结果未附详细证明。另有一篇编辑评注讨论了这些结果的历史接受情况并解释了术语。文中附有重新排版的俄文转写以供对照。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This edition provides an English translation and commentary on V. N. Zasukhin's 1941 note "On the Theory of Multidimensional Stationary Random Processes," originally published in Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR, 33(7-8), 435-437. The note states a multivariate extension of Wold's decomposition: an orthogonal decomposition into singular and regular components, a one-sided moving-average representation of the regular component in terms of orthonormal innovation sequences, and a spectral criterion for regularity of full rank. The latter combines absolute continuity of the spectral functions with positivity and logarithmic integrability of the determinant of the spectral density matrix. Whittle (1953) explicitly credited Zasukhin with the multivariate generalization. The original results are presented without detailed proofs. A separate editorial note discusses their historical reception and explains the terminology. A retypeset Russian transcription is included for comparison.
