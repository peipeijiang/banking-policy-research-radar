# Random Genetic Variation Links Personality to Earnings and Wealth

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06088v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用家庭内随机遗传变异作为识别策略，结合荷兰Lifelines多基因指数与19年行政税务记录，发现情绪稳定性遗传倾向提高收入与家庭财富、尽责性遗传倾向提高家庭财富，从而为人格对经济结果的因果影响提供证据。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人格与经济学结果相关，但这些关系是否具有因果性仍不清楚。我们利用由父母随机遗传给子代所产生的大五人格遗传倾向在家庭内部的变异。将Lifelines队列研究的多基因指数与荷兰19年的行政税务记录相结合，我们发现，情绪稳定性遗传倾向越高，收入和家庭财富越高，而尽责性遗传倾向则提高家庭财富。其他三个人格特质在各项结果和设定中未显示出一致模式。随着我们逐步控制教育 attainment、认知表现和抑郁的遗传倾向，情绪稳定性遗传倾向对收入的影响仍然为正。这些发现强化了人格对收入和财富具有因果影响的证据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Personality is associated with economic outcomes, but whether these relationships are causal remains unclear. We exploit within-family variation in genetic propensity toward the Big Five generated by random genetic transmission from parents to offspring. Combining polygenic indexes from the Lifelines cohort study with 19 years of administrative tax records in the Netherlands, we find that greater genetic propensity toward emotional stability increases earnings and household wealth, while genetic propensity toward conscientiousness increases household wealth. The other three traits show no consistent pattern across outcomes and specifications. The effect of genetic propensity toward emotional stability on earnings remains positive as we progressively account for genetic propensity toward educational attainment, cognitive performance, and depression. These findings strengthen the evidence that personality causally affects earnings and wealth.
