# The maximum concentration of results in incomplete round-robin tournaments

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01750v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文用图论方法研究不完全循环赛（赛程由正则非完全无向图表示）中HHI最大值的下界与上界：下界在独立循环赛分组时达到，上界在二部图赛程时达到，并证明即使对现实赛制（分档、每档固定场次）最大化HHI在计算上也很困难，且复杂度不依赖计分制或每档对手数。

赫芬达尔—赫希曼指数（Herfindahl-Hirschman Index, HHI）是衡量市场集中度的标准指标，被广泛用于量化体育赛事中的竞争平衡。为了能够进行跨时间或跨联赛的比较，其最小值和最大值都应当是已知的。最小值通常是平凡的。本文给出了不完全循环赛（其中比赛集合由一个正则但非完全的无向图表示）中最大HHI的相关结果。我们证明了：当赛事由相互独立的循环赛小组构成时，最大HHI的下界可以达到；而当比赛集合由二部图表示时，最大HHI的上界可以达到。即使对于与现实赛事相对应的高度结构化图类型——其中球队被划分为若干档次（pot），并与来自每个档次的对手进行相同数量的比赛——最大化HHI在计算上也是困难的。该问题的复杂性不依赖于计分系统或来自某一档次的对手数量。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The Herfindahl--Hirschman Index (HHI), a standard measure of market concentration, is widely used to quantify competitive balance in sports tournaments. To enable comparisons over time or across leagues, both its minimum and maximum should be known. The minimum is usually trivial. Our paper provides results on the maximum HHI in incomplete round-robin tournaments, where the set of matches is represented by a regular but not complete undirected graph. We prove that the lower bound of maximum HHI is reached when the tournament consists of independent round-robin groups, while the upper bound of maximum HHI is reached when the set of matches is represented by a bipartite graph. Maximising HHI is computationally difficult even for highly structured graph types corresponding to real-world tournaments, where the teams are partitioned into pots and play the same number of matches against opponents from every pot. The complexity of the problem does not depend on the scoring system or the number of opponents from a pot.
