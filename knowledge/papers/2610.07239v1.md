<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

减缓路径要求化石燃料电厂在技术寿命结束前提前退役，然而退役决策通常源自成本最小化的系统模型，而非电厂所有者面临的决策。本研究考察了使关闭对运营商更为有利的碳价阈值，以及与持续排放相关的持续成本。本文针对欧洲850台机组构成的化石燃料机组群，逐厂求解有限期马尔可夫决策过程（Markov decision process），其中碳价随机变动，机组确定性老化。价格过程的构建使得扩大其范围时预期价格保持不变，从而将不确定性的影响与价格水平的影响分离。对于所考虑的所有燃料类型，关闭阈值均低于近期欧盟配额价格，且敏感区间远低于这些价格，因此进一步的价格上涨不太可能成为促使该机组群退役的约束性工具。由于决定碳价必须侵蚀多少利润空间的是燃料成本而非排放，碳密集度最低的产能最先退役。在预期价格不变的情况下，不确定性使阈值提高约十分之一：一个可信的稳定碳价所退役的产能，与一个高出约十分之一的波动碳价大致相同。因此，政策可信度可以替代价格水平。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Mitigation pathways require fossil power plants to retire well before the end of their technical lives, yet retirement decisions are typically derived from cost-minimizing system models rather than from the decision facing the plant's owner. This study investigates the carbon price threshold at which closure becomes more advantageous for an operator, as well as the ongoing costs associated with continued emissions. A finite-horizon Markov decision process is solved plant by plant for the European fossil fleet of 850 units, with a carbon price that moves stochastically and plants that age deterministically. The price process is constructed so that widening its range leaves the expected price unchanged, isolating the effect of uncertainty from the effect of price level. Closure thresholds fall below recent EU allowance prices for all fuels considered, and the sensitive range lies well beneath them, so further price increases are unlikely to be the binding instrument for retiring this fleet. Because fuel costs rather than emissions determine how much margin a carbon price must erode, the least carbon-intensive capacity retires first. Uncertainty raises the thresholds by about a tenth at an unchanged expected price: a credibly stable carbon price retires the same capacity as a volatile price roughly a tenth higher. Policy credibility therefore substitutes for price level.

</details>
