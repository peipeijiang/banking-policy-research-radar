# What a Shorter Contest Really Costs: Serial Dependence, Effective Trials, and Evidence from a Rule Change in Professional Esports

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05251v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文利用2023年9月职业Counter-Strike将每图常规回合从30减至24的规则变更，基于72,576张地图估计比赛试验的序列相关，提出以N/[1+(N-1)rho]计算有效试验数，发现缩短赛制对判别力的实际损失远小于独立假设下的模拟结果。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

运筹学通过赛制的效力来评估其优劣，即该设计识别出更强竞争者的概率，并且几乎完全通过仿真来计算这一概率，而仿真基于一个很少被检验的假设：赛制所聚合的各次试验是相互独立的。我们表明，这一假设会系统性地高估缩短比赛所付出的代价，并对这一修正进行了测量。当各次试验存在序列相关时，一个赛制所承载的有效试验次数为 N/[1+(N-1)rho]，而非 N，因此在独立性假设下，削减百分之二十的赛程会损失 10.6% 的区分能力，但在我们估计的相关性水平下仅损失 5.2%，而在一个六十轮试验的赛制中仅损失 2.1%。相关参数是从一次已实施的比赛长度变更中估计得到的：2023 年 9 月，职业《反恐精英》（Counter-Strike）将每张地图的常规回合数从三十回合削减至二十四回合。在 72,576 张地图中，我们将区分能力测量为地图前半段的残差标准差上的能力斜率，该半段比赛由规则固定为十五或十二回合，且无终止规则。相对于校准基准值 0.948，在赛制切换前后十个月的窗口内，区分能力下降了 35%（0.648，95% 区间 0.52-0.81），而在整个面板数据上下降了 10%（0.901，0.82-0.99）。该修正只需一个数值，可从普通比赛结果数据中作为设计效应（design effect）恢复得到。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Operational research evaluates tournament formats by their efficacy, the probability that the design identifies the stronger competitor, and computes it almost exclusively by simulation under an assumption that is rarely tested: that the trials a format aggregates are independent. We show that this assumption systematically overstates what shortening a contest costs, and we measure the correction. When trials are serially correlated, a format carries N/[1+(N-1)rho] effective trials rather than N, so a twenty per cent cut costs 10.6 per cent of discriminating power under independence but only 5.2 per cent at the correlation we estimate, and only 2.1 per cent in a sixty-trial format. The ingredients are estimated from an implemented change in contest length: in September 2023 professional Counter-Strike cut regulation rounds per map from thirty to twenty-four. Across 72,576 maps we measure discrimination as the ability slope over the residual standard deviation on the first half of a map, a contest fixed by rule at fifteen or twelve rounds with no stopping rule. Against the calibrated benchmark of 0.948, discrimination falls by thirty-five per cent in a ten-month window around the switch (0.648, 95 per cent interval 0.52-0.81) and by ten per cent over the full panel (0.901, 0.82-0.99). The correction needs one number, recoverable from ordinary results data as a design effect.

</details>
