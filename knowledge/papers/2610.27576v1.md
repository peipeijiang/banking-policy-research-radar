# Who Gains When the Umpire Is a Machine? Evidence from the Automated Ball-Strike System (ABS)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

2024年，韩国棒球组织（Korea Baseball Organization）成为首个用自动化系统取代本垒裁判好坏球判定的顶级联赛。利用2021年至2026年间超过一百万次投球数据，我们将这一变化分解为人类自由裁量权的移除与新好球区几何形状的施加两个部分。机器消除了依赖球数的判罚、捕手接球 framing（偷好球）的价值以及明星打者的小幅优势，这些租值每球员赛季仅值几分。由于机器的好球区随身高缩放且对所有对局一视同仁，它将好球重新分配给了高个子打者并在不同对局间重新分配，但暴露于这些变化的投球很少，且其对打席结果的影响被精确估计并接近于零。投手通过投更高的球迅速重新优化，左投手追上了已有的趋势，而我们未发现打者做出调整的证据。结果表明，以规则取代人类判断的影响取决于其几何形状和暴露程度。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In 2024 the Korea Baseball Organization became the first top-tier league to replace the home-plate umpires ball-strike judgment with an automated system. Using over a million pitches from 2021 to 2026, we separate the change into the removal of human discretion and the imposition of a new zone geometry. The machine eliminated count-dependent calls, the value of catcher framing and a small advantage for star batters, rents worth only a few runs per player-season. Because the machines zone scales with height and treats every matchup alike, it redistributed strikes toward tall batters and across matchups, but few pitches are exposed to these changes, and their effects on plate-appearance outcomes are precisely estimated and close to zero. Pitchers re-optimized quickly by throwing higher, with left-handers catching up to an existing trend, whereas we find no evidence that batters adjusted. The results suggest that the incidence of replacing human judgment with a rule depends on its geometry and on exposure.
