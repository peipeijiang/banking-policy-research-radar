<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在对称风险暴露情境下的公平资源配置，直觉上是可取的，在公共规划中有时甚至是人们所期望的。然而，公共规划者通常也关注缓解风险，更一般地说，还关注缓解不确定性与模糊性。我们研究公共风险配置问题中公平与模糊性规避之间的一个基本冲突。我们的主要结果是在一般对称总损失（其个体损失之间存在相依性不确定性）的框架下表述并建立的。具体到Keeney（1980）的经典模型，我们证明公平性的增加等价于总模糊性的增加。随后，我们通过$α$-maxmin偏好框架给出公平性与模糊性规避的定量刻画，建立各项准则成立所对应的模糊性与风险态度条件。具体而言，公平性对应于模糊性或风险偏好，而模糊性规避对应于模糊性或风险厌恶。该分析可推广到一般类别的对称聚合函数，我们在此表明模糊性态度是偏好的主要决定因素，尽管聚合结构与风险态度也发挥重要作用。数值算例验证了我们的理论发现，并探讨了公平—模糊性权衡。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Equitable resource allocations in the context of symmetric risk exposures are intuitively desirable and sometimes even expected in public planning. However, public planners generally are also concerned with mitigating risk and, more generally, uncertainty and ambiguity. We study a fundamental conflict between equity and ambiguity avoidance in public risk allocation problems. Our main results are framed and established in the setting of general symmetric aggregate losses equipped with dependence uncertainty among individual losses. Specializing to the classical model of Keeney (1980), we prove that an increase in equity is equivalent to an increase in aggregate ambiguity. We then provide a quantitative characterization of equity and ambiguity avoidance through the $α$-maxmin preference framework, establishing conditions on ambiguity and risk attitudes under which each criterion holds. Specifically, equity corresponds to ambiguity or risk proneness, while ambiguity avoidance corresponds to ambiguity or risk aversion. The analysis extends to general classes of symmetric aggregation functions, where we show that ambiguity attitude is the primary determinant of preference, though aggregation structure and risk attitude also play important roles. Numerical illustrations validate our theoretical findings and explore the equity-ambiguity tradeoff.

</details>
