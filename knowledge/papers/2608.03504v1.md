<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们通过扩展Luce（1959）（或多项式Logit）模型，研究广告产品（如“精选推荐”“推荐”“特色”）如何影响数字平台和零售界面上的消费者选择。消费者要么专注于广告商品，要么考虑完整菜单，然后根据Luce/Logit规则在考虑中的备选项中进行选择。我们对该模型进行了刻画，并表明其基本要素可以从选择数据中唯一识别。我们还研究了一个具有管理重要性的广告设计问题，即平台或零售商选择广告子集以最大化预期利润，并推导出可实施的设计规则。随后，我们引入了一个更丰富的框架，其中广告既能影响注意力，也能影响偏好。对于这一更一般的模型，我们提供了特征刻画，并展示了如何利用选择数据将注意力效应与偏好效应分离开来。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how advertised products (e.g., Top Picks, Recommended, Featured) affect consumer choice on digital platforms and retail interfaces by extending the Luce (1959) (or multinomial logit) model. A consumer either focuses on the advertised items or considers the full menu, then chooses among the considered alternatives according to the Luce/logit rule. We characterize this model and show that its underlying primitives are uniquely identified from choice data. We also study a managerially important advertisement-design problem, in which a platform or retailer chooses the advertised subset to maximize expected profit, and we derive implementable design rules. We then introduce a richer framework in which advertising can influence both attention and preference. For this more general model, we provide a characterization and show how choice data can be used to separate the attention effect from the preference effect.

</details>
