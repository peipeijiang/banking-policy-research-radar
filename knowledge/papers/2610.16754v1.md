<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

使用面板数据的事件研究被广泛用于估计处理效应。在重复截面数据中，未处理个体的未来处理日期是不可观测的。伪面板事件研究利用匹配的未处理个体来插补处理组的处理前结果。本文在此类设计中建立了识别，并刻画了当识别条件不满足时的偏差。我证明伪面板回归等价于使用组均值的加权最小二乘法。这一表示简化了计算并给出了解析推断。在模拟中，解析置信区间达到接近名义水平的覆盖率，而传统区间则严重覆盖不足。我还开发了一种使用更丰富协变量的双重稳健估计量、一种带有合成类型固定效应的对称匹配设计，以及一种能够容纳异质性处理效应的完全非参数估计量。在一项韩国面板调查中，伪面板回归得到的就业子女惩罚为42.1%，接近实际面板中的46.9%。应用于重复截面数据得到的就业惩罚约为三分之一。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Event studies with panel data are widely used to estimate treatment effects. With repeated cross-sections, future treatment dates are unobserved for untreated individuals. Pseudo-panel event studies impute pre-treatment outcomes of the treated using matched untreated individuals. This paper establishes identification in such designs and characterizes bias when identification conditions fail. I show that the pseudo-panel regression is equivalent to weighted least squares with group means. This representation simplifies computation and yields analytical inference. In simulations, analytical confidence intervals attain near-nominal coverage, whereas conventional intervals substantially undercover. I also develop a doubly robust estimator using richer covariates, a symmetric matching design with synthetic type fixed effects, and a fully nonparametric estimator that accommodates heterogeneous treatment effects. In a Korean panel survey, the pseudo-panel regression yields an employment child penalty of 42.1%, close to 46.9% in the actual panel. Applications to repeated cross-sections yield employment penalties of about one-third.
