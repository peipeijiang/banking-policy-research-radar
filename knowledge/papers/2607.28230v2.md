<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

观察到的风险承担行为通常通过期望效用曲率来合理化，然而，在一种情境下拟合选择所需的曲率可能与在另一种情境下所需的曲率存在显著差异，这一张力由对期望效用理论的校准批评所凸显。有限乘法系统在达到较低延续阈值时往往停止演化，而标准增长最优基准则假设不间断延续。我们研究了一个有限期界二元乘法过程，其中事前选择固定敞口，穿越吸收边界的路径被赋予残值。精确格点传播得出最优敞口，其为到边界的初始对数距离、期界和残值比率的函数。代价高昂的吸收将敞口压缩至边界附近无边界凯利分数之下。当通过无约束常数相对风险厌恶基准进行解释时，这种压缩表现为升高的风险厌恶。当残值接近边界时，可能出现局部的超凯利反转。因此，在这一最小有限期界设定中，吸收边界几何结构足以生成状态依赖的看似风险厌恶的行为，而无需异质原始偏好参数。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Observed risk-taking behavior is often rationalized through expected-utility curvature, yet the curvature required to fit choices in one context can differ sharply from the curvature required in another, a tension highlighted by calibration critiques of expected-utility theory. Finite multiplicative systems often cease to evolve when a lower continuation threshold is reached, whereas standard growth-optimal benchmarks assume uninterrupted continuation. We study a finite-horizon binary multiplicative process in which a fixed exposure is chosen ex ante and paths crossing an absorbing boundary are assigned a residual value. Exact lattice propagation yields the optimal exposure as a function of initial log distance to the boundary, horizon, and residual ratio. Costly absorption compresses exposure below the no-boundary Kelly fraction near the boundary. When interpreted through an unconstrained constant-relative-risk-aversion benchmark, this compression appears as elevated risk aversion. As the residual value approaches the boundary, a local above-Kelly reversal can occur. In this minimal finite-horizon setting, absorbing-boundary geometry is therefore sufficient to generate state-dependent risk-averse-looking behavior without heterogeneous primitive preference parameters.
