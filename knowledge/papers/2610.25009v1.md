<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考虑对完全网络（complete network）中有序对观测到的有向二元数据（directed dyadic data）进行非参数分位数回归，该网络包含 n 个顶点。观测数据表现出二元相依性（dyadic dependence）：索引不相交的二元组相互独立，而共享一个或两个索引的二元组则相关。采用局部线性平滑技术（local linear smoothing technique）估计分位数回归函数。渐近理论表明，所提出的二元分位数回归估计的收敛速率关键取决于潜在网络生成过程是否发生退化（degeneracy）。在一些温和条件下，推导了极限分布的速率自适应标准化版本（rate-adaptive standardized version）。此外，提出了一种易于实现的方法来估计非参数二元分位数回归估计的渐近方差。通过蒙特卡罗模拟（Monte-Carlo simulation）以及应用于双边投资组合持有（bilateral portfolio holdings）的实际数据，说明了所提方法的有限样本性能和适用性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper considers nonparametric quantile regression for directed dyadic data observed on the ordered pairs of a complete network with n vertices. The observations exhibit dyadic dependence: dyads with disjoint indices are independent, whereas dyads sharing one or two indices are correlated. The local linear smoothing technique is adopted to estimate the quantile regression function. The asymptotic theory reveals that convergence rates of the proposed dyadic quantile regression estimation crucially depend on whether degeneracy occurs for the underlying network generating process. A rate-adaptive standardized version of the limit distribution is derived under some mild conditions. In addition, an easy-to-implement method is proposed to estimate the asymptotic variance of nonparametric dyadic quantile regression estimation. The finite-sample performance and applicability of the proposed methodology are illustrated with Monte-Carlo simulation and a real data application to bilateral portfolio holdings.
