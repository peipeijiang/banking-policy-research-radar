<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

为低收入和中低收入国家（LICs和LMICs）采购儿科疫苗，需要在异质性疫苗市场、相互竞争的利益相关方目标以及不确定需求之间进行长期协调，然而目前招标仍以一系列独立续约的方式安排。我们扩展了假设协调疫苗市场（HCVM）框架，以安排具有灵活期限和全量数量折扣的多年期招标，并比较了三个目标：平衡协调实体模型（UG）、最小化错过剂次的社会效益模型（SB）以及生产者利润最大化模型（MP）。利用联合国儿童基金会十年需求预测以及六种折扣情景下的30次需求重复模拟，我们发现，采购目标而非需求变异性，驱动了招标成本和进度复杂性。数量折扣有利于大型生产商，导致市场集中并增加供应链脆弱性。折扣效果取决于市场成熟度而非价数：五价疫苗和六价疫苗已接近其价格下限，在单价疫苗中只有轮状病毒疫苗对折扣有反应，而HPV和PCV疫苗仍然过于昂贵，难以参与竞争。将五价疫苗和六价疫苗从采购池中移除会急剧增加错过剂次。这些结果为规划者提供了关于目标选择、折扣设计以及保护联合疫苗可及性的实用指导。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Pediatric vaccine procurement for low- and lower-middle-income countries (LICs and LMICs) requires long-term coordination across heterogeneous vaccine markets, competing stakeholder objectives, and uncertain demand, yet tenders are currently scheduled as a series of independent renewals. We extend the Hypothetically Coordinated Vaccine Market (HCVM) framework to schedule multi-year tenders with flexible lengths and all-unit quantity discounts, and compare three objectives: a balanced coordinatingentity model (UG), a social-benefit model that minimizes missed doses (SB), and a producer profit-maximizing model (MP). Using UNICEF ten-year demand forecasts and 30 demand replications across six discount scenarios, we find that the procurement objective, not demand variability, drives cost-to-tender and schedule complexity. Quantity discounts favor large producers, concentrating the market and increasing supply chain vulnerability. Discount effectiveness depends on market maturity rather than valency: pentavalent and hexavalent vaccines are already near their price floor, and among monovalent vaccines only rotavirus responds to discounts, whereas HPV and PCV remain too expensive to compete. Removing pentavalent and hexavalent vaccines from the procurement pool sharply increases missed doses. These results give planners practical guidance on objective selection, discount design, and protecting access to combination vaccines.

</details>
