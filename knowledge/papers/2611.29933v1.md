# Coarse Price Discrimination

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29933v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了当具有独立估值的异质性买家竞争稀缺库存时，将卖家限制为\(L\)个标价（posted prices）所带来的收入成本。我们的大市场假设要求移除任何买家对最优收入的影响都很小。我们首先在每个固定库存下使买家可任意替换，然后让库存增长。在此极限下，一个价格能保证最优机制收入的一半，两个价格能保证\(e/(e+1)\)，约为\(73.1\%\)。对于\(L\ge3\)，相应的保证比例为\(1-1/(2L-1)\)。这些保证适应买家之间需求的任意差异，且不需要为每个客户群体单独定价。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the revenue cost of limiting a seller to $L$ posted prices when heterogeneous buyers with independent values compete for scarce inventory. Our large-market assumption requires that removing any buyer have little effect on optimal revenue. We first make buyers arbitrarily replaceable at each fixed stock and then let stock grow. In this limit, one price guarantees half of optimal mechanism revenue and two guarantee $e/(e+1)$, approximately $73.1\%$. For $L\ge3$, the corresponding guarantee is a $1-1/(2L-1)$ fraction. The guarantees accommodate arbitrary differences in demand across buyers and do not require a separate price for each customer group.

</details>
