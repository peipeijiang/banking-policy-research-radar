<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们将市场稳定储备的设计表述为一个平稳平均场奇异控制问题。配额余额遵循机制转换扩散过程，而监管机构通过有界变差控制注入或撤回配额。目标函数在平均配额余额、其平稳离散度、对长期目标的偏离以及干预成本之间进行权衡。最优政策是一种依赖于机制的带状规则，由四个反射阈值刻画。其计算归结为一个标量不动点问题，该问题将耦合变分不等式与反射过程的不变分布相结合。对欧盟排放交易体系（EU ETS）的校准得到的阈值与现有市场稳定储备规则处于同一数量级。比较静态分析表明，波动性是干预区间的主要决定因素，而危机机制的频率和持续时间影响有限。基于平均机制动态的更简单的双阈值政策在流动性和干预量方面 closely 再现了最优的机制依赖政策。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We formulate the design of a market stability reserve as a stationary mean-field singular control problem. The allowance balance follows a regime-switching diffusion, while the regulator injects or withdraws allowances through bounded-variation controls. The objective trades off the average allowance balance, its stationary dispersion, deviations from a long-run target, and intervention costs. The optimal policy is a regime-dependent band rule characterized by four reflecting thresholds. Its computation reduces to a scalar fixed-point problem combining a coupled variational inequality with the invariant distribution of the reflected process. A calibration to the EU ETS yields thresholds of the same order of magnitude as the existing Market Stability Reserve rule. Comparative statics show that volatility is the main determinant of the intervention band, whereas the frequency and duration of crisis regimes have limited effects. A simpler two-threshold policy based on average regime dynamics closely reproduces the optimal regime-dependent policy in terms of liquidity and intervention volumes.
