# An analytical model to explain under which conditions home batteries create new load peaks in the local grid

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01286v1)

## 一句话结论

> 本文构建解析模型，解释在何种条件下价格响应的家庭电池会在本地电网中造成新负荷峰值，并推导出峰值大小的闭式解，发现内生电价下多数峰值消失，且局部充电峰值取决于电网在全国灵活性中的份额、边际梯度与全国灵活性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

模拟研究发现，价格响应型家用电池和电动汽车会在低压电网中产生新的负荷峰值；而在内生批发电价下，这些峰值大部分会消失。本文通过一个关于变压器处同时峰值的解析模型来解释这两项发现。该模型为代数模型并以闭式解求解，因此可以识别出决定新峰值是否出现及其大小的参数；这些参数数量很少。峰值等于关键小时的背景负荷加上本地车队的充电量。在外生价格下，充电量等于车队的额定功率。在内生价格下，全国车队填充剩余负荷的谷底直至价格持平；填充深度由能量平衡决定，其中价格函数不出现。对于V形谷底，本地充电峰值等于电网在全国灵活性中的份额乘以侧翼梯度（flank gradient）的平方根再乘以全国灵活性。额定功率、价格水平、网络费用和市场规模均不进入模型。该模型在德国案例中再现了模拟峰值，误差在几个百分点以内。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Simulation studies find that price-responsive home batteries and electric vehicles create new load peaks in low-voltage grids; with endogenous wholesale prices most of these peaks disappear. This paper explains both findings with an analytical model of the coincident peak at the transformer. The model is algebraic and solved in closed form, so the parameters that determine whether a new peak emerges and how large it becomes can be identified; they are few. The peak is the background load in the critical hour plus the charging of the local fleet. Under exogenous prices the charging equals the rated power of the fleet. Under endogenous prices the national fleet fills the trough of residual load until the price is flat; the depth of the fill follows from an energy balance in which the price function does not appear. For a V-shaped trough the local charging peak is the share of the grid in national flexibility times the square root of flank gradient times national flexibility. Rated power, price level, network charge and market size do not enter. The model reproduces simulated peaks for a German case within a few percent.

</details>
