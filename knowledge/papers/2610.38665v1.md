<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究带有可加个体固定效应的静态面板数据模型中的分位数回归（QR）和工具变量分位数回归（QIV）。我们推导了当面板两个维度都趋于无穷时固定效应估计量的渐近分布，表明其具有阶为1/T的渐近偏差，其中T为每个个体的观测数。偏差分析依赖于将剖面得分（profiled score）刻画为诱导次序统计量（concomitants）的加权和这一新颖特征，从而得到得分偏差的精确有限T表达式。我们提出Jackknife偏差校正，并揭示了非光滑分位数目标函数所特有的偏差-方差权衡。标准的逐一删除Jackknife无法消除主导偏差，即使经过修改以实现偏差校正，也会使方差膨胀，从而只能达到$\sqrt{N}$-一致性，其中N为个体数。我们提出适用于一般子样本大小的修改Jackknife偏差校正，推导所得估计量的渐近分布，并证明在适当选择子样本大小时可实现$\sqrt{NT}$-一致性和有效推断。作为实证应用，我们使用学校固定效应研究了年龄对学龄儿童考试成绩的影响。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study quantile regression (QR) and instrumental variable quantile regression (QIV) in static panel data models with additive individual fixed effects. We derive the asymptotic distribution of the fixed effects estimator as both panel dimensions grow large, showing that it has an asymptotic bias of order 1/T, where T is the number of observations per individual. The bias analysis relies on a novel characterization of the profiled score as a weighted sum of induced order statistics (concomitants), which yields an exact finite-T expression for the score bias. We propose Jackknife bias corrections and uncover a bias-variance tradeoff that is specific to the non-smooth quantile objective functions. The standard delete-one Jackknife fails to eliminate the leading bias and, even when modified to achieve bias correction, inflates the variance so that only $\sqrt{N}$-consistency is attained, where N is the number of individuals. We propose a modified Jackknife bias correction for general subsample sizes, derive the asymptotic distribution of the resulting estimator, and show that $\sqrt{NT}$-consistency and valid inference are achieved for suitable choices of the subsample size. As an empirical application, we study the effect of age on school children's test scores using school fixed effects.
