<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了带有信用违约互换（credit default swaps, CDS）的金融网络中的近似清算问题。对于弱概念（W）和（I），我们证明了在约 $0.2530$ 的阈值以下具有 PPAD-困难性，并在约 $0.2834$ 的阈值以上给出了多项式时间区间算法。在 CDS 卖方事前偿付能力充足的网络上，相应的界分别为 $(13-\sqrt{153})/4$ 和 $3-2\sqrt{2}$，约等于 $0.1577$ 和 $0.1716$。这些阈值对于有界半径算法是紧的：对于卖方偿付能力充足网络上的（I），以及一般情形下的（I）和（W）。在非退化网络上，（W）和（I）具有相同的困难性和可处理性阈值端点。对于精确偿付能力概念（D），在非退化网络上，对每个有理误差 $\varepsilon<1$ 均成立 PPAD-困难性。在一个受限的单位名义金额类上，设 $d$ 为核心银行持有的 CDS 合约的最大数量（按重数计）。对于有理数 $\varepsilon\in[1/2,1)$，记 $ρ=(1+\varepsilon)/(1-\varepsilon)$，当 $d<(\lceilρ\rceil+1)ρ$ 时该问题可在多项式时间内求解，否则为 PPAD-完全。在 $\varepsilon=1/3$ 时，边界为 $d=5$ 对 $d=6$；在 $\varepsilon=1/9$ 以下，度 $2$ 已经是 PPAD-完全的，而度 $1$ 是可精确求解的。这些度结果针对（D），并不关闭（W）/（I）的近似窗口。我们还建立了一个 Pure-Circuit 归约的障碍，该障碍由所述接口和辅助银行限制下的区间传播所认证。一个有限精度区间算法将卖方偿付能力充足的（I）归约为近停滞盒子中的点选择问题；当残差矩阵在每个强连通分量上是对称或单调时，选择问题是多项式时间可解的。一般情形和卖方偿付能力充足情形的近似窗口仍然开放。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study approximate clearing in financial networks with credit default swaps. For weak notions (W) and (I), we prove PPAD-hardness below a threshold approximately $0.2530$ and give a polynomial-time interval algorithm above a threshold approximately $0.2834$. On networks whose CDS writers are solvent ex ante, the corresponding bounds are $(13-\sqrt{153})/4$ and $3-2\sqrt{2}$, approximately $0.1577$ and $0.1716$. The thresholds are sharp for bounded-radius algorithms: for (I) on writer-solvent networks, and for (I) and (W) in general. On non-degenerate networks, (W) and (I) have identical hardness and tractability threshold endpoints. For exact-solvency notion (D), PPAD-hardness holds at every rational error $\varepsilon<1$ on non-degenerate networks. On a restricted unit-notional class, let $d$ be the maximum number of CDS contracts held by a core bank, counted with multiplicity. For rational $\varepsilon\in[1/2,1)$, writing $ρ=(1+\varepsilon)/(1-\varepsilon)$, the problem is polynomial-time solvable when $d<(\lceilρ\rceil+1)ρ$ and PPAD-complete otherwise. At $\varepsilon=1/3$ the boundary is $d=5$ versus $d=6$; below $\varepsilon=1/9$, degree $2$ is already PPAD-complete, while degree $1$ is exactly solvable. These degree results concern (D) and do not close the (W)/(I) approximation windows. We also establish a barrier to Pure-Circuit reductions certified by interval propagation under stated interface and auxiliary-bank restrictions. A finite-precision interval algorithm reduces writer-solvent (I) to point selection in near-stalled boxes; selection is polynomial-time when the residual matrix is symmetric or monotone on each strongly connected component. The general and writer-solvent approximation windows remain open.
