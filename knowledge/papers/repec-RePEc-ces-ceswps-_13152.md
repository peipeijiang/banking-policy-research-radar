<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究中国进口渗透是否改变了美国地方劳动力市场的内部地理格局。利用2002-2020年精细的工作场所就业数据以及Autor等（2013）的工具变量设计，我发现中国进口渗透降低了通勤区内的就业集中度。进口暴露度的四分位距增加可以解释赫芬达尔-赫希曼指数（Herfindahl-Hirschman index）差异性下降的约15%。考察通勤区内各就业节点，我发现这一下降反映了初始占主导地位的节点就业份额的不成比例减少。这一模式与行业构成密切相关：初始制造业存在度更高的节点经历了更大的就业份额相对下降，而制造业存在度的差异解释了通勤区内这些响应中规模梯度的重要部分。研究结果记录了贸易冲击的一个空间调整边际，这是总体地方就业结果所无法捕捉的。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies whether Chinese import penetration changed the internal geography of U.S. local labor markets. Using granular workplace employment data for 2002-2020 and the instrumental-variable design of Autor et al. (2013), I find that Chinese import penetration reduced employment concentration within commuting zones. An interquartile increase in import exposure explains about 15% of the differential decline in the Herfindahl-Hirschman index. Looking across employment nodes within commuting zones, I find that this decline reflects a disproportionate reduction in the employment shares of initially dominant nodes. This pattern is closely linked to sectoral composition: nodes with greater initial manufacturing presence experienced larger relative declines in employment shares, and differences in manufacturing presence account for a substantial part of the size gradient in these within-CZ responses. The results document a spatial margin of adjustment to trade shocks that is not captured by aggregate local employment outcomes.

</details>
