# Alternative Insurance: Gig Work and Social Insurance Participation

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

灵活就业机会为劳动者提供了一种应对负面经济冲击的新型自我保障手段。利用Uber的逐步推广，我们采用双重差分法、交错处理时点方法以及调查实验，表明获得灵活就业安排的机会降低了劳动者参与社会保障残疾保险、补充保障收入及补充营养援助计划等公共援助项目的比例。在某种程度上，这种新型私人自我保障机制对传统社会保险产生了挤出效应。然而，在地方劳动力市场疲软的情况下，灵活就业机会对项目参与率的影响有所减弱，这意味着在应对总体性、宏观层面的经济风险时，零工工作并不能替代社会保障项目。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Flexible gig opportunities provide a new means for workers to self-insure against negative economic shocks. Exploiting the roll-out of Uber, we use difference-in-differences, staggered treatment timing methods, and a survey experiment to show that access to flexible gig arrangements reduces participation in public assistance programs like Social Security Disability Insurance, Supplemental Security Income, and Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program. To some extent, this new private self-insurance crowds-out traditional social insurance. However, the effect of gig opportunity access on program participation is attenuated in weak local labor markets, meaning gig work does not replace social programs when insuring against aggregate, macroeconomic risks.

</details>
