<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

偏好的凸性（convexity）是经济分析中一个普遍存在的假设，因此理解这一性质如何在数据中显现十分重要。本文提出了一种方法，在仅给定弱序和严格不完全序形式的偏好信息的情况下检验凸性。我们刻画了可扩展为凸偏好的偏好对，并提供了验证该性质的有效算法。要求凸偏好与满足独立性（如欧几里得序或随机占优）的序相一致，并不会使检验变得复杂。我们将结果应用于消费者需求、风险与不确定性下的选择以及信息偏好，并建立了同时获得上半连续性或连续性的条件。我们识别了凸性不施加超出理性之外额外限制的环境，将Afriat型结果推广到无限数据和余凸预算，并建立了精确理性化的凸版本。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Convexity of preferences is a pervasive assumption in economic analysis, so it is important to understand how this property is revealed in data. In this paper, we develop a method to test for convexity given partial preference information in the form of weak and strict incomplete orders. We characterize the pairs that can be extended to convex preferences and provide an efficient algorithm to verify this property. Requiring the convex preference to align with an order that satisfies independence, such as the Euclidean order or stochastic dominance, does not complicate the test. We apply our results to consumer demand, choice under risk and uncertainty, and preferences over information, and establish conditions under which upper semicontinuity or continuity can also be obtained. We identify environments in which convexity imposes no additional restrictions beyond rationality, extending Afriat-type results to infinite data and co-convex budgets and establishing a convex version of exact rationalization.
