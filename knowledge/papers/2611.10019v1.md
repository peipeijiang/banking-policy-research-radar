# Collateral Equilibrium with a Continuum of States and No Ex Post Survival Assumption

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10019v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文证明在单一商品和Geanakoplos-Zame抵押证券经济中，即使第二期禀赋为任意非负状态函数（可在任意状态集上为零），只要第一期禀赋为正，不完全市场均衡仍然存在，无需事后生存假设。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

不完备市场连续状态下的存在性结果要求事后收入非负，或每个状态下的禀赋有界远离零。此类条件排除了灾难状态，即某些代理人完全得不到任何收入的情形。我们证明，在单一商品和Geanakoplos与Zame意义上的抵押证券框架下，当第二期禀赋为状态的任意非负函数时，均衡存在：这些禀赋可以在任意状态集合上为零，包括所有状态。仅要求第一期禀赋为正；事后不施加任何生存假设。原因在于，无追索权抵押使每个投资组合在每个状态下都具有偿付能力，而在单一商品情形下，这正是极限论证所需的全部条件。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Existence results for incomplete markets with a continuum of states require ex post incomes to be nonnegative, or endowments to be bounded away from zero in every state. Such conditions rule out disaster states, in which some agents receive no income at all. We show that, with a single good and collateralized securities in the sense of Geanakoplos and Zame, an equilibrium exists when second-period endowments are arbitrary nonnegative functions of the state: they may vanish on any set of states, including all of them. Only first-period endowments are required to be positive; no survival assumption is imposed ex post. The reason is that non-recourse collateral makes every portfolio solvent in every state, and with one good this is all the limit argument needs.

</details>
