<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个框架，用于量化进口商品关税变化如何通过直接渠道和生产网络渠道影响部门及总体消费价格。利用加拿大的投入产出矩阵以及覆盖加拿大全部企业的行政层面企业-商品进口数据，我们估计了美国进口商品关税的价格弹性。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a framework to quantify how tariff changes on imported goods affect sectoral and aggregate consumption prices through direct and production-network channels. Using Canada's input-output matrix and administrative firm-good import data for the universe of Canadian firms, we estimate price elasticities for tariffs on U.S.-imported goods.
