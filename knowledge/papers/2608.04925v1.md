<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文以人寿保险行业为背景，探讨金融机构如何将利率风险传导至产品市场。我们从理论上证明，保险公司通过跨期限扭曲产品发行以对冲久期缺口是最优策略。我们考察了2008年金融危机后因可变年金负债而外部暴露于利率风险的保险公司。与我们的机制一致，暴露风险的保险公司形成了负久期缺口，提高了长期产品的加价率，并将发行转向短期产品以进行对冲。因此，2005年至2023年间，长期人寿保险覆盖面占GDP的比重下降了31%。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper explores how financial institutions pass interest rate risk through to product markets using the life insurance industry as a setting. We show theoretically that it is optimal for insurers to distort product issuance across maturities to offset duration gaps. We examine insurers exogenously exposed to interest rate risk through their variable annuity liabilities after the 2008 financial crisis. Consistent with our mechanism, exposed insurers developed negative duration gaps, increased markups on long-duration products, and shifted issuance toward shorter-duration products to hedge. As a result, long-term life insurance coverage declined by 31% of GDP between 2005 and 2023.
