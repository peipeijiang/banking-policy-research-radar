<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

道德倒置（moral inversion）是指公众就某种说法达成共识，而该说法错误地颠倒了伤害责任的归属。我们研究在一个序贯社会学习模型中，表达性收益（expressive payoffs）如何影响道德倒置的出现。较大的收益使某种说法更具吸引力从而被 endorse，但也使每一次对该说法的 endorse 成为对其更弱的支持证据。我们证明，在私人信念满足单峰性（single-peakedness）条件下，较大的收益既增加了对受偏好说法形成错误共识的概率，也降低了该共识的可靠性。在没有该条件的情况下，这两种比较都可能反转，且公共记录的信息量在 Blackwell 序下可以严格改善。最后，我们证明，表达性收益的足够大幅增加仍然恢复了基准比较静态结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Moral inversion is a public consensus on an account that falsely reverses responsibility for a harm. We study how expressive payoffs affect its emergence in a sequential social-learning model. A larger payoff makes an account more attractive to endorse, but also makes each endorsement of that account weaker evidence in its favor. We show that under a single-peakedness condition on private beliefs a larger payoff both increases the probability of an incorrect consensus on the favored account and reduces its reliability. Without this condition, both comparisons can reverse, and the informativeness of the public record can strictly improve in the Blackwell order. Finally, we show that a sufficiently large increase in the expressive payoff nevertheless restores the benchmark comparative statics.
