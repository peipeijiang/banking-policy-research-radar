<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一位委托人（principal）为多位关注职业发展的代理人（agents）设计评级，这些代理人的才能相互关联，评级将产出映射为任意有限方差的统计量。同伴产出既作为努力的基准，又传递才能信号，这使得在保密评级下完全披露并非最优。将贝叶斯合理性（Bayes plausibility）放松为线性正交性，可得到封闭形式的评级以及对一般先验下可计算的努力边界；在正态分布下，该放松是精确的。最优保密评级将完全披露工资与努力的Fisher得分相结合：当个体产出对才能具有信息含量时，评级表现为相对绩效评价；当个体产出噪声较大时，评级表现为团队-based评价。公开评级必须满足联合贝叶斯合理性，从而削弱激励；因此，委托人偏好保密评级，而代理人偏好公开评级。在正态分布下，谱分解刻画了公开评级的特征：较小的成本异质性意味着无论噪声结构如何都应完全披露，而较大的成本异质性或较强的努力溢出效应则可能引致部分披露以重新配置激励。上述结果对绩效评级和薪酬透明度具有启示意义。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A principal designs ratings for multiple career-concerned agents with correlated talents, mapping outputs into arbitrary finite-variance statistics. Peer output both benchmarks effort and signals talent, making full disclosure suboptimal under confidential ratings. Relaxing Bayes plausibility to linear orthogonality yields a closed-form rating and computable effort bounds for general priors; under normality, the relaxation is exact. Optimal confidential ratings combine the full-disclosure wage with the Fisher score for effort: they are relative-performance when individual output is informative about talent and team-based when it is noisy. Public ratings must satisfy joint Bayes plausibility, weakening incentives; hence, the principal prefers confidential ratings, whereas agents prefer public ratings. A spectral decomposition characterizes public ratings under normality: small cost heterogeneity implies full disclosure regardless of the noise structure, whereas large cost heterogeneity or strong effort spillovers can induce partial disclosure to reallocate incentives. The results bear on performance ratings and pay transparency.

</details>
