<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察外资并购是否以及如何改善职业安全。利用2003—2017年法国制造业工厂的关联行政数据，并在匹配样本上进行事件研究，我们发现外资并购降低了事故率和工作日损失。安全状况的改善与管理层级扩大、技术管理人员增加、外籍管理人员聘用以及工人培训同时出现。我们几乎没有发现支持自动化、任务重新分配或外包作为替代解释的证据。此外，国内并购并未产生可比拟的改善。我们的发现与外资并购通过管理和组织实践的转移来改善工作场所安全这一解释相一致，凸显了跨国企业活动在传统企业绩效衡量指标之外的益处。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines whether and how foreign acquisitions improve occupational safety. Using linked administrative data on French manufacturing plants over 2003-2017 and event studies on a matched sample, we find that foreign acquisitions reduce accident rates and workdays lost. Safety gains coincide with expanded managerial hierarchies, greater technical-manager presence, foreign manager hiring, and worker training. We find little evidence supporting automation, task reallocation, or outsourcing as alternative explanations. Moreover, domestic acquisitions do not generate comparable improvements. Our findings are consistent with foreign acquisitions improving workplace safety through the transfer of managerial and organisational practices, highlighting a benefit of multinational activity beyond conventional measures of firm performance.

</details>
