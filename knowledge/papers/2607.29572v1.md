<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人工智能依赖于一整套投入要素的堆叠：模型依赖算力，算力依赖芯片，芯片依赖电力和精炼矿物。本文基于可引用且可复现的数据，以赫芬达尔-赫希曼指数（HHI）为尺度，衡量每一层的集中度。研究发现有三点。第一，集中度呈现出清晰的梯度。在下游，集中度较为温和，公共与监管关注最为密集，模型使用和云服务的集中度低于美国监管机构视为“高度集中”的1，800分界线。在上游，集中度急剧上升，而公共讨论却寥寥无几。其中，先进封装得分8，100，领先光刻技术达到10，000的上限，且一个国家主导了若干关键矿物的生产或精炼，镓的集中度接近该上限。第二，这些上游环节是瓶颈，也是整个人工智能经济的战略脆弱点。反垄断法无法触及这些环节，因为它们掌握在外国或国家手中。应对这些瓶颈，只能依靠出口管制和国内产业政策。第三，将储备与精炼并列考察可以看出，这种集中是人为构建的，而非地质条件决定的，是产业政策可以改变的变量。上游投入品价格上涨几乎不会改变由其制成的最终产品的成本，因为该投入品仅占成本的一小部分。因此，瓶颈的威胁在于投入品本身的丧失，而非其价格上涨。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial intelligence depends on a stack of inputs, models on compute, compute on chips, and chips on electricity and refined minerals. This paper measures the concentration of each layer on one scale, the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI), from cited and reproducible data. Three findings follow. First, concentration forms a clear gradient. It is modest downstream, where public and regulatory attention is heaviest and model usage and cloud fall below the 1{,}800 mark United States agencies treat as highly concentrated. It rises steeply upstream, where public discourse is sparse. There advanced packaging scores 8{,}100, leading-edge lithography reaches the ceiling of 10{,}000, and one country dominates the production or refining of several critical minerals, gallium near that maximum. Second, these upstream layers are chokepoints and a strategic vulnerability for the whole AI economy. Antitrust cannot reach them, because they lie in foreign or state hands. Contesting them falls to export controls and domestic industrial policy. Third, placing reserves beside refining shows the concentration is built rather than geological, a variable industrial policy can move. A rise in an upstream input's price barely changes the cost of the product built from it, because the input is only a small share of that cost. A chokepoint's threat is therefore the loss of the input itself rather than a higher price.
