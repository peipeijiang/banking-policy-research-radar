# Who Enters the Record? Paywalls, Contributor Selection, and Social Learning

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25071v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过理论模型研究付费获取历史决策记录时，价格如何筛选贡献者并影响社会学习，发现正价格会阻碍完全学习，而保留初步选择可恢复完全学习但降低当前决策准确性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究当购买获取先前决策的机会同时也决定了谁的决策会被记入记录时的社会学习问题。个体首先接收私人信息，然后决定是否付费获取访问权。正价格会筛选出相对较弱的私人信念。在只有一个创始观测的情况下，每个购买者都会复制该观测，记录不再获得进一步信息。在初始历史更丰富的情况下，购买者可能产生分歧，记录可以改进；然而，即使私人信念无界，价格远离零也会阻止完全学习。随后我们考虑一种规则，即购买者在获取访问权之前做出初步选择，之后做出修正选择。实施初步选择的概率为正，使其遵循私人信号。保留两种选择可在固定有效费用或 pooled myopic pricing 下恢复完全学习，包括在信念有界的情况下。该规则降低了支付意愿并牺牲了一些当前决策准确性。一个实例表明，随后信息质量的改善可以超过这两方面的初始损失。这些结果说明了为什么保留在接触之前做出的判断能够改变后续用户从同一被选择群体中学到的内容。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies social learning when buying access to previous decisions also determines whose decision enters the record. Individuals first receive private information and then decide whether to pay for access. A positive price selects relatively weak private beliefs. With one founding observation, every buyer copies it, and the record acquires no further information. With a richer initial history, buyers can disagree and the record can improve; nevertheless, a price bounded away from zero prevents complete learning, even with unbounded private beliefs. We then consider a rule under which a buyer makes a preliminary choice before access and a revised choice afterward. A positive probability of implementing the preliminary choice makes it follow the private signal. Retaining both choices restores complete learning under a fixed effective fee or pooled myopic pricing, including with bounded beliefs. The rule reduces willingness to pay and sacrifices some current decision accuracy. A worked example shows how the subsequent improvement in information can outweigh both initial losses. The results show why preserving a judgment made before exposure can change what later users learn from the same selected population.
