<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

贝拉哈（2023）的新凯恩斯主义示例在其印刷校准下未被识别：不止一种结构满足其识别步骤的每一项条件。该论文的六个识别限制中有两个与印刷简化形式一致的均衡方程重合，留下十一个条件来确定一个方程的十二个系数。本注释推导了识别步骤所需的秩条件，该条件仅需从简化形式和限制中计算得出。本注释还记录并纠正了论文反事实展示中的一处印刷错误。对论文定理1的单条款修正恢复了其结论。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The New Keynesian example of Beraja (2023) is not identified at its printed calibration: more than one structure satisfies every condition of its identification step. Two of the paper's six identifying restrictions coincide on equilibrium equations consistent with the printed reduced form, leaving eleven conditions to determine an equation's twelve coefficients. The note derives the rank condition the identification step requires, computable from the reduced form and the restrictions alone. The note also documents and corrects a misprint in the paper's counterfactual display. A one-clause amendment to the paper's Theorem 1 restores its conclusion.

</details>
