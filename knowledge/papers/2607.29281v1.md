<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

经济学中的应用研究本质上受到广泛规范性目标的驱动。然而，研究者应如何引导其努力以产生支持此类目标的证据，这一问题并不显而易见。本文回顾了关于政策选择研究设计的近期理论进展，并提供了应用研究者可用于指导其设计选择及传达政策建议的新工具。首先，我聚焦于计量经济学中的理论贡献，并构建了一个通用框架，该框架以一致的符号和叙述方式涵盖了所回顾的所有情境与结果。随后，我提出了两个图表，应用研究者可借助它们从具体情境出发，在理论文献中导航，以做出审慎的设计选择。最后，我介绍了一个新的R软件包，该软件包生成一张表格和两幅图形，应用研究者可将其嵌入其“政策启示”部分，以提供关于其研究中不同政策建议表现情况的证据。本文通过发展经济学中的一个实例展示了这些工具的使用方法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Applied research in economics is intrinsically motivated by broad normative objectives. However, it is not obvious how a researcher should direct their efforts to produce evidence toward such objectives. This paper reviews recent theoretical developments on research design for policy choice and provides new tools applied researchers can use to guide their design choices and communicate their policy recommendations. First, I focus on theoretical contributions in econometrics and provide a general framework that nests all the contexts and results reviewed using a coherent notation and narrative. Then, I present two diagrams applied researchers can use to navigate the theoretical literature starting from concrete scenarios to make thoughtful design choices. Finally, I introduce a new R package that produces one table and two figures applied researchers can plug in their `policy implications' section to provide evidence on the performance of different policy recommendations coming out of their study. The use of such tools is illustrated with an example in development economics.
