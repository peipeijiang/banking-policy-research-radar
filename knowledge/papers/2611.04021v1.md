<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了在参考分布周围的Bregman–Wasserstein球上，扭曲风险度量（distortion riskmetrics）的最坏情况取值。与平方的2-Wasserstein距离不同，Bregman–Wasserstein散度可以对向上和向下的偏差施加不同的惩罚。我们的主要结果适用于有界变差的广义扭曲函数 \(g:[0,1]\to\R\)，其中 \(g(0)=0\) 且具有适当的端点正则性，该函数不必单调、规范化或上半连续。对于满足双侧 \(p\)-增长条件的严格凸生成元，我们得到了最坏情况取值的一维对偶表示。当正Lagrange乘子使散度约束成为紧约束时，我们通过最小凹majorant刻画了唯一的分位函数，并刻画了其何时达到上确界。对于绝对连续的广义扭曲函数，我们通过等渗投影（isotonic projection）刻画了解；我们还研究了更一般的生成元和形状约束，包括单峰性。最后，我们将分析推广到Bregman–Wasserstein球的交集，描述了决策中多个参与方可接受的模型，并用数值方法说明了结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the worst-case values of distortion riskmetrics over Bregman--Wasserstein balls around a reference distribution. Unlike the squared $2$-Wasserstein distance, Bregman--Wasserstein divergences can penalize upward and downward deviations differently. Our main result applies to generalized distortion functions $g:[0,1]\to\R$ of bounded variation, with $g(0)=0$ and suitable endpoint regularity, which need not be monotone, normalized, or upper semicontinuous. For strictly convex generators satisfying a two-sided $p$-growth condition, we obtain a one-dimensional dual representation of the worst-case value. When a positive Lagrange multiplier makes the divergence constraint binding, we identify a unique quantile function through a least concave majorant and characterize when it attains the supremum. For absolutely continuous generalized distortion functions, we characterize the solution through an isotonic projection; we also study more general generators and shape constraints, including unimodality. Finally, we extend the analysis to intersections of Bregman--Wasserstein balls, describing models acceptable to several parties involved in decision making, and illustrate the results numerically.

</details>
