<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文运用小波分析方法和全球向量自回归（GVAR）模型，探讨厄尔尼诺波动对各国通货膨胀率、全球大宗商品价格及国际油价的影响。分析重点聚焦于“ENSO多样性”的通胀效应，以中太平洋（CP）型和东太平洋（EP）型厄尔尼诺为衡量指标。利用自1950年前后起的季度数据，我们大幅延长了现有研究的时间跨度，从而能够观测到数量更多的ENSO循环周期。研究涵盖的国家样本包括直接受厄尔尼诺影响的国家、与厄尔尼诺变化存在遥相关的国家，以及仅通过全球经济关联间接受到这些过程影响的国家。小波分析结果呈现出几个关键特征：第一，对大多数国家而言，无论其是直接受ENSO影响还是通过遥相关受到影响，ENSO变化与通货膨胀之间均呈现出阶段性关系；第二，对CP型和EP型厄尔尼诺效应的识别，凸显了在分析ENSO通胀效应时考虑ENSO多样性的重要性；第三，统计显著的时期同时涵盖ENSO循环中的厄尔尼诺和拉尼娜阶段。用于估算ENSO循环对各国CPI通胀影响程度的GVAR框架进一步证实，将CP型和EP型ENSO指标所反映的ENSO多样性效应纳入模型，对于识别其对各国通货膨胀率产生的复杂非线性影响至关重要。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this paper we apply the wavelet approach and the GVAR methodology to investigate the effects of El Niño fluctuations on national inflation rates, global commodity prices and world oil prices. The focus of our analysis is on the inflation effects of “ENSO diversity”, measured as the central Pacific (CP) and eastern Pacific (EP) El Niño. Using quarterly data from c.1950 onward, we substantially extend the time span of existing studies. This allows us to observe a much larger number of ENSO cycles. The set of countries included in our study comprises countries directly affected by the El Niño, countries that are teleconnected with the El Niño variations and countries that are only indirectly affected by these processes via global economic linkages. The results from wavelet analysis display several key features. First, we observe an episodic relationship between ENSO variations and inflation for most countries, irrespective of whether they are directly impacted by ENSO or impacted via teleconnections. Second, the identification of CP and EP ENSO effects highlights the importance of ENSO diversity when analysing the inflationary effects of ENSO. Third, the periods of statistical significance encompass both El Niño and La Niña phases of the ENSO cycle. The GVAR framework used to estimate the magnitude of the effects of the ENSO cycle on national CPI inflation confirms that modelling the effects of ENSO diversity as captured by CP and EP ENSO measures matters for the identification of their complex nonlinear effects on national inflation rates.
