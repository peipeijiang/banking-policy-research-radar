<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们引入Boom-Busty Marketron模型，这是一个多资产市场模型，其中单一集体投资者的需求推动价格变动，而该需求本身又取决于市场表现。这在索罗斯理论的精神下形成了一个投资者—市场的反身性循环。市场状态是平稳的但并非处于均衡：一种非平衡稳态（NESS），具有破缺的细致平衡以及一个在错误定价、需求与波动率之间循环的概率流。尽管带有杠杆效应的随机波动率已经打破了细致平衡并使市场动力学不可逆，投资者—市场循环进一步将市场推离均衡。通过模拟矩方法对1996年至2024年的标普500指数进行校准，该模型再现了收益率的各种典型事实以及市场的定量指标（包括横截面指标），并且其收益率和周期统计量在1996—2010年输入下在一个半世纪内保持平稳。其核心涌现行为是繁荣—萧条周期，其频率在单标准差范围内与标普的频率相符。该模型还产生了与投资级发行人同量级的违约率、在费希尔·布莱克两倍价值因子范围内的定价、Frazzini和Lamont的笨钱效应，以及一个可测量的时间箭头。由其横截面构建的标准多空因子随周期轮动，其行业分组也沿防御轴随之轮动。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce the Boom-Busty Marketron, a model of a multi-asset market in which the demand of a single collective investor moves prices and itself depends on market performance. This creates an investor-market reflexive loop in the spirit of Soros's theory. The market state is stationary without being at equilibrium: a non-equilibrium steady state (NESS), with broken detailed balance and a probability current circulating between mispricing, demand and volatility. While stochastic volatility with the leverage effect already breaks detailed balance and makes the market dynamics irreversible, the investor-market loop pushes the market further away from equilibrium. Calibrated to the S\&P 500 from 1996 to 2024 by simulated moments, the model reproduces the stylised facts of returns together with quantitative metrics of the market, including cross-sectional ones, and its return and cycle statistics stay stationary for a century and a half on the 1996--2010 inputs. Its central emergent behaviour is a boom-bust cycle, whose frequency meets the S\&P's within one-sigma ranges. The model also produces a default rate of the order of that of investment-grade issuers, prices inside Fisher Black's factor of two of value, the dumb-money effect of Frazzini and Lamont, and a measurable arrow of time. Standard long-short factors built from its cross section rotate through the cycle, and so do its industry groups along the defensive axis.

</details>
