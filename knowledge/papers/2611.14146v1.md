# Estimating multidimensional poverty under incomplete data in North Macedonia

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

多维贫困指数（Multidimensional Poverty Indices, MPIs）已覆盖100多个国家，但很少细化到国家或地区以下层面。地方层面的估计需要了解家庭内部各项剥夺的重叠情况，然而覆盖每个地区的数据来源，如人口普查和地理空间数据，无法在家庭层面进行关联。我们以北马其顿为案例研究，展示了在此类不完整数据条件下如何测量地方多维贫困。我们将定居点层面的服务可及性与市政人口普查数据相结合，采用基于独立性的点估计，并在与数据一致的所有联合分布上给出紧致边界。我们发现，全国范围内5.01%的家庭存在多维贫困，边界为2.01%至17.44%。此外，贫困集中于小型农村市镇，在任何依赖结构下，这些市镇都比首都几乎所有的市镇更为贫困。通过将我们的指数与官方统计数据、全球MPI（Global MPI）以及实地调查剥夺指数进行验证，结果表明指数的社会经济部分与外部剥夺测量指标一致，而地理部分则捕捉了一个独特的偏远性维度。因此，我们的方法为在缺乏关联家庭数据的国家中测量地方层面多维贫困提供了一条有前景的途径。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Multidimensional Poverty Indices (MPIs) are available for more than 100 countries, but rarely below the national or regional level. Local estimates require knowing how deprivations overlap within households, yet the sources that cover every locality, such as censuses and geospatial data, cannot be linked at the household level. We show how to measure local multidimensional poverty under such incomplete data, using North Macedonia as a case study. Combining settlement-level access to services with municipal census data, we pair an independence-based point estimate with sharp bounds over all joint distributions consistent with the data. We find that, nationally, 5.01 percent of households are multidimensionally poor, with bounds of 2.01 to 17.44 percent. Also, poverty is concentrated in small rural municipalities, which are poorer than nearly all municipalities of the capital city under any dependence structure. Validating our index against official statistics, the Global MPI, and a fieldwork deprivation index indicates that the socio-economic part of the index is consistent with external measures of deprivation, while the geographic part captures a distinct dimension of remoteness. Our approach thus presents a promising way to measure multidimensional poverty at the local level in countries where linked household data are unavailable.
