<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在私有信息下维持有效率的交易需要外部补贴。尽管机制设计者平均而言无法避免赤字，但他们可以控制风险敞口。我们刻画了在具有大量买方和卖方的市场中、在中期参与约束下，能够最小化最坏情况赤字的高效率且真实机制。该问题对偶于在相关性模糊下最大化预期交易剩余的问题，且约束条件为移除一个代理人的子市场。在双边交易中，最优赤字上限是基准连续统市场中的竞争性剩余，可通过“竞争性枢轴机制”实现。在更大的市场中，最优机制以最坏情况剩余福利贡献来刻画，但解呈现出复杂的组合结构。我们在自然的参与费用限制下获得了显式保证，并确定了最优赤字超额随市场增厚而衰减的方式。我们的对偶刻画具有一般性，可推广到许多机制设计环境（公共品、合伙关系解散）以及替代性参与约束。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Sustaining efficient trade under private information requires external subsidies. Although a designer cannot avoid deficits on average, they can control exposure. We characterize efficient and truthful mechanisms that minimize the worst-case deficit in markets with many buyers and sellers, under interim participation. This problem is dual to maximizing expected trade surplus under correlation ambiguity, with constraints on submarkets with one agent removed. In bilateral trade, the best deficit ceiling is the competitive surplus in a benchmark continuum market, achieved by a "competitive pivot mechanism". In larger markets, optimal mechanisms are characterized in terms of worst-case residual welfare contributions, but solutions exhibit a complex combinatorial structure. We obtain explicit guarantees under natural participation charge restrictions and establish how the best deficit excess decays as market thickens. Our duality characterization is general and extends to many mechanism design environments (public goods, partnership dissolution), and alternative participation constraints.

</details>
