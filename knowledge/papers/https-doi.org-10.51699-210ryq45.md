<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究考察了商业银行金融风险管理改进方法，特别侧重于信用风险、流动性风险、资本充足性和数据相关风险。研究利用乌兹别克斯坦共和国中央银行的官方数据以及巴塞尔委员会制定的国际原则，分析了乌兹别克斯坦银行业的最新发展。结果表明，尽管银行业已加强了其资本和流动性状况，但资产和贷款的快速增长要求更先进的风险识别与监测机制。本研究提出了一个综合性金融风险管理框架，该框架基于风险导向的信用评估、动态压力测试、预警指标、风险数据聚合和数字化监测。本研究的创新之处在于将银行业定量指标与一种适应乌兹别克斯坦商业银行部门条件的预防性风险管理方法相结合。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study examines approaches to improving financial risk management in commercial banks, with particular emphasis on credit, liquidity, capital adequacy, and data-related risks. The research analyzes the recent development of Uzbekistan’s banking sector using official data from the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan and international principles developed by the Basel Committee. The results indicate that although the banking sector has strengthened its capital and liquidity positions, rapid growth in assets and lending requires more advanced risk identification and monitoring mechanisms. The study proposes an integrated financial risk management framework based on risk-based credit assessment, dynamic stress testing, early-warning indicators, risk data aggregation, and digital monitoring. The novelty of the study lies in combining quantitative banking indicators with a preventive risk-management approach adapted to the conditions of Uzbekistan’s commercial banking sector.
