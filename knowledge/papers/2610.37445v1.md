# Hosting is not capturing: an exposure-capability framework and Hungary's electric-vehicle battery bet

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当多重危机使经济一体化从效率来源转变为脆弱性暴露面时，依附型经济体应如何应对？本文构建了“暴露—能力”框架，该框架沿两个维度——外部依赖的集中度与生产能力的深度——映射一国的战略选项，并得出四种理想策略。本文将这一框架应用于匈牙利对电动汽车电池制造的大规模押注，追问这种集中性暴露换来了什么：是向电池价值链更高位置移动，作为一次追赶性赌博，还是在一种新产品下复制该国既有位置，即依附性固化。本文利用2010—2024年匈牙利最大企业的企业层面面板数据，检验了四个假设，并考察了为这些投资提供融资的支持所附加的条件。该行业规模急剧扩张，但其价值获取既未超出建设亏损恢复后的水平，也未沿价值链向上移动：生产仍集中于最低价值环节，成熟度仅达到可比年轻外资企业的水平，且无法覆盖自身成本。为其提供融资的支持要求投资额、收入和就业保留；与能力相关的标准仅作为考量因素而非条件进入法令，而同一计划内一项以能力为条件的工具则未被使用。截至2024年的证据表明，这一押注是在质量不变条件下的横向替代，即电池电芯制造在同一依附位置上取代汽车组装。该案例表明，承载绿色转型的供应链并不等同于获取其价值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How should a dependent economy respond when a polycrisis turns economic integration from a source of efficiency into a surface of vulnerability? This paper develops the exposure-capability framework, which maps a country's strategic options along two dimensions - the concentration of its external dependence and the depth of its productive capability - and yields four ideal strategies. It applies the framework to Hungary's large-scale bet on electric-vehicle battery manufacturing, asking what the concentrated exposure purchased: movement toward a higher position in the battery value chain, as a catch-up gamble, or the reproduction of the country's existing position under a new product, as dependent entrenchment. Using a firm-level panel of Hungary's largest companies for 2010-2024, it tests four hypotheses and examines the conditions attached to the support that financed the investments. The sector has expanded enormously, but its value capture neither rises beyond the recovery from construction losses nor shifts up the chain: production remains concentrated in the lowest-value stage, matures only to the level of comparably young foreign-owned firms, and does not cover its own costs. The support that financed it required investment volume, revenue and employment retention; capability-relevant criteria entered the decree as considerations rather than conditions, and a capability-contingent instrument within the same scheme went unused. On the evidence to 2024 the bet is a lateral substitution at constant quality, in which battery-cell fabrication replaces automotive assembly at the same dependent position. Hosting the green transition's supply chain, the case shows, is not the same as capturing its value.
