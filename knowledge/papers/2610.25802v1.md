# Amortized Neural Filtering for Calibrated Posteriors in Statistical Arbitrage

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25802v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出摊销神经滤波估计器，在均值回归价差上一次性输出潜状态后验，用于统计套利头寸规模；结果显示后验主要贡献是正确刻画自身不确定性，并在韩国股票上显著缩小点估计的预测偏差。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

统计套利交易基于对残差价差状态的估计，而传统做法忽略了该估计的不确定性。我们保留这种不确定性，并将其用于交易决策。神经滤波器估计器（Neural Filter Estimator, NFE）在一个均值回归过程的模拟路径上训练，该过程的创新分布律通过将候选分布与真实残差进行检验来选定；训练完成后，它通过一次前向传播即可返回横截面中每个价差潜在状态的后验分布。由此得到三项结果。在真值已知的模拟路径上，所有实用估计器（包括我们的方法）都落在一个狭窄的精度区间内，远低于拥有完全信息的估计器，因此后验分布相对于点估计并未获得精度上的提升；其贡献在于对自身不确定性的正确刻画。在韩国股票市场上，扣除成本后，传统点估计在每个持有期的预测值比实际实现值高出约一千个基点，而摊销方法将这一差距缩小了约两到八倍。基于与端到端注意力因子模型（Attention Factor Model）相同的学习因子，利用后验分布确定仓位规模，在该时期的噪声范围内，以不增加额外风险的方式达到了这些因子在基准规则下的夏普比率。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Statistical arbitrage trades on an estimate of the state of a residual spread, and conventional practice discards the uncertainty of that estimate. We keep the uncertainty and use it in the trading decision. A Neural Filter Estimator (NFE) is trained on simulated paths of a mean-reverting process whose innovation law is selected by testing candidates against real residuals; once trained, it returns the posterior over the latent state of every spread in a cross-section in one forward pass. Three results follow. On simulated paths with known truth, every practical estimator, ours included, falls in one narrow band of accuracy well below an estimator given full information, so the posterior gains no accuracy over a point estimate; its contribution is a correct account of its own uncertainty. On Korean equities, net of costs, the conventional point estimate forecasts about a thousand basis points per holding period more than it realizes, and the amortized methods cut that gap by a factor of roughly two to eight. Sizing positions from the posterior on the same learned factors as the end-to-end Attention Factor Model matches the Sharpe ratio of those factors under the base rule within the period's noise, at no more risk.
