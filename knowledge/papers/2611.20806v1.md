<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究由一个非对称信息线性估计器组成的分层网络对常数状态向量的递归估计问题，该网络建模为有向树。只有叶节点观测回归信号，而其他每个估计器通过其子节点转发的含噪信号估计以口口相传的方式学习。层级中的权威通过公共先验信息体现：每一轮之后，每个估计器采用根节点广播的估计值和误差协方差。这种公共信息越精确，局部观测上的有效噪声方差就越大，从而使估计本质上具有自挫性。由此产生的性能是本文的研究主题。我们证明，递归最小二乘（RLS）估计器确实能够在均方意义下学习状态，尽管速度较慢。在强持续激励条件下，广播误差协方差随时间多项式衰减，其衰减速率由匹配阶数的上下界刻画。该指数由叶节点到根节点之间的最小中继数决定，并随该数量呈指数下降。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study recursive estimation of a constant state vector by a hierarchical network of asymmetrically informed linear estimators, modeled as a directed tree. Only the leaves observe the regression signal, whereas every other estimator learns by word of mouth from noisy signal estimates relayed by its children. Authority within the hierarchy is expressed through common prior information: after each round, every estimator adopts the estimate and error covariance broadcast by the root. The more accurate this common information, the larger the effective noise variance on local observations, which makes estimation intrinsically self-defeating. The resulting performance is the subject of this paper. We show that recursive least-squares (RLS) estimators do learn the state in mean square, albeit slowly. Under strong persistency of excitation, the broadcast error covariance decays polynomially in time, at a rate that we characterize by upper and lower bounds of matching order. The exponent is determined by the smallest number of relays between a leaf and the root, and it decreases exponentially with this number.
