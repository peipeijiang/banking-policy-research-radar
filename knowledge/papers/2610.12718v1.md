<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

大型语言模型本质上是随机的。在相同实验条件下重复执行可能会产生不同的响应。我们提出了一个可应用于模型全生命周期的框架，从任务设计到残余行为和持续监控。该方法包括通过蒙特卡洛模拟（Monte Carlo simulation）构建经验响应分布。引入语义干预来研究这些分布如何变化。我们通过两个不同的任务——借款人分类和音乐专辑推荐——来说明该框架，并量化由此产生的分布变化。该框架通过测量稳健性和控制有效性，以及刻画残余行为，与模型风险管理和治理相关联。由于它仅依赖于观测到的响应，因此也可以应用于通过商业API访问的黑盒模型。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Large language models are stochastic by nature. Repeated executions under the same experimental conditions may produce different responses. We propose a framework that can be applied across the model lifecycle, from task design to residual behavior and continuous monitoring. The method consists of building an empirical response distribution estimated through Monte Carlo simulation. Semantic interventions are introduced to study how these distributions change. We illustrate the framework with two different tasks, borrower classification and music album recommendation, and quantify the resulting distributional changes. The framework is linked to model risk management and governance by measuring robustness and control effectiveness, and characterizing residual behavior. Since it relies only on observed responses, it can also be applied to black-box models accessed through commercial APIs.
