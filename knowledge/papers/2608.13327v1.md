# Breaking the Chain: Division Norms and Criminal Deterrence

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13327v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过三阶段博弈模型分析有组织犯罪中分配规范、威慑能力与成员流动的相互作用，发现人口单调分配下最小威慑预算等于最短瓦解路径成本，Shapley值可最大化该预算。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在组织犯罪中，成员流动最快，威慑能力变化较慢，而分裂规范变化最慢。我们将其建模为一个三阶段博弈：分裂规范首先确定每个可能的联盟如何分配其收益；随后，权力机构在知晓哪个联盟将形成之前，将威慑能力赋予指定的成员；最后，成员流动围绕那些未被威慑的人员进行调整。在人口单调的分裂规则下，最小威慑预算等于最短瓦解路径的成本，即每次移除一名成员。当沙普利值满足人口单调性时，它通过使每条路径成本相等，唯一地最大化该预算。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In organized crime, membership moves fastest, deterrence capacity moves more slowly, and division norms move slowest. We model this as a three-stage game: division norms fix how every possible coalition divides its proceeds; the authority then attaches deterrence capacity to named members, before knowing which coalition will form; membership adjusts last, around whoever remains undeterred. Under population-monotone division, the minimum deterrence budget is the cost of a shortest dismantling path, removing one member at a time. When the Shapley value is population monotone, it uniquely maximizes this budget by making every path equally costly.

</details>
