# How to Disrupt a Market

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24389v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过在线实验发现，部分破坏配送能降低市场效率，但增加市场集中度；攻击卖家评分无效。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

经济学中的市场设计研究自然聚焦于如何提升市场效率。而我们的目标恰恰相反——如何设计干预措施以降低市场效率。本研究受线上非法市场增长的启发，在这些市场中降低效率可能减少社会危害。通过一项基于网络的实验，我们发现对物流配送的部分阻断是降低市场效率的有效方法。这种效率下降体现在卖家销售商品数量减少、收益降低。然而，物流阻断的一个后果是市场集中度上升，因为它促进了主导卖家的出现。相比之下，我们发现对卖家评分的攻击在降低市场效率方面效果甚微。本研究为基于证据、因果驱动的调查铺平了道路，以助力制定打击网络犯罪及其他非法市场的政策。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Market design research in economics naturally focusses on how to improve market efficiency. Our objective here is exactly the opposite - how to design interventions that make a market less efficient. Our research is inspired by the growth of illicit markets online where reducing their efficiency may reduce societal harm. Using a web-based experiment, we find that a partial disruption to delivery is an effective method to decrease market efficiency. The decrease is borne by sellers who sell fewer goods and have lower earnings. A consequence of a disruption to delivery, however, is an increase in market concentration because it facilitates the emergence of a dominant seller. In contrast, we find that attacks on seller ratings are ineffective at reducing market efficiency. This study paves the way for evidence-based, causally driven investigations to aid policies to disrupt cybercrime and other illicit markets.
