<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

公众气候关注的变化是否会改变持续性金融风险，并进而对实体经济活动产生影响？我们将美国行业股票的日度收益率与基于互联网搜索强度构建的月度气候关注指数（Climate Concern Index, CCI）相结合，并将该指数分解为不利型（adverse）和转型导向型（transition-oriented）两个组成部分。GARCH-MIDAS估计结果揭示了显著的异质性：不利型气候关注会推高碳密集型能源行业的持续性波动率，而转型导向型关注则倾向于降低受监管公用事业行业的持续性波动率，且各子行业之间存在重要差异。随后，我们构建了能源行业与公用事业行业之间的持续性风险差异指标，并利用局部投影法（local projections）检验其对行业实体经济活动的预测能力。与不利型气候关注相关的冲击预示着随后数个季度石油和天然气工业产出及就业的下降，而公用事业行业的可比响应则较为有限。当使用匹配度更高的石油、天然气、煤炭股票子行业重新构建金融预测指标时，产出方面的结果依然成立，且在多项稳健性检验中保持经济意义上的相似性。总体而言，研究结果确立了气候关注、持续性行业金融风险与后续实体经济部门活动之间的联系。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Do shifts in public climate concern alter persistent financial risk and subsequently matter for real activity? We combine daily U.S. sectoral equity returns with a monthly Climate Concern Index (CCI) constructed from internet search intensity and decomposed into adverse and transition-oriented components. GARCH-MIDAS estimates reveal substantial heterogeneity: adverse climate concern raises persistent volatility in carbon-intensive Energy activities, while transition-oriented attention tends to reduce persistent volatility in regulated Utilities, with important differences across sub-sectors. We then construct a persistent-risk differential between Energy and Utilities and examine its predictive content for sectoral real activity using local projections. Innovations associated with adverse climate concern precede declines in Oil and Gas industrial production and employment over the following quarters, while comparable Utilities responses are limited. The production result is preserved when the financial predictor is reconstructed using the more closely matched Oil, Gas, Coal equity sub-sector and remains economically similar across several robustness checks. Overall, the findings establish a link between climate concern, persistent sectoral financial risk, and subsequent real-sector activity.
