<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究考察基于Transformer的金融新闻情感是否能在传统市场预测因子之外改善股票波动率预测。我们使用11只美国股票的数据，比较了波动率持续性模型与纳入VIX、AAII投资者情绪以及使用ModernFinBERT提取的新闻情感的模型设定。预测目标为年化波动率，该波动率由未来252个交易日的Yang-Zhang估计值的未来均值构建，并在1、5和22个交易日的预测期限上进行评估。模型通过前向滚动检验进行评估，采用扩展窗口和滚动估计窗口、固定的初始超参数选择，以及考虑多重检验和序列相关性的统计比较。结果表明，朴素的持续性基准在所有期限上均取得最低的平均QLIKE损失。尽管HAR-VIX在扩展窗口下产生最多的单次预测胜出次数，但并未取得最低的平均损失。在33次扩展窗口比较中，加入基于文本的情感仅在两次中改善了HAR预测，且这两种改善在较长的HAC带宽下均不具有统计显著性。对2,700条新闻文本进行的盲审进一步刻画了ModernFinBERT分类的一致性和置信度模式。研究结果未能确立基于Transformer的新闻情感具有普遍的增量预测优势。这些结果凸显了强基准、时间对齐、稳健推断以及对文本衍生预测因子进行审慎评估的重要性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study examines whether transformer-based financial news sentiment improves equity volatility forecasts beyond conventional market-based predictors. Using data for eleven US stocks, we compare volatility persistence models with specifications incorporating the VIX, AAII investor sentiment, and news sentiment extracted using ModernFinBERT. The forecasting target is annualized volatility constructed from future averages of trailing 252-session Yang-Zhang estimates, evaluated at horizons of 1, 5, and 22 trading days. Models are assessed through walk-forward testing with expanding and rolling estimation windows, fixed initial hyperparameter selection, and statistical comparisons accounting for multiple testing and serial dependence. Results show that a naive persistence benchmark achieves the lowest average QLIKE loss at all horizons. Although HAR-VIX produces the largest number of individual forecasting wins under expanding windows, it does not achieve the lowest mean loss. Adding text-based sentiment improves HAR forecasts in only two of 33 expanding-window comparisons, and neither improvement remains statistically significant under longer HAC bandwidths. A blinded audit of 2,700 news texts further characterizes the agreement and confidence patterns of ModernFinBERT classifications. The findings do not establish a general incremental forecasting advantage from transformer-based news sentiment. They highlight the importance of strong benchmarks, temporal alignment, robust inference, and careful evaluation of text-derived predictors.
