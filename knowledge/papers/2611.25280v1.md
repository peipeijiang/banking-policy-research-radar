<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究具有纯信息外部性的重复博弈。每个参与人观察到一个条件独立的信号，然后重复采取可公开观察的行动，以最大化其期望贴现收益，该收益仅取决于状态和自身行动。我们证明，当参与人数量足够多且足够有耐心时，存在信息得以聚合的均衡：参与人的收益近似于完全信息下的收益。即使行动在短视行为下不具信息性，这一结论仍然成立。反之，即使短视行为揭示了状态，也存在信息未被聚合的均衡：收益近似于自给自足（autarky）下的收益。我们的结果揭示了具有前瞻性参与人的社会学习博弈可能出现的结果范围。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study repeated games with purely informational externalities. Each player observes a single conditionally independent signal and then repeatedly takes publicly observed actions to maximize their expected discounted payoff, which depends only on the state and their own action. We show that, when there are sufficiently many players and they are sufficiently patient, there exist equilibria where information is aggregated: the players' payoffs approximate the full-information payoff. This holds even if actions are uninformative under myopic play. Conversely, even if myopic play reveals the state, there also exist equilibria where information is not aggregated: payoffs approximate those under autarky. Our results speak to the range of possible outcomes of social learning games with forward-looking agents.

</details>
