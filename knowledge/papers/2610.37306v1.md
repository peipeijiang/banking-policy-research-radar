<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们检验了关于农业起源与传播的多种假说：气温升高、季节性增强、二氧化碳施肥效应、海平面上升，以及广延边际上的技术进步。我们成功复现了Matranga（QJE 2024）的研究。其季节性假说在替代数据、估计方法和竞争性假说下均保持稳健。人口密度在农业起源中发挥了作用，而空间扩散对农业的传播十分重要。海平面上升和气候变化对最早采用农业地区的人口密度影响较小。通过外推农业起源的logit模型，波斯湾、地中海和黄海部分现已被淹没的地区曾适合早期农业。随着时间推移，技术进步是扩展农业广延边际的主要因素。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We test various hypotheses for the invention and spread of agriculture: higher temperatures, increased seasonality, carbon dioxide fertilization, sea level rise, and technological progress on the extensive margin. We successfully replicate Matranga (QJE 2024). His seasonality hypothesis survives alternative data, estimators, and competing hypotheses. Population density played a role in the invention of agriculture, while spatial diffusion was important for its spread. Sea level rise and climate change had a minor effect on the population density of the areas that first adopted agriculture. Extrapolating a logit model for the invention of agriculture, some now-submerged areas in the Persian Gulf, Mediterranean, and Yellow Sea were suitable for early agriculture. Over time, technological progress was the main factor in extending agriculture's extensive margin.
