# Algorithm Transparency and Search Manipulation: Steering vs. Persuasion

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12558v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个偏好于销售两种产品中利润较高者的平台。该平台设计了一种算法，根据消费者最匹配的产品，决定其首先接触到的产品。该算法同时操纵消费者的注意力（引导）并传递关于匹配质量的信息（告知）。当算法不透明时，消费者难以理解产品排序是如何生成的及其所揭示的内容。在平台偏好的均衡中，它将利润较高的产品置于首位。当算法透明时，消费者能够理解该算法及其所传达的信息。因此，算法既能引导，也能说服。在某些情况下，均衡算法会阻止搜索，而在其他情况下则会鼓励搜索。在前一种情况下，透明度有助于消费者；在后一种情况下，透明度则损害部分或全部消费者的利益。扩展或转移透明度要求会将引导与信息提供分离开来，使消费者福利回归至不透明状态。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study a platform that prefers to sell the more profitable of two products. It designs an algorithm that determines the product the consumer encounters first, conditional on her best match. The algorithm simultaneously manipulates consumer attention (steers) and communicates information about match quality (informs). When the algorithm is opaque, it is difficult for a consumer to understand how product order is generated and what it reveals. In the platform's preferred equilibrium, it places the profitable product first. When the algorithm is transparent, the consumer understands the algorithm and what it conveys. Thus, the algorithm can persuade as well as steer. In some cases, the equilibrium algorithm deters search, in others, encourages it. In the former case, transparency helps consumers; in the latter, it harms some or all of them. Extending or shifting transparency requirements uncouples steering from information provision, reverting the consumer's welfare to opacity.

</details>
