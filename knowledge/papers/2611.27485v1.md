<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

风险分担通常被视为对面临独立且均值有限风险的厌恶风险主体有利，但当损失具有无穷均值时，这一情形可能发生逆转。本文利用背景风险模型，探讨正相依无穷均值损失下风险分担的效果。在线性风险分担和条件均值风险分担两种情形下，我们证明无穷均值损失的风险分担在通常随机序意义下可能增加风险。我们的结果涵盖经典多元损失分布，包括Pareto-Clayton模型和Fréchet-Gumbel模型。对于线性风险分担，我们进一步推导了分散化投资组合之间的随机占优关系，包括边缘分布为Pareto分布、由常用Archimedean copula的凸混合耦合的情形。对于条件均值风险分担，我们还讨论了稳定分布的峰度及随机序关系。因此，在上述条件下，对于任何在无穷均值模型下良定义的律不变单调风险度量，保留原始风险或较少分散化的投资组合是更优的选择。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Risk sharing is usually viewed as beneficial for risk-averse agents facing independent risks with finite means, but this situation can be reversed for infinite-mean losses. In this paper, we explore the effects of risk sharing for positively dependent infinite-mean losses using background risk models. Under both linear and conditional mean risk sharing, we show that risk sharing of infinite-mean losses can increase the risk in the sense of usual stochastic order. Our results cover classical multivariate loss distributions, including the Pareto-Clayton and the Fréchet-Gumbel models. For linear risk sharing, we further derive stochastic dominance relations between diversified portfolios, including those with Pareto marginals coupled by convex mixtures of commonly used Archimedean copulas. For conditional mean risk sharing, we additionally discuss peakedness and stochastic order relations for stable distributions. Consequently, under the stated conditions, retaining the original risk or a less diversified portfolio is preferred under every law-invariant monotone risk measure that is well defined for infinite-mean models.
