# Instant Payments After a Betting Ban: Evidence from Brazil

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08859v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文利用2026年巴西联邦政府全国禁止博彩的事件，采用线性趋势规范评估禁令后37天内Pix即时支付交易笔数和结算金额是否显著低于预测值，发现分别低约8.37%和10.83%。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

巴西一直面临着犯罪、家庭债务、高利率、消费和信贷获取等方面的重大问题。博彩（betting）作为可能的促成因素已出现在公共讨论中。2026年9月25日，联邦政府在全国范围内禁止了博彩。我们研究了该禁令如何影响即时支付（Pix）。我们的主要发现是，在线性趋势设定下，37天的累计交易笔数比预测值低约8.37%，而结算金额低10.83%。在该设定下，两项偏差均具有统计显著性。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Brazil has been facing major problems with delinquency, household debt, high interest rates, consumption, and access to credit. Betting has emerged in public debate as a possible contributing factor. On September 25, 2026, the federal government banned betting nationwide. We investigate how the ban affected instant payments (Pix). Our main finding, under the linear-trend specification, is that cumulative transaction counts over 37 days were approximately 8.37% below forecast, while settlement values were 10.83% lower. Both deviations were statistically significant under this specification.
