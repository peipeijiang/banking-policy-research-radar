# Bankruptcy Rules for Strategic Agents

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

破产规则是在债权各异的多人之间分配不足财产的制度。现有规则假定债权是公开已知的。当情况并非如此时，债权人具有强烈的动机超额申报债权，因为破产规则对申报债权更大的债权人给予更大的分配额。法律体系常用的一种补救措施是通过要求举证和施加处罚使超额申报变得昂贵。但这一补救措施同时也增加了证明真实债权的法律费用，这可能导致少报。鼓励如实申报需要对法律成本进行精细调整。

本文将支持真实债权与虚假债权的成本纳入破产模型。债权人为其债权的真实部分和虚假部分承担成本，虚假债权的单位成本至少不低于真实债权。目标是设定成本率，使债权人申报其真实应得份额，既不多也不少。这是否可能取决于分配规则。比例规则（proportional rule）和受限等额分配规则（constrained equal awards rules）在任何正的如实申报成本率下都无法保证如实申报；对于至少有三个债权人的情形，受限等额损失规则（constrained equal losses rule）同样如此。

对于$N\ge2$个债权人，参数化塔木德规则族（Talmud family）中的规则TAL-1/N在如实申报成本率至多为1/N且虚假申报成本率至少为1-1/N时恰好诱导如实申报。该规则是最优的，其如实申报成本区域包含所有分段光滑分配规则的如实申报成本区域：它在设定法律成本方面为法律体系提供了最大可能的灵活性。对于两个债权人，TAL-1/2（即争议衣物规则，Contested Garment rule）唯一达到该最优区域。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Bankruptcy rules are rules for dividing an insufficient estate among people with different claims. Existing rules assume that the claims are publicly known. When this is not the case, claimants have a strong incentive to claim more than they are due, as bankruptcy rules give larger allocations to claimants with larger claims. A remedy often used by legal systems is to make over-reporting costly by requiring evidence and imposing penalties. But this remedy also increases the legal expenses of proving true claims, which might result in under-reporting. Encouraging true reporting requires fine-tuning of the legal costs.
  This paper incorporates the costs of supporting both true and false claims into the bankruptcy model. Claimants incur costs for the true and false parts of their claims, with false claims costing at least as much per unit as true claims. The goal is to set cost rates that induce claimants to report their true entitlements, neither more nor less. Whether this is possible depends on the allocation rule. The proportional and constrained equal awards rules cannot guarantee truthfulness at any positive true-claim cost rate; the same holds for constrained equal losses with at least three claimants.
  For $N\ge2$ claimants, the rule TAL-1/N from the parameterized Talmud family induces truthfulness exactly when the true-claim cost rate is at most 1/N and the false-claim cost rate is at least 1-1/N. This rule is optimal in the sense that its truthful cost region contains that of every piecewise smooth allocation rule: it gives the legal system the maximum possible flexibility in setting the legal costs. For two claimants, TAL-1/2 (aka Contested Garment rule) uniquely attains this optimal region.
