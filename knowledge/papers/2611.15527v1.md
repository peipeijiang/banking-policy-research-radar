<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

假设世界存在一个不确定状态，其分布由累积分布函数（CDF）G 给出。众所周知，由某一信号通过贝叶斯法则诱导出的随机后验均值，其分布 F 必定是 G 的均值保持收缩（mean-preserving contraction）；反之，任何均值保持收缩都可由某个信号诱导产生。这并非 Strassen（1965）的定理！这是一个不同的结果，其若干版本曾由 Hardy、Littlewood 和 Polya（1929）、Blackwell（1951）以及 Kolotilin（2014）给出陈述（或至少有所提及）。本文给出精确的陈述和完整的证明。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Suppose there is an uncertain state of the world, drawn from a CDF G. It is well known that the random posterior mean induced (via Bayes's rule) by a signal must have a distribution F that is a mean-preserving contraction of G, and conversely that any mean-preserving contraction is induced by some signal. This is not Strassen's (1965) theorem! It is a distinct result, versions of which were stated (or at least alluded to) by Hardy, Littlewood and Polya (1929), Blackwell (1951) and Kolotilin (2014). I give a precise statement and a complete proof.
