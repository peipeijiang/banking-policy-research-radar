# Optimal climate policy in incomplete electricity markets

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

电力市场中的不完全长期合约可能使投资者和消费者面临低效率的风险水平。本文研究了不完全性如何影响气候政策的最优选择。我们构建了一个最优政策制定的博弈论模型，该模型预见了在天然气价格和需求不确定且合约不完全的情况下，电力市场参与者对给定政策如何反应。在示例性实验中，我们观察到当市场不完全时，可再生能源补贴和碳价格会产生保险协同效益。这是因为，相对于完全市场基准，不完全性使投资从可再生能源转向天然气，并使消费者面临电价年际波动带来的未保险风险。通过激励对可再生能源的投资，补贴和碳价格间接抵消了这些扭曲。因此，在我们的实验中，不完全性提高了最优补贴和碳价格水平。可再生能源补贴表现出比碳定价更强的保险效应，因为后者导致更多对碳捕集与封存（carbon capture and storage）天然气项目的投资以及更波动的电价。虽然碳定价在完全市场中比补贴更具经济效率，但不完全性缩小了其效率优势，甚至导致补贴更具经济效率的情形。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Incomplete long-term contracting in electricity markets can expose investors and consumers to inefficient levels of risk. This paper investigates how incompleteness impacts the optimal choice of climate policy. We develop a game-theoretic model of optimal policy-making in anticipation of how electricity market agents react to a given policy when gas prices and demand are uncertain and contracting is incomplete. In illustrative experiments, we observe that renewable subsidies and carbon prices induce an insurance co-benefit when markets are incomplete. This is because, relative to a complete market benchmark, incompleteness shifts investment from renewables toward gas and exposes consumers to uninsured risk from inter-annual volatility in electricity prices. By incentivizing investment in renewables, subsidies and carbon prices indirectly counteract these distortions. Consequently, incompleteness increases the optimal subsidy and carbon price levels in our experiments. Renewable subsidies exhibit a stronger insurance effect than carbon pricing, as the latter leads to more investment in gas with carbon capture and storage and more volatile electricity prices. While carbon pricing is more economically efficient than subsidies in complete markets, incompleteness shrinks its efficiency advantage and even leads to cases where subsidies are more economically efficient.
