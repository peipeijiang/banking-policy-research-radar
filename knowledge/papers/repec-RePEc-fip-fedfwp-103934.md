<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

尽管厄尔尼诺现象可能产生重大影响，但人们对其如何影响美国经济知之甚少。厄尔尼诺现象与其他常被研究的天气冲击不同，因为它是一种全球性现象，持续数月展开，可以提前数月预报，发生频率相对较低，并且根据季节和地区的不同对天气结果产生截然不同的影响。这些特征使得传统分析方法不适合研究厄尔尼诺事件的经济影响。本文采用一种新方法来估计厄尔尼诺现象的脉冲响应函数（impulse response functions）。我发现厄尔尼诺现象对PCE价格和就业构成实质性的上行风险。较长期的价格效应由核心服务和食品价格驱动，并取决于厄尔尼诺现象之后是否跟随拉尼娜现象——这是一种非常常见的现象。在初期温和的抵消效应之后，厄尔尼诺现象发生后的一年内，就业在行业和州层面均广泛增加。这些结果表明，尽管美国既非热带地区也不以农业为主，厄尔尼诺现象仍可对美国经济产生实质性影响。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Despite their potentially major impacts, little is known about how El Niño impact the U.S. economy. El Niños are distinct from other commonly studied weather shocks because they are a global phenomenon, unfold over a period of months, can be forecast months in advance, are relatively infrequent, and have very different effects on weather outcomes depending on the time of year and region. These features make traditional analytical methods ill-suited to studying the economic impacts of El Niño events. In this paper I use a new approach to estimating the impulse response functions of El Niños. I find that El Niños pose material upside risk for PCE prices and employment. The longer-run price effects are driven by core services and food prices and depend on whether La Niñas follow El Niños, a very common occurrence. After modest offsetting initial effects, employment increases are widespread in terms of both sectors and states in the year following the El Niño. These results indicate El Niños can have material impacts on the US economy even though it is neither tropical nor agriculturally focused.
