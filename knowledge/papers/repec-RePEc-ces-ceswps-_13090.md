<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

失业劳动者如何形成关于其劳动力市场前景的信念，这些信念又如何影响行为、政策有效性和福利？我们构建并估计了一个具有准贝叶斯更新（quasi-Bayesian updating）的结构性求职搜索模型，将关于信念和求职行为的瑞士面板数据与一项随机化的咨询与辅导干预相结合。求职者进入失业状态时对面试机会的到来持有过度乐观的信念，但随着过去信号的记忆逐渐消退而逐步变得悲观。估计的更新过程兼具动机性信念（motivated beliefs）和近因加权信息处理（recency-weighted information processing）的特征。该模型复现了观察到的申请、面试、工资和就业找到的动态变化，同时将处理效应分解为搜索效率、搜索成本和信念的变化。我们发现，信念形成是干预加速向就业过渡的主要渠道。然而，尽管提高了就业找到率，该干预降低了参与者福利，因为它主要通过降低保留工资（reservation wages）发挥作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How do unemployed workers form beliefs about their labor-market prospects, and how do these beliefs affect behavior, policy effectiveness, and welfare? We develop and estimate a structural job-search model with quasi-Bayesian updating, combining Swiss panel data on beliefs and job-search behavior with a randomized counseling-and-coaching intervention. Job seekers enter unemployment with overly optimistic beliefs about interview arrivals, but gradually become pessimistic as past signals fade from memory. The estimated updating process combines features consistent with both motivated beliefs and recency-weighted information processing. The model replicates the observed dynamics of applications, interviews, wages, and job finding, while decomposing treatment effects into changes in search efficiency, search costs, and beliefs. We find that belief formation is the principal channel through which the intervention accelerates transitions into employment. However, despite increasing job finding, the intervention reduces participant welfare because it operates primarily through lower reservation wages.
