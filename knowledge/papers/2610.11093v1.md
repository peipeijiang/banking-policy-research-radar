# Risk Ceilings and Development Deadlines: Pacing AI under Uncertain Safety Productivity

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11093v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文研究在安全生产率不确定时，监管者如何通过安全知识积累和算力分配同时实现AI风险上限与开发截止日期，发现先学习再回放开发并以安全算力为缓冲可接近最低安全知识要求。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当安全生产力未知时，监管者能否同时承诺风险上限和发展截止日期？若上限低于最终模型未受保护的危险水平，则需要最低限度的安全知识储备，因此只有当弱研究可被排除时，这两项承诺才能同时成立。先学习、再重放开发，并在安全方面保留备用算力，接近这一最低要求。在仅考虑状态风险、恒定安全产出和完全知识转移的校准中，它只需要比必要界限高出3.3%的生产力。客户服务为这一保证买单。固定算力分配的规则固定的是日期，而不是风险。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Can a regulator promise both a risk ceiling and a development deadline when safety productivity is unknown? A ceiling below the final model's unprotected hazard requires a minimum stock of safety knowledge, so both promises hold only if weak research can be ruled out. Learning first and then replaying development, with spare compute in safety, comes close to that minimum. In a calibration with only state risk, constant safety yield, and full knowledge transfer, it needs only 3.3 percent more productivity than the necessary bound. Customer services pay for the guarantee. Rules that fix compute allocation fix dates, not risk.

</details>
