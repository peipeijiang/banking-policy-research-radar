<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文构建了新的企业层面股东地理集中度指标，并考察股权持有中本土偏好上升如何影响欧元区的投资韧性。我们将欧洲央行证券持有统计（Securities Holdings Statistics, SHS-S）中的证券层面持有数据与Compustat中的企业层面财务报表数据相结合，发现股权所有权仍然高度集中于国内，货币联盟内部的跨境多元化程度有限。随后，我们采用围绕COVID-19冲击的差分中差分设计，并结合倾向得分匹配方法，分析股权本土偏好是否影响企业对宏观经济冲击的应对。在受疫情冲击最严重行业的企业出口平均下降16%的背景下，那些股东基础更为集中、更具本土偏好的企业在冲击后投资减少幅度比股东基础更为多元化的同类企业高出20%。该效应主要通过股权韧性减弱发挥作用，股权下降13%，而销售额和银行融资则保持相当。我们的研究结果凸显了更深层次跨境股权一体化对企业韧性的益处。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops novel firm-level measures of shareholder geographical concentration and examines how elevated home bias in equity holdings affects investment resilience in the euro area. We combine security-level holdings data from the ECB’s Securities Holdings Statistics (SHS-S) with firm-level financial-statement data from Compustat, and document that equity ownership remains strongly concentrated domestically, with limited cross-border diversification within the monetary union. Using a difference-in-differences design around the COVID-19 shock, combined with a propensity score matching approach, we then analyse whether equity home bias shapes firms’ responses to macroeconomic disruptions. Against an average 16% decline in exports among the firms in the sectors most affected by the pandemic, those with more concentrated, home-biased shareholder bases reduced investment by 20% more than their more diversified peers after the shock. The effect operates mainly through weaker equity resilience, with equity declining by 13%, while sales and bank financing remain comparable. Our findings highlight the benefits of deeper cross-border equity integration for corporate resilience.
