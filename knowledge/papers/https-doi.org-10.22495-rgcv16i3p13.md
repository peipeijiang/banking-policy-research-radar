# The impact of avoiding the risks of electronic bank transfers on the sustainability of banking performance

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

自2003年以来，伊拉克银行业经历了持续的数字转型，其特征表现为分行网络扩张、电子支付系统的引入以及金融科技（FinTech）的应用。本文考察电子银行转账规模与制约伊拉克商业银行绩效的宏观经济条件之间的关系。本文运用约翰森协整检验、向量误差修正模型（VECM）和脉冲响应函数，对伊拉克中央银行和中央统计组织发布的2011—2022年时间序列数据进行分析。电子转账与利率、汇率、通货膨胀和国内生产总值（GDP）之间存在协整关系。在长期水平回归中，利率呈负向影响，而汇率、通货膨胀和GDP呈正向影响。误差修正项在统计上显著，每期可修正约4.6%的长期均衡偏离，因此调整过程较为缓慢。转账规模的短期变动与政策利率相关，而GDP未产生显著的短期效应。研究结果将电子转账的增长定位于后冲突银行体系的宏观经济约束之中。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Iraq’s banking sector has undergone sustained digital transformation since 2003, marked by branch-network expansion, the introduction of electronic payment systems, and the adoption of financial technology (FinTech). This paper examines how the volume of electronic bank transfers relates to the macroeconomic conditions that govern the performance of Iraqi commercial banks. Series published by the Central Bank of Iraq and the Central Statistical Organisation for 2011–2022 are analysed with Johansen cointegration tests, a vector error correction model (VECM), and impulse response functions. Electronic transfers cointegrate with interest rates, exchange rates, inflation, and gross domestic product (GDP). In the long-run levels regression, the interest rate enters negatively, whereas the exchange rate, inflation, and GDP enter positively. The error-correction term is statistically significant and corrects approximately 4.6 percent of a deviation from long-run equilibrium in each period, so adjustment proceeds slowly. Short-run variation in transfer volumes tracks the policy interest rates, while GDP contributes no significant short-run effect. The findings locate the growth of electronic transfers within the macroeconomic constraints of a post-conflict banking system.
