# Discretion at the Margin: Threshold Manipulation and Electoral Bans in Colombian Public Procurement

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28965v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用哥伦比亚2011-2026年全部采购记录，发现采购方将估算金额精确压在法律阈值之下（bunching），且选举前直接采购禁令导致时间维度上的规避行为，揭示规则-自由裁量权衡的成本。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

公共采购法以价值门槛来权衡规则与自由裁量权：低于门槛时，采购方使用更快捷、竞争性更低的程序。哥伦比亚提供了一个异常清晰的场景，用以衡量采购方在多大程度上操纵估算价值以保持在门槛之下，以及这样做的代价是什么。门槛是实体特定的（法律规定的五个预算区间），并且每年一月随最低工资调整，而通常干扰聚束估计的整数则保持不变。利用哥伦比亚采购记录的全集（SECOP I 和 II，2011-2026年；8，569，218个SECOP II流程）以及每个实体-年份自身的门槛，我们发现估算价值在两个法定截止点下方均出现明显的聚束：小额采购门槛下方有49，631个超额流程（占该门槛下方最后5%区间内观测质量的86%），招标门槛下方有3，157个。峰值跟随门槛而非整数：它随最低工资变动，仅出现在每个实体自身所属的区间，而对于不设价值上限的程序，其规模小了一个数量级。由民选市长和州长管理的地方政府在小额采购门槛处的聚束程度高于任命的中央机构，即使在按预算区间、采购对象和年份重新加权之后也是如此。聚束在招标门槛下方的流程吸引的投标者数量仅为门槛上方流程的一半，获得的折扣更小，且更频繁地在授标后被扩大规模。第二个变异来源——总统选举前四个月对直接签约的禁令——表明采购方也会在时间边际上对规则作出反应：在四个选举周期中的每一个，禁令期间直接签约下降95%，禁令前两周上升209%，并伴有向门槛以下程序的部分转移。智利的固定UTM门槛呈现相同模式，而2024年废除的一个截止点在废除后不再显示聚束。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Public procurement laws trade rules against discretion with value thresholds: below them, buyers use faster and less competitive procedures. Colombia offers an unusually clean setting to measure how much buyers manipulate estimated values to stay below the line and what it costs. Thresholds are entity-specific (five budget bands set by law) and move every January with the minimum wage, while the round numbers that usually confound bunching estimates stay fixed. Using the universe of Colombian procurement records (SECOP I and II, 2011-2026; 8,569,218 SECOP II processes) and each entity-year's own threshold, we find sharp bunching of estimated values just below both legal cutoffs: 49,631 excess processes below the small-purchase threshold (86% of the mass observed in the last five percent below it) and 3,157 below the tender threshold. The spike tracks the threshold, not the round numbers: it moves with the minimum wage, appears only at each entity's own band, and is an order of magnitude smaller for procedures that carry no value cap. Territorial governments, run by elected mayors and governors, bunch more than appointed national agencies at the small-purchase threshold, even after reweighting for budget band, object and year. Processes bunched below the tender threshold attract half as many bidders as those just above it, obtain smaller discounts, and are more often enlarged after award. A second source of variation, the four-month ban on direct contracting before presidential elections, shows that buyers respond to rules on the timing margin: direct contracting falls by 95% during the ban and rises by 209% in the two weeks before it, in each of four electoral cycles, with a partial shift toward below-threshold procedures. Chile's fixed UTM thresholds display the same pattern, and a cutoff abolished in 2024 shows no bunching afterwards.
