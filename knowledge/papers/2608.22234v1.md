<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们刻画了在确定性原则下确保理性社会学习中达成共识的通信网络。一个网络能够确保共识，当且仅当该网络是伪互惠的（pseudo-reciprocal），即互惠性在个体之间可能缺失，但在更粗略的群体层面上得以成立。以往的研究假设信息交换是双向的，然而这种成对互惠性在很大程度上并非不可或缺。我们证明，无论群体规模多大，一条双向链路即可足以确保共识。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We characterize the communication networks that ensure consensus in rational social learning under the sure-thing principle. A network ensures consensus if and only if it is pseudo-reciprocal, a property under which reciprocity may fail between individuals but holds at a coarser group level. Prior work has assumed message exchange to be bidirectional, yet such pairwise reciprocity proves largely dispensable. We show that a single bidirectional link can suffice, however large the population.
