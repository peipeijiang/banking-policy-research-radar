<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究$2/3$-凸博弈的核心，这是一类可转移效用（transferable-utility, TU）博弈，它推广了凸博弈，要求任意三个不同联盟中至少有两个满足超模不等式。我们证明每个$2/3$-凸博弈都存在一个位于核心中的边际价值向量，因而具有非空核心。我们的证明是构造性的，并给出一个线性时间算法来找到这样的核心分配。我们还证明，$2/3$-凸性一般并不蕴含置换凸性，而在对单元素对的额外超可加性条件下该蕴含关系成立。作为应用，我们引入一个受欧安组织（OSCE）“共识减二”程序启发的TU模型，并证明所得博弈是$2/3$-凸的但不必是凸的。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the core of $2/3$-convex games, a class of transferable-utility (TU) games that generalizes convex games, which requires at least two of any three distinct coalitions satisfy the supermodular inequality. We show that every $2/3$-convex game admits a marginal worth vector in the core, and hence has a nonempty core. Our proof is constructive and yields a linear-time algorithm for finding such a core allocation. We also show that $2/3$-convexity does not imply permutational convexity in general, while the implication holds under an additional superadditivity condition on pairs of singletons. As an application, we introduce a TU model motivated by the OSCE's consensus-minus-two procedure and show that the resulting game is $2/3$-convex but need not be convex.

</details>
