<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文刻画了一类在风险下表现出内生参照依赖的二阶连续可微目标-概率偏好表示。弱秩依赖效用（WRDU）保留客观概率，在内生参照点处对结果进行划分，并通过一个得失表示来评估彩票，其中参照点最大化一个惩罚泛函。一阶条件产生一个虚拟损失厌恶指数，该指数等于损失域与收益域边际效用之比，将Köbberling和Wakker（2005）的基于效用的指数以及Tversky和Kahneman（1992）的斜率比作为特例加以恢复。主要定理表明，在满足仿射可容许性、损失分解性、离散单调性和衰减性的一类偏好中，导数比形式是唯一可容许的。在该类中，WRDU在可容许区域上生成模态阿莱斯模式，并通过范围依赖的衰减阻断拉宾校准蕴含。该结果是条件性的，并不声称在风险选择的所有行为模型中具有唯一性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper characterizes a class of twice continuously differentiable objective-probability preference representations exhibiting endogenous reference dependence under risk. Weak rank-dependent utility (WRDU) preserves objective probabilities, partitions outcomes at an endogenous reference point, and evaluates lotteries through a gainloss representation in which the reference point maximizes a penalized functional. The first-order condition yields a virtual loss-aversion index equal to the ratio of marginal utilities across the loss and gain domains, recovering both the utility-based index of Köbberling and Wakker (2005) and the slope ratio of Tversky and Kahneman (1992) as special cases. The main theorem shows that, within a class satisfying affine admissibility, loss-factorization, dispersion monotonicity, and attenuation, the derivative-ratio form is uniquely admissible. In this class, WRDU generates the modal Allais pattern on an admissible region and blocks the Rabin calibration implication through range-dependent attenuation. The result is conditional and does not claim uniqueness over all behavioral models of risky choice.
