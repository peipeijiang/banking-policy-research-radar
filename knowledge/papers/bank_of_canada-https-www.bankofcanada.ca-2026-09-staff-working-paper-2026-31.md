<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们记录了金融危机后劳动力市场复苏的宏观经济模式。利用全球战后衰退事件的样本，我们发现金融危机之后通常伴随的是无就业复苏，即相对于产出而言，就业复苏显得迟缓。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We document the macroeconomic patterns that characterize labor market recovery from financial crises. Using a sample of postwar recession episodes from around the world, we show that financial crises are typically followed by jobless recoveries, with a sluggish recovery in employment relative to output.
