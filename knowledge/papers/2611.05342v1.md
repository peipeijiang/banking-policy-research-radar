<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

需求往往在整数价格处急剧下降。为解释这一被称为左位数偏差（left-digit bias）的现象，我们构建了一个随机效用模型，其中消费者仅当价格低于其异质性注意阈值时才会考虑某商品。具有整数注意阈值的消费者群体产生了需求的不连续性。当引入税收时，该模型预测左位数偏差消费者的需求反应相比理性选择更弱。利用来自税收实验的数据，我们发现该模型与行为矩相匹配，并部分识别了相对于阈值的支付意愿分布。较弱的需求反应并不必然意味着较低的税收超额负担，理解具体机制对于福利分析至关重要。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Demand often drops sharply at whole-dollar prices. To explain this phenomenon, known as left-digit bias, we develop a random utility model in which consumers consider a good only if its price is below a heterogeneous attention threshold. Masses of consumers with integer attention thresholds generate demand discontinuities. When a tax is introduced, the model predicts a weaker demand response compared to rational choice for left-digit biased consumers. Using data from a tax experiment, we find that the model matches behavioral moments and partially identify the distribution of willingness to pay relative to thresholds. A weak demand response need not imply a low excess burden of taxation and understanding the specific mechanism is important for welfare analysis.
