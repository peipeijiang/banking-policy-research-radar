<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当反事实密度局部 Hölder 连续且阈值处可能伴随一个已知背景原子时的收缩税收折点（shrinking tax kinks）。较小的政策激励会产生两个不同的障碍：行为聚集者很少，以及一个不可观测的重新定位区间。对于完整的观测分布，我们为弹性估计和诚实置信集直径建立了匹配的极小极大界。设折点大小为 \(d\)、背景质量为 \(γ\)、光滑度为 \(0<s\leq1\)，收敛速率为 \(\min\{1,\sqrt{(γ+d)/(nd^2)}+d^s\}\)。当背景质量与折点同阶或更小时，随机分量具有稀有事件尺度；当其保持为正时，则具有传统的弱信号尺度。外推分量源于观测等价的反事实密度，而非抽样误差。我们推导了传统聚集比的正态极限和泊松极限，证明了其 Wald 区间的覆盖失败，并通过精确二项反演给出了有限样本置信集，同时带有确定性的光滑度容差。该程序从未受影响的箱中估计反事实密度，并一致地覆盖整个总体识别集，包括零政策激励的情形。似然比计算为光滑、已知的反事实子模型建立了有限参数泊松极限。结构性蒙特卡洛实验记录了覆盖保护及其在集合长度上的代价。结果适用于所述精确原子观测模型；一般的舍入和优化摩擦需要额外假设。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study shrinking tax kinks when the counterfactual density is locally Hölder continuous and may be accompanied by a known background atom at the threshold. Small policy incentives create two distinct obstacles: few behavioral bunchers and an unobserved relocation interval. For the complete observed distribution, we establish matching minimax bounds for elasticity estimation and honest confidence-set diameter. With kink size $d$, background mass $γ$, and smoothness $0<s\leq1$, the rate is $\min\{1,\sqrt{(γ+d)/(nd^2)}+d^s\}$. The stochastic component has a rare-event scale when the background mass is of the kink order or smaller and a conventional weak-signal scale when it remains positive. The extrapolation component follows from observationally equivalent counterfactual densities, rather than sampling error. We derive normal and Poisson limits for a conventional bunching ratio, show a coverage failure of its Wald interval, and give finite-sample confidence sets by exact binomial inversion with a deterministic smoothness allowance. The procedure estimates the counterfactual density from an unaffected bin and covers the entire population identified set uniformly, including zero policy incentives. A likelihood-ratio calculation establishes a finite-parameter Poisson limit for a smooth, known counterfactual submodel. Structural Monte Carlo experiments document both coverage protection and its cost in set length. The results apply to the stated exact-atom observation model; general rounding and optimization frictions require additional assumptions.
