<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

对比特币工作量证明（Proof-of-Work）的一项严肃批评是其巨大的电力消耗。Ball、Rosen、Sabin 和 Vasudevan（2017）提出的有用工作量证明（Proof-of-Useful-Work, PoUW）——即挖矿执行有用的计算——被建议作为减少电力支出的替代方案，其中矩阵乘法（MatMul）是 Komargodski 和 Weinstein（2026）近期提出的一个具体实例。

也许令人惊讶的是，我们证明转向 PoUW 可能会增加总电力消耗，这与杰文斯悖论（Jevons' paradox）相呼应。直观地说，PoUW 机制起到补贴的作用，降低了有用工作的有效价格。回顾一下，如果价格下降 x% 导致需求量增加超过 x%，则需求是弹性的。如果需求是弹性的，由此产生的需求量增加会导致总电力消耗净增加；如果需求是非弹性的，则总电力消耗减少。即便如此，PoUW 导致的电力消耗永远不会消失，因为挖矿补贴被花费在有用工作上，而如果没有补贴，这些工作就不会被生产。这一结果在一个仅考虑电力成本、无 PoUW 开销且挖矿补贴不变的简单模型中得到证明。该分析可推广到具有调整阈值的限制更少的设定中。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A serious criticism of Bitcoin's Proof-of-Work is its enormous electricity consumption. Proof-of-Useful-Work (PoUW), introduced by Ball, Rosen, Sabin, and Vasudevan (2017), in which mining performs useful computations, was suggested as an alternative to reduce electricity expenditure, with matrix multiplication (MatMul) as a recent concrete instantiation by Komargodski and Weinstein (2026).
  Perhaps surprisingly, we show that switching to PoUW may increase the total electricity consumption, echoing Jevons' paradox. Intuitively, the PoUW mechanism acts as a subsidy that lowers the effective price of the useful work. Recall that demand is elastic if a discount of x percent in the price leads to an increase of more than x percent in the quantity demanded. If demand is elastic, the resulting increase in quantity demanded leads to a net increase in total electricity consumption; if inelastic, total electricity consumption decreases. Even then, the electricity consumption due to PoUW never vanishes, since the mining subsidy is spent on useful work that would not have been produced without the subsidy. This result is proved in a simple model with electricity-only costs, no PoUW overhead, and an unchanged mining subsidy. The analysis extends to less restrictive settings with adjusted thresholds.
