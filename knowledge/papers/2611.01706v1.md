# Agent-Based Disruption Simulation on an Edge-Level Product-Annotated Supply Network of the Automotive Industry

当客户无法替换其失去的产品时，中断会通过供应网络蔓延。我们研究当客户寻找新供应商并形成新的供应边时，基于产品组合重叠和产品特定规则的候选供应商选择规则如何改变级联效应。基于MarkLines数据库，我们构建了一个产品标注网络，涵盖全球汽车行业中约77，000家企业和197，000条供应商-客户边。我们扩展了一个基于主体的中断模型，加入了边级别的产品信息、基于产品名称语义相似度的产品特定替代规则，以及经过校准的产能和库存。仅有6%的边带有观测到的产品信息。我们对其余边进行插补，并利用已标注的边检验插补效果。我们运行了119，000次模拟。在所有其他机制固定的情况下，产品特定规则使枢纽故障后的级联效应比原始模型的产品组合重叠规则大四到五倍。在替代插补方法和相似度阈值下，该倍数范围为1.2至18。在模拟中，可替代性还决定了哪些供应商是关键性的。网络拓扑结构在很大程度上决定了冲击能够到达哪些企业，而可替代性决定了其中有多少企业会失败。在随机中断下50个最具破坏性的供应商中，仅凭拓扑结构可识别出20个，而加入产品可替代性的指数可识别出32个。该指数无需重新拟合即可推广应用于基于2011年东日本大地震建模的区域情景。供应网络风险评估应考虑产品级别的可替代性，因此需要供应关系中的产品数据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Disruptions spread through supply networks when customers cannot replace the products they lose. We ask how portfolio-overlap and product-specific rules for selecting candidate suppliers change cascades when customers search for new suppliers and form new supply edges. From the MarkLines database, we construct a product-annotated network of about 77,000 firms and 197,000 supplier-customer edges in the global automotive industry. We extend an agent-based disruption model with edge-level products, a product-specific substitution rule based on the semantic similarity of product names, and calibrated capacities and inventories. Only 6% of the edges carry observed products. We impute the others and test the imputation against the annotated edges. We run 119,000 simulations. With all other mechanics fixed, the product-specific rule makes the cascades after a hub failure four to five times larger than the portfolio-overlap rule of the original model. Under alternative imputations and similarity thresholds, the factor ranges from 1.2 to 18. Within the simulations, substitutability also shapes which suppliers are critical. Network topology largely determines which firms a shock reaches, and substitutability determines how many of them fail. Of the 50 most damaging suppliers under random disruptions, topology alone identifies 20 and an index that adds product substitutability identifies 32. The index carries over without refitting to a regional scenario modelled on the 2011 Tohoku Earthquake. Supply network risk assessment should account for product-level substitutability and therefore needs product data on supply relationships.
