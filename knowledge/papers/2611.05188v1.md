<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

风险下的二元选择可能取决于备选结果如何联合分布。我们证明，在有限结果空间中，连续性、完备性和中间性对这一依赖性施加了局部到全局的限制：若两个具有相同边际分布的备选方案之间无差异，无论其结果如何联合分布，则对同一结果集上的所有彩票均意味着相关性不敏感。在相关性敏感的情形下，仅这三条公理即可刻画相关性加权效用（correlation weighted utility），该效用将逐状态比较与边际彩票的双线性函数相结合。在相关性不敏感的情形下，附加的投影独立性公理可导出Fishburn（1982）的斜对称双线性表示。强独立性将相关性加权效用约化为Lanzani（2022）的相关性期望效用表示。我们将相关性敏感的刻画推广至一般可度量化结果空间上的有限支撑彩票。由此得到的框架将相关性效应与对经典独立性的偏离区分开来，并产生可检验的限制，将同分布彩票之间的选择与相关的Allais行为联系起来。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Binary choice under risk can depend on how the alternative outcomes are jointly distributed. We show that in a finite outcome space, continuity, completeness, and betweenness impose a local-to-global restriction on this dependence: indifference between two alternatives with one common marginal distribution, however their outcomes are jointly distributed, implies correlation insensitivity for all lotteries supported on the same outcomes. In the correlation-sensitive case, these three axioms alone characterize correlation weighted utility, which combines statewise comparisons with a bilinear function of the marginal lotteries. In the correlation-insensitive case, an additional projective independence axiom yields Fishburn's (1982) skew-symmetric bilinear representation. Strong independence reduces correlation weighted utility to Lanzani's (2022) correlation expected utility representation. We extend the correlation-sensitive characterization to finitely supported lotteries on general metrizable outcome spaces. The resulting framework distinguishes correlation effects from departures from classical independence, and yields testable restrictions linking choices between identically distributed lotteries to correlated Allais behavior.
