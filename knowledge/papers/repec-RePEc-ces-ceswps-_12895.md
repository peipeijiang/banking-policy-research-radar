<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文探讨了与估算教师对学生考试成绩因果影响相关的问题。核心挑战在于学生与教师的匹配并非随机进行。为应对这一挑战，我们提出了标准教师增值模型的一种扩展形式，该模型相较于传统模型对内生流动性偏差具有更强的稳健性。随后，我们应用并扩展了方差成分偏差校正的最新创新方法，以量化教师的总体重要性，并估计分类与隔离的模式。研究发现，在丹麦，教师对学生不平等的影响比传统方法所显示的结果更为显著。此外，我们记录了一种新颖的负向分类模式，该模式由补偿性学校投资驱动，表现为高增值教师与低能力学生在校内匹配。我们还表明，高增值教师往往经验更丰富、受教育程度更高，并且尤其擅长课堂管理。最后，我们提供了初步证据，表明这些教师同时也能促进学生非认知技能与幸福感的提升。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper deals with issues related to estimating the causal impact of teachers on student test scores. The central challenge is that students and teachers are matched non-randomly. To deal with this, we propose an extension of the standard teacher value-added model that is more robust to such endogenous mobility bias than conventional models. We then apply and extend recent innovations in bias correction of variance components to quantify the overall importance of teachers and estimate patterns of sorting and segregation. We find that teachers in Denmark are more important for student inequality than the conventional approaches would suggest. Further, we document a novel pattern of negative sorting that is driven by compensatory school investments through the within-school matching of high-value-added teachers to low-ability students. We also show that high-value-added teachers tend to be more experienced and educated and that they are particularly skilled at classroom management. Finally, we present suggestive evidence that these teachers also promote student non-cognitive skills and well-being.
