# Arithmetic Models for Commodity Futures, Vanilla, and Calendar Spread Options

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28071v1)

## 一句话结论

> 构建商品期货与期权的算术多因子随机波动率模型，用无迹卡尔曼滤波估计WTI原油期货、香草期权和日历价差期权，发现三因子可刻画波动率期限结构，日历价差期权提供相邻合约去相关结构信息并改善定价与对冲。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个用于商品期货和期权的算术多因子随机波动率模型。算术设定使得期货曲线具有低维马尔可夫表示，并为普通期权和篮子期权（包括日历价差期权）提供了半解析定价公式。这些特性使该模型适用于大规模实证估计。

我们使用无迹卡尔曼滤波（unscented Kalman filter）对2013—2024年期间的WTI原油期货、普通期权和日历价差期权面板数据进行了模型估计。我们发现，需要三个随机波动率因子才能同时刻画波动率期限结构的水平和斜率，而基于这些因子的对冲几乎消除了普通期权组合的全部日常风险。日历价差期权提供了仅凭期货和普通期权无法捕捉的期货价格相依结构信息。未纳入日历价差期权进行估计的模型会因高估相邻期货合约之间的去相关性而系统性地对其定价过高。将这些期权纳入估计后，模型的去相关性接近观测到的去相关性，从而消除了定价过高问题并改善了其对冲效果，而普通期权的定价和对冲精度仅有小幅损失。最后，我们表明算术模型对期货和普通期权的拟合优于其几何对应模型，且对普通期权的对冲效果略优。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop an arithmetic multi-factor stochastic volatility model for commodity futures and options. The arithmetic specification yields a low-dimensional Markovian representation of the futures curve and semi-closed-form pricing formulas for vanilla and basket options, including calendar spread options. These properties make the model suitable for large-scale empirical estimation.
  We estimate the model using an unscented Kalman filter on a panel of WTI crude oil futures, vanilla options, and calendar spread options spanning 2013-2024. We find that three stochastic volatility factors are needed to capture both the level and slope of the volatility term structure, and that hedges based on these factors remove almost all of the daily risk of a portfolio of vanilla options. Calendar spread options provide information about the dependence structure of futures prices that is not captured by futures and vanilla options alone. A model estimated without calendar spread options systematically overprices them by overstating the decorrelation between adjacent futures contracts. Including these options in the estimation brings the model's decorrelation close to the observed decorrelation, which removes the overpricing and improves their hedges, at only a small loss of accuracy in the pricing and hedging of vanilla options. Finally, we show that the arithmetic model fits futures and vanilla options better than its geometric counterpart and hedges the vanilla options slightly better.
