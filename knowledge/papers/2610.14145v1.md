<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察了可能支撑前沿人工智能公司估值的商业模式，以及除销售模型访问权限之外计划获取收入的公开证据。对OpenAI和Anthropic的反向现金流计算得出，根据不同的财务假设，两家公司第十年的合计收入需求分别为2.79万亿美元或7330亿美元。公开文件显示了可能提供额外收入的拟议付款和所有权权益，例如通过制药、机器人或材料科学领域的合作关系。我们讨论了这些安排与企业边界和价值捕获之间的关系。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We examine business models that could support frontier AI companies' valuations and the public evidence of plans to earn income beyond selling model access. Reverse cash-flow calculations for OpenAI and Anthropic yield combined year-ten revenue requirements of \$2.79 trillion or \$733 billion, depending on financial assumptions. Public documents identify proposed payments and ownership interests that could supply additional income, such as through partnerships on pharmaceuticals, robotics, or materials science. We discuss how these arrangements relate to firm boundaries and value capture.
