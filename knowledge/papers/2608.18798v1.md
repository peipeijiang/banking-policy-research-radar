<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

复数规则容易受到相似备选方案之间选票分裂的影响。与常规认知相反，我们通过一种特定类型的克隆独立性条件来刻画复数规则。我们进一步引入了单参数族$p$-克隆抵抗（$p$-CR）的概念，并在计分规则类中考察该性质。对于$p \in (0,1)$，在至多三个备选方案的情况下存在非平凡规则，但通常只有平凡规则满足该条件。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The Plurality rule is vulnerable to vote splitting between similar alternatives. Defying conventional wisdom, we characterize the Plurality rule through a specific type of clone independence condition. We further introduce the notion of a one-parameter family of $p$-Clone Resistance ($p$-CR), and examine the property within the class of scoring rules. For $p \in (0,1)$, nontrivial rules exist with at most three alternatives, but only the Trivial rule generally satisfies the condition.
