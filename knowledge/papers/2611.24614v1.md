<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文展示了如何构造超模博弈中任意一对均衡的下确界与上确界。该方法依赖于两个单向最优响应过程，我将其称为向上传染（contagion up）和向下传染（contagion down）。从任意行动组合出发，以相反顺序应用这对过程会产生两个均衡，这两个均衡定义了该行动组合的均衡包络（equilibrium envelope）。包络的端点恰好是通过任意异步最优响应动态可达的最小和最大均衡，并且随着参与人修正其行动，这两个端点只会彼此靠近。这些结果及其他结果表明，在熟悉的均衡格结构上附加单向调整过程，如何使我们能够进一步探索均衡之间的关系。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper shows how the meet and join of any pair of equilibria in a super- modular game can be constructed. The method relies on two one-directional best-response processes that I call contagion up and contagion down. Starting from any action profile, applying this pair of processes in opposite orders produces two equilibria that define its equilibrium envelope. The endpoints of the envelope are precisely the least and greatest equilibria reachable through arbitrary asynchronous best-response dynamics, and can only move closer together as players revise their actions. These and other results show how appending one-directional adjustment processes to the familiar equilibrium lattice structure allows us to explore the relationships between equilibria further.
