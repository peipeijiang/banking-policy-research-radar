# Displacement Is Endogenous: AI Workers in a Search-and-Matching Labor Market

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14850v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们构建了一个劳动力市场，其中AI系统作为劳动者进入：它们与人类一样申请工作、通过筛选、被雇用和被解雇。企业发布职位空缺，劳动者在摩擦中搜寻，产品需求向下倾斜。在每种配置下进行30次固定种子运行，AI以人类工资70%的水平进入，使失业率上升4.1个百分点（95% CI 3.5至4.6），并使就业人数减少12.2人（CI 10.5至13.9）。当AI比人类便宜得多时，裁员占主导：裁员边际解释了7.2个失去的工作岗位，而招聘减少解释了5.0个，不过大多数被解雇的劳动者会再次找到工作。更大的价格差距会减少替代，因为廉价的AI使企业扩张：在价格持平且零解雇的情况下，失业效应最大，为9.2个百分点（CI 8.6至9.9），且全部通过招聘渠道发生。这一价格持平结果在将每期AI申请数从五个削减到一个后仍然成立。需求弹性决定了AI替代多少劳动者。筛选改变了谁被雇用，但使替代总量保持不变。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We build a labor market in which AI systems enter as workers: they apply for jobs, pass screenings, get hired, and get dismissed, alongside humans. Firms post vacancies, workers search with friction, and product demand slopes downward. Across 30 seeded runs per configuration, AI entry at 70% of the human wage raises unemployment by 4.1 percentage points (95% CI 3.5 to 4.6) and leaves 12.2 fewer humans employed (CI 10.5 to 13.9). Layoffs dominate when AI is much cheaper than humans: the layoff margin accounts for 7.2 of the lost jobs and reduced hiring for 5.0, though most dismissed workers find work again. A wider price gap reduces displacement, because cheap AI makes firms expand: at price parity, with zero dismissals, the unemployment effect is largest at 9.2 points (CI 8.6 to 9.9), all of it through hiring. That parity result survives cutting AI applications from five per period to one. Demand elasticity decides how many workers AI displaces. Screening changes who gets hired and leaves the displacement total unchanged.

</details>
