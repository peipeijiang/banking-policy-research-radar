<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我研究为何某些群体——无论是政治体还是组织——能够维持独立判断，而其他群体则陷入从众陷阱。社会认可不仅可能压制独立判断的表达，还可能压制维持这种判断可用的上游实践：维持判断会产生可见的质疑，而观察者无法区分勤勉与反对。当质疑普遍时，怀疑便会减少，因此对认可的相同渴望既能维持一种质疑文化，也可能导致从众陷阱。该模型将维持判断与在新情况出现时依据判断采取行动区分开来。当行动决策被唯一确定时，我给出了共存的基本条件；在对数几率—比例基准下，一个单一不等式是必要且充分的。主要贡献在于动态方面。在交错修订下，判断的存量是继承而来的：保护异议会立即释放现有判断——在低于盈亏平衡能力阈值的静态文化中，其即时预期适应值为负——而重建存量则受制于人员更替。一个单调算子从每个继承状态构造极值完美预见路径。当在每种可容许延续下恢复均可实现时，两个截断值对继承状态进行分类——恢复不可能、依赖预期或确定无疑——并且不稳定文化处的一个焦点条件严格区分这些状态，从而打开一个状态带，其中预期选择长期文化。人员更替随后在临时直接形成支持范围内，为使恢复成为可能或稳健提供了明确的物理持续时间界限。一个完全耦合的区间验证示例证实了共存、状态带以及不同的产出与福利含义。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I study why some groups---polities and organizations alike---sustain independent judgment while others fall into conformity traps. Social approval can suppress not only the expression of independent judgment but also the upstream practice that keeps such judgment available: maintaining judgment generates visible questioning, and observers cannot distinguish diligence from opposition. Because suspicion falls when questioning is common, the same desire for approval can sustain a questioning culture or a conformity trap. The model separates maintaining judgment from acting on it when novelty arrives. When the decision to act is uniquely determined, I give primitive conditions for coexistence; in a logit--proportional benchmark, a single inequality is necessary and sufficient. The principal contribution is dynamic. Under staggered revision the stock of judgment is inherited: protecting dissent releases existing judgment at once---with negative expected adaptive value on impact in a stationary culture below a break-even competence threshold---whereas rebuilding the stock is constrained by turnover. A monotone operator constructs the extremal perfect-foresight paths from every inherited state. When recovery under every admissible continuation is attainable, two cutoffs classify inherited states---recovery impossible, expectation-dependent, or assured---and a focus condition at the unstable culture strictly separates them, opening a band of states where expectations select the long-run culture. Turnover then yields sharp physical duration bounds, within temporary direct formation support, for making recovery possible or robust. A fully coupled interval-certified example verifies coexistence, the band, and the distinct output and welfare implications.
