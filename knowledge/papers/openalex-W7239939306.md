<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

组织对人工智能日益增长的兴趣，在商业银行业机构广泛部署之前就带来了劳动力方面的挑战。在组织准备的早期阶段，银行员工可能被期望获得新的能力，而未来的任务职责、学习安排以及应用这些能力的机会仍然不确定。本文通过对银行业、就业和劳动力发展出版物的整合性综述，并结合关于组织变革和员工学习的精选理论与实证文献，考察商业银行如何为员工做好人工智能应用的准备。该综述识别出在数字能力、持续学习、管理者支持和劳动力重新部署等方面趋于一致，并将其视为有效劳动力准备中相互关联的优先事项。然而，所选文献对于这些组织安排如何在实施前影响个体员工和相互依赖工作单元的准备工作，提供了不均衡的解释。基于技术变革的任务视角、组织准备理论和培训动机研究，本文构建了一个框架，将任务清晰度、有支持的集体实践以及可信的技能应用机会与员工为预期工作需求变化所做的准备联系起来。三个命题分别探讨角色不确定性、集体变革效能和持续再技能化参与，将其视为需要适当实证评估的不同结果，同时区分个体学习行为与对集体实施的共同信心。本文的贡献在于聚焦解释将现有再技能化建议与员工反应联系起来的机制，并识别与其未来实证检验相关的边界条件。该框架为在人工智能应用已规划、处于实验阶段或在不同职能间不均衡的银行中开展未来研究提供支持，并为考察可观察的劳动力变化出现之前的员工准备情况提供基础。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The growing organizational interest in artificial intelligence creates workforce challenges before its widespread deployment within commercial banking institutions. Bank employees may be expected to acquire new competencies while future task responsibilities, learning arrangements, and opportunities to apply those competencies remain uncertain during the early stages of organizational preparation. This article examines how commercial banks can prepare employees for AI adoption through an integrative review of banking, employment, and workforcedevelopment publications, supported by selected theoretical and empirical literature on organizational change and employee learning. The review identifies convergence around digital competencies, continuous learning, managerial support, and workforce redeployment as interconnected priorities for effective workforce preparation. However, the selected literature provides uneven explanations of how these organizational arrangements influence preparation before implementation across individual employees and interdependent work units. Drawing on task-based accounts of technological change, organizational readiness theory, and training-motivation research, the article develops a framework connecting task clarity, supported collective practice, and credible skill-application opportunities with employee preparation in anticipation of changing work requirements. Three propositions address role uncertainty, collective change efficacy, and sustained reskilling engagement as distinct outcomes requiring appropriate empirical assessment, while distinguishing individual learning behavior from shared confidence in collective implementation. The contribution is a focused explanation of mechanisms connecting existing reskilling recommendations to employee responses and identification of boundary conditions relevant to their future empirical examination. The framework supports future research in banks where AI adoption is planned, experimental, or uneven across functions and provides a basis for examining employee preparation before observable workforce changes emerge.
