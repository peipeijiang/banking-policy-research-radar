# Persistence of Bounded Rationality in Financial Markets: An Evolutionary Game-Theoretic Model

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

经典理论预测，非理性交易者会将财富输给理性交易者，并最终被逐出市场。然而，噪声交易、羊群行为和启发式交易即使在高度发达的市场中也持续存在。我们将金融市场建模为两种交易行为类型的生态系统的模型，即相对理性型（R）和有限理性型（N），其构成在复制子动态下根据相对表现演化。每种类型的适应度取决于另一类型的份额以及摩擦因素。当交互是互补的，即理性交易者从有限理性交易者造成的错误定价中获利，而后者从前者的流动性和价格纠正中受益，且当摩擦差异适中时，存在唯一的内部构成且全局渐近稳定：有限理性内生地持续存在。当交互是对抗性的，同一点成为两种单一类型 regime 之间的临界阈值。比较静态分析表明，降低自律交易的成本——正如算法交易和基于人工智能的交易所做的那样——会持续地将构成向理性交易方向移动，但除非成本优势超过一个明确的阈值，否则不会消除有限理性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Classical theory predicts that irrational traders lose money to rational traders and are eventually driven out of the market. Yet noise trading, herding, and heuristic trading persist even in highly developed markets. We model a financial market as an ecology of two trading-behavior types, relatively rational (R) and boundedly rational (N), whose composition evolves under replicator dynamics according to relative performance. Each type's fitness depends on the share of the other type and on frictions. When the interaction is complementary, that is, rational traders profit from the mispricing created by boundedly rational traders while the latter benefit from the liquidity and price correction supplied by the former, and when the difference in frictions is moderate, a unique interior composition exists and is globally asymptotically stable: bounded rationality persists endogenously. When the interaction is antagonistic, the same point becomes a tipping threshold between two one-type regimes. Comparative statics show that lowering the costs of disciplined trading, as algorithmic and AI-based trading does, shifts the composition toward rational trading continuously but does not eliminate bounded rationality unless the cost advantage exceeds an explicit threshold.

</details>
