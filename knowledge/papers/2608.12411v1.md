<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

近年来，多篇实证论文采用集体模型来分析配偶间的投资组合选择、其异质性风险偏好以及家庭内部的议价行为。本文对该模型的应用进行了研究，并指出了若干重要缺陷。在其经典形式下，该模型产生了一个有悖直觉的结果：家庭成员风险厌恶程度的提高可能导致家庭整体风险承担的增加。本文对此模式给出了形式化刻画，并将其与既有理论发现相联系。本文进一步指出了在议价和财富不平等背景下，将集体方法应用于投资组合选择问题时存在的其他问题。本文将近期家庭金融领域的论文与上述发现进行对照，并指出了文献中可能存在的混淆之处。本文强调了现有替代方法大多不具上述问题，但同时也指出，目前仍缺乏一种一致且严谨的建模框架。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A number of recent empirical papers rely on a collective model to analyze the portfolio choice of spouses, their heterogeneous risk preferences, and intra-household bargaining. I study applications of this model and highlight some important shortcomings. In its classic form, the model generates a counterintuitive result: an increase in the risk aversion of a household member can lead to an increase in household risk-taking. I offer a formal characterization of this pattern and link it to previous theoretical findings. I highlight further issues with applications of the collective approach to the portfolio choice problem in the contexts of bargaining and wealth inequality. I reconcile recent household finance papers with these findings and point to potential confusion in the literature. I emphasize existing alternatives that do not exhibit most of these issues, yet argue that there is a lack of a consistent and rigorous modeling approach.
