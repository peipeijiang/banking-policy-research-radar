<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

早期生活环境可以在传统结果指标显现之前很久就影响人力资本和生物衰老。通过聚合与年龄相关的表观遗传标记，表观遗传“时钟”已成为研究生物衰老的主要工具。我们开发了一种新的综合测量指标——多表观遗传年龄时钟（Multi EpiGenetic Age clock，MEGA）——它结合了现有的时钟以减少测量误差并提高效率。通过三项实证应用，我们表明青春期表观遗传衰老加速预示着更差的教育、心理健康和劳动力市场结果；儿童虐待会增加表观遗传衰老；延迟入学在七岁时因果性地加速衰老，尤其是在弱势儿童中。总之，这些结果表明，表观遗传衰老的综合测量指标可以如何被系统地纳入经济模型，以研究健康和人力资本形成的动态过程。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Early-life environments can influence human capital and biological aging long before their consequences are visible in conventional outcomes. By aggregating age-related epigenetic markers, epigenetic ‘clocks’ have become the leading tool for studying biological aging. We develop a novel integrated measure – the Multi EpiGenetic Age (MEGA) clock – that combines existing clocks to reduce measurement error and improve efficiency. Using three empirical applications, we show that accelerated epigenetic aging in adolescence predicts worse educational, mental health, and labor market outcomes; that child maltreatment increases epigenetic aging; and that delayed school entry causally accelerates aging by age seven, especially among disadvantaged children. Together, these results show how an integrated measure of epigenetic aging can be systematically incorporated into economic models to study the dynamics of health and human capital formation.
