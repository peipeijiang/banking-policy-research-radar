<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

水力压裂（hydraulic fracturing，即fracking）的扩张导致全球能源市场发生转变，美国墨西哥湾沿岸地区对此感受尤为强烈，路易斯安那州首当其冲。本文以数据为核心，考察能源行业的发展趋势，并揭示这些趋势如何对路易斯安那州经济产生了潜在的逆向负面影响。最值得注意的是，水力压裂导致油气供应大规模扩张，助推2014年末油价大幅下跌，造成大量就业岗位流失。路易斯安那州的经济衰退与具有重大地缘战略意义的全球能源市场转变密切相关。更广泛地说，研究结果表明，石油供应、需求和价格的大规模长周期波动会对就业创造和流失产生广泛影响。在路易斯安那州这样拥有庞大且多元化能源综合体的地区，这些趋势的影响可能极不均衡。21世纪的一个影响似乎是，在区域经济衰退的大格局中，工业发展呈现出高度集中的态势。此外，本研究所识别的趋势表明，天然气市场有可能出现重大周期。以往天然气主要通过管道输送并以长期合同销售，而液化天然气（liquified natural gas）的出现则为天然气市场动态变得更加波动创造了可能。天然气市场周期的影响虽尚不可知，但可能具有重大经济意义。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The expansion of hydraulic fracturing (i.e. fracking) led to global shifts in energy markets that were felt acutely along the US Gulf Coast and particularly in the state of Louisiana. This article presents a data-focused examination of trends in the energy industry, and shows how they led to potentially counterintuitive, negative impacts in Louisiana's economy. Most notably, hydraulic fracturing led to a massive expansion in oil and gas supplies that contributed to a significant decline in oil prices in late 2014, contributing to significant job losses. Louisiana's economic downturns were tied to global shifts in energy markets that had major geostrategic implications. More broadly, results indicate that large and long period cycles in the supply, demand and price of oil can have widespread impacts on job creation and loss. In states like Louisiana with a large and diversified energy complex, the impacts of these trends can be highly uneven. One impact in the 21st Century appears to be highly concentrated areas of industrial development within a broader pattern of regional economic decline. Furthermore, trends identified in this study suggest the potential for the emergence of major cycles in the gas market. Whereas natural gas was previously mostly transported by pipelines and sold through long-term contracts, the emergence of liquified natural gas creates the potential for more volatile gas market dynamics. The implications of gas market cycles, while not yet known, could be of major economic significance.
