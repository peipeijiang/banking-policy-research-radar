<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一般可替代选择模型下的联合库存与品类优化问题。在销售期开始之前，决策者在总库存单位容量约束下选择初始库存，随后顾客依次到达并在所提供的产品中进行选择。我们考虑两种情形：全展示库存选择（show-all inventory selection），即所有有库存的产品都必须被提供；以及动态品类个性化（dynamic assortment personalization），即决策者观察每位顾客的类型，并可根据剩余库存和购买历史调整所提供的品类。我们建立了一个统一框架，将每种情形与一个静态优化问题联系起来。对于全展示库存选择，针对基数约束静态品类优化的多项式时间 $α$-近似算法，在确定性到达或泊松到达下可给出 $0.486α$ 的保证，在递增故障率（increasing-failure-rate, IFR）到达下可给出 $0.4328α$ 的保证。对于个性化情形，当 $α$ 为 El Housni 和 Topaloglu 的联合品类与定制问题的近似因子时，同样的保证成立。我们的方法结合了流体松弛、依赖于需求的分式库存分配、期望截断需求的界以及相依舍入。值得注意的是，我们的个性化策略在销售期开始前为每种顾客类型固定一个品类，然后在该品类内提供所有有库存的产品，因此尽管其与最优完全自适应策略竞争，仍无需动态重新优化。最后，我们构造了若干实例，其中在确定性到达下最优全展示收入仅趋近流体界的一半，而在 IFR 到达下趋近于 $1-w\approx0.4328567$，其中 $we^w=1$。在具备精确静态品类预言机的情况下，我们的 IFR 保证可任意接近后一因子。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study joint inventory and assortment optimization under general substitutable choice models. Before a selling horizon, a decision maker chooses initial inventories subject to a capacity constraint on total units stocked, and customers then arrive sequentially and choose among the offered products. We consider two settings, show-all inventory selection, in which every in-stock product must be offered, and dynamic assortment personalization, in which the decision maker observes each customer's type and may adapt the offered assortment to remaining inventory and purchase history. We develop a unified framework linking each setting to a static optimization problem. For show-all inventory selection, a polynomial-time $α$-approximation for cardinality-constrained static assortment optimization yields guarantees of $0.486α$ under deterministic or Poisson arrivals and $0.4328α$ under increasing-failure-rate (IFR) arrivals. For personalization, the same guarantees hold when $α$ is the approximation factor for the joint assortment and customization problem of El Housni and Topaloglu. Our approach combines a fluid relaxation, demand-dependent fractional inventory allocations, bounds on expected truncated demand, and dependent rounding. Notably, our personalized policy fixes one assortment per customer type before the horizon and then offers every in-stock product within it, so it requires no dynamic re-optimization despite competing against the optimal fully adaptive policy. Finally, we construct instances in which optimal show-all revenue approaches only one-half of the fluid bound under deterministic arrivals and $1-w\approx0.4328567$ under IFR arrivals, where $we^w=1$. With an exact static assortment oracle, our IFR guarantee approaches the latter factor arbitrarily closely.
