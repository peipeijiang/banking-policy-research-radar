<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们将AI代理作为信息收集技术来研究：这些自动化系统通过实时互动从人类那里获取与决策相关的信号。我们利用一项自然田野实验测试这种AI自动化如何影响信息收集和组织结果，实验涉及70,000名申请真实职位的求职者。申请人被随机分配由人类招聘人员或AI语音代理进行面试。随后，人类招聘人员评估面试并做出招聘决定。由AI代理面试的申请人获得工作机会的可能性高出12%，而这些收益转化为更高的入职率和员工留存率，同时被录用员工的生产率没有下降。分析面试记录显示，AI语音代理实现了受控方差：它们的面试更有结构且更一致，同时仍能对个体申请人保持响应性，这与收集到更多与招聘相关的信息有关。我们的结果表明，AI自动化的一个关键优势在于这样的环境：信息收集被委托给许多人类工作者并且重复进行，以至于任务执行中的方差成为决策相关信号中的噪声，而AI通过自适应标准化将其压缩。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study AI agents as information-collection technologies: automated systems that elicit decision-relevant signals from humans through live interactions. We test how such AI automation impacts information collection and organizational outcomes using a natural field experiment with 70,000 applicants applying for real jobs. Applicants were randomly assigned to be interviewed by either human recruiters or AI voice agents. Afterward, human recruiters evaluate the interviews and make hiring decisions. Applicants interviewed by AI agents are 12% more likely to receive job offers, and these gains translate into higher job starts and worker retention, with no decline in the productivity of hired workers. Analyzing interview transcripts reveals that AI voice agents achieve controlled variance: their interviews are more structured and consistent while remaining responsive to individual applicants, which is associated with more hiring-relevant information collected. Our results suggest that a key advantage of AI automation lies in environments where information collection is delegated across many human workers and repeated such that variance in task execution becomes noise in decision-relevant signals, which AI compresses through adaptive standardization.
