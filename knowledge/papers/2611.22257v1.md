<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们考察并说明了应用于平稳输入的长短期记忆（LSTM）预测模型的在线特性。我们讨论了以下因素的影响：a) 定义可容许预测函数的网络架构，b) 用于衡量预测精度的目标函数，以及c) 随机梯度下降（SGD）算法。我们还研究了预测最优性、预测的期限结构以及滤波记忆动力学。我们还引入了一套针对基于记忆的预测模型的诊断工具，重点关注隐藏状态的估计动力学。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We examine and illustrate the properties of the Long Short Term Memory (LSTM) prediction models applied online to stationary inputs. We discuss the effects of: a) the network architecture that defines the admissible prediction functions, b) the objective function used to measure the prediction accuracy, and c) the Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) algorithm. We also study the prediction optimality, the term structure of predictions, the filtered memory dynamics. We also introduce a set of diagnostic tools for memory-based prediction models that focus on the estimated dynamics of the hidden states.
