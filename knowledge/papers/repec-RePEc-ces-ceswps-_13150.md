<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文运用新近发展起来的时变参数自回归框架，考察了中国经济政策不确定性的时间序列持续性。我们将该方法应用于两种不同的基于新闻的测度指标：一种源自中国大陆报纸，另一种源自香港的《南华早报》（South China Morning Post, SCMP）。分析揭示了一个关键性差异：大陆指数表现出较低且波动较大的持续性，而基于《南华早报》的测度则表现出较高且更稳定的持续性。这一发现表明，基于新闻的不确定性测度并非中性——它们反映了产生这些测度的制度环境。研究结果对我们如何测度不确定性具有启示意义，也有助于理解媒体偏见如何不仅塑造信息的内容，还塑造其时间动态。因此，国家媒体控制以可测量的方式塑造着信息。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines the time series persistence of economic policy uncertainty in China using a newly developed time-varying parameter autoregressive framework. We apply this methodology to two distinct news-based measures: one derived from mainland Chinese newspapers and another from Hong Kong's South China Morning Post. The analysis reveals a key divergence: the mainland index exhibits lower, more volatile persistence, while the SCMP-based measure shows higher and more stable persistence. This finding suggests that news-based uncertainty measures are not neutral - they reflect the institutional context that produces them. The results have implications for how we measure uncertainty, and for understanding how media bias shapes not just the content of information, but its temporal dynamics. Thus, state media control shapes information in measurable ways.

</details>
