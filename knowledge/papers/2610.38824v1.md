<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在本注记中，我们研究无限效用流上的社会福利函数，并回答了Asheim等人（2021，注1）提出的一个开放问题。存在一个满足单调性（monotonicity）、平稳性（stationarity）、受限占优（restricted dominance）和强匿名性（strong anonymity）——因而也满足有限匿名性（finite anonymity）——的社会福利偏好的实值表示。我们的社会福利函数满足Asheim等人（2022）和Asheim等人（2025）意义上更强的有限秩敏感性条件，即下极限受限占优（Liminf-Restricted Dominance）。我们的构造表明，在Asheim等人（2021，命题2）中，有限不连续性（limited discontinuity）并非冗余：一旦放弃该对角连续性条件，即使任意置换不变性（arbitrary-permutation invariance）也可以与平稳性和非平凡敏感性共存。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In this note, we consider social welfare functions on infinite utility streams and answer an open question posed in Asheim et al. (2021, Remark 1). There exists a real-valued representation of social welfare preferences that satisfies monotonicity, stationarity, restricted dominance, and strong anonymity, hence also finite anonymity. Our social welfare function satisfies a stronger finite-rank sensitivity condition, Liminf-Restricted Dominance, in the sense of Asheim et al. (2022) and Asheim et al. (2025). Our construction shows that limited discontinuity is not redundant in Asheim et al. (2021, Proposition 2): once that diagonal continuity condition is dropped, even arbitrary-permutation invariance can coexist with stationarity and non-trivial sensitivity.
