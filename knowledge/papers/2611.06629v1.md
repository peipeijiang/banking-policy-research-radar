# Does Frontier AI Strengthen Cyber Defense? Evidence from Project Glasswing

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06629v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用Project Glasswing的准自然实验，发现获得前沿AI访问权的美国科技公司披露并修复了更多软件漏洞，外部研究者转向其他公司代码，且攻击者利用漏洞的比例下降，但受限发布造成国家间软件安全不对称。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

防御者与攻击者竞相寻找相同的软件漏洞。我利用Project Glasswing的发布——该项目让一小群美国大型科技公司提前获得了Claude Mythos Preview，这是一个用于发现软件漏洞的模型——来研究前沿AI的获取权限如何影响防御者、外部研究人员和攻击者。利用公开披露漏洞的全集数据，我发现获得访问权限的公司在披露数量上相比未获得访问权限的中国和俄罗斯科技公司增加了一倍以上，披露的高影响漏洞多出约150%，并且修复所报告漏洞的速度与之前一样快。由于受处理公司自身发现了更多漏洞，通过漏洞赏金出售研究结果的外部研究人员在受处理公司代码中报告的漏洞数量减少了约一半，而在其他公司代码中报告的漏洞数量增加，且并未退出市场。没有证据表明攻击者利用了这些额外漏洞：披露后一个月内被利用的比例下降了约三分之二，主要原因是披露时已被利用的比例较小，而被利用的数量没有变化。前沿AI帮助防御者在攻击者利用漏洞之前发现并修复了更多漏洞，但其受限发布在不同国家的公司之间造成了软件安全的不对称。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Defenders and attackers race to find the same software flaws. I use the launch of Project Glasswing, which gave a small group of large US tech firms early access to Claude Mythos Preview, a model for finding software vulnerabilities, to study how access to frontier AI affects defenders, external researchers, and attackers. Using the universe of publicly disclosed vulnerabilities, I find that firms with access more than doubled their disclosures relative to Chinese and Russian tech firms without access, disclosed about 150 percent more high-impact vulnerabilities, and fixed reported flaws as quickly as before. As treated firms found more flaws themselves, external researchers, who sell their findings through bug bounties, reported about half as many vulnerabilities in treated firms' code and more in other firms' code, without leaving the market. There is no evidence that attackers exploited the additional vulnerabilities: the share exploited within a month of disclosure fell by about two thirds, mainly because a smaller share was already exploited when disclosed, while the number exploited did not change. Frontier AI helped defenders find and fix more flaws before attackers exploited them, but its restricted release creates an asymmetry in software security between firms in different countries.
