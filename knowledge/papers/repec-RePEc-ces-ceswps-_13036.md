<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

镇压会让异议噤声，还是会迫使人们离开？我们利用来自莱比锡的新微观数据，研究了东德和平革命期间对抗议者的逮捕。在有抗议者被捕的街道和房屋中，居民在柏林墙倒塌后更有可能搬离其住址，而且在逮捕发生后的数月内，出境签证申请有所增加。逮捕之后，附近的抗议活动以及对反对派的支持并未出现明显下降。我们的结果表明，镇压提高了退出需求，却没有使发声出现明显退缩；而一旦离开成为可能，居民便付诸行动。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Does repression silence dissent or push people to leave? We study the arrest of protesters during East Germany's Peaceful Revolution, using novel micro-data from Leipzig. Residents of streets and houses with an arrested protester were more likely to have left their address once the Berlin Wall fell, and exit visa applications rose in the months after an arrest. Nearby protest activity and support for the opposition show no visible decline after an arrest. Our results suggest that repression raised the demand for exit without a visible retreat of voice, and that residents acted on this demand once leaving became possible.

</details>
