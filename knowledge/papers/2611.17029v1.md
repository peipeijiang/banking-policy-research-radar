<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究契约结构如何影响多对多匹配中具有可替代偏好（substitutable preferences）的最坏情况和最好情况下的明显操纵。有效的个体理性机制在全可替代域（full substitutable domain）和单位域（unitary domain）上均排除了最好情况下的明显操纵。在医院选择的简单且经济自然的条件下，全域上的每一个稳定机制在两种情况下都允许明显操纵；即使在单位医生偏好下，医院提议的延迟接受机制（hospital-proposing deferred acceptance）也可能允许这两种类型。一个进一步的充分条件使得有效的稳定支配机制（stable-dominating mechanisms）在最坏情况下明显可操纵。在单位域上，医生提议的延迟接受机制（doctor-proposing deferred acceptance）不是明显可操纵的。如果每个医生—医院对至多有一个可行契约，这一正面结论可推广到每一个稳定支配机制。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how contractual structure affects worst-case and best-case obvious manipulation in many-to-many matching with substitutable preferences. Efficient individually rational mechanisms rule out best-case obvious manipulations on both the full substitutable domain and the unitary domain. Under simple and economically natural conditions on hospitals' choices, every stable mechanism on the full domain admits obvious manipulations in both cases; hospital-proposing deferred acceptance can admit both types even under unitary doctors' preferences. A further sufficient condition makes efficient stable-dominating mechanisms obviously manipulable in the worst case. On the unitary domain, doctor-proposing deferred acceptance is not obviously manipulable. If each doctor--hospital pair has at most one feasible contract, this positive conclusion extends to every stable-dominating mechanism.

</details>
