<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

策略防策略性（strategy-proofness）是可预测行为的传统标准，然而参与者往往未能采取占优策略。现有的大多数简洁性概念将这些失败归因于条件推理的成本，并通过比较占优策略的最坏情况与替代策略的最好情况来降低这一成本。我认为还存在另一种方法，它更多地利用策略集的结构，我将其形式化为邻域关系（neighbor relation）。比较相邻策略可以减少对条件推理的需求，而且即使在单一偶然情况下，它也能简化对策略集的搜索。我引入一个新的简洁性标准：如果在某个邻域关系下，反复移动到改进的邻居总能到达占优策略，则称一个机制是局部改进可解的（local improvement solvable）。策略防策略性是最粗关系下的标准，而许多经典机制在更精细的关系下满足该标准。在不存在重复占优策略的扩展式机制中，单一情境邻居（one-situation neighbors）下的标准是严格行动防策略性（strict action-proofness），它不同于显然策略防策略性（obvious strategy-proofness）。在实现相同结果的局部改进可解机制中，当局部比较显著时，该标准预测更多的占优策略行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Strategy-proofness is the traditional standard for predictable behavior, yet participants often fail to play dominant strategies. Most existing simplicity concepts attribute these failures to the cost of contingent reasoning, which they lower by comparing the worst case of the dominant strategy with the best case of an alternative. I argue that there is another approach, one that makes more use of the structure of the strategy set, which I formalize as a neighbor relation. Comparing neighboring strategies can reduce the need for contingent reasoning, and, even with a single contingency, it can simplify the search through the strategy set. I introduce a new simplicity criterion: a mechanism is local improvement solvable under a neighbor relation if repeatedly moving to an improving neighbor always reaches a dominant strategy. Strategy-proofness is the criterion under the coarsest relation, and many classical mechanisms satisfy it under finer ones. In extensive-form mechanisms without duplicate dominant strategies, the criterion under one-situation neighbors is strict action-proofness, which is distinct from obvious strategy-proofness. Among local improvement solvable mechanisms that implement the same outcomes, the criterion predicts more dominant-strategy play where the local comparison is salient.

</details>
