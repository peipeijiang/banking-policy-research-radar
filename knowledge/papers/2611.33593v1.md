像乌干达东部这样的低收入地区往往缺乏可靠的电网供电。本文介绍了索罗提综合可再生能源微电网（SIREM）的技术经济优化，这是一个用于电网接入扩展的混合可再生能源系统，索罗提地区的家庭电网接入率为16.7%。该微电网集成了带储热的聚光太阳能发电（CSP）、沼气联合循环发电以及磷酸铁锂蓄电池储能。利用Xendee优化软件和NASA POWER六年数据，最优配置包括37 MW CSP场、70 MWh熔盐储热、5 MW CSP汽轮机、6 MW沼气联合循环燃气轮机以及10 MWh电池系统。该设计年供电量为61.3 GWh，内部收益率为42.91%，投资回收期为2年，平准化度电成本为发电侧0.1991美元/kWh（负荷供电侧0.3752美元/kWh），相较仅依赖电网供电实现了32.2%的总节省，同时年出口电量为28.8 GWh，证明了混合可再生能源微电网在撒哈拉以南非洲电网扩展中的可行性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Low-income regions like eastern Uganda often lack reliable grid power. This paper presents the techno-economic optimization of the Soroti Integrated Renewable Energy Microgrid (SIREM), a hybrid renewable system for grid-access expansion where Soroti District household grid access stands at 16.7\%. The microgrid integrates concentrating solar power (CSP) with thermal storage, biogas combined cycle generation, and lithium iron phosphate battery storage. Using Xendee optimization software with six years of NASA POWER data, the optimal configuration comprises a 37 MW CSP field, 70 MWh molten-salt storage, a 5 MW CSP turbine, a 6 MW biogas CCGT, and a 10 MWh battery system. The design delivers 61.3 GWh annually with a 42.91% IRR, 2-year payback, and levelized cost of electricity of 0.1991 USD/kWh generation (0.3752 USD/kWh load-served), achieving 32.2% total savings versus grid-only supply while exporting 28.8 GWh annually, demonstrating the viability of hybrid renewable microgrids for grid expansion in sub-Saharan Africa.
