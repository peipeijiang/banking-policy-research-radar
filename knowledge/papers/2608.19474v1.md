<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一位委托人筛选一位其技术具有最小有效规模的代理人，因此斯彭斯-米尔利斯条件在一条单调分界曲线上失效：该曲线是每种类型对边际产出赋予相同价值的轨迹。对于该曲线与松弛解均为严格单调的类别，最优契约遵循一种三分法，其由两者如何相交所决定：当它们永不相交时跳跃不可能发生；当分界曲线平坦时跳跃不可避免；当分界曲线严格递增时跳跃则是一种选择。最优解是被找到的，而非猜测的：通过一个显式权重对约束族进行对偶化，每个解都被证明在所有可实施的配置（无论是确定性的还是随机的）中全局最优；这些证明既不需要线性原始条件，也不需要对契约形状施加任何限制。在温和的正则性条件下，该类别恰好包含四十种配置；每一种都被映射到其强制形状，以闭式解出，并得到验证。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A principal screens an agent whose technology has a minimum efficient scale, so the Spence-Mirrlees condition fails along a monotone dividing curve: the locus at which every type values marginal output equally. For the class in which this curve and the relaxed solution are both strictly monotone, the optimal contract obeys a trichotomy, governed by how the two meet: a jump is impossible when they never meet, unavoidable across a flat dividing curve, a choice across a strictly increasing one. The optimum is found, not conjectured: each solution is certified as globally optimal among all implementable allocations, deterministic or random, by dualizing the family of binding constraints through an explicit weight; the certificates require neither linear primitives nor any restriction on the shape of the contract. Under mild regularity the class comprises exactly forty configurations; each is mapped to its forced shape, solved in closed form, and certified.
