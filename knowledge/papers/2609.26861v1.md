# Rule-Based Pricing Algorithms and Market Outcomes: An Experimental Study

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26861v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过受控市场实验研究基于规则的定价算法设计（价格战警告、预设策略、LLM建议）如何影响序贯Bertrand博弈中的市场价格，发现多数处理提高价格，对竞争政策和平台监管有启示。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

基于规则的定价工具在数字商务中广泛存在，但我们对其设计如何塑造市场结果知之甚少。在一项受控市场实验中，参与者使用仪表盘构建定价算法，在多个周期的序贯伯特兰博弈（sequential Bertrand game）中展开竞争。我们改变了商业重新定价工具中常见的设计特征：关于价格战的警告、预配置策略以及来自大型语言模型（large language model）的建议。大多数处理变体都提高了市场价格，其效应由起始价格上升和更具合作性的算法设计所驱动。这些结果对竞争政策、平台监管以及当前关于监管算法设计工具的讨论具有重要意义。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Rule-based pricing tools are widespread in digital commerce, yet we know little about how their design shapes market outcomes. In a controlled market experiment, participants use dashboards to build pricing algorithms competing in a sequential Bertrand game over multiple periods. We vary design features commonly found in commercial repricing tools: warnings about price wars, pre-configured strategies, and advice from a large language model. Most treatment variations raise market prices with effects driven by an increase in starting prices and more cooperative algorithm designs. The results matter for competition policy, platform regulation and current discussions on regulating algorithm design tools.
