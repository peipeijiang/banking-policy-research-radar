<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们为GMM模型开发了Neyman正交得分（Neyman orthogonal scores），这些得分在矩条件可能不成立时仍然有效。现有的得分构造通常要求矩条件精确成立，这在过度识别模型中可能不成立，即矩函数的数量超过参数的数量。我们构造了一种具有闭式解的\emph{误设定稳健}（misspecification-robust）正交得分；它是传统正交得分加上调整项，调整项涉及雅可比矩阵和权重矩阵的估计，与总体矩成比例，在正确设定下消失。矩函数可能依赖于非参数或机器学习的第一步估计，我们证明双重/去偏机器学习（double/debiased machine learning, DML）结合所提出的得分和交叉拟合（cross-fitting）为低维GMM估计量提供一致有效的推断，而传统的DML标准误在矩条件不成立时是不一致的。我们在部分线性工具变量（partially linear instrumental variables, PLIV）模型和具有多个工具变量的局部平均处理效应（local average treatment effects, LATE）模型中说明了这些结果。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop Neyman orthogonal scores for GMM models that remain valid when the moment conditions may fail to hold. Existing score constructions typically require the moment conditions to hold exactly, which may fail in over-identified models, where the number of moment functions exceeds the number of parameters. We construct \emph{misspecification-robust} orthogonal score that has a closed form; it is the conventional orthogonal score plus adjustment terms, for the estimation of the Jacobian and of the weighting matrix, which are proportional to the population moment and vanish under correct specification. The moment functions may depend on nonparametric or machine learning first steps, and we show that double/debiased machine learning (DML) combined with the proposed scores and cross-fitting provides uniformly valid inference for a low-dimensional GMM estimand, while the conventional DML standard errors are inconsistent when the moment conditions fail. We illustrate the results in the partially linear instrumental variables (PLIV) model and in the local average treatment effects (LATE) model with multiple instruments.
