# Moral AI and the Erosion of Its Own Demand

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25213v1)

> 论文用演化博弈模型研究道德AI（可承诺合作）与无约束AI的采用动态，发现道德AI虽能维持合作，却使条件合作者被自私个体利用而在演化中减少，最终导致其采用率收敛至零，呈现自我消解特性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了当个体可以在排除背叛的道德人工智能（moral AI）与保留自由裁量权的无约束人工智能（unconstrained AI）之间进行选择时，人类偏好频率与人工智能采纳的演化。通过允许用户承诺合作，道德人工智能即使在合作偏好过于稀缺而无法自行维持合作的情况下也能维持合作。但这种成功是有代价的。通过将原本休眠的合作偏好转化为承诺行为，道德人工智能使其用户暴露于自私个体的剥削之下，导致条件合作者（conditional cooperators）的种群份额在演化选择下下降。随着条件合作者变得越来越不普遍，均衡采纳最终收敛至零。因此，道德人工智能可以是自我消解的。对于介于道德人工智能采纳临界值与无人工智能时合作阈值之间的初始份额，道德人工智能在产生合作的同时降低了条件合作者的极限份额。采纳最终消失，留下最初维持承诺需求的那类偏好的更低份额。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the evolution of human preference frequencies and AI adoption when individuals can choose between a moral AI that rules out defection and an unconstrained AI that preserves discretion. By allowing users to commit to cooperation, moral AI can sustain cooperation even when cooperative preferences are too scarce to do so on their own. But this success has a cost. By turning otherwise dormant cooperative preferences into committed behavior, moral AI exposes its users to exploitation by selfish individuals, causing the population share of conditional cooperators to decline under evolutionary selection. As conditional cooperators become less prevalent, equilibrium adoption eventually converges to zero. Moral AI can therefore be self-liquidating. For initial shares between the moral-AI adoption cutoff and the threshold for cooperation without AI, moral AI generates cooperation while lowering the limiting share of conditional cooperators. Adoption ultimately vanishes, leaving a lower share of the preference type that initially sustained demand for commitment.
