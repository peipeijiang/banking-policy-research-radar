<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究当产能可缔约但后续产出不可缔约时的垄断规制问题。企业在安装高成本产能之前私下观察到其生产成本，随后选择产出，而规制者不能使用转移支付。我们给出了充分条件，在这些条件下，单一产能下限能够实施一个具有完全参与的最优机制。该下限通过改变投资来提高产出，同时在安装成本沉没之后将利用率留给企业决定。在附加条件下，配置具有三个区域：自由放任、产能下限的充分利用以及利用不足。高成本类型明知会安装其不会完全使用的产能，但其产量仍高于自由放任下的水平。这种物理浪费是最优规制扩大服务所必需的一种恶。我们还表明，在具有产能 contingent 补贴或统一进入费的最优规制中，利用不足可能持续存在。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study monopoly regulation when capacity is contractible but subsequent output is not. A firm privately observes its production cost before installing costly capacity and then choosing output, while the regulator cannot use transfers. We provide sufficient conditions under which a single capacity floor implements an optimal mechanism with full participation. The floor raises output by changing investment while leaving utilization to the firm after installation costs are sunk. Under additional conditions, the allocation has three regions: laissez-faire, full utilization of the floor, and underutilization. High-cost types knowingly install capacity they will not fully use, yet produce more than under laissez-faire. This physical waste is a necessary evil of optimal regulation that expands service. We also show that underutilization can persist in optimal regulation with capacity-contingent subsidies or a common entry fee.

</details>
