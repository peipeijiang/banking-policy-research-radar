<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了会议中的共识形成与信息获取问题，其中DeGroot型意见更新受到基于Kullback-Leibler散度（Kullback-Leibler divergence）的接受规则的约束。参与者在审议之前选择私人信息实验，这些选择决定了他们的后验分布和接受容忍度。我们建立了在特定会议结构下实现全局共识的充分条件，包括由完全接受的调解人组织的重复双边会议。在调解人会议成本全额报销的情况下，信息获取博弈具有加权势函数，并存在纯策略纳什均衡。我们开发了一种数值方法，利用连续后验域上经过验证的收益界来认证近似均衡，并评估在保持给定均衡剖面下部分报销政策的效力。二元状态、三人参与的示例说明了会议顺序如何与状态方差相互作用。在测试范围内较大的方差下，将要求苛刻的非调解人安排在最后与较低的会议成本和较低的经认证的规划者充分报销相关，但调解人的信息获取支出更高。这些发现揭示了规划者财政支出与调解人私人负担之间的权衡，并表明会议结构在经济上具有重要意义。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study consensus formation and information acquisition in conferences where DeGroot-type opinion updating is constrained by an acceptance rule based on Kullback-Leibler divergence. Players choose private information experiments before deliberation, and these choices determine both their posterior distributions and acceptance tolerances. We establish sufficient conditions for global consensus under specified conference structures, including repeated bilateral conferences organized by a fully receptive mediator. Under full reimbursement of the mediator's conference costs, the information-acquisition game has a weighted potential and admits a pure-strategy Nash equilibrium. We develop a numerical procedure using verified payoff bounds over the continuous posterior domain to certify approximate equilibria and assess partial reimbursement policies that preserve a given equilibrium profile. The binary state, three-player examples illustrate how conference order interacts with state variance. At larger variances in the tested range, placing the demanding nonmediator last is associated with lower conference costs and lower certified sufficient planner reimbursement, but greater information acquisition expenditure by the mediator. These findings reveal a trade-off between the planner's fiscal expenditure and the mediator's private burden, and show that the conference structure matters economically.
