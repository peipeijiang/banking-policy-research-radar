# Floods and the Duplication of Production: Evidence from the Global Car Industry

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01329v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用工厂层面数据和交错双重差分设计，发现跨国车企在洪水后倾向于在海外复制发动机生产而非迁出受淹工厂，且这种冗余与保险成本和公共防洪设施存在替代关系。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

洪水正变得更加频繁，并可能扰乱全球供应链。我们研究跨国汽车制造商如何调整整车和关键零部件生产以应对洪水风险，以及它们的选择如何与保险和公共防洪设施相互作用。利用工厂层面数据和经人工核实的洪水记录，采用交错双重差分设计，我们发现汽车制造商选择复制生产，而非将生产迁离受洪水影响的工厂。即使在洪水发生五年多之后，一个发动机在两个或更多工厂生产的概率仍高出19个百分点，这些工厂通常位于海外，且为附近组装的汽车供货，而受洪水影响工厂的产出除洪水当年的暂时损失外，并未出现持续下降。企业仅在调整成本较低时进行生产复制，即当其海外工厂已经生产同一发动机系列时。受洪水影响的车型版本仅被暂时复制，变速箱项目则未被复制。有提示性证据表明，在保险可能昂贵且工厂缺乏防洪设施的地区，企业进行更多生产复制，这指向冗余生产、保险与公共保护之间的替代关系。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Floods are becoming more frequent and can disrupt global supply chains. We study how multinational car makers adapt vehicle and key parts production to flood risk, and how their choices interact with insurance and public flood defences. Using plant-level data and a manually verified flood record in a staggered difference-in-differences design, we find that car makers duplicate production rather than move it away from flooded plants. Even more than five years after a flood, an engine is 19 percentage points more likely to be made in two or more plants, usually abroad and for cars assembled nearby, while the flooded plant's output shows no persistent decline beyond a temporary loss in the flood year. Firms duplicate only where adjustment costs are low, i.e. when a plant of theirs abroad already makes the same engine family. Flooded vehicle model versions are duplicated only temporarily, and transmission programs are not duplicated. Suggestive evidence that firms duplicate more where insurance is likely expensive and plants lack flood defences points to substitution between redundancy, insurance and public protection.
