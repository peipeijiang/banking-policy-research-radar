# AI and Its Impact on Creativity and Diversity: An Empirical Study of LLM-Generated Product Ideas

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究探讨了大型语言模型（LLMs）在为大学生生成价格低于50美元的新产品创意方面的表现。通过一系列研究，我们识别了使用LLMs进行产品创新的关键优势与不足。第一项研究表明，基于购买意愿，LLM生成的产品创意在平均质量上高于人类创意，并且进入前10%排名的可能性是人类的7倍。第二项研究表明，这种由人工智能引发的创造力提升并不能归因于LLM更具说服力的推销技巧。第三和第四项研究揭示了使用LLMs进行头脑风暴的一个弱点：人工智能生成的创意在单个创意层面新颖性较低，在创意集合层面多样性不足。在第五项研究中，我们分析了以往基于LLM的创造力研究，发现所有研究中的创意多样性均持续偏低，这证明了上述发现的普适性。第六和第七项研究探讨了缓解这种多样性损失的技巧。我们比较了来自不同供应商和版本的LLMs，发现较新的模型能生成更多样化的创意，但仍未达到人类水平的多样性。我们还展示了能够将创意多样性提升至接近人类创意生成水平的技巧：跨供应商汇集创意；提示工程，包括思维链提示和注入异质化角色或约束；以及广泛探索解决方案空间以恢复多样性的创造性智能体。最后，在第八项研究中，我们表明利用人工智能创意生成近乎零的边际成本，通过扩大创意数量可以稳步提升对创意空间的覆盖度，接近人类水平的覆盖度。最后，我们为希望在LLMs帮助下识别更优新产品创意的创新管理者提出了可操作的建议。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This research examines how well large language models, or LLMs, generate new product ideas for college students priced under $50. Across a series of studies, we identify key strengths and weaknesses of using LLMs for product innovation. Our first study shows that LLM-generated product ideas have higher average quality than human ideas, based on purchase intent, and are 7 times more likely to rank in the top 10%. Our second study shows that this AI-induced creativity boost is not explained by the LLM's more persuasive pitching skills. Our third and fourth studies identify a weakness of using LLMs for brainstorming: AI-generated ideas are less novel at the idea level and less diverse at the set level. In our fifth study, we analyze prior LLM-based creativity studies and find consistently lower idea diversity across all of them, demonstrating the generalizability of these findings. Our sixth and seventh studies investigate techniques to mitigate this diversity loss. We compare LLMs from different vendors and versions and find that more recent models generate more diverse ideas, though they still fall short of human-level diversity. We also demonstrate techniques that increase idea diversity almost to the level of human idea generation: pooling ideas across vendors; prompt engineering, including Chain-of-Thought prompting and injecting heterogeneous personas or constraints; and creative agents that broadly explore the solution landscape to restore diversity. Finally, in our eighth study, we show that exploiting the near-zero marginal cost of AI idea generation by scaling the number of ideas steadily improves coverage of the idea space, approaching human-level coverage. We conclude by presenting actionable recommendations for innovation managers who want to identify better new product ideas with the help of LLMs.
