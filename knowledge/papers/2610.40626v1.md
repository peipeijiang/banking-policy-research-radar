# Finding Competitive Equilibria in Online Marketplaces with Platform Fees, Taxes, and Subsidies

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40626v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究多单位交易网络中的均衡计算问题，其中估值满足广义完全替代性，并带有代理人和交易特定的分段线性转移函数。这些转移函数刻画了在线市场中的平台费用、税收、补贴和会员折扣。我们的升价过程以不同速率提高价格，并在有限次迭代内达到最小均衡价格向量，在给定的预言机和算术模型下，每次迭代均可在强多项式时间内实现。随后可以高效地恢复均衡分配。该方法将用于升价拍卖的原始-对偶技术扩展到通过子模流公式处理买卖之间的互补性。在具有替代品和互补品的单卖家市场中，我们的框架还将 Sun 和 Yang (2009) 以及 Shioura 和 Yang (2015) 的双轨拍卖扩展到竞标者特定和商品特定的转移。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study equilibrium computation in multi-unit trading networks with valuations satisfying generalized full substitutes and agent- and transaction-specific piecewise-linear transfer functions. These transfers model platform fees, taxes, subsidies, and membership discounts in online marketplaces. Our ascending procedure raises prices at different rates and reaches the minimum equilibrium price vector in finitely many iterations, each implementable in strongly polynomial time under our oracle and arithmetic models. An equilibrium allocation can then be recovered efficiently. The method extends primal-dual techniques developed for ascending auctions to handle complementarities between purchases and sales through a submodular flow formulation. In single-seller markets with substitutes and complements, our framework also extends the double-track auctions of Sun and Yang (2009) and Shioura and Yang (2015) to bidder- and good-specific transfers.

</details>
