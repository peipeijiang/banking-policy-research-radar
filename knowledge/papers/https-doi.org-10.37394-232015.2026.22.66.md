<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究旨在探讨电子人力资源管理（EHRM）是否影响在安曼证券交易所上市商业银行员工的岗位满意度，以及领导风格是否在电子人力资源管理对员工满意度的影响中起中介作用。研究编制了问卷，并对约旦多家商业银行的员工样本进行了自填式问卷调查。采用回归方法进行假设检验，结果表明：电子人力资源管理对员工岗位满意度及领导风格均具有显著的直接影响。此外，研究结果还显示，领导风格显著影响员工满意度，并且在电子人力资源管理对员工满意度的影响中起完全中介作用。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The study aims to determine whether EHRM affects the satisfaction of employees with their jobs at the listed commercial banks at the Amman Stock Exchange, and whether leadership styles mediate the impact of EHRM on employee satisfaction. A questionnaire was developed and self-administered to a sample of employees of different commercial banks of Jordan. Employing the regression methods in hypotheses testing, the results showed that e-human resource management has a direct significant impact on employee-job satisfaction, and on leadership styles. In addition, the results demonstrate that leadership styles significantly affect employee satisfaction, and leadership styles have a complete mediating relationship in the impact of e-human resource management on employee satisfaction.
