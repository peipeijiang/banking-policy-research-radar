<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文基于多项式保持（polynomial preservation）建立了一个用于波动率建模与衍生品定价的离散时间框架。该框架通过有限维矩阵表示，提供了任意阶精确矩以及方差与收益过程在任意有限期限内的序列交叉矩。在保持这种解析可处理性的同时，该框架能够容纳灵活的杠杆效应和方差之方差（variance-of-variance）动态以及非高斯新息。我们引入了两类新颖的随机波动率模型，它们嵌套了广泛使用的离散时间设定，并研究了其统计性质。在两种不同的标度机制下，我们建立了向多项式扩散（polynomial diffusions）和跳跃-扩散过程的弱收敛性以及多项式矩的收敛性，从而将离散时间矩递推与连续时间矩阵指数公式联系起来。对S\&P~500收益率以及涵盖1996--2023年的大规模期权面板的实证分析表明，相较于其嵌套基准模型，所提出的设定改善了收益率拟合，并将周度隐含波动率均方根误差平均降低约8--41\%，具体幅度取决于方差动态和新息分布。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops a discrete-time framework for volatility modelling and derivative pricing based on polynomial preservation. The framework provides exact moments of arbitrary order and serial cross-moments of variance and return processes over any finite horizon through finite-dimensional matrix representations. This analytical tractability is maintained while accommodating flexible leverage and variance-of-variance dynamics and non-Gaussian innovations. We introduce two novel classes of stochastic volatility models that nest widely used discrete-time specifications and study their statistical properties. Under two distinct scaling regimes, we establish weak convergence to polynomial diffusions and jump-diffusions and convergence of polynomial moments, linking discrete-time moment recursions to continuous-time matrix-exponential formulas. An empirical analysis of S\&P~500 returns and a large panel of options spanning 1996--2023 shows that the proposed specifications improve return fit and reduce mean weekly implied volatility root mean squared errors by approximately 8--41\% relative to their nested benchmarks, depending on the variance dynamics and innovation distribution.
