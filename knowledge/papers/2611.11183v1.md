<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

网络形成的参数模型（如二元对数几率模型，dyadic logit models）日益流行，但检验其设定是否正确的工具却很少。本文基于这类模型赋予子图（即小群体代理人之间连接的模式）的概率，提出了一种针对条件独立二元组模型（conditionally independent dyad models, CID）的设定检验。给定代理人的特征，参数化CID模型不仅决定每条连接的概率，还决定一组代理人形成三角形、星形或任何其他子图的概率。在正确设定下，具有给定特征的代理人中各子图的期望频率等于模型所隐含的概率。这构成了可检验的条件矩限制的基础。检验统计量是这些概率的非参数核估计与估计的参数模型所隐含概率之间的加权积分平方距离。我推导了其在原假设下的渐近分布。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Parametric models of network formation, such as dyadic logit models, are increasingly popular, but few tools exist to test their specification. This paper develops a specification test for conditionally independent dyad (CID) models based on the probabilities that these models assign to subgraphs, the patterns of links among small groups of agents. Given the agents' characteristics, a parametric CID model determines not only the probability of each link but also the probability that a group of agents forms a triangle, a star, or any other subgraph. Under correct specification, the expected frequency of each subgraph among agents with given characteristics equals its model-implied probability. This forms the basis of the testable conditional moment restrictions. The test statistic is the weighted integrated squared distance between a nonparametric kernel estimate of these probabilities and the probabilities implied by the estimated parametric model. I derive its asymptotic distribution under the null hypothesis.

</details>
