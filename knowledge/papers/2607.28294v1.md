<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文针对在固定日历跨度内观测到的自回归条件持续时间（ACD）模型，发展了自助法（bootstrap）推断，此时持续时间数量是随机的。我们研究了两种递归方案：一种固定日历跨度，另一种固定已实现的事件计数。对于固定计数自助法，当持续时间尾部指数满足 \(\kappa\geq1\) 时，我们建立了其一致性。当 \(0<\kappa<1\) 时，经典一致性失效，因为估计量具有混合正态极限，但自助法能够再现其条件高斯分量。因此，基本百分位区间仍具有一阶有效性，且自助法 \(t\) 统计量渐近服从标准正态分布。蒙特卡洛实验表明，在有限均值与无限均值情形下，该方法均能提供准确的有限样本推断，并且对非指数创新具有稳健性。将所提方法应用于加密货币ETF交易持续时间，发现了较强的持续性，并展示了固定计数推断与随机计数推断在实际中的差异。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops bootstrap inference for autoregressive conditional duration (ACD) models observed over a fixed calendar span, so that the number of durations is random. We study recursive schemes that either fix the calendar span or the realized event count. For the fixed-count bootstrap, we establish consistency when the duration tail index satisfies $κ\geq1$. When $0<κ<1$, classical consistency fails because the estimator has a mixed-normal limit, but the bootstrap reproduces its conditional Gaussian component. Consequently, basic percentile intervals remain first-order valid and bootstrap $t$-statistics are asymptotically standard normal. Monte Carlo experiments show accurate finite-sample inference across finite- and infinite-mean regimes and robustness to non-exponential innovations. An application to cryptocurrency ETF transaction durations finds strong persistence and illustrates the practical difference between fixed-count and random-count inference.
