# The Price of Permission: Classification Uncertainty in Constrained Capital Markets

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12634v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文通过伊斯兰股票筛选规则的不确定性和边界变化，研究股票分类不确定性对价格的影响，发现官方许可在特定市场有价格效应，但总体证据不稳健。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

符合伊斯兰教法的股票筛选提供了一个透明的环境，其中制度规则决定谁可以持有某只股票。二元标签标识当前的资格，但并未说明可行的投资者基础是否在不同标准之间分散，或是否接近变化。我们将这种不稳定性定义为分类不确定性，并通过允许的投资者规模将其投资者基础后果形式化。在1999年至2024年的CRSP-Compustat面板数据中，涵盖13，188只证券，依据七套研究者模拟的伊斯兰教法规则手册进行分类，筛选规则的分歧及与活跃边界的接近程度可预测下个月的筛选隐含转换。美国Fama-MacBeth诊断不支持无条件等权重的许可溢价，且2023年9月DJIM/S&P方法变更未产生稳健的匹配重定价。核心事件证据使用马来西亚证券委员会25份官方名单。在先前审查前已交易的410只纳入股票，其匹配收益为正但不精确。应用事件前换手率下限后，295只纳入股票在$[0,10]$个交易日内产生1.76个百分点的超额收益（$p_{\mathrm{date}}=0.008$；$p_{\mathrm{wild}}=0.017$），在$[0,20]$个交易日内产生2.25个百分点的超额收益（$p_{\mathrm{date}}=0.018$；$p_{\mathrm{wild}}=0.035$）。留一日法、仅首次纳入及审查中期安慰剂检验均支持结果，尽管联合20日事件前检验被拒绝。所有权和需求压力诊断未识别出唯一的边际买家或干净的需求冲击。证据支持将分类风险视为投资组合监控状态。官方伊斯兰教法许可在公认的本地市场中，对足够可交易的证券产生价格效应；仅正式资格本身并不充分。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Shariah-compliant equity screening provides a transparent setting in which institutional rules determine who may own a stock. A binary label identifies current eligibility but not whether the feasible investor base is fragmented across standards or close to changing. We define this instability as classification uncertainty and formalize its investor-base consequence through permitted investor mass. In a 1999-2024 CRSP-Compustat panel of 13,188 securities classified under seven researcher-emulated Shariah rulebooks, screening-rule disagreement and proximity to active boundaries rank next-month screen-implied transitions. U.S. Fama-MacBeth diagnostics do not support an unconditional equal-weighted permission premium, and a September 2023 DJIM/S&P methodology change produces no robust matched repricing. The central event evidence uses 25 official Securities Commission Malaysia lists. The 410 inclusions already trading before the preceding review have positive but imprecise matched returns. Applying the pre-event turnover floor yields 295 inclusions with 1.76 percentage points over $[0,10]$ trading days ($p_{\mathrm{date}}=0.008$; $p_{\mathrm{wild}}=0.017$) and 2.25 points over $[0,20]$ ($p_{\mathrm{date}}=0.018$; $p_{\mathrm{wild}}=0.035$). Leave-one-date-out, first-inclusion-only, and mid-review placebo checks are supportive, although a joint 20-day pre-event test rejects. Ownership and demand-pressure diagnostics do not identify a unique marginal buyer or clean causal demand shock. The evidence supports treating classification risk as a portfolio-monitoring state. Official Shariah permission is associated with price effects in a recognized local market among sufficiently tradable securities; formal eligibility alone is insufficient.
