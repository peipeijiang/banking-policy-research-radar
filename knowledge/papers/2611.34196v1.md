<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究了一个通过选择每个用户所观察的帖子来影响集体行动的平台。每个用户希望采取的行动接近其他人的行动，并接近由其私人类型和总体状态决定的理想行动。帖子是非策略性的，但与其作者的类型相关，因此每个帖子都携带关于状态及其作者的信息。我们以闭式形式刻画了唯一的贝叶斯-纳什均衡。两个显式网络给出了平台所能诱导的最小和最大集体行动。此外，集体行动仅通过读者数量依赖于网络。因此，许多网络诱导相同的集体行动，但它们可以诱导不同的个体行动。这些网络对监管者而言可能不同，我们通过福利、极化和跨类型暴露对它们进行比较。特别地，完全网络未必最大化福利，隔离网络未必最大化极化。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies a platform that influences collective action by selecting which posts each user observes. Each user would like to take an action close to those of the others and to an ideal action determined by her private type and an aggregate state. Posts are non-strategic but correlated with the types of their authors, so each post carries information about the state and its author. We characterise the unique Bayes-Nash equilibrium in closed form. Two explicit networks give the minimum and maximum collective action the platform can induce. Moreover, collective action depends on the network only through the numbers of readers. Hence, many networks induce the same collective action, but they can induce different individual actions. These networks may differ for a regulator, and we compare them by welfare, polarisation and cross-type exposure. In particular, the complete network need not maximise welfare, and the segregated network need not maximise polarisation.
