<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

纳什均衡与可理性化位于信息极端的两端：前者假定每个参与人知道所有对手的策略，后者假定参与人仅从理性的共同知识出发进行推理。当可见性是部分且不对称时，我们研究由有向\emph{观察网络}$G$所诱导的解概念，其中一条边$i\to j$表示$i$观察到$j$的策略。如果一个策略组合集合中的每个组合都能对每个参与人而言，由来自同一集合且与该参与人所观察到的一切相一致的猜测所证成，则称该集合是\emph{自维持的}。我们考虑\emph{点}猜测（单一组合）和\emph{信念}猜测（组合上的分布）。两者从完全网络上的纳什均衡分别过渡到空网络上的点可理性化和相关可理性化，并随着连边的增加而单调收缩。
  网络拓扑同时支配着行为与复杂性。在空网络上可以在多项式时间内找到一个预测组合，而仅一条连边就使搜索变为$\mathsf{PPAD}$-困难的：在单侧双人监督下，被观察者的策略恰好是她的纳什均衡策略。在层级网络（传递锦标赛）上，成员资格问题递归地归约为更小的博弈，并允许多项式大小的证书，从而将验证置于$\exists\mathbb{R}$中，而对于两人博弈则置于$\mathsf{P}$中。然而在有向三环上，迭代消去在两种变体下都可能永不终止，且在点猜测下，内部预测可能只允许无限见证集，而信念猜测在同一博弈中避免了这一病态现象。最后，当仅观察到已实现的纯行动时，解集保证非空恰好当网络没有边时成立，并可在$O(n|A|^2)$时间内计算。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Nash equilibrium and rationalizability sit at opposite informational extremes: the former presumes that each player knows all opponents' strategies, the latter that players reason from common knowledge of rationality alone. When visibility is partial and asymmetric, we study the solution concepts induced by a directed \emph{observation network} $G$ in which an edge $i\to j$ means that $i$ observes $j$'s strategy. A set of profiles is \emph{self-sustaining} if every profile in it can be justified, for every player, by conjectures from the same set that agree with everything the player observes. We consider \emph{point} conjectures (single profiles) and \emph{belief} conjectures (distributions over profiles). Both range from Nash equilibrium on the complete network to point- and correlated rationalizability on the empty network, and shrink monotonically as links are added.
  Network topology governs both behavior and complexity. A predicted profile can be found in polynomial time on the empty network, whereas a single link makes search $\mathsf{PPAD}$-hard: under one-sided two-player monitoring, the observed player's strategies are exactly her Nash equilibrium strategies. On hierarchical networks (transitive tournaments), membership reduces recursively to smaller games and admits polynomial-size certificates, placing verification in $\exists\mathbb{R}$, and in $\mathsf{P}$ for two players. On the directed three-cycle, however, iterated elimination may never terminate under either variant, and under point conjectures interior predictions may admit only infinite witness sets, a pathology that belief conjectures avoid in the same game. Finally, when only realized pure actions are observed, the solution set is guaranteed to be non-empty exactly when the network has no edges, and is computable in time $O(n|A|^2)$.
