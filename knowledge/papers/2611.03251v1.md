<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究Shapley-Scarf住房市场（Shapley and Scarf, 1974），其中住房按类型划分，代理人对于同一类型的住房无差异，但对各类型进行严格排序。在这一客观无差异域上，采用固定打破平局规则的顶级交易循环（top trading cycles, TTC）规则重新获得了帕累托效率与群体策略防伪性（Sandholtz and Tai, 2026），而这些性质在一般弱偏好下未必成立。我们追问Ma（1994）关于严格偏好下TTC规则的特征刻画，以及Ehlers（2014）关于弱偏好下采用固定打破平局规则的TTC规则的特征刻画，是否能够推广到该域。答案是否定的。我们以五个代理人和两种住房类型构造了一个规则，该规则满足个体理性、帕累托效率、群体策略防伪性、非专断性和一致性，但其在五代理人群体上的限制与任何采用固定打破平局规则的TTC规则在福利上不等价。在两种类型下，五个代理人是该固定人口结果的最小规模。同一规则还表明，严格偏好下TTC的其他若干特征刻画同样不能推广。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study Shapley-Scarf housing markets (Shapley and Scarf, 1974) in which houses come in types and agents are indifferent between houses of the same type but rank the types strictly. On this domain of objective indifferences, top trading cycles (TTC) rules with fixed tie-breaking regain Pareto efficiency and group strategy-proofness (Sandholtz and Tai, 2026), properties that need not hold under general weak preferences. We ask whether the characterization of the TTC rule for strict preferences by Ma (1994) and the characterization of TTC rules with fixed tie-breaking for weak preferences by Ehlers (2014) extend to this domain. They do not. With five agents and two types of houses, we construct a rule that satisfies individual rationality, Pareto efficiency, group strategy-proofness, non-bossiness, and consistency, but whose restriction to the five-agent population is not welfare equivalent to any TTC rule with fixed tie-breaking. With two types, five agents are minimal for this fixed-population result. The same rule shows that several other characterizations of TTC for strict preferences do not extend either.

</details>
