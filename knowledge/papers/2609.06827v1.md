<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

异质性主体新凯恩斯（HANK）模型刻画了整体横截面分布如何响应结构性冲击。传统的代表性主体模型通常通过总量向量自回归（VARs）的脉冲响应来进行约束校准。HANK模型缺乏可比的经验基准，因为它们不仅对总量做出预测，还对微观层面数据的分布做出预测。我们提出了一种贝叶斯基准方法，该方法联合建模宏观经济总量与来自重复横截面的若干边际分布，包括在不同调查中观测到的分布。我们的方法既可以在宏观经济总量上采用标准的结构性VAR识别方法，也可以对微观层面数据施加识别约束。该模型能够给出冲击分布效应的联合后验分布，而无需家庭面板数据或单独的第一阶段密度估计。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Heterogeneous-agent New Keynesian (HANK) models characterize how entire cross-sectional distributions respond to structural shocks. Traditional representative-agent models are routinely disciplined by impulse responses from aggregate vector autoregressions (VARs). HANK models have no comparable established empirical benchmark because they make predictions not only about aggregates, but also about distributions of micro-level data. We propose a Bayesian benchmark that jointly models macroeconomic aggregates and several marginal distributions from repeated cross sections, including distributions observed in different surveys. Our approach can use both standard structural VAR identification approaches on macroeconomic aggregates and identification restrictions imposed on micro-level data. The model delivers a joint posterior of the distributional effects of shocks, without the need for household panel data or a separate first-stage density estimate.
