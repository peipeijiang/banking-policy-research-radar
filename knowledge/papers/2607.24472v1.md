<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一个用于自动去偏机器学习（DML）的识别与估计的一般框架，其中目标参数 $θ_0$ 通过一个涉及可能高维的 nuisance 参数 $γ_0$ 的矩条件来识别。DML 利用机器学习估计 $γ_0$，同时纠正可能传导至 $θ_0$ 有偏估计的正则化偏差和过拟合偏差。我们建立了 DML 核心的 Riesz 表示子 $α_0$ 被识别的条件，并表明当且仅当 $α_0$ 唯一地优化一个二次泛函时，识别得以实现。这一特征刻画使我们能够为 $α_0$ 开发一个通用的估计程序，该程序允许处理一般的 $γ_0$，包括由内生性模型定义的 $γ_0$，并涵盖经典筛法和深度神经网络等现代架构。为了提高估计精度并缓解维数灾难，我们将对 $γ_0$ 的形状约束嵌入到可能非线性的参数空间中。我们通过模拟和实证应用展示了所提出的估计程序。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a general framework of identification and estimation for automatic debiased machine learning (DML) where the parameter of interest $θ_0$ is identified by a moment condition involving a nuisance $γ_0$ that may be high dimensional. DML leverages machine learning to estimate $γ_0$ while correcting for regularization and overfitting biases that may otherwise transmit to biased estimation of $θ_0$. We establish conditions under which the Riesz representer $α_0$, which is at the core of DML, is identified, and show that the identification occurs precisely when $α_0$ uniquely optimizes a quadratic functional. This characterization enables us to develop a general estimation procedure for $α_0$ that allows for generic $γ_0$ including those defined by models with endogeneity and encompasses both classical sieves and modern architectures such as deep neural networks. To improve estimation precision and mitigate the curse of dimensionality, we incorporate shape constraints on $γ_0$ by embedding them into a possibly nonlinear parameter space. We illustrate our estimation procedure through simulations and empirical applications.
