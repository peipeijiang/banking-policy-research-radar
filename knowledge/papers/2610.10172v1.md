# Price Discrimination and Revealed Consumer Welfare

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10172v1)

> 论文用显示偏好方法研究垄断厂商市场分割导致的价格歧视对消费者剩余的福利影响，在未知需求和厂商信息的情况下给出可观测数据的可理性化条件及消费者剩余变化的尖锐界。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们采用显示偏好方法来研究价格歧视的福利效应。分析者观察垄断者细分市场前后的价格和数量数据。分析者既不知道总需求曲线，也不知道垄断者可获得的信息。我们刻画了可由某种市场细分合理化的可观测价格-数量对的集合。然后，我们得到了由某种细分引起的消费者剩余变化的尖锐界。最后，我们给出了数据能够识别消费者剩余明确增加（减少）的条件。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We take a revealed-preference approach to study the welfare effects of price discrimination. An analyst observes price and quantity data before and after a monopolist segments the market. The analyst knows neither the aggregate demand curve nor the information available to the monopolist. We characterize the set of observable price-quantity pairs that are rationalizable by some market segmentation. We then obtain sharp bounds on the change in consumer surplus arising from some segmentation. Finally, we provide conditions under which the data identifies an unambiguous increase (decrease) in consumer surplus.

</details>
