# Modelling and Calibration of Climate-Related Credit Risk Under the Emissions Trading System

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

排放交易体系（Emission Trading Systems, ETSs）对全球排放的定价份额日益增长，其中一些体系的排放上限将在企业债务到期期限内降至零。我们构建了一个结构性模型，在该模型中，企业在预期碳价下从多种能源中选择其排放量，而实际碳价出清每个部门-区域单元的碳市场，同时存在情景依赖的需求冲击以及通过适应措施缓解的物理损失。我们证明了均衡碳价的存在性，刻画了上限约束生效的条件，并以反馈形式推导了每个企业的最优排放量。在模型的线性化情形中，碳价是确定性的，违约概率具有显式表达式。与奖惩机制不同，ETS在上限宽松时不施加成本，而一旦上限约束生效则使企业恰好满足该上限。以欧盟ETS下35家欧洲电力生产商进行校准，该模型再现了2018年至2024年欧盟配额（European Union Allowance）价格的平均水平。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Emission trading systems (ETSs) price a growing share of global emissions, and some of their caps are set to reach zero within the maturity of corporate debt. We develop a structural model in which firms choose their emissions from several energy sources under an anticipated carbon price, while the realized carbon price clears the carbon market of each sector-region cell, with a scenario-dependent demand shock and physical losses mitigated by adaptation. We show that the equilibrium carbon price exists, characterize when the cap binds, and derive the optimal emission of each firm in feedback form. In a linearized case of the model, the carbon price is deterministic, and the default probability is explicit. Unlike a reward-penalty scheme, the ETS imposes no cost while the cap is slack and makes firms meet it exactly once it binds. Calibrated to 35 European power producers under the EU ETS, the model reproduces the average level of the European Union Allowance price over 2018 to 2024.
