<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

扩展Echenique和Nunez（2025）提出的“价格-选择”（P&C）机制，我们提出了“价格-接受-选择”（PA&C）机制，该机制在保持效率的同时消除了P&C机制中先动者的优势。随后，我们分析了随机化与竞价变体，并表明由此产生的均衡收益对应于可转移效用博弈中的标准解：P&C对应的是分配集中心值，而PA&C对应的是Shapley值。在随机化变体中，这些解以期望形式出现；在竞价变体中，它们则在每条均衡路径上得以实现。我们进一步将其他高效设计（如平衡的VCG支付）与额外的解概念联系起来，从而在实施理论与合作博弈论之间建立起一座可解释的桥梁。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Extending the Price-and-Choose (P&C) mechanism of Echenique and Nunez (2025), we propose the Price-Accept-and-Choose (PA&C) mechanism, which preserves efficiency while eliminating P&C's first-mover advantage. We then analyze randomized and bidding variants and show that the resulting equilibrium payoffs correspond to standard solutions in transferable utility games: the Center of the Imputation Set value for P&C and the Shapley value for PA&C. In the randomized variants, these solutions arise in expectation; in the bidding variants, they are implemented on every equilibrium path. We further relate other efficient designs, such as balanced VCG payments, to additional solution concepts, forging an interpretable bridge between implementation theory and cooperative game theory.

</details>
