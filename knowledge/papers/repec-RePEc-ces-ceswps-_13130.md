<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本章概述了快速发展的心理健康经济学文献，重点关注2015年以来发表的研究。该领域文献数量大幅增长，反映出人们对精神疾病的患病率和经济成本的认识不断提高、污名化程度下降，以及心理健康数据的日益可获取。除精神疾病的经济成本外，本综述聚焦于四大类心理健康决定因素：禀赋(endowments)、经济资源、教育和创伤。我们还考察了心理健康治疗的决定因素及其效果。最后，我们讨论了精神疾病的某些特征——包括其发作性和复发性——如何对经典健康资本模型(canonical health capital model)构成挑战。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This chapter provides an overview of the rapidly evolving economics literature on mental health, focusing on research published since 2015. This literature has grown substantially, reflecting the increased recognition of the prevalence and economic costs of mental illness, declining stigma, and the growing availability of data on mental health. In addition to the economic costs of mental illness, the review focuses on four broad classes of determinants of mental health: endowments, economic resources, education, and trauma. We also examine the determinants and effects of mental health treatment. Finally, we discuss how certain features of mental illness, including its episodic and recurrent nature, pose challenges for the canonical health capital model.
