# Does generative AI narrow education-based productivity gaps? Evidence from a randomized experiment

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04198v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过随机实验发现，生成式AI辅助缩小了不同教育背景工人之间的生产率差距，但未辅助时差距依然存在。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

生成式人工智能（generative AI）是扩大还是缩小了不同劳动者之间的生产率差距？我们通过一项随机在线实验对此进行了研究，实验对象为1,174名年龄在25至45岁之间的成年人，他们在有或没有生成式AI辅助的情况下完成了一项职场风格的问题解决任务，随后完成了一个无辅助模块。AI提高了所有参与者的表现，但受教育程度较低者的收益更大。在没有AI的情况下，高教育程度参与者的表现比低教育程度参与者高出0.548个标准差；而在有AI的情况下，这一差距缩小至0.139，约缩小了初始差异的四分之三。聊天记录显示，低教育程度参与者获得了大量辅助，而高教育程度参与者则更有效地使用AI。收益并非纯粹源于任务委托：一旦移除AI，受试组参与者的表现并未变差，且低教育程度参与者保留了部分提升，尽管较大差距重新出现。密集使用AI能提高辅助任务表现，且不受参与者自身努力程度的影响，但后续无辅助表现仅在密集使用与持续努力相结合时才会改善。生成式AI缩小了任务执行中的有效生产率差异，而人力资本差异仍持续影响无辅助表现及工具使用方式。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Does generative artificial intelligence (AI) widen or narrow productivity gaps across workers? We study this in a randomized online experiment with 1,174 adults aged 25-45 who completed a workplace-style problem-solving task with or without a generative AI assistant, followed by an unassisted module. AI improves performance for all participants, but gains are larger among those with less education. Without AI, higher-education participants outperform lower-education participants by 0.548 standard deviations; with AI, the gap falls to 0.139, closing about three-quarters of the initial difference. Chat logs show that lower-education participants obtain substantial assistance, while higher-education participants use AI more effectively. Gains are not purely due to delegation: treated participants do not perform worse once AI is removed, and lower-education participants retain part of their improvement, although a sizable gap re-emerges. Intensive AI use raises assisted performance regardless of participants' own effort, but follow-up performance improves only when intensive use is combined with sustained effort. Generative AI narrows effective productivity differences in task execution, while human-capital differences continue to shape unassisted performance and tool use.
