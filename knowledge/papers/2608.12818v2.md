<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究当家庭和企业选择价格依赖型计划表时的一般均衡。市场出清决定价格，因此单方面的计划表变更既会改变该主体的配置，也会改变其被评估时所依据的价格。我们将由此产生的结果称为计划表均衡。我们推导了家庭和企业的条件，证明了均衡的存在性，并给出了颤抖手精炼。竞争模型、古诺模型和供给函数模型均为特例。本文还将其应用于垄断、纵向市场势力、进入、所有权、企业创建和技术变革等情形。在每种情形中，策略性项均为出清价格对主体自身计划表的反应。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies general equilibrium when households and firms choose price-contingent schedules. Market clearing determines the price, so a unilateral schedule change changes both the agent's allocation and the price at which it is evaluated. We call the resulting outcome a schedule equilibrium. We derive the household and firm conditions, prove existence, and give a trembling-hand refinement. Competitive, Cournot, and supply-function models arise as special cases. Applications are given to monopoly, vertical market power, entry, ownership, firm creation, and technological change. In each case the strategic term is the response of the clearing price to the agent's own schedule.
