<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多政策决定了社会安排，其效果取决于其后形成的关系。我们研究当受约束的联合行动改变网络形成且结果取决于对所产生网络的暴露时，如何学习此类政策。政策价值将对结果的反应在分配后网络分布上进行积分。可行安排数量庞大，使得难以直接从历史数据评估政策。因此，我们采用基于模型的方法，跨分配汇集信息以评估替代安排。我们刻画了政策价值的可识别性并构建了插入式估计量。我们的遗憾界考虑了抽样变异、第一阶段估计误差和计算误差。我们将其应用于教室座位安排和商业小组，说明了相对于随机分配，学习规则所带来的预测福利增益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many policies determine social arrangements whose effects depend on the relationships formed afterwards. We study how to learn such policies when a constrained joint action changes network formation and outcomes depend on exposures to the resulting network. Policy value integrates an outcome response over the distribution of post-assignment networks. The large number of feasible arrangements makes it difficult to evaluate policies directly from historical data. We therefore adopt a model-based approach that pools information across assignments to evaluate alternative arrangements. We characterize identification of policy values and construct plug-in estimators. Our regret bounds account for sampling variation, first-stage estimation error, and computational errors. Our applications to classroom seating and business groups illustrate predicted welfare gains from learned rules relative to random assignment.
