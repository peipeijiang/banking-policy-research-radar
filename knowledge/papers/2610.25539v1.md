<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究一个序贯学习博弈的稳态均衡，其中每个主体在安全行动和收益未知的风险行动之间进行选择。在选择之前，每个主体观察到最近\(m\)个主体中有多少人采取了风险行动，但不知道他们是否知情，也不知道他们的采纳成本是多少。我们证明，不知情主体的模仿决策可以是策略互补的：不知情主体加入风险行动羊群的可能性越大，这些羊群关于该行动盈利能力的信号就越具有信息含量。当这种互补性导致多重均衡时，使不知情主体模仿概率最大化的均衡同时也使主体福利最大化。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study steady-state equilibria of a sequential learning game in which each agent chooses between a safe action and a risky action of unknown profitability. Before choosing, each agent observes how many of the last $m$ agents adopted the risky action, but not whether they were informed or what their adoption costs were. We show that uninformed agents' imitation decisions can be strategic complements: the more likely uninformed agents are to join risky-action herds, the more informative those herds become about the action's profitability. When this complementarity gives rise to multiple equilibria, the one that maximizes the probability of imitation by uninformed agents also maximizes agents' welfare.
