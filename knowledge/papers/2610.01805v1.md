<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文回顾了拍卖的稳健设计与分析，重点关注计算机科学文献中的主流范式，该范式通过稳健拍卖的绩效与环境最优拍卖绩效之间的最坏情况比率（在所有环境上取最坏情况）来量化稳健性。应用包括对经典拍卖的稳健分析，例如针对福利目标的第一价格拍卖（first-price auction）、针对收益目标的先验稳健拍卖设计，以及针对交易收益目标的交换机制的先验稳健设计。最后，在稳健机制设计中，显示原理（revelation principle）并非无损失。潜在损失可以通过显示缺口（revelation gap）来量化，即从所有机制限制为显示机制时所损失的最优稳健保证的比例。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The article reviews the robust design and analysis of auctions, focusing on the predominant paradigm in the computer science literature, which quantifies robustness by the worst-case, over environments, ratio between the robust auction's performance and that of an optimal auction for the environment. Applications include robust analysis of canonical auctions such as the first-price auction for the welfare objective, prior-robust auction design for the revenue objective, and prior-robust design of exchange mechanisms for the gains-from-trade objective. Finally, the revelation principle is not without loss in robust mechanism design. The potential loss can be quantified by the revelation gap, the fraction of the optimal robust guarantee that is lost when restricting from all mechanisms to revelation mechanisms.
