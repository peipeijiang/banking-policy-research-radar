<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种领域特定风险厌恶的行为概念，该概念将对待风险的态度与确定性效用分离开来：如果一个代理人在某一领域比在另一领域更纯粹地厌恶风险，那么对于在确定性下无差异的奖品，他在前一个领域比在后一个领域更厌恶风险。我们开发了一个超越期望效用的模型，允许风险态度在不同领域之间变化，同时在每个领域内保持期望效用。这些领域是主观的，无需事先指定；它们通过选择行为来识别。我们确立了模型参数的唯一性，并提供了公理化特征描述。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce a behavioral notion of domain-specific risk aversion that separates attitudes toward risk from deterministic utility: an agent is more pure risk averse in one domain than in another if, for prizes that are indifferent under certainty, he is more averse to risk in the former domain than in the latter. We develop a model that goes beyond expected utility by allowing risk attitudes to vary across domains, while preserving expected utility within each domain. The domains are subjective and need not be specified in advance; they are identified from choice behavior. We establish uniqueness of the model's parameters and provide an axiomatic characterization.

</details>
