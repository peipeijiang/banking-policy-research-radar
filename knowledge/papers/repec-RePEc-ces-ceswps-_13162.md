# Beyond Words: Non-Verbal Cues in Virtual Collaborations

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/ces/ceswps/_13162.html)

## 一句话结论

> 通过预注册在线实验研究虚拟团队会议中照片或视频等非语言线索是否提升合作与协调，发现更丰富的视觉线索并未改善合作，照片组合作水平最低。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

虚拟团队在工作中已十分普遍，而当成员彼此不相识时，协作变得困难。组织常常要求员工打开摄像头或添加头像照片，假定非语言线索能够改善协作，但支持这一假设的证据却十分匮乏。我们报告了一项预注册的在线实验，三人团队在实验中举行了一次七分钟的虚拟会议，会议形式为仅音频、音频加照片或实时视频，随后进行了一轮十回合的最弱环节博弈（weakest-link game）。团队在会议期间对博弈并不知情，因此交流纯粹是社交性的。与我们的预注册假设相反，更丰富的视觉线索并未改善合作或协调。实时视频与仅音频在统计上无法区分。与其他形式相比，带有照片的团队表现出最低的合作水平。探索性分析表明，照片与较低的感知人类存在感（perceived human presence）和团队凝聚力相关，而这些因素又与对团队成员努力程度的信念相关。一项预注册的情景研究（vignette study）未发现证据表明声音与照片之间的不匹配会降低合作。对于新组建的团队，我们的结果未提供证据表明添加照片或视频能够改善合作，并提醒人们不要假定静态视觉线索会有所帮助。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Virtual teams are commonplace at work, and collaboration is difficult when members do not know each other. Organizations often ask employees to turn on cameras or add profile photos, assuming that non-verbal cues improve collaboration, yet evidence on whether this holds is scarce. We report a preregistered online experiment in which three-person teams held a seven-minute virtual meeting, using audio only, audio with photos, or live video, before playing a ten-round weakest-link game. Teams did not know about the game during the meeting, so communication was purely social. Contrary to our preregistered hypotheses, richer visual cues did not improve cooperation or coordination. Live video was statistically indistinguishable from audio only. Teams with photos showed the lowest level of cooperation compared to the other formats. Exploratory analyses show that photos were associated with lower perceived human presence and team cohesion, which correlate with beliefs about team members’ effort. A preregistered vignette study finds no evidence that a mismatch between voice and photo reduces cooperation. For newly formed teams, our results provide no evidence that adding photos or video improves cooperation, and they caution against assuming that static visual cues help.
