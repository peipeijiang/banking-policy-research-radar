# Linear Regression with Common Unobserved Heterogeneity

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究具有双向不可观测异质性的线性面板模型中共同斜率参数的推断问题。在线性回归中，偏误来源于残差与协变量之间共享的不可观测异质性。我们的共同不可观测异质性（common unobserved heterogeneity, CUH）估计量直接针对这一共同变异：它在残差化结果的泛函中搜索同时存在于协变量中的变异，并试图进一步仅投影掉诱发偏误的变异。这使得估计量对弱结果因子（weak outcome factors）不那么敏感，同时避免完全剔除协变量中与混杂异质性无关的外生变异。我们基于奇异值强度指标（singular-value strength index）发展了一种弱因子理论，并建立了交叉拟合CUH估计量的\(\sqrt{NT}\)渐近正态性。在同方差误差下，其渐近方差不大于共同相关效应因子估计量（common correlated effects factor estimator）的渐近方差。模拟和实证应用展示了由此产生的偏误—方差权衡以及选择性剔除共同变异所带来的收益。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study inference on common slope parameters in linear panel models with two-way unobserved heterogeneity. In linear regression, bias arises from unobserved heterogeneity that is shared between the residual and the covariates. Our common unobserved heterogeneity (CUH) estimator targets this common variation directly: it searches over functionals of the residualised outcome for variation that also resides in the covariates, and seeks to further project out only bias-inducing variation. This makes the estimator less sensitive to weak outcome factors while avoiding the complete removal of exogenous variation in covariates that is unrelated to the confounding heterogeneity. We develop a weak-factor theory based on a singular-value strength index and establish $\sqrt{NT}$-asymptotic normality of the cross-fitted CUH estimator. Under homoskedastic errors, its asymptotic variance is no larger than that of a common correlated effects factor estimator. Simulations and empirical applications illustrate the resulting bias--variance trade-off and the gains from selectively removing common variation.
