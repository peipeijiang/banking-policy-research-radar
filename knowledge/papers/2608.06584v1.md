# Two Types of Tertiarization: Household Demand, Production Networks, and the Rise of Services

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06584v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用投入产出表分解服务业增长的来源，发现多数经济体服务业增长由生产网络驱动而非家庭消费，并提出了两种服务业增长类型。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我衡量了服务业的崛起最终是由家庭消费支撑，还是通过生产网络、投资、出口和政府由非家庭需求支撑。利用1995年至2022年间76个经济体的协调投入产出表，我将服务增加值归因于其最终的国内最终需求类别，并从水平和增长两方面衡量各服务经济的家庭导向性。传统的服务业份额并不能揭示其类型：它与家庭导向性呈负相关，且在约每十次服务化进程中，仅有四次以家庭需求为主导。在1995年至2018年间服务业份额上升的65个经济体中，77%的经济体变得不那么以家庭为导向；在采用两个百分点的实质性变化阈值时，这一比例为75%。然而，随着收入增加，家庭支撑的生产变得更加服务密集，因为家庭在总体中的位置下降得更快。单一类型的基准模型没有购买者边际；双类型扩展则给出了这种逆转的条件。精确的列昂惕夫核算和沙普利分解将服务业份额增长的中位数73%归因于生产系统渠道，在份额实质性上升的经济体中，80%的经济体其最大贡献来自该渠道。而家庭导向性的下降则主要归因于总体最终需求结构。服务业份额记录的是服务活动存在多少，而非何种经济转型产生了它。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I measure whether the rise of services is ultimately supported by household consumption or by non-household demand through production networks, investment, exports, and government. Using harmonized input-output tables for 76 economies over 1995-2022, I attribute service value added to its ultimate domestic final-demand category and measure the household orientation of each service economy in both levels and growth. The conventional service share does not reveal the type: it is negatively correlated with household orientation, and only about four in ten tertiarization episodes are majority-household. Among the 65 economies whose service share rose over 1995-2018, 77 percent became less household-oriented; the share is 75 percent under a two-percentage-point material-change threshold. Yet household-supported production becomes more service-intensive with income because households' aggregate position falls faster. A one-type benchmark has no purchaser margin; a two-type extension gives the condition for this reversal. An exact Leontief accounting and Shapley decomposition allocate a median 73 percent of service-share growth to production-system channels, which are the largest contribution in 80 percent of economies with materially rising shares. The decline in household orientation is instead allocated mainly to aggregate final-demand structure. The service share records how much service activity exists, not which economic transformation produced it.

</details>
