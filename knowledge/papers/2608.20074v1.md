目标函数（即需要优化的优良性准则），例如在聚类分析、因子分析、（线性）结构方程建模、（线性）回归、多维尺度分析、选择理论和效用理论中所考虑的目标函数，必须是“充分的”（adequate），即需仔细适应观测数据的结构。在我们所用的术语中，目标函数的充分性意味着对其自变量的（某些）变换，例如所涉及量的单位度量的变化，不会影响优化过程的结果。本文重点关注仿射严格递增变换，并相应地将连续充分目标函数的情形纳入考虑。对区间尺度数据的充分相异性系数（dissimilarity coefficients）的特征刻画，表明了本文所发展的充分性概念的适用性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Objective functions (goodness criteria which have to be optimized) that are considered, for instance, in cluster analysis, factor analysis, (linear) structural equation modeling, (linear) regression, multidimensional scaling, choice theory, and utility theory, must be {\em adequate}, i.e. carefully adapted to the structure of the observed data. Adequateness of an objective function means, in our terminology, that (certain) transformations of its arguments, e.g. changes in the unit measures of the quantities involved, do not influence the solutions of the optimization procedure. In this paper we concentrate our attention on affine strictly increasing transformations and we also incorporate the case of continuous adequate objective functions accordingly. The characterization of adequate dissimilarity coefficients for interval scaled data shows the appropriateness of the concept of adequateness that is developed in this paper.
