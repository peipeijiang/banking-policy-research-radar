# Sanctions, Evasion, and Deterrence: Evidence from Speeding Enforcement

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09337v1)

## 一句话结论

> 利用澳大利亚昆士兰州罚款与扣分大幅提高的准自然实验，估计固定罚款和累积扣分对超速再犯及责任转移的因果效应，发现仅提高罚款对减少再犯作用很小，而罚款加扣分可使再犯率显著下降4.6个百分点。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

制裁设计在塑造再犯行为方面发挥着核心作用。我们研究固定金额罚款（fixed monetary fines）与累积记分（accumulating penalty points）对超速再犯及责任转移的影响。利用澳大利亚昆士兰州制裁力度大幅提高这一背景，我们估计了这些制裁对后续违法行为及指认其他驾驶员行为的因果效应。记分增加会提高罚款重新指认率，这与策略性责任转移（strategic blame shifting）相一致。仅面临更高罚款的违法者中，再犯率的估计降幅较小。当更高罚款伴随额外记分时，再犯率显著下降4.6个百分点。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

The design of sanctions plays a central role in shaping reoffending behavior. We study the effects of fixed monetary fines and accumulating penalty points on speeding recidivism and liability transfer. Exploiting a large increase in sanctions in Queensland, Australia, we estimate the causal effects of these sanctions on subsequent offending and nomination of other drivers. Higher demerit points increase fine renomination, consistent with strategic blame shifting. Estimated reductions in reoffending are small among offenders facing higher fines alone. Reoffending falls by a substantial 4.6 percentage points when higher fines are accompanied by additional penalty points.
