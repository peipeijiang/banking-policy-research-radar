<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

企业在何时以及为何会就不容分歧的社会议题公开表明立场？我们构建了一个关于美国企业立场表态的综合性数据集，使用大语言模型阅读了约52,000篇新闻文章和新闻稿，涉及2008年至2025年间任意时点曾属于标普500指数的836家公司。我们识别出9,917项立场表态，涵盖气候变化、堕胎和种族歧视等九个党派性议题。我们发现，立场表态在2008年之后增长了四倍，并于2021年达到峰值。这些表态绝大多数偏自由派，且集中于最大的企业。四分之一的立场表态未给出明确的商业理由。立场表态与游说和政治捐款仅存在弱相关，且与二者不同，它不遵循选举周期。相反，企业往往在媒体关注度和公众对某一议题的兴趣达到高峰时发声。综合来看，这些发现表明，立场表态可以被理解为对广泛政策辩论的贡献，而非影响企业核心业务相关政策的策略。最后，我们考察了企业立场代表了谁的观点。面向消费者的企业做出更多立场表态，而消费者倾向民主党的企业在气候以外的议题上采取更自由的立场。拥有更高比例民主党员工、高管和董事会成员的公司也采取更自由的立场。利用企业内部的变异，我们发现，公司领导层中民主党人越多，自由派立场表态越多；而女性越多，则在性别相关议题和堕胎议题上的立场表态越多。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When do firms take public positions on divisive societal issues and why? We assemble a comprehensive dataset on corporate stance-taking in the United States, using large language models to read 52,000 news articles and press releases about 836 companies that belonged to the S&P 500 at any point between 2008 and 2025. We identify 9,917 stances on nine partisan issues such as climate change, abortion, and racial discrimination. We show that stance-taking increased fourfold after 2008 and peaked in 2021. It is overwhelmingly liberal and concentrated among the largest firms. A quarter of stances give no explicit business justification. Stance-taking is only weakly related to lobbying and campaign contributions and, unlike them, does not follow the electoral cycle. Instead, firms tend to speak when media coverage and public interest in an issue peak. Together, these findings suggest that stance-taking can be understood as a contribution to broad policy debates rather than a strategy to shape policies that affect firms' core business. Finally, we investigate whose views corporate stances represent. Consumer-facing firms take more stances, and firms whose consumers lean Democratic take more liberal stances on issues other than climate. Companies with larger shares of Democratic employees, executives, and board members also take more liberal stances. Exploiting within-firm variation, we find that having more Democrats among a company's leadership increases liberal stance-taking, while having more women increases stance-taking on gender-related issues and abortion.
