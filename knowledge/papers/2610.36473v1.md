<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文介绍了一个由Cox过程驱动的无限服务器队列模型，其随机强度由平方贝塞尔（BESQ）过程给出。利用底层动力学的仿射结构，我们推导出了到达强度、系统内顾客数以及系统内顾客数在一段时间区间上的积分的联合矩生成函数（MGF）的闭式表达式。该MGF也可以在两个（或多个）时间点上获得，从而允许分析由Cox–BESQ过程驱动的无限服务器队列的时间演化。这些显式MGF的可得性使得能够为这些变量开发风险度量，例如到达强度、系统内顾客数以及系统内顾客数在一段时间区间上的积分的联合矩，条件为这些变量之一超过临界阈值。这些风险度量可以在一个或两个时间点上确定。它们本质上依赖于状态变量MGF的显式形式以及风险度量的傅里叶表示。数值实验展示了所提出方法的实际实现，并突出了理论结果的相关性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper introduces a model for infinite-server queues driven by a Cox process with stochastic intensity given by a squared Bessel (BESQ) process. Exploiting the affine structure of the underlying dynamics, we derive a closed-form expression for the joint moment-generating function (MGF) of the arrival intensity, the number of customers in the system, and the integral of the number of customers in the system over a time interval. This MGF can also be obtained at two (or more) dates, thereby allowing an analysis of the time evolution of infinite-server queues driven by a Cox--BESQ process. The availability of these explicit MGFs enables the development of risk measures for these variables, such as the joint moments of the arrival intensity, the number of customers in the system, and the integral of the number of customers in the system over a time interval, conditional on one of these variables exceeding a critical threshold. These risk measures can be determined at one or two dates. They rely essentially on the explicit form of the MGF of the state variables and on Fourier representations of the risk measures. Numerical experiments demonstrate the practical implementation of the proposed methodology and highlight the relevance of the theoretical results.
