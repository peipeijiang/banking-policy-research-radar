<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种得分驱动的波动率模型，该模型无需指定特定的参数化新息分布。所提方法依赖于一个轻量级神经网络来表示新息密度的负得分。通过对学习到的得分进行积分和归一化，并施加位置-尺度识别约束，我们恢复出一个能够容纳不对称性的灵活新息密度，包括金融收益率中常见的负偏度。我们考虑了两种估计程序。两步法通过得分匹配估计神经网络得分，随后在识别出的密度保持固定的情况下拟合波动率递归。一步法在条件似然下联合优化神经网络参数和波动率参数。我们使用八个国际股票指数说明了所提方法的实际相关性，并对2020年上半年高波动时期进行了单独评估。结果表明，神经得分驱动模型与需要预先指定参数化新息分布的高斯和Student-$t$ GAS基准模型相比具有竞争力，在COVID-19期间表现尤为突出。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We develop a score-driven volatility model in which no particular parametric innovation distribution needs to be specified. The proposed method relies on a lightweight neural network to represent the negative score of the innovation density. By integrating and normalizing the learned score and imposing location-scale identification, we recover a flexible innovation density that can accommodate asymmetry, including negative skewness commonly observed in financial returns. Two estimation procedures are considered. A two-step procedure estimates the neural score by score matching and subsequently fits the volatility recursion with the identified density held fixed. A one-step procedure jointly refines the neural and volatility parameters under the conditional likelihood. We illustrate the practical relevance of the proposed method using eight international equity indices, with a separate evaluation of the high-volatility first half of 2020. The results show that the neural score-driven models are competitive with Gaussian and Student-$t$ GAS benchmarks that require a prespecified parametric innovation distribution, with particularly strong performance during the COVID-19 period.
