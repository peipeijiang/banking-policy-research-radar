# Flooded by China? Trade diversion due to US tariffs during Trump’s first mandate

[查看原文](https://ideas.repec.org/p/bde/wpaper/2634e.html)

## 一句话结论

> 本文利用特朗普第一任期对华关税作为准自然实验，采用双重差分局部投影法，发现中国出口向欧盟的贸易转移规模有限（每减少1美元对美出口仅9美分转向欧盟），但在复杂资本品和同质中间品以及特定市场上更为显著，并伴随价格下降。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究美国对华关税是否将中国出口转向欧盟，并以第一次中美贸易战作为能够清晰识别贸易转移效应的研究情境。通过将双重差分局部投影方法应用于高度细分的关税、进口和单位价值数据，本文表明贸易转移确实发生，但规模有限。按实际值计算，美国自中国进口每减少1美元，就有9美分转向欧盟市场。不同产品和目的地市场的效应差异显著。贸易转移在复杂资本品和同质化中间投入品中最为明显，并且在那些对中国进口暴露度更高、来自替代供应国的竞争更弱的欧盟市场中更为突出。经历有效转移的产品还表现出显著的价格下降，竞争压力集中在低价市场细分领域。这些发现表明，与美国总统2025年实施的关税规模相当的关税，可能将中国出口转向欧盟，并可能促成欧洲出现第二次“中国冲击”。尽管总体效应温和，但在特定产品类别和目的地市场中，转移和价格压力可能显著更强。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper investigates whether US tariffs on China diverted Chinese exports towards the EU, exploiting the first US-China trade war as a setting that permits clean identification of trade diversion effects. Applying difference-in-differences local projections to highly disaggregated tariff, import and unit value data, it shows that trade diversion occurred but was limited in size. For each dollar reduction in US imports from China, 9 cents were redirected to the EU market in real terms. The effects varied substantially across products and destination markets. Trade diversion was strongest for complex capital goods and homogeneous intermediate inputs, and was more pronounced in EU markets with greater import exposure to China and weaker competition from alternative suppliers. Products experiencing effective diversion also exhibited significant price declines, with competitive pressures concentrated in lower-price market segments. These findings suggest that tariffs of a magnitude comparable to those imposed by the United States in 2025 could redirect Chinese exports towards the EU, potentially contributing to a second “China shock” in Europe. While the aggregate effects are modest, diversion and price pressures may be considerably stronger in specific product categories and destination markets.
