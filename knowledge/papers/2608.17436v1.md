# The Long-Term Effects of British and French Colonization in Africa on Trust in Traditional Leaders

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

对地方机构的信任对于贸易、公共物品供给、冲突解决以及民主巩固至关重要。利用非洲晴雨表调查第六轮和第七轮的个人数据，我发现前英国殖民地的受访者比前法国殖民地的受访者更有可能信任传统领袖。此类跨国比较受到殖民前后历史中未观测到的异质性的干扰。为了在识别上取得进展，我将焦点放在喀麦隆，该国在现代国家框架内同时包含由英国殖民和法国殖民的地区，并利用前殖民分界线作为地理断点，比较居住在英语-法语边界两侧附近的受访者。理论论点是，两个殖民列强在对待习惯权威的方式上存在系统性差异：英国的间接统治保留了酋长的治理职能，而法国的直接统治则使酋长从属于行政当局，并赋予其最具强制性的任务，尤其是征税和强制劳动，这些不同的经历通过制度延续和社会化塑造了酋长制的合法性。与这一论点一致，前英国一侧的受访者比前法国一侧的邻居信任传统领袖的可能性高出22至26个百分点，并且他们在过去一年中联系传统领袖的可能性也远高于后者。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Trust in local institutions matters for trade, public goods provision, conflict resolution, and democratic consolidation. Using individual data from rounds 6 and 7 of the Afrobarometer surveys, I document that respondents in former British colonies are substantially more likely to trust traditional leaders than respondents in former French colonies. Cross-country comparisons of this kind are confounded by unobserved heterogeneity in pre- and post-colonial histories. To make progress on identification, I focus on Cameroon, which contains regions colonized by Britain and regions colonized by France within a single modern state, and I exploit the former colonial partition line as a geographic discontinuity, comparing respondents who live close to either side of the anglophone-francophone boundary. The theoretical argument is that the two colonial powers differed systematically in how they treated customary authority: British indirect rule preserved the governance functions of chiefs, while French direct rule subordinated chiefs to the administration and assigned them its most coercive tasks, taxation and forced labor above all, and these divergent experiences shaped the legitimacy of chieftaincy in ways that persist through institutional continuity and socialization. Consistent with this argument, respondents on the formerly British side are 22 to 26 percentage points more likely to trust traditional leaders than their neighbors on the formerly French side, and they are far more likely to have contacted a traditional leader in the previous year.
