<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

部署了性能不断提升但仍不完美的人工智能的企业，必须决定在多大程度上让人类员工持续参与。参与度会降低当前产出，但能构建企业在AI失效时所需的备用技能。我们探讨这一备用技能同时向两类受众传递了何种信号：一类是流动型员工，他们根据工作所构建的技能轨迹在不同企业间进行选择；另一类是客户，他们在信任品市场中无法直接观察技能，只能推断能力。我们将Singh、Ghosh和Dai（2026）的参与度-技能动态模型嵌入信号博弈，并引入责任承诺机制。由于技能水平更高的服务提供者在AI失效的状态下恰恰更少出现失败，因此责任承诺的预期成本随备用技能的提升而递减。这在一个内生的——即被构建而非先天赋予的——类型上恢复了Spence-Mirrlees单交叉条件，并产生了一个分离均衡，在该均衡中，责任承诺为被保留的人类能力提供了认证，而一旦AI的写作水平与专家相当，任何人工制品都无法对此类能力进行认证。我们刻画了最低成本分离承诺计划，表明客户的风险敞口会根据承诺规模的大小，将参与度导向技能最低的员工或使其偏离该员工，并推导出一个风险敞口阈值，超过该阈值后，构建技能将优于搭竞争对手培训的便车——从而逆转了底层劳动模型中非对称专业化的结论。两个边界从两侧收紧了市场：小额交易无法支撑执行成本，而大额交易则超出了服务提供者的偿付能力。该市场的基于智能体的版本在存在噪声信念、基于记录的學習以及员工流动的情况下，复现了上述分析阈值。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Firms that deploy improving but imperfect AI must decide how much to keep human workers engaged. Engagement lowers current output yet builds the fallback skill the firm needs when AI fails. We ask what that fallback skill signals to two audiences at once: mobile workers, who sort across firms on the skill trajectory a job builds, and clients, who cannot observe skill in a credence-good market and must infer competence. We embed the engagement-skill dynamics of Singh, Ghosh and Dai (2026) in a signaling game and add a liability commitment. Because a more-skilled provider fails less often precisely in the states where AI is down, the expected cost of a liability pledge is decreasing in fallback skill. This restores Spence-Mirrlees single crossing on a type that is endogenous - built, not drawn - and yields a separating equilibrium in which liability certifies preserved human competence that no artifact can certify once AI writes as well as the expert. We characterize the least-cost separating pledge schedule, show that client stakes shift engagement toward or away from the least-skilled worker depending on the size of the pledge, and derive a stakes threshold above which building skill dominates free-riding on a rival's training - reversing the asymmetric-specialization result of the underlying labor model. Two boundaries close the market from both sides: small tickets cannot fund enforcement, and large tickets exceed the provider's solvency. An agent-based version of the market reproduces the analytical thresholds under noisy beliefs, learning-by-record and worker churn.
