# Paying for Information, Not for Clicks: A Block-Level Publisher Content Marketplace for AI Answers

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30324v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文提出以'lego block'为归因单元的出版商内容付费市场，用Shapley框架比较三种支付规则，并基于1,974条查询的新闻语料评估发现：仅用单次检索来源时各规则分配几乎相同，而全球索引揭示跨出版商重叠后规则选择变得重要，优质出版商在均等分享下获42%、首发规则下获46-50%的价值。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出一个出版商内容市场，为AI生成答案中所使用的信息分配付费。归属的单元是“乐高积木块”：一个可能出现在多个出版商文章中的独立事实或评论片段。一个简单的新闻阅读模型连接了三条付费规则：在所有报道某一块的出版商之间平均分配、奖励最先报道某一块的出版商，以及奖励不可或缺的贡献。每一种都可以在Shapley值框架内表示。
我们使用针对一个被大量报道的新闻事件的1，974条搜索引擎查询的答案来评估该框架。当归属仅考虑为单个查询检索到的文章时，大多数被引用的块看起来是独特的，各规则产生的分配几乎相同。一个覆盖完整新闻语料的全局索引揭示了出版商之间的重叠，使得付费规则的选择具有实际后果。优质出版商，定义为拥有大量新闻采集业务的成熟媒体机构，在平均分配下获得分配价值的42%，而在首次发表规则下获得46-50%，具体取决于对发布时间戳的处理方式。这些发现凸显了一个共享全局索引的重要性，该索引记录哪些出版商提供了每个块以及他们何时发布了它。仅限于为单个答案检索到的来源的归属缺乏区分这些付费政策所需的信息。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a publisher content marketplace that allocates payments for information used in AI-generated answers. The unit of attribution is a ``lego block:'' a distinct fact or piece of commentary that may appear in articles from multiple publishers. A simple news-reading model connects three payment rules: equal sharing among all publishers reporting on a block, rewarding the publisher who reported a block first, and rewarding indispensable contributions. Each can be represented within a Shapley-value framework.
  We evaluate the framework using answers to 1,974 search-engine queries about a heavily covered news episode. When attribution considers only the articles retrieved for an individual query, most cited blocks appear unique, and the rules produce nearly identical allocations. A global index covering the full news corpus reveals overlap across publishers, making the choice of payment rule consequential. Premium publishers, defined as established outlets with substantial newsgathering operations, receive 42% of allocated value under equal sharing, compared with 46-50% under a first-publication rule, depending on the treatment of publication timestamps. These findings highlight the importance of a shared global index that records which publishers provide each block and when they published it. Attribution restricted to the sources retrieved for an individual answer lacks the information needed to distinguish these payment policies.
