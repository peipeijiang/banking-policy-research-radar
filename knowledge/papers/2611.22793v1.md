<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究考察基于人工智能的代码审查工具如何重塑开源软件（OSS）社区中的贡献行为与维护动态。AI审查工具提供可扩展、快速且标准化的评估，既重塑了贡献行为，也改变了维护者的工作负担。我们以2025年GitHub上GitHub Copilot Reviewer的引入作为自然实验，研究AI审查如何影响维护者的工作量、审查效率以及贡献行为。我们发现，在采用AI审查工具后，代码仓库维护者最初面临拉取请求（PR）提交量的急剧增加以及更高的审查工作量。随着时间推移，人类贡献下降，而机器人生成的PR持续增加。有趣的是，生存分析表明，AI审查工具提高了人类PR贡献的审查效率，而机器人PR则需要更长时间来审查和合并。我们的研究结果表明，尽管AI审查工具提高了审查效率，但它们通过鼓励更多来自人类和机器人的代码贡献，加重了维护负担。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study examines how AI-based code reviewers reshape contribution behavior and maintenance dynamics in open-source software (OSS) communities. AI reviewers provide scalable, fast, and standardized evaluations. They reshape contributions as well as maintainers' workload. Leveraging the introduction of GitHub Copilot Reviewer on GitHub as a natural experiment in 2025, we investigate how AI review affects maintainers' workload and review efficiency as well as contribution behavior. We find that repository maintainers initially face a sharp increase in PR submissions and a higher review workload after the adoption of the AI reviewer. Over time, human contributions decline while the increase in bot-generated PRs is sustained. Interestingly, survival analysis reveals that AI reviewers increase review efficiency for human PR contributions, whereas bot PRs take longer to review and merge. Our findings suggest that while AI reviewers increase review efficiency, they aggravate the maintenance burden by encouraging more code contributions via humans and bots.
