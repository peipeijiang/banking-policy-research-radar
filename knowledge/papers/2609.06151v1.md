<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我提出一个在大规模社群中关于亲社会规范的新颖且易于处理的模型，其中包含略微利他的行为主体。行为主体参与规范是为了影响其他行为主体也参与规范，从而进一步影响更多行为主体。一个连续的均衡集合维持着这一亲社会规范，这些均衡在行为主体受其观察影响程度的分布上有所不同。如果行为主体的有效利他性$α$大于其不耐烦程度$1-δ$，则存在能够抵御不良行为者群体的均衡。更大的策略同质性会增强规范的稳健性。将每个行为的观察者数量增加到1以上并不会提高稳健性。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I propose a novel, tractable model of pro-social norms in large communities with slightly altruistic agents. Agents participate in the norm to influence others to participate in the norm, influencing further agents. A continuum of equilibria sustain the pro-social norm, varying in the distribution of how much agents are influenced by their observations. If agents' effective altruism $α$ is larger than their impatience $1-δ$, equilibria exist that are robust to a population of bad actors. Greater strategic homogeneity increases the robustness of the norm. Robustness is not improved by increasing the number of observers of each action beyond 1.

</details>
