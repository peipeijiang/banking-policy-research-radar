<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究Lambda分位数，这是经典分位数的一种推广，其中常数概率水平$λ\in [0,1]$被替换为一个函数参数$Λ\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1]$。我们考虑非单调$Λ$的一般情形，这种情形在要求相应的Lambda分位数类关于下确界聚合或混合具有封闭性时自然出现。作为初步结果，我们刻画了有限性、常值性以及我们称之为经典分位数中已知的达到性质。随后，我们考虑从$Λ$-分位数在合适的简单分布族上的取值来重构$Λ$的问题，证明了在温和假设下的可识别性。接下来，我们大幅改进了文献中关于弱上半连续性和弱下半连续性以及水平集关于混合的凸性性质的若干结果，在两种情况下均获得了无需任何单调性假设的近乎完整的刻画。然后，我们转向$Λ$具有有界变差的情形，这使我们能够证明一个混合表示结果：任何这样的$Λ$-分位数都可以重写为一个具有递增函数参数的Lambda分位数，该分位数在原始分布与一个固定参考分布以固定权重混合后的分布上求值，从而将参数的复杂度从有界变差降低到单调。最后，我们引入并研究了风险测度的序协变群的概念，表明在$Λ$-分位数的情况下，它与$Λ$的合成不变群以及与该$Λ$相关的符号测度的某个保测变换群相一致。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study Lambda-quantiles, a generalisation of classical quantiles in which the constant probability level $λ\in [0,1]$ is replaced by a functional parameter $Λ\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1]$. We consider the general case of non-monotone $Λ$, which arises naturally if closure properties of the class of corresponding Lambda-quantiles with respect to inf-aggregation or with respect to mixtures are required. As preliminary results, we characterise finiteness, constancy, and what we call the attainment property known from classical quantiles. We then consider the problem of reconstructing $Λ$ from the values of $Λ$-quantiles on a suitable family of simple distributions, showing its identifiability under mild assumptions. Next, we substantially refine several results obtained in the literature on weak upper and lower semicontinuity and on the property of convexity of the level sets with respect to mixtures, obtaining in both cases almost complete characterisations without any monotonicity assumption. We then move to the case in which $Λ$ has bounded variation, which enables us to prove a mixture representation result: any such $Λ$-quantile can be rewritten as a Lambda-quantile with an increasing functional parameter, evaluated at a mixture of the original distribution with a fixed reference distribution at a fixed weight, thus reducing the complexity of the parameter from bounded variation to monotone. Finally, we introduce and study the notion of the ordinal covariance group of a risk measure, showing that in the case of a $Λ$-quantile it coincides with the compositional invariance group of $Λ$ and with a certain group of measure-preserving transformations of the signed measure associated with $Λ$.
