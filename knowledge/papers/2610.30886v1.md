<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文比较了2025年1月DeepSeek R1和2026年7月Kimi K3发布时的金融市场反应。两个AI模型均被呈现为接近能力前沿的、可信的中国竞争者，但它们的发布传递了不同的经济信息。DeepSeek R1将开放权重与异常低的报告训练和推理成本相结合，促使投资者重新评估对计算基础设施的预期需求，并导致半导体和数据中心相关公司的股价大幅下跌。Kimi K3则以大致前沿价格进入市场，其市场反应更多地集中在中国 rival AI实验室之间。基于描述性事件窗口证据、模型能力与成本比较、后续行业发展以及开源软件的历史扩散，本文认为开放权重模型在AI价值链上重新分配租金，而非消除租金。现有企业仍受到互补性资产的保护，包括分销渠道、产品捆绑、转换成本、专有数据、服务能力和合同信任。分析表明，投资者和政策制定者在评估风险时，不仅应关注模型能力，还应关注AI价值链上互补性资产的所有权和持久性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper compares financial-market reactions to the releases of DeepSeek R1 in January 2025 and Kimi K3 in July 2026. Both AI models were presented as credible Chinese competitors near the capability frontier, but their releases conveyed different economic information. DeepSeek R1 combined open weights with unusually low reported training and inference costs, prompting investors to reassess expected demand for computing infrastructure and producing large stock-price decreases among semiconductor and data-centre-related firms. Kimi K3 instead entered at broadly frontier prices and generated a market reaction concentrated more heavily among rival Chinese AI laboratories. Drawing on descriptive event-window evidence, model capability and cost comparisons, subsequent industry developments, and the historical diffusion of open-source software, the paper argues that open-weight models redistribute rents across the AI value chain rather than eliminating them. Incumbents remain protected by complementary assets including distribution, product bundling, switching costs, proprietary data, serving capacity, and contractual trust. The analysis suggests that investors and policy makers should assess risks looking not only at model capability, but also at the ownership and durability of complementary assets across the AI value chain.
