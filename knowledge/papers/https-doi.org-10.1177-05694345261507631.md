<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察美国通胀与劳动力市场之间的关系在新冠疫情后是否发生变化，以及应如何解读这一变化。研究使用2010年至2026年的月度数据，将2017—2019年与2023—2026年进行比较，并将2020—2022年这一特殊过渡期排除在主要分析之外。估计的失业率斜率从约0.27变为−1.94，且该差异在Newey-West推断下具有统计显著性。在控制通胀预期、均衡失业率、油价变动、替代性通胀与劳动力市场指标以及有影响力的观测值后，斜率陡峭化仍然显著。然而，该结果对疫情前的比较窗口和灵活时间趋势的稳健性较弱，同时结构稳定性检验识别出多个不稳定时段，而非唯一的疫情断点。本文的贡献不在于识别出因果性的疫情效应或永久性系数，而在于记录了一种非对称的稳健性模式。研究结果支持将菲利普斯曲线解读为一种随时间变化的简化型关系，并提醒不应将任一时期的估计值视为政策不变。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines whether the US inflation-labor-market relationship changed after COVID-19 and how that change should be interpreted. Using monthly data from 2010 through 2026, it compares 2017–2019 with 2023–2026, excluding the exceptional transition years of 2020–2022 from the principal analysis. The estimated unemployment slope changes from approximately 0.27 to − 1.94 , and the difference is statistically significant under Newey-West inference. The steepening remains significant after controlling for inflation expectations, equilibrium unemployment, oil-price movements, alternative inflation and labor-market measures, and influential observations. However, it is less robust to the pre-pandemic comparison window and flexible time trends, while structural-stability tests identify multiple instability episodes rather than a unique pandemic break. The paper’s contribution is not to identify a causal pandemic effect or permanent coefficient, but to document an asymmetric robustness pattern. The results support interpreting the Phillips curve as a time-varying reduced-form relationship and caution against treating either period’s estimate as policy-invariant.
