# The Influence of Profitability, Interest Rates (BI Rate) and Institutional Ownership on Stock Returns at PT Akasha Wira Internasional Tbk

## 一句话结论

> 该研究使用2013-2024年PT Akasha Wira International Tbk的12个年度观测，通过多元线性回归检验盈利能力、印尼央行政策利率和机构持股对股票收益的影响，发现ROA正向显著、BI利率负向显著、机构持股不显著，模型R方为0.799。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本研究考察了2013至2024年期间PT Akasha Wira International Tbk的盈利能力、印度尼西亚银行政策利率、机构持股与股票收益率之间的关系。研究采用定量方法，使用从公司年度报告和印度尼西亚银行出版物获取的二手数据。数据集包含12个年度观测值。盈利能力以资产收益率（return on assets, ROA）衡量，机构持股以机构投资者持有的股份比例衡量。利率变量基于印度尼西亚银行公布的政策利率序列，并考虑了观测期间官方基准术语的变化。使用SPSS 23版进行多元线性回归和假设检验。结果表明，盈利能力与股票收益率呈显著正相关（t = 4.476，p = 0.002），而政策利率与股票收益率呈显著负相关（t = −2.830，p = 0.022）。机构持股与股票收益率之间不存在统计学上显著的偏相关关系（t = 1.803，p = 0.109）。同时，三个自变量在回归模型中具有统计显著性（F = 10.610，p = 0.004）。模型的R方为0.799，表明预测变量共同解释了样本内股票收益率变异的79.9%，而在考虑预测变量数量和观测值数量后，调整R方为0.724。鉴于样本量有限且为单一公司情境，这些发现应谨慎解读。本研究提供了关于企业层面盈利能力、货币政策、所有权结构与股票收益率之间关系的案例特定证据。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This study examines the relationships between profitability, the Bank Indonesia policy interest rate, institutional ownership, and stock returns at PT Akasha Wira International Tbk over the 2013–2024 period. A quantitative approach was employed using secondary data obtained from the company’s annual reports and Bank Indonesia publications. The dataset comprises 12 annual observations. Profitability was measured using return on assets (ROA), while institutional ownership was measured by the proportion of shares held by institutional investors. The interest-rate variable was based on Bank Indonesia’s published policy-rate series, taking into account changes in the official benchmark terminology during the observation period. Multiple linear regression and hypothesis testing were conducted using SPSS version 23. The results indicate that profitability is positively and significantly associated with stock returns (t = 4.476, p = 0.002), whereas the policy interest rate is negatively and significantly associated with stock returns (t = −2.830, p = 0.022). Institutional ownership does not exhibit a statistically significant partial association with stock returns (t = 1.803, p = 0.109). Simultaneously, the three independent variables are statistically significant in the regression model (F = 10.610, p = 0.004). The model yields an R-squared of 0.799, indicating that the predictors jointly account for 79.9% of the in-sample variation in stock returns, while the adjusted R-squared is 0.724 after accounting for the number of predictors and observations. Given the limited sample and single-company setting, these findings should be interpreted cautiously. This study provides case-specific evidence concerning the relationships between firm-level profitability, monetary policy, ownership structure, and stock returns
