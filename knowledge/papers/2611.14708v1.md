# The Rebundling of Freelance Work in the AI Age

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14708v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过比较ChatGPT中的活动分布与Upwork上的自由职业任务需求，发现AI更擅长的任务在市场上份额下降、AI相关需求更多样且项目预算更高，表明AI正在重塑任务外包与项目组合方式。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文考察了人工智能时代自由职业工作的变化。我们比较了人们在ChatGPT中进行的活动与客户在在线自由职业平台Upwork上请求的工作，并研究了客户如何将任务组合成项目。我们发现，在ChatGPT中更为普遍的活动往往在Upwork上失去市场份额，而那些不太普遍的活动则往往获得份额。在AI相关工作中，需求正变得更加多样化，职位发布将生成式AI时代之前很少被同时请求的任务组合在一起。AI相关的职位发布还具有显著更大的项目预算，涉及更多的工作时长，而小时费率的差异较小。综合来看，这些发现表明，AI不仅正在重塑客户外包哪些任务，还正在重塑他们如何将任务组合成更大、更高价值的项目。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper examines how freelance work is changing in the AI era. We compare the activities people undertake in ChatGPT with the work clients request on Upwork, an online freelance platform, and examine how clients combine tasks into projects. We find that activities more prevalent in ChatGPT tend to lose marketplace share on Upwork, while those less prevalent tend to gain share. Within AI-related work, demand is becoming more diverse, and job postings combine tasks that were rarely requested together before the generative AI era. AI-related postings also have substantially larger project budgets and involve more hours of work, while differences in hourly rates are small. Together, these findings suggest that AI is reshaping not only which tasks clients outsource, but also how they combine tasks into larger, higher-value projects.
