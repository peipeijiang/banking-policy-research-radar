<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究当纯风险评价由一般的、可能非仿射的统计量决定时的模糊性下的决策泛函。我们将模糊感知表示为在一阶模型上的贝叶斯二阶模型，并用一个单独的参数刻画模糊态度。我们为结合这些对象的决策泛函制定了四条一致性和单调性性质。我们表明，对于非仿射统计量，包括期望损失（expected shortfall）这一行业标准风险度量，二阶平均通常不保持这些性质。为解决这一局限，我们引入模糊熨斗（ambiguity iron），即从二阶模型和模糊态度到一阶模型模糊集的映射。在该集合上的最坏情况评价将所选统计量保持为纯风险准则。我们通过扭曲二阶模型构造了一个模糊熨斗，并表明它产生凸且紧的预测模型集。我们推导了几类统计量的公式，并在参数设定下给出了扭曲期望的基于样本的近似。投资组合示例说明了模糊感知和态度如何影响评价与配置。在后验集中和参数化连续性的条件下，模糊集收缩，相关的决策泛函收敛到其纯风险值。我们还描述了针对可能的模型误设定的一种基于KL的稳健化方法。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies decision functionals under ambiguity when pure-risk evaluation is determined by a general, possibly non-affine statistic. We represent ambiguity perception by a Bayesian second-order model over first-order models and encode ambiguity attitude with a separate parameter. We formulate four consistency and monotonicity properties for decision functionals combining these objects. We show that second-order averaging does not generally preserve these properties for non-affine statistics, including expected shortfall, the industry-standard risk measure. To address this limitation, we introduce an ambiguity iron, a mapping from a second-order model and an ambiguity attitude to an ambiguity set of first-order models. Worst-case evaluation over this set preserves the chosen statistic as the pure-risk criterion. We construct an ambiguity iron by distorting the second-order model and show that it produces convex and compact sets of predictive models. We derive formulas for several classes of statistics and, in parametric settings, a sample-based approximation for the distorted expectation. Portfolio examples illustrate how ambiguity perception and attitude affect evaluations and allocations. Under posterior concentration and continuity of the parametrization, the ambiguity sets contract, and the associated decision functionals converge to their pure-risk values. We also describe a KL-based robustification for possible model misspecification.
