# Large player classes and approximately weighted voting

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

已知，在简单博弈中，联盟（$C$）-合意关系的完全性并不意味着存在加权多数表示。我们表明，加权性在近似意义上是隐含的。称一个$n$人简单博弈为$\varepsilon$-加权的，如果对于$(n-1)$-单纯形中的某个权重向量，任何失败联盟的权重超过任何获胜联盟的权重至多为$\varepsilon$；令$\varepsilon^{\ast}$为最小的这样的$\varepsilon$。我们在一般$C$-完全博弈中建立了$\varepsilon^{\ast}$的上界，并推出，如果所有同等合意参与者的类都渐近变大，但类的数量有界，则对于最小参与者类规模$n_{\min}$，有$\varepsilon^{\ast}=O(1/n_{\min})$，且该界是紧的。重要的是，当$n_{\min}\rightarrow\infty$且参与者类的数量有界时，加权规则错误标记的联盟的最小可达到比例$δ$也趋于$0$，且有$δ=O(1/\sqrt{n_{\min}})$。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

It is known that completeness of the coalitional ($C$-)desirability relation in a simple game does not imply the existence of a weighted-majority representation. We show that weightedness is implied in an approximate sense. Call an $n$-player simple game $\varepsilon$-weighted if, for some weight vector in the $(n-1)$-simplex, any losing coalition weight exceeds any winning coalition weight by at most $\varepsilon$; let $\varepsilon^{\ast}$ be the smallest such $\varepsilon$. We establish an upper bound on $\varepsilon^{\ast}$ in a general $C$-complete game, and deduce that, if all classes of equally desirable players become large asymptotically but the number of classes is bounded, then $\varepsilon^{\ast}=O(1/n_{\min})$ for the minimal player class size $n_{\min}$, and the bound is tight. Importantly, the minimal attainable fraction $δ$ of coalitions mislabeled by a weighting rule also tends to $0$ when $n_{\min}\rightarrow\infty$ and the number of player classes is bounded, with $δ=O(1/\sqrt{n_{\min}})$.

</details>
