# Accuracy Is Not Welfare: Evaluating AI Buyers under Strategic Price Complexity in a Synthetic Duopoly

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

AI购买代理可能改变卖方的激励以及消费者的决策。在一个合成双寡头市场中，我们在固定菜单上以及经验相关均衡博弈中评估价格比较流程。计算器辅助在84.00%的情况下选择最优报价，而在经核实的总额披露下为68.57%，但披露具有高出20.14个单位的消费者最差均衡剩余。冻结策略的留出比较支持披露，而在情景重采样下重新求解均衡产生的范围跨越零。这些基于模型的结果表明，为何准确性、损失严重程度、卖方激励和福利归属需要分别测量。

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

AI buying agents may alter sellers' incentives as well as consumers' decisions. In a synthetic duopoly, we evaluate price-comparison pipelines on a fixed menu and in empirical correlated-equilibrium games. Calculator assistance selects an optimal offer 84.00% of the time, versus 68.57% under verified-total disclosure, yet disclosure has a 20.14-unit higher consumer-worst equilibrium surplus. A frozen-strategy holdout comparison favors disclosure, while re-solving equilibria under scenario resampling produces a range spanning zero. These model-based results show why accuracy, loss severity, seller incentives, and welfare incidence require separate measurement.
