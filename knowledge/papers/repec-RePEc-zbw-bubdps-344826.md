# Cross-predictability of returns: An equilibrium perspective

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

定向现金流关联仅通过无摩擦、信息有效且完全市场中的均衡定价效应，便引致了关联资产之间收益的交叉可预测性。这一基于风险的机制建立在经济关联资产的基本原理之上。在一个允许闭式解的基于消费的均衡资产定价模型中，预期超额收益与现金流关联强度的差异相联系。我们的均衡机制在两种条件下产生交叉可预测性。第一，现金流关联必须具有方向性。第二，这些关联的强度必须存在横截面异质性。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Directed cash flow linkages give rise to cross-predictability of returns between linked assets solely through equilibrium pricing effects in a frictionless, infor- mationally efficient, and complete market. The risk-based mechanism builds on fundamental principles of economically linked assets. In a consumption-based equilibrium asset pricing model which allows for closed-form solutions, expected excess returns are tied to differences in the strengths of cash flow linkages. Our equilibrium mechanism generates cross-predictability under two conditions. First, cash flow linkages must have a direction. Second, there must be cross-sectional heterogeneity in the strengths of these linkages.

</details>
