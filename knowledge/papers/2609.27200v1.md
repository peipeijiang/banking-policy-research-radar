<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究高维协方差矩阵在未知方向的稠密备择下的恒等性检验，其中 $p/n \to \gamma$。沿一条全局正定的二次精度路径，将高斯备择关于高斯正交系综方向进行混合，得到一个连续实验，其对数似然约化为校正后的Frobenius统计量；其上尾检验在每个固定强度下达到极限加权功效包络。固定先验的Frobenius半径仅使混合在全变差意义下扰动 $O(p^{-1/2})$，而精确白化将实验、统计量及其零分布承载到任意已知零协方差。另外，在乘积坐标零假设下，可行性仅需 $4+\eta$ 阶矩，当均值被估计时还需时间上同分布；学生化及精确自由度校正保持局部功效。一个稳定性不等式将接近包络的达到转化为与Frobenius规则的零假设一致性，因此典型稠密主体上的一致非劣性排除了在任何连续备择处获得增益。对于迹匹配的秩一备择，当 $\vartheta_n \to 0$ 且 $n\vartheta_n \to \infty$ 时，校正统计量是第一个似然方向；在固定强度下，Onatski-Moreira-Hallin固定尖峰基准中的对数似然比在Baik-Ben Arous-Peche阈值以下由一个更丰富的线性谱统计量支配，而在阈值以上特征值分离允许无代价的最大特征值增强。模拟验证了该理论。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study identity testing for high-dimensional covariance matrices against dense alternatives of unknown direction, with $p/n \to γ$. Along a globally positive quadratic precision path, mixing Gaussian alternatives over a Gaussian Orthogonal Ensemble direction yields a contiguous experiment whose log likelihood reduces to the corrected Frobenius statistic; its upper-tail test attains the limiting weighted-power envelope at every fixed strength. Fixing the prior's Frobenius radius perturbs the mixture by only $O(p^{-1/2})$ in total variation, and exact whitening carries the experiment, the statistic, and its null law to any known null covariance. Separately, under a product-coordinate null, feasibility needs only $4+η$ moments, plus identical distributions over time when means are estimated; studentization and an exact degrees-of-freedom correction preserve the local power. A stability inequality turns near-envelope attainment into null agreement with the Frobenius rule, so uniform noninferiority on the typical dense bulk precludes gains at any contiguous alternative. For trace-matched rank-one alternatives, the corrected statistic is the first likelihood direction when $\vartheta_n \to 0$ and $n\vartheta_n \to \infty$; at fixed strength, the log likelihood ratio in the Onatski-Moreira-Hallin fixed-spike benchmark is governed by a richer linear spectral statistic below the Baik-Ben Arous-Peche threshold, while eigenvalue separation permits cost-free largest-eigenvalue enhancement above it. Simulations illustrate the theory.
