<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究将k个相同物品拍卖给n个投标者的问题，其中每个投标者最多想要一个物品。这些物品具有共同但未知的价值，投标者会收到关于该价值的私人信号。我们比较歧视性价格拍卖和统一价格拍卖在拍卖价格（而非出价）向外部观察者/投资者传达真实价值的信息量方面的差异。由于这两种拍卖均存在对称且单调的均衡，问题便简化为比较样本中最高次序统计量与第(k+1)高次序统计量的信息量。我们找到了充分条件，在这些条件下，最高次序统计量在莱曼（Lehmann）意义上更具优势。这些充分条件涉及高信号与低信号的信息量以及物品与投标者数量之比k/n。这些条件在定性上也是必要的。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study auctions of k identical objects to n bidders, each of whom wants at most one. The objects have a common but unknown value and the bidders receive private signals about this value. The discriminatory price auction and the uniform-price auction are compared in terms of how informative the resulting auction prices (not bids) are in conveying the true value to an outside observer/investor. Since both auctions have symmetric, monotone equilibria, the problem reduces to comparing the informativeness of the highest order statistic of a sample to the (k+1)st highest. We find sufficient conditions under which the highest order statistic is superior---in the sense of Lehmann---in this regard. The sufficient conditions involve the informativeness of high versus low signals and the ratio k/n of objects to bidders. These conditions are also qualitatively necessary.

</details>
