<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究时间戳如何影响动态披露。在一个随机日期，发送者私下获得关于一个演化中的二元状态的硬证据，并选择何时披露它。在存在时间戳的情况下，唯一均衡的特征是即时披露好证据以及依赖时间戳的坏证据延迟。在没有时间戳的情况下，相对于不耐烦程度而言较高的先验会生成一批未披露的好证据，这些证据会在永久透明之前被随机清除。高不耐烦程度会导致好证据被即时披露，而坏证据则可以被任意延迟并以爆发式方式披露。时间戳可以防止将旧的好证据伪装成新证据，并证明坏证据是旧的，从而加速好证据的披露并促进坏证据的披露。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study how timestamps affect dynamic disclosure. At a random date, a sender privately obtains hard evidence about an evolving binary state and chooses when to disclose it. With timestamps, the unique equilibrium features immediate good-evidence disclosure and timestamp-dependent bad-evidence delay. Without timestamps, high priors relative to impatience generate a stock of undisclosed good evidence, which is stochastically purged before permanent transparency. High impatience yields immediate good-evidence disclosure, while bad evidence can be arbitrarily delayed and disclosed in bursts. Timestamps prevent pretending old good evidence is fresh and certify bad evidence is old, accelerating good-evidence disclosure and facilitating bad-evidence disclosure.

</details>
