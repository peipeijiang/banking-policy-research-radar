<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们证明，无限合作博弈的Bondareva--Shapley定理可由资产定价基本定理的一个版本推导得出。其联系在于博弈的Choquet扩张，将其视为可测简单收益空间上可能非线性的定价规则。在这一对应下，平衡性转化为无套利，而被定价规则所支配的线性泛函给出反核心中的一个分配；核心命题则由对偶性得出。应用Cornet（2025）的无套利定价定理，我们重新得到了由Schmeidler（1967）提出、并由Bartl和Pinter（2023）对下有界博弈完全一般地建立的核心非空性刻画。与Cornet（2025）中早期的推导不同，我们的论证不要求博弈具有有界变差，也不要求联盟族构成一个$σ$-代数。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We show that the Bondareva--Shapley theorem for infinite cooperative games can be derived from a version of the fundamental theorem of asset pricing. The link is the Choquet extension of a game, viewed as a possibly nonlinear pricing rule on the space of measurable simple payoffs. Under this identification, balancedness translates into no arbitrage, and a linear functional dominated by the pricing rule yields an allocation in the anti-core; the core statement follows by duality.
  Applying the arbitrage-free pricing theorem of Cornet (2025), we recover the characterization of the nonvacuity of the core initiated by Schmeidler (1967) and established in full generality by Bartl and Pinter (2023) for bounded-below games. Unlike the earlier derivation in Cornet (2025), our argument does not require the game to have bounded variation or the collection of coalitions to be a $σ$-algebra.

</details>
