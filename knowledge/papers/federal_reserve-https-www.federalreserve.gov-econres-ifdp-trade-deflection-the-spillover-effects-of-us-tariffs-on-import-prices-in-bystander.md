# IFDP Paper: Trade Deflection: The Spillover Effects of U.S. Tariffs on Import Prices in Bystander Countries

[查看原文](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/ifdp/trade-deflection-the-spillover-effects-of-us-tariffs-on-import-prices-in-bystander-countries.htm)

## 一句话结论

> 论文利用美国对华关税作为准自然实验，发现被美国关税针对的中国产品在旁观国（尤其是新兴市场）的进口价格持续下降，且这一效应在中国对美出口规模大、进口市场集中、地缘政治更亲近中国的国家中更强。

Sofie Grouws, Giulia Lo Forte, Sharon Jeon, 和 Justin Pierce 尽管关税已减少美国从中国的进口，中国的出口在全球范围内仍在持续扩张。然而，尽管这种转向贸易对全球企业和消费者具有重要意义，关于“旁观者”国家进口价格如何反应的系统性证据却寥寥无几。我们发现，被美国关税针对的中国产品在旁观者国家的进口价格持续下降，且这些降幅在新兴市场中最大。在探讨观察到的价格下降机制时，我们发现，对于中国在关税前大量出口至美国的产品、在集中度较高的进口市场以及在地缘政治上与中国更接近的国家，价格降幅更大。这些发现具有新颖性，因为在美国——即实施关税的国家——中国进口价格并未出现调整。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Sofie Grouws, Giulia Lo Forte , Sharon Jeon, and Justin Pierce Even as tariffs have reduced U.S. imports from China, Chinese exports have continued to expand globally. Yet despite the relevance of this redirected trade for firms and consumers, globally, there is little systematic evidence on how import prices have responded in "bystander" countries. We show that Chinese products targeted by U.S. tariffs experience persistent declines in import prices in bystander countries, and that these declines are largest in emerging markets. In exploring mechanisms for observed price declines, we show that they are larger for products that China exported heavily to the U.S. before the tariffs, in concentrated import markets, and in countries that are geopolitically closer to China. These findings are novel given the lack of price adjustment for Chinese imports documented in the U.S., the country that imposed the tariffs.
