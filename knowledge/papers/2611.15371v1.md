<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

人工智能以多种不同方式应用于组织中。然而，关于人工智能的企业层面文献缺乏一个能够描述这种多样性的概念框架。实证研究通常一次只考察人工智能使用的一个维度。本文认为，这一困难源于分析单位的选择：一家企业可能同时拥有许多人工智能应用，每个应用都有其自身的新颖性、获取模式、目标和技术类型。这些维度是在应用层面而非企业层面定义的。因此，我们提出将分析单位从企业转向单个人工智能应用，并构建了一个结合这四个概念维度的类型学。新框架将任何应用定位于一个共同空间中，并特别指出了主流“采用—创新”二分法所忽视的两种类型：共同发明（co-invention），即企业使用其并未开发的技术的新颖用途；以及内部化（internalisation），即企业内部构建本可以外购的人工智能。已记录的用例说明了该框架，随后该框架被用于讨论对企业战略以及人工智能在经济系统中扩散的启示。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Artificial intelligence is applied in organisations in many different ways. However, the firm-level literature on AI lacks a conceptual framework able to describe that variety. Empirical studies typically examine one dimension of AI use at a time. This paper argues that the difficulty follows from the unit of analysis: a firm may hold many AI applications at once, each with its own novelty, sourcing mode, objective and technology type. These dimensions are defined at the level of the application rather than of the firm. We therefore propose shifting the unit of analysis from the firm to the individual AI application, and develop a typology combining these four conceptual dimensions. The new framework locates any application in a common space, and it points out in particular two types that the prevailing adoption-innovation distinction neglects: co-invention, novel uses of technology the firm did not develop, and internalisation, in-house building of AI that could have been bought instead. Documented use cases illustrate the framework, which is then used to discuss implications for firms strategies and the diffusion of AI in economic systems.
