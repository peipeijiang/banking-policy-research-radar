# Observable Self-Restraint in Repeated Cheap Talk

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33390v1)

## 一句话结论

> 研究重复廉价谈话中发送者通过可观测的自我约束（滚动配额）诱导选择性建议的均衡机制，证明在正自我约束成本下发送者标准化均衡收益存在上界，并在耐心程度提高时滚动配额逼近该上界。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一位长期存续的发送者反复向一位仅能观察到推荐记录的短视接收者提供建议。非约束性、可观察的自我约束——例如对有利推荐设定滚动配额——使每一次有利推荐都消耗本可用于未来机会的容量，从而诱导选择性建议。在两种行动的情形下，当服从需要严格为正的自我约束成本时，发送者的归一化均衡收益受到一个上限的约束，该上限等于最佳可信分割信号所带来的收益减去自我约束的期望成本。这一界在任意贴现因子下均成立；在上尾条件下，随着发送者变得更有耐心，滚动配额会趋近该上界。一个足够有价值的机会也可能诱使发送者做出信息性违背，从而终止沟通。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A long-lived sender repeatedly advises a myopic receiver who observes only the recommendation record. Nonbinding, observable self-restraint, such as a rolling quota on favorable recommendations, makes each favorable recommendation use capacity that could serve future opportunities, inducing selective advice. With two actions, when obedience requires a strictly positive cost of self-restraint, the sender's normalized equilibrium payoff is subject to a ceiling equal to the payoff from the best credible partitional signal minus the expected cost of self-restraint. This bound holds at every discount factor; under an upper-tail condition, rolling quotas approach it as the sender becomes patient. A sufficiently valuable opportunity can also induce an informative violation that ends communication.

</details>
