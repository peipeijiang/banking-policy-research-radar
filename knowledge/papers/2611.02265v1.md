<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文研究在同行评审的一般均衡模型中，生成式人工智能如何通过降低研究生产成本影响科学验证。拥有私人信息的研究人员选择可观测的产出，编辑最优地分配评审深度，评审人根据其预期的科学贡献供给时间。高成本的产出有助于筛选研究质量，而更低的生产成本使较弱的研究人员更容易模仿较强的研究人员。超过某一技术门槛后，即使在最优编辑决策下，评审能力和已验证知识也会弱下降，而当筛选约束具有正影子价值时则会严格下降。评审人的退出可能放大这一损失。在质量可观测的情况下，同样的进步会弱增加二者。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper studies how generative AI, by lowering the cost of research production, affects scientific verification in a general-equilibrium model of peer review. Privately informed researchers choose observable output, an editor optimally assigns review depth, and reviewers supply time according to its expected scientific contribution. Costly output helps screen research quality, and cheaper production lets weaker researchers imitate stronger ones more readily. Beyond a technological threshold, reviewer capacity and verified knowledge weakly decline even under optimal editorial decisions, and strictly when a screening constraint has positive shadow value. Reviewer withdrawal can amplify the loss. With observable quality, the same progress weakly increases both.
