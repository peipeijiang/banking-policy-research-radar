<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种估算股票期限结构的新方法。不同于使用股息资产已实现收益的做法，我们推广了隐含资本成本方法，从观测到的股票价格横截面和预测的公司层面现金流中推导出事前预期收益的期限结构。利用1980年至2024年的美国数据，我们发现风险溢价期限结构在无条件情况下呈向上倾斜形态，并在规模、价值和信用风险维度上展现出丰富的横截面特征。引人注目的是，价值型公司和投机级公司的期限结构呈平坦甚至向下倾斜形态。我们还发现，在经济衰退期间，期限结构趋于平坦化。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a new methodology to estimate the equity term structure. Instead of using realized returns of dividend assets, we generalize the implied cost of capital approach and imply the term structure of ex-ante expected returns from the cross-section of observed stock prices and projected firm-level cash flows. Using US data for 1980-2024, we find an unconditionally upward sloping term structure of risk premia with rich cross-sectional patterns in the size, value and credit risk dimensions. Strikingly, value firms and speculative-grade firms have flat or even downward-sloping term structures. We also detect that the term structure flattens out in recessions.

</details>
