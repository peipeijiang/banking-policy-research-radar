<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文针对由核估计量构造的积分成对差分统计量建立了渐近理论，并将其应用于处理效应异质性分析。该类统计量对核估计在成对点处取值的绝对差或正部差进行积分。其两点结构与现有的一点 $L_1$ 型泛函理论不同。我们利用泊松化统计量的 Hájek 型投影建立渐近正态性，并构造可行的中心化和方差估计量。我们考虑两个应用。第一，检验处理效应是否在控制一个协变量块不变的情况下随另一个协变量块变化。第二，对条件处理效应函数的方向 Gini 平均差建立根号 $N$ 推断，该量度量结果单元的异质性。该框架还适用于单调效应修饰检验和估计的伪结果。模拟验证了渐近理论并显示出良好的有限样本性能。一个关于 401(k) 资格与净金融资产的应用说明了所提出检验的实用性。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper develops asymptotic theory for integrated pairwise-difference statistics constructed from kernel estimators, with applications to treatment effect heterogeneity. The statistics integrate absolute or positive-part differences between kernel estimates evaluated at pairs of points. Their two-point structure falls outside existing one-point $L_1$-type functional theory. We establish asymptotic normality using a Hájek-type projection of a Poissonized statistic and develop feasible centering and variance estimators. We consider two applications. First, we test whether treatment effects vary with one covariate block while holding another fixed. Second, we establish root-$N$ inference for the directional Gini mean difference of the conditional treatment effect function, which measures heterogeneity in outcome units. The framework also accommodates tests of monotone effect modification and estimated pseudo-outcomes. Simulations corroborate the asymptotic theory and show good finite-sample performance. An application to 401(k) eligibility and net financial assets illustrates the usefulness of the proposed tests.
