<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们提出了一种在不施加可加可分性假设的情况下，检验边际处理效应模型中本质异质性的方法。在倾向得分的观测支撑集上，不存在本质异质性意味着结果的條件均值关于倾向得分是仿射的，而这一仿射限制正是原假设的尖锐可检验蕴含。我们使用投影高斯过程统计量来实施该检验，该统计量对第一步估计误差具有Neyman正交性，因此乘子自助法避免了重新估计冗余参数。蒙特卡洛实验表明该方法具有准确的规模控制和良好的功效，并且与固定尺度的积分条件矩基准不同，数据驱动的中位数规则在条件集维度增大时仍能保持功效。在乌干达ELA项目的应用中，综合统计量在中线随访阶段拒绝了关于抱负不存在本质异质性的原假设。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We propose a test for essential heterogeneity in the marginal treatment effects model without imposing additive separability. On the observed support of the propensity score, no essential heterogeneity implies that the conditional mean of the outcome is affine in the propensity score, and this affine restriction is the sharp testable implication of the null. We implement the test using a projected Gaussian-process statistic that is Neyman-orthogonal to the first-step estimation error, so the multiplier bootstrap avoids re-estimating nuisance parameters. Monte Carlo experiments show accurate size control and good power, and, unlike the fixed-scale integrated conditional moment benchmark, the data-driven median rule retains power as the conditioning set grows in dimension. In an application to the Ugandan ELA programme, the omnibus statistic rejects the null of no essential heterogeneity for aspirations at the midline follow-up.
