<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在自然参与行动的完全信息连续博弈中，子博弈完美均衡未必存在。在标准正则性假设下，我们证明自然转移概率的弱原子连续性（weak atomic continuity）能够保证有限期与无限期博弈中存在可测的纯策略子博弈完美均衡。该条件源于群体遗传学文献，是子博弈完美均衡在其余原始条件上一致存在的必要条件：一旦该条件被违反，便可构造出一个动态博弈，即使允许行为策略也不存在子博弈完美均衡。该证明在无需凸性的情况下，保持了积分下延续收益对应（continuation payoff correspondences）的紧性与上半连续性。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Subgame-perfect equilibrium need not exist in continuous games with perfect information when Nature moves. Under standard regularity assumptions, we prove that weak atomic continuity of Nature's transition probabilities yields a measurable pure-strategy subgame-perfect equilibrium for finite and infinite horizons. The condition, originating in the population genetics literature, is necessary for the existence of subgame-perfect equilibrium uniformly over the remaining primitives: whenever this condition is violated, one can construct a dynamic game with no subgame-perfect equilibrium, even in behavioral strategies. The proof preserves compactness and upper hemicontinuity of continuation payoff correspondences under integration without requiring convexity.

</details>
