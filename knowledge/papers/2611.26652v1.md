<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

深度对冲（deep hedging）通过优化神经网络策略上的风险偏好来学习对冲策略。由于偏好在训练前即已固定，因此每个新的偏好都需要一个新的策略，在演员-评论家（actor-critic）方法中还需要一个新的评论家。我们提出分布深度对冲（distributional deep hedging），其中对冲损益的分布通过其分位数来学习，风险偏好随后作为这些分位数上的权重加以应用。我们刻画了允许这种表示的偏好，其中包括预期缺口（expected shortfall）及其混合，并证明熵效用（entropic utility）由相同的分位数计算得出。在当前策略下，上述每种偏好都由一个具有相同梯度的期望效用从下方界定。这一界将风险敏感对冲归结为一系列风险中性问题，由此我们推导出策略搜索算法和演员-评论家算法。在数值实验中，学习到的对冲逼近了由动态规划计算得到的参考值，而一个在无偏好条件下训练的单一分布评论家可以服务于训练后所选择的偏好。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Deep hedging learns a hedging strategy by optimising a risk preference over a neural-network policy. The preference is fixed before training, so each new preference requires a new policy and, in actor-critic methods, a new critic. We propose distributional deep hedging, in which the distribution of the hedging profit and loss is learned through its quantiles and the risk preference is applied afterwards, as a weight on those quantiles. We characterise the preferences that admit this representation, which include expected shortfall and its mixtures, and show that the entropic utility is computed from the same quantiles. At the current policy, each of these preferences is bounded below by an expected utility with the same gradient. This bound reduces risk-sensitive hedging to a sequence of risk-neutral problems, from which we derive policy-search and actor-critic algorithms. In numerical experiments, the learned hedges approach references computed by dynamic programming, and a single distributional critic trained without a preference can serve preferences chosen after training.
