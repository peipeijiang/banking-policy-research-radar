<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

团队竞赛的设计者看重的是产出而非支出；非线性转换使得这一区别具有实质性意义。我们研究了在由两两全付战斗（pairwise all-pay battles）决定、且参与者拥有私人能力的多数规则竞赛（majority-rule contests）中的两种非货币工具：披露已决结果以及将战斗日程拆分为更细的区块。两者均不改变一场战斗的平均关键性（pivotality）；它们只是将这种关键性在不同历史路径上重新分配。在嵌套信息结构下，这种重新分配使得均衡的能力调整后支出（ability-scaled expenditure）在逐参与者意义上变得弱更分散，而其均值保持不变；在所有考虑的设计中，期望总支出是不变的。由此产生的凸序比较（convex-order comparison）对期望总产出进行排序：凸产出成本偏好不披露和更粗的时间结构，凹成本偏好完全披露和更细的结构，而线性成本则使两种比较均为中性。这些排序对有限支撑和光滑连续型能力分布均成立。该机制可推广到具有唯一均衡产出分布的零次齐次组件竞赛技术（degree-zero component contest technologies）。不披露与完全披露界定了所有可容许的公开扰动（public garbling）的范围。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A team-contest designer values output rather than expenditure; nonlinear conversion makes the distinction consequential. We study two non-pecuniary instruments in majority-rule contests decided by pairwise all-pay battles with private abilities: disclosing resolved outcomes and splitting the battle schedule into finer blocks. Neither changes a battle's average pivotality; each only redistributes it across histories. Under nested information structures, this redistribution makes equilibrium ability-scaled expenditure weakly more dispersed player by player without changing its mean; expected aggregate expenditure is invariant across all designs considered. The resulting convex-order comparison ranks expected total output: convex output costs favor no disclosure and coarser temporal structures, concave costs favor full disclosure and finer ones, and linear costs make both comparisons neutral. The rankings hold for finite-support and smooth continuous-type ability distributions. The mechanism extends to degree-zero component contest technologies with a unique equilibrium outcome distribution. No and full disclosure bound every admissible public garbling.
