# Do Language Models Mix? Experimental Evidence from Asymmetric Matching Pennies

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33265v1)

## 一句话结论

> 通过非对称匹配 pennies 和指定选择概率任务测试语言模型能否选择并实施混合策略，发现模型受自身收益影响、无随机化工具时无法可靠实施指定概率，且多数模型可被机会主义对手利用。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我测试语言模型是否能使用非对称匹配 pennies（asymmetric matching pennies）和具有规定选择概率的任务来选择和实施混合策略。若干模型会对自身的收益作出反应，这与均衡相悖，却与人类实验被试相似。在没有随机化工具的情况下，没有任何被测试的模型能够可靠地实施规定的概率。仅向模型提供其选择历史并不能解决这一失败。虽然外部随机化器能显著改善实施效果，但我发现大多数模型仍然会被机会主义对手所利用。这些结果为将基于语言模型的 AI 系统中的战略决策委托提供了指导。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

I test whether language models can select and implement mixed strategies using asymmetric matching pennies and a task with prescribed choice probabilities. Several models respond to their own payoffs, contrary to equilibrium and resembling human experimental subjects. Without randomization tools, no tested model reliably implements prescribed probabilities. Prompting models with their choice histories alone does not resolve this failure. While an external randomizer sharply improves implementation, I find most models remain exploitable by an opportunistic opponent. These results provide guidance on the delegation of strategic decisions to AI systems based on language models.
