# Efficiency Adjustments Break the Logarithmic Rank Barrier

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09984v1)

## 一句话结论

> 该论文证明在i.i.d.匹配市场中，效率调整的延迟接受机制（EADA）的期望平均排名为O(log log n)，打破了DA机制的对数障碍，属于匹配理论中的机制设计研究。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究了效率调整延迟接受（Efficiency-Adjusted Deferred Acceptance, EADA）机制在独立同分布匹配市场中的期望平均排名。虽然学生提议的延迟接受（Deferred Acceptance, DA）机制使学生获得的期望平均排名为对数阶，但我们证明EADA的期望平均排名至多为$4\log\log n+O(1)$。因此，EADA改善了学生分配结果的渐近阶数。

在牺牲较弱界（$O((\log\log n)^2)$）的代价下，我们将这一结论推广到更大一类机制。具体而言，每一个弱帕累托支配DA的帕累托有效机制都能突破DA的对数障碍。这些是首次针对EADA以及更广泛的帕累托有效改进DA机制类给出的期望平均排名渐近保证。上述结论还扩展至具有有界配额的多对一市场以及具有相关偏好的随机市场。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the expected average rank achieved by the Efficiency-Adjusted Deferred Acceptance (EADA) mechanism in i.i.d.\ matching markets. While student-proposing Deferred Acceptance gives students an expected average rank of logarithmic order, we prove that EADA's expected average rank is at most $4\log\log n+O(1)$. Therefore, EADA improves the asymptotic order of students' assignments.
  At the cost of a weaker bound, $O((\log\log n)^2)$, we extend this conclusion to a much larger class of mechanisms. Namely, every Pareto-efficient mechanism that weakly Pareto-dominates DA breaks DA's logarithmic barrier. These are the first asymptotic guarantees for the expected average rank of EADA and of the broader class of Pareto-efficient improvements of DA. The conclusions extend to many-to-one markets with bounded quotas and random markets with correlated preferences.

</details>
