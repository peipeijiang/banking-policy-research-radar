<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

因果森林与正交学习器为标量或欧氏结果估计异质性处理效应，然而越来越多的结果是随机对象，例如成分数据、协方差矩阵、分布或网络，它们取值于不具有线性结构的度量空间中。我们提出测地因果森林，用于估计条件测地处理效应。测地传输将每个位于潜在结果的条件Fréchet均值之间的测地线移动到共同参考点，从而使效应在个体之间具有可比性。由于随机对象不能相加或相减，欧氏残差校正不可用；我们转而校正森林用于选择分裂和估计效应的平方距离损失。该损失对每个候选效应都是实值的，因此校正仅需Fréchet均值，并仅留下二阶 nuisance 误差；对于欧氏结果，该方法可恢复双稳健学习。我们在对弯曲空间和非光滑空间所验证的条件下，建立了一致性、收敛速率和分裂选择保证。在以协方差矩阵为结果的模拟中，该校正显著降低了估计误差。对于孟加拉国BRAC随机反贫困项目中妇女的生计构成，新的畜牧工作主要替代了临时性工资工作；在对齐之后，单一效应再现了基线活动之间百分点变化的大部分变异，并且拟合效应在前家庭佣工和年长妇女中最大。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Causal forests and orthogonal learners estimate heterogeneous treatment effects for scalar or Euclidean outcomes, yet outcomes are increasingly random objects, such as compositions, covariance matrices, distributions, or networks, in metric spaces without linear structure. We introduce geodesic causal forests for conditional geodesic treatment effects. Geodesic transport moves each geodesic between conditional Fréchet means of the potential outcomes to a common reference, making effects comparable across individuals. Because random objects cannot be added or subtracted, Euclidean residual corrections are unavailable; we instead correct the squared-distance loss through which the forest selects splits and estimates effects. The loss is real-valued for every candidate effect, so the correction requires only Fréchet means and leaves second-order nuisance error; for Euclidean outcomes, it recovers doubly robust learning. We establish consistency, convergence rates, and split-selection guarantees under conditions verified for curved and nonsmooth spaces. In simulations with covariance-matrix outcomes, the correction substantially reduces estimation error. For women's livelihood compositions in BRAC's randomized antipoverty program in Bangladesh, new livestock work mainly replaces casual wage work; after alignment, a single effect reproduces much of the variation in percentage-point changes across baseline activities, and the fitted effects are largest among former domestic workers and older women.

</details>
