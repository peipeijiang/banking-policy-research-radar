<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

本文综述了关于教育在减贫中作用的证据。教育可以通过提高生产率来减少绝对贫困，通过降低不平等来减少相对贫困，并通过改善收入之外的福祉维度来减少多维贫困。现有研究表明，教育对这三方面均有贡献。学校教育的回报很高，尤其是对于那些使弱势背景儿童能够获得更多教育的政策而言，其回报通常超过提供教育的成本。教育还通过劳动力市场溢出效应使低工资劳动者受益：减少经济中低技能劳动者的比例会提高他们的相对工资。教育的扩展在减少极端贫困方面发挥了重要作用，可以解释自1980年以来世界最贫困人口收入增长的一半以上。学校教育的非货币收益也很大，可能意味着总回报是货币收益的两倍。然而，实质性的教育不平等依然存在，对全球公平和21世纪贫困的演变轨迹具有重要影响。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

This paper surveys the evidence on the role of education in reducing poverty. Education can reduce absolute poverty by increasing productivity, relative poverty by lowering inequality, and multidimensional poverty by improving dimensions of well-being beyond income. Existing research indicates that it contributes to all three. Returns to schooling are large, especially for policies enabling children from disadvantaged backgrounds to acquire more education, and typically exceed the costs of education provision. Education also benefits low-wage workers through labor market spillovers: reducing the share of low-skilled workers in the economy raises their relative wage. The expansion of education has played a major role in reducing extreme poverty and can account for over half of income gains among the world’s poorest individuals since 1980. The non-monetary benefits of schooling are also large and may imply total returns that are twice as high as monetary gains. Substantial educational inequalities persist nonetheless, with important implications for global equity and the trajectory of poverty in the 21st century.
