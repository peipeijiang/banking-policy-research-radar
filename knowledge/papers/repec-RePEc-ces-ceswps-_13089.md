<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当装配技术专业化且重新配置成本高昂时，跨国生产网络的灵活性如何？利用2000-2023年全球汽车行业的数据，我记录了生产是围绕离散的工艺技术（平台）组织的，这些技术约束了每个产品可以在何处装配。以2011年东日本大地震作为准自然实验，我表明平台兼容性限制了企业在局部冲击后重新配置生产的能力。企业通过相互许可技术来克服这一约束。我开发并估计了一个具有离散技术选择和企業间许可的跨国生产结构模型。忽略生产技术的模型高估了关税引致的重新配置，并低估了征税国消费者所承担的价格归宿。该模型还允许研究限制技术获取的政策：这些政策可能引发比广泛关税战更多的重新配置，且成本集中在受受影响技术约束的企业和地点。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How flexible are multinational production networks when assembly technologies are specialized and costly to reconfigure? Using data on the global automotive industry from 2000-2023, I document that production is organized around discrete process technologies (platforms) that constrain where each product can be assembled. Exploiting the 2011 Tohoku earthquake as a quasi-natural experiment, I show that platform compatibility limits firms’ ability to reallocate production after local shocks. Firms overcome this constraint by licensing technologies to each other. I develop and estimate a structural model of multinational production with discrete technology choice and inter-firm licensing. Models abstracting from production technologies overstate the reallocation induced by tariffs and understate the price incidence borne by the tariffing country’s consumers. The model also allows to study policies restricting access to technologies: these can trigger more reallocation than a broad tariff war, with costs concentrated on the firms and locations bound by the affected technologies.

</details>
