<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

许多现代数据集（如跨国面板数据）具有多维结构。传统的结构向量自回归识别结构冲击的方法并不直接适用于这些数据自然组织为矩阵或张量的情形，因为参数数量快速增长且识别约束难以施加。我们引入一类适用于多维数据的结构自回归模型，通过对模型系数施加Kronecker结构来实现可处理性和全局识别。我们开发了最小距离估计量并建立了其渐近分布。为了适应近似Kronecker可分性，我们构建了对局部误设定稳健的结构脉冲响应函数置信区间，并证明了其渐近有效性。蒙特卡洛模拟验证了渐近近似的有效性以及估计量及其（稳健）置信区间良好的有限样本性能。我们将这些方法应用于一个跨国面板数据集，分析美国货币政策冲击的动态效应和溢出效应。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Many modern datasets such as cross-country panels have a multidimensional structure. Conventional approaches on structural vector autoregressions for identifying structural shocks are not directly suited to these settings where data are naturally organized as matrices or tensors, since the number of parameters grows rapidly and identifying restrictions become hard to impose. We introduce a class of structural autoregressions for multidimensional data that achieves tractability and global identification by assuming a Kronecker structure on the model coefficients. We develop a minimum-distance estimator and establish its asymptotic distribution. To accommodate approximate Kronecker separability, we develop confidence intervals for the structural impulse response functions that are robust to local misspecification, and we show their asymptotic validity. Monte Carlo simulations demonstrate the validity of the asymptotic approximations and good finite-sample performance of the estimator and their (robust) confidence intervals. We illustrate the methods on a country panel dataset to analyze the dynamic and spillover effects of US monetary policy shocks.
