<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

AI的进展可以通过两种方式反馈到AI研究中。能力提升使AI能够承担更大份额的AI研究任务，而效率提升则提高了在已自动化任务上每单位算力的AI研究产出。在一个AI研究的任务自动化模型中，我表明，软件驱动的智能爆炸——在算力和人类劳动固定的情况下AI进展的自我维持加速——要求这两个边际都足够快地改善，且任何一个边际都无法替代另一个。为了检验这些条件是否成立，我用近期AI进展的数据对两个边际进行了校准。我估计能力进展可能足够快，足以支撑智能爆炸（SIE），而效率进展则处于临界状态。在蒙特卡洛模拟中，49%的抽样出现了SIE，这表明基于AI进展的历史趋势，SIE是可能的但具有不确定性。最大的单一不确定性来源是效率进展的速度，其不确定性占关于SIE是否发生的不确定性的45%，超过所有其他参数的总和。因此，关于前沿模型中效率进展的更好数据对于理解SIE的可能性至关重要。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

AI progress can feed back into AI research in two ways. Capability improvements allow AIs to perform a larger share of AI research tasks, while efficiency improvements raise AI research output per unit of compute on tasks already automated. In a task automation model of AI research, I show that a software-driven intelligence explosion - a self-sustaining acceleration in AI progress with fixed compute and human labor - requires both margins to improve sufficiently fast, and neither margin can substitute for the other. To see whether these conditions hold, I calibrate both margins with data on recent AI progress. I estimate that capability progress is likely fast enough to support an SIE, while efficiency progress is on the knife-edge. In Monte Carlo simulations, an SIE occurs in 49% of draws, indicating that an SIE is plausible but uncertain based on historical trends in AI progress. The single largest source of uncertainty is the rate of efficiency progress, whose uncertainty is responsible for 45% of the uncertainty about whether an SIE occurs, more than all other parameters combined. Thus, better data on efficiency progress in frontier models is essential for understanding the likelihood of an SIE.

</details>
