<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

在一个任何联盟都能达成协议的市场中，即使代理人具有可替代偏好，稳定结果也未必存在。我们展示了监管如何恢复稳定性。如果监管者参与每一项协议，那么当每个普通代理人的偏好满足一个弱于可替代性的条件时，就存在一个集合稳定结果，而监管者的偏好基本不受限制。另一种存在性结果允许普通代理人拥有任意偏好，但要求监管者支持每个代理人有利可图的合同删除。我们的结果适用于涉及受监管活动的匹配市场。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

In a market in which any coalition can reach agreements, a stable outcome need not exist even when agents have substitutable preferences. We show how regulation can restore stability. If a regulator participates in every agreement, a setwise stable outcome exists when each ordinary agent's preference satisfies a condition weaker than substitutability, while the regulator's preference remains largely unrestricted. An alternative existence result allows ordinary agents to have arbitrary preferences while requiring the regulator to support each agent's profitable contract deletions. Our results apply to matching markets involving regulated activities.

</details>
