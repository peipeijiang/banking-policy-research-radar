# The Limits of Behavioral Simulation With AI

> 综述100余项研究，评估AI行为模拟与人类数据对照的准确性及其跨情境迁移能力，发现模拟对效应方向和排序较准但对效应量、分布和个体选择不可靠，且跨情境迁移证据稀缺。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们能用人工智能多好地模拟人类行为？我们回顾了100多项研究，以探讨证据对可信应用场景意味着什么。第一，在已与人类数据核对过的场景中，模拟的准确性如何？准确性随具体性下降：模拟往往能正确把握有充分文献记录的效应的方向和排序，但在模拟受访者时对效应大小的可靠性要低得多，在响应分布、子组差异和个体选择方面也是如此。第二，在一个情境中测得的准确性是否能迁移到新情境——而新情境正是模拟最有用却无法核对的地方？准确性有时能在熟悉的任务族内迁移，但超出这些任务族的证据稀少且多为负面。证据与以下解释一致：模拟依赖的是关于人已被书写内容的简化式描述，而非行为的结构模型。更好的模型并不能解决迁移问题，因为其准确性只能在有人类数据的地方测量。我们讨论了三种可能方法在新情境中提升表现的潜力。加入行为理论能将预测扩展到训练数据之外，但仅限于理论准确和完整的程度。来自与目标情境极为相似情境的额外行为训练，只有在种类高度相似时才能改善模拟。相反，接近目标情境的人类数据可用于直接测量并校正目标情境中的模拟误差，而这种校正只有在误差结构保持相似时才能迁移。

</details>

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

How well can we simulate human behavior with AI? We review over 100 studies to ask what the evidence means for credible use cases. First, how accurate are simulations where they have been checked against human data? Accuracy falls with specificity: simulations often get the direction and ranking of well-documented effects right but are far less reliable about effect sizes when simulating respondents, and about response distributions, subgroup differences, and individual choices. Second, does accuracy measured in one setting transfer to new settings, where simulation is most useful but cannot be checked? Accuracy sometimes transfers within familiar task families, but evidence beyond them is scarce and mostly negative. The evidence is consistent with simulations drawing on a reduced-form account of what has been written about people rather than a structural model of behavior. Better models do not resolve the transfer question, because their accuracy can only be measured where human data exist. We discuss the promise of three possible approaches to improve performance in new settings. Adding behavioral theory carries predictions beyond the training data, but only as far as the theory is accurate and complete. Additional training on behavior from settings just like the one of interest improves simulation only if they are highly similar in kind. Human data close to the context of interest can instead be used to directly measure and correct simulation error in the target setting, which transfers only as long as the error structure stays similar.
