# Rewarding Informed Voting in PoS Governance

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07959v1)

## 一句话结论

> 论文研究PoS治理中委托代表的投票奖励设计，证明在非中性先验下不存在使真诚投票严格最优的支付规则，并提出一种条件奖励规则可在对称正努力下实现严格真诚投票，且可能提高努力与决策准确性。

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究针对受托代表的报酬设计，这些代表获取成本高昂的信息，并就一项二元治理提案进行投票。努力既会改善私人信息，也会提高受托投票权重。我们表明，在非中性先验下，没有任何支付规则能够使真诚投票在每一个正努力水平组合上都严格最优，而固定预算会限制大型对称委员会中的准确性。随后，我们将按比例分享与一种条件规则进行比较，后者只奖励足够大的获胜联盟中的成员。与仅通过无差异来支持真诚投票的按比例分享不同，条件规则使真诚投票在对称正努力水平组合上严格最优。它支持与按比例分享相同努力水平的真诚均衡，并且在适当的报酬—成本条件下，支持严格更高努力水平和决策准确性的真诚均衡。不过，不真诚均衡仍可能同时存在。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study reward design for delegated representatives who acquire costly information and vote on a binary governance proposal. Effort improves both private information and delegated voting weight. We show that no payment rule can make sincere voting strictly optimal at every positive effort profile under a non-neutral prior, while fixed budgets limit accuracy in large symmetric committees. We then compare proportional sharing with a conditional rule that rewards only members of a sufficiently large winning coalition. Unlike proportional sharing, which supports sincere voting only through indifference, the conditional rule makes sincere voting strictly optimal at symmetric positive effort profiles. It supports sincere equilibria with the same effort as proportional sharing and, under suitable reward-cost conditions, with strictly higher effort and decision accuracy. Insincere equilibria may nevertheless coexist.

</details>
