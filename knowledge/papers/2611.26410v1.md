<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

随机权重神经网络（Random Weight Neural Networks, RWNNs）是一种浅层网络，其隐藏层参数随机生成后保持固定，仅估计输出系数。在给定采样参数的条件下，RWNN回归是一个具有随机逼近空间的线性筛问题。我们证明，此类随机筛在其维度增长时无法保持一致良态。对于满足多项式覆盖条件和Lipschitz条件的参数化特征族，我们证明总体协方差矩阵的特征值具有由参数空间的覆盖几何决定的多项式上界，从而导致最小特征值趋于零。我们建立了显式处理病态性的逼近、估计和推断理论。在$β$-Hölder源条件下，我们同时获得了快于蒙特卡洛的$L^2$逼近速率和基于分层采样的新$L^\infty$逼近界。随后，我们基于岭有效维度、谱条件和杠杆稳定性条件，推导了岭正则化最小二乘的改进一致性保证。在推断方面，我们建立了线性泛函方向的渐近线性性和高斯极限，同时允许总体协方差矩阵的最小特征值趋于零。非退化性具有方向性：我们用岭归一化Rayleigh商来刻画它，并通过Riesz表示将其推广到光滑泛函。我们证明了岭调整方差估计量的一致性，从而实现了可行的studentized推断。数值实验展示了随机特征条件数的恶化以及由此产生的估计和推断过程的有限样本行为。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Random weight neural networks (RWNNs) are shallow networks whose hidden-layer parameters are randomly generated and then kept fixed, while only the output coefficients are estimated. Conditional on the sampled parameters, RWNN regression is a linear sieve problem with random approximation space. We show that such random sieves cannot remain uniformly well-conditioned as their dimension grows. For parametrized feature families satisfying polynomial covering and Lipschitz conditions, the eigenvalues of the population covariance matrix are proven to have polynomial upper bounds determined by the covering geometry of the parameter space, leading to a vanishing smallest eigenvalue. We develop approximation, estimation, and inference theory that explicitly treats the ill-conditioning. Under a $β$-Hölder source condition, we obtain both faster-than-Monte-Carlo $L^2$ approximation rates and new $L^\infty$ approximation bounds based on stratified sampling. We then derive improved consistency guarantees for ridge-regularized least squares driven by ridge effective dimension, spectral, and leverage-based stability conditions. For inference, we establish asymptotic linearity and Gaussian limits for linear functional directions while allowing the smallest eigenvalue of the population covariance matrix to vanish. Nondegeneracy is directional: we characterize it with a ridge-normalized Rayleigh quotient and extend it to smooth functionals via Riesz representation. Consistency of ridge-adjusted variance estimators is proved, yielding feasible studentized inference. Numerical experiments illustrate the deterioration of random-feature conditioning and the finite-sample behavior of the resulting estimation and inference procedures.
