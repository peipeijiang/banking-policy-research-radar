<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们分析了从企业财报电话会议中提取的文本信息是否能够改善对欧元区职位空缺率的预测。利用总部位于欧元区的企业的会议记录，我们基于与劳动力市场压力相关的关键词构建了一个月度劳动力需求指标，并将其与标准的硬指标和软指标一同纳入混合频率贝叶斯向量自回归模型。伪实时评估显示，财报电话会议为追踪职位空缺动态提供了及时且有价值的信号。在软指标中，限制生产的因素带来了最大的预测增益，而一旦纳入定性信号，失业率等实际劳动力市场指标的增量贡献则十分有限。预测改进主要来自制造业部门的信息，其信号在信息含量上显著优于服务业，尤其是与财报电话会议结合使用时更为明显。总体而言，我们的研究结果凸显了基于文本的高频信息在改善欧元区短期劳动力需求预测方面的实用性。JEL分类：C53, E24, E27

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We analyse whether textual information extracted from firms’ earnings calls can improve forecasts of the euro area job vacancy rate. Using transcripts from euro area headquartered firms, we construct a monthly indicator of labour demand based on keywords related to labour market pressures and include it into a mixed frequency Bayesian VAR alongside standard hard and soft indicators. A pseudo–real-time evaluation shows that earnings calls provide timely and valuable signals for tracking vacancy dynamics. Among soft indicators, factors limiting production deliver the largest forecasting gains, while real labour-market indicators such as unemployment add little once qualitative signals are included. Forecast improvements are largely driven by information from the manufacturing sector, whose signals prove substantially more informative than those from services, especially when paired with earnings calls. Taken together, our results highlight the usefulness of high-frequency text-based information for improving short-term labour-demand forecasts in the euro area. JEL Classification: C53, E24, E27
