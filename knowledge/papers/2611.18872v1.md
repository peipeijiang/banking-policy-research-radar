# The opposing informational effects of AI trading

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

更好的人工智能（AI）可以改善个体预测，却未必能使价格更好地揭示新信息。在资源受限的预测环境中，竞争性市场里能力更强的算法可以用对公开记录的分析替代私有观察，从而减少聚合到价格中的私有信息。同样的交易可以将复杂的公开数据分析传递给自身无法完成此类分析的投资者。我们确立了这些效应共存且进一步计算带来的私人收益下降的条件。数值均衡计算和有限数据神经市场实验呈现出相应的模式。样本外盈利预测和受控语言模型任务显示出相关的信息替代模式。在神经实验中，更广泛的采用提高了价格对有限观察者的预测价值，降低了其对高级观察者的预测价值，并将升级带来的收益降低了44%。未升级投资者的再训练可能放大私有信息精度的损失。AI交易的信息后果取决于价格揭示了什么以及谁能够利用这些信息。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Better artificial intelligence (AI) can improve individual forecasts without making prices better at revealing new information. In a competitive market with resource-constrained forecasting, more capable algorithms can substitute analysis of public records for private observations, reducing the private information aggregated into prices. The same trades can convey sophisticated public-data analysis to investors unable to perform it themselves. We establish conditions under which these effects coexist and the private benefit of further computation falls. Numerical equilibrium calculations and finite-data neural-market experiments exhibit corresponding patterns. Out-of-sample earnings forecasts and controlled language-model tasks show related patterns of information substitution. In the neural experiment, wider adoption increases the predictive value of price for limited observers, reduces it for advanced observers, and lowers the benefit of upgrading by 44\%. Retraining by investors who did not upgrade can amplify the loss of private-information precision. The informational consequences of AI trading depend on what prices reveal and who can use them.
