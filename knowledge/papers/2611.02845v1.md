# Persuasion by Parts

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

经典贝叶斯说服假设信息设计者可以基于完整状态来设定信号。我们提出“分部说服”（persuasion by parts）。在该模型中，接收者采取行动所获得的收益为 \(X = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i\)，其中 \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) 是由不同信息源观测到的相互独立的坐标。设计者事先承诺一个乘积实验（product experiment），其中每个信息源 \(i\) 只能基于 \(X_i\) 来设定其信号。给定这些信号后，接收者计算其对 \(X\) 的后验均值 \(y\)，并以概率 \(g(y)\) 采取行动，其中 \(g\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1]\) 为非递减函数。设计者的目标是最大化行动发生的概率。

设 \(V_{\mathrm{prod}}(g)\) 和 \(V_{\mathrm{joint}}(g)\) 分别为分部说服和联合说服下行动概率的上确界。当设计者知道 \(g\) 时，我们证明
$$
V_{\mathrm{prod}}(g) \;\ge\; c\,V_{\mathrm{joint}}(g)^2 \qquad\text{对任意非递减 } g\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1]，$$
其中 \(c > 0\) 为与 \(n\) 和先验无关的绝对常数；指数 \(2\) 是紧的。当 \(g\) 未知时，我们仅从先验出发构造一个乘积实验 \(\Pi\)，其行动概率 \(V_\Pi(g)\) 满足
$$
V_\Pi(g) \;\ge\; c_\varepsilon\,V_{\mathrm{joint}}(g)^{2 + \varepsilon}
\qquad\text{对任意 } \varepsilon > 0 \text{ 及任意非递减 } g\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1]，$$
其中 \(c_\varepsilon > 0\) 仅依赖于 \(\varepsilon\)。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Classical Bayesian persuasion assumes that an information designer can condition signals on the entire state. We propose persuasion by parts. In this model, the receiver's payoff from acting is $X = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ for independent coordinates $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ observed by separate sources. The designer commits in advance to a product experiment, in which each source $i$ can condition its signal only on $X_i$. Given the signals, the receiver computes her posterior mean $y$ of $X$ and acts with probability $g(y)$, where $g\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1]$ is a nondecreasing function. The designer aims to maximize the probability of action.
  Let $V_{\mathrm{prod}}(g)$ and $V_{\mathrm{joint}}(g)$ be the supremum probabilities of action under persuasion by parts and under joint persuasion, respectively. When the designer knows $g$, we show $$
  V_{\mathrm{prod}}(g) \;\ge\; c\,V_{\mathrm{joint}}(g)^2 \qquad\text{for every nondecreasing } g\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1], $$ where $c > 0$ is an absolute constant independent of $n$ and the prior; the exponent $2$ is tight. When $g$ is unknown, we construct a product experiment $Π$ from the prior alone, whose probability of action $V_Π(g)$ satisfies $$
  V_Π(g) \;\ge\; c_\varepsilon\,V_{\mathrm{joint}}(g)^{2 + \varepsilon}
  \qquad\text{for every } \varepsilon > 0 \text{ and every nondecreasing } g\colon \mathbb{R} \to [0,1], $$ where $c_\varepsilon > 0$ depends only on $\varepsilon$.
