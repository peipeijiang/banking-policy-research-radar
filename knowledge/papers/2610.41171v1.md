<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们引入卷积偏t（convolution skew-$t$, CST）分布，该分布通过一个一般载荷矩阵将相互独立的标准化偏t子向量组合起来，以刻画异质尾部、方向不对称性和非椭圆相依性。我们建立了可识别性，并利用解析得分和Fisher信息的一维约化发展似然推断。对于有限方差模型，我们证明了最大似然估计的一致性和渐近正态性，并刻画了施加错误对称性假设所带来的后果。一种原始表示将该分布族推广至所有正尾部指数。模拟评估了有限样本性能，一个已实现波动率应用说明了允许尾部厚度和不对称性在潜在子向量之间变化的经验价值。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We introduce the convolution skew-$t$ (CST) distribution, which combines independent standardized skew-$t$ subvectors through a general loading matrix to accommodate heterogeneous tails, directional asymmetry, and non-elliptical dependence. We establish identification and develop likelihood inference using analytical scores and a one-dimensional reduction of Fisher information. For the finite-variance model, we prove consistency and asymptotic normality of maximum likelihood and characterize the consequences of imposing false symmetry. A raw representation extends the family to all positive tail indices. Simulations assess finite-sample performance, and a realized-volatility application illustrates the empirical value of allowing tail thickness and asymmetry to vary across latent subvectors.
