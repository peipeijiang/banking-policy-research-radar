<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

当选民依次评估备选项时，锚定偏差会使他们的选票取决于备选项的呈现顺序。对于赞成选票（approval ballots），没有任何非恒定投票规则能够对这种依赖性具有稳健性。我们表明，排序选票（ranked ballots）会显著改变这一图景：一个基于排序的规则对呈现顺序具有稳健性，当且仅当它仅依赖于最高偏好（tops-only）。因此，对锚定的稳健性是可行的，但代价是忽略选民在其最偏好备选项之外所报告的一切信息。

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

When voters evaluate alternatives sequentially, an anchoring bias makes their ballots depend on the order in which alternatives are presented. For approval ballots, no non-constant voting rule is robust to this dependence. We show that ranked ballots change the picture sharply: a rank-based rule is robust to the presentation order if and only if it is tops-only. Robustness to anchoring is thus available, but only at the price of ignoring everything a voter reports beyond her favourite alternative.

</details>
