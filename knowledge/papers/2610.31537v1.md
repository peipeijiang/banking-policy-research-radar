<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

因果推断中一种强大的数据结构是交错采纳（staggered adoption）：许多单位在多个时间段内被观测，在此期间一些单位在不同时间点采纳了不可逆的处理，而其他单位则保持未处理状态。适用于这一情境的常用方法包括双重差分（difference in difference）、固定效应、匹配和合成控制。所有这些方法都比较处理采纳者与非采纳者的未来结果，以回答以下问题：对于采纳处理的单位，如果它们保持未处理状态，将会实现什么样的结果？我们表明，这个问题实际上隐藏了两个截然不同的因果问题。第一个是：如果在事实采纳时间点实施阻止处理的瞬时干预，将会发生什么？第二个是：如果对处理单位实施纵向禁令，在所有时期直至结果测量时均禁止处理，该处理单位将会发生什么？我们展示了如何在条件可忽略的处理采纳或平行趋势假设下回答每个问题。我们强调，常用方法无法回答第二个问题，因为该问题需要一个具有时变权重的时变比较集。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

A powerful data structure for causal inference is staggered adoption: many units are observed over many time periods, during which some units adopt an irreversible treatment at various time points while others remain untreated. Popular methods that apply in this setting include difference in difference, fixed effects, matching, and synthetic control. All of these methods compare the future outcomes of treatment adopters and non-adopters to answer the question: for units who adopt treatment, what outcomes would have been realized if they had remained untreated? We show that this question actually hides two very different causal questions of interest. The first is what would have happened under a momentary intervention to prevent treatment at its factual adoption time. The second is what would have happened to a treated unit under a longitudinal ban preventing treatment at all periods until outcome measurement. We show how to answer each question under conditionally ignorable treatment adoption or under parallel trends. We highlight that common methods cannot answer the second question because this question requires a time-varying comparison set with time-varying weights.

</details>
