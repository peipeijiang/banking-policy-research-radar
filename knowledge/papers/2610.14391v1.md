<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

我们研究从有限总体中随机到达的选票的最优序贯聚合问题，以在固定截止时间之前识别未知的多数偏好，并在决策准确性与延迟成本之间进行权衡。最优策略是一种动态多数法定人数规则：对选票领先幅度和票数施加联合的、随时间变化的阈值，其中法定人数和绝对多数要求作为关于未参与者偏好的推断问题的解而出现。在每个时点，继续区域呈透镜形：停止所需的领先幅度随投票率增长，直到剩余选民太少而无法将其推翻，并且在截止时间时消失。我们刻画了边界在利害关系、到达过程和时间上单调的条件。由于精确边界没有闭式解，我们推导出一种大总体近似，将二维规则化简为单一充分统计量，并将该规则表示为由单一误差容限设定的序贯显著性检验。在Polkadot治理数据上进行校准后，样本外评估发现，与现行机制相比，我们的规则在2\%的误差容限下将决策时间减半。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

We study the optimal sequential aggregation of randomly arriving votes from a finite population to identify an unknown majority preference before a fixed deadline, trading off decision accuracy against the cost of delay. The optimal policy is a dynamic majority-quorum rule: a joint, time-varying threshold on vote lead and count, in which quorum and supermajority requirements emerge as the solution to an inference problem about non-participants' preferences. At each date, the continuation region is lens-shaped: the lead required to stop grows with turnout until too few voters remain to overturn it, and it vanishes at the deadline. We characterize conditions under which the boundary is monotone in the stakes, the arrival process, and time. Because the exact boundary admits no closed form, we derive a large-population approximation that reduces the two-dimensional rule onto a single sufficient statistic, and express the rule as a sequential significance test set by a single error tolerance. Calibrated on Polkadot governance data, an out-of-sample evaluation finds that our rule with a 2\% error tolerance halves decision times relative to the incumbent mechanism.
