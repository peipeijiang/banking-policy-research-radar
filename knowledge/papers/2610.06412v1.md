<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

即使分类准确率很高，交易方向符号错误也可能改变测得的交易成本。我们以Polymarket公开交易记录中每笔打印的链上结算吃单方为基准，验证其交易方向。在2026年4月至8月间选取的十二个交易日中，跨越两代交易所，92.1%至100.0%的打印记录与已结算的吃单方匹配，在所有2450万对匹配记录中，方向与代币完全一致。铸造与合并结算使吃单方成为必要：将做市方与吃单方合并会改变测得的买入份额。在结算选择之前对完整缓存磁带进行方向标注，得到1660万笔按每种规则均可标注的打印记录；等日平衡准确率对于Lee-Ready为0.942，对于逐笔测试为0.768，对于回顾性批量成交量分类为0.670。在1640万笔相同的合格成交中，在等成交权重下，Lee-Ready将有效价差提高每股0.429美分；其已实现价差差异为-0.209美分。在份额成交量权重下，吃单方五分钟中点冲击为0.689美分，而逐笔测试和批量分类分别给出-0.374和-0.119美分。当排除并列打印行时，这一总体符号反转消失。扭曲取决于误差加权的带符号结果、样本选择和加权方式。接收时间排序和未观测到的未来报价年龄限制了这些已交付报价核算量；它们并不能识别因果影响或私人信息。辅助的交易场所和采集器分析提供了描述性诊断。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

Trade-sign errors can change measured trading costs even when classification accuracy is high. We validate the side of Polymarket's public trade prints against the taker leg of each print's on-chain settlement. On twelve selected days between April and August 2026, spanning both exchange generations, 92.1% to 100.0% of prints match a settled taker leg, with exact side and token agreement on all 24.5 million matched pairs. Mint-and-merge settlement makes that taker leg essential: pooling maker and taker legs changes the measured buy share. Signing the full cached tape before settlement selection yields 16.6 million prints signed by every rule; equal-day balanced accuracy is 0.942 for Lee-Ready, 0.768 for the tick test and 0.670 for retrospective bulk volume classification. On 16.4 million identical eligible fills, Lee-Ready raises effective spread by 0.429 cents per share under equal-fill weights; its realised-spread difference is -0.209 cents. Under share-volume weights, taker five-minute midpoint impact is 0.689 cents, while tick and bulk classifications give -0.374 and -0.119 cents. That aggregate sign reversal disappears when tied print rows are excluded. Distortion depends on error-weighted signed outcomes, sample selection and weighting. Receipt-time ordering and unobserved future-quote age limit these delivered-quote accounting quantities; they do not identify causal impact or private information. Supporting venue and collector analyses provide descriptive diagnostics.
