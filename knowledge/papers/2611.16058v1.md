# Human Capital in Context: What Employment Survival Reveals About Worker-Firm Match

[查看原文](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16058v1)

<summary><strong>中文摘要</strong></summary>

一般来说，并不存在绝对最好的员工，只存在对特定企业而言最好的员工。我们构建了一种衡量员工—企业匹配质量的指标，将匹配度视为配对本身的属性，并从雇佣关系的存续中学习得到。一个双塔神经网络将员工（基于其职业经历和当前职位）与企业（基于其特征和业务描述）嵌入到同一空间中，匹配质量即为两个向量的余弦相似度。该指标的函数形式取自企业专用人力资本的技能权重模型，其唯一估计目标——离职风险——则取自将工作匹配视为体验品的视角。基于覆盖1.01亿员工和840万家企业、超过十亿人-期的数据估计，匹配质量每提高0.1，离职几率降低48%。匹配质量约四分之一的方差是员工—企业配对所特有的，而员工层面、企业层面和职业层面的度量在构造上将其设为零。匹配更好时员工收入更高，尤其在关系早期；而员工匹配得分更高的企业，在随后五年中比同行业同行增长更快、盈利能力更强、获得更多且更有价值的专利。由于该指标可以对从未形成的配对进行评分，它还能预测跳槽者的去向；当被收购方的员工与收购方的匹配程度高于与其原雇主的匹配程度时，收购方将更大比例的收购价格记为商誉。

<summary><strong>英文摘要</strong></summary>

There is no best worker in general, only a best worker for a particular firm. We develop a measure of worker-firm match quality that treats fit as a property of the pair, learned from the survival of employment relationships. A two-tower neural network embeds workers, from their career histories and current positions, and firms, from their characteristics and business descriptions, into a common space, and match quality is the cosine similarity of the two vectors. The measure takes its functional form from the skill-weights model of firm-specific human capital, and its only estimated objective, a separation hazard, from the view of the job match as an experience good. Estimated on more than a billion person-periods covering 101 million workers and 8.4 million firms, a 0.1 increase in match quality lowers the odds of separation by 48%. About a quarter of the variance of match quality is specific to the worker-firm pair, which worker-, firm-, and occupation-level measures set to zero by construction. Workers earn more when better matched, especially early in a relationship, and firms whose workforces score higher grow faster, become more profitable, and are issued more, and more valuable, patents over the next five years than their industry peers. Because it scores pairs that never formed, the measure also predicts where movers go, and acquirers record a larger share of the price as goodwill when the target's workers would fit the acquirer better than they fit the target.

</details>
